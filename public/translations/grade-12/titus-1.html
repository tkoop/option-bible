Qualifications for Elders in Every City

5For this reason I left you in Crete, so that you might set in order what remained unfinished and appoint elders in each city, just as I directed you. 6An elder must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, with faithful children who are not accused of debauchery or insubordination. 7For an overseer, as God's steward, must be above reproach: not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not addicted to wine, not violent, and not greedy for shameful gain. 8Rather, he must be hospitable, a lover of what is good, sober-minded, just, devout, and self-controlled, 9holding firmly to the trustworthy word as taught, so that he may be able both to encourage others with sound doctrine and to refute those who oppose it.

Silencing Rebellious False Teachers

10For there are many rebellious people—idle talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision— 11whose mouths must be silenced, since they ruin whole households by teaching what they ought not to teach, and do so for shameful gain. 12One of their own prophets has said, "Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons." 13This testimony is true. Therefore rebuke them sharply, so that they may become sound in the faith, 14and pay no attention to Jewish myths or to the commands of people who turn away from the truth. 15To the pure, all things are pure; but to those who are defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure. Indeed, both their mind and their conscience are defiled. 16They profess to know God, yet by their works they deny HimhimHim, being detestable, disobedient, and disqualified for every good work.
