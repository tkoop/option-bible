The Vanity of Idolatry

1Hear the word that the LORD has spoken against you, O house of Israel. 2Thus says the LORD: Do not learn the way of the nations, and do not be dismayed by the signs of the heavens, for the nations are dismayed by them. 3For the customs of the peoples are utterly futile: a man cuts a tree from the forest, and a craftsman shapes it with an axe. 4He overlays it with silver and gold, and they fasten it with nails and hammers so that it will not topple. 5They are like scarecrows in a cucumber patch; they cannot speak, and they must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no evil, nor can they do any good.

The LORD Alone Is God

6There is none like YouyouYou, the LORD; YouyouYou are great, and YouryourYour name is great in might. 7Who would not fear YouyouYou, KingkingKing of the nations? For it is YouryourYour due; for among all the wise men of the nations and in all their kingdoms, there is none like YouyouYou. 8Yet they are altogether brutish and foolish; the teaching of idols is nothing but wood. 9Silver is beaten into plates and brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz—the work of a craftsman and of a goldsmith's hands. Their garments are blue and purple; all of them are the work of skilled men. 10But the LORD is the true God; HeheHe is the living God and an everlasting King. At HishisHis wrath the earth quakes, and the nations cannot endure HishisHis indignation.

11Thus you shall say to them: "The gods that did not make the heavens and the earth shall perish from the earth and from beneath these heavens."

12He made the earth by HishisHis power; HeheHe established the world by HishisHis wisdom, and by HishisHis understanding HeheHe stretched out the heavens. 13When HeheHe utters HishisHis voice, there is a tumult of waters in the heavens, and HeheHe makes the vapors ascend from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain and brings forth the wind from HishisHis storehouses. 14Every person is brutish, devoid of knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his carved image, for his molten idol is a lie, and there is no breath in them. 15They are futile, a work of delusions; in the time of their visitation they shall perish. 16The Portion of Jacob is not like these; for HeheHe is the MakermakerMaker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of HishisHis inheritance. The LORD of hosts is HishisHis name.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Land and Jeremiah's Prayer

17Gather your belongings from the ground, O inhabitant of the besieged city. 18For thus says the LORD: "Behold, I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this time, and I will bring distress upon them, so that they may be captured."

19Woe is me because of my brokenness! My wound is severe; yet I said, "Truly, this is my grief, and I must bear it." 20My tent is plundered, and all my cords are broken. My children have gone from me and are no more; there is no one to pitch my tent again or to set up my curtains. 21For the shepherds have become brutish and have not sought the LORD; therefore they have not prospered, and all their flock is scattered. 22Behold, the sound of a report is coming—a great commotion from the north country—to make the cities of Judah a desolation, a haunt of jackals. 23The LORD, I know that the way of man is not in himself; it is not in man who walks to direct his steps. 24Correct me, the LORD, but with justice; not in YouryourYour anger, lest YouyouYou reduce me to nothing. 25Pour out YouryourYour wrath upon the nations that do not know YouyouYou, and upon the families that do not call on YouryourYour name; for they have devoured Jacob, they have devoured him and consumed him, and they have laid waste his homeland.
