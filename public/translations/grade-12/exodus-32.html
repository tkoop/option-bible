The People Demand an Idol to Lead Them

1When the people saw that Moses was delayed in coming down from the mountain, they gathered around Aaron and said, “Get up, make us gods who will go before us. As for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.”

2Aaron said to them, “Take off the gold earrings that are in the ears of your wives, your sons, and your daughters, and bring them to me.”

3So all the people took off the gold earrings from their ears and brought them to Aaron. 4He took the gold from their hands, fashioned it with an engraving tool, and made it into a molded calf. Then they said, “These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt.”

5When Aaron saw this, he built an altar before it and proclaimed, “Tomorrow is a festival to the LORD.”

6They rose early the next day and offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings. The people sat down to eat and drink, and then rose up to engage in reckless revelry.

The LORD Threatens to Destroy the People

They have made for themselves a molded calf, and have worshiped it and sacrificed to it, and said, ‘These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt.’ 9I have seen this people, and indeed, they are an obstinate people. 10Now leave MemeMe alone, so that MymyMy anger may blaze against them and destroy them, and I will make you into a great nation.”

Moses Breaks the Covenant Tablets

15Moses turned and went down from the mountain, with the two tablets of the testimony in his hand, tablets that were written on both sides; they were written on one side and on the other. 16The tablets were the work of God, and the writing was the writing of God, engraved on the tablets.

17When Joshua heard the noise of the people shouting, he said to Moses, “There is a sound of war in the camp.”

18Moses said, “It is not the sound of victory, nor the sound of defeat; I hear the sound of singing.”

19As he came near the camp, he saw the calf and the dancing, and Moses’ anger burned, and he threw the tablets from his hands and broke them at the foot of the mountain.

20He took the calf that they had made, burned it in the fire, ground it to powder, scattered it on the water, and made the Israelites drink it.

Aaron’s Excuse and Moses’ Confrontation

21Moses said to Aaron, “What did this people do to you, that you have brought a great sin upon them?” 22Aaron said, “Let not my lord’s anger burn; you know the people, that they are prone to evil. 23For they said to me, ‘Make us gods who will go before us; as for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.’ 24I said to them, ‘Whoever has gold, let him take it off.’ So they gave it to me, and I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf.”

25Moses saw that the people were running unrestrained, for Aaron had let them get out of control and become a laughingstock to their enemies.

26Moses stood in the gate of the camp and said, “Whoever is on the LORD’s side, come to me.” And all the sons of Levi gathered around him.

27He said to them, “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: ‘Let each man put his sword on his thigh; go back and forth through the camp from gate to gate, and kill his brother, his friend, and his neighbourneighbourneighbor.’”

28The sons of Levi did as Moses had said, and about three thousand men of the people fell that day.

29Moses said, “Today you have consecrated yourselves to the LORD, because each of you has been against his son and his brother, and HeheHe has bestowed a blessing on you today.”

30The next day Moses said to the people, “You have committed a great sin. Now I will go up to the LORD; perhaps I can make atonement for your sin.”

31Moses returned to the LORD and said, “Alas, this people has sinned a great sin and has made for themselves gods of gold. 32But now, if YouyouYou will forgive their sin—but if not, please blot me out of YouryourYour book that YouyouYou have written.”
