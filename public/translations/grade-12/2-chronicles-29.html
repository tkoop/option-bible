Hezekiah’s Righteous Reign Begins

1Hezekiah was twenty-five years old when he ascended the throne, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah. 2He acted uprightly in the sight of the the LORD, following the example of his father David in every respect. 3During the first month of the first year of his reign, he threw open the doors of the the LORD’s house and restored them. 4He summoned the priests and the Levites and assembled them in the eastern square.

5“Listen to me, Levites,” he said. “Consecrate yourselves at once, and consecrate the house of the the LORD, the God of your fathers. Purge every impurity from the sanctuary. 6For our fathers acted treacherously and did evil in the sight of the the LORD our God. They abandoned HimhimHim, averted their faces from the dwelling place of the the LORD, and turned their backs upon HimhimHim. 7They also closed the doors of the portico and extinguished the lamps; they offered neither incense nor burnt offerings in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace to the God of Israel. 8Therefore the wrath of the the LORD has fallen upon Judah and Jerusalem, and HeheHe has made them an object of terror, horror, and derision, as you can plainly see. 9Our fathers have fallen by the sword, while our sons, daughters, and wives have been carried into captivity. 10Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with the the LORD, the God of Israel, so that HishisHis fierce anger may turn away from us. 11My sons, do not now be negligent, for the the LORD has chosen you to stand before HimhimHim, to serve HimhimHim, to minister before HimhimHim, and to burn incense.”

The Levites Purify the Temple

12Then the Levites rose to the task: from the Kohathites, Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah; from the Merarites, Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehalelel; from the Gershonites, Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah; 13from the Asaphites, Zechariah and Mattaniah; from the Hemanites, Jehiel and Shimei; and from the Jeduthunites, Shemaiah and Uzziel. 14They assembled their brothers, consecrated themselves, and entered to cleanse the house of the the LORD, in accordance with the king’s command and the words of the the LORD. 15They began the consecration on the first day of the first month; by the eighth day they had reached the vestibule of the the LORD, and in eight more days they had consecrated the the LORD’s house, finishing on the sixteenth day of the first month. 16Then the priests entered the inner part of the the LORD’s house to purify it. They removed into the courtyard of the the LORD’s house every unclean thing they discovered in the temple, and the Levites bore them away to the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley.
