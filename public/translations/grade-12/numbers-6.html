Regulations for the Nazirite Vow

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, When a man or woman undertakes a distinctive vow—the vow of a Nazirite—to dedicate himself to the the LORD, 3he must abstain from wine and strong drink; he shall drink no vinegar derived from wine or strong drink, and he shall neither drink grape juice nor eat grapes, whether fresh or dried. 4Throughout the days of his separation, he shall eat nothing produced by the grapevine, not even its seeds or skins. 5Throughout the days of his vow of separation, no razor shall pass over his head; until the period he has consecrated to the the LORD is complete, he shall remain holy, and he shall allow the hair of his head to grow untrimmed. 6Throughout the entire period of his dedication to the the LORD, he shall not approach a corpse. 7He shall not defile himself even for his father or mother, or for his brother or sister, should they die, because the consecration to God rests upon his head. 8Throughout all the days of his separation, he is holy to the the LORD.

Restoration after Defilement

9"If someone dies suddenly in his presence and thereby defiles his consecrated head, he shall shave his head on the day of his purification; on the seventh day he shall shave it. 10On the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance to the tent of meeting, 11and the priest shall offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, making atonement for him because he incurred guilt through contact with the dead; and he shall reconsecrate his head on that same day. 12He shall then dedicate to the the LORD the remaining days of his separation and bring a one-year-old male lamb as a guilt offering; yet the former days shall be forfeited, because his consecration was defiled.

Completion of the Nazirite Vow

13"This is the law governing the Nazirite: when the days of his separation are fulfilled, he shall be brought to the entrance of the tent of meeting, 14and he shall present his offering to the the LORD: one unblemished male lamb a year old for a burnt offering, one unblemished ewe lamb a year old for a sin offering, and one unblemished ram for a peace offering, 15together with a basket of unleavened bread—cakes of fine flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers spread with oil—along with their grain offering and drink offerings. 16The priest shall bring them before the the LORD and present his sin offering and burnt offering, 17and he shall offer the ram as a peace offering to the the LORD, along with the basket of unleavened bread; the priest shall also present its grain offering and drink offering. 18The Nazirite shall then shave his consecrated head at the entrance to the tent of meeting, take the hair from his head, and place it upon the fire beneath the peace offering. 19The priest shall take the shoulder of the ram once it has been boiled, together with one unleavened loaf from the basket and one unleavened wafer, and place them upon the hands of the Nazirite after he has shaved off his hair. 20The priest shall wave them as a wave offering before the the LORD; this portion is holy for the priest, together with the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution. Thereafter the Nazirite may drink wine. 21This is the law of the Nazirite. If he vows an offering to the the LORD in accordance with his separation, beyond what his means allow, he shall fulfillfulfilfulfill the vow he has made, in addition to the requirements of his separation."
