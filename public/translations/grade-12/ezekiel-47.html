The River Flowing from the Temple

1He brought me back to the entrance of the temple, and, behold, water was flowing out from under the threshold of the temple toward the east—for the temple faced east—and the water was flowing down from under the south side of the temple, south of the altar. 2Then he brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around the outside to the outer gate facing east; and, behold, water was trickling from the south side.

3When the man went out toward the east with a measuring line in his hand, he measured a thousand cubits and led me through the water, and it was ankle-deep. 4He measured another thousand cubits and led me through the water, and it was knee-deep; then he measured another thousand and led me through the water, and it was waist-deep. 5He measured another thousand, and it was a river that I could not cross, for the water had risen and was deep enough to swim in—a river that could not be crossed.

6He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen this?” Then he led me and brought me back to the bank of the river.

7When I returned, I saw that on the bank of the river there were very many trees on both sides.

8He said to me, “These waters go out to the eastern region and flow down into the Arabah. When they enter the sea—the sea of stagnant waters—the water will become fresh. 9Wherever the river goes, every living creature that swarms will live, and there will be very many fish, because these waters go there and become fresh; so everything will live wherever the river reaches.”

10Fishermen will stand along its banks; from En-gedi to En-eglaim it will be a place for spreading nets. Its fish will be of many kinds, like the fish of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea, exceedingly abundant.

11But its swamps and marshes will not become fresh; they will be left for salt.

12Along both banks of the river, every tree for food will grow; its leaf will not wither, nor will its fruit fail. It will bear fresh fruit every month, because its water flows from the sanctuary. Its fruit will be for food, and its leaves for healing.

The Boundaries of the Promised Land

13Thus says the Lord, the LORD: “This is the boundary by which you shall inherit the land according to the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall have two portions. 14And you shall inherit it, each as his brother, because I swore to give it to your fathers; this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.

“15This is the boundary of the land: on the north side, from the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea by way of Hethlon toward Zedad. 16Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath; and Hazar-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran. 17The boundary shall be from the sea to Hazar-enan, along the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath to the north; this is the north side. 18On the east side, from between Hauran and Damascus, along the Jordan, between Gilead and the land of Israel, to the eastern sea, as far as Tamar; this is the east side. 19On the south side, from Tamar to the waters of Meribah-kadesh, along the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt to the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea; this is the south side. 20On the west side, the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea shall be the boundary, up to a point opposite Lebo-hamath; this is the west side.

Inheritance for Israel and the Resident Aliens

“21You shall divide this land among you according to the tribes of Israel. 22You shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the strangers who sojourn among you, who bear children among you; and they shall be to you as native-born among the children of Israel. With you they shall be allotted an inheritance among the tribes of Israel. 23In whatever tribe the stranger sojourns, there you shall give him his inheritance, declares the Lord, the LORD.”
