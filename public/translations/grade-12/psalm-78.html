A Reflective Psalm of Asaph

1Listen attentively, my people, to my instruction; turn your ears toward the words of my mouth. 2I will open my mouth in a parable; I will utter enigmatic sayings handed down from antiquity, 3things that we have heard and known, that our fathers have told us. 4We will not hide them from their children, but we will recount to the succeeding generations the renowned acts of the LORD, HishisHis power, and the wonders that HeheHe has done.

5He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed instruction in Israel, which HeheHe commanded our fathers to teach to their children, 6so that the generation to come might know them, even children yet unborn, and that they might arise and declare them to their children, 7so that they might place their confidence in God and not forget God's deeds, but observe HishisHis commandments. 8They were not to be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation whose heart was not resolute and whose spirit was not faithful toward God.

Israel's Unfaithfulness in the Wilderness

9The Ephraimites, though equipped with bows, retreated on the day of battle. 10They did not keep God's covenant, but refused to walk in accordance with HishisHis law. 11They forgot HishisHis works and the wonders that HeheHe had shown them. 12In the sight of their fathers HeheHe performed wonders in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. 13He divided the sea and enabled them to pass through it, causing the waters to stand like a wall. 14By day HeheHe led them with a cloud, and throughout the night with a blazing fire. 15He split rocks in the wilderness and gave them water to drink in abundance, as from the ocean depths. 16He brought forth streams from the rock and made waters flow down like rivers. 17Yet they continued to sin against HimhimHim, rebelling against the MostmostMost HighhighHigh in the wilderness. 18They tested God in their hearts by demanding the food they craved. 19They spoke against God, saying, "Can God set a table in the wilderness? 20He struck the rock so that water gushed out and streams overflowed. Can HeheHe also give bread, or provide meat for HishisHis people?"

21Therefore, when the LORD heard, HeheHe was filled with wrath; a fire was kindled against Jacob, and HishisHis anger rose against Israel, 22because they did not believe in God and did not trust in HishisHis saving power. 23Yet HeheHe commanded the skies above and opened the doors of Heavenheavenheaven, 24and HeheHe rained down manna upon them to eat and gave them the grain of Heavenheavenheaven. 25Mortals ate the bread of the mighty; HeheHe sent them provisions in abundance. 26He caused the east wind to blow through the heavens, and by HishisHis power HeheHe led forth the south wind. 27He rained meat upon them like dust, winged birds like the sand of the seas. 28He let them fall in the midst of their camp, all around their dwellings. 29And they ate and were well filled, for HeheHe gave them what they craved. 30But before they had satisfied their craving, while the food was still in their mouths, 31the anger of God rose against them, and HeheHe struck down the strongest among them and laid low the young men of Israel.

32In spite of all this, they still sinned; despite HishisHis wonders, they did not believe. 33So HeheHe caused their days to vanish like a breath, and their years to end in terror. 34When HeheHe struck them down, they sought HimhimHim; they repented and sought God earnestly. 35They remembered that God was their rock, and that the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God was their Redeemer. 36But they flattered HimhimHim with their mouths and lied to HimhimHim with their tongues. 37Their hearts were not steadfast toward HimhimHim, and they were not faithful to HishisHis covenant. 38Yet HeheHe, being compassionate, atoned for their iniquity and did not destroy them; HeheHe restrained HishisHis anger often and did not stir up all HishisHis wrath. 39He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and does not return. 40How often they rebelled against HimhimHim in the wilderness and grieved HimhimHim in the desert! 41They tested God again and again and provoked the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 42They did not remember HishisHis power or the day when HeheHe redeemed them from the foe.

The Plagues and Deliverance from Egypt

43He performed HishisHis signs in Egypt and HishisHis marvels in the fields of Zoan. 44He turned their rivers to blood, so that they could not drink from their streams. 45He sent among them swarms of flies that devoured them, and frogs that destroyed them. 46He gave their crops to the destroying locust and the fruit of their labourlabourlabor to the locust. 47He destroyed their vines with hail and their sycamore figs with frost. 48He gave their cattle over to the hail and their flocks to thunderbolts. 49He let loose upon them HishisHis burning anger, wrath, indignation, and distress—a company of destroying angels. 50He made a path for HishisHis anger; HeheHe did not spare them from death, but gave their lives over to the plague. 51He struck down every firstborn in Egypt, the firstfruits of their strength in the tents of Ham. 52Then HeheHe led out HishisHis people like sheep and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. 53He led them in safety, so that they were not afraid, but the sea overwhelmed their enemies. 54And HeheHe brought them to HishisHis holy land, to the mountain that HishisHis right hand had won. 55He drove out nations before them; HeheHe apportioned them a possession and settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

Renewed Apostasy, JudgmentJudgementJudgment, and the Choice of David

56Yet they tested and rebelled against the MostmostMost HighhighHigh God and did not keep HishisHis testimonies, 57but turned back and acted treacherously like their fathers; they proved as treacherous as a warped bow. 58For they provoked HimhimHim to anger with their high places and moved HimhimHim to jealousy with their idols. 59When God heard, HeheHe was filled with wrath, and HeheHe utterly rejected Israel. 60He forsook HishisHis dwelling at Shiloh, the tent where HeheHe dwelt among mankind, 61and delivered HishisHis power to captivity and HishisHis glory into the hand of the foe. 62He gave HishisHis people over to the sword and showed wrath against HishisHis heritage. 63Fire devoured their young men, and their maidens had no nuptial song. 64Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows made no lamentation. 65Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a warrior roused by wine. 66And HeheHe put HishisHis adversaries to rout; HeheHe consigned them to everlasting shame. 67He rejected the tent of Joseph; HeheHe did not choose the tribe of Ephraim, 68but HeheHe chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which HeheHe loves. 69He built HishisHis sanctuary like the high heavens, like the earth, which HeheHe has founded forever. 70He chose David HishisHis servant and took him from the sheepfolds; 71from following the nursing ewes HeheHe brought him to shepherd Jacob HishisHis people, Israel HishisHis inheritance. 72With an upright heart he shepherded them and guided them with skilfulskilfulskillful hand.
