Seeking the Things Above

1Therefore, if you have been raised with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God. 2Direct your thoughts toward things above, not toward earthly concerns. 3For you have died, and your life is concealed with Christ in God. 4When Christ, who is your life, is revealed, then you also will be revealed with HimhimHim in glory.

Mortifying Earthly Passions

5Mortify, therefore, the members of your earthly nature: sexual immorality, impurity, lustful passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry. 6It is because of these vices that the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience. 7You too once walked in these things when you were living among them.

The Renewed Life in Christ

8But now you must put away all these: anger, wrath, malice, slander, and obscene speech from your mouth. 9Do not lie to one another, since you have stripped off the old self with its practices 10and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its CreatorcreatorCreator. 11In this renewed order there is no Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, or free; Christ is all and in all.

12Therefore, as God's elect, holy and beloved, clothe yourselves with compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. 13Bear with one another and forgive whatever grievance you may have against another; forgive as the Lord forgave you. 14Above all these, put on love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony. 15Let the peace of Christ govern in your hearts, since you were called to it in one body; and be thankful. 16Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, as you teach and admonish one another in all wisdom, singing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs with gratitude in your hearts to God. 17And whatever you do, in word or deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the FatherfatherFather through HimhimHim.

Instructions for the Christian Household

18Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. 19Husbands, love your wives and do not be embittered against them. 20Children, obey your parents in everything, for this pleases the Lord. 21Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they become discouraged.

22Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything—not merely to please them while they are watching, but with sincere hearts, fearing the Lord. 23Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men. 24Knowing that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as your reward. It is the Lord Christ whom you serve. 25For the wrongdoer will be repaid for his wrongdoing, and there is no partiality.
