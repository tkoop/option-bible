The LORD Lays Waste the Earth

1Behold, the LORD is about to lay waste the earth and devastate it; HeheHe will distort its surface and scatter its inhabitants.

2It will be as with the people, so with the priest; as with the slave, so with his master; as with the maidservant, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the creditor, so with the debtor.

3The EarthEarthearth will be utterly emptied and utterly plundered, for the LORD has spoken this word.

4The EarthEarthearth mourns and withers; the world languishes and fades; the exalted of the earth languish. 5The EarthEarthearth is defiled under its inhabitants, for they have transgressed the laws, violated the statutes, and broken the everlasting covenant. 6Therefore a curse devours the earth, and its inhabitants bear their guilt; therefore the inhabitants of the earth are scorched, and few people are left.

7The new wine mourns, the vine languishes, and all the merry-hearted sigh. 8The mirth of the tambourines ceases, the uproar of the revelers ends, and the mirth of the lyre is stilled. 9They no longer drink wine with singing; strong drink is bitter to those who drink it.

10The ruined city is broken down; every house is shut up so that no one may enter. 11There is an outcry in the streets over the wine; all joy grows dark; the gladness of the earth is banished. 12Desolation remains in the city, and the gate is struck down in ruin.

A Gleaning and a Song of Praise

13For thus it will be in the midst of the earth among the peoples, as at the gleaning of an olive tree, as at the gleanings when the grape harvest is done.

14They lift up their voices and sing for joy; over the majesty of the LORD they shout from the west. 15Therefore in the east give glory to the LORD; in the coastlands of the sea, give glory to the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

16From the ends of the earth we hear songs of praise to the RighteousrighteousRighteous OneoneOne. But I said, "I am wasting away! I am wasting away! Woe is me! The treacherous betray; the treacherous betray utterly."

Terror, Pit, and Snare

17Terror and the pit and the snare are upon you, O inhabitant of the earth! 18He who flees at the sound of terror will fall into the pit, and he who climbs out of the pit will be caught in the snare. For the windows of Heavenheavenheaven are opened, and the foundations of the earth tremble. 19The EarthEarthearth is utterly broken, the earth is split apart, and the earth is violently shaken. 20The EarthEarthearth reels like a drunkard and sways like a hut; its transgression lies heavy upon it, and it falls, never to rise again.

The Final Reckoning

21On that day the LORD will punish the host of Heavenheavenheaven in Heavenheavenheaven, and the kings of the earth on the earth. 22They will be gathered together as prisoners in a pit; they will be shut up in a prison, and after many days they will be punished. 23Then the moon will be confounded, and the sun ashamed, for the LORD of hosts reigns on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, and HishisHis glory is before HishisHis elders.
