Woe to the Land of Whirring Wings

1Woe to the land of whirring wings, beyond the rivers of Cush! 2It sends envoys by sea in papyrus boats across the waters. “Go, swift messengers, to a nation tall and smooth, to a people feared near and far, a nation mighty and conquering, whose land the rivers divide.” 3All you inhabitants of the world and dwellers on EarthEarthearth, when a standard is raised on the mountains, you see it; when a trumpet sounds, you hear it.

The LORD Watches from His Dwelling Place

4For thus says the the LORD to me: “I will be quiet and watch from MymyMy dwelling place, like shimmering heat in the sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.” 5For before the harvest, when the blossom fades and the flower becomes a ripening grape, HeheHe will cut off the shoots with pruning knives and remove the spreading branches. 6They will be left together for the mountain birds of prey and the beasts of the earth; the birds of prey will feed on them through the summer, and all the beasts of the earth will feed on them through the winter.

A Gift Brought to Mount Zion

7At that time a gift will be brought to the the LORD of Hosts from a people tall and smooth, from a people feared near and far, a nation mighty and conquering, whose land the rivers divide—to the place of the name of the the LORD of Hosts, Mount Zion.
