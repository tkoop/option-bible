Jacob Meets Esau

1Upon lifting his eyes, Jacob beheld Esau approaching with four hundred men; consequently, he divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two maidservants. 2He stationed the maidservants and their children foremost, Leah and her children next, and Rachel with Joseph in the rear. 3He himself passed on before them, prostrating himself to the ground seven times until he reached his brother. 4Yet Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, fell upon his neck, kissed him, and they wept together.

5When Esau raised his eyes and perceived the women and children, he inquired, "Who are these with you?"

Jacob replied, "They are the children whom God has graciously bestowed upon your servant."

6Then the maidservants approached with their children and bowed low. 7Leah and her children likewise drew near and prostrated themselves, and afterward Joseph and Rachel approached and did the same.

Esau Receives Jacob's Gift

8Esau inquired, "What do you intend by all this company I encountered?"

Jacob answered, "To find favourfavourfavor in the sight of my lord."

9Esau said, "I have abundance, my brother; keep what you have."

10Jacob said, "No, please—if I have found favourfavourfavor in your sight, accept this gift from my hand. I have seen your face, and it is as though I had seen the face of God, and you have received me graciously. 11Please accept my blessing, which has been brought to you, because God has dealt graciously with me, and I have all I need." So he urged him, and Esau accepted it.

Jacob Declines Esau's Escort

12Then Esau said, "Let us journey on together, and I will go ahead of you."

13Jacob replied, "MymyMy lord knows that the children are frail and that the flocks and herds are nursing; if they are driven hard for even one day, the whole flock will perish. 14Let my lord pass on ahead of his servant, and I will proceed at a leisurely pace, according to the pace of the livestock ahead of me and the pace of the children, until I come to my lord in Seir."

15So Esau said, "Let me leave with you some of the people who are with me."

But Jacob said, "Why should I find favourfavourfavor in the sight of my lord?"

Jacob Settles in Canaan

16So Esau returned that day on his way to Seir. 17But Jacob journeyed to Succoth and built a house for himself, and he made shelters for his livestock; therefore the place was named Succoth.

18Jacob arrived safely at the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan, on his way from Paddan-aram, and he camped before the city. 19From the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, he purchased for a hundred pieces of money the parcel of land where he had pitched his tent. 20There he erected an altar and called it El-Elohe-Israel.
