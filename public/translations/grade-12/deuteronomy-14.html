A Holy People and the Prohibition of Pagan Mourning

1You are children of the LORD your God; you must not lacerate yourselves nor shave a bald patch between your eyes for the dead. 2For you are a holy people to the LORD your God, and the LORD has chosen you to be a people for HimselfhimselfHimself, a treasured possession above all the peoples on the face of the earth.

Clean and Unclean Foods

3You shall not eat any abominable thing. 4These are the beasts you may eat: the ox, the sheep, the goat, 5the deer, the gazelle, the roe deer, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep. 6You may eat every beast that has a divided hoof and is cloven-footed and chews the cud. 7Yet of those that chew the cud or have a divided hoof, you shall not eat these: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger, because they chew the cud but do not part the hoof; they are unclean to you. 8And the pig, because it parts the hoof but does not chew the cud, is unclean to you; you shall not eat their flesh, nor shall you touch their carcass.

9Of all that is in the waters, you may eat these: everything that has fins and scales you may eat. 10But you shall not eat whatever lacks fins and scales; it is unclean to you.

11You may eat any clean bird. 12But these you shall not eat: the eagle, the vulture, the black vulture, 13the kite, the falcon, and the buzzard of any kind; 14every raven of any kind; 15the ostrich, the short-eared owl, the gull, and the hawk of any kind; 16the little owl, the great owl, the white owl, 17the pelican, the carrion vulture, the cormorant, 18the stork, the heron of any kind, the hoopoe, and the bat. 19And every swarming winged creature is unclean to you; it shall not be eaten. 20You may eat any clean bird.

21You shall not eat any carcass; you may give it to the sojourner within your gates, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner, for you are a holy people to the LORD your God. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

Tithes, Pilgrimage Feasts, and Provision for the Needy

22You shall surely tithe all the produce of your seed that comes from the field year by year. 23And you shall eat before the LORD your God, in the place HeheHe chooses to make HishisHis name dwell, the tithe of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and flock, so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God all your days.

24And if the way is too long for you, so that you are not able to carry it, because the LORD your God has blessed you, and the place the LORD your God chooses to set HishisHis name is too far from you, 25then you shall convert it into money, bind the money in your hand, and go to the place the LORD your God chooses. 26And you shall spend the money on whatever your soul desires—on oxen, sheep, wine, strong drink, or whatever your soul asks of you—and you shall eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household.

27And you shall not forsake the Levite within your gates, for he has no portion or inheritance with you. 28At the end of every third year you shall bring out all the tithe of your produce for that year and store it within your gates. 29And the Levite, because he has no portion or inheritance with you, and the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates, shall come and eat and be satisfied, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hand that you do.
