The Righteous Branch from Jesse

1A shoot shall spring from the stump of Jesse, and a BranchbranchBranch from his roots shall bear fruit. 2The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD shall rest upon HimhimHim—a SpiritspiritSpirit of wisdom and understanding, a SpiritspiritSpirit of counsel and might, a SpiritspiritSpirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD. 3His delight shall be in the fear of the LORD; HeheHe shall not judge by the sight of HishisHis eyes, nor decide by the hearing of HishisHis ears.

4Rather, with righteousness HeheHe shall judge the poor, and with equity HeheHe shall decide for the meek of the earth. He shall strike the earth with the rod of HishisHis mouth, and with the breath of HishisHis lips HeheHe shall slay the wicked. 5Righteousness shall be the belt about HishisHis waist, and faithfulness the sash about HishisHis hips.

Peace in the Messiah's Kingdom

6The wolf shall dwell with the lamb, the leopard shall lie down with the goat, the calf and the young lion and the fattened calf together, and a little child shall lead them. 7The cow and the bear shall graze; their young shall lie down together; and the lion shall eat straw like the ox. 8The nursing child shall play over the hole of the cobra, and the weaned child shall put his hand into the viper's den. 9They shall not hurt nor destroy in all MymyMy holy mountain, for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.

The Gathering of God's Remnant

10In that day the RootrootRoot of Jesse shall stand as a banner for the peoples; the nations shall seek HimhimHim, and HishisHis resting place shall be glorious. 11On that day the Lord shall extend HishisHis hand a second time to recover the remnant of HishisHis people from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the coastlands of the sea. 12He shall raise a banner for the nations and gather the outcasts of Israel; HeheHe shall assemble the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

13The jealousy of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off. Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not harass Ephraim. 14They shall swoop down upon the Philistines in the west; together they shall plunder the people of the east. They shall lay their hands on Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites shall obey them. 15The LORD shall dry up the gulf of the SeaseaSea of Egypt; with a scorching wind HeheHe shall wave HishisHis hand over the Euphrates and strike it into seven streams, so that people may cross in sandals. 16There shall be a highway for the remnant of HishisHis people who remain from Assyria, as there was for Israel when they came up from the land of Egypt.
