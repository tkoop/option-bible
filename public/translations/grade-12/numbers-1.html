The Census of Israel in the Wilderness of Sinai

1On the first day of the second month in the second year after the Israelites had departed from Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Sinai, saying:

2"EnrolEnrolEnroll the entire Israelite community according to their clans and ancestral houses, recording every male individually by name. 3You and Aaron shall register all men twenty years old and upward who are fit for military service in Israel, each according to his division. 4One man from each tribe—each the patriarchal head of his ancestral house—shall assist you. 5These are the names of the men who shall serve alongside you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur; 6from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai; 7from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab; 8from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar; 9from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; 10from the sons of Joseph—from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur; 11from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni; 12from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; 13from Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran; 14from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel; 15and from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

16These men were chosen from the congregation; they were the leaders of their ancestral tribes and the heads of the clans of Israel. 17Moses and Aaron took these men, who had been designated by name, 18and on the first day of the second month they assembled the whole congregation. The people registered their lineage by clans and ancestral houses, and every male twenty years old and upward was recorded individually by name, 19just as the LORD had commanded Moses. Thus he counted them in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Sinai.

The Tribe-by-Tribe Enumeration

20From the descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 21The number recorded for the tribe of Reuben was 46,500.

22From the descendants of Simeon, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 23The number recorded for the tribe of Simeon was 59,300.

24From the descendants of Gad, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 25The number recorded for the tribe of Gad was 45,650.

26From the descendants of Judah, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 27The number recorded for the tribe of Judah was 74,600.

28From the descendants of Issachar, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 29The number recorded for the tribe of Issachar was 54,400.

30From the descendants of Zebulun, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 31The number recorded for the tribe of Zebulun was 57,400.

32From the descendants of Ephraim, son of Joseph, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 33The number recorded for the tribe of Ephraim was 40,500.

34From the descendants of Manasseh, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 35The number recorded for the tribe of Manasseh was 32,200.

36From the descendants of Benjamin, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 37The number recorded for the tribe of Benjamin was 35,400.

38From the descendants of Dan, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 39The number recorded for the tribe of Dan was 62,700.

40From the descendants of Asher, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 41The number recorded for the tribe of Asher was 41,500.

42From the descendants of Naphtali, their lineages were recorded by clans and ancestral houses; every male twenty years of age or older who was fit for military service was listed individually by name. 43The number recorded for the tribe of Naphtali was 53,400.

44These were the men counted by Moses and Aaron, together with the twelve leaders of Israel, each one representing his ancestral house. 45Thus all the Israelites twenty years old and upward who were able to serve in Israel's army were counted according to their ancestral houses. 46The aggregate of all those enrolled was 603,550.

47The Levites, however, were excluded from this enumeration according to their ancestral tribe.

The Levites' Separate Appointment

48The LORD had said to Moses:

49"You must not enumerate the tribe of Levi or register it alongside the other Israelites. 50Instead, assign the Levites to the tabernacle of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony and to all its furnishings and everything belonging to it. They shall transport the tabernacle and all its furnishings; they shall attend to it and encamp around it. 51Whenever the tabernacle is to be moved, the Levites shall take it down, and whenever it is pitched, they shall set it up. Any unauthorized person who approaches it shall be executed. 52The Israelites shall encamp by their divisions, each man in his own camp under his own standard. 53The Levites shall encamp around the tabernacle of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony, lest wrath befall the Israelite community; the Levites shall maintain custody of the tabernacle of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony."

54The Israelites did all of this just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
