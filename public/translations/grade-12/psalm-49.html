The Futility of Earthly Riches

1For the choir director: a psalm of the sons of Korah. Hear this, all peoples; give ear, all inhabitants of the world.

2Both the lowborn and the highborn, the rich and the poor together— 3my mouth speaks wisdom, and the meditation of my heart is understanding. 4I will incline my ear to a proverb and unfold my riddle to the music of the harp.

5Why should I fear in days of adversity, when deceitful adversaries close in around me? 6Those who trust in their wealth and boast in the abundance of their riches. 7Yet no one can redeem a brother or pay God his ransom— 8for the price of a life is too costly to be paid forever— 9so that he might live on endlessly and never see the grave.

10For he sees that the wise die; the fool and the brute perish together and leave their wealth to others. 11Their inward thought is that their houses will last forever and their dwellings endure for all generations; they call their lands by their own names. 12Yet man, though honouredhonouredhonored, does not remain; he is like the beasts that perish.

13Such is the path of the foolish, and of those after them who approve their words. Selah.

14Like sheep they are destined for Sheol; death will be their shepherd, and the upright will rule over them in the morning. Their forms will waste away in Sheol, far from their dwellings. 15But God will redeem my soul from the hand of Sheol, for HeheHe will receive me. Selah.

16Do not fear when a man becomes rich, when the glory of his house increases. 17For when he dies he carries nothing away; his glory does not descend after him. 18For in his lifetime he congratulates himself, and people praise you when you prosper— 19yet he must go to the generation of his fathers, and they will never see the light. 20Man, though honouredhonouredhonored and without understanding, is like the beasts that perish.
