The Perpetual Lamp and the Showbread

1The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2“Command the Israelites to bring to you clear oil of beaten olives for the light, so that a lamp may burn continually. 3Outside the veil of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony, in the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, Aaron shall arrange it continually from evening until morning before the LORD, a perpetual statute throughout your generations. 4He shall arrange the lamps on the pure lampstand before the LORD continually.”

5“You shall take fine flour and bake it into twelve loaves; two-tenths of an ephah shall be in each loaf. 6You shall set them in two rows, six in a row, on the pure table before the LORD. 7You shall put pure frankincense on each row, so that it may be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to the LORD. 8Every Sabbath day he shall arrange it before the LORD continually, from the Israelites as an everlasting covenant. 9It shall be for Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to him from the offerings by fire to the LORD, a perpetual statute.”

The Blasphemer in the Camp

10Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the Israelites; and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp. 11The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the NamenameName and cursed, so they brought him to Moses. His mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan. 12They put him in custody until it was declared to them according to the mouth of the LORD.
