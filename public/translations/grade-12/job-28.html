The Hidden Work of Mining the Earth

1Surely silver has its source, and gold a place where it is refined. 2Iron is taken from the dust, and copper is smelted from stone.

3He sets a boundary to darkness and searches out the farthest limit—the stone of gloom and deep shadow. 4A shaft breaks open far from where men dwell; untrodden by the foot, they hang and sway, remote from humankind. 5As for the earth, food comes from it, yet beneath it is overturned as though by fire. 6Its stones are a place of sapphires, and its dust contains gold.

7No bird of prey knows that path, nor has the falcon's eye seen it. 8The proud beasts have not trodden it, and no lion has passed over it. 9He lays his hand upon the flinty rock and overturns mountains by their roots. 10He cuts channels through the rock, and his eye sees every precious thing. 11He dams the streams so they do not trickle, and brings what is hidden into the light.

Wisdom Beyond Human Reach

12But where can wisdom be found, and where is the place of understanding? 13Man does not know its value, nor is it found in the land of the living.

14The deep says, "It is not in me."

And the sea says, "It is not with me."

15It cannot be purchased with gold, nor can silver be weighed out as its price. 16It cannot be valued in Ophir's gold, nor in precious onyx or sapphire. 17Neither gold nor glass can equal it, nor can it be exchanged for vessels of fine gold. 18Coral and crystal are scarcely worth mentioning; the price of wisdom is above pearls. 19The topaz of Cush cannot equal it; it cannot be valued in pure gold.

The Source of Wisdom

20From where, then, does wisdom come, and where is the place of understanding? 21It is hidden from the eyes of all living and concealed from the birds of the sky.

22Abaddon and Death say, "We have heard a rumourrumourrumor of it with our ears."

God's Knowledge of Wisdom's Way
