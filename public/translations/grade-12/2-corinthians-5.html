The Earthly Tent and the Heavenly Building

1For we know that if the tent that constitutes our earthly dwelling is destroyed, we possess an edifice from God—a house not fashioned by human hands, eternal in the heavens. 2For in this present tent we groan, yearning to be clothed with our heavenly habitation, 3if indeed, having been clothed, we shall not be discovered naked. 4For while we remain in this tent, we groan, burdened as we are—not wishing to be stripped of it, but wishing to be further clothed, so that mortality may be swallowed up by life.

5Now HeheHe who prepared us for this very purpose is God, who has also bestowed upon us the SpiritspiritSpirit as a guarantee. 6Therefore we are always courageous, knowing that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord. 7For we walk by faith, not by sight.

8We are courageous, I say, and we would prefer to be absent from the body and present with the Lord. 9Hence, whether we are at home or away, we make it our ambition to be well-pleasing to HimhimHim. 10For we must all be manifested before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat of Christ, so that each may receive recompense for the deeds accomplished in the body, whether good or evil.

Persuasion, Sincerity, and New Creation

11Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade others; but we are manifest to God, and I hope we are also manifest in your consciences. 12We are not commending ourselves to you again, but affording you an occasion for boasting on our behalf, so that you may have a reply for those who boast in outward appearance and not in heart. 13For if we are beside ourselves, it is for God; if we are of sound mind, it is for you.

14For the love of Christ compels us, because we have concluded that one died for all, and therefore all died; 15and HeheHe died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves, but for HimhimHim who died for them and was raised.

16Consequently, from now on we regard no one according to the flesh; even if we once regarded Christ according to the flesh, we no longer do so. 17Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation: the old things have passed away; behold, new things have come.

The Ministry of Reconciliation

18Now all these things are from God, who reconciled us to HimselfhimselfHimself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation; 19namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to HimselfhimselfHimself, not reckoning their trespasses against them, and HeheHe has entrusted to us the word of reconciliation.

20Therefore we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were entreating through us: we beg you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God. 21He made HimhimHim who knew no sin to be sin on our behalf, so that in HimhimHim we might become the righteousness of God.
