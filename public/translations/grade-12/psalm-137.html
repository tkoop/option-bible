By the Rivers of Babylon

1By the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept as we remembered Zion. 2There, among the willows, we hung up our harps. 3For our captors demanded songs, and our tormentors sought mirth, saying, “Sing us one of the songs of Zion.” 4But how could we sing the LORD’s song on foreign soil?

5If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand forget its skill. 6May my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem above my highest joy.

A Prayer for Vengeance

7Remember the LORD against the sons of Edom, the day of Jerusalem, when they said, “Tear it down! Tear it down to its foundations!” 8O devastated daughter of Babylon, blessed is the one who repays you as you have repaid us. 9Blessed is the one who seizes your little ones and dashes them against the rock.
