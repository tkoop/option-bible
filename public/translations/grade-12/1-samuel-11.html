Nahash Threatens Jabesh-gilead

1Nahash the Ammonite advanced and encamped against Jabesh-gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “Make a treaty with us, and we will serve you.”

2Nahash the Ammonite replied, “I will make a treaty with you on this condition: I will gouge out all your right eyes and thereby disgrace all Israel.”

3The elders of Jabesh said to him, “Grant us seven days so that we may send messengers throughout all the territory of Israel. If no one comes to save us, then we will surrender to you.”

Saul Rallies Israel

4When the messengers reached Gibeah of Saul and reported these words to the people, all the people raised their voices and wept. 5Meanwhile, Saul was returning from the fields behind his oxen. Saul asked, “Why are the people weeping?” They told him what the men of Jabesh had said.

6When Saul heard these words, the SpiritspiritSpirit of God came powerfully upon him, and his anger blazed.

7He took a yoke of oxen, dismembered them, and sent the pieces throughout all the territory of Israel by messengers, proclaiming, “This is what will be done to the oxen of anyone who does not march with Saul and Samuel.” Then the dread of the LORD fell upon the people, and they mobilizedmobilisedmobilized as one man.

8He mustered them at Bezek: three hundred thousand men of Israel and thirty thousand men of Judah.

Victory at Jabesh-gilead

9They said to the messengers who had come, “Thus you shall say to the men of Jabesh-gilead: ‘Tomorrow, by the time the sun grows hot, you will be delivered.’” The messengers returned and reported this to the men of Jabesh, and they rejoiced.

10The men of Jabesh said, “Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you may deal with us as you see fit.”

11The next day Saul divided the people into three companies. They entered the camp during the morning watch and struck down the Ammonites until the heat of the day. The survivors were scattered so thoroughly that not even two of them remained together.

The Kingdom Renewed at Gilgal

12The people said to Samuel, “Who was it that said, ‘Shall Saul reign over us?’ Bring those men here so that we may put them to death.”

13Saul said, “No one is to be put to death today, for today the LORD has brought deliverance to Israel.”

14Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the monarchy there.”

15So all the people went to Gilgal, and there they installed Saul as king before the LORD. They offered peace offerings before the LORD, and Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced exceedingly.
