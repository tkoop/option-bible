Zacchaeus the Tax Collector

1As Jesus entered Jericho, HeheHe was passing through the city. 2And there was a man named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector who was also wealthy. 3He tried to see who Jesus was, but he could not because of the crowd, for he was short in stature. 4So he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus, since HeheHe was about to pass that way.

5When Jesus reached the spot, HeheHe looked up and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, for today I must stay at your house.” 6So he hurried down and received HimhimHim joyfully.

7When all who saw it grumbled, they said, “He has gone to be the guest of a sinner.”

8But Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, I give half of my possessions to the poor, and if I have defrauded anyone of anything, I restore it fourfold.”

9Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he too is a son of Abraham. 10For the SonsonSon of ManmanMan came to seek and to save the lost.”

11While they were listening to these things, HeheHe added a parable, because HeheHe was near Jerusalem and they thought the kingdom of God was about to appear at once.

The Parable of the Ten Minas

12So HeheHe said, “A certain nobleman travelledtravelledtraveled to a distant country to receive a kingdom for himself and then return. 13Calling ten of his servants, he gave them ten minas and said, ‘Conduct business until I come.’

14“But his subjects hated him and sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We do not want this man to reign over us.’

15“When he returned, having received the kingdom, he ordered those servants to whom he had given the money to be summoned, so that he might learn what each had gained through trading. 16The first came and said, ‘Master, your mina has earned ten minas.’

17“He said to him, ‘Well done, good servant! Because you have been faithful in a very small matter, have authority over ten cities.’

18“The second came and said, ‘Master, your mina has produced five minas.’

19“He likewise said to him, ‘You too are to be over five cities.’

20“Then another came and said, ‘Master, here is your mina, which I kept laid away in a cloth. 21For I was afraid of you, because you are a severe man. You take up what you did not deposit, and you reap what you did not sow.’

22“He said to him, ‘You wicked servant! I will judge you by your own words. You knew that I am a severe man, taking up what I did not lay down and reaping what I did not sow. 23Why then did you not put my money in the bank, so that when I came I might have collected it with interest?’

24“Then he said to those standing by, ‘Take the mina from him and give it to the one who has the ten minas.’

25“They said to him, ‘Master, he already has ten minas.’

26“He said, ‘I tell you, to everyone who has, more will be given; but from the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away.’

27“But as for these enemies of mine who did not want me to reign over them—bring them here and slaughter them before me.”

28After saying these things, HeheHe went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.

The Triumphal Entry

29As HeheHe drew near to Bethphage and Bethany, at the mount called Olivet, HeheHe sent two of HishisHis disciples, 30saying, “Go into the village ahead of you; as you enter it, you will find a colt tied, on which no one has ever sat. Untie it and bring it here. 31If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ say, ‘The Lord needs it.’”

32Those who were sent went and found it just as HeheHe had told them.

33As they were untying the colt, its owners asked them, “Why are you untying the colt?”

34They said, “The Lord needs it.”

35They brought it to Jesus, and throwing their garments on the colt, they set Jesus on it. 36As HeheHe went along, they spread their garments on the road.

37As HeheHe was now drawing near the descent of the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works they had seen, 38saying, “Blessed is the KingkingKing who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in Heavenheavenheaven and glory in the highest!”

39Some of the Pharisees in the crowd said to HimhimHim, “Teacher, rebuke YouryourYour disciples.”

40He answered, “I tell you, if these were silent, the stones would cry out.”

Jesus Weeps over Jerusalem and Cleanses the Temple

41As HeheHe drew near and saw the city, HeheHe wept over it, 42saying, “If you had known, even you, on this day what would bring you peace—but now it is hidden from your eyes. 43For days will come upon you when your enemies will throw up a barricade around you, surround you, and hem you in on every side. 44They will level you to the ground—you and your children within you—and they will not leave one stone upon another in you, because you did not recognizerecogniserecognize the time of your visitation.”

45Then HeheHe entered the temple and began to drive out those who were selling. 46He said to them, “It is written, ‘My house shall be a house of prayer,’ but you have made it a den of robbers.”

47He was teaching in the temple every day, but the chief priests, the scribes, and the leaders of the people were seeking to destroy HimhimHim. 48Yet they could not find a way to do it, for all the people were hanging on HishisHis words.
