The First Seal: The White Horse

1And I saw when the LamblambLamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures speaking with a voice like thunder, “Come!” 2And I looked, and behold, a white horse, and its rider had a bow; a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering and determined to conquer.

The Second, Third, and Fourth Seals: War, Famine, and Death

3When HeheHe opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come!” 4Then another horse came out, fiery red, and its rider was permitted to take peace from the earth, so that people would slay one another; and a great sword was given to him.

5When HeheHe opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come!” And I looked, and behold, a black horse, and its rider held a pair of scales in his hand. 6And I heard a voice, as it were, in the midst of the four living creatures saying, “A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius; and do not harm the oil and the wine.”

7When HeheHe opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, “Come!” 8And I looked, and behold, a pale horse, and its rider’s name was Death, and Hades followed close behind. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill by sword, by famine, by plague, and by the beasts of the earth.

The Fifth Seal: The Martyrs’ Cry

9When HeheHe opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slain for the word of God and for the testimony they had borne.

10They cried out with a loud voice, “How long, O SovereignsovereignSovereign Lord, holy and true, before YouyouYou judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?”

11Then a white robe was given to each of them, and they were told to rest a little longer, until the full number of their fellow servants and their brothers who were to be killed as they had been should be reached.

The Sixth Seal: Cosmic Upheaval

12And I saw when HeheHe opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake; the sun became black like sackcloth made of hair, and the whole moon became like blood. 13The stars of Heavenheavenheaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree casts down its unripe figs when shaken by a great wind.

14The sky was split apart like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved from its place.

15Then the kings of the earth and the great ones and the commanders and the rich and the strong, and every slave and free man, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains.

16They said to the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the presence of HimhimHim who sits on the throne and from the wrath of the LamblambLamb, 17for the great day of their wrath has come, and who is able to stand?”
