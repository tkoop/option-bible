Philistine War Preparations and David's Commitment

1In those days, the Philistines gathered their forces for war against Israel. Achish said to David, "YouyouYou must understand that you and your men will march out with me in the camp."

2David said to Achish, "Then you know what your servant will do."

Achish replied, "Then I will make you my bodyguard for life."

3Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, his own city. Saul had removed the mediums and the spiritists from the land. 4The Philistines assembled and camped at Shunem, while Saul gathered all Israel and camped at Gilboa.

5When Saul saw the Philistine camp, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly. 6Saul inquired of the LORD, but the LORD did not answer him, either by dreams, or by the Urim, or by the prophets.

Saul Seeks a Medium at En-dor

7Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium, so that I may go to her and consult her."

His servants said to him, "There is a woman who is a medium at En-dor."

8Saul disguised himself, put on other clothes, and went with two men. They came to the woman at night. He said, "Please divine for me by the spirit, and bring up for me whomever I name to you."

9The woman said to him, "YouyouYou know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the mediums and the spiritists from the land. Why then are you setting a trap for my life, to bring about my death?"

10Saul swore to her by the LORD, saying, "As the LORD lives, no punishment shall come upon you for this thing."

11The woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?"

He said, "BringbringBring up Samuel for me."

12When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice. The woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul."

13The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?"

The woman said to Saul, "I see a divine being coming up out of the earth."

14He said, "What is his appearance?"

She said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and paid homage.

Samuel's Prophecy of Doom

15Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?"

Saul answered, "I am in great distress, for the Philistines are making war against me, and God has departed from me and no longer answers me, neither by prophets nor by dreams. Therefore I have called you, to tell me what I should do."

16Samuel said, "Why do you ask me, when the LORD has departed from you and has become your adversary? 17The LORD has done to you as HeheHe spoke through me: the LORD has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbourneighbourneighbor David. 18Because you did not obey the voice of the LORD and did not carry out HishisHis fierce wrath against Amalek, therefore the LORD has done this to you today. 19Moreover, the LORD will deliver Israel, along with you, into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The LORD will also deliver the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines."

20Then Saul quickly fell full length on the ground and was very afraid because of Samuel's words. Moreover, there was no strength in him, for he had eaten no food all day and all night.

The Medium's Final Kindness to Saul

21The woman came to Saul and saw that he was greatly terrified. She said to him, "Behold, your maidservant has obeyed your voice, and I have put my life in my hand and have listened to your words, which you spoke to me. 22Now therefore, please listen to the voice of your maidservant, and let me set a piece of bread before you, and eat, so that you may have strength when you go on your way."

23He refused and said, "I will not eat." But his servants and the woman urged him, and he listened to their voice. He rose from the ground and sat on the bed. 24The woman had a fattened calf in the house. She hurried and slaughtered it, took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread. 25She brought it before Saul and before his servants, and they ate. Then they rose and went away that night.
