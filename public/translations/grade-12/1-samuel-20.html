David Seeks Jonathan's Help

1David fled from Naioth in Ramah and came before Jonathan, saying, "What have I done? What is my guilt, and what is my sin before your father, that he seeks my life?"

2Jonathan said to him, "May that never be! You shall not die. Look, my father does nothing, great or small, without disclosing it to me. Why then should my father hide this matter from me? It is not so."

3David said, "YouryourYour father knows well that I have found favourfavourfavor in your sight, and he has said, 'Let Jonathan not know this, lest he be grieved.' But truly, as the the LORD lives and as your soul lives, I am only a step away from death."

4Jonathan said to him, "Whatever you ask, I will do for you."

The New Moon Plan

5David said to Jonathan, "Look, tomorrow is the new moon, and I should not fail to sit with the king to eat. But let me go, that I may hide myself in the field until the third day at evening. 6If your father misses me at all, then say, 'David earnestly asked leave of me to run to Bethlehem, his city, for there is a yearly sacrifice there for all the family.' 7If he says, 'It is well,' your servant shall have peace; but if he is very angry, then know that evil is determined by him. 8Therefore deal kindly with your servant, for you have brought your servant into a covenant of the the LORD with you. But if there is iniquity in me, kill me yourself; for why should you bring me to your father?"

9Jonathan said, "May that never be! If I knew that evil were determined by my father to come upon you, would I not tell you?"

10David said to him, "Who will tell me if your father answers you roughly?"

11Jonathan said, "Come, let us go out into the field." So they both went out into the field.

12Jonathan said to David, "As the the LORD God of Israel lives, when I have sounded out my father about this time tomorrow, or the third day, and look, if there is goodwill toward David, then I will send to you and disclose it to you. 13But if my father is determined to harm you, may the the LORD punish Jonathan, and even more, if I do not disclose it to you and send you away, that you may go in peace. And may the the LORD be with you, as HeheHe has been with my father. 14And if I am still alive, show me the the LORD's kindness, so that I do not die; 15but do not cut off your kindness from my house forever, not even when the the LORD cuts off every one of the enemies of David from the face of the earth."

16So Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David, saying, "May the the LORD hold David's enemies accountable."

17And Jonathan made David swear again, because he loved him; for he loved him as he loved his own soul.

18Jonathan said to him, "Tomorrow is the new moon, and you will be missed, because your seat will be empty. 19On the third day you shall go down quickly and come to the place where you hid yourself on the day of the incident, and remain by the stone Ezel. 20I will shoot three arrows to the side, as though I shot at a target. 21And look, I will send the boy, saying, 'Go, find the arrows.' If I say to the boy, 'Look, the arrows are on this side of you; take them,' then come, for there is peace for you, and no danger, as the the LORD lives. 22But if I say to the boy, 'Look, the arrows are beyond you,' then go, for the the LORD has sent you away. 23And as for the matter which we have spoken of, look, the the LORD is between you and me forever."

Saul's Anger at the New Moon Feast

24So David hid in the field. And when the new moon came, the king sat down to eat food. 25The king sat on his seat as at other times, on the seat by the wall. Jonathan arose, and Abner sat by Saul, but David's place was empty.

26Yet Saul said nothing that day, for he thought, "Something has happened to him; he is not clean; surely he is unclean."

27On the next day, the second day of the month, David's place was empty. And Saul said to Jonathan his son, "Why has not the son of Jesse come to the meal, either yesterday or today?"

28Jonathan answered Saul, "David earnestly asked leave of me to go to Bethlehem. 29He said, 'Let me go, please, for our family has a sacrifice in the city, and my brother has commanded me. And now, if I have found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, let me get away, please, that I may see my brothers.' Therefore he has not come to the king's table."

30Then Saul's anger was kindled against Jonathan, and he said to him, "YouyouYou son of a perverse and rebellious woman! Do I not know that you have chosen the son of Jesse to your own shame, and to the shame of your mother's nakedness? 31For as long as the son of Jesse lives on the earth, neither you nor your kingdom shall be established. Therefore send and bring him to me, for he is doomed to die."

32Jonathan answered Saul his father, "Why should he be put to death? What has he done?"

33Then Saul hurled the spear at him to strike him, so Jonathan knew that his father was determined to put David to death.

34And Jonathan rose from the table in fierce anger and ate no food the second day of the month, for he was grieved for David, because his father had disgraced him.

The Signal in the Field

35In the morning Jonathan went out into the field to the appointed time with David, and a little boy was with him.

36He said to his boy, "Run and find the arrows that I shoot." As the boy ran, he shot an arrow beyond him. 37When the boy came to the place of the arrow which Jonathan had shot, Jonathan cried after the boy and said, "IsisIs not the arrow beyond you?" 38Then Jonathan cried after the boy, "Hurry, make haste, do not stay!" So Jonathan's boy gathered up the arrows and came to his master.

39But the boy did not know anything; only Jonathan and David knew the matter.

40Jonathan gave his weapons to his boy and said to him, "Go, carry them to the city."

41As soon as the boy was gone, David arose from the south side and fell on his face to the ground and bowed three times. And they kissed one another and wept with one another, until David wept even more.

42Then Jonathan said to David, "Go in peace, since we have sworn both of us in the name of the the LORD, saying, 'The ThetheThe LORD be between me and you, and between my offspring and your offspring, forever.'" And he arose and departed, and Jonathan went into the city.
