Rehoboam Seeks Counsel at Shechem

1Rehoboam went to Shechem, because all Israel had come there to crown him king. 2Jeroboam son of Nebat heard of this while he was still in Egypt, where he had fled from King Solomon, and he remained there.

3So they sent for Jeroboam, and he and all Israel came to Rehoboam and said, 4"YouryourYour father made our yoke heavy. Now lighten the harsh servitude your father imposed on us, and we will serve you."

5He replied, "Go away for three days, then return to me." So the people departed.

6King Rehoboam consulted the elders who had served his father Solomon during his lifetime and asked, "How do you advise me to answer this people?"

7They answered, "If today you will be a servant to this people and serve them, and if you speak kindly to them, they will be your servants forever."

8But he rejected the advice the elders gave him and consulted the young men who had grown up with him and now stood before him.

9He asked them, "What advice do you give? How should we answer these people who have said, 'Lighten the yoke your father put on us'?"

10The young men who had grown up with him replied, 11"I will be far harsher than my father: he burdened you heavily, and I will increase your burden; he punished you with whips, and I will punish you far more severely."

12On the third day, Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, just as the king had said, "Return to me on the third day."

13The king answered the people harshly, rejecting the advice of the elders.

14He spoke to them as the young men had advised: "My father made your yoke heavy, but I will make it heavier. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you far more severely."

15So the king did not listen to the people, because this turn of events was from the the LORD, so that HeheHe might fulfillfulfilfulfill the word HeheHe had spoken through Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam son of Nebat.

Israel Rejects Rehoboam and the Kingdom Divides

16When all Israel saw that the king would not listen to them, they answered, "What share do we have in David? We have no inheritance in the son of Jesse. To your tents, Israel! Look after your own house, David!" Then Israel went to their tents.

17However, Rehoboam continued to reign over the Israelites who lived in the cities of Judah. 18King Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was in charge of the forced labourlabourlabor, but all Israel stoned him to death. King Rehoboam then hurriedly mounted his chariot and fled to Jerusalem. 19Thus Israel has rebelled against the house of David to this day.

20When all Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned, they summoned him to the assembly and made him king over all Israel. Only the tribe of Judah remained loyal to the house of David.

Rehoboam Prepares for War Against Israel

21Rehoboam went to Jerusalem and mustered all the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin—180,000 chosen warriors—to fight against the house of Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam son of Solomon.

22But the word of God came to Shemaiah the man of God: 23"Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people:"

24"Thus says the the LORD: 'YouyouYou shall not go up or fight against your brothers, the sons of Israel. Return, every man, to his home, for this thing is from MemeMe.' " They listened to the word of the the LORD and turned back, going home as the the LORD had said.

Jeroboam Establishes Idolatry in Israel

25Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim and lived there. From there he went out and built Penuel. 26Jeroboam said to himself, "ThetheThe kingdom is likely to return to the house of David."

27"If these people go up to offer sacrifices in the house of the the LORD at Jerusalem, their hearts will turn to their lord, Rehoboam king of Judah. They will kill me and return to Rehoboam king of Judah."

28So the king took counsel and made two calves of gold. Then he said to the people, "It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem. Here are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt."

29He set one in Bethel and the other in Dan. 30This became a sin, for the people went to worship before one of the calves even as far as Dan.

31He also built shrines on the high places and appointed priests from every class of people, not just from the sons of Levi. 32Jeroboam instituted a feast on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, like the feast in Judah, and he went up to the altar in Bethel to sacrifice to the calves he had made. He also stationed in Bethel the priests of the high places he had made. 33On the fifteenth day of the eighth month—the month he had chosen of his own accord—he offered sacrifices on the altar he had made in Bethel. He instituted a feast for the Israelites and went up to the altar to burn incense.
