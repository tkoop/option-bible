The Division of Canaan by Lot

1These are the allotments the Israelites received in the land of Canaan, distributed by Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of Israel's ancestral tribes. 2Their inheritance was assigned by lot to the nine and a half tribes, as the LORD had commanded through Moses, 3because Moses had granted the inheritance of the two and a half tribes east of the Jordan, but he gave no inheritance among them to the Levites. 4For the descendants of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim; and the Levites received no territorial share, except cities in which to dwell, together with pasturelands for their livestock and possessions. 5Thus the Israelites carried out what the LORD had commanded Moses, and they divided the land.

Caleb's Request for Hebron

6Then the sons of Judah approached Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, “You know what the LORD said to Moses, the man of God, at Kadesh-barnea concerning you and me. 7I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh-barnea to reconnoiter the land, and I returned with a report faithful to what was in my heart. 8Nevertheless, my brothers who ascended with me discouraged the people, but I followed the LORD my God wholeheartedly. 9On that day Moses swore, saying, ‘Surely the land your foot has trodden shall be an inheritance to you and your descendants forever, because you have followed the LORD my God wholeheartedly.’ 10Now the LORD has preserved my life, just as HeheHe said, these forty-five years since the LORD spoke this word to Moses while Israel wandered in the wilderness; and now I am eighty-five years old today. 11I am still as strong today as I was when Moses sent me; my strength now is what it was then, for battle and for daily life. 12Now give me this hill country about which the LORD spoke that day, for you heard that day how the Anakim were there, with great fortified cities; perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, just as the LORD said.’”

Joshua Grants Hebron to Caleb

13Then Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh as an inheritance. 14Therefore Hebron became the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he wholly followed the LORD, the God of Israel. 15Now Hebron was formerly called Kiriath-arba; Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. And the land had rest from war.
