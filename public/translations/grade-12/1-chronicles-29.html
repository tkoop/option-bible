Solomon's Enthronement and David's Death

20Then David said to the whole assembly, "Bless the the LORD your God." And the whole assembly blessed the the LORD, the God of their fathers, and bowed down and worshiped the the LORD and the king.

21The next day they offered sacrifices and burnt offerings to the the LORD—a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings—and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel. 22They ate and drank before the the LORD with great gladness on that day. Then they made Solomon the son of David king a second time, and they anointed him before the the LORD as leader, and Zadok as priest. 23So Solomon sat on the throne of the the LORD as king in place of his father David; he prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.

24All the leaders and mighty men, and also all the sons of King David, pledged their allegiance to King Solomon. 25The LORD exalted Solomon greatly in the sight of all Israel and bestowed on him royal splendoursplendoursplendor such as no king over Israel had before him. 26Thus David the son of Jesse reigned over all Israel. 27The length of his reign over Israel was forty years: he reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.
