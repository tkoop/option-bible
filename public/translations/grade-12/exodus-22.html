Laws on Theft and Restitution

1If a man steals an ox or a sheep and kills it or sells it, he shall repay five oxen for an ox and four sheep for a sheep. 2If a thief is found breaking in and is struck so that he dies, there is no bloodguilt for him. 3But if the sun has risen on him, there is bloodguilt for him. He shall surely make restitution; if he has nothing, he shall be sold for his theft. 4If the stolen animal is found alive in his hand—whether ox, donkey, or sheep—he shall repay double.

Laws on Property Damage and Deposits

5If a man causes a field or vineyard to be grazed over, or lets his livestock loose so that they feed in another man's field, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field and vineyard. 6If fire breaks out and catches thorns, so that stacked grain, standing grain, or a field is consumed, the one who started the fire shall surely make restitution.

7If a man gives his neighbourneighbourneighbor silver or goods to keep, and they are stolen from the man's house, then if the thief is found, he shall pay double. 8If the thief is not found, the owner of the house shall be brought before God, to determine whether he has laid hands on his neighbourneighbourneighbor's property. 9In every case of breach of trust—whether concerning an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or any lost thing of which one says, "This is it"—the case of both parties shall come before God, and the one whom God condemns shall pay double to his neighbourneighbourneighbor.

10If a man gives his neighbourneighbourneighbor a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any beast to keep, and it dies, is injured, or is carried off, with no one seeing it, 11an oath by the LORD shall be between the two of them, to determine whether he has taken his neighbourneighbourneighbor's property; the owner shall accept the oath, and the keeper need not make restitution. 12But if it was stolen from him, he shall make restitution to its owner. 13If it was torn by beasts, he shall bring it as evidence; he need not make restitution for what was torn.

14If a man borrows anything from his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he shall surely make restitution. 15If its owner was with it, he need not make restitution; if it was hired, the hire covers the loss.

Social and Moral Laws

16If a man seduces a virgin who is not betrothed and lies with her, he shall pay the bride-price and take her as his wife. 17If her father refuses to give her to him, he shall pay money equal to the bride-price of virgins.

18You shall not permit a sorceress to live. 19Whoever lies with an animal shall be put to death. 20Whoever sacrifices to any god other than the LORD alone must be completely destroyed.

Protection of the Vulnerable and Worship

21Do not mistreat or oppress a foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

22Do not afflict any widow or orphan. 23If you do afflict them, and they cry out to MemeMe, I will surely hear their cry. 24My anger will burn, and I will kill you with the sword; then your wives will become widows and your children fatherless.

25If you lend money to any of MymyMy people who is poor, you must not be like a creditor to him, and do not charge him interest.

26If you ever take your neighbourneighbourneighbor's cloak in pledge, you shall return it to him before the sun goes down, 27for it is his only covering; it is his cloak for his body. In what else shall he sleep? If he cries out to MemeMe, I will hear, for I am gracious.

28You shall not revile God, nor curse a ruler of your people.

29Do not delay to offer the firstfruits of your harvest and the outflow of your presses. You shall give MemeMe the firstborn of your sons. 30You shall do the same with your oxen and your sheep: it shall remain with its mother seven days, and on the eighth day you shall give it to MemeMe.

31You shall be holy people to MemeMe. You shall not eat flesh torn by beasts in the field; throw it to the dogs.
