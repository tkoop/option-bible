Ahithophel’s Counsel and Hushai’s Counter

1Ahithophel said to Absalom, “Let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will set out tonight and pursue David. 2I will come upon him while he is weary and discouraged, terrify him, and make all the people with him flee; then I will strike down the king alone. 3I will bring all the people back to you; the man you seek is as though all had returned, and all the people will be at peace.”

4The proposal pleased Absalom and all the elders of Israel.

5Absalom said, “Call Hushai the Archite also, and let us hear what he too has to say.”

6Hushai came to Absalom, and Absalom said to him, “Ahithophel has spoken thus. Shall we carry out his plan? If not, you speak.”

7Hushai said to Absalom, “The counsel that Ahithophel has given this time is not good. 8You know that your father and his men are mighty men, and they are bitter in spirit, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field; and your father is a man of war who will not spend the night with the troops. 9Even now he has hidden himself in some pit or some other place. If some of them fall at first, whoever hears it will say, ‘A slaughter has occurred among the people who follow Absalom.’ 10Then even the valiant man, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, will melt in fear, for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty man and that those with him are valiant. 11But my counsel is that all Israel be gathered to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as the sand on the seashore in number, and that you go into battle. 12Then we will come upon him in whatever place he is found, and we will fall on him as dew falls on the ground; not one man will be left of him and all his men. 13If he retreats into a city, all Israel will bring ropes to that city, and we will drag it into the river until not a trace remains.”

14Absalom and all the men of Israel said, “The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel.” For the LORD had ordained that the good counsel of Ahithophel be frustrated, so that the LORD might bring disaster upon Absalom.

Hushai Warns David

15Then Hushai said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, “Thus and thus Ahithophel counselledcounselledcounseled Absalom and the elders of Israel, and thus and thus I have counselledcounselledcounseled.”

16Hushai said, “Now therefore send quickly and tell David, ‘Do not spend the night in the plains of the wilderness, but cross over at once, lest the king and all the people with him be overwhelmed.’”

17Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were standing at En-rogel, and a maidservant went and told them; they went and told King David, for they could not be seen entering the city.

18But a young man saw them and told Absalom; so the two hurried away and came to the house of a man in Bahurim, who had a well in his courtyard, and they went down into it.

19The woman took a covering and spread it over the well’s mouth, and scattered crushed grain on it, so that nothing was known.

20Absalom’s servants came to the woman at the house and said, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?”

The woman said to them, “They have crossed over the brook of water.” After they searched and did not find them, they returned to Jerusalem.

21After they had gone, the two came up out of the well and went and told King David. They said to David, “Arise and cross the water quickly, for thus Ahithophel has counselledcounselledcounseled against you.” 22Then David and all the people with him arose and crossed the Jordan; by daybreak not one of them was left behind.

Ahithophel’s Death and David’s Refuge

23When Ahithophel saw that his counsel was not followed, he saddled his donkey, arose, and went home to his city. He put his affairs in order and hanged himself; he died and was buried in his father’s tomb.

24Then David came to Mahanaim. And Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.

25Absalom had set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man whose name was Ithra the Israelite, who had married Abigail the daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, Joab’s mother. 26Israel and Absalom encamped in the land of Gilead.

27When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi the son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites, Machir the son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim 28brought beds, basins, and pottery, and wheat, barley, flour, parched grain, beans, lentils, and parched peas, 29honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd, for David and the people with him to eat; for they said, “The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness.”
