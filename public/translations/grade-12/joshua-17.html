Allotment for the Tribe of Manasseh

1This was the allotment for the tribe of Manasseh, since he was Joseph's firstborn, and it also belonged to Machir, Manasseh's firstborn and the father of Gilead, because he was a man of war; therefore he received Gilead and Bashan. 2The rest of Manasseh's descendants received their allotments by families: the sons of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, listed by family. 3Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, had no sons—only daughters—and their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

The Daughters of Zelophehad Receive an Inheritance

4They came before Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders, and said, "ThetheThe LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers." So they gave them an inheritance among their father's brothers, in keeping with the LORD's command. 5Thus ten portions fell to Manasseh, in addition to the land of Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan. 6Manasseh's daughters inherited among his sons, while the land of Gilead went to the rest of Manasseh's sons.

The Borders of Manasseh

7Manasseh's border ran from Asher to Michmethath, east of Shechem, and then turned south toward the inhabitants of En-tappuah. 8The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself, on Manasseh's border, belonged to the sons of Ephraim. 9The border descended southward to the BrookbrookBrook of Kanah; these cities belonged to Ephraim, though they lay among Manasseh's cities. Manasseh's border ran along the north side of the brook and ended at the sea. 10South of it was Ephraim's territory, and north of it was Manasseh's, with the sea as its boundary; they reached Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

11In Issachar and Asher, Manasseh held Beth-shean and its villages, Ibleam and its villages, Dor and its villages, En-dor and its villages, Taanach and its villages, and Megiddo and its villages—the three regions. 12Yet the sons of Manasseh could not drive out the inhabitants of these cities, because the Canaanites were determined to remain in that land. 13When the Israelites grew strong, they subjected the Canaanites to forced labourlabourlabor, but they never completely drove them out.

The Complaint of Joseph's Descendants

14The sons of Joseph spoke to Joshua and said, "Why have you given me only one lot and one portion as an inheritance, when I am such a numerous people and the LORD has blessed me this far?"

15Joshua replied, "If you are a numerous people, go up into the forest and clear a place for yourselves there in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, because the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you."

16The sons of Joseph said, "ThetheThe hill country is not enough for us, and all the Canaanites who live in the valley have iron chariots—those in Beth-shean and its villages and those in the ValleyvalleyValley of Jezreel."

17Joshua spoke to the house of Joseph—to Ephraim and Manasseh—and said, "YouyouYou are a numerous people with great strength; you will not have only one lot. 18The hill country will be yours; though it is forest, you will clear it, and its borders will be yours. You will drive out the Canaanites, even though they have iron chariots and are strong."
