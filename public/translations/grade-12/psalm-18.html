A Psalm of David: The LORD, My Rock and Deliverer

1For the choirmaster. Of David, the servant of the LORD, who sang this song to the LORD on the day the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He said: “I love YouyouYou, O the LORD, my strength. 2The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer; my God is my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield and the power of my salvation, my stronghold. 3I call upon the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

“4The cords of death entangled me, and the torrents of destruction overwhelmed me. 5The cords of Sheol coiled around me; the snares of death confronted me. 6In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried to my God for help. From HishisHis temple HeheHe heard my voice, and my cry for help reached HishisHis ears.

“7Then the earth reeled and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled; they were shaken because HeheHe was angry. 8Smoke rose from HishisHis nostrils, and consuming fire came from HishisHis mouth; glowing coals blazed forth from HimhimHim. 9He parted the heavens and came down, with thick darkness beneath HishisHis feet. 10He rode upon a cherub and flew; HeheHe soared on the wings of the wind. 11He made darkness HishisHis hiding place, HishisHis canopy around HimhimHim—darkness of waters and thick clouds of the skies. 12From the brightness before HimhimHim, HishisHis clouds passed through, with hail and coals of fire. 13The LORD thundered in Heavenheavenheaven; the voice of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh resounded with hail and coals of fire. 14He shot HishisHis arrows and scattered the foes, and HeheHe hurled lightning and routed them. 15The channels of the sea were exposed, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at YouryourYour rebuke, O the LORD, at the blast of the breath of YouryourYour nostrils.

“16He reached down from on high and took hold of me; HeheHe drew me out of deep waters. 17He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from my foes who were too strong for me. 18They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the LORD was my support. 19He brought me out into a spacious place; HeheHe rescued me because HeheHe delighted in me.

“20The LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; HeheHe has repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands. 21For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. 22For all HishisHis ordinances are before me; I have not turned away from HishisHis statutes. 23I have been blameless before HimhimHim and have kept myself from iniquity. 24So the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in HishisHis sight.

“25To the faithful YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself faithful, to the blameless YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself blameless, to the pure YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself pure, but to the crooked YouyouYou show YourselfyourselfYourself shrewd. 26You save an afflicted people, but YouyouYou humble arrogant eyes. 27For YouyouYou, the LORD, light my lamp; my God illuminates my darkness.

“28For YouyouYou, O the LORD, light my lamp; my God illuminates my darkness. 29For with YouyouYou I can charge through a troop, and with my God I can scale a wall. 30As for God, HishisHis way is perfect; the word of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in HimhimHim.

“31For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the RockrockRock except our God? 32It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way blameless. 33He makes my feet like those of a deer and sets me upon the heights. 34He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze. 35You have given me the shield of YouryourYour salvation; YouryourYour right hand sustains me, and YouryourYour gentleness makes me great. 36You have enlarged my steps beneath me, so that my feet have not slipped.

“37I pursued my enemies and overtook them; I did not turn back until they were consumed. 38I crushed them so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet. 39You have armed me with strength for the battle; YouyouYou have subdued my adversaries beneath me. 40You have made my enemies turn their backs to me, and I annihilated those who hated me. 41They cried for help, but there was no one to save them—even to the LORD, but HeheHe did not answer. 42I ground them as dust before the wind; I trampled them like mud in the streets.

“43You have delivered me from the strife of the people; YouyouYou have made me the head of nations; a people I did not know serve me. 44As soon as they hear me, they obey me; foreigners cringe before me. 45Foreigners fade away and come trembling from their strongholds.

“46The LORD lives! Blessed be my RockrockRock! Exalted be the God of my salvation! 47He is the God who avenges me and subdues nations beneath me, 48who delivers me from my enemies. You exalt me above my foes; YouyouYou rescue me from violent men. 49Therefore I will praise YouyouYou among the nations, O the LORD; I will sing praises to YouryourYour name. 50He gives great victories to HishisHis king; HeheHe shows steadfast love to HishisHis anointed, to David and his descendants forever.”
