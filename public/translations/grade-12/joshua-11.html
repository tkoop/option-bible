The Northern Kings Unite in a Formidable Coalition Against Israel

1When Jabin, king of Hazor, learned of Israel’s triumphs, he dispatched envoys to Jobab, king of Madon, to the kings of Shimron and Achshaph, and to the kings of the north—those in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinnereth, in the lowland, and in the heights of Dor toward the sea. 2He also summoned the Canaanites from east and west, the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites from the highlands, and the Hivites dwelling beneath Hermon in the land of Mizpah. 3They sallied forth with all their armies—a host as innumerable as the sand upon the seashore—accompanied by an overwhelming multitude of horses and chariots. 4All these kings converged, advanced, and encamped in unison at the waters of Merom to make war upon Israel.

The LORD Assures Joshua of Imminent Victory

56The LORD said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them, for by this time tomorrow I will deliver all of them, slain, before Israel. You shall hamstring their horses and incinerate their chariots with fire.”

7Joshua, together with all his men of war, came upon them suddenly at the waters of Merom and struck them. 8The LORD delivered them into Israel’s hand; Israel struck them down and pursued them to GreatgreatGreat Sidon, to Misrephoth-maim, and eastward to the ValleyvalleyValley of Mizpeh, striking them until no survivor remained. 9Joshua did precisely as the LORD had commanded him: he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

Joshua Destroys Hazor and Subjugates the Northern Cities

10At that juncture Joshua turned back, captured Hazor, and struck down its king with the sword, for Hazor had formerly been the paramount power among all those kingdoms. 11He struck every inhabitant with the edge of the sword, devoting them to utter destruction; no breath survived, and Hazor itself he burned with fire. 12Joshua captured all these cities and all their kings, striking them with the edge of the sword and devoting them to destruction, exactly as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded. 13Nevertheless, Israel did not burn any of the cities that stood upon their mounds, save Hazor alone, which Joshua burned. 14Israel seized as plunder all the spoil and livestock of these cities, yet every human being they struck with the edge of the sword until they were annihilated; they left no one breathing. 15As the LORD had commanded Moses HishisHis servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua executed it without omission; he left undone nothing of all that the LORD had commanded Moses.

Israel Takes Possession of the Land and Rests from War

16Joshua took all this land—the hill country, the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowland, the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel together with its lowland. 17From Mount Halak, which ascends toward Seir, to Baal-gad in the ValleyvalleyValley of Lebanon beneath Mount Hermon, he captured all their kings, struck them down, and put them to death. 18Joshua waged war against all these kings for many days.

19No city sought terms of peace with the Israelites except the Hivites dwelling in Gibeon; Israel took everything by force of arms. 20For it was the LORD who hardened their hearts to confront Israel in battle, in order that they might be devoted to destruction and receive no mercy, but rather be destroyed, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

21At that time Joshua came and extirpated the Anakim from the hill country—from Hebron, Debir, and Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and all the hill country of Israel—and he devoted them, along with their cities, to destruction. 22No Anakim remained in the land of the Israelites except in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod, where some survived. 23Joshua took the whole land, in accordance with everything the LORD had spoken to Moses, and he gave it to Israel as an inheritance, according to their tribal divisions; then the land rested from war.
