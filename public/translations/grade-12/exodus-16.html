Israel Murmurs in the Wilderness

1On the fifteenth day of the second month after their departure from Egypt, the whole congregation of the Israelites set out from Elim and came to the wilderness of Sin, between Elim and Sinai. 2There, in the wilderness, the entire congregation murmured against Moses and Aaron. 3The Israelites said to them, "If only we had perished by the hand of the LORD in Egypt, when we sat beside the pots of meat and ate bread to our fill! For you have brought us into this wilderness to destroy this whole assembly by famine."

The LORD Promises Bread from Heaven

4Then the LORD said to Moses, "Behold, I am about to rain bread from Heavenheavenheaven for you, and the people shall go out and gather each day's portion, so that I may test them to see whether they will walk in MymyMy law or not. 5On the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it shall be twice as much as they gather on any other day."

6So Moses and Aaron said to all the Israelites, "In the evening you shall know that it was the LORD who brought you out of Egypt, 7and in the morning you shall see the glory of the LORD, because HeheHe has heard your murmuring against the LORD. For what are we, that you murmur against us?"

8Moses said, "When the LORD gives you meat to eat in the evening and bread to the full in the morning, it is because the LORD has heard your murmuring, which you direct against HimhimHim. What are we? Your murmuring is not against us but against the LORD."

9Then Moses said to Aaron, "Tell the whole congregation of the Israelites, 'Come near before the LORD, for HeheHe has heard your murmuring.'" 10As Aaron spoke to the whole congregation, they turned toward the wilderness, and behold, the glory of the LORD appeared in the cloud.

11The LORD spoke to Moses: 12"I have heard the murmuring of the Israelites. Tell them, 'At twilight you shall eat meat, and in the morning you shall be filled with bread. Then you shall know that I am the LORD your God.'"

Quail and Manna in the Wilderness

13In the evening quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning dew lay all around the camp. 14When the dew had lifted, there on the surface of the wilderness was a fine, flake-like substance, as delicate as frost upon the ground. 15When the Israelites saw it, they said to one another, "What is it?" For they did not know what it was. Moses said to them, "It is the bread that the LORD has given you to eat."

16This is what the LORD had commanded: "Gather of it, each of you, as much as he can eat. You shall each take an omer, according to the number of persons each of you has in his tent." 17The Israelites did so; they gathered, some more and some less. 18But when they measured it by the omer, the one who had gathered much had no surplus, and the one who had gathered little had no deficit. Each gathered exactly as much as he could eat.

19Moses said to them, "Let no one keep any of it until morning." 20But they did not listen to Moses; some kept part of it until morning, and it bred worms and reeked. Moses was angry with them. 21Morning by morning they gathered it, each as much as he could eat; but when the sun grew hot, it melted.

Sabbath Regulations and the Preserved Manna

22On the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread—two omers per person. All the leaders of the congregation came and reported it to Moses. 23He said to them, "This is what the LORD has commanded: 'Tomorrow is a day of solemn rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD. Bake what you will bake and boil what you will boil, and put aside all that is left over to be kept until morning.'" 24So they set it aside until morning, as Moses had commanded; and it did not become putrid, nor were there any worms in it.

25Moses said, "Eat it today, for today is a Sabbath to the LORD; today you will not find it in the field. 26Six days you shall gather it, but on the seventh day, which is a Sabbath, there will be none." 27On the seventh day some of the people went out to gather, but they found none.

28The LORD said to Moses, "How long will you refuse to keep MymyMy commandments and MymyMy laws? 29See, the LORD has given you the Sabbath; therefore on the sixth day HeheHe gives you bread for two days. Let each of you remain in his place; let no one go out of his place on the seventh day." 30So the people rested on the seventh day.

31The house of Israel called its name manna. It was like coriander seed, white, and its taste was like wafers made with honey. 32Moses said, "This is what the LORD has commanded: 'Let an omer of it be kept throughout your generations, so that they may see the bread with which I fed you in the wilderness, when I brought you out of Egypt.'"

33Moses said to Aaron, "Take a jar, and put an omer of manna in it, and place it before the LORD to be kept throughout your generations." 34As the LORD had commanded Moses, Aaron placed it before the testimony, to be kept.

35The Israelites ate the manna for forty years, until they came to the border of the land of Canaan. 36An omer is a tenth of an ephah.
