Jeremiah Released at Ramah

1This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, had released him at Ramah, when he had found him bound in chains among all the captives of Jerusalem and Judah who were being exiled to Babylon.

2The captain of the guard found Jeremiah and said to him, “The LORD your God decreed this disaster for this place, 3and now the LORD has brought it about and has done just as HeheHe warned. Because you people sinned against the LORD and did not obey HishisHis voice, this has happened to you. 4But now, behold, I am freeing you today from the chains on your wrists. If it seems good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will take care of you. But if it seems wrong to you to come with me to Babylon, stay here. Look, the whole land is before you; wherever it seems good and right to you, go there. 5But before Jeremiah could turn back, the captain of the guard added, ‘Return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah, and stay with him among the people; or go wherever else seems right to you.’ Then the captain of the guard gave him a ration of food and a gift, and let him go.

6So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and stayed with him among the people who were left in the land.

Gedaliah Appointed Governor of Judah

7When all the commanders of the armies in the field and their men heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over the land and had put under his charge the men, women, children, and the poorest of the land who had not been exiled to Babylon, 8they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah—Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, along with their men.

9And Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, swore an oath to them and their men, saying, “Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you. 10As for me, I will stay in Mizpah to represent you before the Chaldeans who come to us. As for you, gather in wine, summer fruit, and oil, put them in your vessels, and live in your cities that you have taken.”

Exiles Return to the Land

11When all the Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and all the other lands heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant in Judah and had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, over them, 12they all returned out of all the places to which they had been banished. They came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and gathered an abundant harvest of wine and summer fruit.

A Conspiracy Against Gedaliah

13Then Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah 14and said to him, “Are you aware that Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to take your life?” But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them. 15Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah: “Let me go and kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah. No one will know. Why should he take your life and scatter all the Jews who have gathered to you, so that the remnant of Judah would perish?” 16But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, “Do not do this thing, for you are speaking falsely about Ishmael.”
