Released from the Law through Union with Christ

1Or are you unaware, brothers—for I am addressing those who know the law—that the law holds dominion over a person only as long as he lives? 2For a married woman is bound by law to her husband while he lives; yet if her husband dies, she is released from the law governing that husband. 3Accordingly, she would be called an adulteress if she became another man's while her husband still lived; but if her husband has died, she is free from that law, so that she is not an adulteress when she marries another man. 4Likewise, my brothers, you also have died to the law through the body of Christ, so that you may belong to another—to HimhimHim who was raised from the dead—in order that we might bear fruit for God. 5For while we were living in the flesh, our sinful passions, awakened by the law, were at work in our members to bear fruit for death. 6But now we are released from the law, having died to that which held us captive, so that we serve in the newness of the SpiritspiritSpirit rather than in the oldness of the letter.

The Law's Role in Exposing Sin

7What then shall we say? Is the law sin? By no means! Yet I would not have recognizedrecogniserecognized sin except through the law; indeed, I would not have known covetousness had the law not declared, “You shall not covet.” 8But sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, produced in me every kind of covetousness; for apart from the law, sin is dead. 9I was once alive apart from the law, but when the commandment came, sin revived and I died. 10Thus the very commandment that was intended to bring life brought death to me. 11For sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and, through it, killed me. 12So the law is holy, and the commandment is holy, righteous, and good. 13Did then what is good become death to me? By no means! Rather, sin—so that it might be revealed as sin—worked death in me through what is good, in order that through the commandment sin might become utterly sinful.

The Inner Conflict of the Believer

14For we know that the law is spiritual, but I am fleshly, sold under sin. 15For I do not comprehend my own actions: I do not do what I wish, but I practice the very thing I hate. 16If, then, I do what I do not wish, I concur with the law that it is good. 17So it is no longer I who accomplish it, but sin dwelling within me. 18For I know that nothing good dwells in me—that is, in my flesh. For the desire to do good is present with me, but not the power to accomplish it. 19For I do not do the good I wish, but the evil I do not wish is what I do. 20If I do what I do not wish, it is no longer I who accomplish it, but sin dwelling within me. 21So I discover this law: when I wish to do good, evil is close at hand. 22For I delight in the law of God according to my inner being, 23yet I see another law in my members, waging war against the law of my mind and taking me captive to the law of sin that dwells in my members. 24Wretched man that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death? 25Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then, I myself serve the law of God with my mind, but with my flesh I serve the law of sin.
