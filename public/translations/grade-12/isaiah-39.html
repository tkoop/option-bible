Envoys from Babylon Visit Hezekiah

1At that time Merodach-baladan, son of Baladan and king of Babylon, dispatched letters and a gift to Hezekiah, having heard that Hezekiah had been ill and had recovered.

2Hezekiah welcomed the envoys with gladness and showed them everything in his treasure house—the silver, the gold, the spices, the precious oil, his entire armouryarmouryarmory, and all that was found in his storehouses. Nothing in his palace or throughout his dominion was withheld from them.

3Then Isaiah the prophet went to King Hezekiah and asked, “Where did these men come from, and what did they say to you?”

“They came to me from a distant land,” Hezekiah replied, “from Babylon.”

4“What have they seen in your palace?” Isaiah asked.

“They have seen everything in my palace,” Hezekiah answered. “There is nothing in my storehouses that I did not show them.”

Isaiah Foretells Babylon’s Conquest

5Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of the the LORD of Hosts:”

6“Behold, the days are coming when all that is in your palace, together with everything your fathers have stored up to this day, will be carried away to Babylon. Nothing will remain,” says the the LORD.

7“And some of your descendants—your own flesh and blood—will be taken away and made eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.”

8Then Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The word of the the LORD that you have spoken is good.” For he thought, “There will be peace and security throughout my lifetime.”
