A Psalm of David: Thanksgiving for the LORD’s Justice

1I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all YouryourYour wonders. 2I will be glad and exult in YouyouYou; I will sing praise to YouryourYour name, O MostmostMost HighhighHigh.

3When my enemies turn back, they stumble and perish before YouyouYou. 4For YouyouYou have upheld my just cause; YouyouYou sat enthroned, judging in righteousness. 5You have rebuked the nations; YouyouYou have destroyed the wicked; YouyouYou have blotted out their name forever and ever. 6The enemy has come to an end in everlasting ruins; YouyouYou have uprooted their cities, and even their memory has perished.

7But the LORD sits enthroned forever; HeheHe has established HishisHis throne for judgmentjudgementjudgment. 8He judges the world with righteousness and governs the peoples with equity.

9The LORD is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. 10Those who know YouryourYour name trust in YouyouYou, for YouyouYou, the the LORD, have not forsaken those who seek YouyouYou.

11Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion; declare HishisHis deeds among the nations. 12For the OneoneOne who avenges blood remembers; HeheHe does not forget the cry of the afflicted.

13Be gracious to me, O the LORD; see my affliction from those who hate me. You who lift me up from the gates of death, 14that I may declare all YouryourYour praises in the gates of DaughterdaughterDaughter Zion and rejoice in YouryourYour salvation.

15The nations have sunk into the pit they made; their foot is caught in the net they hid. 16The LORD is known by the justice HeheHe executes; the wicked are snared by the work of their own hands. Meditation. Selah.

17The wicked will return to Sheol—all the nations who forget God. 18For the needy will not always be forgotten, nor will the hope of the afflicted perish forever.

19Arise, O the LORD! Do not let man prevail; let the nations be judged in YouryourYour presence. 20Put them in fear, O the LORD; let the nations know that they are but men. Selah.
