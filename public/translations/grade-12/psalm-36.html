The Transgression of the Wicked and the Steadfast Love of God

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD.

1An oracle stirs within my heart concerning the transgression of the wicked: no fear of God stands before his eyes. 2For in his own sight he flatters himself beyond measure, so that he cannot discern or abhor his sin. 3The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit; he has ceased to act wisely and do good. 4Even upon his bed he devises wickedness; he places himself on a path that is not good; he does not reject evil.

5Your loving devotion, O the LORD, reaches to the heavens; YouryourYour faithfulness extends to the clouds. 6Your righteousness is like the mountains of God; YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are like the great deep. O the LORD, YouyouYou preserve both man and beast.

Both men and the sons of men take refuge in the shadow of YouryourYour wings. 8They feast on the abundance of YouryourYour house, and YouyouYou give them drink from YouryourYour river of delights. 9For with YouyouYou is the fountain of life; in YouryourYour light we see light.

10Extend YouryourYour loving devotion to those who know YouyouYou, and YouryourYour righteousness to the upright in heart. 11Let not the foot of the proud come against me, nor the hand of the wicked drive me away. 12There the evildoers have fallen; they are thrown down and cannot rise.
