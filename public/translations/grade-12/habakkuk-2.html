Habakkuk Waits for the the LORD’s Answer

1I will stand at my watchpost and station myself on the rampart; I will keep watch to see what HeheHe will say to me, and what answer I am to give to my complaint.

The Vision and the Righteous

2Then the the LORD answered me: “Write down the vision and inscribe it plainly on tablets, so that a herald may run with it. 3For the vision awaits an appointed time; it speaks of the end and will not prove false. Though it lingers, wait for it, since it will surely come and will not delay.

4“Behold, his soul is puffed up within him and is not upright, but the righteous shall live by his faith. 5Indeed, wine is treacherous; a proud man is never at rest. He enlarges his appetite like Sheol, and like Death he is never satisfied. He gathers all nations to himself and collects all peoples as his own.

Woes Against the Oppressor

6“Will not all these take up a taunt against him, with mockery and riddles, saying, ‘Woe to him who amasses what is not his own—how long must this go on?—and burdens himself with pledges!’ 7Will not your creditors suddenly arise, and those who torment you awaken? Then you will become plunder for them. 8Because you have plundered many nations, the remnant of the peoples will plunder you—because of human bloodshed and violence against lands, cities, and all their inhabitants.

9“Woe to him who builds his house through unjust gain, setting his nest on high to escape calamity! 10You have devised shame for your house by cutting off many peoples, and you have sinned against your own soul. 11For the stone will cry out from the wall, and the beam will answer it from the woodwork.

12“Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed and establishes a town by iniquity! 13Behold, is it not from the the LORD of Hosts that the peoples labourlabourlabor only to feed the fire, and the nations weary themselves in vain? 14For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.

The Cup of the the LORD and the Folly of Idols

15“Woe to him who gives his neighbourneighbourneighbor drink, pouring out your wrath and making him drunk, so that you may gaze on their nakedness! 16You are filled with shame instead of glory. You too—drink and be exposed! The cup in the the LORD’s right hand will come around to you, and disgrace will cover your glory. 17For the violence against Lebanon will cover you, and the destruction of the beasts will terrify you, because of the blood of men and the violence done to the land, the city, and all who dwell in it.

18“What use is an idol, that its maker should carve it—or a cast image, a teacher of lies? For its maker trusts in his own creation, making idols that cannot speak. 19Woe to him who says to wood, ‘Awake!’ and to a silent stone, ‘Arise!’ Can it teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all within it.

20“But the the LORD is in HishisHis holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before HimhimHim.”
