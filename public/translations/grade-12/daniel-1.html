Daniel and His Companions Exiled to Babylon

1In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it. 2And the LORD delivered Jehoiakim king of Judah into his power, together with some of the vessels of the house of God; he carried them to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and placed the vessels in the treasury of his god.

Selected for the King’s Service

3Then the king commanded Ashpenaz, his chief of the eunuchs, to bring in some of the Israelites from the royal family and from the nobility— 4young men without physical defect, handsome, proficient in all wisdom, endowed with knowledge and insight, and able to serve in the king’s palace. He was to teach them the literature and language of the Chaldeans. 5The king assigned them a daily portion of the royal delicacies and of the wine he drank, and he ordered that they be sustained and trained for three years, after which they were to enter the king’s service. 6Among these were Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah of the tribe of Judah. 7The chief of the eunuchs gave them names: Daniel he called Belteshazzar; Hananiah, Shadrach; Mishael, Meshach; and Azariah, Abednego.

Daniel’s Resolve to Remain Undefiled

8But Daniel resolved in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king’s delicacies or with the wine he drank. Therefore he asked the chief of the eunuchs for permission not to defile himself. 9Now God had given Daniel favourfavourfavor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.

10The chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has assigned your food and your drink. Why should he see your faces looking worse than those of the other young men your age? You would endanger my head before the king.”

11Daniel then said to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, 12“Please test your servants for ten days. Give us vegetables to eat and water to drink. 13Then let our appearance be compared before you with that of the young men who eat the king’s delicacies, and deal with your servants according to what you see.”

14He agreed to this proposal and tested them for ten days. 15At the end of the ten days, their appearance was better and they were healthier than all the young men who ate the king’s delicacies. 16So the steward took away their delicacies and the wine they were to drink, and gave them vegetables.

17To these four young men God gave knowledge and skill in all literature and wisdom; and Daniel understood all visions and dreams. 18At the end of the time the king had set for their training, the chief of the eunuchs brought them before Nebuchadnezzar. 19The king spoke with them, and among all of them none was found like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; so they entered the king’s service. 20In every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king questioned them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters in his entire kingdom. 21Daniel remained there until the first year of King Cyrus.
