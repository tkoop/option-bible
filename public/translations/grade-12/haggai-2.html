The LORD's Message to the Remnant and the Call to Rebuild

1On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came through the prophet Haggai, saying: 2“Say now to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people:”

3“Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory? How do you see it now? Does it not seem to you like nothing in comparison? Yet now be strong, Zerubbabel,” declares the LORD. “Be strong, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest,” declares the LORD. “Be strong, all you people of the land,” declares the LORD. “Work, for I am with you,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 5“This is the covenant I made with you when you came out of Egypt, and MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit remains among you. Do not be afraid.”

The Greater Glory of the Latter House

6For thus says the LORD of Hosts: “Once more, in a little while, I will shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry land. 7I will shake all the nations, and the treasures of all the nations will be brought in, and I will fill this house with glory,” says the LORD of Hosts. 8“The silver is MinemineMine, and the gold is MinemineMine,” declares the LORD of Hosts. 9“The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former,” says the LORD of Hosts, “and in this place I will grant peace,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

Holiness, Hardship, and the Promise of Blessing

10On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet, saying: 11“Thus says the LORD of Hosts: Ask now the priests concerning the law, saying:”

12“If a man carries holy meat in the fold of his garment, and with that fold touches bread, or stew, or wine, or oil, or any food, does it become holy?”

And the priests answered, “No.”

13Then Haggai said, “If one defiled by contact with a corpse touches any of these, does it become unclean?”

And the priests answered, “It becomes unclean.”

14Then Haggai answered and said, “So it is with this people and this nation before MemeMe,” declares the LORD, “and so it is with every work of their hands; whatever they offer there is unclean.”

15“And now, consider carefully from this day forward, from before a stone was laid upon a stone in the temple of the LORD. 16Since those days, when one came to a heap of twenty measures, there were but ten; when one came to the winepress to draw out fifty measures, there were but twenty. 17I struck you with blight and mildew and hail in all the work of your hands, yet you did not return to MemeMe,” declares the LORD. 18“Consider carefully from this day forward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the foundation of the temple of the LORD was laid—consider it carefully. 19Is the seed still in the barn? Even the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate, and the olive tree have not yet produced fruit. But from this day on I will bless you.”

The LORD's Promise to Zerubbabel

20And the word of the LORD came a second time to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month, saying: 21“Speak to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, saying: I will shake the heavens and the earth. 22I will overthrow the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the power of the kingdoms of the nations; I will overturn chariots and their riders, and horses and their riders will fall, each by the sword of his brother.” 23“On that day,” declares the LORD of Hosts, “I will take you, MymyMy servant Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel,” declares the LORD, “and I will make you like MymyMy signet ring, for I have chosen you,” declares the LORD of Hosts.
