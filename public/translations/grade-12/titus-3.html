Exhortations to Civic Submission and Good Works

1Remind them to subordinate themselves to rulers and authorities, to obey, and to stand ready for every good work; 2to slander no one, to be peaceable and gentle, and to demonstrate complete humility toward all people.

The Grace That Transformed Us

3For we ourselves were once foolish, disobedient, and led astray, enslaved to various passions and pleasures, spending our lives in malice and envy, detested by others and detesting one another. 4But when the kindness of God our SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior and HishisHis love for humanity appeared, 5HeheHe saved us—not on the basis of righteous deeds we had performed, but according to HishisHis mercy—through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 6whom HeheHe poured out on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, 7so that, having been justified by HishisHis grace, we might become heirs in keeping with the hope of eternal life.

Trustworthy Teaching and the Rejection of Division

8This saying is trustworthy, and I want you to insist on these matters, so that those who have trusted in God may be intent on practisingpractisingpracticing good works. These things are excellent and beneficial for people. 9But shun foolish controversies, genealogies, quarrels, and disputes about the law, for they are useless and empty. 10Reject a divisive person after a first and second warning. 11Know that such a one is perverted and sinful, being self-condemned.

Final Instructions and Greetings

12When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make every effort to come to me at Nicopolis, for I have decided to winter there. 13Send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way with diligence, so that they lack nothing. 14And let our people learn to devote themselves to good works in order to meet pressing needs, so that they will not be unfruitful. 15All who are with me greet you. Greet those who love us in the faith. Grace be with you all.
