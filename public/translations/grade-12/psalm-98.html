A New Song of Praise to the LORD

1Sing to the LORD a new song, for HeheHe has done wonders; HishisHis right hand and holy arm have won HimhimHim the victory. 2The LORD has made known HishisHis salvation and revealed HishisHis righteousness to the nations. 3He has remembered HishisHis steadfast love and faithfulness to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

4Raise a joyful shout to the LORD, all the earth; break forth in song, rejoice, and sing praises. 5Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and melodious song. 6With trumpets and the blast of the ram's horn, shout for joy before the LORD, the KingkingKing.

7Let the sea roar, and all that fills it; let the world and those who dwell in it resound. 8Let the rivers clap their hands; let the mountains sing together for joy 9before the LORD, for HeheHe comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity.
