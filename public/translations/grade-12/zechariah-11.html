JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Lebanon’s Cedars

1Open your doors, Lebanon, so that fire may devour your cedars. 2Wail, cypress, for the cedar has fallen; the majestic trees are ruined. Wail, oaks of Bashan, for the dense forest has been cut down. 3Listen to the wail of the shepherds, for their glory is ruined. Listen to the roar of the young lions, for the thickets of the Jordan are destroyed.

The LORD Commands the Shepherd

4This is what the LORD my God says: “Shepherd the flock marked for slaughter.”

5Their buyers slaughter them and feel no guilt; those who sell them say, “Blessed be the LORD, for I am rich!” Their own shepherds show them no mercy.

6“For I will no longer have pity on the inhabitants of the land,” declares the LORD. “Behold, I will cause every man to fall into the hand of his neighbourneighbourneighbor and into the hand of his king; they will crush the land, and I will not deliver anyone from their hand.”

The Two Staves and the Broken Covenant

7So I shepherded the flock marked for slaughter, especially the afflicted of the flock. Then I took two staffs for myself: I called one FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor and the other UnionunionUnion. So I shepherded the flock. 8In one month I dismissed the three shepherds. My soul grew impatient with them, and their soul also detested me.

9Then I said, “I will not shepherd you. Let the dying die, and let the perishing perish; let those who remain devour one another’s flesh.”

10Then I took my staff FavourFavourFavorfavorFavor and cut it in pieces, to break the covenant I had made with all the peoples. 11So it was broken on that day, and the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD.

12Then I said to them, “If it is good in your sight, give me my wages; but if not, keep them.” So they weighed out my wages: thirty pieces of silver.

13And the LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter”—this magnificent price at which they valued me. So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them into the house of the LORD, to the potter. 14Then I cut in pieces my second staff, UnionunionUnion, to break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

The Worthless Shepherd

15And the LORD said to me, “Take up once again the equipment of a foolish shepherd. 16For behold, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not care for the lost, nor seek the young, nor heal the broken, nor sustain the healthy, but will devour the flesh of the fat sheep and tear off their hooves. 17Woe to the worthless shepherd who deserts the flock! May the sword strike his arm and his right eye! May his arm be completely withered and his right eye totally blinded!”
