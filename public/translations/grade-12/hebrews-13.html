Final Exhortations for Christian Living

1Let brotherly love continue. 2Never neglect hospitality, for through it some have entertained angels unaware. 3Remember those in prison as if you were bound with them, and those who suffer mistreatment as if you yourselves were suffering in your own bodies.

4Let marriage be held in honourhonourhonor by all, and let the marriage bed remain undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.

5Keep your conduct free from the love of money, and be content with what you have, for HeheHe has said, "I will never leave you, nor will I ever forsake you."

6Hence we may say with confidence, "ThetheThe Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?"

Christ’s Unchanging Truth

7Remember your leaders, those who proclaimed God's word to you; and, weighing the outcome of their conduct, imitate their faith. 8Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. 9Do not be swept away by all kinds of strange teachings, for it is admirable for the heart to be established by grace rather than by foods, which have never benefited those preoccupied with them.

The Altar Outside the Camp

10We possess an altar from which those who minister at the tabernacle have no authority to eat. 11For when the high priest brings the blood of animals into the holy places as a sin offering, their bodies are consumed by fire outside the camp. 12Consequently, Jesus suffered outside the gate, so that HeheHe might sanctify the people through HishisHis own blood. 13Let us therefore go forth to HimhimHim outside the camp, bearing HishisHis reproach. 14For we have no enduring city here; rather, we seek the city that is to come.

15Through HimhimHim, therefore, let us offer up to God continually a sacrifice of praise—that is, the fruit of lips that acknowledge HishisHis name. 16And do not forget to do good and to share with others, for God is well pleased with such sacrifices.

Closing Exhortations and Benediction

17Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they keep watch over your souls as those who will give an account; do this so that their work may be a joy and not a burden of groaning, for that would be of no advantage to you.

18Pray for us, for we are persuaded that we maintain a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves honourablyhonourablyhonorably in everything. 19I urge you even more earnestly to do this, so that I may be restored to you sooner.
