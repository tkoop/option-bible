The Floodwaters Recede and the Ark Comes to Rest

1Then God remembered Noah, together with every living thing and every animal that was with him in the ark, and HeheHe sent a wind sweeping over the earth, so that the waters subsided. 2The springs of the deep and the floodgates of the heavens were shut, and the rain from the sky was restrained. 3The waters receded steadily from the earth, and after one hundred fifty days they had noticeably diminished. 4On the seventeenth day of the seventh month, the ark came to rest upon the mountains of Ararat. 5The waters continued to recede until the tenth month; on the first day of that month, the summits of the mountains became visible.

Noah Tests the Earth with a Raven and a Dove

6After forty days, Noah opened the window he had constructed in the ark 7and dispatched a raven, which flew back and forth until the waters had dried from the earth. 8Then he dispatched a dove to determine whether the waters had receded from the surface of the ground. 9But the dove found no place to rest its foot, and it returned to him in the ark, because the waters still covered the surface of the entire EarthEarthearth; therefore, he reached out his hand, took the dove, and brought it back into the ark. 10He waited another seven days and again dispatched the dove from the ark. 11When the dove returned to him in the evening, it carried a freshly plucked olive leaf in its beak, so Noah knew that the waters had receded from the earth. 12He waited another seven days and sent out the dove, but this time it did not return to him.

The Earth Dries and Noah Leaves the Ark

13In the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters had dried from the earth; consequently, Noah removed the covering of the ark and looked, and indeed the surface of the ground was dry. 14By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was completely dry.

15Then God said to Noah, 16“Come out of the ark, you and your wife, along with your sons and their wives. 17Bring out every living creature that is with you—birds, livestock, and every creature that crawls upon the earth—so that they may swarm over the earth and be fruitful and multiply upon it.”

18So Noah came out, along with his sons, his wife, and his sons’ wives. 19Every living creature, every creeping thing, every bird, and everything that moves upon the earth emerged from the ark by their families.

Noah’s Sacrifice and God’s Enduring Promise

20Then Noah built an altar to the LORD and, selecting from every clean animal and every clean bird, he offered burnt offerings upon the altar. 21And when the LORD smelled the soothing aroma, HeheHe said in HishisHis heart, “Never again will I curse the ground because of man, for the intent of man’s heart is evil from his youth. And never again will I destroy all living creatures, as I have done.” 22As long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night shall never cease.
