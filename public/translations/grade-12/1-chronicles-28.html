David Gathers Israel's Leaders

1David assembled all the officials of Israel in Jerusalem: the leaders of the tribes, the leaders of the divisions in royal service, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, together with the court officials, the mighty men, and all the valiant warriors.

The Temple Plans

David Encourages Solomon

20And David said to Solomon his son, "Be strong and courageous, and do it. Do not be afraid or dismayed, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you or forsake you until all the work for the service of the house of the LORD is finished. 21And behold, the divisions of the priests and Levites are ready for all the service of the house of God, and every willing man skilled in any craft will be with you in all the work; for all the officials and all the people are at your command."
