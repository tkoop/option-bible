The Seven Bowls of God's Wrath Are Poured Out

1I heard a great voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, "Go and pour out on the earth the seven bowls of the wrath of God."

2The first angel went out and poured his bowl on the earth, and a foul and malignant sore broke out on the people who bore the mark of the beast and worshiped its image. 3The second angel poured his bowl into the sea, and it became blood like that of a corpse, and every living creature in the sea died. 4The third angel poured his bowl into the rivers and the springs of water, and they became blood.

The Angel of the Waters Vindicates God's Justice

5Then I heard the angel of the waters say, "YouyouYou are righteous, O HolyholyHoly OneoneOne, who is and who was, because YouyouYou have judged these things. 6For they poured out the blood of saints and prophets, and YouyouYou have given them blood to drink; they are worthy."

7And I heard the altar say, "Yes, Lord God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, true and righteous are YouryourYour judgmentsjudgementsjudgments."

8The fourth angel poured his bowl on the sun, and it was granted to scorch people with fire. 9The people were scorched with great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God, who has authority over these plagues, and they did not repent so as to give HimhimHim glory. 10The fifth angel poured his bowl on the throne of the beast, and his kingdom was plunged into darkness, and they gnawed their tongues in agony. 11They blasphemed the God of Heavenheavenheaven because of their pains and their sores, and they did not repent of their deeds.

The Sixth Bowl and the Gathering at Armageddon

12The sixth angel poured his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water dried up to prepare the way for the kings from the east. 13Then I saw three unclean spirits resembling frogs coming out of the mouth of the dragon, out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet. 14For they are spirits of demons, performing signs, and they go out to the kings of the whole world to gather them for the battle of the great day of God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty.

15"Behold, I am coming like a thief. Blessed is the one who stays awake and keeps his garments, so that he will not walk naked and his shame be exposed."

16And they gathered them into the place called in Hebrew Armageddon.

The Seventh Bowl Shakes the Earth

17The seventh angel poured his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came out of the throne, from the temple, saying, "It is done."

18Then there were flashes of lightning, voices, and thunder, and a great earthquake occurred, such as had not happened since humanity first appeared on the earth—so tremendous was that earthquake. 19The great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell, and Babylon the great was remembered before God, to give her the cup of the wine of the fury of HishisHis wrath. 20Every island fled, and no mountains were found. 21And great hail, weighing about a talent, fell from Heavenheavenheaven on the people, and the people blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail, for its plague was extraordinarily severe.
