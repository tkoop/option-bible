The Desecration of the Dead

1At that time, declares the LORD, the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem will be brought out of their tombs. 2They will be spread out before the sun, the moon, and all the host of Heavenheavenheaven, which they loved and served, after which they walked, which they sought, and to which they bowed down. They will not be gathered or interred; they will be like dung on the surface of the ground. 3Death will be chosen rather than life by all the remnant that remains of this evil family in all the places where I have driven them, declares the LORD of hosts.

A People Who Refuse to Return

4You shall say to them, “Thus says the LORD: Do men fall and not rise again? If one turns away, does he not return?” 5Why then has this people turned away in perpetual backsliding? They hold fast to deceit; they refuse to return. 6I have listened and heard, but they do not speak what is right. No one repents of his wickedness, saying, “What have I done?” Everyone rushes headlong into his own course, like a horse charging into battle. 7Even the stork in the heavens knows her appointed times, and the turtledove, swallow, and crane observe the time of their coming; but MymyMy people do not know the ordinance of the LORD.

False Wisdom and False Peace

8How can you say, “We are wise, and the law of the LORD is with us”? Surely the lying pen of the scribes has made it a lie. 9The wise men will be put to shame; they will be dismayed and taken. Behold, they have rejected the word of the LORD, so what wisdom is in them? 10Therefore I will give their wives to others and their fields to new owners, because from the least to the greatest everyone is greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, everyone practices deceit. 11They have healed the wound of MymyMy people lightly, saying, “Peace, peace,” when there is no peace. 12Were they ashamed when they committed abomination? No, they were not at all ashamed; they did not know how to blush. Therefore they will fall among the fallen; at the time of their punishment they will stumble, says the LORD. 13When I would gather them, declares the LORD, there are no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the fig tree; even the leaf has withered, and what I gave them has passed away from them.

The Approaching Invasion and a Nation's Lament

14“Why do we sit still? Gather together; let us enter the fortified cities and perish there, for the LORD our God has condemned us to perish and has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD.”

15“We looked for peace, but no good came; for a time of healing, but behold, terror.”

16The snorting of their horses is heard from Dan; at the sound of the neighing of their stallions the whole land quakes. They come and devour the land and its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it.

17“For behold, I am sending among you serpents, adders that cannot be charmed, and they will bite you,” declares the LORD.

18My joy is gone; grief is upon me; my heart is sick.

19Behold, the cry of my people from far and wide in the land: “Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her KingkingKing not in her?”

“Why have they provoked MemeMe to anger with their carved images and with their foreign idols?”

20“The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved.”

21For the wound of the daughter of my people I am crushed; I mourn, and dismay has taken hold of me. 22Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no physician there? Why then has the health of the daughter of my people not been restored?
