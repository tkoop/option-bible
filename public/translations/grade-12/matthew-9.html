Questions About Fasting and New Wine

14Then John’s disciples came to HimhimHim and asked, “Why do we and the Pharisees fast often, but YouryourYour disciples do not fast?”

15Jesus answered, “Can the wedding guests mourn while the bridegroom is with them?
