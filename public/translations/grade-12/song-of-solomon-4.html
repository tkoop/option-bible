The Bridegroom Celebrates His Bride’s Beauty

1“How exquisite you are, my darling—how exquisite indeed! Your eyes are like doves behind your veil; your hair descends like a flock of goats streaming down from Mount Gilead. 2Your teeth resemble a flock of shorn ewes ascending from the washing, each bearing twins, with none left barren. 3Your lips are like a scarlet thread, and your speech is altogether lovely; your cheeks glow like a slice of pomegranate behind your veil.”

4“Your neck is like the tower of David, fashioned in courses of stone; upon it hang a thousand shields, every one the shield of a mighty warrior. 5Your breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle, grazing among the lilies.”

The Bridegroom’s Longing and Unqualified Praise

6“Until the day breathes and the shadows flee, I will betake myself to the mountain of myrrh and to the hill of frankincense. 7You are wholly beautiful, my darling; no blemish is found in you.”

The Bridegroom Summons and Cherishes His Bride

8“Come with me from Lebanon, my bride; come with me from Lebanon. Gaze down from the summit of Amana, from the summit of Senir and Hermon, from the dens of lions and the mountains of leopards. 9You have captivated my heart, my sister, my bride; you have captivated my heart with one glance of your eyes, with one jewel of your necklace. 10How delightful is your love, my sister, my bride! Your love surpasses wine in delight, and the fragrance of your perfume exceeds every spice. 11Your lips, my bride, drip with honey; honey and milk are beneath your tongue; and the fragrance of your garments is like the fragrance of Lebanon.”

The Bride as a Sealed and Fragrant Garden

12“You are a garden enclosed, my sister, my bride, a spring enclosed, a fountain sealed. 13Your shoots form an orchard of pomegranates with precious fruits, henna with nard, 14nard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon, with every kind of frankincense, myrrh and aloes, with all the choicest spices. 15You are a garden spring, a well of living water streaming down from Lebanon.”

The Bride Invites Her Beloved

16“Awake, north wind, and come, south wind! Breathe upon my garden, that its fragrance may be diffused. Let my beloved enter his garden and partake of its choicest fruit.”
