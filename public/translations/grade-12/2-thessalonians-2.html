The Day of the Lord and the Man of Lawlessness

1Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered to HimhimHim, we ask you, brothers, 2not to be quickly shaken in mind or alarmed, whether by a spirit, by a spoken word, or by a letter purportedly from us, claiming that the day of the Lord has already come. 3Let no one deceive you in any way, for that day will not come unless the rebellion comes first and the man of lawlessness is revealed—the son of destruction— 4who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God.

5Do you not remember that while I was still with you I told you these things? 6And you know what is now restraining him, so that he may be revealed in his appointed time. 7For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only the one who now restrains it will continue to do so until he is removed. 8Then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will destroy with the breath of HishisHis mouth and bring to nothing by the appearance of HishisHis coming.

9The coming of the lawless one will be in accordance with the working of Satan, with all power, signs, and false wonders, 10and with every kind of wicked deception among those who are perishing, because they refused to love the truth and so be saved. 11For this reason God sends them a powerful delusion, so that they will believe the lie, 12in order that all who did not believe the truth but took pleasure in unrighteousness may be condemned.

Thanksgiving for God's Choosing

13But we ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers beloved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation through sanctification by the SpiritspiritSpirit and belief in the truth. 14To this HeheHe called you through our gospel, so that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Stand Firm in the Truth

15So then, brothers, stand firm and hold fast to the traditions you were taught by us, whether by word of mouth or by letter. 16Now may our Lord Jesus Christ HimselfhimselfHimself, and God our FatherfatherFather, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope by grace, 17encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good work and word.
