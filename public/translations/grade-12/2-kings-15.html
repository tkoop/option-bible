Azariah’s Reign and Leprosy in Judah

1In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam’s reign over Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah became king of Judah. 2He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jecoliah, and she was from Jerusalem. 3He did what was right in the sight of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done. 4Only the high places were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places. 5The LORD struck the king with leprosy, and he remained a leper until his death. He lived in seclusion, while his son Jotham governed the royal household and judged the people of the land. 6The rest of the acts of Azariah, and all that he did, are they not recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? 7Azariah died and was buried with his ancestors in the CitycityCity of David, and his son Jotham succeeded him as king.

Israel’s Rapid Succession: Zechariah, Shallum, and Menahem

8In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah’s reign over Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned six months. 9He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, as his fathers had done; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had caused Israel to sin. 10Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him and struck him down before the people, killing him; then he reigned in his place. 11The rest of the acts of Zechariah are recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 12This was the word of the LORD that HeheHe had spoken to Jehu, saying, “Your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel.” And so it was.

13In the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah’s reign over Judah, Shallum son of Jabesh became king, and he reigned one month in Samaria. 14Menahem son of Gad marched up from Tirzah to Samaria, struck down Shallum son of Jabesh there, killed him, and reigned in his place. 15The rest of the acts of Shallum, including the conspiracy he formed, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 16Then Menahem attacked Tiphsah and all who were in it, along with its territory, because they refused to open their gates to him; he sacked the city and ripped open all its pregnant women.

17In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah’s reign over Judah, Menahem son of Gad became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned ten years. 18He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD throughout his days; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had caused Israel to sin. 19Pul, king of Assyria, invaded the land, and Menahem gave him a thousand talents of silver so that Pul would support him and strengthen his grip on the kingdom. 20Menahem exacted the money from Israel—from every wealthy and influential man—fifty shekels of silver apiece, to give to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria withdrew and did not remain in the land. 21The rest of the acts of Menahem, and all that he did, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 22Menahem died and was buried with his fathers, and his son Pekahiah succeeded him as king.

Pekahiah, Pekah, and the Assyrian Invasion

23In the fiftieth year of Azariah’s reign over Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned two years. 24He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had caused Israel to sin. 25Pekah son of Remaliah, his captain, conspired against him and struck him down in Samaria, in the citadel of the royal palace, with the help of Argob, Arieh, and fifty Gileadites; he killed him and reigned in his place. 26The rest of the acts of Pekahiah, and all that he did, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

27In the fifty-second year of Azariah’s reign over Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned twenty years. 28He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he had caused Israel to sin. 29During the reign of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria invaded and captured Ijon, Abel-beth-maachah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead, Galilee, and the whole territory of Naphtali; he carried their inhabitants into exile in Assyria. 30Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah, struck him down, killed him, and reigned in his place, in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah. 31The rest of the acts of Pekah, and all that he did, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

Jotham’s Reign in Judah

32In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah’s reign over Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah became king of Judah. 33He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jerusha, the daughter of Zadok. 34He did what was right in the sight of the LORD, just as his father Uzziah had done. 35Only the high places were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and burn incense on the high places. He built the Upper GategateGate of the house of the LORD. 36The rest of the acts of Jotham, and all that he did, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 37In those days the LORD began to send Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah. 38Jotham died and was buried with his fathers in the CitycityCity of David, his father’s city, and his son Ahaz succeeded him as king.
