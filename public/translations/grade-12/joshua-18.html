Israel Assembles at Shiloh

1Then the whole congregation of the Israelites assembled at Shiloh and established the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting there, and the land had been subdued before them. 2Seven tribes among the Israelites still remained without an allotted inheritance.

Joshua Urges the Remaining Tribes to Claim Their Inheritance

3Joshua said to the Israelites, "How long will you be slack in going to possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given to you? 4Appoint for yourselves three men from each tribe, and I will send them; they shall arise and traverse the land, writing it down according to their inheritance, and then they shall return to me. 5They shall divide it into seven portions: Judah shall remain in its territory in the south, and the house of Joseph shall remain in their territory in the north. 6And you shall record the seven portions of the land and bring them to me here, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God. 7For the Levites have no portion among you, since the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance; and Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received the inheritance that Moses, the servant of the LORD, gave them beyond the Jordan to the east."

The Land Is Surveyed and Allotted by Lot

8The men rose and went, and Joshua commanded those going to write the land, saying, "Go and walk through the land and write it down, and return to me; I will cast lots for you here before the LORD in Shiloh." 9So the men went and passed through the land and recorded it by cities into seven portions in a book, and they came to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh. 10Then Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before the LORD, and there Joshua divided the land among the Israelites according to their divisions.

The Territory Allotted to Benjamin

11The lot of the tribe of Benjamin came up according to their families, and the border of their lot fell between the descendants of Judah and the descendants of Joseph. 12Their northern border began at the Jordan and went up to the slope north of Jericho; then it went westward into the hill country, and its end was at the wilderness of Beth-aven. 13From there the border passed southward to Luz—that is, Bethel—then descended to Ataroth-addar on the hill south of LowerlowerLower Beth-horon. 14On the west it curved southward from the hill facing Beth-horon on the south, and its end was at Kiriath-baal (that is, Kiriath-jearim), a city of the sons of Judah. This was the western side. 15The southern side began at the edge of Kiriath-jearim and extended westward to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah. 16Then the border descended to the foot of the hill facing the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom on the north, at the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim, and continued down the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom along the southern slope of the Jebusite city—that is, Jerusalem—and went down to En-rogel.

17From there it turned northward, went to En-shemesh, and continued to Geliloth, which faces the Ascent of Adummim; then it descended to the StonestoneStone of Bohan son of Reuben. 18It continued north of the Arabah and descended into the Arabah. 19Then the border passed north of Beth-hoglah and ended at the northern bay of the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea, at the southern end of the Jordan. This was the southern border. 20The Jordan bounded it on the east. This was the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin, with its boundaries all around.

21Now the cities of the tribe of Benjamin according to their families were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz, 22Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 23Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 24Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba—twelve cities with their villages; 25Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 26Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah, 27Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 28Zelah, Eleph, the Jebusite city (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath—fourteen cities with their villages. This was the inheritance of the sons of Benjamin according to their families.
