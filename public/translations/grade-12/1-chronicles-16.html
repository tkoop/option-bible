The Ark Placed in David’s Tent

1They brought in the ark of God and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it, and they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God. 2When David had finished offering the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. 3Then he distributed to every Israelite, man and woman alike, a loaf of bread, a piece of meat, and a raisin cake.

Worship Leaders Appointed

4He appointed some of the Levites to serve before the ark of the LORD, to commemorate, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel: 5Asaph the chief, Zechariah second, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel with harps and lyres; Asaph was to sound the cymbals; 6and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were to blow the trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God.

David’s Song of Thanksgiving

7On that day David first entrusted to Asaph and his brothers this song of thanksgiving to the LORD.

8Give thanks to the LORD; call upon HishisHis name; make known HishisHis deeds among the peoples. 9Sing to HimhimHim; sing praises to HimhimHim; tell of all HishisHis wondrous works. 10Glory in HishisHis holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. 11Seek the LORD and HishisHis strength; seek HishisHis face continually.

12Remember the wondrous works HeheHe has done, HishisHis miracles and the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments of HishisHis mouth, 13O descendants of Israel HishisHis servant, sons of Jacob HishisHis chosen ones. 14He is the LORD our God; HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are in all the earth. 15Remember HishisHis covenant forever, the word HeheHe commanded for a thousand generations— 16the covenant HeheHe made with Abraham, and HishisHis oath to Isaac, 17which HeheHe confirmed to Jacob as a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant— 18saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance,” 19when you were few in number, very few, and strangers in it.

20They wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people. 21He allowed no one to oppress them; HeheHe rebuked kings on their behalf: 22“Do not touch MymyMy anointed ones, and do MymyMy prophets no harm.”

23Sing to the LORD, all the earth; proclaim HishisHis salvation from day to day. 24Declare HishisHis glory among the nations, HishisHis wondrous works among all peoples. 25For great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; HeheHe is to be feared above all gods. 26For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 27SplendourSplendourSplendor and majesty are before HimhimHim; strength and joy are in HishisHis place.

28Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. 29Ascribe to the LORD the glory due HishisHis name; bring an offering and come before HimhimHim; worship the LORD in the splendoursplendoursplendor of holiness. 30Tremble before HimhimHim, all the earth; indeed, the world is established, it cannot be moved.

31Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad; let them say among the nations, “The LORD reigns.” 32Let the sea roar and all that fills it; let the field exult and all that is in it. 33Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD, for HeheHe comes to judge the earth.

34Oh give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; for HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 35And say, “Save us, O God of our salvation, and gather us and deliver us from the nations, that we may give thanks to YouryourYour holy name and glory in YouryourYour praise.”

36Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Then all the people said, “Amen!” and praised the LORD.

Worship Arranged at the Ark and at Gibeon

37So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark regularly, as each day required, 38along with Obed-edom and his sixty-eight brothers; Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah were gatekeepers.

39He also left Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests before the tabernacle of the LORD in the high place at Gibeon, 40to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering continually, morning and evening, and to do all that is written in the LawlawLaw of the LORD, which HeheHe commanded Israel.

41With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of those chosen and designated by name to give thanks to the LORD, because HishisHis steadfast love endures forever. 42With them were Heman and Jeduthun with trumpets and cymbals for those who were to sound aloud, and musical instruments of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.

43Then all the people departed, each to his own house, and David returned to bless his household.
