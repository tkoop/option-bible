The Promise of Restoration

1When the blessings and curses that I have set before you come upon you, and you take them to heart among all the nations where the the LORD your God has driven you, 2you and your children will return to the the LORD your God and obey HishisHis voice in all that I command you today, with all your heart and with all your soul. 3Then the the LORD your God will restore you from captivity, have compassion on you, and gather you again from all the peoples among whom HeheHe has scattered you.

4Even if you have been banished to the farthest horizon, the the LORD your God will gather you from there and take you back. 5He will bring you into the land your fathers possessed, so that you may possess it; HeheHe will prosper you and multiply you beyond your fathers. 6The the LORD your God will also circumcise your heart and the hearts of your descendants, so that you love the the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, and so that you may live.

7The the LORD your God will place all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate and persecute you. 8You, for your part, will return and obey the voice of the the LORD, keeping all the commandments I am giving you today. 9The the LORD your God will make you abundantly prosperous in all your undertakings—in the fruit of your womb, your livestock, and your land—to your good, for the the LORD will once again delight in prospering you, as HeheHe delighted in your fathers. 10This will happen when you obey the voice of the the LORD your God and keep HishisHis commandments and statutes recorded in this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw, and when you return to the the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

The Commandment Is Within Reach

11For this commandment that I am giving you today is not too difficult for you, nor is it beyond your reach. 12It is not in Heavenheavenheaven, that you should say, “Who will ascend to Heavenheavenheaven for us and retrieve it, so that we may hear it and obey it?” 13Nor is it beyond the sea, that you should say, “Who will cross the sea for us and retrieve it, so that we may hear it and obey it?” 14On the contrary, the word is very near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it.

The Choice Between Life and Death

15See, I have set before you today life and prosperity, as well as death and disaster. 16If you love the the LORD your God, walk in HishisHis ways, and keep HishisHis commandments, statutes, and ordinances, you will live and multiply, and the the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are entering to possess. 17But if your heart turns away and you refuse to listen, if you are lured into bowing down to other gods and serving them, 18I declare to you today that you will certainly perish; you will not live long in the land you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess. 19I call Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth to witness against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Therefore choose life, so that you and your offspring may live— 20loving the the LORD your God, obeying HishisHis voice, and holding fast to HimhimHim—for HeheHe is your life and the length of your days, and HeheHe will let you dwell in the land the the LORD swore to give your fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
