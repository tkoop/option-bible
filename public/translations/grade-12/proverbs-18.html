The Folly of Isolation and Conceit

1Whoever isolates himself pursues his own desire and rebels against all sound wisdom. 2A fool takes no delight in understanding; he cares only to disclose what is in his heart. 3The arrival of wickedness brings contempt in its wake, and disgrace follows hard upon shame. 4The words of a man's mouth are deep waters, but the fountain of wisdom is a bubbling brook.

Justice, Speech, and Diligence

5It is not good to show partiality to the wicked or to deny justice to the righteous. 6A fool's lips stir up contention, and his mouth invites blows. 7A fool's mouth is his ruin, and his lips are a snare to his soul. 8A gossip's words are like tasty morsels; they sink deep into one's innermost being. 9Whoever is slack in his work is a brother to the one who destroys.

True Security and Humility

10The name of ThetheThe LORD is a strong tower; the righteous run into it and are safe. 11A rich man's wealth is his fortified city, and it is like a high wall in his imagination. 12Before ruin, a man's heart is haughty, but humility precedes honourhonourhonor. 13He who answers before he listens—this is his folly and his shame. 14A man's spirit can sustain him in sickness, but who can bear a broken spirit?

15The heart of the discerning acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks it out. 16A man's gift makes room for him and leads him before great men. 17The first to state his case seems right, until another comes and cross-examines him. 18Casting the lot settles disputes and separates powerful opponents.

19An offended brother is harder to win than a fortified city, and disputes are like the bars of a castle. 20From the fruit of his mouth a man's stomach is filled; with the harvest of his lips he is satisfied. 21Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruit. 22He who finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favourfavourfavor from the LORD. 23The poor man pleads for mercy, but the rich man answers harshly. 24A man of many companions may come to ruin, but there is a friend who stays closer than a brother.
