Ahaz’s Idolatry and the LORD’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment

1Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, as his father David had done. 2Instead, he walked in the ways of the kings of Israel and even cast molten images for the Baals. 3He burned incense in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom and burned his sons in the fire, following the abominations of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the Israelites. 4He sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every leafy tree.

Defeat by Aram and Israel

5Therefore the LORD his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Aram, who defeated him, took a great number of captives, and brought them to Damascus. He was also given into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great slaughter. 6For Pekah son of Remaliah killed one hundred twenty thousand in Judah in a single day—all valiant men—because they had forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers. 7Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah, the king’s son; Azrikam, the officer over the palace; and Elkanah, second to the king. 8The Israelites carried away two hundred thousand captives—women, sons, and daughters—and they also seized a great deal of plunder, which they brought to Samaria.

Oded’s Rebuke and Mercy for the Captives

9But a prophet of the LORD named Oded was there, and he went out before the army that was coming to Samaria and said to them, “Because the LORD, the God of your fathers, was angry with Judah, HeheHe gave them into your hand, and you have killed them in a rage that reaches to Heavenheavenheaven. 10And now you intend to subjugate the sons and daughters of Judah and Jerusalem as male and female slaves for yourselves. Are you not also guilty before the LORD your God? 11Now therefore hear me, and return the captives you have taken from your brothers, for the fierce wrath of the LORD is upon you.”

12Then certain men of the leaders of the Ephraimites—Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai—stood up against those returning from the war 13and said to them, “You shall not bring the captives here, for you are proposing to bring guilt upon us from the LORD, adding to our sins and guilt; for our guilt is great, and fierce wrath is against Israel.” 14So the armed men left the captives and the plunder before the princes and the whole assembly. 15The men designated by name rose up, took the captives, and from the plunder clothed all who were naked; they clothed them, gave them sandals, food and drink, and anointed them. They put all the feeble on donkeys and brought them to Jericho, the CitycityCity of Palms, near their brothers. Then they returned to Samaria.

Ahaz’s Further Unfaithfulness and Death

16At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria for help. 17The Edomites came again, struck Judah, and carried away captives. 18The Philistines raided the cities of the lowland and the Negev of Judah; they captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco with its villages, Timnah with its villages, and Gimzo with its villages, and settled there. 19For the LORD humbled Judah because of Ahaz king of Israel, for he had thrown off restraint in Judah and had been most unfaithful to the LORD. 20Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came against him and distressed him instead of strengthening him. 21Although Ahaz took a portion from the house of the LORD, from the king’s house, and from the princes, and gave it to the king of Assyria, it did not help him. 22In the time of his distress, King Ahaz became even more unfaithful to the LORD. 23He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus that had defeated him, for he said, “Because the gods of the kings of Aram help them, I will sacrifice to them so that they help me.” But they became the cause of his downfall and of all Israel’s.

24Ahaz gathered the utensils of the house of God, cut them in pieces, shut the doors of the house of the LORD, and made altars for himself in every corner of Jerusalem. 25In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, provoking the LORD, the God of his fathers. 26The rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 27Ahaz slept with his fathers and was buried in the city, in Jerusalem, but they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel. His son Hezekiah reigned in his place.
