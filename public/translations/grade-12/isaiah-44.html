The Emptiness of Idol Making

9All who fashion idols are nothing, and their precious things are worthless. Their witnesses see nothing and know nothing, so they will be put to shame. 10Who fashions a god or casts an idol to no profit? 11Behold, all his companions will be put to shame, and the craftsmen are but men. Let them all gather and stand up; they will tremble and be put to shame.

12The blacksmith takes a tool, works in the coals, and fashions it with hammers; he works with his strong arm. Even he grows hungry and loses strength; he drinks no water and becomes faint. 13The woodworker extends a measuring line, outlines it with chalk, shapes it with planes, and marks it with a compass. He makes it like the form of a man, like the beauty of man, to dwell in a house. 14He cuts down cedars, or he takes a cypress or an oak and makes it strong for himself among the trees of the forest. He plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.

15It becomes fuel for man. He takes some and warms himself; he kindles a fire and bakes bread. He also makes a god and worships it; he makes an idol and bows down to it. 16Half of it he burns in the fire; on that half he eats meat; he roasts meat and is satisfied. He warms himself and says, "Ah, I am warm; I have seen the fire." 17The rest of it he makes into a god, into his idol; he bows to it, worships it, prays to it, and says, "Deliver me, for you are my god."

18They know nothing and understand nothing, for their eyes are plastered shut so they cannot see, and their hearts cannot understand. 19No one considers, nor is there knowledge or understanding to say, "I have burned half of it in the fire, and I have baked bread on its coals; I have roasted meat and eaten it. Shall I make the rest of it into an abomination? Shall I bow down to a block of wood?" 20He feeds on ashes; a deluded heart has led him astray, and he cannot deliver his soul or say, "IsisIs there not a lie in my right hand?"
