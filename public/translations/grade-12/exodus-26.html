The Tabernacle's Linen Curtains and Their Joining

1Construct the tabernacle with ten curtains of finely spun linen, woven with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and embroidered with cherubim. 2Each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, with every curtain of identical dimensions. 3Join five of the curtains to one another, and join the remaining five to one another. 4Make loops of blue yarn along the edge of the last curtain in the first set, and make the same along the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 5Make fifty loops on the one curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set, so that the loops align opposite one another. 6Make fifty gold clasps and fasten the curtains together with them, so that the tabernacle becomes a single unit.

The Goat-Hair Tent and Its Protective Coverings

7Make curtains of goat hair for the tent over the tabernacle—eleven curtains in all. 8Each of the eleven curtains is to be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide, all of the same size. 9Join five of the curtains into one set and the other six into another; then fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent. 10Make fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set and fifty loops along the edge of the corresponding curtain in the second set. 11Make fifty bronze clasps and insert them into the loops to join the tent into one unit. 12The remaining half curtain shall hang over the back of the tabernacle. 13The tent curtains will be a cubit longer on either side, and the excess length shall hang down along the sides of the tabernacle to cover it. 14Make a covering for the tent from ram skins dyed red, and over that a covering of fine leather.

The Acacia Frames, Bases, and Crossbars

15Make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 16Each frame is to be ten cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 17Form two tenons on each frame, fitted to one another; make every frame of the tabernacle in this way. 18Construct twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle, 19with forty silver bases beneath them—two bases under each frame, one for each tenon. 20For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, make twenty frames 21with forty silver bases—two bases under each frame. 22Make six frames for the rear of the tabernacle, on the west side, 23and make two frames for the two corners at the rear of the tabernacle, 24coupled together from bottom to top and fitted into a single ring; both corner frames must be made in this way. 25Thus there are to be eight frames and sixteen silver bases—two bases under each frame. 26Also make five crossbars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle, 27five for the frames on the other side, and five for the frames on the rear side, to the west. 28The central crossbar in the middle of the frames shall run from one end to the other. 29Overlay the frames with gold, make gold rings to hold the crossbars, and overlay the crossbars with gold. 30Set up the tabernacle according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain.
