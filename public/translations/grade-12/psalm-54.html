A Prayer for Deliverance from Treacherous Adversaries

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A contemplative psalm of David, composed when the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?”

1Deliver me, O God, by YouryourYour name; vindicate me by YouryourYour might. 2Hear my prayer, O God; attend to the words of my mouth. 3For strangers have risen against me, and ruthless men seek my life, giving no thought to God. Selah.
