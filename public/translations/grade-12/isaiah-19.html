Oracle Against Egypt

1An oracle concerning Egypt: Behold, the LORD rides upon a swift cloud and comes to Egypt; the idols of Egypt tremble before HimhimHim, and the heart of Egypt melts within it.

2I will stir up Egypt against Egypt, and they will fight—each against his brother, each against his neighbourneighbourneighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom. 3The spirit of Egypt will be emptied within it, and I will confound its counsel; then they will inquire of the idols, the sorcerers, the mediums, and the necromancers. 4I will deliver Egypt into the hand of a harsh master, and a fierce king will rule over them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

The Ruin of Egypt’s Nile and Counsel

5The waters of the sea will dry up, and the river will be drained and parched. 6Its canals will become foul, and the streams of Egypt will diminish and dry up; reeds and rushes will wither. 7The meadows by the Nile, on the bank of the river, and all that is sown along the Nile will dry up, be driven away, and be no more.

8The fishermen will mourn, and all who cast hooks into the Nile will lament; those who spread nets upon the water will pine away. 9The workers in flax will be ashamed, and the weavers of fine linen will be dismayed. 10Her foundations will be crushed, and all who earn wages will be sick at heart.

11The princes of Zoan are utterly foolish; Pharaoh’s wisest counselors give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am a son of the wise, a son of ancient kings”? 12Where, then, are your wise men? Let them tell you, and let them make known what the LORD of Hosts has purposed against Egypt.

13The princes of Zoan have become fools, and the princes of Memphis are deluded; the cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray. 14The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion, so that they make Egypt stagger in everything it does, as a drunkard staggers in his vomit. 15Egypt will be left with no one capable of acting—neither leader nor follower, neither the mighty nor the lowly.

Egypt’s Humiliation and Future Allegiance

16In that day the Egyptians will be like women, trembling and terrified before the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, which HeheHe shakes over them. 17The land of Judah will become a source of dread to Egypt; whenever it is mentioned, they will fear because of the purpose that the LORD of Hosts has purposed against them.

18In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts; one of them will be called the CitycityCity of Destruction.

19In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD at its border. 20It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry to the LORD because of oppressors, HeheHe will send them a SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior and Champion, and HeheHe will deliver them.

21The LORD will make HimselfhimselfHimself known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians will know the LORD in that day. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings, and they will make vows to the LORD and fulfillfulfilfulfill them. 22The LORD will strike Egypt, striking and healing, and they will return to the LORD. He will listen to their pleas and heal them.

A Highway of Peace Among the Nations

23In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria, and Assyria will come into Egypt, and Egypt into Assyria, and the Egyptians will worship together with the Assyrians. 24In that day Israel will be the third partner with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth, 25whom the LORD of Hosts has blessed, saying, “Blessed be Egypt MymyMy people, and Assyria the work of MymyMy hands, and Israel MymyMy inheritance.”
