The Canaanite Kings Lose Heart

1When all the Amorite kings west of the Jordan and all the Canaanite kings along the coast learned that the LORD had dried up the Jordan’s waters before the Israelites until they had crossed, their courage failed and their resolve dissolved before the Israelites.

Circumcision Renewed at Gilgal

2At that time the LORD said to Joshua, “Make flint knives for yourself and circumcise the Israelites a second time.” 3So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelites at Gibeath-haaraloth.

4This is why Joshua performed the circumcision: every male among the people who had left Egypt—all the men of war—had died in the wilderness on the journey after leaving Egypt. 5Although everyone who had departed had been circumcised, none of those born in the wilderness during the journey after leaving Egypt had been circumcised.

6For the Israelites had wandered in the wilderness forty years, until the entire nation of men of war who had left Egypt had perished, because they had disobeyed the LORD. Indeed, the LORD had sworn to them that HeheHe would never let them see the land HeheHe had promised their fathers—a land of abundant prosperity.

7Consequently, HeheHe raised up their sons in their place, and Joshua circumcised them, for they were uncircumcised, having never been circumcised during the journey. 8When the whole nation had been circumcised, they remained in camp until they had recovered.

9Then the LORD said to Joshua, “Today I have rolled away the disgrace of Egypt from you.” Therefore that place has been called Gilgal to this day.

The First Passover in the Land

10On the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, while camped at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho, the Israelites observed the Passover. 11The very next day after the Passover, they ate unleavened bread and roasted grain from the produce of the land.

12The day after they had eaten the produce of the land, the manna ceased, and the Israelites had no more manna; from then on they ate the yield of the land of Canaan.

The Commander of the Army of the the LORD

13Now as Joshua neared Jericho, he lifted his eyes and saw a man standing before him with a drawn sword in his hand. Joshua approached him and asked, “Are you for us or for our enemies?”

14“Neither,” he replied. “I have come now as commander of the army of the LORD.”

Then Joshua fell facedown to the ground and worshiped, and he asked, “What does my Lord say to HishisHis servant?”

15The commander of the army of the LORD said to Joshua, “Remove your sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy.” And Joshua did so.
