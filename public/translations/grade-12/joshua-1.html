Joshua Commands the Officers

10Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people, saying, 11"Pass through the camp and command the people, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, for within three days you will cross this Jordan to go in and possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you to possess.'"
