Job Responds to His Friends

1Then Job answered and said:

2"Listen carefully to my words, and let this be your comfort. 3Bear with me while I speak; after I have spoken, you may mock. 4As for me, is my complaint against a human being? Why, then, should I not be impatient? 5Look at me and be appalled, and lay your hand over your mouth. 6When I remember, I am dismayed, and shuddering seizes my flesh.

Why Do the Wicked Prosper?

7"Why do the wicked live on, grow old, and increase in power? 8Their children are secure around them, and their descendants are before their eyes. 9Their homes are safe from dread, and God's rod does not strike them. 10Their bulls breed without fail; their cows calve and do not miscarry. 11They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children skip about. 12They sing to the tambourine and lyre and rejoice at the sound of the pipe. 13They spend their days in prosperity and descend to Sheol in peace. 14They say to God, 'Depart from us! We do not desire knowledge of YouryourYour ways. 15What is the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, that we should serve HimhimHim? What do we gain by praying to HimhimHim?' 16Behold, their prosperity is not in their own hands; the counsel of the wicked is far from me. 17How often is the lamp of the wicked extinguished, and calamity overtakes them? He allots suffering in HishisHis anger. 18They are like straw before the wind, like chaff swept away by a storm. 19You say, 'God stores up their iniquity for their children.' Let HimhimHim repay them, that they may know it. 20Let their own eyes see their ruin, and let them drink the wrath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 21For what do they care about their house after them, when the number of their months has been cut short? 22Will anyone teach God knowledge, when HeheHe judges those on high?

Death Comes to All Alike

23"One person dies in full vigourvigourvigor, completely at ease and secure. 24His sides are full of milk, and the marrow of his bones is moist. 25Another dies with a bitter soul, never having tasted prosperity. 26They lie down together in the dust, and worms cover them.

Job Confronts Empty Comfort

27"Behold, I know your thoughts and the schemes you devise against me. 28For you say, 'Where is the prince's house? Where is the tent of the wicked?' 29Have you not asked those who travel the roads? Do you not accept their testimony 30that the wicked are spared in the day of calamity and rescued in the day of wrath? 31Who confronts him to his face? Who repays him for what he has done? 32When he is carried to the grave, a watch is kept over his tomb. 33The grave is pleasant to him; all humanity follows him, and countless go before him. 34How then will you comfort me with empty words, when your answers remain nothing but falsehood?"
