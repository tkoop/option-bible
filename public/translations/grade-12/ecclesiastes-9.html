The Common Fate of Humanity

1For I set all this before my heart and sought to examine it thoroughly: the righteous, the wise, and their deeds are in God's hand, yet no one can know whether love or hatred lies ahead for them. 2Everything befalls all alike: one fate overtakes the righteous and the wicked, the good and the evil, the clean and the unclean, the one who sacrifices and the one who refrains. As with the good, so with the sinner; as with the one who swears an oath, so with the one who dreads swearing.

3This is the calamity in everything done on EarthEarthearth: one fate befalls everyone. Furthermore, the hearts of human beings are full of evil and madness throughout their lives, and afterward they join the dead.

4Yet hope remains for anyone still among the living, for even the lowliest living person fares better than the greatest who has died. 5The living know that they must die, but the dead know nothing; they receive no further reward, and even their memory is forgotten. 6Their love, hatred, and jealousy have already vanished, and they will never again have any share in anything done on EarthEarthearth.

Joy in God's Gifts

7Go, then, eat your bread with joy and drink your wine with a glad heart, for God has already approved your works. 8Let your garments be white at all times, and do not allow oil to be lacking on your head.

9Enjoy life with the wife you love through all the days of your fleeting life that HeheHe has given you on EarthEarthearth—all your fleeting days—for this is your portion in life and in your toil on EarthEarthearth. 10Whatever your hand finds to do, accomplish it with all your might, for in Sheol, where you are going, there is no work, planning, knowledge, or wisdom.

A Poor Man's Saving Wisdom

13I have also seen this wisdom on EarthEarthearth, and it seemed great to me: 14there was a small city with few inhabitants, and a great king came against it, besieged it, and erected massive siege works against it. 15Yet a poor wise man was found in it, and he delivered the city by his wisdom; nevertheless, no one remembered that poor man.

16So I said, "Wisdom is better than strength," but the poor man's wisdom is despised, and his words go unheeded. 17The quiet words of the wise are heard more readily than the shouting of a ruler among fools. 18Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner destroys much good.
