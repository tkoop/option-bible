Isaac Prepares to Bless Esau

1When Isaac was old and his eyes were so weak that he could no longer see, he called his older son Esau and said to him, "MymyMy son."

"Here I am," Esau replied.

2"Look," Isaac said, "I am now old, and I do not know the day of my death. 3Now then, take your weapons, your quiver and bow, and go out into the field to hunt some game for me. 4Prepare me the kind of tasty food I love, and bring it to me to eat, so that I may bless you before I die."

Rebekah and Jacob Prepare a Deception

5Now Rebekah was listening while Isaac spoke to his son Esau. So when Esau left for the field to hunt game and bring it back, 6Rebekah said to her son Jacob, "Look, I overheard your father speaking to your brother Esau, saying, 7'Bring me some game and prepare me a tasty dish, that I may eat and bless you before the LORD before I die.' 8Now, my son, listen carefully to my instructions. 9Go to the flock and bring me two choice young goats, so that I can prepare them as a tasty dish for your father, just as he loves. 10Then take it to your father to eat, so that he may bless you before he dies."

11Jacob said to his mother Rebekah, "Look, my brother Esau is a hairy man, while I am smooth-skinned. 12What if my father touches me? Then I would be revealed to him as a deceiver, and I would bring a curse upon myself rather than a blessing."

13His mother said to him, "Let your curse be on me, my son. Just obey my voice and go get them for me."

14So he went and got them and brought them to his mother, and she made the tasty food, just as his father loved. 15Then Rebekah took the best clothes of her older son Esau, which she had in the house, and put them on her younger son Jacob. 16She put the goatskins on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck. 17Then she handed her son Jacob the tasty food and the bread she had made.

Jacob Deceives His Father

18He went to his father and said, "MymyMy father."

"Yes, my son," Isaac answered. "Who are you?"

19Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please sit up and eat some of my game, so that you may bless me."

20Isaac asked his son, "How did you find it so quickly, my son?"

"Because the LORD your God granted me success," he answered.

21Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Please come near so I can touch you, my son, to know whether you are really my son Esau or not."

22So Jacob came near to his father Isaac, who touched him and said, "ThetheThe voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands are the hands of Esau." 23Isaac did not recognizerecogniserecognize him, because his hands were hairy like those of his brother Esau; so he blessed him.

24"Are you really my son Esau?" Isaac asked.

"I am," Jacob replied.

25"Serve me," Isaac said, "and let me eat some of my son's game, so that I may bless you." So Jacob brought it to him, and he ate; he also brought him wine, and he drank.

26Then his father Isaac said to him, "Please come near and kiss me, my son."

27So he came near and kissed him. When Isaac smelled the fragrance of his clothes, he blessed him and said: "Ah, the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that the LORD has blessed. 28May God give you the dew of Heavenheavenheaven and the richness of the earth—an abundance of grain and new wine. 29May peoples serve you and nations bow down to you. Be master over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. May those who curse you be cursed, and may those who bless you be blessed."

Esau Returns and Jacob Flees to Haran

30As soon as Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had barely left his father's presence, his brother Esau returned from the hunt.

31He too made a tasty dish and brought it to his father. "My father," he said, "sit up and eat of your son's game, so that you may bless me."

32"Who are you?" his father Isaac asked.

"I am your firstborn son, Esau," he answered.

33Isaac trembled violently and said, "Who was it, then, who hunted game and brought it to me? I ate it all before you came, and I blessed him—and indeed, he will be blessed!"

34When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out with a loud and bitter cry and said to his father, "Bless me too, my father!"

35But Isaac said, "YouryourYour brother came deceitfully and took your blessing."

36Esau said, "Isn't he rightly named Jacob? He has deceived me twice: He took my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing!" Then he asked, "Haven't you reserved a blessing for me?"

37Isaac answered Esau, "Behold, I have made him your master, and I have given him all his brothers as servants. I have sustained him with grain and new wine. What, then, can I do for you, my son?"

38Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, my father!" And Esau wept aloud.

39His father Isaac answered him: "Behold, your dwelling will be away from the richness of the earth and away from the dew of Heavenheavenheaven above. 40You will live by your sword and serve your brother, but when you grow restless, you will break his yoke from your neck."

41Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing his father had given him, and Esau said in his heart, "ThetheThe days of mourning for my father are near; then I will kill my brother Jacob."

42When Rebekah was told what her older son Esau had said, she sent for her younger son Jacob and said to him, "Look, your brother Esau is consoling himself by planning to kill you. 43Now, my son, obey my voice: Flee to my brother Laban in Haran. 44Stay with him a few days, until your brother's fury subsides. 45When your brother's anger has turned away from you and he forgets what you did to him, I will send for you and bring you back. Why should I be bereaved of both of you in one day?"

46Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am weary of my life because of the Hittite women. If Jacob takes a wife from the Hittite women, like those who are here in the land, what good is my life?"
