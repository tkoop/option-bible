Jacob Journeys to the Land of the East

1Jacob set out and travelledtravelledtraveled to the land of the EasterneasternEastern peoples. 2He looked and saw a well in a field, where three flocks of sheep lay resting, because the flocks were watered from that well. The stone covering the mouth of the well was large. 3When all the flocks gathered there, the shepherds rolled the stone from the mouth of the well, watered the sheep, and then returned the stone to its place.

Jacob Meets Rachel at the Well

4Jacob said to them, "MymyMy brothers, where are you from?"

"We are from Haran," they replied.

5"Do you know Laban son of Nahor?" he asked.

"We know him," they said.

6"Is he well?" Jacob asked.

"He is well," they replied, "and here comes his daughter Rachel with the sheep."

7Jacob said, "It is still broad daylight, and it is not yet time to gather the livestock. Water the sheep and take them out to pasture."

8"We cannot," they said, "until all the flocks have gathered and the stone has been rolled away from the mouth of the well; then we water the sheep."

9While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with her father's sheep, for she was a shepherdess. 10When Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban, his mother's brother, together with Laban's sheep, he went up to the well, rolled the stone from its mouth, and watered the flock of Laban, his mother's brother. 11Then Jacob kissed Rachel and wept aloud. 12He told her that he was Rebekah's son and a relative of her father, and she ran and told her father.

Jacob's Agreement with Laban

13When Laban heard the news about Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him, embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him to his house. Jacob recounted all these things to him. 14Laban said to him, "Surely you are my own flesh and blood." Jacob stayed with him for a month. 15Then Laban said to Jacob, "Because you are my relative, should you serve me for nothing? Tell me what your wages should be."

16Now Laban had two daughters. The older was named Leah, and the younger Rachel. 17Leah had soft, delicate eyes, but Rachel was beautiful in form and appearance. 18Jacob loved Rachel and said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."

19Laban said, "It is better that I give her to you than to another man. Stay with me."

20Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed to him only a few days because of his love for her. 21Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days are complete, so that I may take her as my wife."

The Deception at the Wedding and Leah's Sons

22Laban gathered all the men of the place and gave a feast. 23In the evening he took his daughter Leah and brought her to Jacob, and Jacob consummated the marriage with her. 24Laban also gave his servant Zilpah to his daughter Leah as her servant. 25In the morning, Jacob discovered that it was Leah, and he said to Laban, "What is this you have done to me? Did I not serve you for Rachel? Why have you deceived me?"

26Laban said, "It is not the custom in our country to give the younger daughter before the firstborn. 27Complete this bridal week, and we will give you the other as well, provided you serve me another seven years."

28Jacob did so and completed her week; then Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife. 29Laban also gave his servant Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as her servant. 30Jacob also consummated his marriage with Rachel, and he loved Rachel more than Leah. He served Laban another seven years.

31The LORD saw that Leah was unloved, and HeheHe enabled her to conceive, while Rachel remained barren. 32Leah conceived and bore a son, and she named him Reuben, for she said, "Because the LORD has seen my affliction; surely now my husband will love me." 33She conceived again and bore a son, and said, "Because the LORD has heard that I am unloved, HeheHe has given me this son also." So she named him Simeon. 34Again she conceived and bore a son, and said, "Now at last my husband will become attached to me, because I have borne him three sons." Therefore she named him Levi. 35Once more she conceived and bore a son, and said, "This time I will praise the LORD." Therefore she named him Judah. Then she stopped bearing children.
