Generosity and Diligence amid Uncertainty

1Be generous with your resources, for after many days you will recover them. 2Distribute a portion among seven recipients, or even eight, because you cannot foresee what calamity may strike the land.

3If the clouds grow full, they pour rain upon the earth; and if a tree falls to the south or to the north, it remains wherever it falls. 4Whoever obsesses over the wind will not sow, and whoever stares at the clouds will not reap.

The Mystery of God's Work and the Call to Perseverance

5As you do not know the path of the wind, nor how bones are formed in the womb of a pregnant woman, so you cannot understand the work of God, the MakermakerMaker of all things. 6Sow your seed in the morning, and do not let your hand grow slack in the evening, for you do not know which will prosper—whether this or that—or whether both alike will be good.

Joy, Memory, and Accountability

7Light is sweet, and it is pleasant for the eyes to behold the sun. 8If a man lives many years, let him rejoice in all of them, but let him remember the days of darkness, for they will be many; everything that is to come is futility. 9Rejoice, young man, in your youth, and let your heart cheer you in the days of your youth; walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes, but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgmentjudgementjudgment. 10Remove vexation from your heart, and put away evil from your flesh, for youth and the dawn of life are futility.
