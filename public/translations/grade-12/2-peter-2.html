Warning Against False Teachers

1But there were also false prophets among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you, who will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them and bringing swift destruction on themselves. 2Many will follow their depraved conduct, and because of them the way of truth will be blasphemed. 3In their greed they will exploit you with fabricated words. Their condemnation, decreed long ago, is not idle, and their destruction does not slumber.

God's JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Ungodly

4For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into HellHellhell and committed them to chains of gloomy darkness to be kept until the judgmentjudgementjudgment; 5and if HeheHe did not spare the ancient world, but brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly and preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness, with seven others; 6and if HeheHe condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction by reducing them to ashes, making them an example of what is coming to the ungodly; 7and if HeheHe rescued righteous Lot, distressed by the depraved conduct of the lawless— 8for that righteous man, living among them day after day, was tormented in his righteous soul by the lawless deeds he saw and heard— 9then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials and to hold the unrighteous for punishment on the day of judgmentjudgementjudgment.

The Character and Corruption of False Teachers

10especially those who follow the flesh in its corrupt desires and despise authority. Bold and arrogant, they do not tremble as they blaspheme glorious ones, 11whereas angels, though greater in strength and power, do not bring a slanderous charge against them before the Lord. 12But these people are like irrational animals, creatures of instinct born to be caught and destroyed; they blaspheme about things they do not understand, and in their destruction they too will be destroyed, 13receiving harm as the wages of their wrongdoing. They consider it pleasure to carouse in broad daylight. They are stains and blemishes, carousing in their deceit while they feast with you. 14Their eyes are full of adultery, insatiable for sin; they entice unstable souls; they have hearts trained in greed—accursed children! 15They have forsaken the straight way and wandered off, following the way of Balaam son of Beor, who loved the wages of wrongdoing, 16but was rebuked for his own transgression: a speechless donkey spoke with a human voice and restrained the prophet's madness.

The Empty Promises of False Freedom

17These men are springs without water and mists driven by a storm; blackest darkness is reserved for them. 18For by speaking arrogant, empty words, they entice by sensual passions of the flesh those who have barely escaped from people living in error. 19They promise them freedom, while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for whatever overcomes a person enslaves him. 20If they have escaped the defilements of the world through the knowledge of our Lord and SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior Jesus Christ and are again entangled in them and overcome, their last condition is worse than the first. 21For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than, having known it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them. 22As the true proverb says, "A dog returns to its own vomit," and, "a sow, after washing, wallows in the mud."
