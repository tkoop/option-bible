Paul's Pastoral Concern for Colossae and Laodicea

1For I want you to comprehend the magnitude of the struggle I sustain for you and for those in Laodicea, as well as for all who have never seen my face in the flesh, 2that their hearts may be encouraged, being united in love, and attaining to all the riches of full assurance of understanding and knowledge of God's mystery, which is Christ, 3in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

4I declare this lest anyone delude you with plausible but hollow reasoning. 5For although I am absent in body, I am present with you in spirit, rejoicing as I observe your good order and the steadfastness of your faith in Christ.

Walking in Christ and Resisting Philosophical Deception

6Therefore, just as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, continue to walk in HimhimHim, 7rooted and built up in HimhimHim, established in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thanksgiving.

8Take heed that no one captivates you through philosophy and empty deceit, in accordance with human tradition and the elemental forces of the world rather than in accordance with Christ. 9For in HimhimHim the entire fullness of Deity dwells bodily, 10and you have been filled in HimhimHim, who is the head of all rule and authority.

United with Christ in Circumcision and Resurrection

11In HimhimHim you were also circumcised with a circumcision not performed by hands, through the putting off of the body of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ, 12having been buried with HimhimHim in baptism, in which you were also raised with HimhimHim through faith in the powerful working of God, who raised HimhimHim from the dead.

13And you, who were dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, God made alive together with HimhimHim, having forgiven us all our trespasses, 14by canceling the record of debt that stood against us with its legal demands; HeheHe set it aside, nailing it to the cross. 15He disarmed the rulers and authorities and subjected them to public disgrace, triumphing over them in HimhimHim.

Freedom from Legalistic Regulations

16Therefore let no one judge you in matters of food and drink, or in respect to a festival, a new moon, or Sabbaths. 17These things are a shadow of what is to come, but the substance belongs to Christ.

18Let no one disqualify you by delighting in false humility and the worship of angels, dwelling on visions, and being vainly inflated by his fleshly mind, 19while not holding fast to the Head, from whom the whole body, nourished and united through its joints and ligaments, grows with the growth that comes from God.

20If you died with Christ to the elemental forces of the world, why, as though you still lived in the world, do you submit to regulations— 21“Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch”— 22things that all perish as they are used, in accordance with human commandments and teachings? 23These indeed possess an appearance of wisdom in promoting self-made religion, false humility, and severity to the body, yet they are of no value in restraining the indulgence of the flesh.
