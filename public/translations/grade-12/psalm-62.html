Waiting in Silence for God

1Truly my soul waits in silence for God; my salvation comes from HimhimHim. 2He alone is my rock and my salvation; HeheHe is my fortress; I will not be greatly shaken.

The Malice of His Enemies

3How long will you assail a man? All of you will be slain, like a leaning wall, like a tottering fence. 4They conspire only to bring him down from his lofty place; they delight in lies. With their mouths they bless, but inwardly they curse. Selah.

Renewed Confidence in God

Trust in God, Not in Human Power
