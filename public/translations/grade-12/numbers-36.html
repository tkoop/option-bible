The Appeal Concerning Inherited Land

1The heads of the families of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh—one of the clans of Joseph—approached Moses and the leaders, who were the heads of the Israelite families. 2They said, “The LORD commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance to the Israelites by lot, and my lord was commanded by the LORD to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters. 3But if they marry any of the men from the other tribes of Israel, their inheritance will be withdrawn from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry; thus a portion of our allotted inheritance would be taken away. 4And when the Jubilee for the Israelites comes, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry, and their inheritance will be removed from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers.”

The LORD’s Command Through Moses

5So at the the LORD’s command, Moses gave this order to the Israelites: “The tribe of the sons of Joseph has spoken correctly. 6This is what the the LORD has commanded concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: They may marry anyone they please, provided they marry within the clan of their father’s tribe. 7No inheritance in Israel may be transferred from tribe to tribe, because each Israelite is to retain the inheritance of his fathers’ tribe. 8Every daughter who inherits property in any Israelite tribe must marry within the clan of her father’s tribe, so that every Israelite will retain the inheritance of his fathers. 9No inheritance may be transferred from one tribe to another, for each Israelite tribe must retain its inheritance.”

The Obedience of Zelophehad’s Daughters

10So the daughters of Zelophehad did as the the LORD had commanded Moses. 11Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, married their cousins, 12and they married within the clans of the sons of Manasseh son of Joseph; thus their inheritance remained within their father’s tribe and clan.

Conclusion of the Book

13These are the commandments and ordinances that the the LORD gave the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
