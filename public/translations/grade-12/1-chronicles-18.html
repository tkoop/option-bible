David's Conquests of the Philistines and Moab

1After these events, David defeated the Philistines and subdued them, seizing Gath and its dependent towns from Philistine control. 2He also defeated Moab, and the Moabites became subject to David, paying him tribute.

Campaigns against Hadadezer and Aram

3David defeated Hadadezer, king of Zobah, near Hamath while Hadadezer was advancing to establish his dominion along the Euphrates River. 4David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand infantrymen; he hamstrung every chariot horse, preserving only a hundred.

5When the Arameans of Damascus came to assist Hadadezer, David struck down twenty-two thousand of their men. 6He then stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became subject to David and paid him tribute. Thus the LORD gave David victory wherever he marched.

Spoils Dedicated to the LORD

7David took the gold shields borne by Hadadezer's servants and brought them to Jerusalem. 8From Tibhath and Cun, cities belonging to Hadadezer, David carried off an exceptionally large quantity of bronze; Solomon later fashioned from it the bronze SeaseaSea, the pillars, and other bronze furnishings.

9When Tou, king of Hamath, heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer, king of Zobah, 10he sent his son Hadoram to King David to greet him and bless him, because David had waged war against and defeated Hadadezer, who had been Tou's adversary. Hadoram brought articles of every kind—gold, silver, and bronze.

11King David consecrated these gifts to the LORD, together with the silver and gold he had taken from all the nations—from Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and the Amalekites.

Edom Subdued and the Kingdom Organized

12Moreover, Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 13He stationed garrisons throughout Edom, and all the Edomites became subject to David. Thus the LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

14David reigned over all Israel, administering justice and righteousness to all his people.

15Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud served as recorder. 16Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar served as priests; Shavsha served as scribe. 17Benaiah son of Jehoiada oversaw the Cherethites and Pelethites, and David's sons served as chief officials at the king's side.
