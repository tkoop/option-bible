Ananias and Sapphira’s Deception

1Now a man named Ananias, together with his wife Sapphira, sold a piece of property. 2With his wife’s full knowledge, he kept back part of the money for himself, yet brought the rest and laid it at the apostles’ feet.

3Then Peter said, “Ananias, how is it that Satan has so filled your heart that you have lied to the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit and kept back part of the proceeds from the land? 4While it remained unsold, did it not belong to you? And after it was sold, was not the money at your disposal? What prompted you to conceive this deed in your heart? You have not lied to human beings but to God.”

5When Ananias heard these words, he fell down and died, and profound fear seized all who heard of it. 6The young men then rose, wrapped his body, carried him out, and buried him.

7About three hours later his wife came in, unaware of what had happened. 8Peter asked her, “Tell me, did you and Ananias sell the land for this price?”

“Yes,” she answered, “for that price.”

9Peter said to her, “How could you and Ananias agree to test the SpiritspiritSpirit of the Lord? Listen! The feet of the men who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out as well.” 10Immediately she fell down at his feet and died. When the young men entered and found her dead, they carried her out and buried her beside her husband. 11Profound fear seized the whole church and everyone who heard about these events.

Apostolic Signs and a Growing Church

12Meanwhile, through the apostles’ hands many signs and wonders were performed among the people, and all the believers were united in Solomon’s Colonnade. 13Although the people held them in high esteem, none of the rest dared to join them.

14Yet more and more men and women believed in the Lord and were added to their number. 15As a result, people even carried the sick into the streets and laid them on cots and mats, hoping that as Peter passed by, at least his shadow might fall on some of them. 16Crowds also gathered from the cities surrounding Jerusalem, bringing the sick and those tormented by unclean spirits, and all of them were healed.

Arrest, Imprisonment, and Angelic Release

17Then the high priest rose up, along with all his associates who belonged to the party of the Sadducees, and they were filled with jealousy. 18They arrested the apostles and put them in the public jail.

19But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the prison, led them out, and said, 20“Go and stand in the temple, and speak to the people all the words of this life.” 21At daybreak they entered the temple and began to teach. Now when the high priest and his associates arrived, they convened the Sanhedrin and the entire council of the elders of Israel, and sent to the prison to bring the apostles.

22But when the officers arrived at the prison, they did not find them there; so they returned and reported, 23“We found the prison securely locked and the guards standing at the doors, but when we opened them, we found no one inside.”

24When the captain of the temple guard and the chief priests heard this report, they were perplexed about the apostles and wondered what would come of it. 25Then someone arrived and announced, “Look! The men you put in prison are standing in the temple and teaching the people.” 26So the captain went with the officers and brought them, though not by force, for they feared the people would stone them.

Trial Before the Council and Gamaliel’s Counsel

27They brought them in and set them before the Sanhedrin, and the high priest questioned them: 28“We gave you strict orders not to teach in this name, yet you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching and intend to bring this man’s blood upon us.”

29Peter and the other apostles answered, “We must obey God rather than men. 30The God of our ancestors raised Jesus, whom you killed by hanging HimhimHim on a tree. 31God exalted HimhimHim to HishisHis right hand as Prince and SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, to grant repentance to Israel and forgiveness of sins. 32We are witnesses of these things, and so is the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, whom God has given to those who obey HimhimHim.”

33When they heard this, they were enraged and wanted to put them to death. 34But a Pharisee named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law held in honourhonourhonor by all the people, stood up in the Sanhedrin and ordered the men to be put outside for a short time.

35Then he said to them, “Men of Israel, be careful what you are about to do to these men. 36For some time ago Theudas rose up, claiming to be someone, and about four hundred men joined him. He was killed, and all who followed him were dispersed and came to nothing. 37After him Judas the Galilean rose up in the days of the census and drew away people after him. He too perished, and all who followed him were scattered. 38So in the present case I advise you: leave these men alone and let them go. For if this plan or this undertaking is of human origin, it will be overthrown. 39But if it is from God, you will not be able to overthrow them; indeed, you may even be found fighting against God.”

40They were persuaded by him. After calling in the apostles, they flogged them, ordered them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and released them. 41So they left the Sanhedrin, rejoicing that they had been counted worthy to suffer dishonourdishonourdishonor for the NamenameName. 42And every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not cease teaching and proclaiming Jesus as the Christ.
