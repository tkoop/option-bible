Praise the LORD for His Greatness and Enduring Name

1Praise the LORD! Praise the name of the LORD; praise HimhimHim, O servants of the LORD— 2those who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God. 3Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good; sing to HishisHis name, for it is pleasant. 4For the LORD has chosen Jacob for HimselfhimselfHimself, Israel as HishisHis treasured possession.

5For I know that the LORD is great, and our Lord is above all gods. 6The LORD does whatever pleases HimhimHim in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and all their depths. 7He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth; HeheHe sends lightning with the rain and brings the wind out from HishisHis storehouses.

8He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both human and beast. 9He sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants. 10He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings— 11Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan. 12He gave their land as an inheritance, an inheritance to HishisHis people Israel.

13Your name, O the LORD, endures forever; YouryourYour renown, O the LORD, through all generations. 14For the LORD will vindicate HishisHis people and have compassion on HishisHis servants.

15The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of human hands. 16They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see. 17They have ears, but cannot hear; nor is there breath in their mouths. 18Those who make them will become like them, as will all who trust in them.

19O house of Israel, bless the LORD! O house of Aaron, bless the LORD! 20O house of Levi, bless the LORD! You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD! 21Blessed be the LORD from Zion—HeheHe who dwells in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!
