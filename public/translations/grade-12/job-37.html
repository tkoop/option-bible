Elihu Trembles at God's Thunder and Lightning

1At this my heart pounds and leaps from its place. 2Listen carefully to the thunder of HishisHis voice and the rumbling that comes from HishisHis mouth. 3He unleashes it beneath the whole sky, and HishisHis lightning flashes to the ends of the earth. 4After it HishisHis voice roars; HeheHe thunders with HishisHis majestic voice. He does not hold back the lightning when HishisHis voice is heard. 5God thunders wondrously with HishisHis voice; HeheHe does great things we cannot comprehend.

God Commands the Snow, Rain, and Storm

6For HeheHe says to the snow, "Fall on the earth," and to the rain shower, "Be a mighty downpour." 7He seals up the hand of every man, so that all men may know HishisHis work. 8Then the beasts take cover in their lairs and remain in their dens. 9The tempest comes from its chamber, and the cold from the driving winds. 10By the breath of God ice is formed, and the broad waters are frozen solid. 11He also loads the clouds with moisture and scatters HishisHis lightning through the clouds. 12And they swirl about, turning round and round at HishisHis direction, accomplishing whatever HeheHe commands them over the face of the whole EarthEarthearth. 13Whether for punishment, or for HishisHis land, or for mercy, HeheHe makes it happen.

Job Is Challenged to Understand God's Wonders

14Listen to this, Job; stand still and consider the wondrous works of God. 15Do you know how God directs them, making the lightning flash in HishisHis cloud? 16Do you know the balancing of the clouds, the wondrous works of HimhimHim who is perfect in knowledge— 17you whose garments grow hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? 18Can you, with HimhimHim, spread out the skies, hard as a mirror of bronze? 19Teach us what we should say to HimhimHim; we cannot draw up our case because of darkness. 20Should it be reported to HimhimHim that I would speak? If a man speaks, surely he would be swallowed up. 21Now no one can look at the sun when it is bright in the skies, after the wind has swept through and cleared them.

The Unsearchable Majesty of the Almighty

22Out of the north comes golden splendoursplendoursplendor; God is clothed with awesome majesty. 23The AlmightyalmightyAlmighty—we cannot find HimhimHim; HeheHe is great in power and justice, and abundant righteousness HeheHe does not oppress. 24Therefore men fear HimhimHim; HeheHe does not regard any who are wise in their own heart.
