Jehoiada’s Covenant and the Gathering of the Levites

1In the seventh year, Jehoiada consolidated his authority and brought into covenant with him the commanders of hundreds—Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri. 2They travelledtravelledtraveled throughout Judah, gathered the Levites from every city in Judah and the heads of Israel’s ancestral houses, and came to Jerusalem.

The Coronation of Joash

3The whole assembly made a covenant with the king in the house of God. Jehoiada said to them, “Indeed, the king’s son will reign, just as the LORD has declared concerning the sons of David. 4This is what you are to do: a third of you who are priests and Levites, those entering service on the Sabbath, shall serve as gatekeepers at the thresholds; 5a third shall be at the royal palace, and a third at the Foundation GategateGate, while all the people remain in the courts of the house of the LORD.

6“No one may enter the house of the LORD except the priests and Levites on duty; they may enter because they are consecrated, but all the people must observe the LORD’s mandate. 7The Levites shall surround the king on every side, each with his weapon in hand, and anyone who enters the temple shall be put to death. You must stay with the king wherever he goes.”

8So the Levites and all Judah did everything Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each man took his men—both those coming on duty and those going off duty on the Sabbath—for Jehoiada the priest had not dismissed any of the divisions. 9Then Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears, shields, and bucklers of King David that were in the house of God. 10He stationed all the people, each with his weapon in hand, around the king—from the south side of the temple to the north side, near the altar and the temple. 11Then they brought out the king’s son, placed the crown on him, gave him the TestimonytestimonyTestimony, and proclaimed him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and cried, “Long live the king!”

Athaliah’s Downfall

12When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she went to the people in the house of the LORD. 13She looked and saw the king standing by his pillar at the entrance, with the officers and trumpeters beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets, while the singers, accompanied by musical instruments, led the praise. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and shouted, “Treason! Treason!”

14Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of hundreds who were in charge of the army and said to them, “Bring her out between the ranks, and put to the sword anyone who follows her.” For the priest had said, “She must not be put to death in the house of the LORD.” 15So they seized her, and when she reached the entrance of the Horse GategateGate of the royal palace, they put her to death there.

Covenant Renewal and the Restoration of Worship

16Then Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king that they would be the LORD’s people. 17So all the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and idols, and they killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. 18Moreover, Jehoiada entrusted the priests and the Levites whom David had assigned to the house of the LORD with the service of the house of the LORD, to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, as it is written in the LawlawLaw of Moses, with rejoicing and singing, according to David’s arrangement. 19He stationed gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD, so that nothing unclean might enter.

20He took with him the commanders of hundreds, the nobles, the rulers of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They entered the royal palace through the Upper GategateGate and seated the king on the royal throne. 21All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet, because Athaliah had been put to death by the sword.
