Moses Calls Israel to Obedience

1Now, O Israel, listen to the statutes and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments I am teaching you to perform, so that you may live and go in and possess the land that the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you. 2You must not add to the word I command you, nor take away from it, so that you may keep the commandments of the LORD your God, which I am commanding you.

3Your eyes have seen what the LORD did at Baal Peor, how the LORD your God destroyed from among you everyone who followed Baal Peor. 4But you who held fast to the LORD your God are alive today, every one of you.

5See, I have taught you statutes and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments, just as the LORD my God commanded me, so that you may follow them in the land you are entering to possess. 6Keep them and do them, for this will be your wisdom and understanding in the sight of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, “Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.”

7For what great nation has a god so near to it as the LORD our God is to us whenever we call on HimhimHim? 8And what great nation has statutes and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments as righteous as all this law that I am setting before you today?

9Only be very careful and watch yourself closely, lest you forget the things your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart all the days of your life. Teach them to your sons and to your grandsons. 10On the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD said to me, “Assemble the people before MemeMe, that I may let them hear MymyMy words, so that they may learn to fear MemeMe all the days they live on the earth, and so that they may teach them to their children.”

11You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain, while the mountain burned with fire to the heart of Heavenheavenheaven, with darkness, cloud, and thick darkness. 12Then the LORD spoke to you out of the fire. You heard the sound of words but saw no form; there was only a voice. 13He declared to you HishisHis covenant, which HeheHe commanded you to follow—the TentenTen CommandmentscommandmentsCommandments—and HeheHe wrote them on two tablets of stone. 14At that time the LORD commanded me to teach you the statutes and ordinances, so that you may follow them in the land you are crossing over to possess.

15So since you saw no form of any kind on the day the LORD spoke to you out of the fire at Horeb, be very careful to guard your lives. 16That you do not act corruptly and make for yourselves an idol in the form of any figure—male or female— 17the form of any beast on the earth, the form of any winged bird that flies in the sky, 18the form of anything that creeps on the ground, or the form of any fish in the waters below the earth.

19And when you look to the heavens and see the sun and moon and stars—all the host of Heavenheavenheaven—do not be led astray to bow down to them and serve them. The LORD your God has allotted them to all the nations under Heavenheavenheaven. 20But the LORD has taken you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be a people for HishisHis inheritance, as you are this day.

21Now the LORD was angry with me on account of your words, and HeheHe swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance. 22For I am going to die in this land; I will not cross the Jordan. But you are going to cross over and possess that good land. 23Be careful not to forget the covenant of the LORD your God that HeheHe made with you, and do not make for yourselves an idol in the form of anything the LORD your God has forbidden you. 24For the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.

Warning Against Idolatry

25When you have children and grandchildren and have grown old in the land, if you act corruptly and make an idol in the form of anything, and do evil in the sight of the LORD your God, provoking HimhimHim to anger, 26I call Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth as witnesses against you today that you will quickly perish from the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess. You will not live there long, but will be utterly destroyed. 27Then the LORD will scatter you among the peoples, and you will be left few in number among the nations where the LORD will drive you. 28There you will worship man-made gods of wood and stone, which cannot see or hear or eat or smell.

29But from there you will seek the LORD your God, and you will find HimhimHim if you seek HimhimHim with all your heart and with all your soul. 30When you are in distress and all these things have happened to you, then in the latter days you will return to the LORD your God and obey HishisHis voice. 31For the LORD your God is a merciful God; HeheHe will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the covenant with your fathers, which HeheHe swore to them.

The LORD Alone Is God

32For ask now about the former days that were before you, since the day God created man on the earth, and ask from one end of Heavenheavenheaven to the other whether anything as great as this has ever happened or been heard of. 33Has any people ever heard the voice of God speaking out of the fire, as you have heard, and survived? 34Or has any god tried to take for himself a nation out of another nation, by trials, signs, wonders, war, a mighty hand, an outstretched arm, and great terrors, as the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

35You were shown these things so that you might know that the LORD is God; there is no other besides HimhimHim. 36He let you hear HishisHis voice from Heavenheavenheaven to discipline you, and on EarthEarthearth HeheHe showed you HishisHis great fire, and you heard HishisHis words out of the fire. 37Because HeheHe loved your fathers and chose their descendants after them, HeheHe brought you out of Egypt by HishisHis great power, 38to drive out before you nations greater and stronger than you, to bring you into their land and give it to you as an inheritance, as it is today. 39Know therefore today, and take it to heart, that the LORD is God in Heavenheavenheaven above and on EarthEarthearth below; there is no other. 40Keep HishisHis statutes and commandments, which I am giving you today, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, and that you may live long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you for all time.

Cities of Refuge and the Law's Setting
