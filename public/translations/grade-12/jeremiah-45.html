The Word Given to Baruch

1The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a book at Jeremiah's dictation, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

2"This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says concerning you, Baruch: 3'You said, "Woe to me, for the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am exhausted with groaning and have found no rest."'

The LORD's Decree for the Land

4"Thus you shall say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Behold, what I have built I am breaking down, and what I have planted I am uprooting—that is, the whole land.

5And are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them, for I am bringing disaster on all flesh, declares the LORD. But I will give you your life as plunder wherever you go.'"
