Sexual Immorality in the Church

1It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and of a kind that does not occur even among pagans: A man has his father’s wife. 2And you are arrogant! Shouldn’t you rather have gone into mourning and have put out of your fellowship the man who has been doing this?

3For my part, even though I am absent in body, I am present with you in spirit; and I have already passed judgmentjudgementjudgment on the one who did this, just as if I were present. 4When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus and I am with you in spirit, together with the power of our Lord Jesus, 5hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved on the day of the Lord.

Purge the Old Leaven

6Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough? 7Cleanse out the old yeast, so that you may be a new, unleavened batch—as you truly are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed. 8Therefore let us keep the Festival, not with old yeast or with yeast of malice and wickedness, but with unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

Judging Within the Church

9I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people— 10not at all meaning the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would have to leave the world. 11But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who claims to be a brother if he is sexually immoral or greedy, an idolater, a slanderer, a drunkard, or a swindler—not even to eat with such a person.

12For what have I to do with judging outsiders? Is it not those inside the church whom you are to judge? 13God judges those outside. “Expel the wicked person from among you.”
