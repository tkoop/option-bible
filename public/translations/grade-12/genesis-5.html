The Book of Adam’s Lineage

1This is the book of the genealogy of Adam. On the day that God created humankind, HeheHe fashioned him in HishisHis own likeness. 2Male and female HeheHe created them, and HeheHe blessed them; and on the day of their creation, HeheHe designated them by the name Adam. 3When Adam had lived one hundred thirty years, he fathered a son in his own likeness, according to his image, and he named him Seth. 4After he had fathered Seth, Adam lived eight hundred years and fathered sons and daughters. 5Thus all the days of Adam were nine hundred thirty years, and he died.

6When Seth had lived one hundred five years, he fathered Enosh. 7After he had fathered Enosh, Seth lived eight hundred seven years and fathered sons and daughters. 8Thus all the days of Seth were nine hundred twelve years, and he died. 9When Enosh had lived ninety years, he fathered Kenan. 10After he had fathered Kenan, Enosh lived eight hundred fifteen years and fathered sons and daughters. 11Thus all the days of Enosh were nine hundred five years, and he died.

The Antediluvian Patriarchs

12When Kenan had lived seventy years, he fathered Mahalalel. 13After he had fathered Mahalalel, Kenan lived eight hundred forty years and fathered sons and daughters. 14Thus all the days of Kenan were nine hundred ten years, and he died. 15When Mahalalel had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Jared. 16After he had fathered Jared, Mahalalel lived eight hundred thirty years and fathered sons and daughters. 17Thus all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred ninety-five years, and he died.

Enoch’s Translation and the Final Generations

18When Jared had lived one hundred sixty-two years, he fathered Enoch. 19After he had fathered Enoch, Jared lived eight hundred years and fathered sons and daughters. 20Thus all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty-two years, and he died. 21When Enoch had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Methuselah. 22After he had fathered Methuselah, Enoch walked with God three hundred years and fathered sons and daughters. 23Thus all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty-five years. 24Enoch walked with God, and he was no more, because God had taken him away.

25When Methuselah had lived one hundred eighty-seven years, he fathered Lamech. 26After he had fathered Lamech, Methuselah lived seven hundred eighty-two years and fathered sons and daughters. 27Thus all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty-nine years, and he died.

Lamech’s Prophecy and Noah’s Birth

28When Lamech had lived one hundred eighty-two years, he fathered a son. 29He named him Noah, saying, "This one will comfort us in the labourlabourlabor and painful toil of our hands, toil that arises from the ground that the the LORD has cursed." 30After he had fathered Noah, Lamech lived five hundred ninety-five years and fathered sons and daughters. 31Thus all the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy-seven years, and he died. 32When Noah had lived five hundred years, he fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
