Persecution and the Scattering of the Church

1Saul consented to Stephen’s execution. On that very day a severe persecution broke out against the church in Jerusalem, and everyone except the apostles was scattered throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria.

2Devout men took charge of Stephen’s burial and lamented him with profound grief.

3Saul, meanwhile, was devastating the church. He entered house after house, dragged off both men and women, and consigned them to prison.

4Those who had been dispersed proclaimed the word as they travelledtravelledtraveled.

Philip’s Proclamation in Samaria

5Philip descended to the city of Samaria and proclaimed the Christ to them.

6The crowds attended unanimously to Philip’s message, for they heard his words and witnessed the signs he performed.

7Unclean spirits shrieked as they departed from many of the possessed, while numerous paralytics and lame persons were healed.

8Consequently, that city was filled with great joy.

9A certain man named Simon, who had long practisedpractisedpracticed sorcery in the city, astounded the Samaritans and represented himself as a person of extraordinary stature.

10Everyone, from the least to the greatest, heeded him, declaring, “This man is the Power of God, the one called GreatgreatGreat.” 11They had followed him for a considerable time because his sorcery had captivated them.

12Yet when they believed Philip’s proclamation concerning the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, both men and women were baptized.

13Simon himself also believed and was baptized; thereafter he remained with Philip, astonished by the great signs and miracles he witnessed.

Peter and John Confirm the Samaritan Believers

14When the apostles in Jerusalem learned that Samaria had embraced the word of God, they dispatched Peter and John to them.

15The two descended and prayed that the believers might receive the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 16for it had not yet descended upon any of them; they had merely been baptized into the name of the Lord Jesus.

17Then Peter and John laid their hands on them, and they received the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit.

18Observing that the SpiritspiritSpirit was conferred through the apostles’ laying on of hands, Simon offered them money 19and said, “Grant me this authority too, so that everyone on whom I lay my hands may receive the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit.”

20Peter said to him, “May your money perish along with you, because you supposed the gift of God could be purchased with money! 21You have neither portion nor inheritance in this matter, for your heart is not upright before God. 22Repent, therefore, of this wickedness, and implore the Lord that, if possible, the design of your heart may be forgiven. 23For I perceive that you are consumed by bitter resentment and enslaved to wickedness.”

24Simon answered, “You pray to the Lord for me, that none of what you have spoken may befall me.”

25After bearing witness and speaking the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, proclaiming the good news in many Samaritan villages.

Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

26An angel of the Lord told Philip, “Rise and travel southward along the road that descends from Jerusalem to Gaza”—a road through the desert.

27Philip therefore arose and went. He encountered an Ethiopian eunuch, a high official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who administered her entire treasury and had come to Jerusalem to worship.

28He was returning, seated in his chariot, and reading the prophet Isaiah.

29The SpiritspiritSpirit said to Philip, “Approach and join that chariot.”

30Philip hurried to him and heard him reading the prophet Isaiah. He asked, “Do you comprehend what you are reading?”

31The eunuch replied, “How could I possibly understand it unless someone interprets it for me?” And he urged Philip to mount and sit beside him.

38He commanded the chariot to halt, and both Philip and the eunuch descended into the water, where Philip baptized him.

39When they emerged from the water, the SpiritspiritSpirit of the Lord carried Philip away, and the eunuch saw him no more. He continued on his journey rejoicing.
