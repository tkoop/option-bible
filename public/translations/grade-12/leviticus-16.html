The LORD's Instructions After Aaron's Sons Died

1The LORD spoke to Moses after Aaron's two sons died because they approached the LORD. 2The LORD said to Moses, "Tell your brother Aaron that he must not enter the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace behind the veil at any time, in front of the mercy seat that is on the ark, or he will die; for I will appear in the cloud over the mercy seat."

Preparing the Offerings and Lots

3Aaron shall enter the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace with a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering. 4He must bathe his body with water and then put on the holy linen tunic, the linen undergarments, the linen sash, and the linen turban; these are holy garments. 5From the congregation of the Israelites he shall take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

6Aaron shall present the bull of the sin offering, which is for himself, and make atonement for himself and for his household. 7He shall take the two goats and present them before the LORD at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 8Aaron shall cast lots for the two goats—one lot for the LORD and the other for Azazel. 9Aaron shall present the goat on which the lot fell for the LORD and make it a sin offering. 10But the goat on which the lot fell for Azazel shall be presented alive before the LORD, to make atonement for it by sending it away into the wilderness as a scapegoat.

Atonement for the Priest, the Sanctuary, and the Altar

11Aaron shall present the bull of the sin offering that is for himself and make atonement for himself and his household; then he shall slaughter the bull. 12He shall take a censer full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD, and two handfuls of finely ground sweet incense, and bring them inside the veil. 13He shall put the incense on the fire before the LORD, so that the cloud of incense covers the mercy seat over the testimony, and he will not die. 14He shall take some of the blood of the bull and sprinkle it with his finger on the east side of the mercy seat; he shall sprinkle the blood with his finger seven times in front of the mercy seat.

15Then he shall slaughter the goat of the sin offering that is for the people and bring its blood inside the veil. He shall do with its blood as he did with the blood of the bull: he shall sprinkle it on the mercy seat and in front of the mercy seat. 16In this way he shall make atonement for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace because of the uncleannesses of the Israelites and because of their transgressions, all their sins. He shall do the same for the tent of meeting, which dwells among them in the midst of their uncleannesses. 17No person shall be in the tent of meeting when he enters to make atonement in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace until he comes out. He shall make atonement for himself, for his household, and for the whole assembly of Israel.

18Then he shall go out to the altar that is before the LORD and make atonement for it. He shall take some of the blood of the bull and some of the blood of the goat and put it on the horns of the altar all around. 19He shall sprinkle some of the blood on it with his finger seven times, and so cleanse it and consecrate it from the uncleannesses of the Israelites. 20When he has finished making atonement for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, the tent of meeting, and the altar, he shall present the live goat.

The Scapegoat, Final Offerings, and Permanent Statute

21Aaron shall lay both his hands on the head of the live goat and confess over it all the iniquities of the Israelites, all their transgressions, and all their sins. He shall put them on the head of the goat and send it away into the wilderness by the hand of a man appointed for the task. 22The goat shall carry all their iniquities to a remote, uninhabited land, and the man shall release the goat in the wilderness.

23Then Aaron shall come into the tent of meeting, remove the linen garments that he put on when he entered the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, and leave them there. 24He shall bathe his body with water in a holy place, put on his own garments, and come out. Then he shall offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for the people. 25He shall also burn the fat of the sin offering on the altar.

26The man who released the goat for Azazel shall wash his clothes and bathe his body with water; after that he may return to the camp. 27The bull of the sin offering and the goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought into the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace to make atonement, shall be carried outside the camp. Their hides, flesh, and dung shall be burned with fire. 28The one who burns them shall wash his clothes and bathe his body with water; after that he may return to the camp.

29This shall be a permanent statute for you: on the tenth day of the seventh month, you shall deny yourselves and do no work—neither the native nor the foreigner who lives among you. 30For on this day atonement shall be made for you, to cleanse you; you shall be clean from all your sins before the LORD. 31It is a Sabbath of solemn rest for you, and you shall deny yourselves; it is a permanent statute.

32The priest who is anointed and consecrated to serve as priest in his father's place shall make atonement. He shall put on the holy linen garments. 33He shall make atonement for the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, for the tent of meeting and the altar, and for the priests and all the people of the assembly. 34This shall be a permanent statute for you, to make atonement for the Israelites once a year for all their sins. And Aaron did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
