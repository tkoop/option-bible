The Vision and Impending JudgmentJudgementJudgment of Edom

1The vision of Obadiah: This is what the Lord, the LORD, says concerning Edom: We have heard a message from the LORD, and an envoy has been sent among the nations to say, 'Rise up, and let us go to war against her!'

Edom's Pride and Its Coming Humiliation

2"Look, I will make you small among the nations; you will be utterly despised. 3The pride of your heart has deceived you, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, whose home is on high, who say in your heart, 'Who can bring me down to the ground?' 4Even if you soar as high as an eagle and set your nest among the stars, from there I will bring you down," declares the LORD.

5If thieves had come to you, or robbers in the night—how devastated you would be!—would they not steal only as much as they needed? If grape pickers had come to you, would they not leave some gleanings? 6How thoroughly Esau has been plundered; his hidden treasures have been uncovered!

7All your allies have driven you to the border; your trusted friends have deceived and overpowered you; those who shared your bread have set a trap beneath you, and you have no discernment.

8"On that day," declares the LORD, "will I not destroy the wise from Edom and understanding from Mount Esau?" 9Your mighty men, O Teman, will be terrified, so that everyone on Mount Esau will be cut off in the slaughter.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment for Violence Against Jacob

10Because of the violence you did to your brother Jacob, shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever. 11On the day you stood by and did nothing, when strangers carried off his wealth and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots for Jerusalem, you were like one of them. 12You should not have gloated over your brother in his day of calamity, nor rejoiced over the people of Judah in the day of their ruin, nor boasted on the day of their distress. 13You should not have entered the gate of MymyMy people in the day of their disaster; you should not have gloated at their misfortune in the day of their disaster; and you should not have seized their wealth in the day of their disaster. 14You should not have stood at the crossroads to cut down their fugitives, nor should you have handed over their survivors in the day of distress.

15For the day of the LORD is near for all nations. As you have done, so it will be done to you; your recompense will return upon your own head. 16For just as you drank on MymyMy holy mountain, so all the nations will drink continually; they will drink and swallow, and they will be as though they never existed.

Israel's Restoration and the LORD's Sovereignty

17But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance, and it will be holy, and the house of Jacob will possess its inheritance. 18The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble; they will set them ablaze and devour them, and no survivor will remain from the house of Esau, for the LORD has spoken.

19Those of the Negev will possess the mountain of Esau, and those of the lowlands the land of the Philistines; they will possess the fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria, and Benjamin will possess Gilead. 20The exiles of this host of the children of Israel who are among the Canaanites as far as Zarephath, and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad, will possess the cities of the Negev. 21Deliverers will ascend Mount Zion to judge the mountain of Esau, and the kingdom will be the LORD's.
