An Urgent Prayer for Deliverance

1Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, the LORD. 2Let those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; let those who delight in my calamity be turned back and disgraced.

3Let those who cry, "Aha! Aha!" recoil in disgrace. 4Let all who seek YouyouYou rejoice and be glad in YouyouYou; let those who love YouryourYour salvation say continually, "May God be exalted!"

5Yet I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; the LORD, do not delay.
