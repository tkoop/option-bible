A Psalm of David: Thanksgiving for the the LORD's Mercy

1Bless the the LORD, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless HishisHis holy name. 2Bless the the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all HishisHis benefits: 3He forgives all your iniquities and heals all your diseases; 4HeheHe redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with steadfast love and tender mercy; 5HeheHe satisfies you with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's.

6The ThetheThe LORD executes righteousness and justice for all who are oppressed. 7He made known HishisHis ways to Moses and HishisHis deeds to the children of Israel. 8The ThetheThe LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. 9He will not always accuse, nor will HeheHe harbourharbourharbor HishisHis anger forever. 10He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our iniquities.

11For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is HishisHis steadfast love toward those who fear HimhimHim. 12As far as the east is from the west, so far has HeheHe removed our transgressions from us. 13As a father has compassion on his children, so the the LORD has compassion on those who fear HimhimHim. 14For HeheHe knows how we are formed; HeheHe remembers that we are dust.

15As for man, his days are like grass; he flourishes like a flower of the field. 16When the wind passes over it, it is gone, and its place remembers it no more. 17But the steadfast love of the the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon those who fear HimhimHim, and HishisHis righteousness to their children's children, 18to those who keep HishisHis covenant and remember to do HishisHis commandments.

19The ThetheThe LORD has established HishisHis throne in Heavenheavenheaven, and HishisHis kingdom rules over all. 20Bless the the LORD, you HishisHis angels, mighty in strength, who do HishisHis word, obeying the voice of HishisHis word. 21Bless the the LORD, all you HishisHis hosts, HishisHis servants who do HishisHis will. 22Bless the the LORD, all HishisHis works, in all places of HishisHis dominion. Bless the the LORD, O my soul.
