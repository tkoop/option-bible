The Second Banquet

1So the king and Haman came to drink with Queen Esther. 2On the second day, at the wine banquet, the king said to Queen Esther, "What is your petition, Queen Esther? It will be granted to you. And what is your request? Even to half the kingdom, it will be done."

3Queen Esther answered, "If I have found favourfavourfavor with you, O king, and if it pleases the king, let my life be granted to me as my petition, and my people as my request. 4For I and my people have been sold to be destroyed, killed, and annihilated. If we had merely been sold as slaves and maidservants, I would have kept silent, for no enemy could compensate for the king's loss."

Esther Names the Enemy

5King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther, "Who is he, and where is he, who has dared to do this?"

6Esther said, "ThetheThe adversary and enemy is this wicked Haman!" Then Haman was terrified before the king and the queen.

The King's Fury

7In his wrath the king rose from the wine banquet and went into the palace garden, while Haman stood up to beg Queen Esther for his life, for he realizedrealisedrealized that the king had decreed his ruin.

8When the king returned from the palace garden to the banquet hall, Haman was falling on the couch where Esther lay. The king said, "WillwillWill he even assault the queen in my own house?" As soon as the word left the king's mouth, they covered Haman's face.

Haman's Execution

9Then Harbonah, one of the eunuchs before the king, said, "Look, the gallows that Haman made for Mordecai, who spoke in the king's favourfavourfavor, stands in Haman's house, fifty cubits high."

The king said, "Hang him on it!"

10So they hanged Haman on the gallows he had prepared for Mordecai, and the king's wrath subsided.
