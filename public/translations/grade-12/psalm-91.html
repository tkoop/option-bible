Psalm 91: Refuge in the Almighty

1He who dwells in the sanctuary of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh shall abide under the shadow of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 2I will say to the LORD, “He is my refuge and my stronghold, my God, in whom I place my trust.”

3For HeheHe will surely rescue you from the fowler's snare and from the deadly pestilence. 4He will cover you with HishisHis feathers, and beneath HishisHis wings you will find refuge; HishisHis faithfulness is your shield and rampart. 5You will not dread the terror of night, nor the arrow that flies by day, 6nor the plague that stalks in darkness, nor the devastation that lays waste at midday. 7Though a thousand fall at your side and ten thousand at your right hand, it shall not approach you. 8You will only observe it with your eyes and behold the recompense of the wicked.

9Because you have made the LORD your dwelling—your refuge, the MostmostMost HighhighHigh— 10no evil shall befall you, nor shall any plague draw near your tent. 11For HeheHe will command HishisHis angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways. 12They will bear you up in their hands, lest you strike your foot against a stone. 13You will tread upon the lion and the cobra; YouyouYou will trample the young lion and the serpent.

14“Because he loves MemeMe, I will deliver him; because he knows MymyMy name, I will protect him. 15When he calls upon MemeMe, I will answer him; I will be with him in distress; I will rescue him and honourhonourhonor him. 16With long life I will satisfy him and show him MymyMy salvation.”
