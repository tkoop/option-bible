No Condemnation in Christ

1Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. 2For in Christ Jesus the law of the SpiritspiritSpirit of life has set you free from the law of sin and death. 3For what the law was powerless to do, because it was weakened by the flesh, God did by sending HishisHis own SonsonSon in the likeness of sinful flesh, as an offering for sin; HeheHe condemned sin in the flesh. 4So that the righteous requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us, who do not live according to the flesh but according to the SpiritspiritSpirit.

Life According to the Spirit

5For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the SpiritspiritSpirit set their minds on the things of the SpiritspiritSpirit. 6For the mindset of the flesh is death, but the mindset of the SpiritspiritSpirit is life and peace. 7For the mindset of the flesh is hostile to God, because it does not submit to God's law; indeed, it cannot. 8Those who are in the flesh cannot please God.

9You, however, are not in the flesh but in the SpiritspiritSpirit, if indeed the SpiritspiritSpirit of God dwells in you. And if anyone does not have the SpiritspiritSpirit of Christ, he does not belong to Christ. 10But if Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, yet the SpiritspiritSpirit is life because of righteousness. 11And if the SpiritspiritSpirit of HimhimHim who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, HeheHe who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit who dwells in you.

Adoption as God's Children

12So then, brothers and sisters, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh. 13For if you live according to the flesh, you will die; but if by the SpiritspiritSpirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. 14For all who are led by the SpiritspiritSpirit of God are sons of God. 15For you did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you received the SpiritspiritSpirit of adoption, by whom we cry out, "Abba, FatherfatherFather!" 16The SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God. 17And if we are children, then we are heirs—heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ, provided we suffer with HimhimHim in order that we may also be glorified with HimhimHim.

Hope and the Spirit's Intercession

18For I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is to be revealed to us. 19For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the sons of God. 20For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but because of HimhimHim who subjected it, in hope 21that the creation itself will be set free from its bondage to decay and obtain the freedom of the glory of the children of God. 22For we know that the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until now. 23And not only that, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the SpiritspiritSpirit, groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for adoption as sons, the redemption of our bodies.

24For in this hope we were saved. Now hope that is seen is not hope, for who hopes for what he sees? 25But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with patience. 26Likewise the SpiritspiritSpirit helps us in our weakness, for we do not know what to pray for as we ought, but the SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words. 27And HeheHe who searches the hearts knows what the mindset of the SpiritspiritSpirit is, because HeheHe intercedes for the saints according to the will of God.

God's Purpose and Unshakable Love

28And we know that God works all things together for good for those who love HimhimHim, who are called according to HishisHis purpose. 29For those whom HeheHe foreknew HeheHe also predestined to be conformed to the image of HishisHis SonsonSon, so that HeheHe might be the firstborn among many brothers. 30And those whom HeheHe predestined HeheHe also called, and those whom HeheHe called HeheHe also justified, and those whom HeheHe justified HeheHe also glorified.

31What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us? 32He who did not spare HishisHis own SonsonSon but gave HimhimHim up for us all, how will HeheHe not also, along with HimhimHim, graciously give us all things? 33Who will bring any charge against God's elect? It is God who justifies. 34Who is to condemn? Christ Jesus is the OneoneOne who died—more than that, who was raised—and who is at the right hand of God, and who indeed intercedes for us.

35Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword? 36As it is written, "For YouryourYour sake we are being killed all the day long; we are regarded as sheep to be slaughtered." 37No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through HimhimHim who loved us. 38For I am convinced that neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers, 39nor height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
