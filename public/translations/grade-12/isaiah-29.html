Woe to Ariel and the Coming Siege

1Woe to Ariel, Ariel, the city where David camped! Year after year, let the festivals run their course. 2I will besiege Ariel, and she will mourn and lament; she will be to MemeMe like an altar hearth. 3I will encamp all around you, and besiege you with towers; I will raise siegeworks against you. 4You will be brought low, and you will speak from the earth; your words will be muffled in the dust. Your voice will be like a ghost rising from the ground, and your speech will whisper from the dust. 5But the multitude of your enemies will be like fine dust, and the horde of the ruthless like blowing chaff. Then suddenly, in an instant, 6you will be judged by the LORD of Hosts with thunder and earthquake and a great roar, with whirlwind and storm and a consuming fire. 7The multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel—all who war against her and her stronghold and distress her—will be like a dream, a vision in the night. 8It will be as when a hungry man dreams he is eating, but awakens to find his soul empty; or as when a thirsty man dreams he is drinking, but awakens faint, with his thirst unquenched. So will it be with all the nations that wage war against Mount Zion.

Spiritual Blindness and Hollow Worship

9Be stunned and amazed; blind yourselves and remain sightless! They are drunk, but not on wine; they stagger, but not from strong drink. 10For the LORD has poured out on you a spirit of deep sleep; HeheHe has shut your eyes—the prophets—and veiled your heads—the seers. 11So the entire vision is to you like the words of a sealed scroll. If it is handed to someone who can read, and he is asked to read it, he will say, "I cannot, because it is sealed." 12Or if the scroll is handed to one who cannot read, and he is asked to read it, he will say, "I cannot read."

13Therefore the Lord said: "Because this people draw near to MemeMe with their mouths and honourhonourhonor MemeMe with their lips, while their hearts are far from MemeMe, and their reverence for MemeMe is merely a commandment taught by men, 14I will again confound this people with wonder upon wonder. The wisdom of the wise will vanish, and the discernment of the discerning will be hidden."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Secret Schemes

15Woe to those who dig deep to hide their plans from the LORD! In darkness they do their work and say, "Who sees us? Who knows us?" 16You have turned everything upside down. Shall the potter be regarded as clay? Shall what is made say of its maker, "HeheHe did not make me"? Or shall what is formed say of HimhimHim who formed it, "HeheHe has no understanding"?

Restoration and Redemption

17In just a little while, will not Lebanon be turned into a fertile field, and the fertile field seem like a forest? 18In that day the deaf will hear the words of the scroll, and out of darkness and gloom the eyes of the blind will see. 19The humble will increase their joy in the LORD, and the poor among men will rejoice in the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 20For the ruthless will vanish, the mockers will disappear, and all who look for evil will be cut down: 21those who with a word make a person out to be a sinner, who ensnare the mediator at the gate, and who with false charges deprive the innocent of justice.

22Therefore this is what the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, says to the house of Jacob: "No longer will Jacob be ashamed; no longer will his face grow pale. 23For when he sees his children, the work of MymyMy hands, among him, they will honourhonourhonor MymyMy name; they will sanctify the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Jacob and stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24Those who stray in spirit will come to understanding, and those who complain will accept instruction."
