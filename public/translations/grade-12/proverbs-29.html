The Folly of Stubborn Defiance and the Stability of Righteous Rule

1A person who remains obstinately defiant after repeated rebukes will be shattered suddenly, beyond remedy. 2When the righteous flourish, the people rejoice, but when the wicked rule, the people groan. 3Whoever loves wisdom brings joy to his father, but one who consorts with prostitutes squanders his wealth. 4By justice a king gives stability to the land, but one who exacts tribute tears it down.

5A person who flatters his neighbourneighbourneighbor sets a trap for his feet. 6An evil person is caught in his own sin, while the righteous sing and rejoice. 7The righteous concern themselves with justice for the poor, but the wicked have no regard for such matters.

Wisdom, Folly, and the Moral Order of a City

8Mockers inflame a city, but the wise turn away anger. 9When a wise person contends with a fool, the fool rages and mocks, and there is no peace. 10Bloodthirsty men hate a person of integrity and seek to kill the upright. 11A fool gives free rein to his temper, but a wise person restrains it. 12If a ruler listens to lies, all his officials become wicked. 13The poor and the oppressor have this in common: the LORD gives light to the eyes of both. 14If a king judges the poor with truth, his throne will be established forever.

Discipline, Vision, and Trust in the LORD

15A rod of correction and reproof imparts wisdom, but a child left to himself disgraces his mother. 16When the wicked are in authority, transgression increases, but the righteous will see their downfall. 17Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will bring delight to your soul. 18Where there is no prophetic vision, the people cast off restraint, but blessed is the one who keeps the law. 19A servant is not corrected by words alone; though he understands, he will not respond. 20Do you see a person hasty in his words? There is more hope for a fool than for him. 21A servant pampered from youth will turn out to be insolent. 22An angry person stirs up strife, and a hot-tempered person abounds in transgression. 23A person’s pride will bring him low, but a humble spirit will obtain honourhonourhonor. 24Whoever partners with a thief hates his own life; though he hears the curse, he will not testify. 25The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe. 26Many seek a ruler’s favourfavourfavor, but justice for a person comes from the LORD. 27An unjust person is an abomination to the righteous, and one who is upright in the way is an abomination to the wicked.
