A Prayer for the Righteous King

1Give the king your judgmentsjudgementsjudgments, O God, and your righteousness to the king's son. 2He will judge your people in righteousness and your afflicted with justice. 3The mountains will bring peace to the people, and the hills will bring righteousness. 4He will vindicate the afflicted among the people; HeheHe will save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor. 5They will fear you while the sun endures, and as long as the moon shines, throughout all generations. 6He will come down like rain upon freshly mown grass, like showers that water the earth. 7In HishisHis days the righteous will flourish, and peace will abound until the moon is no more.

The King's Universal Dominion

8He will rule from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth. 9Desert nomads will bow before HimhimHim, and HishisHis enemies will be humbled to the dust. 10The kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands will bring tribute; the kings of Sheba and Seba will present gifts. 11All kings will bow down to HimhimHim, and all nations will serve HimhimHim.

Compassion for the Needy and National Prosperity

12For HeheHe will deliver the needy who cry out, and the afflicted who have no helper. 13He will have compassion on the poor and needy and preserve the lives of the needy. 14He will redeem their lives from oppression and violence, and their blood will be precious in HishisHis sight. 15Long may HeheHe live! May gold from Sheba be given to HimhimHim. May people pray for HimhimHim continually, and may they bless HimhimHim all day long. 16May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it wave upon the mountaintops. May its fruit be like the abundance of Lebanon, and may people flourish in the cities like grass upon the earth. 17May HishisHis name endure forever; may it increase as long as the sun shines. May all nations be blessed in HimhimHim, and may they call HimhimHim blessed.

Doxology and Conclusion

18Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonders. 19And blessed be HishisHis glorious name forever; may the whole EarthEarthearth be filled with HishisHis glory. Amen and AmenamenAmen. 20The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended.
