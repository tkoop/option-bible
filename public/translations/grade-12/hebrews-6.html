Pressing on to Maturity

1Therefore, leaving behind the elementary teaching about Christ, let us press on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God, 2of instruction concerning baptisms, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgmentjudgementjudgment. 3And this we will do, if God permits.

The Danger of Falling Away

4For it is impossible to restore again to repentance those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, who have become partakers of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, 5and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come, 6and then have fallen away, because they are crucifying the SonsonSon of God all over again and subjecting HimhimHim to public disgrace. 7For land that has drunk the rain that often falls upon it and produces vegetation useful to those for whom it is cultivated receives a blessing from God. 8But if it bears thorns and thistles, it is worthless and near to being cursed, and its end is to be burned.

Confidence in God's Faithfulness

9Though we speak in this way, beloved, we are convinced of better things concerning you—things that accompany salvation. 10For God is not unjust so as to forget your work and the love that you showed for HishisHis name in serving the saints, as you continue to do. 11And we desire each one of you to show the same earnestness in attaining the full assurance of hope to the end, 12so that you may not become sluggish, but may be imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

An Anchor for the Soul

13For when God made a promise to Abraham, since HeheHe had no one greater by whom to swear, HeheHe swore by HimselfhimselfHimself, 14saying, "I will surely bless you and greatly multiply you." 15And thus, having patiently endured, Abraham obtained the promise. 16For people swear by someone greater than themselves, and an oath is final for confirmation, ending every dispute. 17So when God desired to show more convincingly to the heirs of the promise the unchangeable character of HishisHis purpose, HeheHe confirmed it with an oath, 18so that by two unchangeable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have taken refuge may have strong encouragement to hold fast to the hope set before us. 19We have this as an anchor for the soul, sure and steadfast, which enters into the inner place behind the veil, 20where Jesus has entered as a forerunner on our behalf, having become a high priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.
