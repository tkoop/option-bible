The Covenant and Its Curse

1The word that came to Jeremiah from the the LORD, saying: 2“Hear the words of this covenant and speak them to the men of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 3Tell them that this is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Cursed is the man who does not obey the words of this covenant 4that I commanded your fathers when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, saying, “Obey MemeMe, and do everything I command you, and you will be MymyMy people, and I will be your God,” 5in order to fulfillfulfilfulfill the oath I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day.’”

Then I answered, “Amen, O the LORD.”

The Covenant Proclaimed

6Then the the LORD said to me, “Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem: ‘Hear the words of this covenant and carry them out.’ 7For from the time I brought your fathers out of the land of Egypt until this day, I have warned them earnestly and without ceasing, saying, ‘Obey MymyMy voice.’ 8Yet they refused to listen or give heed, but each walked in the stubbornness of his evil heart. So I brought upon them all the curses of this covenant, which I had commanded them to obey, but they did not keep them.”

Judah's Conspiracy and Idolatry

9Then the the LORD said to me, “A conspiracy has been found among the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem. 10They have returned to the iniquities of their fathers who refused to heed MymyMy words. They have pursued other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have violated the covenant I made with their fathers. 11Therefore this is what the the LORD says: ‘I am about to bring upon them a calamity from which they cannot escape. They will cry out to MemeMe, but I will not listen to them. 12Then the cities of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods to whom they have been burning incense, but these gods cannot save them in their time of disaster. 13Your gods are as numerous as your cities, O Judah, and the altars you have set up to shame—altars to burn incense to Baal—are as numerous as the streets of Jerusalem.’”

14“As for you, do not pray for these people. Do not raise a cry or a prayer on their behalf, for I will not listen when they call to MemeMe in their time of disaster. 15What right does MymyMy beloved have to be in MymyMy house, when she has done so many evil things? Can consecrated meat avert your punishment when you rejoice in your evil? 16The the LORD formerly called you a flourishing olive tree, beautiful with good fruit. But with the roar of a mighty storm HeheHe will set fire to it, and its branches will be consumed. 17The the LORD of Hosts, who planted you, has decreed disaster upon you, because of the evil that the house of Israel and the house of Judah have done, provoking MemeMe to anger by burning incense to Baal.”

18And the the LORD informed me, so I knew. Then HeheHe showed me their deeds. 19I was like a gentle lamb led to slaughter; I did not know that they had devised plans against me:

“Let us destroy the tree with its fruit; let us cut him off from the land of the living, that his name may be remembered no more.”

20O the LORD of Hosts, who judges righteously, who tests the heart and mind, let me see YouryourYour vengeance upon them, for to YouyouYou I have committed my cause.

21Therefore this is what the the LORD says concerning the men of Anathoth who are seeking your life, saying:

“You must not prophesy in the name of the the LORD, or you will die by our hand.”

22Therefore this is what the the LORD of Hosts says: “I will punish them. The young men will die by the sword; their sons and daughters will die by famine. 23Not even a remnant will be left, for I will bring disaster on the men of Anathoth in the year of their punishment.”
