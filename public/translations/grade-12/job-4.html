Eliphaz Answers Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: 2"If someone presumes to speak a word to you, will you grow weary? Yet who can keep from speaking? 3Surely you have instructed many, and you have strengthened feeble hands. 4Your words have upheld those who were stumbling, and you have made firm the knees that gave way. 5But now adversity has come to you, and you are weary; it touches you, and you are dismayed. 6Is not your reverence your confidence, and the integrity of your ways your hope?

The Wicked Reap What They Sow

7"Remember now: who is the innocent man who perished? Where were the upright ever cut off? 8As I have seen, those who cultivate iniquity and sow trouble reap what they have sown. 9By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of HishisHis anger they are consumed. 10The roar of the lion, the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken. 11The old lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

Eliphaz Recounts a Night Vision

12"Now a word was brought to me secretly, and my ear caught a whisper of it. 13In thoughts from night visions, when deep sleep falls on people, 14dread and trembling seized me and made all my bones shake. 15A spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood on end. 16It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance; a form stood before my eyes; there was silence, and then I heard a voice:

17"'Can mortal man be righteous before God? Can a man be pure before HishisHis MakermakerMaker? 18Behold, HeheHe puts no trust in HishisHis servants, and HeheHe charges HishisHis angels with error. 19How much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed as easily as a moth! 20Between morning and evening they are broken; they perish forever without anyone regarding. 21Are not their lives uprooted? They die, and not in wisdom.'"
