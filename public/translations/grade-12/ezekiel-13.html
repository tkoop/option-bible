The Word of the LORD Against False Prophets

1The word of the LORD came to me:

Woe to the Foolish Prophets

2“Son of ManmanMan, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are prophesying, and say to those who prophesy from their own hearts, ‘Hear the word of the LORD!’ 3Thus says the Lord GOD: Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing! 4Your prophets, O Israel, have been like foxes among ruins. 5You have not gone up into the breaches, nor built up a wall for the house of Israel, that it might stand in battle in the day of the LORD.

“6They have seen false visions and lying divinations. They say, ‘Declares the the LORD!’ when the the LORD has not sent them, yet they expect HimhimHim to fulfillfulfilfulfill their word. 7Have you not seen a false vision and uttered a lying divination whenever you have said, ‘Declares the the LORD!’ although I have not spoken?

“8Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Because you have uttered falsehood and seen lying visions, therefore behold, I am against you, declares the Lord GOD. 9My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and who give lying divinations. They shall not be in the council of MymyMy people, nor be enrolled in the register of the house of Israel, nor shall they enter the land of Israel. And you shall know that I am the Lord GOD.

The Whitewashed Wall and the Coming Storm

“10Precisely because they have misled MymyMy people, saying, ‘Peace,’ when there is no peace, and because, when the people build a wall, these prophets smear it with whitewash, 11say to those who smear it with whitewash that it shall fall! There shall be a deluge of rain, and you, O great hailstones, shall fall, and a stormy wind shall break out. 12And when the wall falls, will it not be said to you, ‘Where is the coating with which you smeared it?’

“13Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: I will make a stormy wind break out in MymyMy wrath, and there shall be a deluge of rain in MymyMy anger, and great hailstones in wrath to make a full end. 14And I will break down the wall that you have smeared with whitewash, and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundation will be laid bare. And it will fall, and you shall perish in the midst of it. And you shall know that I am the the LORD.

“15Thus I will spend MymyMy wrath upon the wall and upon those who smeared it with whitewash, and I will say to you, ‘The wall is no more, and those who smeared it are no more— 16the prophets of Israel who prophesied concerning Jerusalem and saw visions of peace for her, when there was no peace,’ declares the Lord GOD.”

“17And you, SonsonSon of ManmanMan, turn against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own hearts; prophesy against them 18and say, ‘Thus says the Lord GOD: Woe to the women who sew magic bands upon all wrists and make veils over the heads of persons of every stature, to hunt souls! Will you hunt down the souls of MymyMy people and keep your own souls alive? 19You have profaned MemeMe among MymyMy people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, putting to death souls who should not die and keeping alive souls who should not live, by your lying to MymyMy people, who listen to lies.’

“20Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am against your magic bands with which you hunt the souls like birds, and I will tear them from your arms, and I will let the souls go, the souls that you hunt like birds. 21I will also tear off your veils and deliver MymyMy people out of your hand, and they shall be no more in your hand as prey, and you shall know that I am the the LORD.

“22Because you have disheartened the righteous with falsehood, although I did not grieve them, and have strengthened the hands of the wicked, so that he does not turn from his evil way and live, 23therefore you shall no more see false visions nor practice divination. I will deliver MymyMy people from your hand. And you shall know that I am the the LORD.”
