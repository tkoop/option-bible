Ephraim Confronts Jephthah

1The men of Ephraim mustered their forces, crossed to the north, and challenged Jephthah: “Why did you march to fight the Ammonites without calling us to go with you? We will burn your house down over you.”

2Jephthah answered them, “My people and I were in a severe conflict with the Ammonites, and when I cried out to you, you did not rescue me from their power. 3When I saw that you would not save me, I risked my own life and crossed against the Ammonites, and the LORD delivered them into my hand. Why, then, have you come up against me today to make war on me?”

Jephthah Defeats Ephraim

4Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought against Ephraim. The Gileadites struck them down because the Ephraimites had taunted, “You are fugitives of Ephraim—Gileadites living among Ephraim and Manasseh.”

5The Gileadites seized the fords of the Jordan leading to Ephraim. Whenever an Ephraimite fugitive asked to cross, the Gileadites challenged him, “Are you an Ephraimite?”

6If he denied it, they told him, “Then say ‘Shibboleth.’”

When he could produce only “Sibboleth,” because he could not pronounce the word correctly, they seized him and slaughtered him at the fords. At that time forty-two thousand Ephraimites fell.

Jephthah’s Death and Later Judges

7Jephthah judged Israel for six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and was buried in the cities of Gilead.

8After him, Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel. 9He had thirty sons and thirty daughters. He sent his daughters away in marriage and brought in thirty young women from outside for his sons. He judged Israel for seven years. 10Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.

11After him, Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel for ten years. 12Then Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried at Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.

13After him, Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel. 14He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode on seventy donkeys. He judged Israel for eight years. 15Then Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried at Pirathon in the hill country of Ephraim, in the territory of the Amalekites.
