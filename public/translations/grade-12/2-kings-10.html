Jehu's Letters to the Rulers of Samaria

1Now Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. So Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria—to the rulers of the city, to the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab's sons—saying, 2“Now, then, when this letter reaches you, you have your master's sons with you, together with chariots, horses, fortified cities, and weapons. 3Choose the best and most capable of your master's sons, set him on his father's throne, and fight for your master's house.”

4But they were terrified and said, “Look—the two kings could not withstand him. How, then, can we stand?”

5So the palace administrator, the city governor, the elders, and the guardians sent word to Jehu: “We are your servants, and we will do whatever you say. We will not proclaim anyone king. Do whatever seems right in your eyes.”

6Then he wrote to them a second letter, saying, “If you are for me and will obey me, take the heads of your master's sons and bring them to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow.” Now the king's sons—seventy men—were with the leading men of the city, who were raising them. 7As soon as the letter reached them, they took the king's sons and slaughtered all seventy, placed their heads in baskets, and sent them to him at Jezreel.

8When the messenger arrived and reported, “They have brought the heads of the king's sons,”

Jehu said, “Lay them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until morning.”

9Then in the morning, when he went out, he stood before all the people and said, “You are innocent. It was I who conspired against my master and killed him, but who struck down all these? 10Know, then, that not one word of the LORD concerning the house of Ahab will fail to come to pass, for the LORD has accomplished what HeheHe spoke through HishisHis servant Elijah.”

The Purge of Ahab's House and Its Allies

11So Jehu struck down everyone left of Ahab's house in Jezreel—his chief men, his close friends, and his priests—until no survivor remained.

12Then he set out for Samaria. While he was on the way, at Beth-eked of the ShepherdsshepherdsShepherds, 13Jehu met the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah and asked, “Who are you?”

They replied, “We are the brothers of Ahaziah, and we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother.”

14He ordered, “Seize them alive.” So they seized them alive and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked—forty-two men—and spared none of them.

Jehu's Zeal for the LORD

15When he left there, he met Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. He greeted him and asked, “Is your heart as true to mine as mine is to yours?”

Jehonadab answered, “It is.”

Jehu said, “If so, give me your hand.” So he gave him his hand, and Jehu drew him up into the chariot beside him.

16And he said, “Come with me and witness my zeal for the LORD.” So he had him ride in his chariot.

17When he reached Samaria, he struck down everyone remaining to Ahab in Samaria until he had destroyed them, according to the word of the LORD that HeheHe had spoken to Elijah.

The Destruction of Baal Worship

18Then Jehu assembled all the people and said to them, “Ahab served Baal only a little, but Jehu will serve him much more. 19Now summon to me all the prophets of Baal, all his servants, and all his priests; let no one be absent, for I intend to offer a great sacrifice to Baal. Anyone who is absent shall not live.” But Jehu acted deceitfully, intending to destroy the worshipers of Baal.

20Jehu ordered, “Consecrate a solemn assembly for Baal.” So they proclaimed it.

21Jehu sent word throughout all Israel, and all the worshipers of Baal came, so that not a man was left who did not come. They entered the house of Baal, and it was filled from end to end.

22He said to the keeper of the wardrobe, “Bring out robes for all the worshipers of Baal.” So he brought out robes for them.

23Then Jehu went into the house of Baal with Jehonadab son of Rechab and said to the worshipers of Baal, “Search carefully and make sure no servant of the LORD is here among you—only worshipers of Baal.”

24Then they went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Meanwhile, Jehu had posted eighty men outside and commanded, “If any man lets one of those I am bringing into your hands escape, his life shall be forfeited for the life of the one who escapes.”

25As soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu said to the guards and officers, “Go in and kill them; let no one escape.” So they put them to the sword, and the guards and officers threw the bodies out and went into the inner chamber of the house of Baal.

26They brought out the sacred pillar from the house of Baal and burned it. 27They tore down the pillar of Baal, demolished the temple of Baal, and made it a latrine, which it remains to this day. 28Thus Jehu eradicated Baal worship from Israel.

29But Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit—the golden calves at Bethel and Dan.

30And the LORD said to Jehu, “Because you have done well in carrying out what is right in MymyMy eyes and have dealt with the house of Ahab according to all that was in MymyMy heart, your sons will sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation.”

31But Jehu was not careful to walk in the law of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart; he did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, by which he had made Israel sin.

32In those days the LORD began to reduce the territory of Israel. Hazael defeated them throughout all Israel's borders: 33from the Jordan eastward—all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the Manassites—from Aroer by the Arnon Gorge through Gilead and Bashan.

34Now the rest of the acts of Jehu, all that he did, and all his might—are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

35So Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria, and Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

36The time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.
