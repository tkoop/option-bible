The Ark and the Atonement Cover

1Bezalel fashioned the ark from acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high. 2He overlaid it with pure gold, both inside and outside, and he fashioned a gold molding around it. 3He cast four gold rings for it—two on one side and two on the other. 4He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. 5Then he inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark so that it could be carried.

6He fashioned the atonement cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 7He made two cherubim of hammered gold at the ends of the atonement cover. 8One cherub stood at one end and the other at the opposite end, all hammered from the same piece as the atonement cover. 9The cherubim spread their wings upward, overshadowing the atonement cover; their faces were turned toward each other and toward the atonement cover.

The Table for the Bread of the Presence

10He made the table of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high. 11He overlaid it with pure gold and fashioned a gold molding around it. 12He made a rim around it a handbreadth wide and put a gold molding on the rim. 13He cast four gold rings for it and attached them to the four corners at its four legs. 14The rings were placed close to the rim to serve as holders for the poles used to carry the table. 15He made the poles of acacia wood for carrying the table and overlaid them with gold. 16He also made the utensils for the table—its plates, dishes, bowls, and pitchers for pouring drink offerings—out of pure gold.

The Golden Lampstand

17Then he made the lampstand of pure gold, hammered out of one piece. Its base, shaft, branches, bowls, buds, and petals were all one piece. 18Six branches extended from its sides, three on one side and three on the other. 19Three cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals, were on one branch, three on the next, and so on for the six branches extending from the lampstand. 20On the lampstand itself were four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with buds and petals. 21A bud was under the first pair of branches extending from the lampstand, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair. 22The buds and branches were all one piece with the lampstand, hammered out of pure gold. 23He also made its seven lamps, wick trimmers, and trays of pure gold. 24He made the lampstand and all its utensils from a talent of pure gold.

The Altar of Incense and Sacred Preparations

25He made the altar of incense out of acacia wood. It was square, a cubit long and a cubit wide, and two cubits high, and its horns were of one piece with it. 26He overlaid it with pure gold—its top, all around its sides, and its horns—and he made a gold molding around it. 27He made two gold rings beneath the molding on opposite sides to hold the poles for carrying it. 28He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. 29He also made the holy anointing oil and the pure, fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.
