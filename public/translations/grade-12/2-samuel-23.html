David's Final Oracle

1These constitute David's final words: the oracle of David son of Jesse, the utterance of the man exalted on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob, Israel's sweet psalmist.

2"The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD spoke through me, and HishisHis word rested upon my tongue. 3The God of Israel said, the RockrockRock of Israel spoke to me: 'When one rules justly over men, ruling in the fear of God, he is like the light of morning at sunrise on a cloudless day, like the brightness after rain that brings forth grass from the earth.' 4IsisIs not my house right with God? For HeheHe has established with me an everlasting covenant, ordered and secured in every particular. 5Will HeheHe not bring to fruition my salvation and every desire? 6But the worthless are all like thorns raked aside, for they cannot be gathered by hand. 7Whoever touches them must be armed with iron and the shaft of a spear; they are utterly consumed in fire."

David's Mighty Men

8These are the names of David's mighty men: Josheb-basshebeth the Tahkemonite was chief of the ThreethreeThree. He wielded his spear against eight hundred men and slew them in a single encounter. 9Next to him stood Eleazar son of Dodai the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men. He remained with David when the Israelites taunted the Philistines who had gathered for battle and the rest of the army withdrew. 10Yet Eleazar held his ground and struck down the Philistines until his hand grew weary and clung to his sword. 11The LORD brought about a great victory that day, and the troops returned to him, though only to strip the slain. After him came Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines had gathered into a troop where there was a field full of lentils, and the people fled from them. 12But Shammah took his stand in the midst of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. So the LORD brought about a great victory.

13At harvest time, three of the thirty chief warriors went down to David at the cave of Adullam, while a Philistine force was encamped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 14David was then in the stronghold, and the Philistine garrison held Bethlehem. 15David longed for water and said, "I wish someone would bring me a drink from the well by the gate of Bethlehem." 16So the three mighty men broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well by the gate of Bethlehem, and carried it to David. But he refused to drink it; instead, he poured it out as an offering to the LORD. 17He said, "I could never do such a thing before the LORD! Is it not the blood of the men who risked their lives?" So he refused to drink it. Such were the exploits of the three mighty men.

18Now Abishai, the brother of Joab son of Zeruiah, was chief of the ThreethreeThree. He wielded his spear against three hundred men, killed them, and won a name among the ThreethreeThree. 19Was he not more honouredhonouredhonored than the ThreethreeThree? He became their commander, even though he was not reckoned among the ThreethreeThree. 20Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a valiant warrior from Kabzeel, a man of many exploits. He struck down two champions of Moab, and on a snowy day he went down into a pit and killed a lion. 21He also struck down an Egyptian, a man of imposing stature. Although the Egyptian wielded a spear, Benaiah went against him with a staff, snatched the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear. 22These were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoiada, who won a name among the three mighty men. 23He was more honouredhonouredhonored than the ThirtythirtyThirty, but he did not attain to the ThreethreeThree. David appointed him over his bodyguard.
