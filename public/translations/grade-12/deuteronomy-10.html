Renewing the Covenant Tablets

1At that time, the LORD said to me, “Carve out two stone tablets for yourself, like the first ones, and come up to MemeMe on the mountain. Make an ark of wood for yourself. 2I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets—the ones you broke—and you are to put them in the ark.”

3So I made an ark of acacia wood and carved two stone tablets like the first ones. Then I went up the mountain with the two tablets in my hand. 4He wrote on the tablets what had been written before—the TentenTen CommandmentscommandmentsCommandments that the LORD had spoken to you on the mountain out of the fire on the day of the assembly—and the LORD gave them to me. 5I turned and came down from the mountain and put the tablets in the ark I had made, and there they remain, just as the LORD commanded me.

The Levites Set Apart

8At that time the LORD set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the LORD’s covenant, to stand before the LORD and minister to HimhimHim, and to bless in HishisHis name, as they do to this day. 9That is why Levi has no portion or inheritance among his brothers: the LORD is his inheritance, just as the LORD your God promised him.

10I remained on the mountain forty days and forty nights, as I had the first time, and the LORD listened to me then as well. The LORD was unwilling to destroy you. 11Then the LORD said to me, “Get up and go on ahead of the people, so that they may enter and possess the land I swore to give their fathers.”

What the LORD Requires

12And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God ask of you? He asks that you fear the LORD your God, walk in all HishisHis ways, love HimhimHim, and serve the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul. 13He asks that you keep the LORD’s commands and statutes, which I am giving you today, for your good.

14Look, to the LORD your God belong the heavens, even the highest heavens, and the earth with everything in it. 15Yet the LORD set HishisHis affection on your fathers and loved them; HeheHe chose their descendants after them—yes, you—above all peoples, as it is today.

16Purify your hearts, then, and do not be stubborn any longer.

17For the LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great, mighty, and awesome God, who shows no partiality and accepts no bribe. 18He executes justice for the fatherless and the widow, and loves the foreigner, giving him food and clothing. 19So you must love the foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

20You shall fear the LORD your God. You shall serve HimhimHim and cling to HimhimHim, and you shall swear by HishisHis name. 21He is your praise and your God, who has done for you these great and awesome things your eyes have seen. 22Your fathers went down to Egypt with seventy persons, and now the LORD your God has made you as numerous as the stars of Heavenheavenheaven.
