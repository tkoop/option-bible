Paul’s Proclamation in Weakness

1When I came to you, brothers, I did not announce God’s testimony to you with superior eloquence or wisdom. 2For I resolved to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and HimhimHim crucified. 3I was with you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling. 4My speech and my preaching were not with persuasive words of human wisdom, but with a demonstration of the SpiritspiritSpirit and of power, 5so that your faith would not rest on human wisdom but on the power of God.

God’s Wisdom for the Mature

6Among the mature, however, we do speak wisdom—not the wisdom of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing. 7Instead, we speak the hidden wisdom of God in a mystery, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory. 8None of the rulers of this age understood it, for if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

Revelation through the Spirit

9But as it is written: “What no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no human heart has conceived—the things God has prepared for those who love HimhimHim.” 10But God has revealed them to us through the SpiritspiritSpirit. For the SpiritspiritSpirit searches all things, even the deep things of God. 11For who among men knows the thoughts of a man except the spirit of the man within him? So too, no one knows the things of God except the SpiritspiritSpirit of God. 12Now we have received not the spirit of the world, but the SpiritspiritSpirit from God, so that we may know the things freely given to us by God.

13And we speak of these things not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the SpiritspiritSpirit, expressing spiritual truths in spiritual words. 14The natural person does not accept the things of the SpiritspiritSpirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he cannot understand them, because they are spiritually discerned. 15The spiritual person judges all things, but is himself judged by no one. 16For “Who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to instruct HimhimHim?” But we have the mind of Christ.
