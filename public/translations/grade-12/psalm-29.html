The Majesty of the LORD’s Voice

1A psalm of David. Ascribe to the LORD glory and strength, O sons of God; ascribe to the LORD the glory due HishisHis name. 2Worship the LORD in the splendoursplendoursplendor of HishisHis holiness.

3The voice of the LORD resounds over the waters; the God of GlorygloryGlory thunders; the LORD presides over the many waters. 4The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic.

5The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; indeed, the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. 6He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox.

7The voice of the LORD hews forth flames of fire.

8The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. 9The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare; and in HishisHis temple all cry, “Glory!”

10The LORD sat enthroned over the deluge; the LORD is enthroned as KingkingKing forever.

11The LORD gives strength to HishisHis people; the LORD blesses HishisHis people with peace.
