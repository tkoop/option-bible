The Fifth Trumpet and the Locust Plague

1When the fifth angel sounded his trumpet, I beheld a star that had fallen from Heavenheavenheaven to the earth, and to it was granted the key to the shaft of the abyss. 2He opened that shaft, and smoke ascended from it like the smoke of a vast furnace, so that both the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke rising from the shaft.

3Out of the smoke emerged locusts upon the earth, and they were granted authority comparable to that of EarthEarthearth's scorpions. 4They were instructed not to harm the grass of the earth, nor any green plant, nor any tree, but only those people who lack God's seal upon their foreheads.

5They were permitted not to kill these people, but to torment them for five months; and their torment resembled the torment inflicted when a scorpion strikes a person. 6In those days people will seek death yet fail to find it; they will long to die, but death will flee from them.

The Locusts' Appearance and Their King

7The locusts' forms resembled horses arrayed for battle; upon their heads were something like golden crowns, and their faces were like human faces. 8They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth.

9They wore breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the thunder of many horses and chariots charging into battle. 10They possess tails and stings resembling scorpions', and in their tails lies the power to injure people for five months.

11They have over them as king the angel of the abyss, whose name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he is called Apollyon. 12The first woe has passed; behold, two woes remain and are yet to come.

The Sixth Trumpet and the Euphrates Host

13Then the sixth angel sounded his trumpet, and I heard a single voice from the four horns of the golden altar before God, 14saying to the sixth angel who held the trumpet, "Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates."

15So the four angels, who had been prepared for that hour, day, month, and year, were released to kill a third of mankind. 16The number of the cavalry troops was two hundred million; I heard their number.

17In the vision I saw the horses and their riders thus: they wore breastplates of fire, hyacinth, and sulphursulphursulfur; the horses' heads were like lions' heads; and from their mouths issued fire, smoke, and sulphursulphursulfur. 18By these three plagues a third of mankind was killed—the fire, the smoke, and the sulphursulphursulfur that proceeded from their mouths. 19For the horses' power resides in their mouths and in their tails; indeed, their tails resemble serpents with heads, and with them they inflict harm.

The Survivors' Refusal to Repent

20The rest of mankind, who were not killed by these plagues, did not repent of the works of their hands, so that they would not worship demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood—idols that can neither see nor hear nor walk. 21Nor did they repent of their murders, their sorceries, their sexual immorality, or their thefts.
