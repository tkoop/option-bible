Judah’s Engraved Sin and Impending Exile

1Judah’s sin is inscribed with an iron stylus, engraved with a diamond point upon the tablet of their heart and upon the horns of your altars. 2Even their children remember their altars and their Asherah poles beside verdant trees on the high hills.

3O MymyMy mountain in the open country, I will surrender your wealth and all your treasures as plunder, and your high places, because of sin throughout your territory. 4You yourself will relinquish the inheritance I gave you, and I will enslave you to your enemies in a land you do not know; for you have kindled a fire in MymyMy anger that will burn forever.

Trust in Mortals or in the LORD

5Thus says the LORD: “Cursed is the man who trusts in mankind, who makes flesh his strength and turns his heart away from the LORD. 6He will be like a stunted shrub in the wasteland; he will not perceive prosperity when it comes. He will dwell in the parched places of the desert, in a salt land where no one lives. 7Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD, whose hope is the LORD. 8He is like a tree planted by the waters, sending out its roots toward the stream. It does not fear when heat comes; its leaves remain green. It has no anxiety in a year of drought and never fails to bear fruit. 9The heart is deceitful above all things and incurably sick. Who can fathom it? 10I, the LORD, search the heart and test the mind, to repay each person according to his conduct, according to the fruit of his deeds.”

False Wealth and Jeremiah’s Prayer

11Like a partridge that broods over eggs it did not lay is the one who amasses wealth unjustly. In the middle of his days his riches will desert him, and in the end he will prove a fool. 12From the beginning, a glorious throne, exalted on high, has been the place of our sanctuary.

13O the LORD, the Hope of Israel, all who forsake YouyouYou will be put to shame; those who turn away from me will be written in the dust, for they have forsaken the LORD, the fountain of living waters. 14Heal me, O the LORD, and I will be healed; save me, and I will be saved, for YouyouYou are my praise.

15Behold, they keep saying to me, ‘Where is the word of the LORD? Let it come now!’

16As for me, I have not shrunk from shepherding in YouryourYour service, nor have I longed for the day of calamity; YouyouYou know that what passed my lips was spoken before YouyouYou. 17Do not be a terror to me; YouyouYou are my refuge in the day of disaster. 18Let my persecutors be put to shame, but do not let me be put to shame; let them be dismayed, but do not let me be dismayed. Bring upon them the day of evil; crush them with double ruin.

The Sabbath and Jerusalem’s Future

19Thus says the LORD to me: “Go and stand at the gate of the sons of the people, through which the kings of Judah enter and leave, and at all the gates of Jerusalem, 20and say to them, ‘Hear the word of the LORD, you kings of Judah and all Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem who enter through these gates. 21Thus says the LORD: Guard yourselves, and do not carry a burden or bring it through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. 22Do not carry a burden out of your houses on the Sabbath day, and do no work, but consecrate the Sabbath day, as I commanded your fathers.’

23“But they did not listen or incline their ear; they stiffened their necks and refused to hear or receive instruction. 24But if you diligently listen to MemeMe—declares the LORD—and bring no burden through the gates of this city on the Sabbath, and consecrate the Sabbath day by doing no work on it, 25then kings and princes who sit on David’s throne will enter through the gates of this city, riding in chariots and on horses—they and their officials, the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem—and this city will endure forever. 26People will come from the cities of Judah, from the region around Jerusalem, from Benjamin, from the Shephelah, from the hill country, and from the Negev, bringing burnt offerings, sacrifices, grain offerings, incense, and thank offerings to the house of the LORD. 27But if you do not listen to MemeMe to consecrate the Sabbath day and to refrain from carrying a burden as you enter the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath, then I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will consume the palaces of Jerusalem and will not be quenched.”
