Jehoshaphat's Return and Rebuke

1King Jehoshaphat of Judah returned safely to his home in Jerusalem. 2Jehu son of Hanani, the seer, went out to meet him and said to King Jehoshaphat, “Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the the LORD? Because of this, the wrath of the the LORD is upon you. 3Nevertheless, good things are found with you, for you have removed the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart to seek God.”

Jehoshaphat Reforms the Nation's Worship

4Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem, and he went out again among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and brought them back to the the LORD, the God of their fathers.

Judges Appointed Throughout Judah

5He appointed judges in the land, in all the fortified cities of Judah. 6He said to the judges, “Consider what you are doing, for you are not judging for man but for the the LORD, and HeheHe is with you in the matter of judgmentjudgementjudgment. 7Now let the fear of the the LORD be upon you; be careful and act, for with the the LORD our God there is no injustice, no partiality, and no accepting of bribes.”

A Supreme Court in Jerusalem

8In Jerusalem, Jehoshaphat also appointed some of the Levites, priests, and heads of Israelite families to judge for the the LORD and to settle disputes, and they returned to Jerusalem. 9He commanded them, “Thus you shall act in faithfulness and with a whole heart, in the fear of the the LORD. 10In every dispute that comes to you from your brothers who dwell in their cities—whether it involves bloodshed, law, commandment, statute, or judgmentjudgementjudgment—warn them so that they do not incur guilt before the the LORD, and wrath will not come upon you and upon your brothers. Do this, and you will not be guilty. 11Behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in all matters of the the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in all matters of the king, and the Levites and officers are before you. Act with courage; the the LORD will be with those who do good.”
