A Desperate Prayer in the Face of Betrayal

1Attend to my prayer, O God, and do not conceal YourselfyourselfYourself from my supplication. 2Hear me and answer me, for I am restless in my complaint, and I groan aloud. 3I am distraught because of the enemy's voice and the oppression of the wicked, for they cast iniquity upon me and persecute me with fury. 4My heart is in anguish within me, and the terrors of death have fallen upon me. 5Fear and trembling have seized me, and horror has overwhelmed me.

6I said, "If only I had the wings of a dove, I would fly away and find rest." 7See how far I would flee; I would take refuge in the wilderness. 8I would hasten to my refuge, away from the sweeping wind and storm.

9O Lord, confound their speech, for I see violence and strife in the city. 10Day and night they encircle its walls; iniquity and turmoil are within it. 11Desolation is within it; oppression and deceit never leave its streets.

12For it is not an enemy who taunts me—otherwise I could bear it; it is not a foe who exalts himself against me—otherwise I could conceal myself from him. 13But you are a man like me—my companion, my close friend. 14We shared intimate counsel; together we walked among the throng in the house of God. 15Let death seize them; let them descend alive into Sheol, for iniquity dwells within them.

16I call upon God, and the LORD delivers me. 17Morning, noon, and evening I lament and cry out, and HeheHe hears my voice. 18He redeems my soul in peace from the battle waged against me, for many contend against me. 19God will hear and afflict them—HeheHe who sits enthroned from eternity—because they have no intention of changing and do not fear God.

20He has stretched out his hand against those at peace with him; he has profaned his covenant. 21His speech is smooth as butter, yet war is in his heart; his words are softer than oil, but they are drawn swords.

22Cast your burden upon the LORD, and HeheHe will sustain you; HeheHe will never permit the righteous to be shaken. 23But YouyouYou, O God, will cast them into the pit of destruction; bloodthirsty and deceitful men will not live out half their days. But I will trust in YouyouYou.
