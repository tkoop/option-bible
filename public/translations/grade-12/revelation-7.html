1After this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding back the earth's four winds so that no wind would blow across the land, the sea, or any tree. 2Then I saw another angel ascending from the east, bearing the seal of the living God; he cried out with a thunderous voice to the four angels who had been authorized to harm the earth and the sea, 3saying, "Do not injure the land, the sea, or the trees until we have sealed the servants of our God upon their foreheads."
