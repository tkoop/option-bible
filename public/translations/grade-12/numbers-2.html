The LORD Commands Israel's Camp

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying, 2“The Israelites shall camp on every side of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, each man by his standard, with the banners of his ancestral house.”

The Eastern and Southern Camps

3On the east side, toward the sunrise, the standard of the camp of Judah shall be pitched by its armies, and the leader of the children of Judah shall be Nahshon son of Amminadab. 4His army, whose mustered number was seventy-four thousand six hundred, 5shall be joined by the tribe of Issachar, whose leader shall be Nethanel son of Zuar. 6His army, whose mustered number was fifty-four thousand four hundred, 7shall be joined by the tribe of Zebulun, whose leader shall be Eliab son of Helon. 8His army, whose mustered number was fifty-seven thousand four hundred. 9All who were numbered for the camp of Judah were one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred, and they shall set out first.

10On the south side shall be the standard of the camp of Reuben by its armies, and the leader of the children of Reuben shall be Elizur son of Shedeur. 11His army, whose mustered number was forty-six thousand five hundred, 12shall be joined by the tribe of Simeon, whose leader shall be Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. 13His army, whose mustered number was fifty-nine thousand three hundred, 14shall be joined by the tribe of Gad, whose leader shall be Eliasaph son of Reuel. 15His army, whose mustered number was forty-five thousand six hundred fifty. 16All who were numbered for the camp of Reuben were one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty, and they shall set out second.

The Levites and the Western and Northern Camps

17Then the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps; as they camp, so shall they set out, each man in his place, by his standard.

18On the west side shall be the standard of the camp of Ephraim by its armies, and the leader of the children of Ephraim shall be Elishama son of Ammihud. 19His army, whose mustered number was forty thousand five hundred, 20shall be joined by the tribe of Manasseh, whose leader shall be Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. 21His army, whose mustered number was thirty-two thousand two hundred, 22shall be joined by the tribe of Benjamin, whose leader shall be Abidan son of Gideoni. 23His army, whose mustered number was thirty-five thousand four hundred. 24All who were numbered for the camp of Ephraim were one hundred eight thousand one hundred, and they shall set out third.

25On the north side shall be the standard of the camp of Dan by its armies, and the leader of the children of Dan shall be Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. 26His army, whose mustered number was sixty-two thousand seven hundred, 27shall be joined by the tribe of Asher, whose leader shall be Pagiel son of Ocran. 28His army, whose mustered number was forty-one thousand five hundred, 29shall be joined by the tribe of Naphtali, whose leader shall be Ahira son of Enan. 30His army, whose mustered number was fifty-three thousand four hundred. 31All who were numbered for the camp of Dan were one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred, and they shall set out last, by their standards.

The Total Numbers and Israel's Obedience

32These are the Israelites who were numbered according to their ancestral houses. All who were numbered of the camps by their armies were six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty. 33But the Levites were not numbered among the Israelites, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 34So the Israelites did everything the LORD commanded Moses: they camped by their standards, and then set out, each man by his family and by his ancestral house.
