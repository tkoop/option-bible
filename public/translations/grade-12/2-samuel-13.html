Amnon's Fixation on Tamar

1After these events, Absalom son of David had a beautiful sister named Tamar, and Amnon, another son of David, loved her. 2Amnon grew so obsessed with his sister Tamar that he fell ill; since she was a virgin, it seemed impossible for him to have his way with her. 3Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, son of Shimeah, David's brother, and Jonadab was a very shrewd man.

4He asked him, "Why are you, the king's son, so haggard every morning? Will you not confide in me?"

Amnon replied, "I am in love with Tamar, my brother Absalom's sister."

5Jonadab said to him, "Lie down on your bed and feign illness. When your father comes to see you, say to him, 'Please let my sister Tamar come and prepare food before my eyes, so that I may eat what she prepares.'"

6So Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill. When the king came to see him, Amnon said to the king, "Please let my sister Tamar come and make two cakes in my sight, so that I may eat what she prepares."

7David sent word to Tamar at the palace: "Go to the house of your brother Amnon and prepare food for him." 8Tamar went to the house of her brother Amnon, who was lying down. She took dough, kneaded it, made cakes in his sight, and baked them.

9She took the pan and served them before him, but he refused to eat. Amnon said, "Send everyone out of the room." So everyone left.

10Amnon said to Tamar, "BringbringBring the food into the bedroom so that I may eat from your hand." Tamar took the cakes she had made and brought them to her brother Amnon in the bedroom.

The Assault on Tamar

11As she brought them to him to eat, he seized her and said, "Come to bed with me, my sister."

12"No, my brother!" she cried. "Do not violate me, for such a thing is not done in Israel. Do not commit this disgraceful act."

13"Where would I go with my shame? And as for you, you would be counted among the fools in Israel. Now please speak to the king, for he will not refuse to give me to you."

14But he refused to listen to her, and being stronger than she, he violated her and lay with her.

15Then Amnon hated her with a very great hatred, so that the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her. And Amnon said to her, "Get up and go."

16"No," she said, "for sending me away now would be a greater wrong than the one you have already done to me." But he would not listen to her.

17He called his young servant and said, "Get this woman out of here and bolt the door behind her."

18Now his servant had on a robe with long sleeves, for this is what the king's virgin daughters wore. So his servant put her out and bolted the door behind her.

19Tamar put ashes on her head, tore the long-sleeved robe she was wearing, placed her hand on her head, and went away crying aloud.

20Her brother Absalom said to her, "Has Amnon your brother been with you? Now keep quiet, my sister; he is your brother. Do not take this to heart." So Tamar lived as a desolate woman in the house of her brother Absalom.

21When King David heard all this, he was very angry.

22Absalom never spoke to Amnon either good or bad, because he hated Amnon for having violated his sister Tamar.

Absalom's Vengeance

23After two full years, Absalom had sheepshearers at Baal-hazor near Ephraim, and he invited all the king's sons.

24Absalom came to the king and said, "YouryourYour servant is shearing sheep. Please let the king and his servants go with your servant."

25The king said to Absalom, "No, my son, we should not all go, lest we be a burden to you." Although Absalom urged him, he refused to go, but he gave him his blessing.

26"Then please let my brother Amnon go with us."

"Why should he go with you?" the king asked.

27But Absalom urged him so strongly that he sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him.

28Then Absalom commanded his servants, "Watch Amnon until he is merry with wine, and when I tell you, 'Strike Amnon and kill him,' do not be afraid. Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous."

29So Absalom's servants did to Amnon as Absalom had commanded. Then all the king's sons mounted their mules and fled.

David's Grief

30While they were on the way, the report reached David: "Absalom has killed all the king's sons; not one of them survives."

31Then the king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground, and all his servants stood by with their clothes torn.

32But Jonadab son of Shimeah, David's brother, spoke up: "Do not let my lord the king think that all the young men, the king's sons, are dead. Only Amnon is dead, for Absalom has planned this since the day Amnon violated his sister Tamar. 33So now, my lord the king, do not take this to heart and think that all the king's sons are dead. Only Amnon is dead."

34Meanwhile Absalom fled. The young man on watch looked up and saw many people coming along the road from the side of the hill behind him.

35Jonadab said to the king, "Look, the king's sons are coming. It is just as your servant said."

36As soon as he finished speaking, the king's sons arrived and wept aloud; the king and all his servants also wept very bitterly.

37Absalom fled and went to Talmai son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. And David mourned for his son every day. 38Absalom fled and stayed in Geshur for three years.

39King David longed to go to Absalom, for he was comforted concerning Amnon, who had died.
