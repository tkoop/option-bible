The Purpose and Meaning of the Parables

16"But blessed are your eyes, because they see, and your ears, because they hear. 17For truly I tell you, many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see and did not see it, and to hear what you hear and did not hear it."

18"Listen, then, to the parable of the sower. 19When anyone hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away what was sown in his heart. This is what was sown along the path. 20The seed sown on rocky ground is the one who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy. 21Yet he has no root in himself, so he lasts only a short time; and when tribulation or persecution arises on account of the word, immediately he stumbles. 22The seed sown among thorns is the one who hears the word, but the cares of the world and the deceitfulness of riches choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. 23But the seed sown on good soil is the one who hears the word and understands it; he indeed bears fruit and yields, in one case a hundredfold, in another sixty, and in another thirty."
