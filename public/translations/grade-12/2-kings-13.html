Jehoahaz's Reign and Israel's Oppression

1In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria and reigned seventeen years. 2He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD by walking in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit, and he did not depart from them.

3So the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and throughout those days HeheHe delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram and into the hand of Ben-Hadad his son. 4Then Jehoahaz implored the LORD, and the LORD heeded him, because HeheHe saw the oppression that the king of Aram was inflicting upon Israel. 5The LORD granted Israel a deliverer, so that they escaped from the power of Aram, and the Israelites dwelt in their homes as they had formerly.

6Nevertheless, they did not turn aside from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, which he had caused Israel to commit, but persisted in them; moreover, the Asherah remained standing in Samaria. 7For the king of Aram had reduced the army of Jehoahaz to a mere fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers, having destroyed the rest and reduced them to dust, as grain is threshed.

8As for the remaining acts of Jehoahaz, all that he did and his might, are they not recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 9Jehoahaz slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria, and his son Joash reigned in his place.

Jehoash's Reign and Elisha's Final Prophecy

10In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria and reigned sixteen years. 11He did what was evil in the sight of the LORD and did not depart from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit, but persisted in them.

12As for the remaining acts of Jehoash, all that he did and his might, including the war he waged against Amaziah king of Judah, are they not recorded in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel? 13Jehoash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam ascended his throne. Jehoash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

14Now Elisha had fallen ill with the sickness that would kill him. Jehoash king of Israel went down to him and wept over him, crying, “My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!”

15Elisha said to him, “Take a bow and arrows,” so he took a bow and arrows. 16“Take the bow in your hands,” he said to the king of Israel. When he had taken it, Elisha laid his hands upon the king's hands.

17“Open the east window,” he said, and he opened it. “Shoot!” Elisha commanded, and he shot. “The LORD's arrow of victory, the arrow of victory over Aram!” Elisha proclaimed. “You will completely destroy the Arameans at Aphek.”

18Then he said, “Take the arrows,” and he took them. “Strike the ground,” Elisha told him. He struck it three times and then stopped. 19The man of God grew angry with him and said, “You should have struck the ground five or six times; then you would have completely destroyed Aram. But now you will defeat it only three times.”

Israel's Recovery after Elisha's Death

20Elisha died and was buried. Now bands of Moabite raiders were invading the land each spring. 21Once, as some Israelites were burying a man, they suddenly saw a raiding band, so they threw the man's body into Elisha's tomb. When the body touched Elisha's bones, the man revived and stood on his feet.

22Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel throughout all the days of Jehoahaz. 23But the LORD was gracious to them and had compassion on them; HeheHe turned toward them because of HishisHis covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and to this day HeheHe has neither destroyed them nor cast them from HishisHis presence.

24Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-Hadad reigned in his place. 25Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from Ben-Hadad son of Hazael the cities that he had seized in battle from his father Jehoahaz. Three times Jehoash defeated him and recovered the cities of Israel.
