The Sons of Israel in Egypt

1These are the names of the sons of Israel who entered Egypt with Jacob, each accompanied by his household: 2Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah; 3Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin; 4Dan, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. 5All those descended from Jacob numbered seventy persons, and Joseph was already in Egypt. 6Joseph died, along with all his brothers and that entire generation. 7Yet the Israelites were fruitful and multiplied exceedingly; they grew exceedingly mighty, until the land teemed with them.

A New Pharaoh's Oppression

8Then a new king arose over Egypt, one who had not known Joseph. 9He said to his people, "Look, the people of the sons of Israel are more numerous and mightier than we. 10Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, lest they multiply and, should war befall us, join our enemies, fight against us, and depart from the land." 11So they appointed taskmasters over them to afflict them with their burdens, and they built for Pharaoh the storage cities Pithom and Raamses. 12But the more they were afflicted, the more they multiplied and spread, so that the Egyptians came to dread the Israelites. 13The Egyptians compelled the Israelites to serve with harshness, 14and they embittered their lives with arduous labourlabourlabor in mortar and brick, together with every kind of field labourlabourlabor; all the work they imposed upon them was severe.

The Hebrew Midwives Defy Pharaoh

15The king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives—one named Shiphrah and the other Puah— 16and said, "When you assist the Hebrew women in childbirth and see them on the birthstools, if the child is a son, then you shall kill him; but if it is a daughter, then she shall live." 17But the midwives feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt had commanded them; instead, they let the boys live. 18The king of Egypt summoned the midwives and said to them, "Why have you done this, that you have let the boys live?" 19The midwives said to Pharaoh, "Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women, for they are vigorous and give birth before the midwife reaches them." 20God treated the midwives well, and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly mighty. 21Because the midwives feared God, HeheHe established households for them. 22Pharaoh commanded all his people, saying, "Every son who is born you shall cast into the Nile, and every daughter you shall let live."
