Ephraim's Futile Pursuits and the LORD's Charge

1Ephraim pursues futile and ruinous schemes all day long; he multiplies lies and violence. He makes a covenant with Assyria, and he sends olive oil to Egypt. 2The LORD also has a charge to bring against Judah, and HeheHe will punish Jacob according to his ways and repay him according to his deeds.

Jacob's Struggle and the Call to Return

3In the womb he took his brother by the heel, and in his manhood he wrestled with God. 4He struggled with the angel and prevailed; he wept and sought HishisHis favourfavourfavor. He found HimhimHim at Bethel, and there HeheHe spoke with us. 5The LORD God of Hosts—the the LORD is HishisHis name of renown. 6But you must return to your God; uphold steadfast love and justice, and wait continually for your God.

7A merchant loves to defraud with dishonest scales in his hands. 8And Ephraim boasts, “I am very rich; I have found wealth for myself. In all my labourslabourslabors, they can find in me no iniquity that is sin.”

9“But I am the LORD your God ever since the land of Egypt. I will again make you dwell in tents, as in the days of the appointed feast. 10I spoke through the prophets and multiplied their visions; I gave parables through the prophets.”

Gilead's Guilt and Israel's Deliverance

11Is there iniquity in Gilead? They will surely come to nothing. In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls, and their altars are like piles of stones in the furrows of a field. 12Jacob fled to the land of Aram, and Israel served for a wife—for a wife he tended sheep.

13By a prophet the LORD brought Israel up out of Egypt, and by a prophet he was preserved. 14Ephraim has provoked bitter anger, so his Lord will leave his bloodguilt upon him and repay him for his contempt.
