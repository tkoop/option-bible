Paul's Ministry to the Gentiles

1For this reason I, Paul, am the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles— 2if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of God's grace that was given to me for you, 3namely, that the mystery was made known to me by revelation, just as I wrote briefly before. 4When you read this, you can understand my insight into the mystery of Christ, 5which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men as it has now been revealed in the SpiritspiritSpirit to HishisHis holy apostles and prophets. 6This mystery is that, through the gospel, the Gentiles are fellow heirs, fellow members of the body, and fellow partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus.

The Revelation of the Mystery

7I became a servant of this gospel according to the gift of God's grace, given to me by the working of HishisHis power. 8To me, the very least of all the saints, this grace was given: to preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ 9and to enlighten everyone as to what is the stewardship of the mystery that was hidden for ages in God, who created all things. 10His intent was that now, through the church, the manifold wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms, 11according to the eternal purpose that HeheHe accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord. 12In HimhimHim and through faith in HimhimHim we have bold and confident access. 13Therefore I ask you not to become discouraged at my sufferings on your behalf, for they are your glory.

Paul's Prayer for Spiritual Strength

14For this reason I bow my knees before the FatherfatherFather, 15from whom every family in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth derives its name. 16I pray that, according to the riches of HishisHis glory, HeheHe may strengthen you with power through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit in your inner being, 17so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, as you are rooted and grounded in love. 18And I pray that you, together with all the saints, may have power to comprehend the breadth and length and height and depth— 19that is, to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge—so that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

Doxology

20Now to HimhimHim who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine, according to the power that is at work within us, 21to HimhimHim be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
