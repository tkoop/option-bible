Ephraim’s Complaint and Gideon’s Reply

1Then the men of Ephraim said to him, “What is this that you have done to us? Why did you not summon us when you went to fight against Midian?” And they argued with him vehemently. 2Gideon answered, “What have I accomplished now in comparison with you? Are not the gleanings of Ephraim superior to the vintage of Abiezer? 3God has delivered the princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb, into your hands. What could I have done in comparison with you?” Then their anger toward him subsided when he said this.

The Pursuit of Zebah and Zalmunna

4Gideon and the three hundred men who accompanied him came to the Jordan, weary yet in pursuit. 5He said to the men of Succoth, “Please provide loaves of bread for the people who are with me, for they are weary, and I am pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”

6The officials of Succoth replied, “Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your power, that we should give bread to your troops?”

7Gideon answered, “When the the LORD has delivered Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will tear your flesh with thorns of the wilderness and briers.”

8From there he ascended to Penuel and addressed them in the same manner, and the men of Penuel answered him as the men of Succoth had answered. 9He said to the men of Penuel, “When I return in peace, I will demolish this tower.”

10Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their camp, about fifteen thousand men—all that remained of the entire host of the people of the East, for one hundred twenty thousand swordsmen had fallen. 11Gideon ascended by the route of those who dwelt in tents east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and he struck the camp while it was unsuspecting. 12Zebah and Zalmunna fled, and he pursued them; he seized the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and threw the whole army into confusion. 13Gideon son of Joash returned from the battle by the ascent of Heres.

Gideon’s Vengeance on Succoth and Penuel

14He captured a young man of Succoth and interrogated him, and the young man wrote down for him the officials and elders of Succoth—seventy-seven men. 15He came to the men of Succoth and said, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, ‘Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna already in your power, that we should give bread to your weary men?’”

16He took the elders of the city, together with thorns and briers from the wilderness, and with them he scourged the men of Succoth. 17He demolished the tower of Penuel and killed the men of the city.

18Then he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, “Where are the men you killed at Tabor?” They answered, “As you are, so were they; each one resembled a son of a king.”

19He said, “They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As the the LORD lives, if you had spared them, I would not kill you.”

20He said to Jether his firstborn, “Rise and kill them.” But the boy did not draw his sword, for he was afraid, because he was still young.

21Zebah and Zalmunna said, “Rise and strike us down yourself, for as a man is, so is his strength.” So Gideon rose and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent ornaments from their camels’ necks.

Gideon’s Later Years and Israel’s Relapse

22The men of Israel said to Gideon, “Rule over us—you and your son and your grandson as well—for you have delivered us from the hand of Midian.”

23Gideon replied, “I will not rule over you, nor shall my son rule over you; the the LORD will rule over you.”

24Gideon said to them, “Let me make a request of you: each of you give me an earring from his plunder.” (They had golden earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.) 25They answered, “We will gladly give them.” They spread out a garment, and each man threw an earring from his plunder onto it.

26The weight of the gold earrings that he requested was one thousand seven hundred shekels of gold, besides the crescents, the pendants, the purple garments of the kings of Midian, and besides the chains on their camels’ necks. 27Gideon made an ephod from it and placed it in his city, Ophrah, and all Israel turned to it in idolatry there, so that it became a snare to Gideon and his household. 28Thus Midian was subdued before the Israelites, and they did not lift their heads again; the land had rest for forty years in the days of Gideon.

29Jerubbaal son of Joash went and lived in his own house. 30Gideon had seventy sons, his own offspring, for he had many wives. 31His concubine who was in Shechem also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech.

32Gideon son of Joash died at a good old age and was buried in the tomb of Joash his father at Ophrah of the Abiezrites. 33As soon as Gideon died, the Israelites turned again and gave themselves to the Baals in idolatry, and they made Baal-berith their god. 34The Israelites did not remember the the LORD their God, who had saved them from the hand of all their enemies on every side. 35Nor did they show steadfast love to the house of Jerubbaal—that is, Gideon—in keeping with all the good he had done for Israel.
