The LORD Commands Noah to Enter the Ark

1Then the LORD said to Noah, “Enter the ark—you and your entire household—for I have seen that you alone are righteous before MemeMe in this generation. 2Take with you seven pairs of every clean animal, a male with its mate, and two of every unclean animal, a male with its mate; 3and take seven pairs of every bird of the sky, male and female, to keep their kind alive on the face of the whole EarthEarthearth. 4For in seven days I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will blot out from the face of the ground every living thing that I have made.”

Noah Obeys and Enters the Ark

5And Noah carried out everything that the LORD had commanded him. 6Noah was six hundred years old when the floodwaters came upon the earth. 7He entered the ark with his wife, his sons, and his sons' wives to escape the waters of the deluge. 8Clean animals and unclean animals, birds and every creature that crawls on the ground, 9came to Noah two by two, male and female, just as God had commanded Noah. 10After seven days, the floodwaters came upon the earth.

The Deluge Begins

11In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, all the fountains of the great deep burst forth, and the floodgates of Heavenheavenheaven were opened. 12Rain fell upon the earth for forty days and forty nights. 13On that very day Noah and his sons—Shem, Ham, and Japheth—and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons entered the ark. 14They came with every kind of wild animal, every kind of livestock, every creature that crawls on the earth, and every kind of bird— 15two by two, all flesh that possessed the breath of life. 16Those that entered, male and female of every living creature, came just as God had commanded Noah; then the LORD shut him in.

The Waters Prevail and Cover the Earth

17The flood continued on the earth for forty days; the waters increased and lifted the ark high above the earth. 18The waters prevailed and swelled exceedingly upon the earth, and the ark drifted on the surface of the waters. 19The waters prevailed so overwhelmingly on the earth that all the high mountains under the whole Heavenheavenheaven were covered. 20The waters rose and covered the mountains to a depth of fifteen cubits.

21Every living thing that moved on the earth perished—birds, livestock, animals, every swarming creature on the earth, and all humanity. 22Everything on dry land that had the breath of life in its nostrils died. 23Thus the LORD blotted out every living thing that was on the face of the earth—humanity, livestock, creeping things, and birds of the sky. They were blotted out from the earth, and only Noah and those with him in the ark survived. 24The waters prevailed on the earth for one hundred fifty days.
