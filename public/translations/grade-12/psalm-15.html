1A psalm of David. The LORD, who may sojourn in YouryourYour tent? Who may dwell upon YouryourYour holy hill?

2He who walks in integrity and practices righteousness, 3who does not slander with his tongue, who does no evil to his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and who does not take up a reproach against his friend; 4who regards the vile as contemptible but honourshonourshonors those who fear the the LORD, who keeps his oath even when it proves costly; 5who lends his money without usury and does not accept a bribe against the innocent.
