Praise the LORD with Joyful Song

1Rejoice in the LORD, O righteous ones; praise is fitting for the upright. 2Praise the LORD with the harp; make music to HimhimHim on the ten-stringed lyre. 3Sing to HimhimHim a new song; play skillfully with a shout of joy.

The LORD's Word, Works, and Sovereign Plans

4For the word of the LORD is upright, and all HishisHis work is done in faithfulness. 5He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the LORD's steadfast love. 6By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and by the breath of HishisHis mouth all their host. 7He gathers the waters of the sea into a heap; HeheHe stores the ocean depths in HishisHis storehouses. 8Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of HimhimHim. 9For HeheHe spoke, and it came to be; HeheHe commanded, and it stood firm.

10The LORD nullifies the counsel of the nations and frustrates the plans of the peoples. 11The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the plans of HishisHis heart to all generations. 12Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people HeheHe has chosen as HishisHis inheritance.

The LORD's Watchful Care and Steadfast Love

13The LORD looks down from Heavenheavenheaven; HeheHe sees all the sons of men. 14From HishisHis dwelling place HeheHe gazes on all the inhabitants of the earth. 15He who fashions their hearts considers all their works. 16The king is not saved by a great army; a warrior is not delivered by great strength. 17The horse is a false hope for salvation, and its great strength cannot deliver.

18Behold, the eye of the LORD is on those who fear HimhimHim, on those who hope in HishisHis steadfast love, 19to deliver their lives from death and to sustain them in famine. 20Our soul waits for the LORD; HeheHe is our help and our shield. 21For our heart rejoices in HimhimHim, because we have trusted in HishisHis holy name. 22Let YouryourYour steadfast love, O LORD, be upon us, according as we have hoped in YouyouYou.
