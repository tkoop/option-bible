The Philistines Seek Counsel About the Ark

1The ark of the the LORD remained in Philistine territory for seven months.

2The Philistines summoned their priests and diviners and asked, "What should we do with the ark of the the LORD? Tell us how we ought to send it back to its place."

The Priests Advise a Guilt Offering

3"If you send away the ark of the God of Israel, do not send it empty; rather, you must return a guilt offering to HimhimHim. Then you will be healed, and it will become clear to you why HishisHis hand has not withdrawn from you. 4As for the guilt offering you should return to HimhimHim, let it be five gold tumors and five gold mice, corresponding to the number of Philistine rulers, for the same plague has afflicted all of you and your rulers. 5Therefore make images of your tumors and of your mice—the very creatures that are ravaging the land—and give glory to the God of Israel. Perhaps HeheHe will lighten HishisHis hand from you, from your gods, and from your land. 6Why should you harden your hearts, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened theirs? When HeheHe dealt severely with them, did they not send the people away so that they could go?

The Ark Is Sent Back to Israel

7"Now then, prepare a new cart and take two milk cows that have never been yoked. Hitch the cows to the cart, but take their calves away and keep them at home. 8Take the ark of the the LORD and place it on the cart. Put the gold objects that you are returning as a guilt offering into a chest beside it, and send it off so that it may go on its way. 9Then watch: if it goes up the road toward Beth Shemesh, then HeheHe has brought this great disaster upon us; but if not, then we will know that HishisHis hand did not strike us, and that what happened to us was merely chance."

10The men did so. They took two milk cows, hitched them to the cart, and penned their calves at home.

11They placed the ark of the the LORD on the cart, along with the chest containing the gold mice and the images of their tumors.

12The cows went straight along the road toward Beth Shemesh, keeping to the highway and lowing as they went; they did not turn aside to the right or to the left. The Philistine rulers followed them as far as the border of Beth Shemesh.

The Ark Arrives at Beth Shemesh

13The people of Beth Shemesh were harvesting wheat in the valley. When they looked up and saw the ark, they rejoiced at the sight.

14The cart came into the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh and stopped there beside a large stone. They split the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to the the LORD.

15The Levites lowered the ark of the the LORD and the chest beside it, which held the gold objects, and placed them on the large stone. That day the men of Beth Shemesh offered burnt offerings and presented sacrifices to the the LORD.

16When the five Philistine rulers saw this, they returned to Ekron that same day.

17These are the gold tumors the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to the the LORD: one for Ashdod, one for Gaza, one for Ashkelon, one for Gath, and one for Ekron.

18The gold mice corresponded to the number of all the Philistine cities belonging to the five rulers—fortified cities and unwalled villages alike—even as far as the great stone of Abel, on which they set down the ark of the the LORD. That stone remains to this day in the field of Joshua of Beth Shemesh.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment at Beth Shemesh

19But HeheHe struck down some of the men of Beth Shemesh because they had looked into the ark of the the LORD. He struck down fifty thousand and seventy men, and the people mourned because the the LORD had struck the people with a great slaughter.

20The men of Beth Shemesh said, "Who can stand before the the LORD, this holy God? And to whom shall HeheHe go up from us?"

21So they sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath Jearim, saying, "ThetheThe Philistines have returned the ark of the the LORD. Come down and take it up to yourselves."
