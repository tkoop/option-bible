Job Responds to His Friends

1Indeed, my eye has seen all this; my ear has heard and understood it. 2What you know, I also know; I am not inferior to you. 3Yet I desire to speak to the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty and to argue my case with God. 4You, however, smear with lies; you are all worthless physicians.

5If only you would be completely silent, for that would be your wisdom! 6Hear now my argument, and listen to the pleas of my lips. 7Will you speak wickedly on God’s behalf and speak deceitfully for HimhimHim? 8Will you show HimhimHim partiality? Will you contend for God?

9Would it be well if HeheHe examined you? Could you deceive HimhimHim as you deceive a man? 10Surely HeheHe would rebuke you if you secretly showed partiality. 11Would not HishisHis majesty terrify you, and the dread of HimhimHim fall upon you? 12Your maxims are proverbs of ashes; your defenses are defenses of clay.

Job Demands a Hearing Before God

13Be silent before me so that I may speak; then let come to me what may. 14Why do I put myself in jeopardy and take my life in my hands? 15Behold, HeheHe will slay me; I will have no hope, yet I will argue my ways before HishisHis face. 16He also is my salvation, for no godless man will come before HimhimHim.

17Listen carefully to my words; let my declaration be in your ears. 18See now, I have prepared my case; I know that I am right. 19Who can contend with me? If I were to yield now, I would die.

Job Appeals for Relief from God

20Only grant me these two things, O God, and then I will not hide from YouyouYou: 21Withdraw YouryourYour hand from me, and do not let YouryourYour terror frighten me. 22Then summon me, and I will answer; or let me speak, and YouyouYou reply.

23How many are my iniquities and sins? Reveal to me my transgression and sin. 24Why do YouyouYou hide YouryourYour face and consider me as YouryourYour enemy? 25Will YouyouYou frighten a windblown leaf? Will YouyouYou chase after dry chaff? 26For YouyouYou record bitter things against me and make me inherit the iniquities of my youth. 27You put my feet in the stocks and stand watch over all my paths; YouyouYou set a limit for the soles of my feet. 28So man wastes away like something rotten, like a garment eaten by moths.
