Sarai Gives Hagar to Abram

1Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children, but she had an Egyptian maidservant named Hagar. 2So Sarai said to Abram, "Please observe that the LORD has kept me from bearing children. Have relations with my maidservant; perhaps I can have children through her." And Abram listened to Sarai's voice. 3After Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her maidservant, and gave her to her husband Abram to be his wife.

Hagar Conceives and Despises Her Mistress

4He had relations with Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, she looked with contempt on her mistress. 5Then Sarai said to Abram, "YouyouYou are responsible for the wrong done to me. I gave my maidservant into your embrace, and when she saw that she had conceived, I was despised in her eyes. May the LORD judge between me and you." 6Abram said to Sarai, "Look, your maidservant is under your authority; do to her whatever you think best." So Sarai treated her harshly, and she fled from her presence.

The Angel of the LORD Finds Hagar

7The Angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Shur.

8He said, "Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?"

She said, "I am fleeing from the presence of my mistress Sarai."

9The Angel of the LORD said to her, "Return to your mistress and submit to her authority."

The Promise to Hagar and Ishmael's Birth

10The Angel of the LORD said to her, "I will greatly multiply your descendants, so that they cannot be counted for multitude." 11The Angel of the LORD said to her, "Behold, you are pregnant and will bear a son, and you shall call his name Ishmael, because the LORD has heard your affliction. 12He will be a wild and untamed man; his hand will be against everyone, and everyone's hand against him, and he will live in conflict with all his brothers."

13She called the name of the LORD who spoke to her, "YouyouYou are the God who sees me," for she said, "Have I really seen the OneoneOne who sees me?" 14Therefore the well was called Beer-lahai-roi; indeed, it is between Kadesh and Bered.

15Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram named his son Ishmael. 16Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore him Ishmael.
