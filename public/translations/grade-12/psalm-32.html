The Blessedness of Forgiveness

1Blessed is the one whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered. 2Blessed is the man whose iniquity the LORD does not count against him and in whose spirit there is no deceit.

3When I kept silent, my bones became brittle through my groaning all day long. 4For day and night YouryourYour hand was heavy upon me; my strength was drained as in the summer heat. Selah

5Then I acknowledged my sin to YouyouYou and did not cover up my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and YouyouYou forgave the guilt of my sin. Selah

6Therefore let every godly man pray to YouyouYou while YouyouYou may be found; surely when great waters rise, they will not reach him. 7You are my hiding place; YouyouYou will protect me from trouble and surround me with songs of deliverance. Selah

8“I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will counsel you with MymyMy eye upon you. 9Do not be like the horse or the mule, which lack understanding and must be controlled by bit and bridle, or they will not come near you.”

10Many are the sorrows of the wicked, but loving devotion surrounds the one who trusts in the LORD. 11Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, you righteous; shout for joy, all you upright in heart.
