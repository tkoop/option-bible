Joy in the LORD's Restoration

1When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion, we seemed to be dreaming. 2Then our mouths overflowed with laughter, and our tongues rang with shouts of joy; then the nations declared, “The LORD has done great things for them.” 3The LORD has done great things for us, and we are glad.

A Prayer for Renewed Fortunes

4Restore our fortunes, O the LORD, like streams flooding the Negev. 5Those who sow in tears will reap with shouts of joy. 6He who goes out weeping, bearing seed to sow, will return with shouts of joy, bringing his sheaves with him.
