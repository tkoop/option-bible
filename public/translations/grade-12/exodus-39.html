Fashioning the Priestly Vestments

1From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the artisans fashioned woven garments for ministering in the sanctuary, and they also fashioned the holy garments for Aaron, just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 2He fashioned the ephod from gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and from finely spun linen. 3They hammered the gold into thin sheets and cut threads from them, weaving those threads into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and the fine linen—a work of consummate craftsmanship. 4They fashioned shoulder pieces for the ephod, joined at its two ends, so that it could be fastened. 5The skillfully woven waistband of the ephod was of one piece with it, fashioned from the same materials—gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen—just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

6They mounted the onyx stones in gold filigree settings, engraved like a seal with the names of the sons of Israel. 7Then they fastened them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as memorial stones for the sons of Israel, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Breastpiece of JudgmentJudgementJudgment

8He fashioned the breastpiece with the same craftsmanship as the ephod—of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. 9It was square when folded, a span in length and a span in width. 10They mounted four rows of gemstones upon it: the first row held carnelian, chrysolite, and emerald; 11the second row, turquoise, sapphire, and diamond; 12the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst; 13and the fourth row, beryl, onyx, and jasper. They set each stone in gold filigree. 14The stones corresponded to the names of the sons of Israel—twelve in all—each engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes.

15For the breastpiece they fashioned braided chains of pure gold, like cords. 16They also made two gold settings and two gold rings, fastening the rings to the two corners of the breastpiece. 17Then they attached the two gold chains to the rings at the corners of the breastpiece, 18and they secured the other ends of the chains to the two settings, attaching them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front. 19They made two additional gold rings and attached them to the two lower corners of the breastpiece, on the inner edge adjacent to the ephod. 20They fashioned two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, on the front, near the seam just above the woven band of the ephod. 21Then they laced the rings of the breastpiece to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue yarn, so that the breastpiece rested above the waistband of the ephod and would not swing away from it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

The Robe, Tunics, and Sacred Insignia

22They fashioned the robe of the ephod entirely of blue cloth, the work of a weaver, 23with an opening in its centrecentrecenter like the opening of a garment, and a collar around the opening so that it would not tear. 24They fashioned pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely spun linen around the lower hem of the robe. 25They also made bells of pure gold and attached them around the lower hem of the robe, between the pomegranates, 26alternating a bell and a pomegranate all around the lower hem of the robe to be worn for ministry, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

27They fashioned tunics of fine linen, woven work, for Aaron and his sons, 28the turban of fine linen, the ornate headbands of fine linen, and the undergarments of finely spun linen. 29And the sash of finely spun linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, was of woven work, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

30They fashioned the plate of the holy crown from pure gold and engraved upon it, like an inscription on a seal: HOLY TO the LORD. 31Then they fastened to it a blue cord to mount it on the turban above, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

42The Israelites had done all the work just as the LORD had commanded Moses. 43And Moses inspected all the work and saw that they had done it just as the LORD had commanded. So Moses blessed them.
