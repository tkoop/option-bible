The Weakening of Saul's House

1When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his courage failed, and all Israel was thrown into consternation. 2Now Saul's son had two men who were commanders of raiding parties—Baanah and Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the Benjaminites—for Beeroth was counted as belonging to Benjamin.

3The Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have lived there as resident aliens to this day. 4Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was lame in both feet; he was five years old when the report about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel. His nurse lifted him and fled, but as she hurried to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

The Murder of Ish-bosheth

5Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, set out and came to the house of Ish-bosheth at the heat of the day, at noon, while he lay taking his midday rest. 6They made their way into the inner room of the house, as if to fetch wheat, and struck him in the abdomen; then Rechab and his brother Baanah slipped away. 7When they entered the house, he was lying on his bed in his bedroom; they struck him, killed him, beheaded him, took his head, and travelledtravelledtraveled all night by way of the Arabah.

David's JudgmentJudgementJudgment on the Murderers

8They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth, son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. Today the LORD has given my lord the king vengeance on Saul and his line." 9David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, "As the LORD lives, who redeemed my soul from all distress, 10when someone told me that Saul was dead, thinking he was bringing good news, I seized him and put him to death at Ziklag—that was the reward I gave him for his tidings. 11How much more, then, when wicked men have murdered a righteous man in his own house on his bed, shall I not now demand his blood from your hand and purge you from the earth?" 12Then David commanded his young men, and they killed them, cut off their hands and feet, and hanged them beside the pool in Hebron. But they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in Abner's tomb at Hebron.
