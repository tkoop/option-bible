The Remnant Rejects the LORD's Command

1When Jeremiah had finished telling all the people every word of the LORD their God—everything the LORD their God had sent him to tell them— 2Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the arrogant men said to Jeremiah, “You are speaking falsely. The LORD our God has not sent you to say, ‘Do not go to Egypt to reside there.’ 3Rather, Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, so that we may be handed over to the Chaldeans to be killed or exiled to Babylon.”

4So Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the forces, and all the people disobeyed the voice of the LORD and did not remain in the land of Judah. 5Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces took all the remnant of Judah who had returned from all the nations where they had been scattered, in order to dwell in the land of Judah— 6the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, and every person whom Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, as well as Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah. 7They entered the land of Egypt because they did not obey the voice of the LORD, and they went as far as Tahpanhes.

Jeremiah's Sign and Prophecy in Tahpanhes

8The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes: 9“Take some large stones in your hand and hide them in mortar in the brickwork at the entrance of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah. 10Then say to them, ‘Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will send for and take Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, MymyMy servant, and I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he will spread his royal canopy over them. 11He will come and strike the land of Egypt—those destined for death to death, those for captivity to captivity, and those for the sword to the sword. 12I will kindle a fire in the temples of the gods of Egypt, and he will burn them and carry them off. He will wrap himself in the land of Egypt as a shepherd wraps himself in his garment, and he will depart from there in peace. 13He will shatter the sacred pillars of Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt and burn down the temples of the gods of Egypt with fire.’”
