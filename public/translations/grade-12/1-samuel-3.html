The LORD Calls Samuel

1Now the youth Samuel was ministering to the LORD in Eli's presence, and in those days revelations from the LORD were scarce; visions were not commonly granted. 2By then Eli's eyesight had begun to fail, rendering him unable to see, and he was reclining in his customary place. 3The lamp of God had not yet been extinguished, and Samuel was reclining within the sanctuary of the LORD, where the ark of God rested.

4Then the LORD summoned Samuel, and he responded, "Here I am!"

5He hastened to Eli and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me."

But Eli said, "I did not call; lie down again." So Samuel went and lay down.

6And the LORD called again, "Samuel!"

Samuel arose, went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me."

But Eli said, "I did not call, my son; lie down again."

7Now Samuel had not yet come to know the LORD, nor had the word of the LORD yet been disclosed to him.

8The LORD summoned Samuel a third time. He arose, went to Eli, and said, "Here I am, for you summoned me." Then Eli discerned that the LORD was calling the boy.

9Therefore Eli instructed Samuel, "Go, lie down; and should HeheHe call you, you are to say, 'Speak, the LORD, for YouryourYour servant hears.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

10Then the LORD came and stood by him, calling as HeheHe had before, "Samuel! Samuel!"

And Samuel said, "Speak, for YouryourYour servant hears."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Eli's House

11Then the LORD said to Samuel, "Behold, I am about to accomplish a deed in Israel that will cause all who hear of it to shudder. 12On that day I will execute against Eli every word I have spoken concerning his household, from first to last. 13I have declared to him that I will judge his house in perpetuity because of the iniquity he acknowledged: his sons were blaspheming God, and he failed to restrain them. 14Therefore I have sworn to the house of Eli that the guilt of Eli's house shall never be expiated by sacrifice or offering."

15Samuel remained there until morning, when he opened the doors of the house of the LORD; yet he dreaded disclosing the vision to Eli.

Samuel Tells Eli the Vision
