God Presides Over the Divine Assembly

1God presides in the divine assembly; HeheHe judges among the gods. 2“How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah.

A Mandate to Defend the Vulnerable

3Defend the weak and the fatherless; vindicate the afflicted and the needy. 4Rescue the weak and needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked.

5They know nothing; they understand nothing. They walk in darkness, and all the foundations of the earth are shaken.

The Mortality of Those Called Gods

6I said, “You are gods; you are all sons of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 7Yet you will die like mortals; you will fall like any prince.”

A Prayer for Universal Justice

8Arise, O God, and judge the earth, for YouyouYou will inherit all the nations.
