Josiah's Early Reforms and the Removal of Idolatry

1Josiah was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. 2He did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD and walked in the ways of David his father, without turning aside to the right or to the left. 3In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a youth, he began to seek the God of David his father; and in the twelfth year he began to purge Judah and Jerusalem of the high places, the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images. 4In his presence they tore down the altars of the Baals; the incense altars that stood above them he cut down; and the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images he smashed and ground to powder, then scattered the dust over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them. 5He burned the bones of the priests on their altars and so cleansed Judah and Jerusalem. 6He did the same in the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim, and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, and in the ruins all around them. 7He broke down the altars and beat the Asherah poles to pieces; he pulverized the carved images and cut down all the incense altars throughout the land of Israel, and then returned to Jerusalem.

Repairing the Temple and Discovering the Law

8In the eighteenth year of his reign, after purifying the land and the temple, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city, and Joah son of Joahaz the recorder, to repair the house of the the LORD his God. 9They came to Hilkiah the high priest and gave him the money that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites who guarded the threshold had collected from Manasseh, Ephraim, and the whole remnant of Israel, from all Judah and Benjamin, and from the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 10They put it into the hands of the workmen appointed to oversee the house of the the LORD, and these workmen gave it to those who were repairing the house of the the LORD, to restore and strengthen it. 11They gave it to the carpenters and builders to purchase quarried stone and timber for braces, and to floor the houses that the kings of Judah had destroyed. 12The men did the work faithfully. Over them were appointed the Levites Jahath and Obadiah of the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam of the sons of Kohath, to supervise; and the other Levites, all of them skilled musicians, 13were over the burden bearers and directed all who did the work in every service; and some of the Levites were scribes, officers, and gatekeepers.

14When they brought out the money that had been brought into the house of the the LORD, Hilkiah the priest found the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the the LORD given by Moses. 15Hilkiah said to Shaphan the scribe, "I have found the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw in the house of the the LORD." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan. 16Shaphan brought the book to the king and reported, "YouryourYour servants are carrying out everything entrusted to them." 17They melted down the money found in the house of the the LORD and handed it over to the overseers and the workmen. 18Then Shaphan the scribe told the king, "Hilkiah the priest has given me a book." And Shaphan read from it before the king. 19When the king heard the words of the LawlawLaw, he tore his clothes. 20The king commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king's servant, saying, 21"Go and inquire of the the LORD for me and for the remnant in Israel and Judah concerning the words of the book that has been found, for great is the wrath of the the LORD that has been poured out on us, because our fathers did not keep the word of the the LORD, to do all that is written in this book."

Huldah's Prophetic Oracle

22So Hilkiah and those the king had sent went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath son of Hasrah, keeper of the wardrobe; she lived in Jerusalem in the SecondsecondSecond Quarter. They spoke to her about this. 23She said to them, "Thus says the the LORD, the God of Israel: Tell the man who sent you to me, 24Thus says the the LORD: I am bringing disaster on this place and on its inhabitants—all the curses written in the book that was read before the king of Judah. 25Because they have forsaken MemeMe and burned incense to other gods, provoking MemeMe to anger by all the works of their hands, MymyMy wrath will be poured out on this place and will not be quenched.

26But to the king of Judah who sent you to inquire of the the LORD, you shall say this: Thus says the the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning the words you have heard: 27Because your heart was tender and you humbled yourself before God when you heard HishisHis words against this place and its inhabitants, and because you humbled yourself before MemeMe and tore your clothes and wept before MemeMe, I have heard you, declares the the LORD. 28Behold, I will gather you to your fathers, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace; your eyes will not see all the disaster I am bringing on this place and on its inhabitants.' Then they brought this word back to the king.

Josiah Renews the Covenant with the LORD

29The king sent and gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem. 30The king went up to the house of the the LORD with all the men of Judah, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests, the Levites, and all the people, from the greatest to the least; and he read in their hearing all the words of the BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant that had been found in the house of the the LORD. 31The king stood in his place and made a covenant before the the LORD to walk after the the LORD and to keep HishisHis commandments, HishisHis testimonies, and HishisHis statutes with all his heart and all his soul, and to perform the words of the covenant written in this book. 32He made all who were found in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand to it. The inhabitants of Jerusalem acted according to the covenant of God, the God of their fathers. 33Josiah removed all the abominations from all the lands belonging to the Israelites, and he made all who were found in Israel serve the the LORD their God. All his days they did not turn aside from following the the LORD, the God of their fathers.
