A Call to Worship the LORD

1Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. 2Sing to the LORD and bless HishisHis name; proclaim HishisHis salvation from day to day. 3Declare HishisHis glory among the nations and HishisHis wonders among all peoples.

4For the LORD is great and greatly to be praised; HeheHe is to be feared above all gods. 5For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 6SplendourSplendourSplendor and majesty stand before HimhimHim; strength and beauty fill HishisHis sanctuary.

7Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. 8Ascribe to the LORD the glory due HishisHis name; bring an offering and enter HishisHis courts. 9Worship the LORD in the splendoursplendoursplendor of holiness; tremble before HimhimHim, all the earth.

10Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity.”

11Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea roar and everything that fills it. 12Let the field exult and all that is in it; then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy 13before the LORD, for HeheHe is coming—for HeheHe is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in HishisHis faithfulness.
