Abram Returns to Bethel

1Abram went up from Egypt to the Negev—he, his wife, all that he owned, and Lot with him. 2Abram had become very wealthy in livestock, silver, and gold. 3He travelledtravelledtraveled in stages from the Negev to Bethel, to the place where his tent had first been pitched, between Bethel and Ai. 4He went to the site of the altar he had made earlier, and there Abram called on the name of the LORD.

Abram and Lot Part Ways

5Lot, who travelledtravelledtraveled with Abram, also had flocks, herds, and tents. 6The land could not sustain both of them dwelling together, because their possessions were so abundant that they could not remain together. 7Quarreling arose between Abram's herdsmen and Lot's herdsmen, and at that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land.

8Abram said to Lot, "Please let there be no strife between me and you, between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are brothers. 9Is not the whole land before you? Please separate from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; if you go to the right, I will go to the left."

10Lot lifted his eyes and saw the whole plain of the Jordan, that it was well watered throughout—before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah—like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as you come to Zoar. 11So Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan, and he journeyed eastward; thus they separated from each other. 12Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled in the cities of the plain and pitched his tent as far as Sodom. 13Now the men of Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful against the LORD.

God's Promise to Abram

14After Lot had parted from him, the LORD said to Abram, "Lift up your eyes from the place where you are and look northward and southward, eastward and westward. 15For all the land that you see I will give to you and your offspring forever. 16I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone can count the dust of the earth, your offspring also can be counted. 17Arise and walk through the land, its length and its breadth, for I will give it to you."

18So Abram moved his tent and came and settled by the oaks of Mamre in Hebron, and there he built an altar to the LORD.
