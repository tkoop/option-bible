The Flying Scroll of JudgmentJudgementJudgment

1I turned once more, lifted my eyes, and beheld a scroll flying through the air.

2He asked me, "What do you see?"

I replied, "I see a flying scroll, twenty cubits in length and ten cubits in width."

3He then said to me, "This is the curse that goes forth over the face of the whole land: every thief shall be purged by it, and every perjurer shall be purged by it."

4"I have sent it out," declares the LORD of Hosts, "and it shall enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by MymyMy name. It shall lodge in the midst of his house and consume it—both its timber and its stones."

The Ephah and the Woman Called Wickedness

5Then the angel who was speaking with me came forward and said, "Lift your eyes and see what this is that goes out."

6I asked, "What is it?"

He replied, "This is the ephah that is going out." He added, "This is their iniquity throughout all the earth."

7And behold, a leaden cover was lifted, revealing a woman sitting inside the ephah.

8He said, "This is Wickedness." Then he thrust her down into the ephah and cast the leaden stone upon its mouth.

Wickedness Carried Away to Shinar

9I lifted my eyes again and saw two women coming forth, with wind in their wings; they had wings like those of a stork, and they lifted the ephah between Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.

10I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "Where are they taking the ephah?"

11He answered, "To build a house for it in the land of Shinar; and when it has been prepared, it will be set there upon its base."
