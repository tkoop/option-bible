The LORD Speaks to the Jews in Egypt

1The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews living in the land of Egypt—at Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and in the land of Pathros—saying:

2“This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: You have seen all the disaster I brought on Jerusalem and on all the cities of Judah. They lie in ruins today, without inhabitant, 3because of the wickedness they committed, provoking MemeMe to anger by burning incense and serving other gods that neither they nor you nor your fathers ever knew. 4Yet I sent you all MymyMy servants the prophets again and again, saying, ‘Do not do this detestable thing that I hate.’ 5But they did not listen or incline their ear; they did not turn from their wickedness or stop burning incense to other gods. 6Therefore MymyMy wrath and anger poured out and burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, so that they became the desolate ruin they are today.”

7“Now this is what the LORD, the God of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Why are you bringing such great disaster on yourselves by cutting off from Judah man and woman, child and infant, leaving yourselves without a remnant? 8Why are you provoking MemeMe to anger with the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt where you have gone to reside? You will be cut off and become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth. 9Have you forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, the wickedness of the kings of Judah, the wickedness of their wives, your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem? 10To this day they have not humbled themselves or shown reverence, nor have they followed MymyMy law or MymyMy statutes that I set before you and your fathers.”

11“Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am determined to bring disaster on you and to cut off all Judah. 12I will take away the remnant of Judah who have set their faces to go to Egypt to reside there; they will all perish in Egypt. They will fall by the sword and die by famine. From the least to the greatest, they will die by sword and famine. They will become an object of cursing and horror, of condemnation and reproach. 13I will punish those who live in Egypt, just as I punished Jerusalem, by sword, famine, and plague, 14so that none of the remnant of Judah who have gone to reside in Egypt will escape or survive to return to the land of Judah, except a few fugitives.”

The People Refuse to Heed Jeremiah

15Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense to other gods, and all the women standing by—a great assembly—and all the people living in Pathros in the land of Egypt, answered Jeremiah:

16“As for the word you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you! 17Instead, we will surely do whatever we have promised: We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, just as we, our fathers, our kings, and our officials did in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Then we had plenty of food, were well off, and saw no disaster. 18But from the time we stopped burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything and have been consumed by sword and famine.”

19And the women added, “When we burned incense to the Queen of Heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, did we make her cakes in her image and pour out drink offerings to her without our husbands’ knowledge?”

Jeremiah Answers the People

20Then Jeremiah said to all the people, both men and women, who were answering him: 21“As for the incense you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem—you, your fathers, your kings, your officials, and the people of the land—did not the LORD remember and bring it to mind? 22So the LORD could no longer endure it, because of your evil deeds and the abominations you committed. Therefore your land has become a desolation, a horror, and a curse, without inhabitant, as it is this day. 23Because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD, and did not obey the voice of the LORD or walk in HishisHis law, HishisHis statutes, and HishisHis testimonies, this disaster has befallen you, as it is this day.”

The LORD’s Final Word Against the Remnant

24Then Jeremiah said to all the people and all the women, “Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who are in the land of Egypt. 25This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: You and your wives have spoken with your mouths and fulfilled with your hands your promise, ‘We will surely fulfillfulfilfulfill our vows that we have made to burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.’ So go ahead, then! Keep your vows and carry them out!”

26“But hear this word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who live in the land of Egypt: I have sworn by MymyMy great name, says the LORD, that MymyMy name will never again be invoked by anyone of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, ‘As surely as the Lord the LORD lives.’ 27I am watching over them for harm and not for good, and every man of Judah who is in the land of Egypt will meet his end by sword or famine until they are finished. 28Yet a small number who escape the sword will return from Egypt to the land of Judah, and all the remnant of Judah who went to dwell in Egypt will know whose word will stand, MinemineMine or theirs.”

29“This will be a sign to you, declares the LORD, that I will punish you in this place, so that you will know that MymyMy words of doom will surely come to pass against you. 30This is what the LORD says: I am going to hand Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt over to his enemies, to those who seek his life, just as I handed Zedekiah king of Judah over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy who was seeking his life.”
