The LORD Confronts Jerusalem

1The word of the LORD came to me: 2“Son of man, confront Jerusalem with her abominations.”

3Say, “Thus says the Lord GOD to Jerusalem: Your lineage and your nativity are rooted in the land of the Canaanites; your father was an Amorite and your mother a Hittite.

4On the day of your birth, your umbilical cord remained uncut, nor were you cleansed with water. You were neither salted nor swaddled. 5No eye pitied you enough to perform even one of these offices for you out of compassion. Rather, you were abandoned in the open field, held in contempt on the very day of your birth.

6Then I passed by and beheld you weltering in your blood, and as you lay in your blood I declared to you, ‘Live!’ 7I caused you to thrive like a field plant. You grew, matured, and became surpassingly beautiful; your breasts were formed and your hair grew, though you were still naked and bare.

8Then I passed by and saw you, and behold, your time had come, the time for love. So I spread MymyMy cloak over you and covered your nakedness. I swore to you and entered into a covenant with you, and you became MinemineMine, declares the Lord GOD. 9Then I bathed you with water, washed your blood from you, and anointed you with oil.

10I clothed you in embroidered cloth and shod you with sandals of fine leather. I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk. 11I bedecked you with jewelleryjewelleryjewelry and put bracelets on your wrists and a chain around your neck. 12I put a ring on your nose, earrings on your ears, and a splendid crown on your head.

13So you were decked with gold and silver, and your clothing was fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey, and oil. You grew surpassingly beautiful and rose to queenship. 14Your renown spread among the nations on account of your beauty, for it was flawless in the splendoursplendoursplendor I had conferred upon you, declares the Lord GOD.

Jerusalem’s Unfaithfulness

15But you reposed confidence in your beauty and played the harlot, and you squandered your charms upon every passerby, so that your beauty became theirs. 16You took some of your garments and fashioned them into garish shrines, and on them you prostituted yourself. Such things ought never to have transpired; indeed, they should not have occurred at all.

17You also took the fine jewelleryjewelleryjewelry of gold and silver that I had given you, and you fashioned male idols from them, prostituting yourself with them. 18You took your embroidered garments to cover them, and you set MymyMy oil and MymyMy incense before them. 19You also placed before them the food I had given you—the fine flour, oil, and honey with which I nourished you—and presented it to them as a fragrant offering, declares the Lord GOD.

20You even took the sons and daughters you bore to MemeMe and sacrificed them as sustenance to idols. Was your harlotry not sufficient? 21You slaughtered MymyMy children and passed them through the fire to idols. 22And amid all your abominations and harlotries, you did not recall the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, weltering in your blood.

23‘Alas, alas for you!’ declares the Lord GOD. And after all your wickedness— 24you erected a mound for yourself and established a high place in every square. 25At the head of every street you built your high places and prostituted your beauty, offering yourself to every passerby and multiplying your harlotries.

26You prostituted yourself with your libidinous neighbors, the Egyptians, and escalated your promiscuity to provoke MemeMe to anger. 27Therefore I stretched out MymyMy hand against you and reduced your portion.
