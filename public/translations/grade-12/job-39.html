The Mountain Goats and Does

“1Do you know when the mountain goats give birth? Do you watch over the does as they calve? 2Can you count the months they carry to term? Do you know the time they give birth? 3They crouch, they bring forth their young, and they cast off their birth pangs. 4Their young grow strong in the wild; they leave and do not return to them.

The Wild Donkey and the Wild Ox

“5Who set the wild donkey free? Who untied the bonds of the wild ass, 6to whom I have made the wilderness a home and the salt flats a dwelling? 7He scorns the uproar of the town and does not hear the driver's shouts. 8The mountains are his pasture, and he seeks out every green thing.

“9Will the wild ox consent to serve you? Will he stay by your manger? 10Can you bind the wild ox with a rope in the furrow? Or will he plow the valleys behind you? 11Will you trust him because his strength is great? Or will you leave your toil to him? 12Will you rely on him to bring your grain home and gather it to your threshing floor?

The Ostrich's Folly

“13The ostrich's wings flap joyously, but are they the pinions and plumage of a stork? 14For she leaves her eggs on the ground and warms them in the dust, 15forgetting that a foot may crush them or that a wild beast may trample them. 16She treats her young harshly, as though they were not her own; her labourlabourlabor is in vain, and she feels no fear. 17For God has deprived her of wisdom and has not imparted understanding to her. 18Yet when she rears herself up, she scorns the horse and its rider.

The Horse and the Birds of Prey

“19Do you give the horse his strength? Do you clothe his neck with a mane? 20Can you make him leap like a locust? His majestic snorting is terrifying. 21He paws in the valley and exults in his strength; he charges out to meet the weapons. 22He mocks fear and is not dismayed; he does not turn back from the sword. 23The quiver rattles against him, along with the flashing spear and javelin. 24In frenzy and rage he races across the ground; he does not halt at the trumpet's sound. 25At the sound of the trumpet he cries, 'Aha!' HeheHe scents the battle from afar—the thunder of commanders and the shouting.

“26Does the hawk soar by your wisdom and spread her wings toward the south? 27Does the eagle soar at your command and build her nest on high? 28She dwells on the rock and lodges there, on the crag and the stronghold. 29From there she spies out food; her eyes see it from afar. 30Her young feed on blood, and where the slain are, there she is.”
