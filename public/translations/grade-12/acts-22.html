The Crowd Turns Against Paul

22They listened to him up to this statement, and then they raised their voices and said, “Away with such a fellow from the earth, for he is not fit to live!” 23As they were shouting and throwing off their cloaks and tossing dust into the air, 24the commander ordered that he be brought into the barracks and examined under scourging, so that he might learn the reason they were shouting against him like this.

Paul's Roman Citizenship

25But as they stretched him out for the whips, Paul said to the centurion standing there, “Is it lawful for you to flog a man who is a Roman citizen and uncondemned?”

26When the centurion heard this, he went to the commander and reported it. “What are you about to do?” he asked. “This man is a Roman citizen.”

27The commander went to Paul and asked, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?”

“Yes,” he answered.

28“I paid a large sum for this citizenship,” said the commander.

“But I was born a citizen,” Paul replied.

29At once those who were about to examine him withdrew from him, and the commander was also afraid when he realizedrealisedrealized that Paul was a Roman citizen and that he had bound him. 30The next day, wishing to know for certain why he was being accused by the Jews, the commander released him and ordered the chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin to assemble. Then he brought Paul down and set him before them.
