Ben-Hadad Besieges Samaria

1Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, gathered his entire army, and thirty-two kings marched with him, along with horses and chariots. He advanced, besieged Samaria, and attacked it. 2He sent messengers into the city to Ahab, king of Israel.

3They said to him, "This is what Ben-Hadad says: 'YouryourYour silver and gold are mine, and your best wives and children are mine as well.'"

4ThetheThe king of Israel answered, "As you say, my lord the king, I am yours, and all that I have is yours."

5The messengers returned and said, "This is what Ben-Hadad says: 'I already sent to you, saying, "Give me your silver and gold, your wives and children."'"

6"But tomorrow at this time I will send my servants to you. They will search your house and your servants' houses and take everything you value most."

7Then the king of Israel summoned all the elders of the land and said, "Please take note and see that this man is seeking trouble, for he has demanded my wives, my children, my silver, and my gold, and I did not refuse him."

8All the elders and the people said to him, "Do not listen to him or consent."

9So he said to Ben-Hadad's messengers, "Tell my lord the king, 'All that you first demanded of your servant I will do, but this I cannot do.'" ThetheThe messengers departed and brought this reply back to him.

10Then Ben-Hadad sent word to him and said, "May the gods do so to me, and even more, if the dust of Samaria is enough to provide a handful for each of the people who follow me."

11The king of Israel answered, "ThetheThe man who is just buckling on his armourarmourarmor should not boast like the man who is taking it off."

12When Ben-Hadad received this message, he and the kings who were helping him were drinking in temporary shelters. He said to his servants, "Prepare for battle," and they advanced against the city.

The LORD Gives Israel Its First Victory

13Then a prophet approached Ahab, king of Israel, and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'Have you seen all this great multitude? Behold, I will give it into your hand today, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

14Ahab asked, "By whom?"

The prophet replied, "This is what the LORD says: 'By the young officers of the provincial governors.'"

Ahab asked, "Who will begin the battle?"

The prophet answered, "YouyouYou."

15So he mustered the young officers of the provincial governors, and they numbered two hundred thirty-two. After them he mustered all the people of Israel, seven thousand men.

16They marched out at noon, while Ben-Hadad was drinking himself drunk in the shelters, along with the thirty-two kings who were helping him.

17The young officers of the provincial governors led the advance. Ben-Hadad sent out scouts, and they reported, "Men are coming out of Samaria."

18He said, "If they have come out for peace, capture them alive; if they have come out for war, capture them alive."

19So those young officers led the advance, and the army followed them.

20Each man struck down his opponent. The Arameans fled, and Israel pursued them. Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, escaped on horseback with the cavalry.

21Then the king of Israel went out and struck the horses and chariots, inflicting a severe defeat on Aram.

22Afterward the prophet approached the king of Israel and said to him, "Go, strengthen yourself, and carefully consider what you must do, for at the turn of the year the king of Aram will march against you."

The Arameans' Second Campaign

23Meanwhile the servants of the king of Aram said to him, "TheirtheirTheir gods are gods of the hills, which is why they overpowered us. But if we fight them on the plain, we will surely overpower them."

24"Do this: remove the kings from their commands and appoint governors in their place. 25Then muster an army like the force you lost—horse for horse and chariot for chariot—and we will fight them on the plain. Surely we will overpower them." So he listened to their advice and did so.

26At the turn of the year, Ben-Hadad mustered the Arameans and went up to Aphek to fight against Israel.

27The Israelites were also mustered and provisioned, and they went to meet them. The Israelites camped opposite them like two small flocks of goats, while the Arameans filled the land.

28Then the man of God approached the king of Israel and said, "This is what the LORD says: 'Because the Arameans said, "ThetheThe LORD is a god of the hills, but HeheHe is not a god of the valleys," I will give all this great multitude into your hand, and you will know that I am the LORD.'"

29For seven days they camped opposite each other. On the seventh day the battle was joined, and the Israelites struck down one hundred thousand Aramean foot soldiers in a single day. 30The rest fled into the city of Aphek, but the wall collapsed on twenty-seven thousand of the survivors. Ben-Hadad also fled and hid in an inner room of the city.

31His servants said to him, "Look, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are merciful. Let us put sackcloth around our waists and ropes around our heads and go out to the king of Israel. Perhaps he will spare your life."

32So they put sackcloth around their waists and ropes around their heads, went to the king of Israel, and said, "YouryourYour servant Ben-Hadad says, 'Please let me live.'"

HeheHe asked, "IsisIs he still alive? He is my brother."

33The men seized on this as a good omen and quickly said, "YouryourYour brother Ben-Hadad!"

Ahab said, "Go and bring him." So Ben-Hadad came out to him, and Ahab had him ride in the chariot.

34Ben-Hadad said to him, "I will restore the cities my father took from your father, and you may establish marketplaces for yourself in Damascus, as my father did in Samaria."

Ahab replied, "On these terms I will release you," and he made a covenant with him and sent him away.

The Prophet Condemns Ahab

35By the word of the LORD, one of the sons of the prophets said to his companion, "Strike me, please." But the man refused to strike him.

36Then he said to him, "Because you did not obey the voice of the LORD, as soon as you leave me a lion will strike you down." And as soon as he left him, a lion found him and struck him down.

37Then he found another man and said, "Strike me, please." The man struck him and wounded him.

38So the prophet went and waited for the king by the road, disguising himself with a bandage over his eyes.

39As the king passed by, he cried out to him, "YouryourYour servant went out into the thick of the battle, and someone turned aside and brought a man to me and said, 'Guard this man. If he is missing, your life will be for his life, or you must pay a talent of silver.' But while your servant was busy here and there, the man disappeared."

40The king of Israel said to him, "So your judgmentjudgementjudgment stands; you yourself have decided it."

41Then he quickly removed the bandage from his eyes, and the king of Israel recognizedrecogniserecognized him as one of the prophets.

42He said to him, "This is what the LORD says: 'Because you let slip from your hand the man I had devoted to destruction, your life will be for his life, and your people for his people.'"

43So the king of Israel went to his house sullen and angry, and he came to Samaria.
