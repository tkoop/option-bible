Hoshea’s Reign and Assyria’s Conquest

1In the twelfth year of Ahaz’s reign over Judah, Hoshea son of Elah ascended to the throne of Israel in Samaria and reigned nine years. 2He perpetrated evil in the sight of the LORD, though not to the extent of the kings of Israel who had preceded him. 3Shalmaneser king of Assyria campaigned against him, and Hoshea became his vassal, paying him tribute.

4But the king of Assyria discovered Hoshea’s conspiracy—his envoys to So king of Egypt—and his failure to remit tribute as in previous years. Accordingly, the king of Assyria arrested and imprisoned him. 5The king of Assyria then overran the whole land, advanced upon Samaria, and besieged it for three years. 6In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported the Israelites to Assyria, settling them in Halah, in Gozan on the Habor River, and in the cities of the Medes.

Israel’s Sin and the LORD’s JudgmentJudgementJudgment

7All this transpired because the Israelites sinned against the LORD their God, who had brought them up from the land of Egypt, out from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt. They had venerated other gods 8and conformed to the customs of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the Israelites, as well as to the practices instituted by the kings of Israel.

9The Israelites clandestinely committed acts against the LORD their God that were not right. From watchtower to fortified city, they erected high places in every city. 10They set up sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and beneath every verdant tree.

11They burned incense on all the high places, as the nations had done whom the LORD had driven out before them, and they did wicked things, provoking the LORD. 12They served idols, although the LORD had said to them, “You shall not do this thing.”

13Yet through all HishisHis prophets and seers, the LORD warned Israel and Judah, saying, “Turn from your evil ways and keep MymyMy commandments and statutes, according to all the law that I commanded your fathers and delivered to you through MymyMy servants the prophets.”

14But they would not listen; rather, they stiffened their necks like their fathers, who had not believed in the LORD their God. 15They rejected HishisHis statutes and the covenant HeheHe had made with their fathers, together with the decrees HeheHe had given them. They pursued worthless idols and thereby became worthless themselves; they followed the surrounding nations, although the LORD had commanded them not to imitate them.

16They abandoned all the commandments of the LORD their God and made for themselves two cast-metal calves. They made an Asherah pole, worshiped the whole host of Heavenheavenheaven, and served Baal. 17They sacrificed their sons and daughters in the fire and practisedpractisedpracticed divination and soothsaying. 18So the LORD was exceedingly angry with Israel, and HeheHe removed them from HishisHis presence; only the tribe of Judah remained. 19Yet even Judah did not keep the commandments of the LORD their God, but followed the practices Israel had introduced. 20Therefore the LORD rejected all the descendants of Israel. He afflicted them and delivered them into the hands of plunderers, until HeheHe had banished them from HishisHis presence.

So Israel was carried away from its land to Assyria, where they remain to this day.

Foreign Settlers and the Mixed Worship of Samaria

24Then the king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the Israelites. They took possession of Samaria and settled in its cities. 25At the beginning of their residence there, they did not fear the LORD, so the LORD sent lions among them, and the lions killed some of them.

26So they spoke to the king of Assyria, saying, “The people you have resettled in the cities of Samaria do not know the customs of the God of the land. Therefore HeheHe has sent lions among them, and the lions are killing them.”

27Then the king of Assyria commanded, “Send back one of the priests you carried off from there, and let him go and live there and teach them the customs of the God of the land.”

28So one of the priests who had been carried away from Samaria came and lived in Bethel, and he taught them how to fear the LORD. 29Nevertheless, the people of each nation continued to make their own gods and placed them in the shrines on the high places that the Samaritans had made. Each nation made gods in the cities where it lived.

30The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth; the men of Cuth made Nergal; the men of Hamath made Ashima. 31The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32So they feared the LORD, but they also appointed priests from among themselves for the high places, and those priests sacrificed for them in the shrines of the high places. 33They feared the LORD, yet they also served their own gods according to the customs of the nations from which they had been carried away. 34To this day they persist in their former practices: they do not fear the LORD, nor do they follow the statutes, ordinances, law, and commandments that the LORD gave to the descendants of Jacob, whom HeheHe named Israel.

35The LORD had made a covenant with them and commanded them: “Do not fear other gods; do not bow down to them; do not serve them; and do not sacrifice to them. 36Instead, fear the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm. Bow down to HimhimHim and sacrifice to HimhimHim. 37And you must carefully observe the statutes, ordinances, law, and commandments HeheHe wrote for you. Do not fear other gods. 38Do not forget the covenant I have made with you, and do not fear other gods. 39Rather, fear the LORD your God, and HeheHe will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies.”

40But they would not listen; instead, they persisted in their former practices. 41So these nations feared the LORD, but they also served their carved images. To this day their children and grandchildren continue to do as their fathers did.
