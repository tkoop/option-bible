Joy Amid Trials and Prophetic Hope

6In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials, 7so that the proven genuineness of your faith—more precious than gold, which perishes though refined by fire—may be found to result in praise, glory, and honourhonourhonor at the revelation of Jesus Christ. 8Though you have not seen HimhimHim, you love HimhimHim; and though you do not now see HimhimHim, you believe in HimhimHim and rejoice with an inexpressible and glorious joy, 9receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

Holiness and Redemption by Precious Blood

13Therefore, with minds alert and fully sober, set your hope completely on the grace to be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ. 14As obedient children, do not be conformed to the passions of your former ignorance. 15But just as HeheHe who called you is holy, so be holy in all your conduct, 16for it is written, “Be holy, because I am holy.”

17If you call on the FatherfatherFather who judges each one’s work impartially, conduct yourselves in reverent fear during the time of your sojourn, 18knowing that you were ransomed from the futile way of life handed down from your forefathers, not with perishable things such as silver or gold, 19but with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish or spot. 20He was foreknown before the foundation of the world, but was revealed in these last times for your sake. 21Through HimhimHim you believe in God, who raised HimhimHim from the dead and glorified HimhimHim, so that your faith and hope are in God.

Regeneration Through the Enduring Word

22Having purified your souls by obedience to the truth, so that you have sincere love for the brothers, love one another earnestly from a pure heart. 23For you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.

24For “All flesh is like grass, and all its glory like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls, 25but the word of the LORD endures forever.” And this is the word that was preached to you.
