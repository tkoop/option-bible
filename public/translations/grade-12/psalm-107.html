Thanksgiving for the LORD's Enduring Love

1Give thanks to the the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis faithful love endures forever. 2Let the redeemed of the the LORD proclaim it—those HeheHe ransomed from the hand of the foe. 3He gathered them from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.

4Some wandered in the wilderness, in a barren wasteland, and found no road to an inhabited city. 5Hungry and thirsty, their spirits failed within them. 6Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe delivered them from their distress. 7He led them along a straight path until they reached a city where they could settle.

8Let them thank the the LORD for HishisHis faithful love, for HishisHis wonders to humankind. 9For HeheHe satisfies the longing soul and fills the hungry with good things.

10Some sat in darkness and mortal gloom, prisoners in misery and chains, 11because they had rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 12So HeheHe humbled their hearts with hard labourlabourlabor; they stumbled, and no one came to help.

13Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them from their distress. 14He brought them out of darkness and mortal gloom and shattered their bonds.

15Let them thank the the LORD for HishisHis faithful love, for HishisHis wonders to humankind. 16For HeheHe shattered bronze doors and severed iron bars.

17Some became fools through their rebellious ways and suffered affliction because of their sins. 18They loathed all food and drew near to the brink of death.

19Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them from their distress. 20He sent HishisHis word and healed them, rescuing them from destruction.

21Let them thank the the LORD for HishisHis faithful love, for HishisHis wonders to humankind. 22Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and proclaim HishisHis deeds with joyful songs.

23Some went down to the sea in ships, carrying on trade upon the great waters. 24They saw the works of the the LORD and HishisHis wonders in the deep. 25For HeheHe spoke and stirred up a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea. 26They rose to the heavens and plunged to the depths; their courage melted in the crisis. 27They staggered and reeled like drunkards, and all their skill availed nothing.

28Then they cried out to the the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe brought them out of their distress. 29He stilled the storm and hushed the waves. 30Then they rejoiced in the calm, and HeheHe guided them to the harbourharbourharbor they longed for.

31Let them thank the the LORD for HishisHis faithful love, for HishisHis wonders to humankind. 32Let them exalt HimhimHim in the congregation of the people and praise HimhimHim in the assembly of the elders.

33He turns rivers into desert and springs of water into thirsty ground, 34a fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it. 35He turns a desert into pools of water and parched land into springs of water.

36There HeheHe settles the hungry, and they build a city to live in. 37They sow fields and plant vineyards, and they gather a fruitful harvest. 38He blesses them so that they multiply greatly, and HeheHe does not let their herds diminish.

39Yet when they are diminished and brought low by oppression, calamity, and sorrow, 40HeheHe pours contempt on princes and makes them wander in trackless wastes. 41But HeheHe lifts the needy out of affliction and makes their families like flocks. 42The upright see it and rejoice, while all wickedness is silenced.

43Whoever is wise, let him heed these things and consider the faithful love of the the LORD.
