A Famine and the Gibeonites' Request

1During the reign of David there was a famine for three successive years, and David sought the the LORD's guidance. And the the LORD said, "It is because of Saul and his bloodstained house, because he put the Gibeonites to death."

2So the king summoned the Gibeonites and spoke to them. (Now the Gibeonites were not Israelites, but a remnant of the Amorites. The Israelites had sworn an oath to them, but Saul had sought to kill them in his zeal for the Israelites and Judah.)

3So David asked the Gibeonites, "What shall I do for you? How can I make restitution so that you may bless the inheritance of the the LORD?"

4The Gibeonites said to him, "We do not seek silver or gold from Saul or his house, nor do we demand that any man in Israel be put to death."

"Whatever you say, I will do for you," David replied.

5"The man who devastated us and plotted against us to exterminate us from existing within any border of Israel—

6"let seven of his male descendants be given to us so that we may hang them before the the LORD at Gibeah of Saul, the chosen of the the LORD."

"I will give them to you," said the king.

The Execution of Saul's Descendants and Rizpah's Vigil

7Now the king spared Mephibosheth son of Jonathan son of Saul, because of the oath before the the LORD between David and Jonathan son of Saul.

8But the king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the two sons whom Rizpah daughter of Aiah had borne to Saul, along with the five sons of Michal daughter of Saul, whom she had borne to Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.

9And he delivered them into the hands of the Gibeonites, who hanged them on the hill before the the LORD. So the seven of them fell together; they were put to death in the first days of the harvest, at the beginning of the barley harvest.

10And Rizpah daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and spread it out for herself on the rock from the beginning of the harvest until the rain poured down from Heavenheavenheaven upon them. She did not allow the birds of the air to settle on them by day, nor the beasts of the field by night.

David Retrieves and Buries Saul's Bones

11When David was told what Rizpah daughter of Aiah, the concubine of Saul, had done, 12he went and took the bones of Saul and of his son Jonathan from the men of Jabesh-gilead, who had stolen them from the public square of Beth-shan, where the Philistines had hung them the day the Philistines struck down Saul at Gilboa.

13So David brought up the bones of Saul and of his son Jonathan from there, and they also gathered the bones of those who had been hanged. 14And they buried the bones of Saul and of his son Jonathan in Zela in the land of Benjamin, in the tomb of Kish his father. After they had done everything the king had commanded, God answered prayers for the land.

David's Final Battles with the Philistines

15Once again war broke out between the Philistines and Israel. David and his men went down and fought against the Philistines, and David became exhausted.

16Then Ishbi-benob, one of the descendants of Rapha, whose bronze spear weighed three hundred shekels and who was carrying a new sword, resolved to kill David.

17But Abishai son of Zeruiah came to his rescue, struck the Philistine, and killed him. Then David's men swore to him, "YouyouYou will never again go out with us to battle, so that Israel's guiding light will not be extinguished."

18After this, there was another battle with the Philistines at Gob, and Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Saph, one of the descendants of Rapha. 19Once again there was a battle with the Philistines at Gob, and Elhanan son of Jaare-oregim the Bethlehemite struck down Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver's beam.

20And there was still another battle at Gath, where there was a man of great stature who had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He too was descended from Rapha. 21And when he taunted Israel, Jonathan son of Shimei, David's brother, struck him down. 22So these four were born to Rapha in Gath, and they were struck down by David and his men.
