Oracle Concerning the Wilderness of the Sea

1An oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea: As whirlwinds sweep through the Negev, so it advances from the wilderness, from a land of terror. 2A harsh vision has been declared to me: the traitor betrays, and the ravager ravages. Go up, Elam; besiege, Media; I have brought all her sighing to an end.

3Therefore my loins are filled with anguish; pangs seize me like the pangs of a woman in labourlabourlabor. I am so bewildered that I cannot hear; I am so dismayed that I cannot see. 4My heart reels; dread terrifies me. The twilight I longed for has been turned into trembling for me.

5They prepare the banquet, they post the watch, they eat, they drink. Arise, princes; anoint the shield!

The Watchman's Vision of Babylon's Fall

6For thus the Lord said to me: "Go, set a watchman; let him report what he sees." 7He sees chariots, pairs of horsemen, chariots of donkeys, and chariots of camels; and he listens with meticulous attention.

8Then he cried like a lion: "O Lord, I stand on my watchtower continually by day, and at my post I am stationed every night."

9And behold, here comes a chariot of men, with a pair of horsemen. And he answered and said, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon, and all the carved images of her gods he has shattered to the ground."

10O my people, crushed on the threshing floor, I declare to you what I have heard from the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel.

Oracle Concerning Dumah

11An oracle concerning Dumah. A voice calls to me from Seir: "Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?"

12The watchman says, "MorningmorningMorning comes, and also the night. If you will inquire, inquire; return again."

Oracle Concerning Arabia

13An oracle concerning Arabia. In the thickets of Arabia you will lodge, O caravans of Dedanites. 14Bring water to the thirsty; meet the fugitive with bread, O inhabitants of the land of Tema. 15For they have fled from the swords, from the drawn sword, from the bent bow, and from the weight of battle.

16For thus the Lord said to me: "Within a year, according to the years of a hired worker, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end." 17And the remainder of the number of archers, the mighty men of the sons of Kedar, will be few; for the LORD God of Israel has spoken.
