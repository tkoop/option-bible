Praise for the LORD’s Wondrous Works

1You, the LORD, are my God. I will exalt YouyouYou; I will praise YouryourYour name, for YouyouYou have done wonders—plans formed long ago in perfect faithfulness. 2For YouyouYou have made the city a heap, the fortified city a ruin; the palace of foreigners is a city no more, and it will never be rebuilt. 3Therefore a strong people glorify YouyouYou, and cities of ruthless nations fear YouyouYou.

4For YouyouYou have been a refuge to the poor, a refuge to the needy in his distress, a shelter from the storm and a shade from the heat; for the breath of the ruthless is like a storm against a wall. 5Like heat in a parched land, YouyouYou subdue the uproar of foreigners; like heat in the shade of a cloud, the song of the ruthless is silenced.

The LORD Prepares a Feast for All Peoples

6On this mountain the LORD of Hosts will prepare a feast of rich food for all peoples, a banquet of aged wine—rich food full of marrow, aged wine well refined. 7On this mountain HeheHe will swallow up the shroud that enfolds all peoples, the veil that is spread over all nations. 8He will swallow up death forever. The Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces, and the reproach of HishisHis people HeheHe will remove from all the earth, for the LORD has spoken.

9In that day they will say, “Surely this is our God; we trusted in HimhimHim, and HeheHe saved us. This is the LORD; we trusted in HimhimHim. Let us rejoice and be glad in HishisHis salvation.”

Moab’s Humiliation

10For the hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain, but Moab will be trampled in his place as straw is trampled in the dung pile. 11He will spread out his hands within it, as a swimmer spreads out his hands to swim; his pride will be brought low, along with the skill of his hands. 12He has brought down your high walls and fortresses; HeheHe has brought them down to the ground, to the dust.
