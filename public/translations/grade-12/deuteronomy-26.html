The Offering of Firstfruits

1When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and dwell in it, 2you shall take some of the first of all the fruit of the ground that you harvest from your land, which the LORD your God is giving you, place it in a basket, and go to the place the LORD your God will choose for HishisHis name to dwell. 3You shall go to the priest who is in office in those days and say to him, “I declare today to the LORD your God that I have come into the land the LORD swore to our fathers to give us.” 4Then the priest shall take the basket from your hand and set it before the altar of the LORD your God.

Reciting Israel's Deliverance

5You shall respond before the LORD your God, “My father was a wandering Aramean. He went down into Egypt and sojourned there with only a few people, and there he became a nation—great, mighty, and numerous. 6But the Egyptians treated us harshly and afflicted us, and they laid heavy labourlabourlabor upon us. 7Then we cried out to the LORD, the God of our fathers, and the LORD heard our voice and saw our affliction, our toil, and our oppression.

8“The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, and with signs and wonders. 9He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey. 10And now, behold, I have brought the firstfruits of the ground that YouyouYou, the LORD, have given me.” Then you shall set it before the LORD your God and worship before the LORD your God. 11And you shall rejoice—you, the Levite, and the sojourner who is among you—in all the good that the LORD your God has given to you and to your house.

The Third-Year Tithe

12When you have finished tithing all the tithe of your produce in the third year, the year of tithing, and have given it to the Levite, the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that they may eat within your towns and be filled,

13you shall say before the LORD your God, “I have removed the sacred portion from my house, and moreover I have given it to the Levite, the sojourner, the fatherless, and the widow, according to all YouryourYour commandment that YouyouYou have commanded me. I have not transgressed any of YouryourYour commandments, nor have I forgotten them. 14I have not eaten of it while mourning, or removed any of it while unclean, or given any of it for the dead. I have obeyed the voice of the LORD my God. I have done according to all that YouyouYou have commanded me.

15“Look down from YouryourYour holy habitation, from Heavenheavenheaven, and bless YouryourYour people Israel and the ground that YouyouYou have given us, as YouyouYou swore to our fathers—a land flowing with milk and honey.”

Affirming the Covenant

16This day the LORD your God commands you to do these statutes and rules. You shall therefore be careful to do them with all your heart and with all your soul. 17You have declared today that the LORD is your God, and that you will walk in HishisHis ways and keep HishisHis statutes and HishisHis commandments and HishisHis rules, and will obey HishisHis voice. 18And the LORD has declared today that you are a people for HishisHis treasured possession, as HeheHe said to you, and that you are to keep all HishisHis commandments, 19and that HeheHe will set you in praise and in fame and in honourhonourhonor above all the nations that HeheHe has made, and that you shall be a people holy to the LORD your God, as HeheHe has spoken.
