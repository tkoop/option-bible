Moses Calls Heaven and Earth to Witness

1“Listen, O heavens, and I will speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth. 2Let my teaching fall like rain and my words settle like dew, like showers on fresh grass and gentle rain on tender shoots. 3For I will proclaim the name of the LORD; give greatness to our God. 4The RockrockRock—HishisHis work is perfect, for all HishisHis ways are justice. God is faithful and without injustice; righteous and upright is HeheHe.

Israel’s Ungrateful Rebellion

5They have dealt corruptly with HimhimHim; their defect is not that of HishisHis children—they are a crooked and perverse generation. 6Do you thus repay the LORD, O foolish and unwise people? Is HeheHe not your FatherfatherFather, your CreatorcreatorCreator, who made you and established you? 7Remember the days of old; consider the years of many generations. Ask your father, and he will show you; your elders, and they will tell you. 8When the MostmostMost HighhighHigh gave the nations their inheritance, when HeheHe separated the sons of man, HeheHe set the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel.

9For the LORD’s portion is HishisHis people, Jacob HishisHis allotted inheritance. 10He found him in a desert land, in the howling waste of the wilderness; HeheHe encircled him, HeheHe cared for him, HeheHe kept him as something infinitely precious. 11Like an eagle that stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, that spreads its wings and takes them up and carries them on its pinions, 12the LORD alone guided him, and no foreign god was with him. 13He made him ride on the high places of the earth, and he ate the produce of the fields; HeheHe nourished him with honey from the rock and oil from the flinty crag. 14He fed him curds from cattle and milk from sheep, with fat of lambs and rams of Bashan and goats, with the finest wheat, and you drank the blood of the grape, the wine.

Jeshurun’s Prosperity and Fall

15But Jeshurun grew fat and turned rebellious; you grew fat, thick, and sleek. Then he forsook God who made him and scorned the RockrockRock of his salvation. 16They made HimhimHim jealous with strange gods; with abominations they provoked HimhimHim. 17They sacrificed to demons that are not God, to gods they had not known, new gods that had come recently, whom your fathers had not feared. 18You neglected the RockrockRock who begot you, and you forgot the God who gave you birth.

The LORD’s Anger and JudgmentJudgementJudgment

19The LORD saw it and spurned them, because of the provocation of HishisHis sons and daughters. 20And HeheHe said, “I will hide MymyMy face from them; I will see what their end will be, for they are a perverse generation, children in whom there is no faithfulness. 21They have made MemeMe jealous with what is no god; they have provoked MemeMe to anger with their idols. So I will make them jealous with those who are no people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation.”

22For a fire is kindled in MymyMy anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol; it devours the earth and its produce and sets ablaze the foundations of the mountains. 23I will heap disasters upon them; I will spend MymyMy arrows on them. 24They shall be wasted with hunger and devoured by plague and bitter destruction; I will send the teeth of beasts against them, with the venom of things that crawl in the dust. 25Outdoors the sword shall bereave, and indoors terror, for young man and virgin, infant and greygreygray-haired man alike.

26I would have said, “I will scatter them to distant places; I will make the memory of them cease from mankind”— 27had I not feared the provocation by the enemy, lest their adversaries misunderstand and say, “Our hand is exalted, and the LORD has not done all this.” 28For they are a nation void of counsel, and there is no understanding in them. 29If they were wise, they would understand this; they would discern their latter end. 30How could one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their RockrockRock had sold them and the LORD had given them up? 31For their rock is not like our RockrockRock; our enemies are judges. 32For their vine is of the vine of Sodom and of the fields of Gomorrah; their grapes are grapes of poison, and their clusters are bitter. 33Their wine is the venom of serpents and the cruel poison of asps.

34“Is not this laid up in store with MemeMe, sealed up in MymyMy treasuries? 35Vengeance is MinemineMine, and recompense, for the time when they stumble; for the day of their calamity is at hand, and their doom comes swiftly.” 36For the LORD will judge HishisHis people and have compassion on HishisHis servants, when HeheHe sees that their power is gone and there is none remaining, bond or free.

37Then HeheHe will say, “Where are their gods, the rock in which they took refuge, 38who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offering? Let them rise up and help you; let them be your shelter. 39See now that I, even I, am HeheHe, and there is no god besides MemeMe; I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; and there is none who can deliver from MymyMy hand. 40For I lift up MymyMy hand to Heavenheavenheaven and swear, As I live forever, 41if I sharpen MymyMy flashing sword and MymyMy hand takes hold on judgmentjudgementjudgment, I will render vengeance to MymyMy adversaries and repay those who hate MemeMe. 42I will make MymyMy arrows drunk with blood, and MymyMy sword shall devour flesh, with the blood of the slain and the captives, from the leaders of the enemy.” 43Rejoice, O nations, with HishisHis people, for HeheHe avenges the blood of HishisHis servants and renders vengeance to HishisHis adversaries, and makes atonement for HishisHis land, for HishisHis people.

Moses Commands Israel to Heed the Law

44Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people. 45When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel, 46he said to them, “Take to heart all the words by which I am warning you today, that you may command your sons to be careful to do all the words of this law. 47For it is no empty word for you, but it is your life, and by this word you shall live long in the land that you are going over the Jordan to possess.”

Moses’ Death Foretold

48That very day the LORD spoke to Moses: 49“Go up this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho, and view the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the people of Israel for a possession. 50And die on the mountain which you are going up, and be gathered to your ancestors, as Aaron your brother died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his ancestors. 51Because you broke faith with MemeMe in the midst of the people of Israel at the waters of Meribah Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin, and because you did not treat MemeMe as holy in the midst of the people of Israel. 52For you shall see the land before you, but you shall not go there, into the land that I am giving to the people of Israel.”
