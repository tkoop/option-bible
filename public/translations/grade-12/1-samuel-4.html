Israel Goes Out Against the Philistines

1The word of Samuel reached all Israel. Then Israel marched out to confront the Philistines in battle; they encamped at Ebenezer, while the Philistines encamped at Aphek.

2The Philistines drew up in formation against Israel, and when the fighting spread, Israel was routed before the Philistines, who struck down about four thousand men on the field of battle.

The Ark Is Brought into the Camp

3When the people returned to camp, the elders of Israel asked, "Why has the the LORD defeated us today before the Philistines? Let us take the ark of the covenant of the the LORD from Shiloh, that HeheHe may come among us and deliver us from the power of our enemies."

4So the people sent to Shiloh and brought from there the ark of the covenant of the the LORD of Hosts, who is enthroned between the cherubim. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, accompanied the ark of the covenant of God.

5When the ark of the covenant of the the LORD entered the camp, all Israel shouted so loudly that the earth trembled.

6The Philistines heard the sound of the shouting and asked, "What is this tremendous shouting in the camp of the Hebrews?" When they learned that the ark of the the LORD had entered the camp,

7the Philistines were terrified, for they said, "God has come into the camp!" And they cried, "Woe to us! Nothing like this has happened before. 8Woe to us! Who will rescue us from the power of these mighty gods? These are the gods who struck Egypt with every kind of plague in the wilderness."

9Then the Philistines urged one another, "Be strong and act like men, O Philistines, lest you become slaves to the Hebrews as they once were to you. Be men, and fight!"

Israel's Crushing Defeat

10So the Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated; every man fled to his tent. The slaughter was immense—thirty thousand Israelite foot soldiers fell. 11The ark of God was captured, and Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

12That same day a Benjamite ran from the battle line and reached Shiloh with his clothes torn and dust on his head.

13When he arrived, Eli was sitting on his seat beside the road, watching anxiously, because his heart trembled for the ark of God. When the man entered the city to report the news, the whole city cried out.

14Eli heard the outcry and asked, "What is this commotion?" So the man hurried in and reported to Eli.

15Now Eli was ninety-eight years old, and his eyes had grown dim, so that he could not see.

16The man said to Eli, "I have just come from the battle; I fled from the battle today."

"What happened, my son?" Eli asked.

17The messenger answered, "Israel has fled before the Philistines, and there has been a great slaughter among the people. Your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead, and the ark of God has been captured."

18When he mentioned the ark of God, Eli fell backward off his seat beside the gate. His neck was broken, and he died, for he was an old man and heavy. He had judged Israel forty years.

The Death of Eli and the Birth of Ichabod

19Now his daughter-in-law, Phinehas's wife, was pregnant and about to give birth. When she heard the news that the ark of God had been captured and that her father-in-law and husband were dead, she knelt down and gave birth, for her labourlabourlabor pains overwhelmed her.

20As she was dying, the women attending her said, "Do not be afraid, for you have given birth to a son!" But she did not respond or pay any attention.

21She named the child Ichabod, saying, "ThetheThe glory has departed from Israel," because the ark of God had been captured and because of her father-in-law and her husband.

22She said, "ThetheThe glory has departed from Israel, for the ark of God has been captured."
