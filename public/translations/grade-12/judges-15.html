Samson's Revenge for His Wife

1After some days, during the wheat harvest, Samson went to visit his wife, bringing a young goat. He said, "I will go in to my wife in her chamber." But her father would not let him enter.

2Her father said, "I was certain you hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Is not her younger sister more beautiful than she? Please let her be yours instead."

3Samson said to them, "This time I am justified in bringing harm upon the Philistines." 4So Samson went and caught three hundred foxes. He took torches, turned the animals tail to tail, and placed one torch between each pair of tails. 5Then he set fire to the torches and released the foxes into the Philistines' standing grain. The fire consumed the shocks, the standing grain, and even the olive orchards.

The Philistines Burn Samson's Wife and Her Father

6When the Philistines asked, "Who did this?"

they were answered, "Samson, the Timnite's son-in-law, because he took his wife and gave her to his companion." So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father with fire.

7Samson said to them, "If you do this, I will not rest until I have avenged myself on you." 8He struck them down in a devastating slaughter, and then went down and stayed in the cleft of the rock at Etam.

Judah Delivers Samson to the Philistines

9The Philistines went up, encamped in Judah, and spread out at Lehi. 10The men of Judah asked, "Why have you come up against us?"

They answered, "We have come to bind Samson and do to him as he did to us."

11Then three thousand men of Judah went down to the cleft of the rock at Etam and said to Samson, "Do you not know that the Philistines rule over us? What have you done to us?"

He replied, "As they did to me, so I have done to them."

12They said to him, "We have come down to bind you and hand you over to the Philistines."

Samson said to them, "Swear to me that you will not attack me yourselves."

13They answered, "No; we will only bind you and hand you over to them. We will not kill you." So they bound him with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

14When he reached Lehi, the Philistines shouted as they met him. Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD rushed upon him, and the ropes on his arms became like flax scorched by fire; his bindings melted from his hands. 15He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey, reached out his hand, took it, and struck down a thousand men with it. 16Samson said, "With a donkey's jawbone I have piled them into heaps. With a donkey's jawbone I have slain a thousand men." 17When he had finished speaking, he threw the jawbone from his hand and named that place Ramath-lehi.

The LORD Provides Water at En-hakkore

18He was very thirsty, so he called to the LORD and said, "YouyouYou gave this great deliverance through YouryourYour servant, and now must I die of thirst and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?"

19Then God split the hollow place at Lehi, and water came out of it. Samson drank, his spirit returned, and he revived. Therefore he named it En-hakkore; it is in Lehi to this day. 20He judged Israel twenty years during the days of the Philistines.
