The Commandment and the Shema

1Now this is the commandment—the statutes and the ordinances—that the LORD your God commanded me to teach you, so that you may do them in the land that you are crossing over to possess. 2so that you may fear the LORD your God—you and your son and your grandson—all the days of your life, and so that your days may be prolonged. 3Hear therefore, Israel, and be careful to do them, that it may go well with you, and that you may multiply greatly, as the LORD, the God of your fathers, promised you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.

Love the LORD Your God

4Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one. 5You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might. 6And these words that I command you today shall be on your heart. 7You shall teach them diligently to your children, and you shall talk of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise. 8You shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes. 9You shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

Warning Against Forgetting the LORD

10And when the LORD your God brings you into the land that HeheHe swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give you—with great and good cities that you did not build— 11and houses full of all good things that you did not fill, and cisterns that you did not dig, and vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant—and when you eat and are full— 12then take care lest you forget the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. 13It is the LORD your God whom you shall fear. Him you shall serve, and by HishisHis name you shall swear.

14You shall not go after other gods, the gods of the peoples who are around you— 15for the LORD your God in your midst is a jealous God—lest the anger of the LORD your God be kindled against you, and HeheHe destroy you from off the face of the earth. 16You shall not put the LORD your God to the test, as you tested HimhimHim at Massah. 17You shall diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God, and HishisHis testimonies and HishisHis statutes, which HeheHe has commanded you. 18And you shall do what is right and good in the sight of the LORD, that it may go well with you, and that you may go in and take possession of the good land that the LORD swore to give your fathers, 19by thrusting out all your enemies from before you, as the LORD has spoken.

Answering the Next Generation

20When your son asks you in time to come, “What is the meaning of the testimonies and the statutes and the rules that the LORD our God has commanded you?”

21then you shall say to your son, “We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt, and the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand. 22And before our eyes the LORD did great and grievous signs and wonders in Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his house. 23And HeheHe brought us out from there, that HeheHe might bring us in and give us the land that HeheHe swore to our fathers.

“24And the LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, that HeheHe might preserve us alive, as we are this day. 25And it will be righteousness for us, if we are careful to do all this commandment before the LORD our God, as HeheHe has commanded us.”
