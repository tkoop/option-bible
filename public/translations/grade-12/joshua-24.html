Joshua's Final Charge to Israel

1Joshua assembled all the tribes of Israel at Shechem and summoned the elders, heads, judges, and officers of Israel, and they presented themselves before God.

2Joshua said to all the people, “Thus says the the LORD, the God of Israel: Long ago your fathers dwelt beyond the River—Terah, the father of Abraham and of Nahor—and they served other gods. 3I took your father Abraham from beyond the River and led him through all the land of Canaan; I multiplied his descendants and gave him Isaac. 4To Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau; to Esau I gave Mount Seir as his possession, while Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.

5“I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt with the plagues I inflicted there; afterward I brought you out. 6I brought your fathers out of Egypt, and you came to the sea; the Egyptians pursued your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Red SeaseaSea. 7When they cried out to the the LORD, HeheHe placed darkness between you and the Egyptians and brought the sea over them and covered them; your eyes saw what I did in Egypt. You then lived in the wilderness for many days.

8“I brought you into the land of the Amorites, who lived beyond the Jordan; they fought against you, but I gave them into your hand, and you took possession of their land; I destroyed them before you. 9Then Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and fought against Israel; he sent for Balaam son of Beor to curse you. 10Yet I would not listen to Balaam, so he blessed you instead, and I delivered you from his hand.

11“You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho; the men of Jericho fought against you, as did the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites; but I gave them into your hand. 12I sent hornets ahead of you, and they drove out before you the two kings of the Amorites—not by your sword or your bow. 13I gave you a land on which you did not toil and cities you did not build; you dwell in them and eat from vineyards and olive groves you did not plant.

14“Now therefore fear the the LORD and serve HimhimHim in sincerity and truth; remove the gods your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the the LORD. 15If it seems displeasing in your eyes to serve the the LORD, choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve—whether the gods your fathers served beyond the River or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you dwell. But as for me and my household, we will serve the the LORD.”

Israel Reaffirms Its Loyalty to the the LORD

16The people answered, “Far be it from us that we should forsake the the LORD to serve other gods! 17For the the LORD our God, HeheHe brought us and our fathers up from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery, and performed those great signs before our eyes; HeheHe preserved us throughout the journey and among all the peoples through whom we passed. 18The the LORD drove out before us all the peoples, including the Amorites who inhabited the land. Therefore we too will serve the the LORD, for HeheHe is our God.”

19Joshua said to the people, “You cannot serve the the LORD, for HeheHe is a holy God; HeheHe is a jealous God. He will not forgive your transgressions and your sins. 20If you forsake the the LORD and serve foreign gods, HeheHe will turn and bring harm upon you and consume you, after having done good to you.”

21The people said to Joshua, “No; rather, we will serve the the LORD.”

22Joshua said to the people, “You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the the LORD, to serve HimhimHim.”

They said, “We are witnesses.”

23Joshua then said, “Now then, put away the foreign gods that are among you, and incline your hearts to the the LORD, the God of Israel.”

24The people said to Joshua, “We will serve the the LORD our God and obey HishisHis voice.”

The Covenant Renewed at Shechem

25On that day Joshua made a covenant for the people at Shechem and established for them a statute and an ordinance. 26Joshua wrote these words in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of God; he took a great stone and set it up there under the oak that was by the sanctuary of the the LORD.

27Joshua said to all the people, “Behold, this stone shall be a witness against us, for it has heard all the words of the the LORD that HeheHe spoke to us; it shall be a witness against you, lest you deny your God.” 28Then Joshua sent the people away, each to his own inheritance.

The Deaths of Joshua and Israel's Leaders

29After these things Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the the LORD, died at the age of one hundred and ten years. 30They buried him within the border of his inheritance at Timnath-serah, in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash. 31Israel served the the LORD all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua and who had known all the work that the the LORD had done for Israel.

32The bones of Joseph, which the children of Israel had brought up from Egypt, were buried at Shechem in the parcel of ground that Jacob had bought from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for one hundred pieces of silver; it became the inheritance of the descendants of Joseph. 33Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him at Gibeah of Phinehas his son, which had been given him in the hill country of Ephraim.
