Naomi’s Kinsman Boaz

1Now Naomi had a kinsman on her husband’s side, a man of considerable standing from the clan of Elimelech, and his name was Boaz.

Ruth Gleans in Boaz’s Field

2Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, “Let me go into the fields and gather the leftover grain behind anyone in whose eyes I find favourfavourfavor.”

Naomi answered, “Go ahead, my daughter.”

3So she departed and entered a field, gleaning behind the harvesters. It happened that the portion of the field belonged to Boaz, who was of the clan of Elimelech.

4Just then Boaz arrived from Bethlehem and said to the harvesters, “The LORD be with you!”

“The LORD bless you!” they answered.

5Boaz asked the overseer of his harvesters, “To whom does this young woman belong?”

6The overseer replied, “She is the Moabite woman who returned from Moab with Naomi. 7She asked, ‘Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves behind the harvesters.’ She has been here since morning, working steadily, and has rested only briefly in the shelter.”

Boaz Extends Kindness to Ruth

8So Boaz said to Ruth, “My daughter, listen to me. Do not go to glean in another field, and do not leave this one; stay here with my young women.” 9“Keep your eyes on the field where the men are harvesting, and follow along after the girls. I have told the men not to lay a hand on you. And whenever you are thirsty, go and get a drink from the water jars the men have filled.”

10At this she fell on her face, bowing to the ground, and said to him, “Why have I found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, that you should notice me, though I am a foreigner?”

11Boaz answered, “I have been fully informed of all that you have done for your mother-in-law after your husband’s death—how you left your father and mother and your homeland and came to a people you did not know before.” 12“May the LORD repay your work, and may you receive a full reward from the LORD, the God of Israel, under Whose wings you have come to take refuge.”

13Then she said, “May I continue to find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and spoken kindly to your servant, though I am not even one of your servant girls.”

14At mealtime Boaz said to her, “Come over here and eat some bread, dipping your morsel in the wine vinegar.” When she sat down beside the harvesters, he offered her roasted grain. She ate until she was satisfied and had some left over.

15When she rose to glean, Boaz commanded his young men, “Let her glean even among the sheaves, and do not humiliate her.” 16“Moreover, pull out some handfuls for her from the bundles and leave them for her to gather, and do not rebuke her.”

Ruth Returns to Naomi

17So Ruth gleaned in the field until evening. Then she threshed what she had gathered, and it was about an ephah of barley.

18She carried it into the town and showed her mother-in-law what she had gleaned. She also brought out and gave her the leftover food from her meal.

19Her mother-in-law asked her, “Where did you glean today, and where did you work? Blessed be the man who took notice of you!”

Then Ruth told her mother-in-law, “The man I worked with today is named Boaz.”

20Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, “The LORD bless him! He has not ceased to show kindness to the living and the dead.” She added, “This man is a close relative of ours; he is one of our redeemers.”

21Ruth the Moabite added, “He also told me, ‘Stay with my young men until they have finished all my harvest.’”

22Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, “It will be good for you, my daughter, to go with his young women, because in another field you might be harmed.”

23So Ruth stayed close to the young women of Boaz to glean until the barley harvest and the wheat harvest were completed. And she continued to live with her mother-in-law.
