Paul’s Final Admonition Before His Third Visit

1This marks the third time I am coming to you, and every charge must be established on the testimony of two or three witnesses. 2As I warned you when I was present the second time, so now, though absent, I forewarn those who sinned previously and everyone else that if I come again, I will not spare anyone.

Christ’s Power Disclosed Through Weakness

3Since you seek proof that Christ speaks through me, consider that HeheHe is not weak in dealing with you but is powerful among you. 4For although HeheHe was crucified in weakness, HeheHe lives by the power of God; and although we are weak in HimhimHim, we will live with HimhimHim by God’s power for your benefit.

Self-Examination and the Integrity of Apostleship

5Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Do you not realizerealiserealize that Jesus Christ is in you—unless, of course, you fail the test? 6But I trust you will recognizerecogniserecognize that we have not failed the test. 7Now we pray to God that you will do nothing wrong—not so that we may appear approved, but so that you may do what is right, even if we seem to have failed. 8For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth. 9Indeed, we rejoice whenever we are weak and you are strong, and this is our prayer: that you may be fully restored.

10This is why I write these things while absent, so that when I am present I will not have to deal harshly with you in accordance with the authority the Lord gave me for building up, not for tearing down.

Concluding Exhortations and Trinitarian Benediction

11Finally, brothers and sisters, rejoice. Aim for restoration, be comforted, agree with one another, and live in peace. And the God of love and peace will be with you. 12Greet one another with a holy kiss. 13All the saints send you their greetings.
