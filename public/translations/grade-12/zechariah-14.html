The LORD's Day of Reckoning and Rescue

1Behold, a day is coming for the LORD, when the spoil taken from you will be divided in your midst. 2For I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem for battle, and the city will be captured; the houses will be plundered, the women will be violated, half the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.

3Then ThetheThe LORD will go out and fight against those nations, as HeheHe fights on a day of battle. 4On that day HishisHis feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, which lies before Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west by a very wide valley, so that half the mountain will move northward and the other half southward.

5And you will flee through the valley of MymyMy mountains, for the valley of the mountains will reach to Azal; and you will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with HimhimHim.

A Day Unlike Any Other and Living Waters

6On that day there will be no light, cold, or frost. 7It will be a unique day—known to the LORD—neither day nor night; yet at evening time there will be light. 8On that day living waters will flow out from Jerusalem, half of them to the eastern sea and half to the western sea; it will be so in summer and in winter. 9And the LORD will be KingkingKing over all the earth. On that day the LORD is one and HishisHis name is one.

Jerusalem Exalted and Secure

10The whole land will be turned into a plain from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem, while Jerusalem will be raised up and remain in its place—from the GategateGate of Benjamin to the site of the former gate, to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate, and from the TowertowerTower of Hananel to the king's winepresses. 11It will be inhabited, and there will no longer be any decree of utter destruction; Jerusalem will dwell in security.

Plague upon the Invaders

12And this will be the plague with which the LORD will strike all the peoples who wage war against Jerusalem: their flesh will rot while they are still standing on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths. 13On that day a great panic from the LORD will seize them, so that each will grasp the hand of his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and one hand will be raised against another. 14Even Judah will fight at Jerusalem, and the wealth of all the surrounding nations will be gathered—gold, silver, and garments in very great abundance. 15A plague like this will also fall on the horse, the mule, the camel, the donkey, and every beast in those camps.

The Nations Worship and the Feast of Booths

16Then everyone who survives from all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the KingkingKing, the LORD of Hosts, and to celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Booths. 17If any of the families of the earth do not go up to Jerusalem to worship the KingkingKing, the LORD of Hosts, no rain will fall on them. 18And if the family of Egypt does not go up and present itself, it will suffer the plague with which the LORD afflicts the nations that do not go up to celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Booths. 19This will be the punishment of Egypt and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Booths.

Holiness Engraved on Every Vessel

20On that day, "HolyholyHoly to the LORD" will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, and the pots in the house of the LORD will be like the bowls before the altar. 21Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Hosts, and all who sacrifice will come and take them and boil the meat of the sacrifice in them. And there will no longer be a trader in the house of the LORD of Hosts on that day.
