David Anointed King over All Israel

1All Israel assembled at Hebron and said to David, “Look, we are your own kin.” 2“Even in the past—even yesterday and the day before—even when Saul was king, you were the one who led Israel out and brought them back; and the LORD your God said to you, ‘You shall shepherd MymyMy people Israel, and you shall be ruler over MymyMy people Israel.’” 3All the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and there David made a covenant with them before the LORD; then they anointed David king over Israel, in accordance with the word of the LORD spoken through Samuel.

David Captures Jerusalem

4David and all Israel went to Jerusalem—that is, Jebus—where the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land, were living. 5The inhabitants of Jebus said to David, “You will not come in here.” Nevertheless, David captured the fortress of Zion, that is, the CitycityCity of David. 6David had said, “Whoever strikes the Jebusites first shall be chief and commander.” Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, so he became chief. 7David settled in the stronghold; therefore it was called the CitycityCity of David. 8He built up the city all around, from the Millo to the surrounding wall, while Joab restored the rest of the city. 9David kept growing greater and greater, because the LORD of hosts was with him.

David’s Mighty Warriors

10These are the chiefs of the mighty men who supported David and strengthened his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel: 11Jashobeam son of Hachmoni, chief of the thirty, wielded his spear against three hundred men and killed them at one time. 12After him came Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men. 13He was with David at Pas-dammim, where the Philistines had gathered for battle. The field was full of barley, and the people fled before the Philistines. 14But they took their stand in the middle of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines, and the LORD brought about a great victory. 15Three of the thirty chiefs went down to David at the rock near the cave of Adullam, while the Philistine army was camped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 16At that time David was in the stronghold, and a Philistine garrison was in Bethlehem. 17David longed for water and said, “Oh, that someone would give me a drink from the well of Bethlehem by the gate!” 18So the three broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well by the gate of Bethlehem, and carried it to David. But David refused to drink it and poured it out to the LORD. 19He said, “God forbid that I should do such a thing before my God! Shall I drink the blood of these men who risked their lives? For they brought it at the risk of their lives.” These three mighty men did this. 20Abishai, Joab’s brother, was chief of the three. He wielded his spear against three hundred men and killed them, and he won a name among the three. 21He was doubly honouredhonouredhonored above the three and became their commander, yet he did not attain to the first three. 22Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a valiant man from Kabzeel, great in deeds. He struck down two sons of Ariel of Moab, and he went down into a pit and killed a lion on a snowy day. 23He also struck down an Egyptian of great stature—five cubits tall. The Egyptian had a spear like a weaver’s beam, but Benaiah went down to him with a club, snatched the spear from the Egyptian’s hand, and killed him with his own spear. 24These are the deeds Benaiah son of Jehoiada performed; he had a name among the three mighty men. 25He was more honouredhonouredhonored than the thirty, but he did not attain to the three; and David put him in charge of his bodyguard. 26The mighty men of the armies were Asahel, Joab’s brother; Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem; Shammoth the Harorite; Helez the Pelonite; Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; Abiezer the Anathothite; Sibbecai the Hushathite; Ilai the Ahohite; Maharai the Netophathite; Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite; Ithai son of Ribai of Gibeah of the Benjaminites; Benaiah the Pirathonite; Hurai of the brooks of Gaash; Abiel the Arbathite; Azmaveth the Baharumite; Eliahba the Shaalbonite; the sons of Hashem the Gizonite; Jonathan son of Shagee the Hararite; Ahiam son of Sacar the Hararite; Eliphal son of Ur; Hepher the Mecherathite; Ahijah the Pelonite; Hezro the Carmelite; Naarai son of Ezbai; Joel, brother of Nathan; Mibhar son of Hagri; Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai the Beerothite, armourarmourarmor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah; Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite; Uriah the Hittite; Zabad son of Ahlai; Adina son of Shiza the Reubenite, chief of the Reubenites, with thirty men under him; Hanan son of Maacah; Joshaphat the Mithnite; Uzzia the Ashterathite; Shama and Jeiel, sons of Hotham the Aroerite; Jediael son of Shimri and his brother Joha the Tizite; Eliel the Mahavite; Jeribai and Joshaviah, sons of Elnaam; Ithmah the Moabite; Eliel; Obed; and Jaasiel the Mezobaite.
