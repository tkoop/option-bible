Judah's Departure and Marriage

1At that time, Judah left his brothers and went down to stay with a certain Adullamite named Hirah. 2There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite named Shua; he married her and went in to her. 3She conceived and bore a son, whom he named Er. 4She conceived again and bore another son, whom she named Onan. 5She bore yet a third son and named him Shelah; he was born in Chezib.

Tamar and the Levirate Duty

6Judah arranged a wife for his firstborn, Er; her name was Tamar. 7But Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD put him to death. 8Then Judah said to Onan, “Go in to your brother's wife and fulfillfulfilfulfill your duty as a brother-in-law to her, and raise up offspring for your brother.” 9Onan knew, however, that the offspring would not be his; so whenever he went in to his brother's wife, he spilled his semen on the ground, so that he would not produce offspring for his brother. 10The thing he did was evil in the sight of the LORD, and HeheHe put him to death as well. 11Then Judah told his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Remain a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up.” For he feared that Shelah too might die, as his brothers had. So Tamar went and lived in her father's house.

The Encounter at Timnah

12After a long time, Judah's wife, the daughter of Shua, died. When Judah had finished mourning, he went up with his friend Hirah the Adullamite to Timnah, where his sheep were being sheared. 13Tamar was told, “Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.” 14So she removed her widow's garments, covered herself with a veil, wrapped herself, and sat at the entrance to Enaim, on the road to Timnah; for she saw that Shelah had grown up, and she had not been given to him as a wife.

15When Judah saw her, he took her for a prostitute, because she had covered her face.

16He turned aside to her by the road and said, “Come now, let me come in to you.” He did not know that she was his daughter-in-law.

She replied, “What will you give me, that you may come in to me?”

17He said, “I will send you a young goat from the flock.”

She said, “Will you give me a pledge until you send it?”

18He asked, “What pledge shall I give you?”

She answered, “Your signet, your cord, and your staff that is in your hand.” He gave them to her, came in to her, and she conceived by him.

19Then she arose, went away, removed her veil, and put on her widow's garments again.

20Judah sent the young goat by his friend the Adullamite, to recover the pledge from the woman, but he could not find her. 21He asked the men of that place, “Where is the prostitute who was by the roadside at Enaim?”

They replied, “There has been no prostitute here.”

22He returned to Judah and said, “I could not find her, and the men of the place also said that no prostitute had been there.”

23Judah said, “Let her keep them, lest we be held in contempt. I sent this young goat, and you did not find her.”

Tamar Vindicated and the Twins Born

24About three months later, Judah was told, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has engaged in prostitution, and moreover she is pregnant by her prostitution.”

Judah said, “Bring her out, and let her be burned.”

25As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law: “By the man to whom these belong I am pregnant.” She also said, “Please identify whose these are—the signet, the cord, and the staff.”

26Judah recognizedrecogniserecognized them and said, “She is more righteous than I, since I did not give her to my son Shelah.” And he did not have sexual relations with her again.

27When the time came for her to give birth, there were twins in her womb. 28As she was giving birth, one of them put out a hand; the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his hand, saying, “This one came out first.”

29But as he drew back his hand, his brother came out; and she said, “How have you broken out!” So he was named Perez. 30Afterward his brother came out, the one with the scarlet thread on his hand, and he was named Zerah.
