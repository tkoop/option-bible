The Triumphal Entry

1As they drew near to Jerusalem—approaching Bethphage and Bethany at the Mount of Olives—Jesus dispatched two of HishisHis disciples ahead. 2He instructed them, “Proceed into the village opposite you; the moment you enter it, you will find a colt tethered there, one that no one has ever ridden. Untie it and bring it to MemeMe. 3Should anyone challenge you, asking, ‘Why are you doing this?’ reply, ‘The Lord needs it and will return it here immediately.’”

4So they went and found a colt outside on the road, tied at a doorway, and they untied it. 5Some of the bystanders demanded, “What are you doing, untying that colt?” 6They responded as Jesus had directed, and the bystanders permitted them to leave.

7They brought the colt to Jesus, threw their cloaks over it, and HeheHe mounted it. 8Many people spread their cloaks along the road, while others spread branches they had cut from the fields.

9Those who went ahead and those who followed shouted, “Hosanna! Blessed is HeheHe who comes in the name of the Lord! 10Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David! Hosanna in the highest!”

11Jesus entered Jerusalem and went into the temple. After surveying everything, and because the hour was already late, HeheHe withdrew to Bethany with the TwelvetwelveTwelve.

The Fig Tree and the Temple

12The next day, as they were leaving Bethany, Jesus was hungry. 13Seeing from a distance a fig tree in leaf, HeheHe approached it to see whether it bore any fruit; when HeheHe reached it, however, HeheHe found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs. 14He declared to it, “May no one ever eat fruit from you again.” His disciples heard this.

15They came to Jerusalem, and Jesus entered the temple. He began driving out those who were buying and selling there, overturning the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling doves. 16He would not permit anyone to carry merchandise through the temple courts.

17He taught them, saying, “Is it not written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all nations’? But you have made it a den of robbers.” 18The chief priests and the scribes heard this and began seeking a way to destroy HimhimHim, for they feared HimhimHim, because the entire crowd was astonished at HishisHis teaching.

19When evening came, they went out of the city.

Faith, Prayer, and Forgiveness

20The next morning, as they passed by, they saw the fig tree withered from its roots. 21Peter remembered and said to HimhimHim, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree you cursed has withered.”

22Jesus answered, “Have faith in God. 23Truly I tell you, if anyone says to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,’ and does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will come to pass, it will be done for him. 24Therefore I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours. 25And whenever you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive, so that your FatherfatherFather in Heavenheavenheaven may forgive you your trespasses.”

The Authority of Jesus Questioned

27They came again to Jerusalem, and as Jesus was walking in the temple, the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders came to HimhimHim. 28They asked, “By what authority are you doing these things? Or who gave you this authority?”

29Jesus replied, “I will ask you one question. Answer MemeMe, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things. 30Was John’s baptism from Heavenheavenheaven or from men? Answer MemeMe.”

31They reasoned among themselves, “If we say, ‘From Heavenheavenheaven,’ HeheHe will ask why they did not believe him. 32But if we say, ‘From men’—” They were afraid of the crowd, for everyone regarded John as a true prophet.

33So they answered Jesus, “We do not know.”

Jesus said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”
