11When the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, 12all the valiant men rose, marched through the night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan. They came to Jabesh and burned them there. 13Then they took their bones and buried them beneath the tamarisk tree in Jabesh, and they fasted seven days.
