The Promulgation of the Law and Separation from Foreigners

1On that day, the BookbookBook of Moses was read aloud in the hearing of the people, and it was discovered that the text recorded that no Ammonite or Moabite was ever to be admitted into the assembly of God. 2For they had not welcomed the Israelites with provisions and water; instead, they had retained Balaam to pronounce a curse upon them, though our God transformed the curse into a blessing. 3Upon hearing this law, the people separated from Israel all those of mixed descent.

Nehemiah Purges the Temple of Corruption

4Before this, Eliashib the priest, who had been appointed over the storerooms of the house of our God and was intimately allied with Tobiah, 5had furnished him with a spacious chamber where they had previously housed the grain offerings, the frankincense, the vessels, the tithes of grain, the new wine, and the oil ordained for the Levites, the singers, and the gatekeepers, together with the contributions for the priests. 6During these events, I was absent from Jerusalem, for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes, king of Babylon, I had returned to the king; and after an interval I requested permission from the king. 7When I returned to Jerusalem, I uncovered the wrongdoing that Eliashib had committed on Tobiah's behalf by allocating him a chamber in the courts of the house of God. 8I was deeply indignant, so I expelled all of Tobiah's household effects from the chamber. 9Then I commanded that the chambers be purified, and I restored to them the vessels of the house of God, together with the grain offerings and the frankincense.

10I also learned that the Levites' allotments had been withheld, so that each of the Levites and singers who performed the service had withdrawn to his own estate. 11So I reprimanded the leaders and asked, “Why is the house of God forsaken?” Then I assembled them and reinstated them in their offices. 12And all Judah delivered a tithe of the grain, the new wine, and the oil into the storerooms. 13I designated as custodians over the storerooms Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites, with Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, to assist them, because they were deemed reliable. They were charged with apportioning the supplies to their brothers.

14“Remember me for this, O my God, and do not erase the acts of steadfast devotion that I have performed for the house of my God and for its ministrations.”

Enforcing Sabbath Observance

15In those days I saw people in Judah operating winepresses on the Sabbath and transporting sheaves and loading them on donkeys, along with wine, grapes, figs, and every manner of burden. And they were bringing all this into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. So I admonished them for marketing provisions on that day. 16Tyrians who dwelt there were importing fish and all kinds of merchandise and selling them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah in Jerusalem.

17Then I rebuked the nobles of Judah and asked, “What is this wickedness you are perpetrating—profaning the Sabbath day? 18Did not your ancestors act likewise, so that our God inflicted all this calamity upon us and upon this city? Yet you are compounding HishisHis indignation against Israel by profaning the Sabbath.”

19When the gates of Jerusalem were nearing closure before the Sabbath, I commanded that the portals be closed and not reopened until after the Sabbath. I stationed several of my attendants at the gates so that no burden might be imported on the Sabbath day. 20On one or two occasions the merchants and sellers of all kinds of goods encamped beyond Jerusalem.

21But I admonished them, “Why are you encamping outside the wall? Should you repeat this, I will have you arrested.” Thereafter they did not come on the Sabbath.

22Then I directed the Levites to consecrate themselves and keep watch at the gates in order to sanctify the Sabbath day. “Remember me for this as well, O my God, and extend mercy to me in accordance with YouryourYour abundant loving devotion.”

Confronting Intermarriage and Priestly Defilement

23In those days I also saw Jews who had married Ashdodite, Ammonite, or Moabite women. 24Half of their offspring spoke the language of Ashdod or the tongue of each respective people, but none was able to speak the language of Judah.

25So I confronted them and denounced them and struck certain of them and plucked out their hair. I compelled them to swear an oath before God: “You must not give your daughters to their sons or take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.”

26“Did not Solomon, king of Israel, transgress in these matters? No king among the nations surpassed him, and he was beloved of his God, who appointed him king over all Israel—yet foreign women led him into sin. 27Are we now to hear that you are committing this great evil, acting treacherously toward our God by marrying foreign women?”

28Moreover, one of the sons of Joiada son of Eliashib the high priest had become son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite, so I expelled him from my presence. 29“Remember them, O my God, because they defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.” 30Accordingly, I cleansed them of all foreign elements and assigned the priests and Levites to their respective duties. 31I also arranged for the wood offering at the prescribed times and for the firstfruits. “Remember me, O my God, for good.”
