Joy When God Restores His People

1When the the LORD restored Zion, we felt like we were dreaming. 2Then our mouths were filled with laughter, and our tongues with songs of joy. Then the nations said, "ThetheThe LORD has done great things for them."

3The LORD has done great things for us, and we are glad.

Hope for a New Harvest

4Bring back our people, the LORD, like rivers flowing through the desert.

5Those who plant with tears will harvest with songs of joy. 6The one who goes out crying, carrying seed to plant, will come home with songs of joy, carrying his bundles of grain.
