The LORD’s Mountain in the Last Days

1This is the message that Isaiah son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem.

2In the last days, the mountain of the house of the LORD will be set up as the highest of the mountains. It will be lifted above the hills, and all nations will stream to it. 3Many peoples will come and say, “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us HishisHis ways, so we can walk in HishisHis paths.” For the law will go out from Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem. 4He will judge between the nations and settle disputes for many peoples. They will turn their swords into plow blades and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will not take up the sword against nation, and they will not learn war anymore.

A Warning to Jacob’s House

5Come, people of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the LORD. 6For YouyouYou have abandoned YouryourYour people, the people of Jacob, because they are full of EasterneasternEastern ways and fortune-tellers like the Philistines, and they make deals with foreigners. 7Their land is full of silver and gold, with no end to their treasures. Their land is full of horses, with no end to their chariots. 8Their land is full of idols. They bow down to the work of their own hands, to what their fingers have made. 9So people are brought low, and everyone is humbled. Do not forgive them!

Hide from the LORD’s Majesty

10Go into the rocks and hide in the dust, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendoursplendoursplendor of HishisHis majesty. 11The proud look of man will be humbled, and the pride of people will be brought low. The LORD alone will be raised high on that day.

The Day of the LORD

12For the DaydayDay of the LORD of Hosts will come against all who are proud and high, and against all that is lifted up. It will be brought low. 13It will come against all the tall cedars of Lebanon and all the oaks of Bashan. 14It will come against all the high mountains and all the high hills. 15It will come against every high tower and every strong wall. 16It will come against every ship from Tarshish and every beautiful treasure. 17So human pride will be brought low, and the pride of people will be humbled. The LORD alone will be raised high on that day. 18The idols will vanish completely. 19People will run to caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendoursplendoursplendor of HishisHis majesty, when HeheHe rises to shake the earth. 20On that day, people will throw away their silver and gold idols—the idols they made to worship—to the moles and the bats. 21They will run to cracks in the rocks and gaps in the cliffs, away from the terror of the LORD and the splendoursplendoursplendor of HishisHis majesty, when HeheHe rises to shake the earth. 22Stop trusting in people. They are only human, with breath in their noses. What are they worth?
