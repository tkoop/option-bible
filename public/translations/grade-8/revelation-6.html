The First Four Seals

1I saw the LamblambLamb open the first of the seven seals. Then I heard one of the four living creatures say in a voice like thunder, "Come!" 2I looked, and there was a white horse. Its rider held a bow and was given a crown. He rode out to win, and he will keep on winning.

3When the LamblambLamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, "Come!" 4Then another horse came out, bright red. Its rider was given power to take peace from the earth so that people would kill each other. He was also given a large sword.

5When the LamblambLamb opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, "Come!" I looked, and there was a black horse. Its rider held a pair of scales in his hand. 6Then I heard a voice from among the four living creatures say, "A quart of wheat costs a full day's pay, and three quarts of barley cost a full day's pay. But do not harm the olive oil and the wine."

7When the LamblambLamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, "Come!" 8I looked, and there was a pale horse. Its rider was named Death, and Hades followed close behind. They were given power over a fourth of the earth to kill people with swords, hunger, plague, and wild animals.

The Fifth Seal: Souls Under the Altar

9When the LamblambLamb opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of people who had been killed because they stayed true to God's word and to their testimony. 10They cried out in a loud voice, "How long, O Lord, holy and true, until YouyouYou judge the people living on the earth and punish them for killing us?" 11Each of them was given a white robe. They were told to rest a little longer, until the full number of their fellow servants and brothers and sisters who were to be killed, just as they had been, was complete.

The Sixth Seal: The Sky and Earth Shake

12I watched as the LamblambLamb opened the sixth seal. There was a great earthquake. The sun turned black like rough cloth, and the whole moon turned red like blood. 13The stars in the sky fell to the earth, like green figs dropping from a fig tree shaken by a strong wind. 14The sky split apart like a scroll being rolled up. Every mountain and island was moved from its place.

15Then the kings of the earth, the great leaders, the army commanders, the rich, the powerful, and every slave and free person hid themselves in caves and among the rocks of the mountains. 16They called out to the mountains and rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the face of HimhimHim who sits on the throne and from the anger of the LamblambLamb, 17because the great day of TheirtheirTheir anger has come. Who can stand?"
