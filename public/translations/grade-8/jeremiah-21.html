Zedekiah Sends Messengers to Jeremiah

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent Pashhur son of Malkijah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah to him. They said, 2"Please ask the LORD to help us, because King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon is attacking us. Maybe the LORD will do for us one of HishisHis wonderful miracles and make Nebuchadnezzar leave."

The LORD's Answer to the King

3Then Jeremiah said to them, "Tell Zedekiah this:"

4"This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I am about to turn the weapons in your hands against you—the ones you are using to fight the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are attacking you outside the wall. I will gather them into the middle of this city. 5I will fight against you MyselfmyselfMyself with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, with anger, fury, and great wrath. 6I will strike down the people who live in this city and their animals; they will die from a terrible disease. 7After that, declares the LORD, I will hand over King Zedekiah of Judah, his officials, and the people left in this city—those who survive the disease, the sword, and the famine—to King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, to their enemies, and to those who want to kill them. He will kill them with the sword. He will not spare them or show them pity or mercy."

Life or Death for the People

8Jeremiah also said, "Tell these people:"

"This is what the LORD says: I am putting before you the way to life and the way to death. 9Anyone who stays in this city will die by the sword, famine, or disease. But anyone who goes out and surrenders to the Chaldeans who are attacking you will live. He will escape with his life. 10I have turned MymyMy face against this city to bring trouble, not good, declares the LORD. It will be handed over to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it down."

A Warning to David's House

11Concerning the royal family of Judah, Jeremiah said, "Listen to the LORD's word:"

12"This is what the LORD says, O house of David: Judge fairly every morning. Rescue the person who has been robbed from the one who oppresses him. Otherwise MymyMy anger will break out like fire and burn, and no one will put it out, because of the evil you have done. 13I am against you, Jerusalem—you who live in the valley on the rock of the plain, declares the LORD—you who say, 'Who can attack us? Who can enter our homes?' 14I will punish you for what your actions deserve, declares the LORD. I will set fire to your forest, and it will burn up everything around you."
