King Ahasuerus Holds a Great Feast

1In the days of King Ahasuerus, who ruled over 127 provinces from India to Ethiopia, 2this happened. In those days King Ahasuerus was sitting on his royal throne in the fortress at Susa. 3In the third year of his reign, he gave a feast for all his officials and servants. The army of Persia and Media was there, along with the nobles and governors of the provinces. 4For 180 days he showed them the wealth and glory of his kingdom and the splendoursplendoursplendor of his greatness.

5When those days were over, the king gave a seven-day feast in the palace garden for all the people in Susa, from the greatest to the least. 6The garden was decorated with white and violet hangings, held by cords of fine linen and purple to silver rings on marble pillars. Gold and silver couches rested on a mosaic floor of porphyry, marble, mother-of-pearl, and other costly stones. 7Drinks were served in gold cups, and the cups were all different. Royal wine was plentiful, because the king was generous. 8The drinking followed the king's rule: no one was forced, because the king had told his officials to serve each man as he wished.

Queen Vashti Refuses the King's Command

9Queen Vashti also gave a feast for the women in the royal palace of King Ahasuerus.

10On the seventh day, when the king was happy from wine, he commanded the seven eunuchs who served him: Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carkas. 11He told them to bring Queen Vashti before him, wearing the royal crown, so he could show her beauty to the people and officials, because she was lovely to look at. 12But Queen Vashti refused to come when the king sent for her through the eunuchs. The king became very angry, and his wrath burned within him.

The King Seeks Advice

13Then the king spoke to the wise men who understood the times, for this was the king's usual way with everyone who knew law and justice. 14The men closest to him were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan, seven princes of Persia and Media. They saw the king's face and sat first in the kingdom. 15He asked, "What does the law say should be done to Queen Vashti, because she did not obey the command of King Ahasuerus given through the eunuchs?"

16Memucan answered in front of the king and the princes, "Queen Vashti has done wrong not only against the king, but against all the princes and all the peoples in every province of King Ahasuerus. 17When the queen's action becomes known to all women, they will look down on their husbands. They will say, 'King Ahasuerus commanded Queen Vashti to come before him, but she did not come.' 18This very day the noble ladies of Persia and Media who have heard about the queen's behaviourbehaviourbehavior will say the same thing to all the king's officials. Then there will be much contempt and anger."

A Royal Decree Is Sent Out

19"If it pleases the king, let a royal decree go out from him and be written among the laws of the Persians and Medes, so it cannot be changed. Let it say that Vashti may never again come before King Ahasuerus, and let the king give her royal position to another woman who is better than she is. 20When the king's decree is announced throughout his whole kingdom, every woman, great and small, will honourhonourhonor her husband."

21The king and the princes liked this advice, and the king did what Memucan said. 22He sent letters to all the provinces of his kingdom, to each province in its own writing and to each people in its own language. The letters said that every man should be master in his own house and speak in the language of his own people.
