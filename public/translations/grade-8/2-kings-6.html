Elisha and the Floating Axe Head

1The prophets' disciples said to Elisha, "This place where we live with you is too small for us. 2Let us go to the Jordan River. Each of us can get a log there and build a place for us to live."

Elisha said, "Go."

3One of them said, "Please come with your servants."

Elisha said, "I will go."

4So he went with them. They came to the Jordan and began cutting down trees. 5While one man was cutting a beam, the iron axe head fell into the water. He cried out, "Oh no, my master! It was borrowed!"

6The man of God asked, "Where did it fall?" When the man showed him the place, Elisha cut a stick and threw it there, and the iron floated. 7Elisha said, "Pick it up." The man reached out his hand and took it.

Aram's Secret Plans Are Discovered

8The king of Aram was at war with Israel. He talked with his officers and said, "I will set up camp at a certain place."

9But the man of God sent a message to the king of Israel: "Be careful not to pass through that place, because the Arameans are moving down there." 10So the king of Israel sent a warning to the place the man of God had named. This happened more than once or twice.

11This made the king of Aram very angry. He called his officers and said, "Which of you is working for the king of Israel?"

12One of his officers said, "None of us, my lord the king. Elisha the prophet in Israel tells the king of Israel the words you speak in your bedroom."

13The king said, "Go and see where he is, so I can send men to get him." They told him, "HeheHe is in Dothan."

14So the king sent horses, chariots, and a large army there. They came at night and surrounded the city.

The Unseen Army Around Elisha

15When the servant of the man of God got up early and went out, he saw an army with horses and chariots all around the city. He said, "Oh no, my master! What will we do?"

16Elisha said, "Do not be afraid. Those who are with us are more than those who are with them."

17Then Elisha prayed, "ThetheThe LORD, please open his eyes so he can see." The LORD opened the young man's eyes, and he saw the mountain full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.

18As the Arameans came down to him, Elisha prayed to the LORD, "Please strike this people with blindness." The LORD struck them with blindness, just as Elisha had asked.

19Elisha said to them, "This is not the right road, and this is not the right city. Follow me, and I will lead you to the man you are looking for." He led them to Samaria.

20When they entered Samaria, Elisha said, "ThetheThe LORD, open their eyes so they can see." The LORD opened their eyes, and they saw that they were in the middle of Samaria.

21When the king of Israel saw them, he said to Elisha, "MymyMy father, should I kill them? Should I kill them?"

22Elisha said, "Do not kill them. Would you kill people you captured with your sword and bow? Put food and water in front of them so they can eat and drink, and then let them go back to their master."

23So the king made a great feast for them. They ate and drank, and he sent them away. After that, Aramean raiding parties stopped coming into the land of Israel.

Famine and Siege in Samaria

24Later, Ben-Hadad king of Aram gathered all his army, went up, and besieged Samaria.

25There was a great famine in Samaria while they surrounded it. A donkey's head cost eighty pieces of silver, and a small amount of dove dung cost five pieces of silver.

26As the king of Israel was walking on the wall, a woman cried out to him, "Help, my lord the king!"

27He said, "If the LORD does not help you, how can I help you? Can I get grain from the threshing floor or wine from the winepress?"

28Then the king asked her, "What is wrong?"

She answered, "This woman said to me, 'Give me your son so we can eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.'

"So we cooked my son and ate him. The next day I said to her, 'Give me your son so we can eat him,' but she hid her son."

29So we cooked my son and ate him. The next day I said to her, 'Give me your son so we can eat him,' but she hid her son."

30When the king heard the woman's words, he tore his clothes. As he walked along the wall, the people saw that he was wearing sackcloth under his clothes.

31Then he said, "May God punish me, and even more, if Elisha son of Shaphat still has his head by the end of today!"

32Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him. Before the messenger arrived, Elisha said to the elders, "Do you see that this murderer's son has sent someone to cut off my head? When the messenger comes, shut the door and hold him at the door. Don't you hear his master's footsteps behind him?"

33While Elisha was still speaking, the messenger came down to him. The king said, "This trouble is from the LORD. Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?"
