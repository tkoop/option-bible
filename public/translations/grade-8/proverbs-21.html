The LORD Directs Kings and Hearts

1A king’s heart is like a stream of water in the hand of the the LORD. He turns it wherever HeheHe wants. 2Every person thinks his own way is right, but the LORD weighs the heart. 3Doing what is right and fair is more pleasing to the LORD than offering a sacrifice. 4Proud looks and a proud heart are the lamp of the wicked, and that is sin.

Plans, Wealth, and Justice

5The plans of the diligent bring profit, but everyone who rushes comes to poverty. 6Wealth gained by lying is like a mist that disappears, and it brings death.

7The violence of the wicked will sweep them away, because they refuse to do what is just. 8The way of the guilty is crooked, but the conduct of the pure is upright.

Home, Neighbors, and the Wicked

9It is better to live on a corner of the roof than to share a house with a quarrelsome wife. 10The soul of the wicked craves evil, and his neighbourneighbourneighbor finds no favourfavourfavor in his eyes.

11When a mocker is punished, the simple gain wisdom; and when a wise man is instructed, he gains knowledge. 12The RighteousrighteousRighteous OneoneOne considers the house of the wicked and brings the wicked to ruin.

13Whoever shuts his ears to the cry of the poor will also cry out and not be answered. 14A gift given in secret soothes anger, and a bribe in the pocket calms great wrath.

15Justice done brings joy to the righteous, but terror to evildoers. 16The man who strays from the path of understanding will rest among the dead.

Pride, Words, and True Success

17He who loves pleasure will become poor; whoever loves wine and oil will not become rich. 18The wicked become a ransom for the righteous, and the treacherous for the upright.

19It is better to live in the desert than with a quarrelsome and ill-tempered wife. 20Precious treasure and oil are in the home of the wise, but a foolish man uses them up.

21He who pursues righteousness and faithful love finds life, righteousness, and honourhonourhonor. 22A wise man climbs the city of the mighty and brings down the stronghold they trust in.

23He who guards his mouth and tongue keeps his soul from trouble. 24Mocker is the name of the proud and arrogant man who acts with bursts of pride.

25The craving of the lazy man kills him, because his hands refuse to work. 26All day long he wants more, but the righteous give without holding back.

27The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable—how much more when it is brought with evil intent! 28A false witness will perish, but the man who listens will speak forever.

29A wicked man puts on a bold face, but the upright man considers his ways. 30There is no wisdom, no understanding, and no counsel that can stand against the LORD. 31A horse is prepared for the day of battle, but victory comes from the LORD.
