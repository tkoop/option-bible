A Song of Praise to the LORD

1Sing for joy in the LORD, you righteous people. Praise looks good on those who are upright. 2Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre. Sing praises to HimhimHim with the ten-stringed harp. 3Sing a new song to HimhimHim. Play well with a joyful shout.

4For the word of the LORD is upright, and all HishisHis work is done in faithfulness. 5He loves righteousness and justice. The EarthEarthearth is full of the faithful love of the LORD.

6By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all their stars by the breath of HishisHis mouth. 7He gathers the waters of the sea like a heap. He stores the deep waters in warehouses. 8Let all the earth fear the LORD. Let all who live in the world stand in awe of HimhimHim. 9For HeheHe spoke, and it happened. He gave the command, and it stood firm.

10The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations. He stops the schemes of the peoples. 11The plans of the LORD stand forever. His heart's thoughts last from generation to generation. 12Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people HeheHe chose as HishisHis own inheritance.

13The LORD looks down from Heavenheavenheaven. He sees all people. 14From where HeheHe lives, HeheHe watches all who live on the earth. 15He forms the hearts of all. He understands all their deeds.

16No king is saved by a large army. No warrior is rescued by great strength. 17The horse is a false hope for rescue. Even with its great strength, it cannot save.

18The eyes of the LORD are on those who fear HimhimHim, on those who hope in HishisHis faithful love. 19He saves their lives from death and keeps them alive in famine.

20We wait for the LORD. He is our help and our shield. 21For our hearts rejoice in HimhimHim, because we trust in HishisHis holy name. 22May YouryourYour faithful love be upon us, O the LORD, as we wait for YouyouYou.
