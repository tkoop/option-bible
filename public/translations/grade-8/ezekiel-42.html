Rooms Next to the Temple

1The man led me out through the north gate into the outer courtyard. Then he brought me to the rooms that faced the separate area and the building on the north side. 2That space was one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide.

3The upper rooms were set back from the lower ones, making three levels. They faced the twenty cubits of the inner courtyard and the pavementroadwaypavement of the outer courtyard. 4In front of the rooms was a walkway ten cubits wide, with an entrance one cubit wide. Their doors faced north.

5The upper rooms were smaller because the walkways took more space from them than from the lower and middle rooms. 6The rooms had three levels and no pillars like the courtyard pillars, so the upper rooms were set back more than the lower and middle rooms.

7An outer wall in front of the rooms was fifty cubits long. It ran along the rooms toward the outer courtyard. 8The rooms facing the outer courtyard were fifty cubits long, while the rooms facing the temple were one hundred cubits long.

9Below these rooms was an entrance on the east side, used when entering from the outer courtyard. 10In the wall of the outer courtyard on the east side, in front of the separate area and the building, there were rooms. 11A walkway ran in front of them, just like the one in front of the north rooms. They had the same length and width, and their exits, plans, and doors were the same. 12Like the doors of the south rooms, there was a door at the start of the walkway. It faced the garden wall on the east side as people entered.

Holy Rooms for the Priests

13Then he said to me, "ThetheThe north rooms and the south rooms that face the separate area are holy rooms. The priests who serve near the LORD will eat the most holy offerings there. They will also place the most holy offerings there, along with the grain offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering, because the place is holy."

14When the priests enter, they must not leave the holy area for the outer courtyard. They must leave the holy clothes they serve in there, because those clothes are holy. Then they must put on other clothes before they go near the people.

Measuring the Outer Wall

15After he finished measuring the inner temple, he brought me out through the east gate and measured all around. 16He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds. 17He measured the north side: five hundred reeds. 18He measured the south side: five hundred reeds. 19He measured the west side: five hundred reeds. 20He measured the whole area on all four sides. It had a wall all around, five hundred reeds long and five hundred reeds wide, to separate the holy place from the common place.
