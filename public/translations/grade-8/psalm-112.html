The Blessing of a God-Fearing Life

1Praise the LORD! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD and greatly delights in HishisHis commands. 2His children will be mighty in the land, and the generation of the upright will be blessed. 3Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever. 4Light dawns in the darkness for the upright—gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

5Good is the man who is gracious and lends; he will uphold his cause in judgmentjudgementjudgment. 6For he will never be shaken; the righteous will be remembered forever. 7He will not fear bad news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.

8His heart is secure; he will not be afraid until he sees his enemies fall. 9He has scattered his gifts to the poor; his righteousness endures forever; his horn will be lifted high in honourhonourhonor. 10The wicked will see and be angry; they will grind their teeth and waste away; the desire of the wicked will perish.
