Atonement for an Unsolved Murder

1If someone is found killed and lying in a field in the land the LORD your God is giving you to own, and no one knows who killed him, 2your elders and judges must go out and measure the distance from the body to the cities around it. 3The elders of the city closest to the body must take a young cow that has never been worked and never pulled a yoke. 4They must bring it down to a valley with running water that has not been plowed or planted, and break the cow's neck there. 5The priests, the sons of Levi, must come forward, because the LORD your God chose them to serve HimhimHim and to bless in the LORD's name, and by their decision every dispute and every assault is settled. 6All the elders of that city closest to the body must wash their hands over the cow whose neck was broken in the valley.

7They must say, “Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it. 8Forgive, O LORD, YouryourYour people Israel whom YouyouYou have redeemed, and do not hold the guilt of innocent blood against YouryourYour people Israel.” Then the bloodshed will be forgiven.

9So you will remove the guilt of innocent blood from among you, because you are doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD.

Marrying a Woman Taken in War

10When you go out to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God hands them over to you, you will take captives. 11If you see a beautiful woman among the captives and want her, you may take her as your wife. 12Bring her into your home. She must shave her head and trim her nails. 13She must take off the clothes she wore as a captive. Then she may live in your house and mourn her father and mother for a full month. After that, you may sleep with her and be her husband, and she will be your wife. 14If you are not pleased with her, let her go wherever she wishes. But you must not sell her for money, and you must not treat her like a slave, because you have humiliated her.

Rights of the Firstborn Son

15If a man has two wives, one he loves and one he hates, and both have sons, and the firstborn son belongs to the wife he hates, 16then on the day he divides his property among his sons, he may not treat the son of the loved wife as the firstborn instead of the firstborn son of the hated wife. 17He must recognizerecogniserecognize the firstborn son of the hated wife by giving him a double share of everything he owns, because that son is the first sign of his strength. The right of the firstborn belongs to him.

A Rebellious Son and Burial of the Executed

18If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey his father or mother, and even when they correct him he will not listen, 19his father and mother must take hold of him and bring him to the elders of his city, at the gate of his town. 20They must say to the elders, “This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious. He will not obey us. He is a glutton and a drunkard.” 21Then all the men of his city must stone him to death. You must remove the evil from among you, and all Israel will hear and be afraid.

22If a man commits a sin worthy of death and is put to death, and you hang his body on a tree, 23his body must not stay on the tree overnight. You must bury him that same day, because a person hung on a tree is under God's curse. Do not make your land unclean, which the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
