Passover

1Watch for the month of Abib, and celebrate the Passover for the LORD your God. In that month, the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt at night. 2Sacrifice the Passover to the LORD your God from your flock and herd. Do it at the place the LORD chooses for HishisHis name to live. 3Do not eat bread with yeast in it with the Passover. For seven days eat flatbread, the bread of hardship, because you left Egypt in a hurry. This will help you remember the day you left Egypt for as long as you live. 4For seven days, no yeast should be seen anywhere in your land. Also, none of the meat from the animal you sacrifice on the first evening may be left overnight until morning.

5You may not sacrifice the Passover in just any town the LORD your God gives you. 6Instead, sacrifice it at the place the LORD your God chooses for HishisHis name. Do it in the evening, when the sun goes down, at the time you left Egypt. 7Cook it and eat it there, at the place the LORD your God chooses. Then in the morning, turn and go back to your tents. 8Eat flatbread for six days. On the seventh day, hold a special meeting for the LORD your God. Do not do any work that day.

The Feast of Weeks

9Count seven weeks. Start counting when the sickle is first put to the standing grain. 10Then celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of WeeksweeksWeeks for the LORD your God. Give a freewill offering from your hand, as much as the LORD your God blesses you. 11Rejoice before the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses for HishisHis name. You, your sons, your daughters, your male and female servants, the Levite in your towns, and the stranger, the orphan, and the widow among you should all rejoice. 12Remember that you were a slave in Egypt. Keep and follow these rules.

The Feast of Tabernacles

13Celebrate the FeastfeastFeast of Tabernacles for seven days, after you gather grain from your threshing floor and wine from your winepress. 14Rejoice in your feast. You, your sons, your daughters, your male and female servants, the Levite, and the stranger, the orphan, and the widow in your towns should all rejoice. 15For seven days, celebrate this feast for the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses. The LORD your God will bless you in all your crops and in all the work of your hands. You will be very happy. 16Three times a year, all your men must appear before the LORD your God at the place HeheHe chooses: at the FeastfeastFeast of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread, the FeastfeastFeast of WeeksweeksWeeks, and the FeastfeastFeast of Tabernacles. No one should appear before the LORD empty-handed. 17Each person should give as much as he can, based on the blessing the LORD your God has given you.

Judges and Justice

18Appoint judges and officers for your tribes in all the towns the LORD your God gives you. They must judge the people fairly. 19Do not twist justice. Do not show favouritismfavouritismfavoritism. Do not take a bribe, because a bribe blinds the wise and twists the words of good people. 20Follow justice and only justice, so that you may live and take the land the LORD your God gives you. 21Do not plant an Asherah pole beside the altar of the LORD your God. 22Do not set up a sacred stone, which the LORD your God hates.
