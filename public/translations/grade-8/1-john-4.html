Testing the Spirits

1Dear friends, do not believe every spirit. Instead, test the spirits to see if they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. 2This is how you can know the SpiritspiritSpirit of God: Every spirit that says Jesus Christ came in the flesh is from God. 3But every spirit that does not agree that Jesus Christ came in the flesh is not from God. That is the spirit of the antichrist. You have heard it is coming, and it is already in the world.

4You are from God, dear children. You have won the victory over them, because the OneoneOne who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world. 5They belong to the world, so they speak like the world, and the world listens to them. 6We belong to God. Whoever knows God listens to us. Whoever does not belong to God does not listen to us. This is how we know the SpiritspiritSpirit of truth and the spirit of error.

God Is Love

7Dear friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 8Anyone who does not love does not know God, because God is love. 9God showed HishisHis love to us by sending HishisHis only SonsonSon into the world so that we could live through HimhimHim. 10This is love: not that we loved God, but that HeheHe loved us and sent HishisHis SonsonSon as the sacrifice that takes away our sins.

11Dear friends, since God loved us that much, we should love one another too. 12No one has ever seen God. But if we love one another, God lives in us, and HishisHis love is made complete in us. 13We know we live in HimhimHim and HeheHe lives in us, because HeheHe has given us HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit. 14We have seen and tell others that the FatherfatherFather sent HishisHis SonsonSon to save the world. 15If anyone says that Jesus is the SonsonSon of God, God lives in him, and he lives in God. 16We have known and believed the love God has for us. God is love. Whoever stays in love stays in God, and God stays in him.

Love Gives Us Confidence

17Love is made complete among us so that we can be brave on the day of judgmentjudgementjudgment. We can be brave because in this world we are like HimhimHim. 18There is no fear in love. Perfect love throws fear out, because fear brings punishment. The person who is afraid has not been made complete in love. 19We love because HeheHe first loved us.

20If someone says, “I love God,” but hates his brother, he is a liar. The person who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, cannot love God, whom he has not seen. 21We have this command from HimhimHim: Anyone who loves God must also love his brother.
