1So you have no excuse, whoever you are, when you judge someone else. When you judge another person, you condemn yourself, because you who judge do the same things. 2We know that God’s judgmentjudgementjudgment on people who do such things is true. 3Do you think you will escape God’s judgmentjudgementjudgment, you who judge others for doing these things but do them yourself? 4Or do you look down on the riches of HishisHis kindness, patience, and self-control? Don’t you know that God’s kindness leads you to turn from sin? 5But because of your hard and unrepentant heart, you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath, when God’s righteous judgmentjudgementjudgment will be revealed.

God Judges by What We Do

6He will repay each person according to what they have done. 7To those who patiently do good and seek glory, honourhonourhonor, and life that never ends, HeheHe will give eternal life. 8But to those who are selfish, who do not obey the truth but follow evil, there will be wrath and anger. 9There will be trouble and distress for every person who does evil, first for the Jew, then for the Gentile. 10But there will be glory, honourhonourhonor, and peace for everyone who does good, first for the Jew, then for the Gentile. 11For God does not show favouritismfavouritismfavoritism.

12All who sin without the law will also perish without the law. All who sin under the law will be judged by the law. 13For it is not those who hear the law who are right with God, but those who do what the law says who will be made right. 14When people who do not have the law do by nature what the law requires, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law. 15They show that the work of the law is written on their hearts. Their conscience also bears witness, and their thoughts sometimes accuse them and sometimes defend them. 16This will take place on the day when God, through Jesus Christ, judges people’s hidden secrets, as my good news says.

The Law and the Jews

17Now, if you call yourself a Jew and rely on the law and take pride in God, 18and you know HishisHis will and can choose what is best because you have been taught from the law, 19and you are sure that you are a guide for the blind and a light for people in the dark, 20and you are a teacher of foolish people and a teacher of children, because you have in the law the form of knowledge and truth. 21So, you teach others, but do you not teach yourself? You preach, “Do not steal,” but do you steal? 22You say, “Do not commit adultery,” but do you commit adultery? You hate idols, but do you rob temples? 23You brag about the law, but do you dishonourdishonourdishonor God by breaking the law? 24As it is written, “Because of you, God’s name is insulted among the Gentiles.”

True Circumcision

25Circumcision has value if you obey the law. But if you break the law, your circumcision becomes like no circumcision at all. 26So, if an uncircumcised man keeps the law’s requirements, won’t he be treated as if he were circumcised? 27And the one who is uncircumcised by nature but keeps the law will judge you, who have the written law and circumcision but break the law. 28A person is not a true Jew just because he looks like one on the outside, and circumcision is not just a physical act. 29No, a true Jew is one inwardly, and true circumcision is of the heart, by the SpiritspiritSpirit, not by the written law. That person’s praise comes from God, not from people.
