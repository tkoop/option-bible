Hoshea Rules Israel and Assyria Attacks

1In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king of Israel in Samaria. He ruled for nine years. 2He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, but not like the kings of Israel who had ruled before him.

3Shalmaneser king of Assyria attacked him. Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute. 4But the king of Assyria found out that Hoshea was plotting against him. Hoshea had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and he had not sent the yearly tribute to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria arrested Hoshea and put him in prison.

5Then the king of Assyria marched through the whole land. He came to Samaria and surrounded it for three years. 6In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria. He carried the Israelites away to Assyria and settled them in Halah, Gozan, the Habor River, and the cities of Media.

Why Israel Was Carried Away

7This happened because the Israelites had sinned against the LORD their God. He had brought them up out of Egypt, away from Pharaoh king of Egypt. But they worshiped other gods. 8They followed the customs of the nations that the LORD had driven out ahead of the Israelites. They also followed customs that the kings of Israel had made.

9The Israelites secretly did things that were not right against the LORD their God. They built high places in all their cities, from small guard towers to fortified cities. 10They set up sacred stone pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.

11They burned incense on all the high places, just as the nations had done that the LORD drove out ahead of them. They did evil things that made the LORD angry. 12They worshiped idols, even though the LORD had told them not to do that.

They copied the nations around them, even though the LORD had told them not to act like those nations. 16They ignored all the commands of the LORD their God. They made two metal calves for themselves. They made an Asherah pole. They bowed down to all the stars in the sky. They served Baal.

23Finally the LORD removed Israel from HishisHis presence, just as HeheHe had said through all HishisHis servants the prophets. He carried the Israelites away from their own land to Assyria, where they are to this day.

New Peoples Settle in Samaria

24The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim. He settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the Israelites. They took over Samaria and lived in its cities. 25At first, when they lived there, they did not worship the LORD. So the LORD sent lions among them, and the lions killed some of them.

26They told the king of Assyria, "ThetheThe nations you moved and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the law of the God of this land. So HeheHe has sent lions among them. The lions are killing them, because they do not know the law of the God of this land."

27The king of Assyria ordered, "Send one of the priests you took from there. Let him go live there and teach them the law of the God of this land."

28So one of the priests who had been carried away from Samaria came and lived in Bethel. He taught them how to worship the LORD.

Mixed Worship in Samaria

29But each nation made its own gods. They put them in the high places the people of Samaria had built. Each nation did this in the cities where it lived. 30The men of Babylon made Succoth Benoth. The men of Cuth made Nergal. The men of Hamath made Ashima. 31The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

32They worshiped the LORD, but they also chose priests from among themselves for the high places. These priests sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places. 33They worshiped the LORD, but they also served their own gods, following the customs of the nations they had come from. 34Even today they still follow their old customs. They do not worship the LORD. They do not follow the rules, laws, or commands that the LORD gave the descendants of Jacob. He named them Israel.

35The LORD had made an agreement with them. He commanded them, "Do not worship other gods. Do not bow down to them. Do not serve them. 40But they did not listen. They kept following their old ways. 41So these nations worshiped the LORD, but they also served their idols. Their children and grandchildren did the same thing their ancestors did, and they still do it today.
