Gideon Destroys Baal’s Altar

25That night the the LORD said to him, “Take your father’s second bull, the one that is seven years old, and tear down your father’s altar to Baal. Cut down the Asherah pole beside it. 26Then build an altar to the the LORD your God on top of this stronghold, using the wood of the Asherah you cut down. Take the second bull and offer it as a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah.”

27Gideon took ten of his servants and did what the the LORD had told him. But because he was afraid of his family and the men of the town, he did it at night instead of during the day.

28When the men of the town got up early the next morning, they saw that Baal’s altar was torn down, the Asherah beside it was cut down, and the second bull had been offered on the new altar.

29They asked one another, “Who did this?” After they looked into it, they were told, “Gideon son of Joash did it.”

30The men of the town said to Joash, “Bring out your son so he may die, because he tore down Baal’s altar and cut down the Asherah beside it.”

31Joash said to all who stood against him, “Are you going to defend Baal? Will you save him? Anyone who defends him should be put to death by morning. If Baal is a god, let him defend himself, because someone tore down his altar.”

32So that day they called Gideon “Jerub-Baal,” saying, “Let Baal contend with him, because he tore down Baal’s altar.”

33All the Midianites, Amalekites, and people of the east gathered together. They crossed the Jordan and camped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Jezreel.

35He sent messengers throughout Manasseh, and they were called to arms. He also sent messengers to Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they came up to meet him.

Gideon Tests God with the Fleece

36Gideon said to God, “If YouyouYou are going to save Israel by my hand, as YouyouYou said, 37then I will put a wool fleece on the threshing floor. If there is dew only on the fleece and all the ground is dry, I will know that YouyouYou will save Israel by my hand, as YouyouYou said.”

38That is what happened. When Gideon got up early the next morning and squeezed the fleece, he wrung enough dew from it to fill a bowl with water.

39Gideon said to God, “Do not be angry with me. Let me speak once more. Let me test the fleece one more time. Let the fleece be dry, and let all the ground be covered with dew.”

40That night God did so. Only the fleece was dry, and dew covered all the ground.
