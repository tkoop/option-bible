Praise God

1Praise the LORD! It is good to sing praises to our God, and it is pleasant and fitting to praise HimhimHim. 2The LORD builds up Jerusalem and gathers the exiles of Israel. 3He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. 4He counts the stars and calls each one by name. 5Great is our Lord and mighty in power; HishisHis understanding has no limit. 6The LORD lifts up the humble and throws the wicked down to the ground.

God Cares for People and Creation

7Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the harp. 8He covers the sky with clouds and sends rain to the earth. He makes grass grow on the hills. 9He feeds the cattle and gives food to young ravens when they call. 10He does not delight in the strength of a horse, and HeheHe does not take pleasure in a man’s strong legs. 11The LORD delights in those who fear HimhimHim, in those who hope in HishisHis faithful love.

God’s Word to Israel

12Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion. 13For HeheHe has strengthened the bars of your gates and blessed your children within you. 14He makes peace within your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat. 15He sends HishisHis command to the earth, and HishisHis word runs swiftly. 16He gives snow like wool and scatters frost like ashes. 17He hurls down hail like pebbles. Who can stand against HishisHis freezing wind? 18He sends HishisHis word and melts them; HeheHe stirs up HishisHis winds, and the waters flow. 19He declares HishisHis word to Jacob and HishisHis laws and judgmentsjudgementsjudgments to Israel. 20He has not done this for any other nation; they do not know HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments. Praise the LORD!
