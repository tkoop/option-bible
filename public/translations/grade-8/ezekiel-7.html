The End Has Come for Israel

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“And you, son of man, thus says the Lord the LORD to the land of Israel: An end! The end has come upon the four corners of the earth. 3The end is now upon you, and I will send MymyMy anger against you. The Day of Wrath

10“Look, the day! Look, it comes! Doom has gone out; the rod has budded; arrogance has blossomed. 11Violence has risen into a rod of wickedness. None of them will remain, none of their multitude, none of their wealth, nor anything of value among them. 12The time has come; the day has arrived. Let the buyer not rejoice, and the seller not mourn, for wrath is upon all their multitude. 13For the seller will not recover what he sold while both remain alive. For the vision concerning all their multitude will not be reversed. Because of their iniquity, no one will preserve his life. 14They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready, but no one goes to war, for MymyMy wrath is upon all their multitude.”

15“The sword is outside; plague and famine are inside. Whoever is in the field will die by the sword, and whoever is in the city will be consumed by famine and plague. 16Their survivors who escape will be on the mountains, mourning like doves of the valleys, each for his own iniquity. 17Every hand will go limp, and every knee will be weak as water. 18They will put on sackcloth, and terror will cover them. Shame will be on every face, and all their heads will be shaved. 19They will throw their silver into the streets, and their gold will be like something unclean. Their silver and gold will not save them in the day of the wrath of the LORD. They will not satisfy their hunger or fill their stomachs, for their iniquity has become a stumbling block.
