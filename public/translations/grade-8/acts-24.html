Tertullus Accuses Paul

1Five days later, the high priest Ananias went down to Caesarea with some elders and a lawyer named Tertullus. They brought charges against Paul before the governor. 2When Paul was called in, Tertullus began to accuse him. He said, “Because of you, most excellent Felix, we enjoy great peace, and because of your care, good reforms have come to this nation. 3We accept this everywhere and in every way with full thanks. 4But so I will not take more of your time, I ask you, in your kindness, to listen to us for a short time.”

5“We have found this man to be a troublemaker. He stirs up riots among all the Jews throughout the world. He is a leader of the Nazarene group. 6He even tried to make the temple unclean, and we seized him. 8If you examine him, you yourself can learn the truth about all these charges we are making against him.” 9The Jews agreed and said these things were true.

Paul Defends Himself

10The governor motioned for Paul to speak. Paul replied, “I know you have judged this nation for many years, so I am glad to defend myself. 11You can check and see that it has not been more than twelve days since I went up to Jerusalem to worship. 12No one found me arguing with anyone in the temple, causing a crowd in the synagogues, or stirring up trouble in the city. 13They cannot prove to you the charges they are making against me now. 14But I admit this to you: I worship the God of our fathers according to the Way, which they call a sect. I believe everything in the LawlawLaw and what the ProphetsprophetsProphets wrote. 15I have hope in God, which these men also share: there will be a resurrection of both the righteous and the wicked. 16Because of this, I always try to keep a clear conscience before God and before people. 17After many years, I came to bring gifts to help my people and to offer sacrifices. 18They found me in the temple after I had been purified. There was no crowd and no uproar. Some Jews from Asia started the trouble. 19They should be here before you to accuse me if they have anything against me. 20Or let these men here say what wrong they found when I stood before the Jewish council. 21The only thing is this one cry I shouted while standing among them: ‘It is about the resurrection of the dead that I am on trial before you today.’”

Felix Delays the Decision

22Felix knew a lot about the Way, so he put the case off. He said, “When Commander Lysias comes down, I will decide your case.” 23He ordered the centurion to keep Paul under guard, but to give him some freedom and let his friends care for him.

Paul Speaks to Felix and Drusilla

24Several days later, Felix came with his wife Drusilla, who was Jewish. He sent for Paul and listened to him talk about faith in Christ Jesus. 25As Paul talked about doing what is right, self-control, and the coming judgmentjudgementjudgment, Felix became afraid. He said, “Go away for now. When I have time, I will send for you.” 26At the same time, he hoped Paul would give him money. So he sent for him often and talked with him. 27After two years, Porcius Festus took Felix’s place. Because Felix wanted to please the Jews, he left Paul in prison.
