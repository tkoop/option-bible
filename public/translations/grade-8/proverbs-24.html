Do Not Envy the Wicked

1Do not envy wicked people, and do not wish to be with them. 2Their hearts plan violence, and their lips talk about causing trouble.

Wisdom Builds and Strengthens

3By wisdom a house is built, and by understanding it is made strong. 4Through knowledge its rooms are filled with every precious and pleasant treasure.

5A wise man is strong, and a man who has knowledge grows stronger. 6With wise plans you can fight your battles, and with many advisers there is victory. 7Wisdom is too high for a fool; he has nothing to say when important matters are discussed at the city gate. 8Anyone who plans evil will be called a schemer. 9Foolish plans are sinful, and people hate a mocker.

Rescue Those in Danger

10If you give up in a time of trouble, your strength is small. 11Rescue those who are being led away to death, and hold back those stumbling toward slaughter. 12If you say, "We did not know about this," does not HeheHe who weighs hearts see it? Does not HeheHe who guards your life know it? And will HeheHe not pay back a person for what he has done?

13Eat honey, my son, because it is good, and honeycomb is sweet to your taste. 14Know that wisdom is like that for your soul: if you find it, there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.

15Do not, wicked person, lie in wait against the home of a good person, and do not destroy his resting place. 16A good person may fall seven times, but he gets up again, while wicked people stumble into ruin.

17Do not be glad when your enemy falls, and do not let your heart rejoice when he stumbles. 18Otherwise the the LORD will see it and be displeased, and HeheHe will turn HishisHis anger away from him.

19Do not be upset by evil people, and do not envy wicked people. 20For evil people have no future, and their hope will be snuffed out.

Wise Sayings About Justice and Laziness

21Fear the the LORD and the king, my son, and do not join those who rebel. 22For they will bring sudden trouble; who knows what ruin they both can cause?

23These are also sayings for wise people: It is not good to show favouritismfavouritismfavoritism in court. 24Whoever says to a wicked person, "YouyouYou are innocent," peoples will curse him, and nations will denounce him.

25But it will go well with those who convict the guilty, and a rich blessing will come to them. 26An honest answer is as welcome as a kiss on the lips.

27Finish your outdoor work and get your field ready; after that, build your house. 28Do not testify against your neighbourneighbourneighbor without a good reason, and do not deceive with your lips.

29Do not say, "I will do to him what he did to me; I will pay him back for what he has done." 30I walked past the field of a lazy person and by the vineyard of someone with no sense.

31Thorns had grown all over the field, weeds covered the ground, and the stone wall was broken down. 32I looked at it and thought about it; I saw it and learned a lesson.

33"A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest," 34and your poverty will come like a robber, and your need like an armed man.
