Balak and Balaam Prepare Sacrifices

1Balaam said to Balak, "Build me seven altars here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me here." 2Balak did what Balaam said. They offered a bull and a ram on each altar. 3Then Balaam said to Balak, "Stay here beside your offering while I go off. Perhaps the LORD will come to meet me. Whatever HeheHe shows me, I will tell you." So Balaam went to a bare hill. 4God met him there, and Balaam said, "I have set up seven altars, and I have offered a bull and a ram on each one." 5The LORD put a message in Balaam's mouth and said, "Go back to Balak and say this." 6So Balaam returned and found Balak standing beside his offering, with all the Moabite leaders.

Balaam's First Oracle

7Then Balaam spoke his message: "Balak brought me from Aram. The king of Moab brought me from the eastern mountains. He said, 'Come, curse Jacob for me. Come, speak against Israel.' 8How can I curse those God has not cursed? How can I speak against those the LORD has not spoken against? 9From the rocky peaks I see them. From the hills I look down on them. I see a people who live alone. They do not count themselves among the nations. 10Who can count Jacob's dust? Who can number even a fourth of Israel? Let me die like good people die. Let my end be like theirs!"

11Balak said to Balaam, "What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, but you have blessed them!" 12Balaam answered, "Must I not speak what the LORD puts in my mouth?"

13Then Balak said to him, "Come with me to another place. From there you will see them, but you will see only part of them, not all of them. Curse them for me from there." 14So Balak took him to the field of Zophim on top of Pisgah. There Balaam built seven altars and offered a bull and a ram on each one. 15Balaam said to Balak, "Stay here beside your offering while I meet with HimhimHim over there."

16The LORD met with Balaam and put a message in his mouth. He said, "Go back to Balak and say this." 17So Balaam went back and found Balak standing beside his offering, with the Moabite leaders. Balak asked him, "What did the LORD say?"

Balaam's Second Oracle

18Then Balaam spoke his message: "Get up, Balak, and listen. Hear me, son of Zippor. 19God is not a man, so HeheHe does not lie. He is not a human being, so HeheHe does not change HishisHis mind. Does HeheHe say something and not do it? Does HeheHe promise something and not keep it? 20I have been told to bless. He has blessed, and I cannot take it back. 21He sees no wrong in Jacob and no trouble in Israel. The LORD their God is with them. The shout of the KingkingKing is among them. 22God brought them out of Egypt. They are as strong as a wild ox. 23No magic can hurt Jacob. No spell can harm Israel. Now people will say about Jacob and Israel, 'Look what God has done!' 24ThetheThe people rise like a lioness. They rouse themselves like a lion. They will not lie down until they eat their prey and drink the blood of those they have killed."

25Then Balak said to Balaam, "Do not curse them at all, and do not bless them at all!" 26Balaam answered, "Did I not tell you I must do whatever the LORD says?"

Balak Tries Again

27Then Balak said to Balaam, "Come, let me take you to another place. Maybe God will be pleased to let you curse them for me from there." 28So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, looking out over the wasteland. 29Balaam said to Balak, "Build me seven altars here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me here." 30Balak did what Balaam said and offered a bull and a ram on each altar.
