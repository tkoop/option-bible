The Sayings of Agur

1The words of Agur son of Jakeh—an oracle. This man declared to Ithiel, to Ithiel and Ucal.

2Surely I am more foolish than any man, and I do not have human understanding.

3I have not learned wisdom, and I do not know the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne.

4Who has gone up to Heavenheavenheaven and come down? Who has gathered the wind in HishisHis hands? Who has wrapped the waters in HishisHis cloak? Who has set up all the ends of the earth? What is HishisHis name, and what is HishisHis Son's name? Surely you know!

5Every word of God is pure; HeheHe is a shield to those who take refuge in HimhimHim.

6Do not add to HishisHis words, or HeheHe may rebuke you and prove you a liar.

7Two things I ask of YouyouYou; do not keep them from me before I die: 8keep falsehood and lies far from me; give me neither poverty nor riches, but give me only the food I need. 9Otherwise, I may have too much and deny YouyouYou, saying, "Who is the LORD?" Or I may become poor and steal, and so dishonourdishonourdishonor the name of my God.

10Do not slander a servant to his master, or he will curse you, and you will be guilty.

Four Troubling Generations

11There is a generation that curses its father and does not bless its mother. 12There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes but is not washed from its filth. 13There is a generation whose eyes are proud and whose eyelids are lifted high. 14There is a generation whose teeth are swords and whose molars are knives, to devour the poor from the earth and the needy from among men.

Things That Never Have Enough

15The leech has two daughters: "Give! Give!" Three things are never satisfied, and four never say, "Enough!": 16Sheol, a barren womb, land that is never satisfied with water, and fire that never says, "Enough!" 17The eye that mocks a father and scorns obedience to a mother will be pecked out by the ravens of the valley and eaten by young eagles.

18Three things are too wonderful for me, four that I cannot understand: 19the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a snake on a rock, the way of a ship on the high seas, and the way of a man with a young woman.

20So is the way of an adulterous woman: she eats and wipes her mouth and says, "I have done nothing wrong." 21Under three things the earth trembles, under four it cannot bear up: 22a servant who becomes king, a fool who is filled with food, 23an unloved woman who marries, and a maidservant who displaces her mistress.

Small but Wise Creatures

24Four things on EarthEarthearth are small, yet they are very wise: 25ants are creatures of little strength, yet they store up their food in the summer; 26rock badgers are creatures of little power, yet they make their homes in the rocks; 27locusts have no king, yet they march in ranks; 28a lizard can be caught by hand, yet it is found in kings' palaces.

29Three things are stately in their walk, four that move with stately bearing: 30a lion, mightiest among beasts, who does not turn back from anything; 31a strutting rooster; a he-goat; and a king with his army around him. 32If you have acted foolishly by exalting yourself, or if you have planned evil, put your hand over your mouth. 33For as churning milk produces butter, and twisting the nose produces blood, so stirring up anger produces strife.
