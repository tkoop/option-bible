Jerusalem Under Siege

1In the ninth year of King Zedekiah's rule, on the tenth day of the tenth month, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon marched against Jerusalem with his whole army. They camped outside the city and built siege works all around it. 2The city stayed under siege until the eleventh year of King Zedekiah's rule. 3By the ninth day of the fourth month, the famine in the city had become so bad that the people had no food.

Zedekiah Is Captured and Blinded

4Then the city wall was broken through. At night the whole army fled through the gate between the two walls near the king's garden, even though the Babylonians were all around the city. They fled toward the Arabah, a dry desert valley. 5But the Babylonian army chased the king and caught him on the plains of Jericho. All his soldiers scattered. 6They captured him and took him to King Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah, where he was sentenced. 7They killed Zedekiah's sons in front of him. Then they put out his eyes, tied him with bronze chains, and took him to Babylon.

The Temple Is Destroyed and the People Exiled

8On the seventh day of the fifth month, in the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar's rule, Nebuzaradan, the commander of the guard and an official of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem. 9He burned down the temple of the LORD, the royal palace, and all the houses in Jerusalem. He burned every important building. 10The whole Babylonian army under the commander tore down the walls around Jerusalem. 11Nebuzaradan carried into exile the people still left in the city, along with the rest of the people and those who had gone over to the king of Babylon. 12But he left behind some of the poorest people to work the vineyards and fields.

13The Babylonians broke up the bronze pillars, the movable stands, and the great bronze basin called the SeaseaSea in the temple of the LORD. They carried all that bronze to Babylon. 14They also took the pots, shovels, wick trimmers, dishes, and all the bronze tools used in the temple service. 15The commander took the incense pans and the sprinkling bowls, both gold and silver. 16The bronze from the two pillars, the SeaseaSea, and the movable stands that Solomon had made for the temple of the LORD was too heavy to weigh. 17Each pillar was about 27 feet tall. The bronze top on one pillar was about 4.5 feet high and was decorated with a network and bronze pomegranates all around. The second pillar was the same.

18The commander took as prisoners Seraiah the chief priest, Zephaniah the second priest, and the three gatekeepers. 19From the people still in the city, he took the officer in charge of the soldiers, five of the king's close advisers, the secretary in charge of drafting people, and sixty men found in the city. 20Nebuzaradan took them all to King Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah. 21There, in the land of Hamath, the king had them put to death. So Judah was carried away from its land into captivity.

Gedaliah Is Killed and Jehoiachin Is Freed

22King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon put Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, in charge of the people left in Judah. 23When the army officers and their men heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah governor, they came to him at Mizpah. They were Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth from Netophah, Jaazaniah son of the Maakathite, and their men.

24Gedaliah swore to them and their men, "Do not be afraid of the Babylonian officials. Settle down in the land, serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you." 25But in the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, who was from the royal family, came with ten men. They attacked and killed Gedaliah and his men, both the Jews and the Babylonians who were with him at Mizpah. 26Then all the people, no matter their status, along with the army officers, fled to Egypt because they were afraid of the Babylonians.

27In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of Jehoiachin king of Judah, on the twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month, Evil-Merodach king of Babylon, in his first year as king, released Jehoiachin king of Judah from prison. 28He spoke kindly to him and gave him a seat of honourhonourhonor above the other kings who were with him in Babylon. 29So Jehoiachin took off his prison clothes and ate at the king's table for the rest of his life. 30Day by day the king gave him a regular allowance as long as he lived.
