Greeting to the Chosen Lady

1The elder writes to the chosen lady and her children. I love them in the truth. I am not the only one—everyone who knows the truth loves them too. 2We love them because the truth stays in us and will be with us forever. 3Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the FatherfatherFather and from Jesus Christ, the Father's SonsonSon. They will stay with us in truth and love.

Walking in Truth and Love

4I was very glad to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the FatherfatherFather commanded us. 5Now I ask you, lady, not to write a new command for you, but to remember the command we have had from the start: love one another. 6This is love: walk by HishisHis commands. This is the command you have heard from the start, so you should walk in it.

Beware of Deceivers

7Many deceivers have gone out into the world. They do not admit that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. Anyone like that is a deceiver and an antichrist. 8Watch yourselves so you do not lose what we worked for. Instead, you should receive a full reward. 9Everyone who runs ahead and does not stay in Christ's teaching does not have God. The person who stays in that teaching has both the FatherfatherFather and the SonsonSon. 10If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not take him into your house, and do not even greet him. 11Anyone who greets him shares in his evil deeds.

Final Words

12I have a lot to write to you, but I do not want to use paper and ink. I hope to visit you and talk face to face, so our joy will be complete. 13The children of your chosen sister send their greetings to you.
