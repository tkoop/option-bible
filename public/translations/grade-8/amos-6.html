Warning to the Comfortable in Zion

1Woe to those who are at ease in Zion and to those who feel secure on Mount Samaria, the distinguished leaders of the foremost nation, to whom the house of Israel comes. 2Cross over to Calneh and see; go from there to great Hamath; then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are they better than these kingdoms? Is their territory larger than your territory? 3You push off the day of disaster, yet you bring near a reign of violence.

4You lie on beds of ivory and sprawl on your couches. You eat lambs from the flock and calves from the stall. 5You strum on harps like David and make up songs on musical instruments. 6You drink wine from bowls and anoint yourselves with the finest oils, but you do not grieve over the ruin of Joseph.

7So now you will be the first to go into exile, and your feasts and lazy lounging will come to an end.

The LORD Swears JudgmentJudgementJudgment

8The Lord, the LORD, has sworn by HimselfhimselfHimself—the the LORD God of Hosts declares: "I abhor the pride of Jacob and detest his citadels; I will deliver up the city and everything in it."

9If ten men are left in one house, they will die. 10When a relative who is to burn the bodies picks them up to carry them out of the house, he will say to one inside, "IsisIs anyone else with you?" "No," the man will reply. Then the relative will say, "Hush! We must not mention the name of the LORD."

The Coming Destruction

11For look, the LORD gives a command, and HeheHe will smash the great house to pieces and the small house into bits. 12Do horses run on rocky cliffs? Does anyone plow rocks with oxen? But you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into bitterness.

13You who celebrate over nothing, who say, "Didn't we gain power by our own strength?" 14"For look, I will raise up a nation against you, O house of Israel," says the LORD, the God of Hosts, "and they will oppress you from the entrance of Hamath to the Arabah ValleyvalleyValley."
