Sheba Rebels Against David

1A worthless man named Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite, happened to be there. He blew a trumpet and shouted, "We have no share in David, and no part in Jesse's son! Men of Israel, go home to your tents!" 2So all the men of Israel turned away from David and followed Sheba son of Bichri. But the men of Judah stayed with their king, from the Jordan all the way to Jerusalem. 3When David came to his house in Jerusalem, he took the ten concubines he had left to watch the house. He put them in a guarded room, took care of them, but did not sleep with them. They lived like widows until they died.

4The king said to Amasa, "Call the men of Judah together for me in three days, and be here yourself." 5Amasa went to call Judah, but he took longer than the time set for him.

6David said to Abishai, "Sheba son of Bichri will hurt us more than Absalom did. Take your master's men and chase him. If he finds a fortified city, he may get away from us."

Joab Kills Amasa

7Joab's men, the Kerethites, the Pelethites, and all the mighty men went out. They left Jerusalem to chase Sheba son of Bichri. 8When they reached the great stone at Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Joab was wearing his armourarmourarmor, with a sword strapped at his waist in its sheath. As he moved forward, the sword slipped out.

9Joab said to Amasa, "Are you well, my brother?" Then Joab took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him. 10Amasa did not notice the sword in Joab's hand. Joab stabbed him in the stomach, and Amasa's insides spilled onto the ground. He died without another blow. Then Joab and his brother Abishai chased Sheba son of Bichri.

11One of Joab's young men stood by Amasa and shouted, "Whoever is for Joab and whoever is for David, follow Joab!"

12Amasa was rolling in his blood in the middle of the road. When the man saw that everyone stopped to look, he dragged Amasa off the road into a field and threw a cloth over him. 13Once Amasa was moved from the road, everyone passed by and followed Joab to chase Sheba son of Bichri.

14Sheba passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel Beth Maacah. All the Berites joined him and followed him in. 15Joab's men came and surrounded him in Abel Beth Maacah. They built a siege ramp against the city wall, and the whole army with Joab was tearing down the wall.

The Wise Woman at Abel

16A wise woman called out from the city, "Listen! Listen! Please tell Joab to come here so I can speak with him."

17Joab came near, and the woman asked, "Are you Joab?"

He said, "I am."

She said, "Listen to your servant."

He said, "I am listening."

18She said, "Long ago people said to ask for advice at Abel, and that settled things." 19"I am one of the peaceful, faithful people of Israel. You are trying to destroy a city that is like a mother in Israel. Why would you destroy the inheritance of the the LORD?"

20Joab answered, "Far be it from me! Far be it from me to swallow up or destroy!"

21"That is not what I want. A man named Sheba son of Bichri, from the hill country of Ephraim, has rebelled against King David. Hand him over alone, and I will leave the city."

The woman said to Joab, "HishisHis head will be thrown to you over the wall."

22The woman went to all the people with her wise plan. They cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it to Joab. He blew the trumpet, and the men left the city and went home. Joab returned to the king in Jerusalem.

David's Officials

23Joab was over all the army of Israel. Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Kerethites and Pelethites. 24Adoram was in charge of forced labourlabourlabor. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder. 25Sheva was the secretary. Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 26Ira the Jairite was David's priest.
