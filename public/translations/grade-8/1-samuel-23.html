David Rescues Keilah

1People told David, "ThetheThe Philistines are fighting against Keilah and stealing grain from the threshing floors."

2David asked the LORD, "Should I go and attack these Philistines?"

The LORD said, "Go and attack the Philistines and save Keilah."

3David's men said to him, "We are afraid here in Judah. How much more if we go to Keilah to fight the Philistine armies!"

4David asked the LORD again.

The LORD answered him, "Go down to Keilah, because I am handing the Philistines over to you."

5David and his men went to Keilah and fought the Philistines. They struck them with a great blow, drove off their livestock, and saved the people of Keilah.

6When Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, he came with an ephod in his hand.

Saul Plans to Trap David

7Saul was told that David had gone to Keilah. Saul said, "God has handed him over to me, because he has trapped himself in a town with gates and bars."

8Saul called all the people to war, to go down to Keilah and besiege David and his men.

9David learned that Saul was plotting harm against him. He said to Abiathar the priest, "BringbringBring the ephod here."

10David said, "ThetheThe LORD God of Israel, YouryourYour servant has heard that Saul is trying to come to Keilah to destroy the city because of me.

11"Will the men of Keilah hand me over to him? Will Saul come down, as YouryourYour servant has heard? The LORD God of Israel, please tell YouryourYour servant."

The LORD said, "HeheHe will come down."

12David asked, "WillwillWill the men of Keilah hand me and my men over to Saul?"

The LORD said, "They will."

13David and his men, about six hundred, got up and left Keilah. They went wherever they could. When Saul heard that David had escaped from Keilah, he stopped pursuing him.

14David stayed in the wilderness strongholds and in the hill country of the wilderness of Ziph. Saul looked for him every day, but God did not hand David over to him.

Jonathan Encourages David

15David saw that Saul had come out to seek his life. David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh.

16Jonathan, Saul's son, came to David at Horesh and encouraged him to trust in God.

17He said to David, "Don't be afraid. My father Saul won't find you. You will be king over Israel, and I will be second to you. Even my father Saul knows that."

18The two of them made a covenant before the LORD. David stayed at Horesh, and Jonathan went home.

David Escapes Saul

19The Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Isn't David hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh, on the hill of Hachilah south of Jeshimon? 20Now, O king, come down whenever you want. Our part is to hand him over to you."

21Saul said, "May the LORD bless you, for you have had pity on me. 22Go and make sure of it. Learn and watch where he usually goes and who has seen him there, because I am told he is very crafty. 23Look around and learn about all the hiding places where he hides. Then come back to me with sure information, and I will go with you. If he is in the land, I will search him out among all the clans of Judah."

24They got up and went to Ziph ahead of Saul. David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the Arabah south of Jeshimon.

25Saul and his men went to look for David. When David was told, he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. Saul heard about it and chased David into the wilderness of Maon.

26Saul was on one side of the mountain, and David and his men were on the other side. David was hurrying to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to capture them.

27Then a messenger came to Saul and said, "Hurry! The Philistines have raided the land."

28So Saul turned back from chasing David and went to meet the Philistines. That is why that place is called the RockrockRock of Escape.

29David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds of En Gedi.
