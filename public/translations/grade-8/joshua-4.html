Stones Taken from the Jordan

1After the whole nation had crossed the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua, "Choose twelve men from the people, one from each tribe. 2Tell them, 'Take twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests stood. 3Carry them with you and set them down where you camp tonight.'"

4So Joshua called the twelve men he had chosen from the Israelites, one from each tribe.

5Joshua said to them, "Go ahead of the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each man should lift a stone onto his shoulder, one for each tribe of the Israelites. 6This will be a sign among you. In the future, your children will ask what these stones mean to you. 7Tell them that the Jordan's water was cut off before the ark of the covenant of the LORD when it crossed the Jordan. These stones will remind the Israelites forever."

Israel Crosses the Jordan

8The Israelites did as Joshua commanded. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, one for each tribe, carried them to the camp, and set them there. 9Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, where the priests who carried the ark had stood. They are still there today. 10The priests carrying the ark stood in the Jordan until everything the LORD commanded Joshua to tell the people was finished, just as Moses had commanded Joshua. Then the people hurried across. 11After everyone had crossed, the ark of the LORD and the priests crossed in front of the people. 12The men of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over in front of the Israelites, ready for battle, as Moses had told them. 13About forty thousand armed men crossed before the LORD to the plains of Jericho. 14That day the LORD made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel, and they respected him all his life, just as they had respected Moses.

The Priests Leave the River

15Then the LORD said to Joshua, 16"Command the priests carrying the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan."

17So Joshua commanded the priests, "Come up out of the Jordan." 18As soon as the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the middle of the Jordan, their feet touched dry ground. Then the water of the Jordan returned to its place and overflowed all its banks, just as it had before.

Stones Set Up at Gilgal

19The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and camped at Gilgal on the east side of Jericho. 20Joshua set up there the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan. 21He said to the Israelites, "In the future, your children will ask their fathers what these stones mean. 22Tell them that Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground. 23For the LORD your God dried up the water of the Jordan before you until you crossed, just as the LORD your God did to the Red SeaseaSea when HeheHe dried it up before us until we crossed. 24So all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is strong, and so you may fear the LORD your God forever."
