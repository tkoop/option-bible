Korah and His Followers Rebel

The whole community is holy, every one of them, and the LORD is among them. Why do you put yourselves above the LORD's people?"

4When Moses heard this, he fell facedown.

As for Aaron, who is he that you grumble against him?"

Dathan and Abiram Refuse to Come

12Then Moses sent for Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, but they said, "We will not come! 13Isn't it enough that you have brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness? Must you also make yourself ruler over us? 14Besides, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey or given us fields and vineyards to own. Do you think you can fool us? No, we will not come!"

15Then Moses was very angry and said to the LORD, "Do not accept their offering. I have not taken one donkey from them, and I have not wronged any of them."

16Moses said to Korah, "YouyouYou and your whole community must appear before the LORD tomorrow—you, they, and Aaron. 17Each man must take his censer, put incense in it, and bring it before the LORD—250 censers. You and Aaron must bring yours too."

18So each man took his censer, put fire and incense in it, and stood with Moses and Aaron at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 19When Korah had gathered the whole community against them at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, the glory of the LORD appeared to the whole community.

20The LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 21"Separate yourselves from this community so I can destroy them in a moment."

22But Moses and Aaron fell facedown and said, "O God, God of the spirits of all people, if one man sins, will YouyouYou pour out YouryourYour wrath on the whole community?"

23Then the LORD said to Moses, 24"Tell the community to move away from the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram."

25So Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel followed him. 26He warned the community, "Move away now from the tents of these wicked men. Do not touch anything that belongs to them, or you may be swept away because of all their sins."

27So they moved away from the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram came out and stood at the entrances to their tents with their wives, sons, and little children.

The Ground Swallows the Rebels

28Then Moses said, "By this you will know that the LORD sent me to do all these things, that I did not plan them myself. 29If these men die a normal death like everyone else, then the LORD has not sent me. 30But if the LORD does something new, and the ground opens its mouth and swallows them and all that belongs to them, and they go down alive into the grave, then you will know that these men have treated the LORD with contempt."

31As soon as Moses finished speaking all these words, the ground under them split open. 32The EarthEarthearth opened its mouth and swallowed them, their households, all Korah's men, and all their goods. 33They went down alive into the grave, and the earth closed over them. They disappeared from the community. 34At their cries, all the Israelites around them ran away, saying, "ThetheThe EarthEarthearth may swallow us too!"

35Then fire came out from the LORD and burned up the 250 men who were offering incense.

36The LORD said to Moses, 37"Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to take the censers out of the fire and scatter the coals far away, because they are holy. 38The censers of these men who sinned at the cost of their lives must be hammered into sheets to cover the altar, because they were offered before the LORD and are holy. In this way they will be a sign to the Israelites."

39So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers used by those who had been burned and had them hammered into a covering for the altar, 40as a reminder to the Israelites that no one who is not a descendant of Aaron may come near to burn incense before the LORD, or he will be like Korah and his followers, just as the LORD had said through Moses.

Aaron Stops the Plague

41The next day the whole community of Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron. They said, "YouyouYou have killed the LORD's people!"

42But when the community gathered against Moses and Aaron at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, they saw the cloud cover it, and the glory of the LORD appeared. 43Moses and Aaron went to the front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting.

44The LORD said to Moses, 45"Get away from this community so I can destroy them in a moment." Then Moses and Aaron fell facedown.

46Then Moses said to Aaron, "Take your censer, put fire from the altar in it, along with incense, and hurry to the community to make atonement for them. Wrath has come from the LORD; the plague has started."

47So Aaron took the censer, just as Moses had said, and ran into the middle of the assembly. When he saw the plague had begun among the people, he put the incense on it and made atonement for them.

48He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague stopped.

49But 14,700 people died in the plague, besides those who died because of Korah. 50Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, and the plague stopped.
