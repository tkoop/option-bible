Pray for All People

1First of all, I urge that people pray, ask God for help, pray for others, and give thanks for everyone. 2Pray for kings and all who are in authority, so that we can live peaceful and quiet lives with godliness and respect.

God Wants Everyone Saved

5For there is one God and one mediator between God and people: the man Christ Jesus. 6He gave HimselfhimselfHimself as a ransom for all people. This truth was made known at the right time. 7For this I was appointed a preacher and apostle. I am telling the truth; I am not lying. I am a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.

How Men and Women Should Worship

8So I want the men in every place to pray. They should lift up holy hands, without anger or arguing.

9In the same way, I want the women to dress modestly, with decency and self-control. They should not focus on fancy braided hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothes. 10Instead, they should do good works, which is proper for women who claim to worship God.

Women Learning and Teaching

11A woman should learn quietly and be fully submissive. 12I do not allow a woman to teach or to have authority over a man. She must be quiet.

13For Adam was made first, and then Eve. 14Adam was not deceived. The woman was deceived and sinned. 15But she will be saved through having children, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness with self-control.
