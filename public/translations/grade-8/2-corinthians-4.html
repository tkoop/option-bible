Paul's Ministry of the Gospel

1Since God has been merciful and given us this ministry, we do not give up. 2Instead, we have turned away from secret and shameful ways. We do not use tricks, and we do not twist God's word. By speaking the truth openly, we show that we are honest before every person's conscience and before God. 3Even if our gospel is hidden, it is hidden from people who are being lost. 4The god of this age has blinded the minds of people who do not believe. They cannot see the light of the gospel that shows Christ's glory. Christ is the image of God. 5We do not preach about ourselves. We preach Jesus Christ as Lord, and we are your servants for Jesus' sake. 6For God, who said, "Let light shine out of darkness," has made HishisHis light shine in our hearts. He gives us the light of knowing God's glory, and we see that glory in the face of Jesus Christ.

Treasure in Clay Jars

7But we have this treasure in jars of clay. This shows that the great power is from God, not from us. 8We are pressed hard on every side, but not crushed. We are confused, but not hopeless. 9We are persecuted, but not abandoned. We are struck down, but not destroyed. 10We always carry Jesus' death in our bodies, so that Jesus' life may also be shown in our bodies. 11We who are alive are always being handed over to death for Jesus' sake, so that HishisHis life may be shown in our dying bodies. 12So death is at work in us, but life is at work in you.

Living by Faith

13It is written, "I believed; therefore I spoke." We have that same spirit of faith, so we also believe and speak. 14We know that God, who raised the Lord Jesus, will also raise us with Jesus. He will bring us and you into HishisHis presence. 15All this is for your benefit. As God's grace reaches more and more people, thanksgiving will overflow to God's glory.

Eternal Glory

16So we do not give up. Even if our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being made new day by day. 17Our troubles are light and last only a moment. But they are producing for us an eternal glory that is far greater than they are. 18We do not focus on what can be seen, but on what cannot be seen. What is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.
