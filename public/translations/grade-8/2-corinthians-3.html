Our Letter of Recommendation

1Are we starting to praise ourselves again? Or do we need letters that recommend us to you, or from you, like some people do? 2You are our letter, written on our hearts. Everyone knows it and reads it. 3You show that you are a letter from Christ, which we delivered. It is not written with ink but with the SpiritspiritSpirit of the living God. It is not written on stone tablets but on human hearts.

4We have this kind of confidence toward God through Christ. 5Not that we can take credit for anything on our own. Our ability comes from God.

Servants of the New Covenant

6He has made us able to be servants of a new covenant. It is not based on the written code but on the SpiritspiritSpirit. The written code kills, but the SpiritspiritSpirit gives life.

Glory That Lasts

7The old way, which brought death, was written in letters on stone. It came with glory, so the people of Israel could not look steadily at Moses' face. That glory was fading. 8Then won't the ministry of the SpiritspiritSpirit be even more glorious? 9If the ministry that brought condemnation had glory, how much more does the ministry that brings righteousness overflow with glory! 10In fact, what once had glory now has no glory at all, because the glory that surpasses it is so great. 11And if what was fading away came with glory, how much more glorious is what lasts!

Hope and Boldness

12Since we have such a hope, we speak with great boldness. 13We are not like Moses, who put a veil over his face so the Israelites would not see the glory fading away. 14But their minds were made dull. To this day the same veil remains when the old covenant is read. It has not been removed, because only in Christ is it taken away. 15Yes, even today, when Moses is read, a veil covers their hearts.

Freedom in the LORD

16But whenever anyone turns to the LORD, the veil is taken away. 17Now the LORD is the SpiritspiritSpirit, and where the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD is, there is freedom. 18And we all, with unveiled faces, are looking at the glory of the LORD as in a mirror. We are being changed into HishisHis image with ever greater glory. This comes from the LORD, who is the SpiritspiritSpirit.
