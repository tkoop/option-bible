Accept Those Weak in Faith

1Accept the person whose faith is weak, but don't argue about opinions. 2One person believes he can eat anything, but the weak person eats only vegetables. 3The one who eats should not look down on the one who doesn't eat, and the one who doesn't eat should not judge the one who eats, because God has accepted him. 4Who are you to judge someone else's servant? He stands or falls before his own master. And he will stand, because the Lord is able to make him stand. 5One person thinks one day is more important than another; another thinks every day is the same. Each person should be fully sure in his own mind. 6The one who honourshonourshonors a special day does it for the Lord. The one who eats does it for the Lord and gives thanks to God. We Live and Die for the Lord

7None of us lives for himself, and none of us dies for himself. 8If we live, we live for the Lord; if we die, we die for the Lord. So whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord. 9Christ died and came back to life for this reason, so that HeheHe might be Lord of both the dead and the living.

Do Not Cause a Brother to Stumble

13So let's stop judging each other. Instead, make up your mind not to put a stumbling block or a trap in front of a brother. 14I know and am sure in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean by itself. But if someone thinks something is unclean, then it is unclean for him. 15If your brother is hurt because of food, you are no longer living by love. Don't destroy with food the person Christ died for. 16Don't let your good be called evil. 17For God's kingdom is not about eating and drinking. It is about doing right, having peace, and having joy in the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit. 18The person who serves Christ in this way pleases God and is approved by people. 19So let's chase after the things that bring peace and build each other up. 20Don't tear down God's work because of food. All food is clean, but it is wrong for a person to eat something that makes another person stumble. 21It is better not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything that makes your brother stumble. 22Keep the faith you have between yourself and God. The person who doesn't condemn himself for what he approves is blessed. 23But the person who doubts is condemned if he eats, because he doesn't eat from faith. And everything that is not from faith is sin.
