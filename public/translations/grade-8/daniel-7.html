Daniel's Dream of Four Beasts

1In the first year of Belshazzar's rule as king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream. Visions passed through his mind while he lay on his bed. Then he wrote down the dream and told its main points.

2Daniel said, "In my vision at night I saw the four winds of Heavenheavenheaven stirring up the great sea. 3Four great beasts came up out of the sea. Each one was different from the others. 4The first beast was like a lion, but it had eagle's wings. I watched until its wings were torn off. It was lifted from the ground and made to stand on two feet like a person. A human heart was given to it."

5Another beast, which looked like a bear, was raised up on one side. It had three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. It was told, "Get up and eat plenty of flesh." 6After that, I looked, and there was another beast, like a leopard. It had four wings like a bird's wings on its back. It also had four heads, and it was given authority to rule.

7After that, in my vision at night, I saw a fourth beast. It was dreadful, terrifying, and very strong. It had large iron teeth. It devoured and crushed its victims, and it trampled down whatever was left with its feet. It was different from all the beasts before it, and it had ten horns. 8While I was thinking about the horns, another horn, a little one, came up among them. Three of the first horns were pulled out by the roots to make room for it. This horn had eyes like a person's eyes and a mouth that spoke boastfully.

The Meaning of the Vision

15I, Daniel, was deeply troubled in my spirit, and the visions in my mind terrified me. 16I approached one of those standing there and asked him the true meaning of all this. So he told me and gave me the interpretation.
