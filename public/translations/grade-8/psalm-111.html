Praise for the LORD's Great Works

1Hallelujah! I will praise the LORD with all my heart, in the meeting of good people and in the crowd. 2The works of the LORD are great, and everyone who loves them studies them. 3His deeds are glorious and majestic, and HishisHis right ways last forever.

4He made HishisHis miracles unforgettable. The LORD is kind and full of mercy. 5He gives food to those who respect HimhimHim. He will never forget HishisHis promise. 6He showed HishisHis people how powerful HishisHis works are by giving them the land of other nations.

7What HishisHis hands do is faithful and fair. All HishisHis commands can be trusted. 8They last forever and ever, built on truth and justice. 9He brought freedom to HishisHis people. He made HishisHis promise last forever. His name is holy and amazing.

10Respect for the LORD is the start of wisdom. Everyone who lives by it has good sense. His praise lasts forever.
