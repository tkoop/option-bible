A Psalm of David in the Wilderness of Judah

1God, YouyouYou are my God; I seek YouyouYou eagerly. My soul thirsts for YouyouYou; my body longs for YouyouYou in a dry, weary land with no water. 2I have seen YouyouYou in the holy place, to see YouryourYour power and YouryourYour glory. 3Because YouryourYour faithful love is better than life, my lips will praise YouyouYou. 4So I will bless YouyouYou as long as I live; in YouryourYour name I will lift my hands. 5My soul will be satisfied as with the richest food; with joyful lips my mouth will praise YouyouYou.

6When I remember YouyouYou on my bed, I think about YouyouYou through the night watches. 7Because YouyouYou have been my help, I will sing for joy in the shadow of YouryourYour wings. 8My soul clings to YouyouYou; YouryourYour right hand holds me up.

9But those who want to kill me will go down into the depths of the earth. 10They will be handed over to the sword; they will become food for jackals. 11The king will rejoice in God; all who swear by HimhimHim will boast, for the mouths of liars will be silenced.
