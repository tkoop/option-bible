Freedom in Christ

1Christ set us free so we could be free. So stand firm, and don't let yourselves be tied up again under a heavy load of slavery. 2Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you get circumcised, Christ won't help you at all.

3I warn every man who gets circumcised that he must obey the whole law. 4You who try to be made right with God by the law have cut yourselves off from Christ. You have fallen away from God's grace.

5But through the SpiritspiritSpirit, by faith, we eagerly wait for the hope of being made right with God. 6In Christ Jesus, being circumcised or not being circumcised means nothing. What matters is faith that shows itself through love.

7You were doing well. Who stopped you from following the truth? 8That kind of persuasion doesn't come from HimhimHim who calls you. 9A little yeast spreads through the whole batch of dough.

10I trust the Lord that you won't think differently. The one who is troubling you will face judgmentjudgementjudgment, whoever he is. 11Brothers, if I were still preaching circumcision, why would people still persecute me? Then the cross wouldn't be a stumbling block anymore. 12I wish those troublemakers would cut themselves off completely.

Use Freedom to Love

13You were called to freedom, brothers. Just don't use your freedom as an excuse to follow your sinful nature. Instead, serve each other with love.

14The whole law is summed up in one command: "You must love your neighbourneighbourneighbor as yourself." 15But if you bite and tear each other apart, watch out, or you will destroy each other.

Walk by the Spirit

16So I say, live by the SpiritspiritSpirit, and you won't give in to sinful desires. 17The sinful nature wants what goes against the SpiritspiritSpirit, and the SpiritspiritSpirit wants what goes against the sinful nature. They fight each other, so you don't do the things you want to do.

18But if the SpiritspiritSpirit leads you, you are not under the law. 19The sinful nature shows itself in obvious ways, such as sexual sin, impurity, and reckless living. 20It also shows up as worshiping false gods, sorcery, hatred, quarrels, jealousy, angry outbursts, selfish ambition, divisions, factions, 21envy, drunkenness, wild parties, and other things like these. I warn you now, just as I warned you before, that people who live like this won't inherit God's kingdom.

The Fruit of the Spirit

22But the SpiritspiritSpirit produces love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, 23gentleness, and self-control. There is no law against these things. 24Those who belong to Christ Jesus have nailed their sinful nature to the cross, along with its passions and desires. 25Since we live by the SpiritspiritSpirit, let's also follow the Spirit's lead. 26Let's not become proud, stirring up trouble with each other or being jealous of each other.
