Everything in Heaven Praises the LORD

1Hallelujah! Praise the the LORD from the heavens; praise HimhimHim in the heights. 2Praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis angels; praise HimhimHim, all HishisHis heavenly hosts. 3Praise HimhimHim, sun and moon; praise HimhimHim, all you shining stars. 4Praise HimhimHim, highest heavens, and you waters above the skies. 5Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for HeheHe commanded and they were created. 6He established them forever and ever; HeheHe issued a decree that will never pass away.

Everything on Earth Praises the LORD

7Praise the the LORD from the earth, all great sea creatures and ocean depths, 8lightning and hail, snow and clouds, powerful wind fulfilling HishisHis word, 9mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars, 10wild animals and all cattle, crawling creatures and flying birds, 11kings of the earth and all nations, princes and all rulers of the earth, 12young men and maidens, old and young alike.

God Is Exalted Above All

13Let them praise the name of the the LORD, for HishisHis name alone is exalted; HishisHis splendoursplendoursplendor is above the earth and the heavens. 14He has given strength to HishisHis people, praise for all HishisHis saints, for the Israelites, a people near to HimhimHim. Hallelujah!
