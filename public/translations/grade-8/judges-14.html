Samson Wants a Philistine Wife

1Samson went down to Timnah and saw a Philistine woman there. 2He went back and told his father and mother, “I have seen a Philistine woman in Timnah. Get her for me as a wife.” 3His parents said, “Isn’t there a woman among the daughters of your brothers or among all our people? Do you have to go to the uncircumcised Philistines to get a wife?” But Samson said to his father, “Get her for me, because she is the one I want.” 4His parents did not know this was from the LORD, because HeheHe was looking for a chance to act against the Philistines. At that time the Philistines ruled over Israel.

The Lion and the Honey

5Samson went down to Timnah with his father and mother. They came to the vineyards near Timnah, and suddenly a young lion roared at him. 6The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came powerfully on him, and he tore the lion apart as easily as tearing a young goat. He had nothing in his hand. He did not tell his father or mother what he had done. 7Then he went down and talked to the woman, and she pleased him. 8Some time later, Samson went back to marry her. On the way he turned aside to look at the lion’s carcass. He found a swarm of bees and honey inside it. 9He scraped the honey into his hands and ate it as he walked. He gave some to his father and mother, and they ate it. He did not tell them the honey came from the lion’s carcass.

The Wedding Feast and the Riddle

10Samson’s father went down to the woman, and Samson gave a feast there, as young men used to do. 11When they saw him, they brought thirty companions to be with him. 12Samson said to them, “Let me tell you a riddle. If you can solve it during the seven days of the feast and find the answer, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes. 13But if you can’t explain it to me, you must give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes.” They said, “Tell us your riddle; we want to hear it.” 14He said to them, “Out of the eater came something to eat; out of the strong came something sweet.” For three days they could not solve the riddle.

15On the seventh day they said to Samson’s wife, “Get your husband to explain the riddle to us, or we will burn you and your father’s house with fire. Did you invite us here to rob us?”

16Samson’s wife wept in front of him and said, “You only hate me; you don’t love me. You gave a riddle to my people and didn’t explain it to me.”

He said, “I haven’t even explained it to my father and mother. Should I explain it to you?”

17She wept in front of him all seven days of the feast. On the seventh day he told her the answer because she kept pressing him. Then she told the riddle to her people.

18Before sunset on the seventh day, the men of the city said to him, “What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?”

Samson replied, “If you hadn’t used my wife, you wouldn’t have solved my riddle.”

19The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came powerfully on him. He went down to Ashkelon and killed thirty of their men. He took their clothes and gave them to the men who had explained the riddle. His anger burned, and he went up to his father’s house. 20Samson’s wife was given to his companion, who had been his friend.
