The Fool Says There Is No God

1For the choir director. A Psalm of David. The fool says in his heart, "There is no God." They are corrupt, and their actions are vile. No one does good.

2The LORD looks down from Heavenheavenheaven on the people to see if anyone understands and seeks God. 3They have all turned away; together they have become corrupt. No one does good, not even one.

4Will those who do evil never learn? They eat up my people like bread, and they do not call on the the LORD. 5There they are, filled with dread, for God is with those who do right.

6You shame the plans of the poor, but the the LORD is their refuge. 7Oh, that salvation for Israel would come from Zion! When the the LORD restores HishisHis captive people, let Jacob rejoice and Israel be glad.
