A Warning About a Different Gospel

6I'm amazed that you are turning away so quickly from the OneoneOne who called you by the grace of Christ and are following a different gospel. 7It is not that there is another gospel, but some people are troubling you and want to twist the gospel of Christ. 8But even if we or an angel from Heavenheavenheaven should preach to you a gospel different from the one we preached to you, let that person be cursed. 9As we said before, I now say again: If anyone preaches to you a gospel different from the one you received, let that person be cursed.

10Am I now trying to win the approval of people or of God? Am I trying to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I would not be a servant of Christ.

Paul's Calling and Early Travels

11For I want you to know, brothers, that the gospel I preached is not a human message. 12I did not receive it from a person, and I was not taught it. I received it through a revelation from Jesus Christ. 13For you have heard about my former life in Judaism—how I persecuted the church of God terribly and tried to destroy it. 14I was moving ahead in Judaism faster than many others my age among my people, and I was extremely devoted to the traditions of my ancestors.

15But when God, who set me apart before I was born and called me by HishisHis grace, was pleased 16to reveal HishisHis SonsonSon in me so that I might preach HimhimHim among the nations, I did not immediately ask advice from any person. 17I did not go up to Jerusalem to see those who were apostles before me. Instead, I went to Arabia, and later I returned to Damascus.

18Then, after three years, I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas, and I stayed with him for fifteen days. 19I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother. 20Before God, I'm not lying in what I'm writing to you.

21Then I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia. 22The churches in Judea that belong to Christ did not know me in person. 23They had only heard that the man who once persecuted us was now preaching the faith he had tried to destroy. 24And they praised God because of me.
