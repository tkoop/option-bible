Saul's Army and Jonathan's Attack

1Saul was one year old when he became king, and he reigned over Israel for two years. 2Saul chose three thousand men from Israel. Two thousand were with Saul at Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and one thousand were with Jonathan in Gibeah of Benjamin. He sent the rest of the troops home.

3Then Jonathan attacked the Philistine outpost at Geba, and the Philistines heard about it. So Saul blew a trumpet throughout the land and said, "Let the Hebrews hear!"

4All Israel heard the news: "Saul has attacked a Philistine outpost, and now Israel has become a stench to the Philistines." Then the people were called together to join Saul at Gilgal.

The Philistines Gather and Israel Hides

5The Philistines gathered to fight Israel. They had thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and so many foot soldiers that they were like sand on the seashore. They went up and camped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.

6When the Israelites saw that they were in trouble, because their army was hard pressed, they hid in caves, in thickets, among rocks, and in cellars and cisterns. 7Some Hebrews even crossed the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead. But Saul stayed at Gilgal, and all the troops with him were trembling with fear.

Saul Offers an Unlawful Sacrifice

8Saul waited seven days for the time Samuel had set, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the troops began to scatter. 9So Saul said, "BringbringBring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." Then he offered the burnt offering.

10Just as he finished offering it, Samuel arrived. Saul went out to meet him and greet him.

11Samuel said, "What have you done?"

Saul answered, "I saw that the troops were leaving me, and that you did not come at the set time, and that the Philistines were gathering at Michmash. 12So I thought, 'Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not sought the favourfavourfavor of the LORD.' So I felt forced to offer the burnt offering."

13Samuel said, "YouyouYou have acted foolishly. You have not kept the command that the LORD your God gave you. If you had, the LORD would have made your kingdom over Israel last forever. 14But now your kingdom will not last. The LORD has found a man after HishisHis own heart and appointed him ruler over HishisHis people, because you have not kept the LORD's command."

Samuel Leaves and Israel Lacks Weapons

15Then Samuel left and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah in Benjamin. Saul counted the troops who were with him. There were about six hundred men. 16Saul, his son Jonathan, and the troops with them were staying in Geba of Benjamin, while the Philistines camped at Michmash.

17Raiders went out from the Philistine camp in three groups. One group headed toward Ophrah in the land of Shual, 18another toward Beth-horon, and the third toward the border that overlooks the ValleyvalleyValley of Zeboim and the wilderness.

19There was no blacksmith in all Israel, because the Philistines had said, "ThetheThe Hebrews must not make swords or spears." 20So all Israel went down to the Philistines to sharpen their plowshares, hoes, axes, and sickles.

21The price was two-thirds of a shekel for sharpening plowshares and hoes, and one-third of a shekel for forks, axes, and for fixing an oxgoad. 22So on the day of battle, no sword or spear was found in the hand of anyone with Saul or Jonathan. Only Saul and his son Jonathan had them. 23A Philistine garrison went out to the pass at Michmash.
