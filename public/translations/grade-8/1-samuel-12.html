Samuel Speaks to All Israel

1Then Samuel said to all Israel, "I have listened to everything you said to me, and I have placed a king over you. 2Now here is the king walking ahead of you. I am old and greygreygray, and my sons are here with you. I have walked before you since I was young until today. 3Here I am. Testify against me before the LORD and before HishisHis anointed: Whose ox have I taken? Whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I cheated or oppressed? From whom have I accepted a bribe to look the other way? Tell me, and I will make it right."

4"You have not cheated us or oppressed us," they replied, "and you have not taken anything from anyone."

5Samuel said, "ThetheThe LORD is a witness against you, and HishisHis anointed is a witness today, that you have not found anything in my hand."

They answered, "HeheHe is a witness."

God's Goodness to Israel

6Then Samuel said to the people, "ThetheThe LORD is the OneoneOne who appointed Moses and Aaron and brought your fathers out of Egypt. 7Now stand here, and I will present my case against you before the LORD, reminding you of all the right things the LORD has done for you and your fathers. 8When Jacob went to Egypt, your fathers cried out to the LORD. He sent Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them here."

9"But they forgot the LORD their God, so HeheHe handed them over to Sisera, commander of Hazor's army, to the Philistines, and to the king of Moab, and their enemies fought against them. 10Then they cried out to the LORD and said, 'We have sinned because we left the LORD and served the Baals and Ashtoreths. Now save us from our enemies, and we will serve YouyouYou.' 11So the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and Samuel, and HeheHe rescued you from your enemies on every side, so you could live in safety. 12But when you saw that Nahash, king of the Ammonites, was coming against you, you said to me, 'No, we want a king to rule over us'—even though the LORD your God was your king."

A Warning and a Sign

13"Now here is the king you chose, the one you asked for. The LORD has placed a king over you. 14If you fear the LORD, serve HimhimHim, and obey HishisHis voice, and if you do not rebel against the LORD's command, then you and the king who rules over you will follow the LORD your God. 15But if you do not obey the LORD's voice and rebel against HimhimHim, then the LORD's hand will be against you, just as it was against your fathers."

16"Now stand still and see this great thing the LORD is about to do before your eyes. 17Isn't today the wheat harvest? I will call on the LORD, and HeheHe will send thunder and rain. Then you will know and see how great the wrong is that you have done in the LORD's sight by asking for a king."

18So Samuel called on the LORD, and that same day the LORD sent thunder and rain. All the people were very afraid of the LORD and Samuel.

19Then all the people said to Samuel, "Pray to the LORD your God for us, your servants, so we will not die. We have added to all our sins the wrong of asking for a king."

Samuel's Promise and Warning

20"Do not be afraid," Samuel replied. "You have done all this wrong, but do not turn away from following the LORD. Serve the LORD with all your heart. 21Do not turn aside after worthless things that cannot help or save you, because they are worthless."

22"For the sake of HishisHis great name, the LORD will not abandon HishisHis people, because it pleased the LORD to make you HishisHis people. 23I would never sin against the LORD by stopping my prayers for you. I will also teach you the good and right way. 24Only fear the LORD and serve HimhimHim faithfully with all your heart. Think about the great things HeheHe has done for you. 25But if you keep doing wrong, both you and your king will be swept away."
