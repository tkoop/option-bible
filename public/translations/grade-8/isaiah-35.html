The Desert Will Bloom

1The desert and the dry land will be glad. The wilderness will rejoice and bloom like a crocus. 2It will bloom in abundance and rejoice with joy and singing. The glory of Lebanon will be given to it, along with the splendoursplendoursplendor of Carmel and Sharon. They will see the glory of the the LORD, the splendoursplendoursplendor of our God.

Strength for the Weak

3Strengthen weak hands and steady shaking knees. 4Say to those with fearful hearts, “Be strong; do not be afraid! Your God will come with vengeance. He will repay and save you.”

Healing and New Life

5Then the eyes of the blind will be opened, and the ears of the deaf will be unstopped. 6Then the lame will leap like a deer, and the mute tongue will shout for joy. Water will break out in the wilderness, and streams will flow in the desert. 7The burning sand will become a pool, and the thirsty ground will become springs of water. In the place where jackals rest, grass will grow into reeds and papyrus.

A Highway of Holiness

8A highway will be there, and a road. It will be called the Way of Holiness. Unclean people will not travel on it; it is for God’s people. Travelers, even fools, will not lose their way. 9No lion will be there, and no wild beast will go up on it. They will not be found there. Only the redeemed will walk there. 10The people the the LORD has rescued will return. They will come to Zion with singing. Everlasting joy will rest on their heads. They will be filled with gladness and joy, and sorrow and sighing will disappear.
