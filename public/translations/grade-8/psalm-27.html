The LORD Is My Light and Salvation

1The LORD is my light and my salvation. Who should I fear? The LORD is the strength of my life. Who can make me afraid? 2When evil people came close to attack me, my enemies and foes stumbled and fell. 3Even if an army camps against me, my heart will not be afraid. Waiting on the LORD
