The Law Is Read and Foreigners Are Separated

1On that day, the people listened as the BookbookBook of Moses was read. They found a written rule that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever be allowed into the assembly of God. 2This was because those nations had not met the Israelites with bread and water. They had also hired Balaam to curse Israel, but our God turned the curse into a blessing. 3When the people heard the law, they separated everyone of mixed background from Israel.

Nehemiah Cleans the Temple Rooms

4Before this happened, Eliashib the priest, who was close to Tobiah, had been put in charge of a room in the house of our God. 5He had made a large room for Tobiah. Earlier, that room had held the grain offering, the incense, the temple vessels, the tithes of grain, wine, and olive oil, and the supplies for the Levites, singers, and gatekeepers. It had also held the gifts for the priests. 6While all this was happening, I was not in Jerusalem. I had gone to King Artaxerxes of Babylon in the thirty-second year of his reign. After some time, I asked the king for permission to return. 7When I came back to Jerusalem, I learned about the evil thing Eliashib had done for Tobiah by giving him a room in the courtyards of God's house. 8I was very angry, so I threw all of Tobiah's belongings out of the room. 9I ordered the rooms to be cleaned, and then I brought back the vessels of God's house, along with the grain offering and the incense.

Support for the Levites Is Restored

10I also learned that the Levites had not been given their shares. Because of that, the Levites and singers who were supposed to do the work had gone back to their own fields. 11So I rebuked the officials and said, "Why has the house of God been neglected?" Then I gathered the Levites and singers and put them back in their places. 12After that, all Judah brought the tithes of grain, new wine, and olive oil into the storehouses. 13I put Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, Pedaiah the Levite, and Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, in charge of the storehouses. They were trusted men, and their job was to hand out supplies to their brothers. 14I prayed, "Remember me for this, my God. Do not wipe out the good things I have done for YouryourYour house and for its services."

The Sabbath Is Protected and Mixed Marriages End

15In those days, I saw people in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath. They were bringing in grain, loading it on donkeys, and carrying wine, grapes, figs, and all kinds of loads into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I warned them not to sell food on that day. 16People from Tyre who lived there brought in fish and all kinds of goods, and they sold them on the Sabbath to the people of Judah in Jerusalem. 17I argued with the nobles of Judah and said, "What evil thing are you doing? You are ruining the Sabbath by working on it." 18I also said, "Didn't your fathers do the same thing? That is why our God brought all this trouble on us and on this city. Now you are bringing even more anger on Israel by ruining the Sabbath."

19As evening came before the Sabbath, I ordered the doors of Jerusalem's gates to be shut, and I said they should not be opened until after the Sabbath. I also stationed some of my servants at the gates so that no load could be brought in on the Sabbath day. 20Once or twice, merchants and sellers of all kinds of goods spent the night outside Jerusalem. 21I warned them, "Why do you camp outside the wall? If you do this again, I will use force against you." After that, they did not come on the Sabbath. 22Then I told the Levites, "Purify yourselves and come guard the gates, so that the Sabbath day will be kept holy." I also prayed, "Remember me for this too, my God, and be kind to me because of YouryourYour great faithful love."

23In those days, I also saw Jews who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab. 24Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod or the language of another people. They did not know how to speak the language of Judah. 25I argued with them, cursed them, struck some of them, and pulled out their hair. Then I made them promise before God, "YouyouYou will not give your daughters to their sons, and you will not take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves." 26I said, "Didn't King Solomon of Israel sin because of women like these? Among many nations there was no king like him. He was loved by his God, and God made him king over all Israel. Even so, foreign women led him into sin. 27Shall we then listen to you and do all this great evil, trespassing against our God by marrying foreign women?"

28One of the sons of Joiada, son of Eliashib the high priest, was married to a daughter of Sanballat the Horonite. I drove him away from me. 29I prayed, "Remember them, my God, because they made the priesthood and its covenant unclean, along with the Levites." 30So I cleansed them from everything foreign. I also set up duties for the priests and Levites, giving each man his own work. 31I made sure wood was brought for the offerings at the right times, and I made sure the first crops were brought in. I prayed, "Remember me, my God, and treat me well."
