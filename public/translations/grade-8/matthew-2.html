Wise Men Seek the New King

1After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea during the time of King Herod, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem. 2They asked, "Where is the one who has been born king of the Jews? We saw HishisHis star in the east and have come to worship HimhimHim."

3When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. 4He called together all the chief priests and teachers of the law and asked them where the Christ was to be born.

5They said, "In Bethlehem in Judea, because this is what the prophet wrote: 6'And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah, for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd MymyMy people Israel.'"

7Then Herod secretly called the wise men and found out from them the exact time the star had appeared. 8He sent them to Bethlehem and said, "Go and search carefully for the child. When you find HimhimHim, report to me, so that I too may go and worship HimhimHim."

9After they heard the king, they went on their way, and the star they had seen in the east went ahead of them until it stopped over the place where the child was. 10When they saw the star, they were overjoyed. 11They went into the house and saw the child with HishisHis mother Mary. They bowed down and worshiped HimhimHim. Then they opened their treasures and gave HimhimHim gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh. 12They were warned in a dream not to go back to Herod, so they returned to their own country by another road.

Herod's Plan and the Escape to Egypt

13After they left, an angel of the LORD appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, "Get up, take the child and HishisHis mother and escape to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is going to search for the child to kill him." 14So Joseph got up, took the child and HishisHis mother during the night, and left for Egypt. 15Joseph stayed there until Herod died. This happened so that what the LORD had said through the prophet would come true: "Out of Egypt I called MymyMy SonsonSon."

He sent soldiers to kill all the boys in Bethlehem and in the surrounding area who were two years old or younger, based on the time he had learned from the wise men. 17Then what the prophet Jeremiah had said came true: 18"A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and great mourning. Rachel wept for her children and refused to be comforted, because they are gone."

The Family Returns from Egypt

19After Herod died, an angel of the LORD appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt. 20The angel said, "Get up, take the child and HishisHis mother and go to the land of Israel, for those who were trying to kill the child are dead." 21So Joseph got up, took the child and HishisHis mother, and went to the land of Israel. 22But when he heard that Archelaus was ruling Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there. After being warned in a dream, he went to the region of Galilee. 23Joseph settled in a town called Nazareth. This fulfilled what the prophets had said: "He will be called a Nazarene."
