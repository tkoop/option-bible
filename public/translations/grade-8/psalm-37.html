Trust in the LORD, Not the Wicked

1Do not worry about evil people, and do not envy those who do wrong. 2They will soon fade away, like grass that withers, and like green plants they will die.

3Trust in the the LORD and do good. Live in the land and rely on HishisHis faithfulness. 4Delight in the the LORD, and HeheHe will give you what your heart desires.

5Give your plans to the the LORD; trust in HimhimHim, and HeheHe will act. 6He will make your goodness shine like the dawn, and your fairness like the noonday sun.

7Be quiet before the the LORD and wait patiently for HimhimHim. Do not worry about people who get ahead by doing wrong or who carry out evil plans. 8Turn away from anger and leave rage behind; do not fret, because it leads only to harm.

The Wicked Will Fade Away

9For evildoers will be destroyed, but those who hope in the the LORD will receive the land. 10In a little while, the wicked will be gone. Even if you look for them, they will not be found. 11But humble people will receive the land and enjoy great peace.

12The wicked plot against those who do right and grind their teeth in anger. 13But the Lord laughs at them, because HeheHe sees that their day is coming. 14The wicked draw their swords and bend their bows to bring down the poor and needy and to kill those whose ways are upright. 15But their swords will pierce their own hearts, and their bows will be broken.

16It is better to have a little and do right than to have the wealth of many wicked people. 17For the strength of the wicked will be broken, but the the LORD upholds those who do right. 18The ThetheThe LORD knows how long those who live with integrity will live, and the land they receive will last forever. 19They will not be disgraced in hard times; in days of famine they will have enough.

20But the wicked and the the LORD's enemies will perish. Like the beauty of a field, they will vanish, and like smoke they will disappear. 21The wicked borrow and do not repay, but those who do right give generously. 22Those the the LORD blesses will receive the land, but those HeheHe curses will be destroyed.

The LORD Guides and Protects

23The ThetheThe LORD makes firm the steps of the person who pleases HimhimHim. 24Though he may stumble, he will not fall, because the the LORD holds him up with HishisHis hand. 25I was young and now am old, yet I have never seen those who do right abandoned, or their children begging for food. 26They are always generous and lend freely, so their children are blessed.

27Turn from evil and do good, and you will live in the land forever. 28For the the LORD loves justice and will not abandon HishisHis faithful ones. They are kept safe forever, but the children of the wicked will be destroyed. 29Those who do right will receive the land and live in it forever. 30The mouth of those who do right speaks wisdom, and their tongue tells what is right. 31God's instruction is in their hearts, and their feet do not slip.

32The wicked watch those who do right and seek to kill them. 33But the the LORD will not leave them in the wicked person's power or let them be found guilty when they are judged. 34Wait for the the LORD and keep HishisHis way, and HeheHe will lift you up to receive the land; you will see the wicked destroyed.

A Final Lesson About the Wicked and the Righteous

35I have seen a wicked and violent person thriving like a healthy tree in its own soil. 36But he passed away and was gone. Though I looked for him, he could not be found. 37Notice those who live with integrity and watch those who do right, because the future belongs to people of peace. 38But rebels will be destroyed together, and the future of the wicked will be destroyed. 39The salvation of those who do right comes from the the LORD; HeheHe is their safe place in times of trouble. 40The ThetheThe LORD helps them and delivers them. He rescues them from the wicked and saves them, because they take refuge in HimhimHim.
