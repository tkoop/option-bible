Nebuchadnezzar's Troubling Dream

1In the second year of Nebuchadnezzar's reign, he had dreams. His spirit was troubled, and sleep left him.

2The king ordered that the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and astrologers be brought in to tell him his dreams. When they came, they stood before the king.

3The king said to them, "I had a dream, and my spirit is anxious to know what it means."

The Wise Men Cannot Explain the Dream

4The astrologers answered the king in Aramaic, "May the king live forever! Tell your servants the dream, and we will explain its meaning."

5The king replied, "This is my decision: If you do not tell me the dream and its meaning, you will be cut to pieces and your houses destroyed. 6But if you tell me the dream and its meaning, you will receive gifts, rewards, and great honourhonourhonor from me. So tell me the dream and its meaning."

7They answered, "Let the king tell his servants the dream, and we will explain its meaning."

8The king said, "I know you are trying to buy time, because you see that my word is final. 9If you do not tell me the dream, there is only one fate for you. You have agreed to tell me lies and nonsense until things change. So tell me the dream, and I will know that you can truly explain it."

10The astrologers answered, "No one on EarthEarthearth can do what the king asks. No king, no matter how great and powerful, has ever asked such a thing of any magician, enchanter, or astrologer. 11What the king asks is too hard. No one can reveal it except the gods, and they do not live among people."

12Because of this, the king became very angry and furious. He ordered that all the wise men of Babylon be destroyed.

13So the order went out, and the wise men were about to be killed. Men were sent to find Daniel and his friends so they could be executed.

Daniel Asks God for Help

14Then Daniel spoke wisely and carefully to Arioch, the captain of the king's guard, who had gone out to kill the wise men of Babylon.

15He asked Arioch, "Why has the king made such a harsh rule?" Then Arioch explained the situation to Daniel.

16Daniel went to the king and asked for time, so he could explain the dream's meaning to him.

17Then Daniel went home and told his friends Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah what had happened.

18He urged them to ask the God of Heavenheavenheaven for mercy about this mystery, so that Daniel and his friends would not be destroyed with the rest of Babylon's wise men.

19During the night, the mystery was revealed to Daniel in a vision. Then Daniel praised the God of Heavenheavenheaven.

20"Praise be to God's name forever and ever, for wisdom and power belong to HimhimHim. 21He changes times and seasons. He removes kings and sets up kings. He gives wisdom to the wise and knowledge to those who understand.

22"He reveals deep and hidden things. He knows what lies in darkness, and light lives with HimhimHim. 23I thank and praise YouyouYou, God of my fathers, because YouyouYou have given me wisdom and strength. You have now shown me what we asked of YouyouYou, for YouyouYou have made known to us the king's matter."

24Then Daniel went to Arioch, whom the king had appointed to destroy Babylon's wise men, and said, "Do not destroy Babylon's wise men. Take me to the king, and I will explain the dream to him."

25Arioch quickly brought Daniel before the king and said, "I have found a man among the exiles from Judah who can explain the dream's meaning to the king."

Daniel Interprets the Dream and Is HonouredHonouredHonored

26The king asked Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, "Are you able to tell me the dream I saw and its meaning?"

27"No wise man, enchanter, magician, or diviner can explain to the king the mystery he asked about.

28"But there is a God in Heavenheavenheaven who reveals mysteries. He has shown King Nebuchadnezzar what will happen in the days to come. Your dream and the visions that came into your mind as you lay on your bed are these:

29"As you lay on your bed, O king, your thoughts turned to what would happen after this, and the Revealer of MysteriesmysteriesMysteries showed you what is to come.

30"This mystery was revealed to me, not because I have more wisdom than anyone else, but so that the interpretation may be made known to the king and you may understand the thoughts of your heart.

31"You looked, O king, and there before you stood a huge statue—enormous and dazzling. It stood in front of you, and its appearance was terrifying.

32"The head of the statue was pure gold, its chest and arms were silver, and its belly and thighs were bronze.

33"Its legs were iron, and its feet were partly iron and partly baked clay.

34"While you watched, a stone was cut out, but not by human hands. It struck the statue on its feet of iron and clay and crushed them.

35"Then the iron, clay, bronze, silver, and gold were all broken to pieces and became like chaff on a threshing floor in summer. The wind carried them away, and no trace of them was left. But the stone that struck the statue became a huge mountain and filled the whole EarthEarthearth.

36"This was the dream. Now we will explain its meaning to you.

37"You, O king, are the king of kings. The God of Heavenheavenheaven has given you kingdom, power, strength, and glory.

38"Wherever people, wild animals, and birds live, HeheHe has given them into your hand and made you ruler over them all. You are the head of gold.

39"After you, another kingdom will rise, not as strong as yours. Then a third kingdom, one of bronze, will rule over the whole EarthEarthearth.

40"Finally, there will be a fourth kingdom, strong as iron—for iron breaks and smashes everything. As iron crushes all things, so it will crush and break the others.

41"Just as you saw feet and toes partly of baked clay and partly of iron, so this kingdom will be divided. Yet it will have some of the strength of iron in it, just as you saw iron mixed with clay.

42"As the toes were partly iron and partly clay, so this kingdom will be partly strong and partly brittle.

43"And just as you saw iron mixed with clay, people will mix but will not truly unite, any more than iron mixes with clay.

44"In the time of those kings, the God of Heavenheavenheaven will set up a kingdom that will never be destroyed. It will not be handed over to another people. It will crush all those kingdoms and bring them to an end, but it will stand forever.

45"This is the meaning of the vision of the stone cut from the mountain without hands—the stone that crushed the iron, bronze, clay, silver, and gold. The great God has shown the king what will happen after this. The dream is true, and its meaning can be trusted."

46Then King Nebuchadnezzar fell facedown and honouredhonouredhonored Daniel. He ordered that an offering and incense be presented to him.

47The king said to Daniel, "Truly your God is the God of gods and the Lord of kings, and the Revealer of MysteriesmysteriesMysteries, for you were able to reveal this mystery."

48Then the king promoted Daniel and gave him many great gifts. He made him ruler over the whole province of Babylon and chief official over all its wise men.

49At Daniel's request, the king appointed Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to manage the affairs of the province of Babylon, while Daniel stayed at the king's court.
