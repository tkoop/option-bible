A Psalm of David

1The LORD said to my Lord, "Sit at MymyMy right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet."

2the LORD will send out your strong sceptresceptrescepter from Zion. Rule in the middle of your enemies.

3Your people will freely step forward on the day you show your power. They will shine in holy beauty, and your young people will be like the morning dew.

4The LORD has sworn an oath and will not change HishisHis mind: "You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek."

5The Lord stands at your right hand. He will crush kings on the day HeheHe is angry.

6He will judge the nations. He will fill the land with dead bodies. He will crush the head of the enemy across the wide EarthEarthearth.

7He will drink from a stream beside the road, and then HeheHe will hold HishisHis head high.
