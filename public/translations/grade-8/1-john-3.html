God's Love Makes Us His Children

1See how much love the FatherfatherFather has given us, so that we can be called children of God. And that is what we are. That is why the world does not know us, because it did not know HimhimHim. 2Dear friends, we are already God's children. We do not yet know what we will be, but we know that when HeheHe appears, we will be like HimhimHim, because we will see HimhimHim as HeheHe really is.

3Everyone who has this hope in HimhimHim keeps himself pure, just as HeheHe is pure.

Living Right and Avoiding Sin

4Everyone who keeps on sinning breaks God's law, because sin is lawlessness. 5And you know that HeheHe came to take away our sins, and there is no sin in HimhimHim. 6No one who stays in HimhimHim keeps on sinning. No one who keeps on sinning has seen HimhimHim or known HimhimHim.

7Dear children, do not let anyone fool you. The one who does what is right is righteous, just as HeheHe is righteous. 8The one who keeps on sinning belongs to the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The SonsonSon of God came to destroy the devil's work. 9No one born of God keeps on sinning, because God's seed stays in him. He cannot keep on sinning, because he has been born of God. 10This is how we can tell God's children from the devil's children: Anyone who does not do what is right is not from God, and neither is anyone who does not love his brother.

Love One Another

11This is the message you heard from the beginning: We should love one another. 12We should not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother. Why did he murder him? Because his own actions were evil, but his brother's actions were right.

13Do not be surprised, brothers and sisters, if the world hates you. 14We know that we have moved from death to life, because we love our brothers and sisters. Anyone who does not love stays in death. 15Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life in him.

16This is how we know what love is: He gave HishisHis life for us, so we should give our lives for our brothers and sisters. 17If anyone has enough of this world's goods and sees a brother or sister in need but does not help, how can God's love stay in that person? 18Dear children, let us love not just with words or speech, but with actions and in truth.

Confidence Before God

19This is how we know we belong to the truth, and this is how we can set our hearts at rest before HimhimHim. 20If our hearts make us feel guilty, God is greater than our hearts, and HeheHe knows everything. 21Dear friends, if our hearts do not make us feel guilty, we can be bold before God. 22We receive from HimhimHim whatever we ask, because we obey HishisHis commands and do what pleases HimhimHim.

23His command is this: We must believe in the name of HishisHis SonsonSon, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as HeheHe commanded us. 24Whoever obeys HishisHis commands stays in HimhimHim, and HeheHe stays in him. We know HeheHe stays in us because of the SpiritspiritSpirit HeheHe gave us.
