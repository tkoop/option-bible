Enemies Try to Stop the Work

1When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of our enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall and that no gap was left in it—though I had not yet put doors in the gates— 2Sanballat and Geshem sent me this message: "Come, let us meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono." But they were planning to harm me.

3I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and come down to you?" 4Four times they sent me the same message, and I answered them the same way.

Sanballat's Open Letter

5The fifth time, Sanballat sent his servant to me with an open letter in his hand. 6It said, "It is reported among the nations—and Gashmu says it—that you and the Jews are planning to rebel. That is why you are building the wall. You are also to be their king, according to these reports. 7You have also set up prophets in Jerusalem to proclaim about you, 'A king is in Judah.' Now these things will be reported to the king. So come now, and let us talk together."

8I sent back this reply: "No such things as you say have been done. You are inventing them in your own mind." 9For they were all trying to frighten us, thinking, "TheirtheirTheir hands will drop from the work, and it will not be done." But now, strengthen my hands.

A False Warning from Shemaiah

10I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah, son of Mehetabel, who was confined. He said, "Let us meet in the house of God, inside the temple, and let us close the temple doors, for they are coming to kill you—by night they are coming to kill you."

11I answered, "Should a man like me run away? What man like me could go into the temple and live? I will not go in." 12Then I realizedrealisedrealized that God had not sent him. He had spoken this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him. 13He was hired to frighten me into sinning, so they could give me a bad name and discredit me.

14"My God, remember Tobiah and Sanballat for what they have done, and also the prophetess Noadiah and the rest of the prophets who were trying to frighten me."

The Wall Is Finished

15So the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth day of Elul, in fifty-two days. 16When all our enemies heard about this, all the nations around us were afraid and lost their confidence, because they realizedrealisedrealized that this work had been done with the help of our God.

17Also in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters to Tobiah, and letters from Tobiah came to them. 18For many in Judah were bound to him by oath, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah. 19They also kept telling me about his good deeds and reporting my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to frighten me.
