No Condemnation in Christ

1So now there is no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. 2For the law of the SpiritspiritSpirit of life in Christ Jesus has set you free from the law of sin and death. 3For what the law could not do, because it was weak through the flesh, God did. He sent HishisHis own SonsonSon in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin HeheHe condemned sin in the flesh. 4He did this so that what the law requires would be fulfilled in us, who do not live by the flesh but by the SpiritspiritSpirit.

Life in the Spirit

5For those who live by the flesh think about fleshly things, but those who live by the SpiritspiritSpirit think about spiritual things. 6The mindset of the flesh brings death, but the mindset of the SpiritspiritSpirit brings life and peace. 7The mindset of the flesh is hostile to God. It does not obey God's law, and it cannot. 8People who are controlled by the flesh cannot please God.

9But you are not in the flesh but in the SpiritspiritSpirit, if indeed God's SpiritspiritSpirit lives in you. And if anyone does not have the SpiritspiritSpirit of Christ, that person does not belong to HimhimHim. 10But if Christ is in you, your body is dead because of sin, but the SpiritspiritSpirit gives life because of righteousness. 11And if the SpiritspiritSpirit of HimhimHim who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you, then HeheHe who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit who lives in you. 12So then, brothers and sisters, we do not owe the flesh anything, so we should not live by it. 13For if you live by the flesh, you are about to die; but if by the SpiritspiritSpirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live.

14For all who are led by God's SpiritspiritSpirit are God's sons. 15For you did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you received the SpiritspiritSpirit of adoption, by whom we cry out, "Abba, FatherfatherFather!" 16The SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself testifies with our spirit that we are God's children. 17And if we are children, then we are heirs—heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ—if indeed we suffer with HimhimHim so that we may also share HishisHis glory.

Future Glory and God's Purpose

18I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is going to be revealed to us. 19For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the sons of God. 20For the creation was subjected to frustration, not willingly, but because of HimhimHim who subjected it, in hope 21that the creation itself will be set free from its slavery to decay and brought into the freedom of the glory of the children of God. 22For we know that the whole creation has been groaning together and suffering birth pains until now. 23And not only the creation, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the SpiritspiritSpirit, groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for adoption as sons—the redemption of our bodies. 24For in this hope we were saved. Now hope that is seen is not hope, for who hopes for what he sees? 25But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with patience. 26In the same way, the SpiritspiritSpirit helps us in our weakness. For we do not know what we should pray for as we ought, but the SpiritspiritSpirit HimselfhimselfHimself prays for us with groans too deep for words. 27And HeheHe who searches the hearts knows what the mind of the SpiritspiritSpirit is, because HeheHe prays for the saints according to God's will.

28And we know that God works all things together for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to HishisHis purpose. 29For those whom HeheHe foreknew HeheHe also destined ahead of time to be conformed to the image of HishisHis SonsonSon, so that HeheHe would be the firstborn among many brothers and sisters. 30And those whom HeheHe destined ahead of time HeheHe also called, and those whom HeheHe called HeheHe also made right with God, and those whom HeheHe made right with God HeheHe also gave glory to.

God's Love That Never Ends

31What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us? 32He who did not spare HishisHis own SonsonSon, but gave HimhimHim up for us all, how will HeheHe not also, with HimhimHim, freely give us all things? 33Who will bring any charge against God's chosen ones? God is the OneoneOne who makes us right. 34Who is the one who condemns? Christ Jesus is the OneoneOne who died, and more than that, who was raised. He is at the right hand of God, and HeheHe also prays for us. 35Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Will hardship, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword? 36As it is written, "For your sake we face death all day long; we are considered as sheep to be slaughtered." 37No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through HimhimHim who loved us. 38For I am convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor authorities, neither things present nor things to come, nor powers, 39neither height nor depth, nor anything else in creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
