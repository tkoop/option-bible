The LORD Makes Gideon’s Army Smaller

1Early in the morning, Jerubbaal (that is, Gideon) and all the people with him camped by the spring of Harod. The Midianite camp was north of them in the valley near the hill of Moreh. 2The LORD said to Gideon, "ThetheThe people with you are too many for MemeMe to give Midian into their hands. Israel might brag against MemeMe, saying, 'My own hand saved me.'"

3Now announce to the people, "Whoever is afraid and trembling, let him go back and leave Mount Gilead." So 22,000 of the people went back, and 10,000 stayed. 4The LORD said to Gideon, "There are still too many people. Take them down to the water, and I will test them for you there. Anyone I tell you, 'This one will go with you,' will go with you. Anyone I tell you, 'This one will not go,' will not go."

5So he took the people down to the water. The LORD said to Gideon, "Set apart everyone who laps water with his tongue, the way a dog laps, and everyone who kneels down to drink." 6The number of men who lapped water with their hands to their mouths was 300. All the rest of the people knelt down to drink.

7The LORD said to Gideon, "With the 300 men who lapped I will save you, and I will give Midian into your hand. Let all the other people go home." 8So the people took their food and their trumpets. Gideon sent all the Israelites to their tents, but he kept the 300 men. The Midianite camp was below him in the valley.

Gideon Hears a Dream in the Enemy Camp

9That night the LORD said to him, "Get up and go down to the camp, because I have given it into your hand." 10"But if you are afraid to go down, go down to the camp with Purah your servant." 11"You will hear what they say, and then your hands will be strong, and you will go down to the camp." So Gideon and his servant Purah went down to the edge of the armed men in the camp.

12The Midianites, Amalekites, and all the people of the east were lying in the valley. There were so many of them, like locusts. Their camels could not be counted; they were as many as the sand on the seashore.

13When Gideon arrived, a man was telling his friend about a dream. He said, "I dreamed that a loaf of barley bread rolled into the Midianite camp. It came to a tent, struck it, and the tent fell. It turned the tent upside down, and the tent collapsed."

14His friend answered, "This can only mean the sword of Gideon son of Joash, a man of Israel. God has given Midian and the whole camp into his hand."

15When Gideon heard the dream and its meaning, he bowed in worship. Then he returned to the Israelite camp and said, "Get up, for the LORD has given the Midianite camp into your hands."

Gideon’s Three Hundred Men Attack

16He divided the 300 men into three groups. He gave each man a trumpet and an empty jar with a torch inside it. 17He said to them, "Watch me and do what I do. When I reach the edge of the camp, do as I do." 18"I and all who are with me will blow our trumpets. Then you also blow your trumpets all around the camp and shout, 'For the LORD and for Gideon!'"

19Gideon and the 100 men with him came to the edge of the camp at the start of the middle watch, just as the guards had been posted. They blew their trumpets and broke the jars in their hands. 20The three groups blew their trumpets and broke their jars. They held torches in their left hands and trumpets in their right hands to blow them. They shouted, "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!" 21Each man stood in his place around the camp. The whole camp ran, cried out, and fled. 22When the 300 trumpets sounded, the LORD caused every man to turn his sword against his companion throughout the camp. The army fled to Beth Shittah toward Zererah, as far as the border of Abel Meholah by Tabbath.

Israel Chases the Midianites

23The men of Israel from Naphtali, Asher, and Manasseh were called out, and they pursued the Midianites. 24Gideon sent messengers throughout the hill country of Ephraim. He said, "Come down to meet Midian, and take control of the waters of the Jordan before they reach Beth Barah." So all the men of Ephraim were called out. They took control of the waters of the Jordan as far as Beth Barah. 25They captured Oreb and Zeeb, the two princes of Midian. They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb. They pursued Midian and brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon across the Jordan.
