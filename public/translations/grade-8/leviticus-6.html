Making Things Right After Wronging Someone

1The LORD spoke to Moses:

2"If a person sins and is unfaithful to the LORD by lying to a neighbourneighbourneighbor about something left in his care, or about a pledge, or by robbery, or by cheating a neighbourneighbourneighbor, 3or if he finds something lost and lies about it, and swears falsely about any of these sins a person may do, 4then, when he sins and becomes guilty, he must return what he stole, or what he cheated someone out of, or the item left in his care, or the lost thing he found. 5He must also pay back anything else he swore falsely about. He must pay it back in full and add one-fifth more. He must give it to the owner on the day he is found guilty. 6He must bring his guilt offering to the LORD: a ram without defect from the flock, at the value you set, as a guilt offering. 7The priest will make atonement for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for any of these things he did that made him guilty."

Rules for Burnt and Grain Offerings

8The LORD spoke to Moses:

9"Command Aaron and his sons: This is the law for the burnt offering. The burnt offering must stay on the altar all night until morning, and the fire must keep burning on the altar. 10The priest must put on his linen robe and linen undergarments. Then he must remove the ashes left from the burnt offering and place them beside the altar. 11Then he must take off those clothes, put on other clothes, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. 12The fire on the altar must keep burning; it must not go out. Every morning the priest must add wood to it, arrange the burnt offering on it, and burn the fat of the peace offerings on it. 13The fire must always burn on the altar; it must never go out.

14"This is the law for the grain offering: Aaron's sons must bring it before the LORD, in front of the altar. 15The priest must take a handful of the fine flour and oil, along with all the frankincense on the grain offering, and burn this memorial portion on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 16Aaron and his sons may eat what is left of it. It must be eaten without yeast in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting. 17It must not be baked with yeast. I have given it to them as their share of MymyMy offerings. It is MostmostMost HolyholyHoly, like the sin offering and the guilt offering. 18Every male among Aaron's sons may eat it. It is a lasting rule for your descendants from the LORD's food offerings. Anyone who touches them will become holy."

The Priest's Daily Grain Offering

19The LORD spoke to Moses:

20"This is the offering Aaron and his sons must bring to the LORD on the day he is anointed: one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half in the morning and half in the evening. 21It must be prepared with oil on a griddle. Bring it well mixed, and offer it as baked pieces, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 22The priest from among Aaron's sons who is anointed to take his place must prepare it. It is a permanent rule to the LORD; it must be burned completely. 23Every grain offering of a priest must be burned completely; it must not be eaten."

Rules for Sin Offerings

24The LORD spoke to Moses:

25"Tell Aaron and his sons: This is the law for the sin offering. The sin offering must be killed in the same place as the burnt offering, before the LORD. It is MostmostMost HolyholyHoly. 26The priest who offers it for sin must eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting. 27Anything that touches its meat will become holy, and if any of its blood is splashed on clothing, you must wash that clothing in a holy place. 28The clay pot in which it is boiled must be broken. If it is boiled in a bronze pot, that pot must be scrubbed and rinsed with water. 29Every male among the priests may eat it.
