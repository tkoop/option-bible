Aaron's Family and Priests

1These are the family lines of Aaron and Moses from the time the LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai. 2Aaron's sons were Nadab, the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. 3These were Aaron's sons, the anointed priests, who were set apart to serve as priests. 4Nadab and Abihu died before the the LORD when they offered unauthorized fire before the the LORD in the Sinai wilderness. They had no sons, so Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests while their father Aaron was still alive.

Levites Given to Serve Aaron

5The LORD said to Moses, 6"BringbringBring the tribe of Levi and present them before Aaron the priest so they can serve him. 7They must guard his duties and the duties of the whole community before the tent of meeting as they work on the tabernacle. 8They must guard all the furnishings of the tent of meeting and do the work of the tabernacle for the Israelites. 9Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. They are given to him completely from among the Israelites. 10Appoint Aaron and his sons, and they must take care of their priesthood. Anyone else who comes near must be put to death."

11The LORD said to Moses, 12"See, I have taken the Levites from among the Israelites in place of all the firstborn Israelites. The Levites will be MinemineMine. 13For every firstborn is MinemineMine. On the day I struck down every firstborn in Egypt, I set apart for MyselfmyselfMyself every firstborn in Israel, both people and animals. They are MinemineMine. I am the LORD."

14The LORD said to Moses in the Sinai wilderness, 15"Count the sons of Levi by their family groups and clans. Count every male who is one month old or more." 16So Moses counted them as the LORD had commanded.

Counting the Levites and Their Duties

17Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. 18Gershon's sons, by their clans, were Libni and Shimei. 19Kohath's sons, by their clans, were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 20Merari's sons, by their clans, were Mahli and Mushi. These were the Levite clans by family group.

21The Gershonite clans were Libni and Shimei. 22All the males one month old or more were counted, and there were 7,500. 23The Gershonite clans camped on the west side of the tabernacle. 24Their leader was Eliasaph son of Lael. 25The Gershonites were in charge of the tabernacle, the tent, its covering, the curtain at the entrance to the tent of meeting, 26the curtains of the courtyard, the curtain at the courtyard entrance around the tabernacle and altar, and their ropes. They did all the work connected with these items.

27The Kohathite clans were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. 28All the males one month old or more were counted, and there were 8,600. They were responsible for the duties of the sanctuary. 29The Kohathite clans camped on the south side of the tabernacle. 30Their leader was Elizaphan son of Uzziel. 31They were in charge of the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the sacred articles used with them, the curtain, and all the work connected with them. 32Eleazar son of Aaron the priest was chief over the Levite leaders and had oversight of those responsible for the sanctuary duties.

33The Merarite clans were Mahli and Mushi. 34All the males one month old or more were counted, and there were 6,200. 35Their leader was Zuriel son of Abihail, and they camped on the north side of the tabernacle. 36They were assigned the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, pillars, bases, and all its equipment. 37They also cared for the pillars of the courtyard all around, with their bases, tent pegs, and ropes.

38Moses, Aaron, and Aaron's sons camped east of the tabernacle, in front of the tent of meeting. They guarded the sanctuary for the Israelites. Anyone else who came near was to be put to death. 39The total number of Levites that Moses and Aaron counted by their clans, counting every male one month old or more, was 22,000.

Counting the Firstborn and Redeeming the Extra

40The LORD said to Moses, 41"Count every firstborn male among the Israelites who is one month old or more, and record their names. 42You are to take the Levites for MemeMe in place of all the firstborn Israelites, and the Levites' livestock in place of all the firstborn livestock of the Israelites." 43So Moses counted all the firstborn Israelites, as the LORD had commanded him. 44The total number of firstborn males one month old or more, listed by name, was 22,273.

44The LORD said to Moses, 45"Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn Israelites, and the Levites' livestock in place of their livestock. The Levites will be MinemineMine; I am the LORD. 46For the 273 firstborn Israelites who are more than the Levites, 47you are to collect five shekels for each one, using the sanctuary shekel of twenty gerahs. 48Give that money to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for the extra firstborn."

49So Moses collected the redemption money from those who were more than the number redeemed by the Levites. 50From the firstborn of the Israelites he collected 1,365 shekels, using the sanctuary shekel. 51Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
