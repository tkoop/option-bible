Ezekiel Eats the Scroll

1He said to me, “Son of man, eat what you find; eat this scroll, then go and speak to the people of Israel.” 2So I opened my mouth, and HeheHe fed me the scroll. 3He also said to me, “Son of man, eat this scroll I am giving you and fill your stomach with it.” So I ate it, and it was as sweet as honey in my mouth.

Sent to a Stubborn People

4He said to me, “Son of man, go now to the people of Israel and speak MymyMy words to them. 5You are not being sent to people who speak a strange, hard language, but to the people of Israel— 6not to many nations whose words you cannot understand. If I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you. 7But the people of Israel will not listen to you, because they will not listen to MemeMe. All of them are stubborn and hard-hearted. 8Look, I have made your face as hard as theirs, and your forehead as hard as theirs. 9I have made your forehead like a diamond, harder than flint. Do not be afraid of them or discouraged by them, because they are a rebellious people.”

10He also said to me, “Son of man, take all MymyMy words into your heart and listen carefully. 11Go to your people in exile and speak to them. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Whether they listen or refuse to listen.’”

Ezekiel Among the Exiles

12Then the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up, and I heard a loud rumbling behind me: “Blessed be the glory of the LORD in HishisHis place!” 13It was the sound of the living creatures’ wings brushing together and the wheels beside them—a loud rumbling sound.

14So the SpiritspiritSpirit lifted me up and carried me away. I went in bitterness and anger of spirit, with the strong hand of the LORD on me. 15I came to the exiles at Tel Abib by the River Chebar. There I sat among them for seven days, overwhelmed.

Ezekiel’s Duty as Watchman

16At the end of seven days the word of the LORD came to me, saying: 17“Son of man, I have made you a watchman for the people of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from MymyMy mouth, warn them for MemeMe.

“18If I tell a wicked person, ‘You will surely die,’ and you do not warn him to turn from his wicked way and save his life, that wicked person will die in his sin, and I will hold you responsible for his blood. 19But if you warn him and he does not turn from his wicked ways, he will die in his sin, but you will have saved yourself.

“20If a good person turns from doing right and does wrong, and I cause him to fall, he will die. Because you did not warn him, he will die in his sin, and his good deeds will not be remembered. I will hold you responsible for his blood. 21But if you warn a good person not to sin, and he does not sin, he will live because he listened to the warning, and you will have saved yourself.”

22Then the hand of the LORD was upon me there, and HeheHe said to me, “Get up and go out to the plain, and there I will speak with you.”

23So I got up and went to the plain. There I saw the glory of the LORD standing, like the glory I had seen by the River Chebar, and I fell facedown.

24Then the SpiritspiritSpirit entered me and set me on my feet. He spoke with me and said to me, “Go, shut yourself inside your house.

“25Now, son of man, people will tie you up with ropes and bind you so you cannot go out among them. 26I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth. You will be silent and unable to correct them, because they are a rebellious people.

“27But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you will say to them, ‘This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: Let the one who will listen, listen; and let the one who refuses, refuse. For they are a rebellious people.’”
