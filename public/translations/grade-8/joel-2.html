The Day of the LORD Is Near

1Blow the trumpet in Zion. Sound the alarm on MymyMy holy mountain. Let everyone who lives in the land tremble, because the day of the LORD is coming. It is very close. 2That day will be dark and gloomy, a day of clouds and thick darkness. Like dawn spreading over the mountains, a great and mighty army is coming. Nothing like it has happened since long ago, and nothing like it will happen again for many generations. 3Fire burns in front of them, and flames blaze behind them. The land in front of them is like the garden of Eden, but behind them it is an empty desert. Nothing escapes them.

4They look like horses, and they run like war horses. 5They sound like chariots as they leap over the mountaintops. They crackle like fire burning dry stalks. They are a mighty army ready for battle. 6Nations shake with fear when they see them. Every face turns pale.

7They charge like warriors and climb walls like soldiers. They march straight ahead and do not turn aside. 8They do not push one another. Each one stays in his own path. They break through defenses, but their ranks do not break. 9They rush into the city and run along the walls. They climb into houses and enter through windows like thieves.

10The EarthEarthearth shakes before them, and the sky trembles. The sun and moon grow dark, and the stars stop shining. 11The LORD thunders at the head of HishisHis army. His forces are too many to count, and mighty are those who obey HishisHis command. The day of the LORD is great and terrifying. Who can survive it?

A Call to Turn Back to God

12“Even now,” declares the LORD, “return to MemeMe with all your heart, with fasting and weeping and mourning.” 13Tear your heart, not just your clothes, and return to the LORD your God. He is gracious and kind. He is slow to anger and full of faithful love, and HeheHe may hold back the disaster HeheHe planned. 14Who knows? He may turn and show mercy and leave a blessing behind HimhimHim—grain and drink offerings for the LORD your God.

15Blow the trumpet in Zion. Set apart a fast. Call a special meeting. 16Gather the people and make the meeting holy. Bring the elders together. Gather the children, even babies who are still nursing. Let the groom leave his room and the bride leave her wedding room. 17Let the priests who serve the LORD cry out between the porch and the altar. Let them say, “Spare your people, the LORD. Do not make your inheritance a shame among the nations. Why should the nations say, ‘Where is their God?’”

God Promises to Restore His People

18Then the LORD was jealous for HishisHis land and felt sorry for HishisHis people. 19The LORD answered them: “I am sending you grain, new wine, and olive oil, enough to fill you up. I will never again make you a shame among the nations. 20I will drive the army from the north far away from you. I will push it into a dry and empty land. Its front rows will fall into the eastern sea, and its back rows will fall into the western sea. Its bad smell will rise up.” Yes, HeheHe has done great things!

21Do not be afraid, land of Zion. Be glad and rejoice, because the LORD has done great things. 22Do not be afraid, wild animals, because the open pastures are turning green again. The trees are bearing fruit, and the fig tree and vine are giving their harvest. 23Be glad, people of Zion, and rejoice in the LORD your God. He has given you the autumn rains because HeheHe is faithful. He sends you showers in the fall and in the spring, just as before. 24The threshing floors will be full of grain, and the wine vats will overflow with new wine and olive oil.

25“I will pay you back for the years the locusts ate—the big locust, the young locust, the other locusts, and the locust swarm. That was MymyMy great army that I sent against you. 26You will have plenty to eat, until you are full. You will praise the name of the LORD your God, who has done amazing things for you. My people will never be shamed again. 27Then you will know that I am with Israel. I am the LORD your God, and there is no other. My people will never be shamed again.”

God Pours Out His Spirit

28“After that, I will pour out MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy. Your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions. 29I will pour out MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit even on MymyMy servants, both men and women, in those days. 30I will show wonders in the sky and on the earth: blood, fire, and thick smoke. 31The sun will turn to darkness, and the moon will turn to blood, before the great and terrifying day of the LORD comes. 32Everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved. On Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be rescue, just as the LORD has said. It will even reach the survivors the LORD calls.”
