The Flying Scroll

1I turned and looked up again, and I saw a flying scroll. 2The angel asked me, "What do you see?" I answered, "I see a flying scroll. It is twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide."

3He said to me, "This is the curse that is going out over the whole EarthEarthearth. On one side it says every thief will be removed, and on the other side it says every person who swears falsely will be removed." 4The LORD of Hosts declares, "I will send it out. It will enter the house of the thief and the house of the person who swears falsely by MymyMy name. It will stay in that house and destroy it, along with its wood and stones."

The Woman in the Basket

5The angel who was speaking with me came forward and said, "Look up and see what this is that is going out." 6I asked, "What is it?" He answered, "It is a measuring basket that is going out. This is what their sin looks like all over the earth."

7Then I saw a heavy lead cover being lifted, and inside the basket sat a woman. 8The angel said, "This is Wickedness." He threw her back into the basket and put the heavy lead cover over its opening.

The Basket Carried Away

9I looked up again and saw two women coming out with the wind in their wings. Their wings were like stork wings. They lifted the basket up between Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth. 10I asked the angel, "Where are they taking the basket?" 11He answered, "They are taking the basket to build a house for the woman in the land of Shinar. When the house is ready, she will be placed there on her base."
