Ezekiel's Vision of the Temple

1In the twenty-fifth year of our exile, at the start of the year, on the tenth day of the month, fourteen years after Jerusalem was struck down, on that very day the hand of the the LORD was on me, and HeheHe brought me there. 2In visions from God HeheHe brought me to the land of Israel and set me on a very high mountain. On its south side was a building like a city. 3He brought me there, and I saw a man whose look was like bronze. He stood in the gateway with a linen cord and a measuring rod in his hand. 4The man said to me, "SonsonSon of man, look with your eyes, listen with your ears, and pay attention to everything I show you. You have been brought here so I can show it to you. Tell the people of Israel everything you see."

The Wall and the East Gate

5There was a wall all around the temple area. The measuring rod in the man's hand was six cubits long. Each cubit was a cubit plus a handbreadth. He measured the wall to be one rod thick and one rod high. 6Then he went to the gate that faced east and climbed its steps. He measured the threshold of the gate to be one rod deep, and the other threshold to be one rod deep. 7Each gate chamber was one rod long and one rod wide, and the space between the chambers was five cubits. The threshold of the gate by the portico facing the temple was one rod deep. 8Then he measured the portico of the gate facing the temple: one rod. 9He measured the portico of the gate to be eight cubits, and its jambs to be two cubits. The portico faced the temple.

10Inside the east gate were three chambers on each side. All three had the same measurements, and the gateposts on either side had the same measurements too. 11He measured the width of the gateway to be ten cubits, and the length of the gateway to be thirteen cubits. 12In front of the chambers was a ledge one cubit wide, and the chambers were six cubits square. The space between the chambers was one cubit. 13Then he measured the gateway from the roof of one chamber to the roof of the opposite chamber: twenty-five cubits. The entrances faced each other.

14He measured the gateposts to be sixty cubits, and the courtyard gatepost ran all around. 15The distance from the front of the gateway at the entrance to the inner portico was fifty cubits. 16The gate chambers and their side pillars had beveled windows all around. The porticoes also had windows all around on the inside, and each pillar was decorated with palm trees.

The Outer Court and Gates

17Then he brought me into the outer courtyard. There were chambers and a pavementroadwaypavement laid out all around the courtyard. Thirty chambers faced the pavementroadwaypavement. 18The pavementroadwaypavement ran along the gates and matched the length of the gates. This was the lower pavementroadwaypavement. 19Then he measured the distance from the front of the lower gateway to the front of the inner court: one hundred cubits, on the east side and on the north.

20He also measured the length and width of the gateway of the outer court that faced north. 21Its three chambers on each side, its gateposts, and its portico had the same measurements as the first gate: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 22Its windows, portico, and palm trees had the same measurements as those of the gate facing east. Seven steps led up to it, and its portico was in front of them.

23There was a gate to the inner court facing the north gate, just as there was to the east. He measured the distance from gate to gate: one hundred cubits. 24Then he led me to the south side, and I saw a gate facing south. He measured its gateposts and portico, and they had the same measurements as the others. 25Both the gate and its portico had windows all around, like the other windows. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 26Seven steps led up to it, and its portico was in front of them. It had palm trees on its gateposts, one on each side. 27The inner court also had a gate facing south, and he measured the distance from gate to gate toward the south: one hundred cubits.

The Inner Court and Priest Chambers

28Then he brought me to the inner court through the south gate, and he measured the south gate; it had the same measurements as the others. 29Its chambers, gateposts, and portico had the same measurements as the others. Both it and its portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 30The porticoes around the inner court were twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide. 31Its portico faced the outer court, and palm trees decorated its gateposts. Eight steps led up to it.

32Then he brought me to the inner court on the east side, and he measured the gate; it had the same measurements as the others. 33Its chambers, gateposts, and portico had the same measurements as the others. Both it and its portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 34Its portico faced the outer court, and it had palm trees on its gateposts on each side. Eight steps led up to it.

35Then he brought me to the north gate and measured it; it had the same measurements as the others. 36Its chambers, gateposts, and portico had the same measurements as the others. Both it and its portico had windows all around. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide. 37Its portico faced the outer court, and it had palm trees on its gateposts on each side. Eight steps led up to it.

38There was a chamber with a doorway by the gateposts of the gates, where the burnt offering was to be washed. 39Inside the portico of the gate were two tables on each side, where the burnt offering, sin offering, and guilt offering were to be slaughtered. 40Outside, on the north side of the entrance to the outer court, there were two tables, and on the other side of the portico of the gate there were two tables. 41So there were four tables on each side of the gateway, a total of eight tables, on which the sacrifices were to be slaughtered. 42There were four tables of cut stone for the burnt offering, each one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high. On them were placed the utensils used to slaughter the burnt offering and the other sacrifices. 43The double-pronged hooks, each a handbreadth long, were fastened all around the inside of the room. And the flesh of the offering was to be placed on the tables.

44Outside the inner gate were chambers for the singers in the inner court, one beside the north gate, facing south, and one beside the south gate, facing north.

45The man said to me, "This chamber that faces south is for the priests who keep charge of the temple, 46and the chamber that faces north is for the priests who keep charge of the altar. These are the sons of Zadok, who alone among the sons of Levi may approach the the LORD to minister before HimhimHim."

The Altar and Temple Portico

47Then he measured the court: one hundred cubits long and one hundred cubits wide, square, with the altar in front of the temple. 48Then he brought me to the portico of the temple and measured the jambs of the portico: five cubits on either side. The width of the gateway was three cubits on each side. 49The length of the portico was twenty cubits, and its width was eleven cubits. It was reached by steps, and there were pillars beside the jambs, one on each side.
