The LORD Commands the Passover

1In the first month of the second year after the Israelites left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Sinai. He said, 2“Command the Israelites to celebrate the Passover at its appointed time. 3You must observe it at its appointed time, at twilight on the fourteenth day of this month. Follow all its rules and decisions.” 4So Moses told the Israelites to celebrate the Passover, 5and they did so in the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Sinai at twilight on the fourteenth day of the first month. The Israelites did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

Some Men Are Unclean

6But some men were unclean because they had touched a dead body, so they could not celebrate the Passover that day. They came before Moses and Aaron that same day 7and said to him, “We are unclean because of a dead body. Why should we be kept from presenting the LORD’s offering with the other Israelites at the appointed time?” 8Moses answered them, “Wait here until I find out what the LORD commands concerning you.”

A Second Chance to Celebrate

9Then the LORD said to Moses, “Tell the Israelites: ‘When any one of you or your descendants is unclean because of a dead body, or is away on a journey, he may still celebrate the Passover to the LORD. 10Such people are to observe it at twilight on the fourteenth day of the second month. They are to eat the lamb with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. 11They must not leave any of it until morning or break any of its bones. They must observe the Passover according to all its rules. 12But the man who is clean and is not on a journey, yet does not celebrate the Passover, must be cut off from his people, because he did not present the LORD’s offering at its appointed time. That man will be responsible for his sin. 13If a foreigner living among you wants to celebrate the Passover to the LORD, he must follow the Passover rules and laws. 14You must have the same rule for the foreigner and the native-born.’”

The Cloud Over the Tabernacle

15On the day the tabernacle was set up, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the TenttentTent of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony. From evening until morning, the cloud above the tabernacle looked like fire. 16That is how it continued: The cloud covered it by day, and at night it looked like fire. 17Whenever the cloud rose from above the TenttentTent, the Israelites would set out. Wherever the cloud settled, the Israelites would camp.

18At the LORD’s command the Israelites set out, and at the LORD’s command they camped. As long as the cloud stayed over the tabernacle, they stayed camped. 19Even when the cloud stayed over the tabernacle for many days, the Israelites obeyed the LORD’s command and did not set out. 20Sometimes the cloud stayed over the tabernacle for only a few days. They camped at the LORD’s command and set out at the LORD’s command. 21Sometimes the cloud stayed only from evening until morning. When it lifted in the morning, they set out. Whether by day or by night, when the cloud was taken up, they set out. 22Whether the cloud stayed for two days, a month, or longer, the Israelites camped and did not set out as long as the cloud stayed over the tabernacle. But when it was lifted, they set out. 23They camped at the LORD’s command and set out at the LORD’s command. They carried out the LORD’s command through Moses.
