Micah and His Mother Make an Idol

1Now there was a man from the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Micah. 2He said to his mother, “The 1,100 pieces of silver that were taken from you—you put a curse on them and said where I could hear, ‘The silver is with me; I took it.’”

His mother said, “May the LORD bless my son.”

3He gave the silver back to his mother. His mother said, “I dedicated this silver to the LORD from my own hand for my son, to make a carved image and a metal image. Now I give it back to you.”

4So he returned the silver to his mother. She took 200 pieces of silver and gave them to a silversmith, who made them into a carved image and a metal image. They were kept in Micah’s house. 5Micah had a house of gods. He made an ephod and household idols, and he put one of his sons in charge of them. That son became his priest.

Israel Had No King

6In those days Israel had no king, and everyone did what seemed right to him.

A Levite Comes to Micah

7There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah. He was a Levite, and he was living there among the clan of Judah. 8He left that city, Bethlehem in Judah, to find a place to stay wherever he could. He travelledtravelledtraveled until he reached Micah’s house in the hill country of Ephraim.

9Micah asked him, “Where do you come from?”

He answered, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to find a place to stay wherever I can.”

10Micah said, “Stay with me and be my father and priest. I will give you ten pieces of silver a year, a set of clothes, and your food.” So the Levite stayed. 11The Levite agreed to live with the man, and the young man became like one of Micah’s sons.

12Micah put the Levite in charge of his shrine, and he became Micah’s priest. He lived in Micah’s house. 13Then Micah said, “Now I know that the LORD will be good to me, because I have a Levite as my priest.”
