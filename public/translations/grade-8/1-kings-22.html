Ahab and Jehoshaphat Plan to Take Ramoth Gilead

1For three years there was no war between Aram and Israel. 2In the third year, Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went down to visit the king of Israel.

3The king of Israel said to his servants, "Do you know that Ramoth in Gilead belongs to us, but we are doing nothing to take it from the king of Aram?"

4He said to Jehoshaphat, "WillwillWill you go with me to fight at Ramoth Gilead?"

Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "I am like you, my people are like your people, and my horses are like your horses. 5Please ask today for the word of the the LORD."

6The king of Israel gathered about four hundred prophets and said to them, "Should I go to war against Ramoth Gilead, or should I hold back?"

They said, "Go up, and the Lord will give it to the king."

Micaiah Warns Ahab

8The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man we can ask the the LORD through. His name is Micaiah son of Imlah. But I hate him because he never says anything good about me, only bad."

10The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, were sitting on their thrones, wearing royal robes, at the threshing floor near the gate of Samaria. All the prophets were prophesying in front of them.

11Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns for himself and said, "This is what the the LORD says: With these you will attack the Arameans until they are destroyed."

12All the prophets said the same thing: "Go up to Ramoth Gilead and win, because the the LORD will hand it over to the king."

13The messenger who had gone to get Micaiah said to him, "Look, all the prophets are saying the same good thing to the king. Please make your answer match theirs and say something good."

14Micaiah said, "As surely as the the LORD lives, I will say only what the the LORD tells me."

15When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, should we go to war against Ramoth Gilead, or should we hold back?"

Micaiah said, "Go up and win. The the LORD will hand it over to the king."

16The king said to him, "How many times must I make you swear to tell me nothing but the truth in the name of the the LORD?"

Ahab Dies in Battle

29The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went up to Ramoth Gilead.

30The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into the battle, but you wear your robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself and went into the battle.

31The king of Aram had commanded his thirty-two chariot commanders, "Do not fight with anyone, small or great, except the king of Israel."

32When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "Surely this is the king of Israel!" So they turned to fight him, and Jehoshaphat cried out.

33When the chariot commanders saw that he was not the king of Israel, they turned back from chasing him.

34But one soldier shot an arrow at random and hit the king of Israel between the plates of his armourarmourarmor. The king said to his chariot driver, "Turn around and get me out of the battle, because I am wounded."

35The battle got worse that day, and the king stayed propped up in his chariot facing the Arameans. The blood from his wound ran down into the bottom of the chariot, and he died that evening.

36At sunset, a shout went through the army: "Every man go back to his city, and every man go back to his own land!"

37The king died and was brought to Samaria, and they buried him there.

38They washed the chariot at the pool of Samaria, and the dogs licked up his blood, just as the the LORD had said.

39Everything else Ahab did, including the ivory palace he built and all the cities he built, is written in the book of the history of the kings of Israel.

40Ahab died, and his son Ahaziah became king after him.

Jehoshaphat and Ahaziah Rule

41Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king of Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.

42Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.

43He followed the good example of his father Asa and did not turn from it. He did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD. But the high places were not removed, and the people still sacrificed and burned incense on them.

44Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.

45Everything else Jehoshaphat did, including his brave deeds and the wars he fought, is written in the book of the history of the kings of Judah.

46He also removed the rest of the male temple prostitutes who were left from the time of his father Asa.

47There was no king in Edom at that time; a governor ruled instead.

48Jehoshaphat built trading ships to go to Ophir for gold, but they never sailed because they were wrecked at Ezion Geber.

49Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants sail with your servants in the ships," but Jehoshaphat refused.

50Jehoshaphat died and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David his father. His son Jehoram became king after him.

51Ahaziah son of Ahab became king of Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah. He ruled Israel for two years.

52He did evil in the eyes of the the LORD. He followed the ways of his father and mother, and of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who led Israel into sin.

53He served Baal and worshiped him, and he made the the LORD, the God of Israel, angry, just as his father had done.
