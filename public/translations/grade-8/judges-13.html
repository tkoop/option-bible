Israel Falls Under Philistine Rule

1Again the Israelites did evil in the eyes of the the LORD, and the the LORD handed them over to the Philistines for forty years.

An Angel Promises a Son

2There was a man from Zorah, from the family of Dan, named Manoah. His wife could not have children.

3The angel of the the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, “You are barren and have no children, but you will become pregnant and give birth to a son. 4Now be careful. Do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean. 5For you are pregnant and will give birth to a son. No razor will touch his head, because the boy will be a Nazirite to God from birth. He will begin to save Israel from the Philistines.”

6The woman went in and told her husband, “A man of God came to me. He looked like an angel of God, very awesome. I did not ask where he came from, and he did not tell me his name. 7He said to me, ‘You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. Now do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean, because the boy will be a Nazirite to God from birth until the day he dies.’”

Manoah Asks How to Raise the Child

8Manoah prayed to the the LORD and said, “O Lord, please let the man of God YouyouYou sent come to us again and teach us what to do for the boy who will be born.”

9God listened to Manoah, and the angel of God came again to the woman while she was sitting in the field. Her husband Manoah was not with her.

10The woman hurried and told her husband, “The man who came to me the other day has appeared to me again today.”

11Manoah got up and followed his wife. When he came to the man, he asked, “Are you the man who spoke to my wife?”

“I am,” he said.

12Then Manoah asked, “When your words come true, what should the boy’s way of life be, and what should he do?”

13The angel of the the LORD said to Manoah, “Your wife must be careful to do everything I told her. 14She must not eat anything from the grapevine. She must not drink wine or strong drink, and she must not eat anything unclean. She must obey everything I commanded her.”

The Angel’s Wonderful Sign

15Manoah said to the angel of the the LORD, “Please let us keep you here so we can prepare a young goat for you.”

16The angel of the the LORD said to Manoah, “Even if you keep me, I will not eat your food. But if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the the LORD.” Manoah did not know that he was the angel of the the LORD.

17Manoah asked the angel of the the LORD, “What is your name, so we can honourhonourhonor you when your words come true?”

18“Why do you ask my name?” said the angel of the the LORD. “It is too wonderful to explain.”

19Manoah took a young goat and a grain offering and offered them on the rock to the the LORD. The angel did an amazing thing while Manoah and his wife watched.

20As the flame went up from the altar toward Heavenheavenheaven, the angel of the the LORD went up in the flame. Manoah and his wife saw it, and they fell facedown on the ground.

21The angel of the the LORD did not appear to Manoah and his wife again. Then Manoah knew he was the angel of the the LORD.

22Manoah said to his wife, “We will surely die, because we have seen God!”

23She said to him, “If the the LORD had wanted to kill us, HeheHe would not have accepted our burnt offering and grain offering. He would not have shown us all these things, and HeheHe would not have told us this now.”

Samson Is Born

24The woman gave birth to a son and named him Samson. The boy grew, and the the LORD blessed him.

25The SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD began to move him in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.
