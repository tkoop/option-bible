Azariah Speaks to Asa

1The SpiritspiritSpirit of God came upon Azariah son of Oded. 2He went out to meet Asa and said, "Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: The LORD is with you when you are with HimhimHim. If you seek HimhimHim, HeheHe will be found by you; but if you forsake HimhimHim, HeheHe will forsake you. 3For a long time Israel has been without the true God, without a teaching priest, and without law. 4But in their trouble they turned to the LORD, the God of Israel, and sought HimhimHim, and HeheHe was found by them. 5In those days there was no peace for the one who went out or the one who came in, because many troubles were on all the people living in the lands. 6Nation crushed nation, and city crushed city, because God troubled them with every kind of distress. 7But you, be strong, and do not let your hands grow weak, for your work will be rewarded."

Asa Removes Idols and Renews the Altar

8When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the hated idols from all the land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities he had taken in the hill country of Ephraim. He also renewed the altar of the LORD in front of the porch of the LORD. 9He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the people from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who were living among them, because many people from Israel had come over to him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.

The People Gather in Jerusalem

10They gathered in Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of Asa's reign. 11At that time they sacrificed to the LORD seven hundred bulls and seven thousand sheep from the plunder they had brought. 12They made an agreement to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and all their soul. 13Anyone who would not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, would be put to death, whether small or great, man or woman. 14They swore an oath to the LORD with a loud voice, with shouting, and with trumpets and horns. 15All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and sought HimhimHim with all their desire, and HeheHe was found by them. The LORD also gave them rest on every side.

Asa Removes the Queen Mother

16King Asa also removed Maacah, his grandmother, from being queen mother, because she had made a hateful image for Asherah. Asa cut down her image, crushed it, and burned it in the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 17But the high places were not removed from Israel. Yet Asa's heart was fully devoted to the LORD all his days. 18He brought into the house of God the holy things his father had dedicated and the holy things he had dedicated: silver, gold, and utensils. 19There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.
