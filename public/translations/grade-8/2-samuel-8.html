David’s Military Victories

1After this, David defeated the Philistines and brought them under his rule. He took Metheg-ammah from them. 2He also defeated Moab. He made the Moabites lie down on the ground and measured them with a rope. He measured two lines to be put to death and one full line to be kept alive. So the Moabites became David’s servants and brought him tribute.

3David defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, when Hadadezer went to restore his power at the Euphrates River. 4David captured 1,700 horsemen and 20,000 foot soldiers from him. He crippled all the chariot horses but left enough for a hundred chariots.

5When the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David killed 22,000 of them. 6David placed military posts in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became his servants and brought tribute. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

7David took the gold shields carried by Hadadezer’s servants and brought them to Jerusalem. 8From Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took a large amount of bronze.

Gifts Dedicated to the the LORD

9When Toi king of Hamath heard that David had defeated all of Hadadezer’s army, 10he sent his son Joram to King David to greet him and bless him, because David had fought and defeated Hadadezer. Hadadezer had been at war with Toi. Joram brought articles of silver, gold, and bronze. 11King David dedicated these to the the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had dedicated from all the nations he had subdued: 12from Aram, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, Amalek, and the plunder of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

Victory Over Edom

13David became famous when he returned and struck down 18,000 Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt. 14He put military posts throughout Edom, and all Edom became David’s servants. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went.

David’s Rule and Officials

15David reigned over all Israel and did what was just and right for all his people. 16Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder. 17Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests. Seraiah was the secretary. 18Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites, and David’s sons were priests.
