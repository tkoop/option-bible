The Angel Warns Israel at Bochim

1The angel of the the LORD went up from Gilgal to Bochim and said, "I brought you up out of Egypt and led you into the land I promised your fathers. I said, 'I will never break MymyMy agreement with you, 2and you must not make an agreement with the people of this land. You must tear down their altars.' But you did not listen to MymyMy voice. What have you done? 3So now I say, 'I will not drive them out ahead of you. They will be a constant trouble to you, and their gods will trap you.'"

4When the angel of the the LORD had spoken these words to all the Israelites, the people lifted up their voices and wept. 5So they called that place Bochim, and they offered sacrifices there to the the LORD.

Israel Serves the the LORD After Joshua

6After Joshua sent the people away, the Israelites went to take possession of the land, each to his own inheritance. 7The people served the the LORD all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who lived after Joshua, who had seen all the great work the the LORD had done for Israel.

8Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the the LORD, died at the age of 110. 9They buried him within the borders of his inheritance at Timnath-heres in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.

10After that whole generation had been gathered to their fathers, another generation grew up who did not know the the LORD or the work HeheHe had done for Israel.

Israel Turns to Other Gods

11Then the Israelites did evil in the sight of the the LORD and served the Baals. 12They turned away from the the LORD, the God of their fathers, who had brought them out of Egypt. They followed other gods, the gods of the peoples around them, and worshiped them. They angered the the LORD. 13They left the the LORD and served Baal and the Ashtoreths.

14Then the the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and HeheHe handed them over to raiders who robbed them. He sold them into the hands of their enemies all around, and they could no longer stand against their enemies. 15Wherever Israel went, the the LORD was against them and brought disaster, just as HeheHe had sworn. They were in great distress.

16Then the the LORD raised up judges, who saved them from the hands of their raiders. 17Yet they would not listen to their judges. They were unfaithful to the the LORD and worshiped other gods. They quickly turned from the way their fathers had walked in obeying the the LORD's commands. They did not do as their fathers had done.

18Whenever the the LORD raised up a judge for them, HeheHe was with that judge and saved them from their enemies during that judge's lifetime. The the LORD felt sorry for them because of their crying out under those who oppressed and crushed them. 19But when the judge died, they turned back to ways even more corrupt than their fathers'. They followed other gods, serving and worshiping them. They refused to give up their evil practices and stubborn ways.

The LORD Tests Israel

20So the the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and HeheHe said, "Because this nation has broken MymyMy agreement that I commanded their fathers, and they have not obeyed MymyMy voice, 21I will no longer drive out before them any of the nations Joshua left when he died. 22In this way I will test Israel to see whether they will keep the way of the the LORD by walking in it, as their fathers did." 23So the the LORD left those nations in place. He did not drive them out all at once, and HeheHe did not let Joshua defeat them.
