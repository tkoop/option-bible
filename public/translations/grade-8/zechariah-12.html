The LORD’s Message About Israel

1This is a message from the LORD about Israel. The LORD declares it. He is the OneoneOne who stretches out the heavens, lays the earth’s foundation, and forms the human spirit inside each person.

Jerusalem Will Trouble the Nations

2“Look, I will make Jerusalem like a cup of strong drink that makes all the surrounding nations stagger. Judah will be surrounded and attacked along with Jerusalem. 3On that day, when all the nations of the earth gather against her, I will make Jerusalem a heavy stone that no one can move. Everyone who tries to lift it will hurt themselves. 4On that day, declares the LORD, I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness. I will keep MymyMy eyes on Judah, but I will strike all the horses of the nations with blindness.”

5Then the leaders of Judah will say in their hearts, “The people of Jerusalem are my strength through the LORD of Hosts, their God.”

Judah Will Be Like Fire

6“On that day I will make the leaders of Judah like a firepot in a pile of wood and like a torch among dry grain. They will burn up all the surrounding nations on every side. Jerusalem will be lived in again, safe in its own place. 7The LORD will save the people of Judah first, so that the glory of David’s family and the glory of the people of Jerusalem will not be greater than Judah’s glory. 8On that day the LORD will defend the people of Jerusalem. The weakest among them will be like David, and David’s family will be like God, like the Angel of the LORD going before them. 9On that day I will destroy every nation that comes against Jerusalem.”

A Spirit of Grace and Mourning

10“Then I will pour out on David’s family and on the people of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and prayer. They will look on MemeMe, the OneoneOne they have pierced, and they will mourn for HimhimHim as one mourns for an only child. They will grieve bitterly for HimhimHim as one grieves for a firstborn.”

11On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be as great as the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo. 12The whole land will mourn, family by family, each alone. The family of David’s house and their wives will mourn alone. The family of Nathan’s house and their wives will mourn alone. 13The family of Levi’s house and their wives will mourn alone. The family of Shimei’s house and their wives will mourn alone. 14All the other families left, and their wives, will mourn alone too.
