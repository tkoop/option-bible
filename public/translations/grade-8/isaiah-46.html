Idols Cannot Save

1Bel bows down, and Nebo stoops. Their idols are loaded onto animals. The images you carry are heavy burdens for tired animals. 2The idols stoop and bow down together. They cannot rescue the heavy load, and they themselves are carried off.

God Carries His People

3Listen to MemeMe, people of Jacob, and all who are left of the house of Israel. I have carried you since before you were born. 4Even in your old age, I am the same. Even when your hair turns greygreygray, I will carry you. I made you, and I will carry you. I will hold you up and save you.

The Foolishness of Idols

5Who will you compare MemeMe to? Who is equal to MemeMe? Who is like MemeMe? 6People pour out gold from a bag and weigh silver on scales. They hire a goldsmith to make a god. Then they bow down and worship it. 7They lift it onto their shoulders and carry it. They set it in place, and it stands there. It cannot move from its spot. Even if someone cries out to it, it does not answer or save him from trouble.

God's Plan Will Stand

8Remember this and be ashamed. Take it to heart, you who have turned against MemeMe. 9Remember the things that happened long ago. I am God, and there is no other. I am God, and no one is like MemeMe. 10I tell the end from the beginning. From long ago I tell things that have not happened yet. I say, "MymyMy plan will stand, and I will do everything I want." 11I call a bird of prey from the east, a man from a far country to carry out MymyMy plan. I have spoken, and I will make it happen. I have planned it, and I will do it. 12Listen to MemeMe, you stubborn people who are far from doing right. 13I bring MymyMy righteousness near. It is not far off, and MymyMy salvation will not wait. I will give salvation in Zion to Israel, MymyMy glory.
