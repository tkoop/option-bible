Ezra's Family Line

1After these things, during the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah, 2son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub, 3son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth, 4son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki, 5son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest.

Ezra Returns to Jerusalem

6This Ezra came up from Babylon. He was a skilled scribe in the LawlawLaw of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given. The king gave him everything he asked for, because the hand of the LORD his God was on him. 7In the seventh year of King Artaxerxes, Ezra went up to Jerusalem with some of the Israelites, including priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants. 8He arrived in Jerusalem in the fifth month of the seventh year of the king. 9He had started the trip from Babylon on the first day of the first month, and he reached Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month, because the good hand of his God was on him. 10For Ezra had set his heart to study the LawlawLaw of the LORD, to obey it, and to teach its rules and decisions in Israel.

King Artaxerxes' Letter to Ezra

11This is a copy of the letter that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, who understood the words of the LORD's commands and rules for Israel:

12“Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven: Greetings.

13“I make a decree that any of the people of Israel in my kingdom, including their priests and Levites, who wants to go to Jerusalem with you, may go. 14You are sent by the king and his seven counselors to check on Judah and Jerusalem, using the LawlawLaw of your God that is in your hand. 15You are to take the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have freely given to the God of Israel, whose home is in Jerusalem. 16Take also all the silver and gold you find in the whole province of Babylon, along with the freewill offerings of the people and priests for the house of their God in Jerusalem.

17“With this money, buy bulls, rams, and lambs, along with their grain offerings and drink offerings, and offer them on the altar of the house of your God in Jerusalem. 18You and your brothers may do whatever seems best to you with the rest of the silver and gold, according to the will of your God.

19“Deliver to the God of Jerusalem the articles given to you for worship in the house of your God. 20You may take any other needs for the house of your God from the royal treasury. 21I, King Artaxerxes, give this order to all the treasurers in the province west of the Euphrates: Give Ezra the priest, the scribe of the LawlawLaw of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, everything he asks for, 22up to one hundred talents of silver, one hundred cors of wheat, one hundred baths of wine, one hundred baths of olive oil, and salt without limit. 23Whatever the God of Heavenheavenheaven commands must be done fully for the house of the God of Heavenheavenheaven, so that wrath will not come on the kingdom of the king and his sons. 24We also tell you that no tax, tribute, or toll may be collected from any of the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or other workers of this house of God. 25And you, Ezra, using the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint judges and officials to govern all the people in the province west of the Euphrates—all who know the laws of your God. Teach those who do not know them. 26Anyone who does not obey the LawlawLaw of your God and the law of the king must be punished quickly—by death, banishment, loss of property, or prison.”

Ezra Thanks God

27Blessed be the LORD, the God of our fathers, who put this in the king's heart to honourhonourhonor the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. 28He also showed me kindness before the king, his counselors, and all the king's powerful officials. Because the hand of the LORD my God was on me, I gained courage and gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.
