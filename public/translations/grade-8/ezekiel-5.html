Ezekiel's Hair as a Sign

1"And you, son of man, take a sharp sword. Use it as a barber's razor to shave your head and your beard. Then take scales and weigh the hair, and divide it. 2When the siege is over, burn a third of the hair in a fire inside the city. Take another third and strike it with the sword all around the city. Scatter the last third to the wind, and I will chase it with a sword."

3"Take a few of those hairs and tie them in the folds of your clothes. 4Take some of them and throw them into the fire to burn. From that fire, flames will spread to all the people of Israel."
