A Widow's Oil

1One of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, "YouryourYour servant my husband is dead. You know that your servant feared the the LORD. Now the creditor has come to take my two children as slaves."

2Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?"

She said, "YouryourYour servant has nothing in the house except a jar of oil."

3He said, "Go, borrow empty jars from all your neighbors. Don't borrow just a few. 4Then go in and shut the door behind you and your sons. Pour oil into all these jars, and set aside the full ones."

5So she left him and shut the door behind her and her sons. They brought the jars to her, and she poured the oil.

6When the jars were full, she said to her son, "BringbringBring me another jar."

He said, "There are no more jars." Then the oil stopped.

7She went and told the man of God. He said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt. You and your sons can live on what is left."

The Shunammite Woman

8One day Elisha went to Shunem. A wealthy woman lived there, and she urged him to eat. So whenever he passed by, he stopped there to eat.

9She said to her husband, "Look, I know that this man who keeps coming by is a holy man of God. 10Let's make a small room upstairs and put a bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp there for him. Then whenever he comes to us, he can stay there."

11One day he came there and went up to the room and lay down.

12He said to his servant Gehazi, "Call the Shunammite woman." He called her, and she stood before him.

13He said to Gehazi, "Say to her, 'YouyouYou have gone to all this trouble for us. What can we do for you? Do you want me to speak to the king or to the commander of the army for you?'"

She said, "I live among my own people."

14Elisha asked, "What then can be done for her?"

Gehazi said, "She has no son, and her husband is old."

15Elisha said, "Call her." So Gehazi called her, and she stood in the doorway.

16Elisha said, "At this time next year, you will hold a son in your arms."

She said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your servant."

17The woman became pregnant, and at that time the next year she gave birth to a son, just as Elisha had said.

The Boy's Death and Restoration

18The boy grew. One day he went out to his father, who was with the reapers.

19He said to his father, "MymyMy head! My head!"

His father said to a servant, "Carry him to his mother."

20The servant carried him to his mother. He sat on her lap until noon, and then he died.

21She went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God. Then she shut the door and went out.

22She called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and a donkey. I will hurry to the man of God and come back."

23He said, "Why go to him today? It is not a new moon or a Sabbath."

She said, "All is well."

24She saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Ride fast. Don't slow down unless I tell you."

25So she set out and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When he saw her coming, he said to his servant Gehazi, "Look, the Shunammite woman is coming."

26Elisha said to Gehazi, "Run to meet her and ask, 'Are you well? Is your husband well? Is the boy well?'"

She answered, "We are well."

27When she reached the man of God at the mountain, she grabbed his feet. Gehazi came to push her away, but the man of God said, "Leave her alone. She is very upset, and the the LORD has hidden it from me and has not told me."

28She said, "Did I ask my lord for a son? Didn't I say, 'Don't give me false hope'?"

29Elisha said to Gehazi, "Tuck in your belt, take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet anyone, don't greet him. If anyone greets you, don't answer. Lay my staff on the boy's face."

30The boy's mother said, "As surely as the the LORD lives and as you live, I will not leave you." So Elisha got up and followed her.

31Gehazi went on ahead and laid the staff on the boy's face, but there was no sound or movement. He came back to meet Elisha and said, "ThetheThe boy has not woken up."

32Elisha came into the house and saw the boy lying dead on his bed.

33He went in, shut the door behind the two of them, and prayed to the the LORD.

34He went up and lay on the child. He put his mouth on the child's mouth, his eyes on the child's eyes, and his hands on the child's hands. As he stretched himself over the child, the child's body grew warm.

35He got up, walked back and forth in the house, and then went up and stretched himself over the boy again. The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

36Elisha called Gehazi and said, "Call the Shunammite woman." He called her, and she came to him.

Elisha said, "Pick up your son."

37She came in, fell at his feet, and bowed to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.

Food During the Famine

38Elisha returned to Gilgal. There was a famine in the land. The sons of the prophets were sitting before him. He said to his servant, "Put on the large pot and cook some stew for the sons of the prophets."

39One of them went out into the field to gather herbs. He found a wild vine and gathered from it a lapful of wild gourds. He came back and cut them up into the pot of stew, but no one knew what they were.

40They poured out the stew for the men to eat. As they ate it, they cried out, "There is death in the pot, man of God!" And they could not eat it.

41Elisha said, "BringbringBring some flour." He threw it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the people to eat." Then there was nothing harmful in the pot.

42A man from Baal-shalishah came bringing the man of God twenty loaves of barley bread from the first ripe grain, along with some heads of new grain in his sack. Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat."

43His servant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?"

Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat. For this is what the the LORD says: 'They will eat and have some left over.'"

44So he set it before them. They ate and had some left over, just as the the LORD had said.
