The LORD Rules in Holiness

1The LORD reigns; let the peoples tremble. He sits enthroned above the cherubim; let the earth quake. 2Great is the LORD in Zion; HeheHe is exalted above all the peoples. 3Let them praise YouryourYour great and awesome name—HeheHe is holy.

4The KingkingKing’s strength loves justice. You have established fairness; YouyouYou have carried out justice and right in Jacob. 5Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at HishisHis footstool; HeheHe is holy.

6Moses and Aaron were among HishisHis priests, and Samuel among those who called on HishisHis name. They called to the LORD, and HeheHe answered them. 7He spoke to them in a pillar of cloud; they kept HishisHis commands and the law HeheHe gave them.

8O the LORD our God, YouyouYou answered them. You were a forgiving God to them, yet YouyouYou punished them for their wrongs. 9Exalt the LORD our God and worship at HishisHis holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.
