Micah's Grief Over Israel's Sin

1Woe is me! I am like someone gathering summer fruit after the harvest, like someone picking leftover grapes. There is no cluster to eat, no ripe fig that I crave. 2The faithful have vanished from the land; no honest person remains. They all lie in wait for blood; they trap each other with a net. 3Their hands are skilled at doing evil. The prince and the judge ask for a bribe. The powerful man says whatever he wants, and they plot together to do wrong.

4The best of them is like a briar; the most honest is worse than a thorn hedge. The day your watchmen warned about has come—your day of punishment. Now they will be confused. 5Do not trust a friend; do not rely on a close companion. Guard the doors of your mouth from the one who lies in your arms. 6For a son dishonoursdishonoursdishonors his father, a daughter rises against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's enemies are the people in his own house.

Hope in the LORD's Salvation

7But I will look to the the LORD; I will wait for the God who saves me. My God will hear me. 8Do not rejoice over me, my enemy. Though I have fallen, I will rise again. Though I sit in darkness, the the LORD will be my light.

9I have sinned against HimhimHim, so I will endure the the LORD's anger until HeheHe argues my case and does what is right for me. He will bring me into the light, and I will see HishisHis goodness. 10Then my enemy will see and be covered with shame—she who said to me, 'Where is the the LORD your God?' MymyMy eyes will see her; now she will be trampled like mud in the streets.

11The day will come to rebuild your walls; on that day your borders will be extended. 12In that day people will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt, from Egypt to the Euphrates, from sea to sea and mountain to mountain. 13The EarthEarthearth will become empty because of those who live in it, as a result of what they have done.

A Prayer for God's Mercy

16The nations will see and be ashamed of all their power. They will put their hands over their mouths, and their ears will be deaf. 17They will lick dust like a snake, like creatures that crawl on the ground. They will come trembling out of their strongholds. They will turn in fear to the the LORD our God, and they will be afraid of YouyouYou.

18Who is a God like YouyouYou, who forgives sin and passes over the wrongdoing of the remnant of HishisHis people?
