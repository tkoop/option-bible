Rules for the Guilt Offering

1This is the law of the guilt offering. It is most holy.

2The guilt offering must be killed in the same place where the burnt offering is killed, and its blood must be splashed all around the altar.

3All of its fat must be offered: the fat tail, the fat that covers the inner parts, 4the two kidneys with the fat on them near the lower back, and the long part of the liver. The priest must remove that with the kidneys. 5The priest must burn them on the altar as an offering made by fire to the LORD. It is a guilt offering.

6Every male among the priests may eat it. It must be eaten in a holy place. It is most holy. 7The guilt offering follows the same rule as the sin offering. One law covers both. The priest who makes atonement with it gets to keep it.

8When a priest offers a burnt offering for someone, the skin of that burnt offering belongs to that priest. 9Every grain offering baked in an oven, and everything made in a pan or on a griddle, belongs to the priest who offers it. 10Every grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, must be shared equally among all of Aaron's sons.

Rules for Peace Offerings

11This is the law of the peace offering that a person may bring to the LORD.

12If he brings it as a thank offering, he must bring with it unleavened loaves mixed with oil, unleavened wafers spread with oil, and loaves of fine flour mixed well with oil. 13Along with his peace offering for thanksgiving, he must also bring leavened bread with his offering. 14He must bring one loaf from each kind as an offering lifted up to the LORD. That loaf belongs to the priest who splashes the blood of the peace offerings.

15The meat of the thank offering must be eaten on the day it is offered. None of it may be left until morning. 16If the sacrifice is a vow offering or a freewill offering, it may be eaten on the day he offers it, and whatever is left may be eaten the next day. 17But any meat left from the sacrifice on the third day must be burned in the fire. 18If any of the meat from his peace offering is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not count for the person who offered it. It is unacceptable, and the person who eats it must bear the blame.

19Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten. It must be burned in the fire. As for other meat, anyone who is clean may eat it. 20But if a person eats meat from the LORD's peace offering while he is unclean, that person must be removed from his people. 21If a person touches anything unclean—whether human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean creature that crawls—and then eats meat from the LORD's peace offering, that person must be removed from his people.

Forbidden Fat and Blood

22The LORD spoke to Moses:

23"Speak to the Israelites and say: You must not eat any fat from an ox, a sheep, or a goat. 24The fat of an animal that dies on its own or is torn by wild animals may be used for other things, but you must not eat it. 25If anyone eats the fat of an animal from which an offering is made by fire to the LORD, that person must be removed from his people. 26You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal in any of your homes. 27Anyone who eats blood must be removed from his people."

Portions for the Priests

28The LORD spoke to Moses:

29"Speak to the Israelites and say: Anyone who brings a peace offering to the LORD must bring his offering to the LORD from that sacrifice. 30He must bring it with his own hands as an offering made by fire to the LORD. He must bring the fat with the breast, so the breast can be waved as a wave offering before the LORD. 31The priest must burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons. 32You must give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offerings. 33The priest among Aaron's sons who offers the blood and fat of the peace offerings gets the right thigh as his share. 34I have taken the waved breast and the contributed thigh from the Israelites, from their peace offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons. This is a lasting rule for all generations."

35This is the share of Aaron and his sons from the LORD's offerings made by fire. It began the day they were set apart to serve as priests to the LORD, 36the day the LORD commanded the Israelites to give these things to them. This is a lasting rule for all generations. 37This is the law of the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering, and the peace offering, 38which the LORD commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day HeheHe commanded the Israelites to bring their offerings to the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.
