Praise the LORD

1Praise the LORD! Praise the name of the LORD; praise HimhimHim, you servants of the LORD. 2You who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God. 3Praise the LORD, because the LORD is good; sing praises to HishisHis name, because it is pleasant. 4For the LORD chose Jacob for HimselfhimselfHimself, Israel as HishisHis special treasure.

5I know that the LORD is great, and our Lord is above all gods. 6The LORD does whatever HeheHe pleases, in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth, in the seas and all the deep places. 7He makes clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain and brings out the wind from HishisHis store rooms.

8He struck down Egypt's firstborn, both people and animals. 9He sent signs and wonders among you, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants. 10He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings: 11Sihon, king of the Amorites; Og, king of Bashan; and all the kingdoms of Canaan. 12He gave their land to HishisHis people Israel as their own.

13The LORD, YouryourYour name lasts forever; YouryourYour fame, the LORD, lasts through all generations. 14For the LORD will judge HishisHis people and show mercy to HishisHis servants.

15The idols of the nations are silver and gold, made by human hands. 16They have mouths, but they do not speak; they have eyes, but they do not see. 17They have ears, but they do not hear; there is no breath in their mouths. 18Those who make them become like them, and so do all who trust in them.

19House of Israel, bless the LORD! House of Aaron, bless the LORD! 20House of Levi, bless the LORD! You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD! 21Blessed be the LORD from Zion, HeheHe who lives in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!
