Slaves and Masters

1All who are slaves under a yoke should treat their own masters as worthy of full honourhonourhonor. Then God’s name and teaching won’t be spoken against. 2Those who have believing masters shouldn’t look down on them just because they are brothers. Instead, they should serve them even better, because the masters who benefit from their good work are faithful and loved. Teach and urge these things.

False Teaching and Contentment

3If anyone teaches a different message and doesn’t agree with the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ and with teaching that leads to godly living, 4that person is proud and knows nothing. He is sick with arguing and word fights. These things lead to envy, quarreling, insults, evil suspicions, 5and constant friction among people whose minds are ruined and who are robbed of the truth. They think godliness is a way to get rich. 6But godliness with contentment is great gain. 7We brought nothing into the world, and we can’t take anything out. 8So if we have food and clothing, we should be content with that.

The Danger of Money and the Fight of Faith

9People who want to get rich fall into temptation and a trap. They are caught by many foolish and harmful desires that drag them down to ruin and destruction. 10The love of money is the root of all kinds of evil. Some people, eager for money, have wandered from the faith and pierced themselves with many sorrows. 11But you, man of God, run from these things. Chase after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, and gentleness. 12Fight the good fight of faith. Take hold of eternal life, to which you were called and about which you made the good confession before many witnesses. 13I charge you before God, who gives life to everything, and before Christ Jesus, who gave the good confession before Pontius Pilate. 14Keep this command pure and blameless until our Lord Jesus Christ appears. 15God will reveal it at the right time. He is the blessed and only Ruler, the KingkingKing of kings and Lord of lords. 16He alone has immortality and lives in unapproachable light. No human has seen HimhimHim, and no one can see HimhimHim. To HimhimHim be honourhonourhonor and eternal power. Amen.

Instructions for the Rich and Final Words

17Command those who are rich in this present world not to be proud and not to put their hope in wealth, which is so uncertain. Instead, they should put their hope in God, who richly gives us everything to enjoy. 18Tell them to do good, to be rich in good works, and to be generous and willing to share. 19In this way they store up treasure for themselves, a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of the life that is truly life. 20Timothy, guard what has been entrusted to you. Turn away from godless, empty talk and from the opposing ideas of what is falsely called knowledge. 21Some people who claim to have it have wandered from the faith. Grace be with you.
