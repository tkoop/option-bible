Sons and Heirs

1I mean that as long as an heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, even though he owns everything. 2He is under guardians and managers until the time his father has set. 3In the same way, when we were children, we were slaves to the basic principles of the world. 4But when the right time had come, God sent HishisHis SonsonSon, born of a woman, born under the law, 5to buy freedom for those under the law, so that we could be adopted as HishisHis sons. 6Because you are sons, God sent the SpiritspiritSpirit of HishisHis SonsonSon into our hearts, crying, "Abba! Father!" 7So you are no longer a slave, but a son; and if you are a son, then you are an heir through God.

Turning Back to Slavery

8At one time, when you did not know God, you were slaves to things that are not really gods. 9But now that you know God, or rather are known by God, how can you turn back again to the weak and worthless basic principles? Do you want to be their slaves all over again? 10You are observing special days, months, seasons, and years! 11I am afraid for you, that my work among you may have been wasted.

Paul's Concern for the Galatians

12Brothers, I beg you to become like me, for I became like you. You did me no wrong. 13You know it was because of an illness that I first preached the good news to you. 14Even though my illness tested you, you did not look down on me or reject me. Instead, you welcomed me as if I were an angel of God, as if I were Christ Jesus HimselfhimselfHimself. 15What happened to your joy? I can tell you that, if it were possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me. 16Have I now become your enemy by telling you the truth?

17Those people are eager to win you over, but not for a good reason. They want to cut you off from us so that you will be eager for them. 18It is always good to be eager for a good purpose, and not only when I am with you. 19My children, I am in the pains of childbirth for you again until Christ is formed in you. 20I wish I could be with you now and change my tone, because I am confused about you.

Hagar and Sarah: An Allegory

21Tell me, you who want to be under the law, do you not understand what the law says? 22For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by a slave woman and one by a free woman. 23The son of the slave woman was born in the ordinary way, but the son of the free woman was born because of God's promise. 24This can be understood as a picture: these two women stand for two covenants. One covenant comes from Mount Sinai and gives birth to children who are slaves. That woman is Hagar. 25Now Hagar stands for Mount Sinai in Arabia, and she matches the present Jerusalem, because she is in slavery with her children. 26But the Jerusalem in Heavenheavenheaven is free, and she is our mother.

27For it is written, "Rejoice, you who have no children! Shout for joy, you who have never given birth! For the woman left alone will have more children than the woman with a husband."

28Now you, brothers, like Isaac, are children of the promise. 29But just as the one born in the ordinary way persecuted the one born through the SpiritspiritSpirit, so it is now. 30But what does the ScripturescriptureScripture say? "Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman will not share the inheritance with the son of the free woman." 31So, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman but of the free woman.
