Woe to Those Who Plan Evil

1Woe to those who plan evil and do wrong while lying on their beds! When morning comes, they do it because they have the power to do so. 2They covet fields and seize them, and houses, and they take them. They oppress a man and his home, a man and his inheritance.

The LORD's Warning

3Therefore, this is what the LORD says: "Look, I am planning disaster against this family. You will not be able to lift your necks from it, and you will not walk proudly, because it is an evil time."

People Will Mourn Their Loss

4On that day people will use a saying against you and mourn with bitter sorrow: "We are ruined! He has changed the share of my people. How HeheHe has taken it from me! He has given our fields to traitors." 5So you will have no one in the LORD's assembly to measure out land for you by lot.

False Prophets

6"Do not prophesy," they say. "Do not prophesy such things; disgrace will not overtake us."

11If a man walks after wind and lies, saying, "I will prophesy to you about wine and strong drink," he would be the prophet for this people!

The LORD Will Gather His People

12I will surely gather all of you, Jacob; I will gather the remnant of Israel. I will put them together like sheep in a pen, like a flock in the middle of a pasture. They will make a lot of noise because so many people are there. 13The one who breaks through goes ahead of them; they break through and pass through the gate and go out. Their king goes before them, with the LORD at their head.
