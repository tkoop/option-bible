Moses Sings to Israel

1Listen, heavens, and I will speak. Let the earth hear the words from my mouth. 2Let my teaching fall like rain. Let my words drop like dew, like gentle rain on new grass, like showers on plants. 3I will announce the name of the LORD. Give glory to our God!

4The RockrockRock is perfect in HishisHis work, because all HishisHis ways are fair. He is a faithful God without evil. He is good and right. 5They have ruined themselves before HimhimHim. They are not HishisHis children because of their faults. They are a crooked and twisted generation. 6Is this how you repay the LORD, you foolish and senseless people? Isn't HeheHe your FatherfatherFather who created you? He made you and set you firm.

Remember the LORD's Care

7Remember the old days. Think about the years of many generations. Ask your father, and he will show you. Ask your elders, and they will tell you. 8When the MostmostMost HighhighHigh gave the nations their land, when HeheHe divided the people, HeheHe set the borders of the nations to match the number of Israel's people. 9For the LORD's share is HishisHis people. Jacob is HishisHis chosen inheritance.

10He found him in a desert land, in an empty and howling wilderness. He surrounded him and cared for him. He guarded him like HishisHis most precious treasure. 11Like an eagle that stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, HeheHe spread HishisHis wings and caught him. He carried him on HishisHis wings. 12The LORD alone led him. No foreign god was with him.

13He made him ride on the high places of the land. He ate the crops of the field. He nursed him with honey from the rock and oil from the hard rock. 14He gave him curds from cows and milk from sheep, with fat from lambs and goats and rams from Bashan. He fed him with the best wheat and gave him wine from grapes to drink.

Israel Turns Away from God

15But Israel grew fat and kicked. You grew fat, heavy, and well-fed. Then he left the God who made him and turned away from the RockrockRock who saved him. 16They made HimhimHim jealous with strange gods. They angered HimhimHim with disgusting idols. 17They sacrificed to demons, not to God. These were gods they did not know, new gods that came lately, which your fathers never feared. 18You forgot the RockrockRock who gave you life. You forgot the God who created you.

19The LORD saw this and rejected them. He was angry with HishisHis sons and daughters. 20He said, "I will hide MymyMy face from them. I will see what happens to them in the end. They are a twisted generation, children who cannot be trusted." 21They made MemeMe jealous with what is not a god. They angered MemeMe with their useless idols. So I will make them jealous with a people who are not a nation. I will anger them with a foolish nation.

22For a fire is burning in MymyMy anger. It burns down to the deepest grave. It eats up the earth and its crops. It sets fire to the foundations of the mountains. 23I will bring many troubles on them. I will use up MymyMy arrows on them. 24They will waste away from hunger and be eaten by disease and bitter sickness. I will send wild animals against them, along with the poison of snakes in the dust. 25Outside, the sword will strike. Inside, there will be terror. It will strike young men and young women, nursing babies and old men.

26I thought I would scatter them and erase their memory from mankind. 27But I held back because I feared the enemy would brag. I feared their foes would misunderstand and say, "OurourOur hand won this victory. The LORD did not do all this." 28They are a nation without good sense, and they have no understanding. 29If they were wise, they would understand this. They would think about what will happen to them in the end.

30How could one person chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their RockrockRock had sold them and the LORD had given them up? 31For their rock is not like our RockrockRock. Even our enemies can see the difference. 32Their vine comes from Sodom's vine. Their grapes come from Gomorrah's fields. Their grapes are bitter and poisonous. 33Their wine is like snake venom, like deadly cobra poison.

The LORD Declares His Power

34"Isn't this stored up with MemeMe, sealed in MymyMy treasuries?" 35Vengeance belongs to MemeMe. I will pay them back when their foot slips. Their day of trouble is near. What is coming for them will happen soon. 36For the LORD will judge HishisHis people and have mercy on HishisHis servants. He will see that their strength is gone and no one is left, slave or free. 37Then HeheHe will say, "Where are their gods, the rock they trusted? 38Who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their offerings? Let them rise up and help you. Let them be your protection!"

39See now that I am HeheHe, and there is no god besides MemeMe. I bring death and I give life. I wound and I heal. No one can rescue anyone from MymyMy hand. 40For I raise MymyMy hand to Heavenheavenheaven and swear that, as I live forever, 41if I sharpen MymyMy flashing sword and take hold of judgmentjudgementjudgment, I will take vengeance on MymyMy enemies and repay those who hate MemeMe. 42My arrows will be soaked in blood, and MymyMy sword will devour flesh. It will shed the blood of the slain and the captives, even the enemy leaders. 43Rejoice, nations, with HishisHis people! For HeheHe will avenge the blood of HishisHis servants. He will take vengeance on HishisHis enemies and show mercy to HishisHis land and HishisHis people.

44Moses came with Joshua son of Nun and spoke all the words of this song to the people. 45When Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel,

46he said to them, "Take to heart all the words I am using to warn you today. Command your children to obey carefully all the words of this law. 47For these words are not empty for you. They are your very life. By these words you will live a long time in the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

48That same day the LORD spoke to Moses, 49"Go up into the Abarim Mountains, to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, across from Jericho. Look at the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their own. 50Die on the mountain you are climbing, and be gathered to your people, just as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people. 51This is because you and Aaron broke faith with MemeMe among the Israelites at the waters of Meribah Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin. You did not honourhonourhonor MemeMe as holy among the Israelites. 52You may see the land from a distance, but you will not enter it, the land I am giving to the Israelites."
