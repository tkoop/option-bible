Daniel’s Vision by the Ulai Canal

1In the third year of King Belshazzar’s reign, I, Daniel, had another vision. It came after the one I had seen before. 2In the vision I saw myself in the fortified city of Susa, in the province of Elam. I was standing by the Ulai CanalcanalCanal.

3I looked up and saw a ram with two horns standing by the canal. Both horns were high, but one was higher than the other. The higher one grew up last. 4I watched the ram push west, north, and south. No animal could stand up to it, and no one could rescue them from its power. It did whatever it wanted and became great.

The Goat and the Little Horn

5While I was thinking about this, a goat came from the west. It moved across the whole EarthEarthearth without touching the ground. It had one large horn between its eyes. 6It came up to the ram with the two horns that I had seen standing by the canal. It ran at the ram with all its strength. 7I saw it reach the ram. It was furious with the ram and struck it, breaking both horns. The ram had no strength to stand against it. The goat threw the ram to the ground and trampled it. No one could rescue the ram from its power.

8The goat became very great. But at the height of its power, its large horn broke. In its place, four large horns grew toward the four winds of Heavenheavenheaven. 9From one of those horns came a small horn. It grew very large toward the south, the east, and the beautiful land. 10It grew until it reached the army of Heavenheavenheaven. It threw some of that army and some of the stars to the ground and trampled them. 11It even set itself up against the Prince of the army. It took away the daily offering and threw down the place of HishisHis sanctuary. 12Because of rebellion, the army and the daily offering were given over to it. It threw truth to the ground, and it succeeded in what it did.

How Long Will It Last?

13Then I heard a holy one speaking. Another holy one said to him, “How long will the vision about the daily offering and the sin that causes ruin last? How long will the holy place and the army be trampled?” 14He said to me, “It will last 2,300 evenings and mornings. Then the holy place will be made right.”

Gabriel Explains the Vision

15After I, Daniel, saw this vision, I tried to understand it. Then someone who looked like a man stood in front of me. 16I heard a human voice call from the banks of the Ulai CanalcanalCanal, “Gabriel, help this man understand the vision.”

17Gabriel came near where I stood. I was terrified and fell facedown. He said, “Son of man, understand that this vision is about the time of the end.”

18While he was speaking, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground. Then he touched me and stood me up.

19He said, “I will tell you what will happen at the end of the time of wrath, because the vision is about the appointed time of the end.”

20“The ram you saw with two horns stands for the kings of Media and Persia. 21The goat is the king of Greece. The large horn between its eyes is the first king. 22The horn that broke and was replaced by four horns means four kingdoms will rise from that nation, but they will not be as strong as the first king. 23Near the end of their rule, when those who rebel have filled up their sin, a harsh and clever king will rise up.”

24“His power will be great, but not his own. He will cause terrible destruction and succeed in what he does. He will destroy powerful people and the holy people. 25Because of his cleverness, his deceit will succeed. He will think highly of himself. In a time of peace, he will destroy many. He will even stand against the Prince of princes, but he will be broken without human help. 26The vision about the evenings and mornings is true. But seal up the vision, because it is for many days in the future.”

27I, Daniel, was exhausted and sick for days. Then I got up and did the king’s work. I was amazed by the vision, but no one could explain it.
