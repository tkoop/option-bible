Nations Left to Test Israel

1These are the nations the the LORD left to test Israel. They would test all the Israelites who had not fought in Canaan's wars. 2He did this only to teach war to the later generations of Israelites, especially those who had not known war before. 3The nations were the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived in the Lebanon mountains from Mount Baal Hermon to the entrance to Hamath.

4They were left to test Israel. The LORD wanted to know if the Israelites would obey the commands HeheHe had given their fathers through Moses. 5So the Israelites lived among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 6They married the daughters of those people and gave their own daughters to those sons. They also served those people's gods.

Othniel Rescues Israel

7The Israelites did what was evil in the the LORD's sight. They forgot the the LORD their God and served the Baals and the Asherahs. 8So the the LORD was very angry with Israel. He handed them over to Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram-naharaim. The Israelites served him for eight years. 9But when the Israelites cried out to the the LORD, HeheHe raised up Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, as a rescuer to save them.

10The SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD came on Othniel. He judged Israel and went out to war. The LORD gave him victory over Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram. Othniel overpowered him. 11So the land had peace for forty years, until Othniel son of Kenaz died.

Ehud Rescues Israel

12Again the Israelites did what was evil in the the LORD's sight. So the the LORD gave Eglon, king of Moab, power over Israel, because they had done what was evil in the the LORD's sight. 13Eglon gathered the Ammonites and Amalekites to himself. He came and attacked Israel, and he took the CitycityCity of Palms. 14The Israelites served Eglon, king of Moab, for eighteen years.

15When the Israelites cried out to the the LORD, HeheHe raised up Ehud son of Gera, a Benjamite, as a rescuer. Ehud was left-handed. The Israelites sent him with a tribute payment to Eglon, king of Moab. 16Ehud made a sword with two sharp edges. It was about a foot and a half long. He strapped it to his right thigh under his clothes. 17He brought the tribute to Eglon, king of Moab. Eglon was a very fat man.

18After Ehud finished giving the tribute, he sent away the men who had carried it. 19But Ehud himself turned back at the stone idols near Gilgal. He said, "I have a secret message for you, O king." The king said, "Be quiet!" Then everyone standing near him left. 20Then Ehud came to him. Eglon was sitting alone in his cool upper room. Ehud said, "I have a message from God for you." The king stood up from his throne.

21Ehud reached with his left hand, took the sword from his right thigh, and drove it into Eglon's belly. 22Even the handle went in after the blade. The fat closed over it, so Ehud could not pull the sword out of his belly. Then Eglon's bowels emptied. 23Then Ehud went out through the porch. He closed and locked the doors of the upper room behind him.

24After Ehud left, Eglon's servants came. They saw that the doors of the upper room were locked. They said, "HeheHe must be using the bathroom in the cool room." 25They waited until they were embarrassed. But when he did not open the doors, they took the key and opened them. There was their master, fallen dead on the floor. 26Ehud escaped while they waited. He passed by the stone idols and escaped to Seirah.

27When he arrived, he blew the trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim. The Israelites went down from the hills with him, and he led them. 28He said to them, "Follow me, because the the LORD has handed your enemies, the Moabites, over to you." So they followed him down. They took control of the places where people crossed the Jordan River into Moab. They did not let anyone cross over. 29At that time they killed about ten thousand Moabites. All were strong and brave men. Not one escaped.

30So Moab was brought under Israel's control that day. The land had peace for eighty years.

Shamgar Rescues Israel

31After Ehud came Shamgar son of Anath. He also saved Israel. He killed six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad.
