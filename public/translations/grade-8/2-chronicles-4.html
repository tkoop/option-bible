The Bronze Altar and the Sea

1He made a bronze altar. It was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high. 2He also made the SeaseaSea from cast metal. It was round, ten cubits across from rim to rim, and five cubits high. A line of thirty cubits went all the way around it. 3Below the rim, figures of bulls circled it. There were ten bulls in each cubit, making ten rows around it. The bulls were cast in two rows as part of the SeaseaSea. 4The SeaseaSea stood on twelve bulls. Three faced north, three faced west, three faced south, and three faced east. The SeaseaSea rested on them, and their back ends pointed toward the centrecentrecenter. 5The SeaseaSea was a hand's width thick. Its rim was like the rim of a cup and like a lily flower. It could hold three thousand baths.

Basins, Lampstands, and Tables

6He made ten basins for washing. He put five on the south side and five on the north. The things used for burnt offerings were rinsed in them, but the SeaseaSea was for the priests to wash in. 7He made ten gold lampstands, following their design, and put them in the temple. Five were on the south side and five on the north. 8He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the south side and five on the north. He also made one hundred gold sprinkling bowls. 9He made the courtyard for the priests, the large courtyard, and doors for the courtyard. He covered the doors with bronze. 10He set the SeaseaSea on the south side, at the southeast corner.

Huram's Work and the Bronze Furnishings

11Huram also made the pots, shovels, and sprinkling bowls. So Huram finished the work he had taken on for King Solomon in the house of God: 12the two pillars; the two bowl-shaped capitals on the pillars; the two sets of network that covered the two bowl-shaped capitals on the pillars; 13the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, with two rows of pomegranates on each network to cover the two bowl-shaped capitals on the pillars; 14the stands and the basins on the stands; 15the SeaseaSea and the twelve bulls under it; 16the pots, shovels, meat forks, and all the other articles. Huram-abi made all these things from polished bronze for King Solomon for the house of the the LORD. 17The king had them cast in clay molds in the plain of the Jordan, between Succoth and Zeredah. 18Solomon made all these things in such great number that the weight of the bronze could not be measured.
