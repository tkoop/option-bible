Dead in Sin, Alive in Christ

1You were dead in your sins and wrong choices. 2You used to live that way, following the ways of this world and the ruler of the evil spirits in the air, the spirit that is now working in people who refuse to obey. 3We all lived like that too at one time. We followed our selfish desires and did what our bodies and minds wanted. Like everyone else, we were under God's anger by nature.

4But God is rich in mercy. Because HeheHe loved us so much, 5HeheHe made us alive with Christ even when we were dead in our sins. You are saved by grace. 6God raised us up with Christ and seated us with HimhimHim in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus. 7He did this so that in the coming ages HeheHe might show the amazing riches of HishisHis grace through HishisHis kindness to us in Christ Jesus.

Saved by Grace Through Faith

8You are saved by grace through faith. This is not from your own doing; it is God's gift. 9It is not from works, so no one can brag. 10For we are God's work, created in Christ Jesus for good works. God prepared these works ahead of time so that we would walk in them.

Christ Breaks Down the Wall

11So remember that you Gentiles by birth were called "uncircumcised" by those called "circumcised" in the flesh by human hands. 12At that time you were without Christ. You were cut off from Israel's community, strangers to God's promises, with no hope and without God in the world. 13But now in Christ Jesus, you who were far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

God's Household and Temple
