A Prayer for Deliverance

1Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; lift me above those who rise against me. 2Rescue me from those who do evil, and save me from bloodthirsty men.

Enemies Without Cause

3Look, they lie in wait for my life; strong men gather against me, though I have not sinned or done wrong, O the LORD. 4They rush to attack me even though I am innocent. Wake up, come to help me, and see. 5You, the LORD God of Hosts, God of Israel, wake up to punish all the nations. Do not show mercy to any who are treacherous and do evil. Selah

Threats and God’s Power

6They return at evening, growling like dogs, and prowl around the city. 7Look, they pour out threats with their mouths; their words cut like swords, for they say, “Who will hear us?”

Justice and Praise
