The LORD Speaks Against Israel's Mountains

1The word of the LORD came to me and said, 2“Son of man, turn your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them. 3Say, ‘Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord the LORD. This is what the Lord the LORD says to the mountains and hills, to the ravines and valleys: Look, I am bringing a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places. 4Your altars will be ruined, and your incense altars will be smashed. I will throw down your slain in front of your idols. 5I will place the bodies of the Israelites in front of their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars. 6In every place where you live, the cities will be destroyed and the high places will be empty. Then your altars will be ruined and left empty, your idols will be broken and abandoned, your incense altars will be cut down, and your works will be wiped out. 7The slain will fall among you, and you will know that I am the LORD.’”

A Remnant Will Remember the LORD

8Yet I will leave a few survivors. Some of you will escape the sword when you are scattered among the nations and lands. 9Then, in the nations where they are taken captive, your survivors will remember MemeMe. They will remember how I was hurt by their unfaithful hearts that turned away from MemeMe, and by their eyes that lusted after their idols. They will hate themselves for the evil they did and for all their hateful acts. 10And they will know that I am the LORD. I did not speak in vain about this disaster coming on them.

A Call to Mourn Israel's Sins

11This is what the Lord the LORD says: “Clap your hands, stomp your feet, and cry out, ‘Alas!’ because of all the wicked, hateful sins of the house of Israel. They will fall by sword, famine, and plague. 12The one who is far away will die of plague; the one who is near will fall by the sword; and the one who remains and is kept safe will die of hunger. So I will pour out MymyMy wrath on them.”

13Then you will know that I am the LORD, when their slain lie among their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on every mountaintop, and under every green tree and every leafy oak—the places where they offered a pleasing smell to all their idols. 14I will stretch out MymyMy hand against them and make the land empty and ruined, from the desert to Diblah, in every place where they live.
