The Seventh Seal Is Opened

1When HeheHe opened the seventh seal, there was silence in Heavenheavenheaven for about half an hour. 2I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.

The Angel at the Altar

3Another angel, who had a golden censer, came and stood at the altar. He was given much incense to offer with the prayers of all God's holy people on the golden altar before the throne. 4The smoke of the incense rose with the prayers of God's holy people to God from the angel's hand.

5Then the angel took the censer, filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it to the earth. There were thunder, loud sounds, lightning, and an earthquake. 6The seven angels with the seven trumpets got ready to blow them.

The First Four Trumpets

7The first angel blew his trumpet, and hail and fire mixed with blood came down on the earth. A third of the earth was burned up, along with a third of the trees and all the green grass. 8The second angel blew his trumpet, and something like a huge mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea turned into blood. 9A third of the living creatures in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.

10The third angel blew his trumpet, and a great star burning like a torch fell from Heavenheavenheaven. It fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water. 11The star's name is Wormwood, and a third of the waters turned bitter like wormwood. Many people died because the waters had become bitter.

12The fourth angel blew his trumpet, and a third of the sun, a third of the moon, and a third of the stars were struck. So a third of them went dark, and a third of the day did not shine, and the night was the same.

The Eagle's Warning

13I looked and heard an eagle flying in the middle of the sky. It called out in a loud voice, "Woe! Woe! Woe to those who live on the earth, because of the trumpet blasts that the other three angels are about to sound!"
