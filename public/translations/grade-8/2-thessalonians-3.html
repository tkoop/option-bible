Pray for the Message to Spread

1Finally, brothers, pray for us that the word of the Lord may spread quickly and be honouredhonouredhonored, just as it was among you. 2Pray also that we may be saved from harmful and evil people, because not everyone has faith. 3But the Lord is faithful. He will make you strong and guard you from the evil one. 4We trust in the Lord that you are doing and will keep doing what we command. 5May the Lord guide your hearts into God’s love and into Christ’s patience.

Stay Away from Idle Believers

6Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to stay away from every brother who lives in a disorderly way and not according to the teaching we gave you. 7For you yourselves know how you should follow our example, because we were not disorderly among you. 8We did not eat anyone’s bread for free. Instead, we worked hard day and night so we would not be a burden to any of you. 9We did this not because we lack authority, but to give you an example to follow. 10Even when we were with you, we gave you this rule: “If anyone is not willing to work, he should not eat.” 11We hear that some among you are living in a disorderly way. They do no work, but they meddle in other people’s business. 12We command and urge such people in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and eat their own bread. 13But you, brothers, do not get tired of doing good.

Correcting Those Who Disobey

14If anyone does not obey our message in this letter, take note of that person. Do not associate with him, so he may be ashamed. 15Do not treat him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.

Final Blessing

16Now may the Lord of peace HimselfhimselfHimself give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you. 17This greeting is in my own hand—Paul. This is a sign in every letter, and this is how I write. 18The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.
