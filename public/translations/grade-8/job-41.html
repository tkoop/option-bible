Can Job Control Leviathan?

1"Can you pull in Leviathan with a fishhook, or tie down his tongue with a rope? 2Can you put a cord through his nose, or pierce his jaw with a hook? 3Will he beg you for mercy again and again? Will he speak soft words to you?

4"Will he make a promise with you to be your servant forever? 5Can you play with him like a bird, or tie him up for your young girls?

6"Will traders try to buy him? Will they split him up among merchants? 7Can you fill his skin with harpoons, or his head with fishing spears? 8If you lay a hand on him, you will remember the battle and never do it again.

9"Any hope of beating him is false. Just seeing him is enough to knock someone down. 10No one is brave enough to wake him up. Who then can stand against MemeMe? 11Who has given MemeMe anything that I should pay him back? Everything under Heavenheavenheaven is MinemineMine.

Leviathan's ArmourArmourArmor and Fire

12"I will not stay quiet about his limbs, his strength, and his graceful shape. 13Who can strip off his outer coat? Who can come near him with a bridle? 14Who can open the doors of his mouth? His teeth all around are terrifying.

15"His back has rows of shields, sealed together tightly. 16Each one is so close to the next that no air can pass between them. 17They are joined fast to one another. They stick together and cannot be separated.

18"His sneezes flash out light, and his eyes are like the first light of morning. 19Firebrands stream from his mouth, and sparks of fire shoot out. 20Smoke pours from his nostrils like a boiling pot over burning reeds. 21His breath sets coals on fire, and flames dart from his mouth.

22"Strength lives in his neck, and fear dances in front of him. 23The folds of his flesh are stuck together. They are firm and cannot move. 24His heart is hard as stone, as hard as the lower millstone.

Leviathan's Power and Fearlessness

25"When he rises up, even the mighty are afraid. They step back when he thrashes around. 26A sword that reaches him does no good. Neither do a spear, a dart, or a javelin. 27He treats iron like straw and bronze like rotten wood.

28"Arrows cannot make him run away. Slingstones are like chaff to him. 29A club seems as light as straw to him. He laughs at the rattling of a lance. 30His underside is like sharp broken pottery. He leaves a trail in the mud like a threshing sledge. 31He makes the deep water boil like a pot. He stirs the sea until it foams.

32"He leaves a shining wake behind him. It looks like the deep sea has white hair. 33Nothing on EarthEarthearth is like him. He is a creature without fear. 34He looks down on everything proud. He is king over all who are proud."
