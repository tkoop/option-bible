Elijah Warns King Ahab

1Elijah the Tishbite, who lived in Gilead, said to Ahab, "As the LORD, the God of Israel, lives—the God I serve—there will be no dew or rain in the coming years unless I say so."

The LORD Provides for Elijah

2Then the word of the LORD came to Elijah: 3"Leave here and go east. Hide yourself by the Kerith BrookbrookBrook, east of the Jordan. 4You can drink from the brook, and I have told the ravens to feed you there."

5So Elijah did what the LORD said. He went and stayed by the Kerith BrookbrookBrook, east of the Jordan. 6The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the brook. 7After a while, the brook dried up because no rain had fallen in the land.

Elijah Goes to Zarephath

8Then the word of the LORD came to him again: 9"Get up and go to Zarephath in Sidon and stay there. I have told a widow there to feed you."

10So Elijah got up and went to Zarephath. When he reached the city gate, he saw a widow gathering sticks. He called to her and said, "Please give me a little water in a cup so I can drink."

11As she went to get it, he called after her, "Please bring me a piece of bread in your hand."

12She said, "As the LORD your God lives, I have no bread. I only have a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am gathering two sticks so I can go home and make a small meal for myself and my son. We will eat it and then die."

13Elijah said to her, "Don't be afraid. Go and do as you said. But first make me a small cake from what you have and bring it to me. Then make something for yourself and your son." 14"For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'The jar of flour will not run out, and the jug of oil will not run dry, until the day the LORD sends rain on the land.'"

15So the woman went and did what Elijah said. She and her family ate for many days. 16The jar of flour did not run out, and the jug of oil did not run dry, just as the LORD had said through Elijah.

Elijah Raises the Widow's Son

17After some time, the woman's son became sick. His illness was so bad that he stopped breathing.

18The woman said to Elijah, "What do you have against me, man of God? Have you come to remind me of my sin and kill my son?"

19Elijah said, "Give me your son." He took the boy from her arms, carried him to the upper room where he was staying, and laid him on his bed.

20Then Elijah cried out to the LORD, "the LORD my God, have YouyouYou also brought tragedy on this widow I am staying with by killing her son?"

21Then he stretched himself over the boy three times and cried out to the LORD, "the LORD my God, please let this boy's life return to him."

22The LORD heard Elijah's prayer, and the boy's life returned to him, and he lived.

23Elijah took the boy and brought him down from the upper room into the house. He gave him to his mother and said, "Look, your son is alive."

24The woman said to Elijah, "Now I know that you are a man of God and that the word of the LORD from your mouth is true."
