Wise Warnings for Everyday Life

1Wine makes people act like fools, and strong drink starts fights. Anyone led astray by them isn't wise. 2A king's anger is like a lion's roar. Anyone who makes him angry risks his own life. 3It's honourablehonourablehonorable to stay away from a fight, but every fool starts one. 4A lazy person doesn't plow when it's time, so at harvest he begs and has nothing.

Honesty, Justice, and Good Sense

5Advice hidden in a person's heart is like deep water, but someone with understanding can draw it out. 6Many people say they are loyal, but who can find a truly faithful person? 7A good person who lives with integrity—his children are blessed after him. 8A king who sits on the throne of justice sees through all evil.

9Who can say, "I have kept my heart pure; I am clean from sin"? 10The LORD hates dishonest weights and measures. 11Even a child is known by what he does, whether his actions are pure and right. 12The LORD made both the ear that hears and the eye that sees.

Wealth, Words, and Relationships

13Don't love sleep, or you'll become poor. Open your eyes, and you'll have enough food. 14The buyer says, "It's no good, it's no good," but after he leaves, he brags about the deal.

15There may be gold and plenty of pearls, but wise words are the most precious treasure. 16If someone guarantees a stranger's debt, take his coat as security. If he does it for a foreigner, get security from him. 17Food gained by lying tastes sweet at first, but later it fills his mouth with gravel. 18Plans are made firm by good advice, and wise guidance wins wars.

19A gossip reveals secrets, so don't associate with someone who can't keep his mouth shut. 20If someone curses his father or mother, his future will be snuffed out in darkness. 21An inheritance grabbed too quickly at first won't be blessed in the end.
