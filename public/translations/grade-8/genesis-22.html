God Tests Abraham

1After these things, God tested Abraham. He said to him, "Abraham!" Abraham answered, "Here I am." 2God said, "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will tell you about."

Abraham and Isaac Travel to Moriah
