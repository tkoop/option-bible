King Ahasuerus Taxes the Land

1King Ahasuerus placed a tax on the land and on the islands of the sea.

Mordecai's Greatness Is Recorded

2All the acts of his power and might, and the full account of Mordecai's greatness, which the king raised him to, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia? 3For Mordecai the Jew was second only to King Ahasuerus. He was great among the Jews and well liked by many of his brothers. He worked for the good of his people and spoke peace to all his kindred.
