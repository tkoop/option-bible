Rehoboam Turns Away from the LORD

1When Rehoboam had made his kingdom strong and had become powerful, he and all Israel with him turned away from the law of the LORD.

Shishak Invades Judah

2In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, because they had sinned against the LORD, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem. 3He came with 1,200 chariots and 60,000 horsemen. A huge crowd came with him from Egypt—Libyans, Sukkiites, and Cushites—and no one could count them. 4He captured the fortified cities of Judah and reached Jerusalem.
