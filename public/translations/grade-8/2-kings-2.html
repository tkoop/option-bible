Elijah Is Taken Up to Heaven

1Now when the the LORD was about to take Elijah up to Heavenheavenheaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were leaving Gilgal.

2Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, for the the LORD has sent me to Bethel."

But Elisha said, "As the the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

3The prophets' followers at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the the LORD will take away your master from over you today?"

Elisha answered, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

4Then Elijah said to him, "Stay here, please, for the the LORD has sent me to Jericho."

But Elisha said, "As the the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

5The prophets' followers at Jericho came near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that the the LORD will take away your master from over you today?"

Elisha answered, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

6Then Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, for the the LORD has sent me to the Jordan."

But Elisha said, "As the the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

7Fifty men from the prophets' followers went and stood at a distance from them, and the two of them stood by the Jordan.

8Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the water. It divided to the right and left, and the two of them crossed on dry ground.

Elisha Asks for a Double Share of Elijah's Spirit

9When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I can do for you before I am taken from you."

Elisha said, "Please let me have twice as much of your spirit."

10Elijah said, "YouyouYou have asked a hard thing. If you see me when I am taken from you, it will be yours; but if not, it will not."

11While they were walking and talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah went up to Heavenheavenheaven in a whirlwind.

12Elisha saw it and cried, "MymyMy father, my father! Israel's defender is gone!" He could not see Elijah anymore. Then he grabbed his own clothes and tore them in two.

Elisha Takes Elijah's Place

13He picked up Elijah's cloak, which had fallen from him, and went back and stood by the bank of the Jordan.

14He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him and struck the water. He said, "Where is the the LORD, the God of Elijah?" When he struck the water, it divided to the right and left, and Elisha crossed over.

15When the prophets' followers at Jericho saw him from a distance, they said, "ThetheThe spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed to the ground before him.

16They said to him, "Look, there are fifty strong men among your servants. Please let them go and search for your master. Perhaps the SpiritspiritSpirit of the the LORD has carried him away and thrown him on one of the mountains or in one of the valleys."

Elisha said, "YouyouYou must not send them."

17But they kept urging him until he gave in, so he said, "Send them." They sent fifty men, who searched for three days but did not find him.

18When they came back to Elisha, he was staying in Jericho. He said to them, "Did I not tell you not to go?"

Elisha Makes the Water Good

19The men of the city said to Elisha, "Look, master, the city is in a good place, as you can see. But the water is bad, and the land is barren."

20Elisha said, "BringbringBring me a new bowl, and put salt in it." So they brought it to him.

21He went out to the spring of water and threw the salt into it. He said, "Thus says the the LORD, 'I have healed this water; from now on it will not cause death or make the land barren.'"

22So the water was healed, just as Elisha had said. It is still good today.

23He went up from there to Bethel. As he was walking along the road, some young boys came out of the city and made fun of him. They said to him, "Go up, baldy! Go up, baldy!"

24He turned around, looked at them, and cursed them in the name of the the LORD. Then two bears came out of the woods and tore forty-two of the boys to pieces.

25From there he went to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.
