Daniel's Vision by the Tigris

1In the third year of Cyrus, king of Persia, a message was revealed to Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar. The message was true and concerned a great war. Daniel understood the message and gained understanding from the vision. 2In those days I, Daniel, mourned for three full weeks. 3I ate no rich food. No meat or wine touched my mouth, and I did not put oil on myself, until the three weeks were over. 4On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was standing beside the great river, the Tigris.

The Heavenly Messenger

5I looked up and saw a man dressed in linen. A belt of fine gold from Uphaz was around his waist. 6His body was like beryl. His face shone like lightning, and his eyes were like flaming torches. His arms and legs gleamed like polished bronze, and his voice sounded like a crowd. 7Only I, Daniel, saw the vision. The men with me did not see it, but great fear came over them, and they ran away to hide. 8So I was left alone, staring at this great vision. I had no strength left. My face grew deathly pale, and I could not hold myself up. 9Then I heard the sound of his words. As I heard them, I fell into a deep sleep, with my face to the ground.

The Messenger Speaks to Daniel

10Suddenly a hand touched me and made me tremble on my hands and knees. 11He said to me, "Daniel, man greatly loved, understand the words I am speaking to you. Stand up, for I have now been sent to you." While he spoke this word to me, I stood up trembling.

12"Do not be afraid, Daniel," he said, "for from the first day you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard. I have come because of your words. 13The prince of the kingdom of Persia stood against me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I was left there with the kings of Persia. 14Now I have come to help you understand what will happen to your people in the last days, for the vision concerns days yet to come."

Daniel's Response and Final Message

15While he spoke these words to me, I bowed my face to the ground and could not speak. 16Then someone who looked like a son of man touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke to the one standing before me. "My lord, because of the vision, anguish has come upon me, and I have no strength. 17How can this servant of my lord speak with my lord? I have no strength left, and no breath is left in me."

18Again the one who looked like a man touched me and strengthened me. 19He said, "Do not be afraid, man greatly loved. Peace be to you. Be strong, yes, be strong!" When he spoke to me, I was strengthened.

Then I said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

20Then he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? Now I will return to fight against the prince of Persia, and when I leave, the prince of Greece will come. 21But I will tell you what is written in the BookbookBook of TruthtruthTruth. No one helps me against them except Michael, your prince."
