Remember Your Creator While You Are Young

1Remember your CreatorcreatorCreator in the days of your youth, before the days of trouble come and the years draw near when you will say, “I have no pleasure in them”— 2before the sun and the light and the moon and the stars grow dark, and the clouds return after the rain; 3when the keepers of the house tremble, and the strong men stoop, when the grinders stop because they are few, and those who look through the windows grow dim; 4when the doors to the street are closed and the sound of grinding fades; when people rise at the sound of birds, but all their songs grow faint; 5when people are afraid of heights and of dangers in the streets; when the almond tree blossoms, the grasshopper drags itself along, and desire fails. Then people go to their lasting home, and mourners walk the streets. 6Remember HimhimHim before the silver cord is snapped, or the golden bowl is broken; before the pitcher is shattered at the spring, or the wheel broken at the well, 7and the dust returns to the earth it came from, and the spirit returns to God, who gave it.

Everything Is Meaningless

8“Meaningless! Meaningless!” says the TeacherteacherTeacher. “Everything is meaningless!”

The Teacher's Final Thoughts

9Not only was the TeacherteacherTeacher wise, but he also taught the people. He thought carefully, searched things out, and put many proverbs in order. 10The TeacherteacherTeacher tried to find the right words, and what he wrote was honest and true.

11The words of the wise are like goads. Their collected sayings are like nails driven in firmly, and they are given by one ShepherdshepherdShepherd. 12My son, be careful about anything beyond these. There is no end to making many books, and too much study wears out the body.

13Now that everything has been heard, here is the final point: Fear God and keep HishisHis commands, for this is the whole duty of people. 14For God will bring every deed into judgmentjudgementjudgment, including every hidden thing, whether it is good or evil.
