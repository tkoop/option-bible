Asa's Good Reign

1Abijah died and was buried in the CitycityCity of David. His son Asa became king after him. In his days the land had peace for ten years. 2Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God.

3He took away the foreign altars and high places. He broke the sacred stones and cut down the Asherah poles. 4He told the people of Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to obey the law and HishisHis commands. 5He removed the high places and incense altars from all the cities of Judah. The kingdom was at peace under him.

Asa Prepares for War

6Because the land was at peace, Asa built fortified cities in Judah. There was no war in those years, because the LORD gave him rest. 7He said to Judah, “Let us build up these cities and surround them with walls, towers, gates, and bars. The land is still ours, because we have sought the LORD our God. We have sought HimhimHim, and HeheHe has given us rest on every side.” So they built and did well. 8Asa had an army of 300,000 men from Judah. They carried shields and spears. From Benjamin there were 280,000 men who carried shields and bows. All these men were brave soldiers.

Zerah Invades Judah

9Zerah the Cushite marched against them with an army of one million men and three hundred chariots. He came as far as Mareshah. 10Asa went out to meet him, and they set up for battle in the ValleyvalleyValley of Zephathah near Mareshah.

Asa Prays and Wins the Battle

11Asa cried out to the LORD his God, “the LORD, no one but YouyouYou can help, whether an army is large or weak. Help us, the LORD our God, because we trust in YouyouYou. We have come against this huge army in YouryourYour name. the LORD, YouyouYou are our God. Do not let people win against YouyouYou.”

12So the LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and the Cushites ran away. 13Asa and his men chased them all the way to Gerar. The Cushites fell until no one was left alive, because they were crushed before the LORD and HishisHis army. Asa's men carried off a great deal of plunder.

14They attacked all the cities around Gerar, because the fear of the LORD had fallen on them. They plundered all the cities, because there was much wealth in them. 15They also attacked the tents where livestock were kept. They took many sheep and camels and went back to Jerusalem.
