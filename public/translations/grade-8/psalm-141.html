David Calls on the LORD for Help

1The LORD, I call to YouyouYou; come quickly to me. Hear my voice when I call to YouyouYou. 2May my prayer be set before YouyouYou like incense, and may my hands lifted up be like the evening sacrifice.

A Prayer for a Clean Heart and Lips

3Set a guard, O the LORD, over my mouth; keep watch over my lips. 4Do not let my heart turn to evil, or let me join evil people in doing wrong. Do not let me share their rich food.

5Let a good person correct me; that is kindness. Let him rebuke me; it will be like oil soothing my head. My head will not refuse it. For my prayer is still against the deeds of evil people. 6Their judges are thrown down from cliffs; they will hear my words, because they are pleasant. 7As when someone plows and breaks up the ground, so our bones are scattered at the mouth of the grave.

Trusting the LORD for Protection

8But my eyes are fixed on YouyouYou, O the LORD, my Lord; in YouyouYou I take refuge. Do not leave my soul empty. 9Keep me from the trap they set for me, and from the snares of evil people. 10Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I pass by safely.
