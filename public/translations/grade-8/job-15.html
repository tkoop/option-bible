Eliphaz Rebukes Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

2"Should a wise man answer with empty words and fill his belly with hot air? 3Should he argue with useless words, or speak in ways that do no good? 4But you even destroy reverence and weaken prayer before God. 5Your sin teaches your mouth, and you choose the language of the crafty. 6Your own mouth condemns you, and your lips testify against you.

No One Is Pure Before God

7"Were you the first man ever born? Were you brought forth before the hills? 8Do you hear God's secret counsel? Do you keep wisdom for yourself? 9What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that is not with us? 10The greygreygray-haired and the aged are among us, men older than your father. 11Are God's comforts too small for you, or a gentle word spoken to you? 12Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash? 13so that you turn your spirit against God and pour out such words from your mouth? 14What is man, that he should be pure, or one born of woman, that he should be righteous? 15If God puts no trust in HishisHis holy ones, and even the heavens are not pure in HishisHis sight, 16how much less man, who is vile and corrupt, who sins as easily as drinking water!

The Fate of the Wicked

17"Listen to me; I will show you, and what I have seen I will declare, 18what wise men have declared and have not hidden, passed down from their fathers. 19To them alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them. 20The wicked person suffers all his days, and the years stored up for the ruthless are hidden. 21Sounds of terror are in his ears; in peace the destroyer comes upon him. 22He does not believe he will escape the darkness; he is marked for the sword. 23He wanders looking for food, knowing that a day of darkness is ready for him. 24Distress and anguish terrify him; they overpower him, like a king ready for battle. 25For he has stretched out his hand against God and acts arrogantly against the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, 26rushing at HimhimHim with a thick, strong shield. 27Though his face is covered with fat and his waist bulges with flesh, 28he will dwell in ruined cities, in houses no one inhabits, which are destined to become rubble. 29He will not grow rich; his wealth will not last, and his possessions will not spread over the land. 30He will not escape the darkness; a flame will wither his shoots, and the breath of the LORD's mouth will carry him away. 31Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself, for emptiness will be his reward. 32It will be paid in full before his time, and his branch will not stay green. 33He will shake off his unripe grapes like a vine, and cast off his blossoms like an olive tree. 34For the company of the godless is barren, and fire consumes the tents of those who take bribes. 35They conceive trouble and give birth to evil; their womb prepares deceit."
