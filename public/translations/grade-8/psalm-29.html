Give Glory to the LORD

A Psalm of David. 1Give to the LORD, O sons of God, give to the LORD glory and strength. 2Give to the LORD the glory HishisHis name deserves; worship the LORD in the beauty of HishisHis holiness.

3The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is over many waters. 4The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is full of majesty.

5The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; yes, the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. 6He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox. 7The voice of the LORD strikes with flames of fire.

8The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Kadesh. 9The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare, and in HishisHis temple all cry, "GlorygloryGlory!"

10The LORD sits as KingkingKing over the flood; the LORD is KingkingKing forever. 11the LORD gives strength to HishisHis people; the LORD blesses HishisHis people with peace.
