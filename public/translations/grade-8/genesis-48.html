Joseph Visits His Sick Father

1After these things, Joseph was told, “Your father is sick.” So he took his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, along with him.

2When Jacob was told, “Your son Joseph has come to you,” Israel gathered his strength and sat up in bed.

Jacob Adopts Ephraim and Manasseh

3Jacob said to Joseph, “God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan and blessed me. 4He said to me, ‘I will make you fruitful and multiply you. I will make you into a large group of peoples, and I will give this land to your descendants after you as a lasting possession.’”

5“And now your two sons, who were born to you in Egypt before I came to you here, are mine. Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, just as Reuben and Simeon are mine. 6But any children you have after them will be yours. They will be counted under their brothers’ names when the land is divided. 7As for me, when I was coming back from Paddan, Rachel sadly died on the way in the land of Canaan, still some distance from Ephrath. I buried her there beside the road to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem).”

Jacob Blesses Joseph's Sons

8When Israel saw Joseph’s sons, he asked, “Who are these?”

9Joseph said to his father, “They are the sons God has given me here.”

Then Jacob said, “Please bring them to me so I can bless them.”

10Israel’s eyes were weak from old age, so he could not see well. Joseph brought his sons to him, and Israel kissed them and hugged them.

11Israel said to Joseph, “I never thought I would see your face again, but God has let me see your children too.”

12Then Joseph took them from Israel’s knees and bowed down with his face to the ground.

13Joseph took both boys—Ephraim in his right hand, toward Israel’s left hand, and Manasseh in his left hand, toward Israel’s right hand—and brought them close to him.

14But Israel reached out his right hand and put it on Ephraim’s head, even though Ephraim was the younger. He crossed his hands and put his left hand on Manasseh’s head, even though Manasseh was the firstborn.

15Then he blessed Joseph and said, “May the God my fathers Abraham and Isaac followed, the God who has been my shepherd all my life until today, 16the Angel who has saved me from all harm—may HeheHe bless these boys. May they be called by my name and by the names of my fathers Abraham and Isaac, and may they grow into a great number on the earth.”

17When Joseph saw that his father had put his right hand on Ephraim’s head, he was upset. He took his father’s hand to move it from Ephraim’s head to Manasseh’s head.

18Joseph said to his father, “No, my father. This one is the firstborn. Put your right hand on his head.”

19But his father refused and said, “I know, my son, I know. He will also become a people, and he will also be great. But his younger brother will be greater than he is, and his descendants will become many nations.”

20So he blessed them that day, saying, “Israel will bless its children by saying, ‘May God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh.’” In this way he put Ephraim before Manasseh.

Jacob's Final Words to Joseph

21Then Israel said to Joseph, “Look, I am about to die, but God will be with you and bring you back to the land of your fathers. 22And to you I give one more share than your brothers: the ridge of Shechem, which I took from the Amorites with my sword and bow.”
