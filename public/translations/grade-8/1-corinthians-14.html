Desire the Gifts That Build Up the Church

1Pursue love, and be eager for spiritual gifts, but especially for prophecy. 2For the one who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people but to God. No one understands him; he speaks mysteries by the SpiritspiritSpirit. 3But the one who prophesies speaks to people for their strengthening, encouragement, and comfort. 4The one who speaks in a tongue builds up himself, but the one who prophesies builds up the church.

5I wish all of you could speak in tongues, but I would rather you prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, so the church may be built up. 6Now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I help you unless I bring you a revelation, knowledge, prophecy, or teaching?

7Even lifeless instruments, like a flute or harp, must make clear sounds. How will anyone know the tune unless the notes are different? 8If a trumpet gives an unclear call, who will get ready for battle? 9In the same way, unless you speak clear words with your tongue, how will anyone know what you are saying? You will just be speaking into the air.

10There are many kinds of languages in the world, and none is without meaning. 11If I do not understand the meaning of a language, I am a foreigner to the speaker, and he is a foreigner to me. 12So it is with you. Since you are eager for spiritual gifts, strive to excel in the gifts that build up the church.

Use Tongues and Minds Together

13Therefore, the one who speaks in a tongue should pray that he may interpret. 14For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind gets no benefit. 15What then? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing with my spirit, but I will also sing with my mind.

16Otherwise, if you bless with your spirit, how can someone who does not understand say “Amen” to your thanksgiving? He does not know what you are saying. 17You may be giving thanks well enough, but the other person is not built up.

18I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you. 19But in the church I would rather speak five clear words with my mind to instruct others than ten thousand words in a tongue.

Tongues and Prophecy Have Different Purposes

20Brothers, do not be children in your thinking. Be like babies in evil, but be mature in your thinking. 21It is written in the LawlawLaw: “With strange tongues and foreign lips I will speak to this people, but even then they will not listen to MemeMe,” says the Lord.

22So tongues are a sign, not for believers but for unbelievers. Prophecy, however, is for believers, not for unbelievers. 23So if the whole church comes together and everyone speaks in tongues, and outsiders or unbelievers come in, will they not say that you are out of your minds? 24But if an unbeliever or an outsider comes in while everyone is prophesying, he will be corrected and examined by all. 25The secrets of his heart will be made plain. So he will fall on his face and worship God, saying, “God is really among you!”

Keep Worship Orderly

26What then, brothers? When you come together, each one has a hymn, a teaching, a revelation, a tongue, or an interpretation. Everything must be done to build up the church. 27If anyone speaks in a tongue, two or at most three should speak, one at a time, and someone should interpret. 28But if there is no interpreter, the speaker should be quiet in the church and speak to himself and to God.

29Two or three prophets should speak, and the others should judge carefully what is said. 30If a revelation comes to someone else while he is sitting, the first speaker should stop. 31For you can all prophesy one at a time, so that everyone may learn and be encouraged. 32The spirits of prophets are under the control of prophets. 33For God is not a God of disorder but of peace, as in all the churches of the saints.

34Women should remain silent in the churches. They are not permitted to speak, but must be submissive, as the LawlawLaw says. 35If they want to learn something, they should ask their own husbands at home, for it is shameful for a woman to speak in church. 36Did the word of God come from you? Or are you the only ones it has reached? 37If anyone thinks he is a prophet or spiritual person, he should recognizerecogniserecognize that what I am writing to you is the Lord's command. 38But if anyone ignores this, he will be ignored. 39So, my brothers, be eager to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues. 40But everything should be done properly and in order.
