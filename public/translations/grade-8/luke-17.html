Jesus Teaches About Faith and Forgiveness

1Jesus told HishisHis disciples, "Things that cause people to sin are sure to come, but how terrible it will be for the person through whom they come. 2It would be better for him to have a large millstone tied around his neck and be thrown into the sea than to cause one of these little ones to stumble. 3Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him. 4If he sins against you seven times in one day and seven times comes back to you saying, 'I repent,' you must forgive him."

5The apostles said to the Lord, "Give us more faith."

6The Lord replied, "If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you."

7"Which of you, if you had a servant plowing or tending sheep, would say to him when he came in from the field, 'Come right in and sit down to eat'? 8No, you would say, 'Prepare my supper, get dressed, and serve me while I eat and drink. After that, you can eat and drink.' 9Does he thank the servant because he did what he was told? 10So you too, when you have done everything you were told, should say, 'We are unworthy servants; we have only done what we ought to do.'"
