Helping Neighbors and Lost Animals

1If you see your brother's ox or sheep wandering away, do not pretend you do not see them. Be sure to take them back to your brother. 2If your brother is not nearby or you do not know him, bring the animal to your home and keep it with you until he comes looking for it. Then give it back to him. 3Do the same for his donkey, his clothes, or anything else your brother loses and you find. Do not act like you did not see it. 4If you see your brother's donkey or ox fall down on the road, do not ignore it. Help him get it back on its feet.

Rules for Daily Life

5A woman must not wear men's clothing, and a man must not wear women's clothing. Anyone who does this is detestable to the LORD your God.

6If you come across a bird's nest along the road, in a tree or on the ground, and the mother is sitting on the young or on the eggs, do not take the mother with the young. 7You may take the young, but let the mother go free. Then things will go well for you, and you will live a long time.

8When you build a new house, put a railing around your roof. Then you will not be guilty if someone falls from it.

9Do not plant two kinds of seed in your vineyard. If you do, the whole crop will be unfit to use, both the seed you planted and the fruit of the vineyard. 10Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together. 11Do not wear clothing made of wool and linen woven together. 12Make tassels on the four corners of the cloak you wear.

Marriage and Purity Laws

13If a man marries a woman and later hates her, 14and slanders her by saying, "I married this woman, but when I was with her I found she was not a virgin."

15Then the young woman's father and mother must bring proof of her virginity to the city elders at the gate.

16The young woman's father must say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, but he hates her. 17Now he has accused her of shameful things and says, 'I found that your daughter was not a virgin.' But here is proof that my daughter was a virgin." Then they must spread the cloth before the elders of the city.

18The elders of that city must take the man and punish him. 19They must fine him one hundred shekels of silver and give the money to the young woman's father, because the man gave an Israelite virgin a bad name. She must remain his wife, and he may never divorce her.

20But if the charge is true and the young woman was not a virgin, 21they must bring her to the door of her father's house. The men of her city must stone her to death, because she did a disgraceful thing in Israel by being unfaithful while still in her father's house. You must remove the evil from among you.

Punishments for Sexual Sins

22If a man is caught sleeping with another man's wife, both the man and the woman must die. You must remove this evil from Israel.

23If a man meets a young woman who is engaged to another man, and he sleeps with her in the city, 24bring both of them to the gate of that city and stone them to death. Stone the young woman because she did not cry out for help in the city, and stone the man because he violated another man's wife. You must remove the evil from among you.

25But if a man meets an engaged young woman in the countryside and forces her and sleeps with her, only the man who slept with her must die. 26Do nothing to the young woman, because she has done nothing deserving death. This case is like a man attacking and murdering his neighbourneighbourneighbor. 27The man found her in the countryside, and the engaged young woman cried out, but no one was there to save her.

28If a man meets a young woman who is not engaged, and he seizes her and sleeps with her, and they are found out, 29the man who slept with her must pay the young woman's father fifty shekels of silver. She must become his wife, because he violated her. He may never divorce her.

30A man must not marry his father's wife or sleep with her.
