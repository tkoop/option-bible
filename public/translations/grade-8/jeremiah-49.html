JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Ammon

1Concerning the Ammonites, this is what the LORD says: "Does Israel have no sons? Does he have no heir? Why then has Milcom taken possession of Gad, and his people settled in its cities?"

2"Therefore, behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will sound the battle cry against Rabbah of the Ammonites; and it shall become a heap of ruins, and her villages will be burned with fire. Then Israel will drive out those who drove him out," says the LORD.

3"Wail, Heshbon, for Ai is destroyed! Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! Put on sackcloth and mourn; run back and forth along the walls. For Milcom will go into exile, together with his priests and officials. 4Why do you boast in your valleys, your flowing valleys, O faithless daughter? You trust in your treasures and say, 'Who will come against me?'"

5"Behold, I will bring terror upon you, declares the Lord GOD of Hosts, from all around you. You will be driven away, each man in a different direction, and no one will gather the fugitives. 6But afterward I will restore the Ammonites from captivity, declares the LORD."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Edom

7Concerning Edom, this is what the LORD of Hosts says: "Is there no more wisdom in Teman? Has counsel perished from the prudent? Has their wisdom vanished?"

8"Turn and flee! Dwell deep, O inhabitants of Dedan! For I will bring disaster on Esau when I punish him."

9"If grape gatherers came to you, would they not leave gleanings? If thieves came by night, would they not steal only as much as they needed? 10But I will strip Esau bare; I will uncover his hiding places, and he will be unable to hide. His offspring will be destroyed, along with his brothers and neighbors, and he will be no more. 11Leave your orphans; I will keep them alive. Let your widows trust in MemeMe."

12"For this is what the LORD says: 'If those who do not deserve to drink the cup must drink it, should you go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, but you must surely drink it.' 13For I have sworn by MyselfmyselfMyself, declares the LORD, that Bozrah will become a desolation, a reproach, a waste, and a curse, and all her cities will be perpetual ruins."

14"I have heard a message from the LORD; an envoy has been sent among the nations: 'Gather together and come against her; rise up for battle!'"

15"Behold, I will make you small among the nations, despised among men. 16Your terror has deceived you, and the pride of your heart, O you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, who hold the height of the hill. Though you make your nest as high as the eagle, from there I will bring you down, declares the LORD."

17"Edom will become an object of horror. All who pass by will be appalled and will hiss at all her wounds. 18As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighboring cities, says the LORD, no one will live there, and no son of man will dwell in it. 19Behold, one will come up like a lion from the thickets of the Jordan to the watered pasture. For in an instant I will chase him from it, and I will appoint over it whomever I choose. For who is like MemeMe, and who can challenge MemeMe? What shepherd can stand before MemeMe?"

20"Therefore hear the counsel of the LORD that HeheHe has planned against Edom, and the thoughts HeheHe has intended against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely the little ones of the flock will be dragged away; surely their pasture will be made desolate because of them. 21At the sound of their fall the earth will tremble; the cry will be heard at the Red SeaseaSea. 22Behold, one will soar like an eagle and swoop down, spreading his wings over Bozrah. In that day the hearts of Edom's mighty men will be like the heart of a woman in labourlabourlabor."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Damascus

23"Concerning Damascus: 'Hamath and Arpad are put to shame, for they have heard bad news; they are disheartened. Troubled like the sea, they cannot be quiet.' 24Damascus has become feeble; she has turned to flee, and panic has seized her. Anguish and pain have gripped her, like a woman in labourlabourlabor. 25How is the city of praise not forsaken, the town of MymyMy joy?"

26"Therefore her young men will fall in her streets, and all her men of war will be silenced in that day, declares the LORD of Hosts. 27I will set fire to the walls of Damascus, and it will consume the palaces of Ben-hadad."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Kedar, Hazor, and Elam

28"Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon defeated, this is what the LORD says: 'Arise, go up against Kedar, and destroy the people of the east!' 29They will take their tents and their flocks, their tent curtains and all their goods, and carry off their camels for themselves. They will proclaim terror on every side. 30Flee! Run far away! Dwell deep, O inhabitants of Hazor, declares the LORD; for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devised a plan against you and formed a purpose against you."

31"Arise, go up against a nation at ease, one that dwells securely, declares the LORD; a nation with no gates or bars, that dwells alone. 32Their camels will become plunder, and their many livestock a spoil. I will scatter to the wind those with shaved temples, and I will bring disaster on them from every side, declares the LORD. 33Hazor will become a haunt of jackals, a desolation forever. No one will live there; no son of man will dwell in it."

34This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah:

35"This is what the LORD of Hosts says: 'Behold, I will break the bow of Elam, the chief of their might.' 36I will bring the four winds against Elam from the four corners of Heavenheavenheaven, and I will scatter them to all these winds. There will be no nation to which Elam's exiles will not go. 37I will shatter Elam before their enemies, before those who seek their lives. I will bring disaster upon them, MymyMy fierce anger, declares the LORD. I will send the sword after them until I have finished them off. 38I will set MymyMy throne in Elam and destroy its king and officials, declares the LORD. 39Yet in the last days I will restore Elam from captivity, declares the LORD."
