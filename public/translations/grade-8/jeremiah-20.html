Pashhur Punishes Jeremiah

1When Pashhur the priest, son of Immer and chief officer in the house of the LORD, heard Jeremiah prophesying these things, 2he had Jeremiah the prophet beaten and put in the stocks at the Upper GategateGate of Benjamin in the LORD's temple.

3The next day Pashhur brought Jeremiah out of the stocks. Then Jeremiah said to him, "ThetheThe LORD doesn't call your name Pashhur. He calls you Terror on Every Side. 4For this is what the LORD says: 'I will make you a terror to yourself and to all your friends. They will fall by the sword of their enemies while you watch. I will give all Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon. He will carry them captive to Babylon and strike them down with the sword. 5I will give away all the wealth of this city, all its hard work, and all its treasures. I will hand over all the treasures of Judah's kings to their enemies. The enemies will plunder them, take them, and carry them off to Babylon. 6And you, Pashhur, and everyone in your house will go into captivity. You will go to Babylon and die there. You will be buried there—you and all your friends to whom you prophesied lies.'"

Jeremiah Curses the Day He Was Born

14"Cursed be the day I was born!
