Melchizedek, King and Priest

1For this Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God MostmostMost HighhighHigh. He met Abraham as he was coming back from defeating the kings, and he blessed him. 2Abraham gave him a tenth of everything. First, Melchizedek's name means “king of righteousness.” Then it also means “king of Salem,” which is “king of peace.” 3He has no father or mother and no family line. He has no beginning of days and no end of life. Instead, he is like the SonsonSon of God and remains a priest forever.

4Think how great this man was. Even Abraham, the great ancestor, gave him a tenth of the best things taken in war. 5The law says that the sons of Levi who become priests can collect a tenth from the people. That means they collect it from their own brothers, even though those brothers also come from Abraham. 6But Melchizedek did not come from Levi's family line, yet he collected a tenth from Abraham and blessed the man who had God's promises. 7Everyone knows that the greater person blesses the lesser one.

8In one case, men who die receive the tenth. In the other case, it is received by someone who is said to live. 9In a way, even Levi, who receives tithes, paid tithes through Abraham. 10This is because Levi had not yet been born; he was still part of Abraham's family line when Melchizedek met Abraham.

A New and Better Priesthood

11If the Levite priesthood had made people perfect, why would another priest need to come, one like Melchizedek instead of one from Aaron's family? The people received the law under that priesthood. 12When the priesthood changes, the law must change too. 13The OneoneOne this speaks about belongs to a different tribe. No one from that tribe ever served at the altar. 14It is clear that our Lord descended from Judah, and Moses never said anything about priests from that tribe.

15This point becomes even clearer if another priest like Melchizedek appears. 16He became a priest not because of a rule about family descent, but because of the power of a life that cannot be destroyed. 17Scripture says about HimhimHim, “You are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek.” 18So the old command is set aside because it was weak and useless. 19The law did not make anything perfect. But a better hope has been brought in, and through it we come near to God.

Jesus, Our Perfect High Priest

20This change came with an oath. The earlier priests became priests without an oath. 21But Jesus became a priest with an oath. God said to HimhimHim, “The LORD has sworn and will not change HishisHis mind, ‘You are a priest forever.’” 22Because of this, Jesus is the guarantee of a better covenant.

23There were many priests before because death kept them from continuing in office. 24But Jesus lives forever, so HeheHe holds a priesthood that never ends. 25That is why HeheHe can save completely those who come to God through HimhimHim. He always lives to pray for them.

26It was right for us to have such a high priest: holy, innocent, pure, set apart from sinners, and lifted above the heavens. 27He does not need to offer sacrifices every day, first for HishisHis own sins and then for the people's sins. He offered HimselfhimselfHimself once for all. 28The law appoints weak men as high priests. But the oath, which came after the law, appoints the SonsonSon, who has been made perfect forever.
