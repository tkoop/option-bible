Family Leaders Who Returned with Ezra

1These are the family leaders and their family records of the people who came back with me from Babylon while King Artaxerxes ruled. 2From the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; from the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; from the sons of David, Hattush. 3From the sons of Shecaniah, from the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him were listed 150 men. 4From the sons of Pahath-Moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him 200 men. 5From the sons of Shecaniah, the son of Jahaziel, and with him 300 men. 6From the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him 50 men. 7From the sons of Elam, Isaiah son of Athaliah, and with him 70 men. 8From the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him 80 men. 9From the sons of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him 218 men. 10From the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, and with him 160 men. 11From the sons of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him 28 men. 12From the sons of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him 110 men. 13From the last of the sons of Adonikam, these were their names: Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah, and with them 60 men. 14From the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zabbud, and with them 70 men.

Gathering and Fasting at Ahava

15I gathered them at the river that flows to Ahava, and we camped there three days. I looked over the people and the priests, but I found no Levites there. 16So I sent for these leaders: Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam. I also sent for Joiarib and Elnathan, who were teachers. 17I sent them to Iddo, the leader at Casiphia. I told them exactly what to say to Iddo and his relatives, the temple servants at Casiphia, so they would bring us workers for the house of our God. 18Because the gracious hand of our God was on us, they brought us Sherebiah, a wise and thoughtful man, from the sons of Mahli son of Levi son of Israel, along with his sons and brothers, 18 men. 19They also brought Hashabiah and with him Jeshaiah from the sons of Merari, along with his brothers and their sons, 20 men. 20They also brought 220 temple servants, whom David and the leaders had assigned to help the Levites. All of them were listed by name. 21There by the Ahava River I called for a fast, so we could humble ourselves before our God and ask HimhimHim for a safe journey for us, our children, and all our possessions. 22I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us from enemies on the road, because we had told the king, "ThetheThe hand of our God is good to all who seek HimhimHim, but HishisHis power and anger are against all who forsake HimhimHim." 23So we fasted and prayed to our God about this, and HeheHe answered us.

Entrusting the Temple Treasures

24Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests, along with Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers. 25I weighed out to them the silver, gold, and articles that the king, his advisers, his officials, and all the Israelites present had given for the house of our God. 26I weighed out 650 talents of silver, 100 talents of silver articles, and 100 talents of gold. 27I also weighed out 20 gold bowls worth 1,000 darics, and two fine bronze articles as valuable as gold. 28I said to them, "YouyouYou are holy to the the LORD, and these articles are holy. The silver and gold are a freewill offering to the the LORD, the God of your fathers." 29Guard them carefully until you weigh them in the rooms of the house of the the LORD in Jerusalem before the leading priests, Levites, and family leaders of Israel. 30So the priests and Levites took charge of the silver, gold, and articles to carry them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.

Journey to Jerusalem and Worship

31On the twelfth day of the first month we left the Ahava River to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was on us, and HeheHe protected us from enemies and ambushes along the way. 32We arrived in Jerusalem and rested there three days. 33On the fourth day, in the house of our God, we weighed the silver, gold, and articles into the hands of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest, along with Eleazar son of Phinehas, and the Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui. 34Everything was counted and weighed, and the total weight was recorded at that time. 35The exiles who had returned from captivity offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering. All of this was a burnt offering to the the LORD. 36They also gave the king's orders to the royal officials and governors west of the Euphrates, and these officials supported the people and the house of God.
