The Watchman's Duty

1The word of the LORD came to me:

2"Son of man, speak to your people and tell them: 'When I bring the sword against a land, the people of that land choose one of their men and make him their watchman. 3If he sees the sword coming against the land and blows the trumpet to warn the people, 4then if anyone hears the trumpet but does not listen to the warning, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head. 5He heard the trumpet but did not listen to the warning; his blood will be on himself. But if he had listened to the warning, he would have saved his life. 6But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the trumpet, so that the people are not warned, and the sword comes and takes one of them, that person is taken away in his sin, but I will hold the watchman accountable for his blood.

7"Now, son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel; so hear the word I speak and give them warning from MemeMe. 8When I say to the wicked, 'YouyouYou will surely die,' and you do not warn him or speak out to turn him from his wicked way, then that wicked man will die in his sin, and I will hold you accountable for his blood. 9But if you warn the wicked man to turn from his way, and he does not turn, he will die in his sin, but you will have saved your life.

10"Son of man, say to the house of Israel: 'You say, "OurourOur offencesoffencesoffenses and sins weigh us down, and we are wasting away because of them. How can we live?"' 11Say to them: 'As surely as I live, declares the Lord the LORD, I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that the wicked turn from their ways and live. Turn! Turn from your evil ways! Why will you die, house of Israel?'

12"Therefore, son of man, say to your people: 'The righteousness of a righteous man will not save him on the day he sins, and the wickedness of a wicked man will not cause him to stumble on the day he turns from his wickedness. Nor will a righteous man be able to live by his righteousness on the day he sins.' 13If I tell a righteous man that he will surely live, but he trusts in his righteousness and does wrong, then none of his righteous acts will be remembered; he will die for the wrong he has done. 14And if I say to the wicked man, 'You will surely die,' but he turns from his sin and does what is just and right— 15if he returns what he took as a pledge, pays back what he stole, and follows the laws that lead to life without doing wrong, he will surely live; he will not die. 16None of the sins he has committed will be remembered against him. He has done what is just and right; he will surely live. 17Yet your people say, 'The way of the Lord is not just.' But it is their own way that is not just. 18If a righteous man turns from his righteousness and does wrong, he will die for it. 19And if a wicked man turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will live because of it. 20Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord is not just.' But I will judge each of you according to your own ways, house of Israel."

21In the twelfth year of our exile, on the fifth day of the tenth month, a survivor from Jerusalem came to me and said, "ThetheThe city has been struck down." 22Now the evening before the survivor arrived, the hand of the LORD had been upon me, and HeheHe opened my mouth. So when the man came to me in the morning, my mouth was opened, and I was no longer silent.

Hearing God's Word but Not Obeying

30"As for you, son of man, your people are talking about you by the walls and in the doorways of their houses. They say to one another, 'Come and hear the word that comes from the LORD.' 31They come to you as a crowd, and they sit before you as MymyMy people. They hear your words, but they do not do them. With their mouths they express love, but their hearts chase after dishonest gain. 32Indeed, to them you are like a singer with a beautiful voice who plays an instrument well. They hear your words, but they do not put them into practice. 33When all this happens—and it surely will—then they will know that a prophet has been among them."
