Rules for the East Gate

1The Lord, the LORD, says, “The inner courtyard gate that faces east must stay closed on the six working days, but it must be opened on the Sabbath and on the day of the new moon.” 2The prince will come in through the porch of the gate from outside and stand by the gatepost. The priests will offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he will worship at the gate’s threshold. Then he will go out, but the gate will not be closed until evening. 3On the Sabbaths and new moons, the people of the land will bow down at the entrance of that gate before the LORD.

Offerings on Special Days

4The burnt offering the prince gives to the LORD on the Sabbath will be six lambs without blemish and one ram without blemish. 5With the ram he will give a grain offering of one ephah, and with the lambs he will give as much as he can, along with a hin of oil for each ephah. 6On the day of the new moon he will offer one young bull from the herd without blemish, six lambs, and one ram, all without blemish. 7He will give a grain offering of one ephah with the bull and one ephah with the ram. With the lambs he will give as much as he can, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.

8When the prince enters, he will enter through the porch of the gate, and he will go out the same way. 9When the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed times, anyone who enters through the north gate to worship will go out through the south gate, and anyone who enters through the south gate will go out through the north gate. No one will go back through the gate he used to enter; each person will go straight ahead and leave. 10When they enter, the prince will enter with them, and when they leave, he will leave with them.

11At the festivals and appointed times, the grain offering will be one ephah with a bull, one ephah with a ram, and with the lambs as much as he can give, along with a hin of oil for each ephah. 12When the prince gives a freewill offering to the LORD, whether a burnt offering or a peace offering, the east-facing gate will be opened for him. He will offer his burnt offering or peace offering just as he does on the Sabbath. Then he will go out, and after he leaves, the gate will be closed.

Daily Offerings and the Prince's Inheritance

13You are to provide a one-year-old lamb without blemish for a burnt offering to the LORD each day. Morning by morning you are to provide it. 14You are also to provide a grain offering with it each morning: one-sixth of an ephah, with one-third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour. This grain offering to the LORD is a permanent rule. 15So they will provide the lamb, the grain offering, and the oil morning by morning as a regular burnt offering.

16The Lord, the LORD, says, “If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is their inheritance. It will belong to his sons and be their property by inheritance. 17But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it will belong to that servant only until the year of freedom. Then it will return to the prince. His inheritance belongs to his sons alone. 18The prince must not take any of the people’s inheritance or force them from their land. He must give an inheritance to his sons from his own property, so that none of MymyMy people are scattered from their property.”

The Priests' Cooking Places

19Then he brought me through the entrance at the side of the gate to the north side of the holy rooms for the priests. I saw a place at the far west end of them. 20He said to me, “This is the place where the priests will boil the guilt offering and the sin offering and bake the grain offering. They do this so they will not carry them out into the outer courtyard and pass holiness on to the people.”

21Then he brought me out into the outer courtyard and led me around to the four corners of the courtyard. In every corner there was another courtyard. 22Each of the four corner courtyards was the same size: forty cubits long and thirty cubits wide. 23A row of stonework ran around the inside of all four, and hearths were built under the rows all around.

24He said to me, “These are the boiling places where the temple servants will boil the people’s sacrifices.”
