The Glory of Zion Is Gone

1How the gold has lost its shine, and the fine gold has changed! The sacred stones are scattered at every street corner. 2How the precious people of Zion, once as valuable as gold, are now treated like cheap clay jars made by a potter.

3Even jackals feed their young, but my people have become cruel, like ostriches in the desert. 4The tongue of the nursing baby sticks to the roof of its mouth from thirst. Little children beg for bread, but no one gives them any.

Hunger and Suffering in the City

5Those who once ate fine food now starve in the streets. Those raised in luxury now cling to garbage heaps. 6The punishment of my people is greater than Sodom's, which was destroyed in a moment with no one to help.

7Her leaders were purer than snow and whiter than milk. Their bodies were redder than rubies, and their form was like sapphires. 8Now their faces are blacker than soot; they are not recognizedrecogniserecognized in the streets. Their skin has shrunk over their bones and become dry as wood.

9Those killed by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger, who slowly waste away, starved for food from the fields. 10Even loving mothers have boiled their own children; they became food for them when my people were destroyed.

The Lord's Anger Against Jerusalem

11The LORD has finished HishisHis wrath; HeheHe has poured out HishisHis fierce anger. He set fire to Zion, and it burned to its foundations. 12The kings of the earth did not believe, and no one in the world believed, that an enemy or foe could enter Jerusalem's gates.

13This happened because of the sins of her prophets and the wrongs of her priests, who shed the blood of innocent people in the city. 14They wandered blind in the streets, stained with blood, so that no one could touch their clothes.

15People shouted, "Go away! You are unclean! Go away! Go away! Don't touch us!" So they fled and wandered.

Among the nations people said, "They can't stay here."

16The LORD HimselfhimselfHimself scattered them; HeheHe no longer shows them favourfavourfavor. People did not respect the priests or show kindness to the elders. 17Our eyes wore out watching for help that never came. From our towers we watched for a nation that could not save us.

18They hunted our steps, so we could not walk in our streets. Our end was near; our days were numbered, for our end had come. 19Our pursuers were faster than eagles in the sky. They chased us over the mountains and waited to ambush us in the desert.

The Anointed King and Edom's JudgmentJudgementJudgment

20The LORD's anointed, our very life, was caught in their traps. We had said, "Under his shadow we will live among the nations."

21Rejoice and be glad, daughter of Edom, you who live in the land of Uz. But the cup will pass to you too; you will get drunk and strip yourself naked.

22Your punishment is complete, daughter of Zion; HeheHe will not send you into exile again. But HeheHe will punish your sin, daughter of Edom; HeheHe will uncover your wickedness.
