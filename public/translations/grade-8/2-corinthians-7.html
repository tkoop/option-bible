Live Pure and Holy Lives

1Therefore, dear friends, since we have these promises, let us make ourselves clean from everything that makes the body or spirit dirty. Let us keep growing in holiness, with a healthy fear of God. 2Make room for us in your hearts. We have not wronged anyone. We have not ruined anyone. We have not taken advantage of anyone. 3I am not saying this to judge you. I have already said that you are in our hearts. We are ready to live or die with you. 4I speak very openly to you. I am very proud of you. I am full of comfort. Even in all our trouble, I overflow with joy.

Trouble and Comfort in Macedonia

5When we came to Macedonia, our bodies had no rest at all. We were troubled in every way. There were fights outside and fears inside. 6But God, who comforts discouraged people, comforted us when Titus came. 7He comforted us not only by coming, but also by the comfort you gave him. He told us about your strong desire to see me, your sorrow, and your strong support for me. That made me even happier.

Godly Sorrow Brings Change

8Even if my letter made you sad, I do not regret it. I did feel bad for a moment, but I see that the letter made you sad only for a short time. 9Now I am glad. I am not glad that you were sad, but I am glad that your sadness led you to repent. You were sad in the way God wants, so we did not harm you at all. 10Godly sorrow brings repentance that leads to salvation, and no one regrets it. But worldly sorrow brings death. 11Look at what this godly sorrow has done for you. It made you eager to do right. It gave you a defencedefencedefense. It made you angry at the wrong. It made you afraid. It made you long for what is right. It made you care deeply. It made you want justice. In every way you have shown that you are pure in this matter. 12So when I wrote to you, it was not only because of the person who did wrong or the person who was wronged. I wrote so that your strong care for us would be clear to you before God.

Titus's Joy in the Church

13Because of this, we have been comforted. We were even happier when we saw Titus's joy, because all of you refreshed his spirit. 14I had told Titus good things about you, and I was not embarrassed. Just as everything we said to you was true, what we told Titus about you proved true too. 15His love for you has grown even more because he remembers how all of you obeyed. You welcomed him with fear and trembling. 16I am happy because I can trust you in everything.
