Woe to Ariel

1Woe to Ariel, Ariel, the city where David camped! Keep going, year after year; let your festivals come back around. 2I will bring trouble on Ariel. There will be mourning and crying, and to MemeMe she will be like an altar hearth.

3I will camp all around you. I will set up towers and build siege works against you. 4You will be brought low. You will speak from the ground, and your words will come out muffled from the dust. Your voice will sound like a faint spirit from the ground; your speech will whisper from the dust.

The Lord Defends Zion

5But the crowd of your enemies will be like fine dust, and the crowd of cruel people like blowing chaff. Then suddenly, in an instant, 6the LORD of Hosts will punish you with thunder and earthquake and a great roar, with windstorm and tempest and flames of a consuming fire.

7All the nations fighting against Ariel—all who attack her, lay siege to her, and trouble her—will be like a dream, like a night vision. 8It will be like a hungry person who dreams of eating but wakes up still hungry, or like a thirsty person who dreams of drinking but wakes up still thirsty and weak. So it will be with all the nations that fight against Mount Zion.

Empty Worship and Hidden Plans

9Be stunned and amazed. Blind yourselves until you cannot see. They are drunk, but not with wine; they stagger, but not with strong drink. 10For the LORD has poured a spirit of deep sleep on you. He has shut your eyes—the prophets—and covered your heads—the seers.

11The whole vision has become to you like words sealed in a scroll. If it is handed to someone who can read, he will say, "I cannot read it, because it is sealed." 12Or if it is handed to someone who cannot read, he will say, "I do not know how to read a scroll."

13And the Lord said: "These people draw near to MemeMe with their mouths and honourhonourhonor MemeMe with their lips, but their hearts are far from MemeMe. Their worship of MemeMe is only rules taught by men." 14Therefore I will again do amazing things for these people—wonder after wonder. The wisdom of the wise will disappear, and the understanding of the clever will be hidden.

15Woe to those who go to great depths to hide their plans from the LORD, who do their work in darkness and say, "Who sees us? Who knows what we do?" 16You turn things upside down! Shall the potter be treated like the clay? Shall what is formed say to the one who formed it, "he did not make me"? Can the pottery say of the potter, "he has no understanding"?

Hope for God's People

17In just a little while, won't Lebanon become a fertile field, and won't that fertile field look like a forest? 18In that day the deaf will hear the words of the scroll, and out of gloom and darkness the eyes of the blind will see.

19The humble will increase their joy in the LORD, and the poor will rejoice in the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 20For the ruthless will vanish, the mockers will disappear, and all who look for evil will be cut off.

21They trap people with a word, set snares for those who argue for justice at the gate, and push aside the innocent with empty charges.

They will set apart the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Jacob as holy, and they will stand in awe of the God of Israel. 24Then those who wander in spirit will gain understanding, and those who grumble will accept instruction."
