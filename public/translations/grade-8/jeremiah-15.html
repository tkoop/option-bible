The LORD Refuses to Spare Judah

1The LORD said to me, "Even if Moses and Samuel stood before MemeMe, MymyMy heart would not turn toward this people. Send them away from MemeMe, and let them go. 2When they ask you, 'Where should we go?' you must answer, 'This is what the LORD says: Those meant for death will die; those meant for the sword will be killed by the sword; those meant for famine will starve; and those meant for captivity will be taken captive.' 3I will punish them with four kinds of disaster," declares the LORD. "The sword will kill, the dogs will drag them away, and the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth will eat and destroy. 4I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, did in Jerusalem."

"5Who will have pity on you, Jerusalem? Who will mourn for you? Who will turn aside to ask how you are? 6You have rejected MemeMe," declares the LORD. "You keep going backward. So I will stretch out MymyMy hand against you and destroy you. I am tired of showing mercy. 7I will winnow them with a winnowing fork at the gates of the land. I will take their children from them; I will destroy MymyMy people, because they did not turn from their ways. 8Their widows will be more than the sand of the sea. At noon I will send a destroyer against them. I will suddenly bring anguish and terror upon the mother of a young man. 9The mother of seven will grow faint and breathe her last. Her sun will set while it is still day; she will be ashamed and disgraced. I will give the rest of them to the sword before their enemies," declares the LORD.

Jeremiah's Complaint

10Woe to me, my mother, because you gave birth to me, a man of strife and conflict to the whole land! I have not lent money, and no one has lent money to me, yet everyone curses me.

11The LORD said, "Surely I will set you free for a good purpose. Surely I will make your enemies plead with you in times of disaster and times of distress. 12Can iron break iron from the north, or bronze? 13I will give your wealth and your treasures as plunder, without cost, for all your sins throughout your territory. 14I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know, for MymyMy anger has kindled a fire that will burn against you."

15You know, O LORD. Remember me and care for me. Take revenge for me on my persecutors. Because YouyouYou are slow to anger, do not take me away. Know that I suffer insult for YouryourYour sake. 16Your words were found, and I ate them. Your words became my joy and the delight of my heart, because I bear YouryourYour name, O LORD God of Hosts. 17I did not sit with partygoers, and I did not rejoice. Because of YouryourYour hand, I sat alone, for YouyouYou filled me with indignation. 18Why is my pain endless and my wound incurable, refusing to heal? Will you be to me like a deceitful brook, like waters that fail?

The LORD's Promise to Jeremiah

19The LORD said, "If you return, I will restore you, and you will stand before MemeMe. If you bring out the precious from the worthless, you will be like MymyMy mouth. They will turn to you, but you will not turn to them. 20I will make you a fortified wall of bronze to this people. They will fight against you, but they will not overcome you, for I am with you to save you and rescue you," declares the LORD. "I will deliver you from the hand of the wicked and redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless."
