Elihu Urges Job to Listen

1Elihu also continued and said:

2“Give me a little time, and I will show you. I still have more to say for God. 3I will bring knowledge from far away and show that my MakermakerMaker is right. 4My words are truly not false. Someone who is fully wise is with you.

God’s Power and Justice

5“Look, God is powerful, but HeheHe does not look down on anyone. He is powerful in understanding. 6He does not keep wicked people alive, but HeheHe gives justice to those who are hurting. 7He does not take HishisHis eyes off the people who do right. He sets them on thrones with kings and lifts them up for all time.

8“If they are bound in chains and caught in ropes of trouble, 9HeheHe tells them what they did and how they sinned. He shows that they acted proudly. 10He opens their ears to teaching and tells them to turn away from sin. 11If they listen and serve HimhimHim, they will live out their days in good times and their years in pleasure. 12But if they do not listen, they will die by the sword and die without understanding.

Elihu Warns Job

13“People with godless hearts hold on to anger. They do not cry for help when God binds them. 14They die while young, and their lives end among male temple prostitutes. 15But God saves suffering people through their suffering and opens their ears while they are in trouble. 16He drew you out of trouble into a wide, open place, and your table was full of good food.

17“But you are full of the judgmentjudgementjudgment meant for wicked people. JudgmentJudgementJudgment and justice have caught you. 18Be careful that anger does not trick you into mocking. Do not let a large payment turn you away. 19Will your cry for help save you from trouble, or will all your strength? 20Do not long for the night, when people disappear from their place. 21Be careful. Do not turn to sin, for you chose this instead of suffering.

God’s Greatness in the Storm

22“Look, God is lifted up by HishisHis power. Who is a teacher like HimhimHim? 23Who has told HimhimHim how to live, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? 24Remember to praise HishisHis work, which people have praised. 25All people have seen it. People look at it from far away. 26Look, God is great, and we do not know HimhimHim. The number of HishisHis years cannot be found out.

27“He lifts up the drops of water, and they turn into rain from HishisHis mist. 28The clouds pour it down and shower it heavily on people. 29Can anyone understand how HeheHe spreads out the clouds or the thunder from HishisHis tent? 30Look, HeheHe spreads HishisHis lightning all around HimhimHim and covers the depths of the sea. 31He uses these things to judge people and to give plenty of food. 32He fills HishisHis hands with lightning and tells it to hit the target. 33His thunder announces HimhimHim, and even the cattle sense the storm coming.”
