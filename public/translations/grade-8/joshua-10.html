The Amorite Kings Attack Gibeon

1Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, heard that Joshua had captured Ai and destroyed it. He had done to Ai and its king what he had done to Jericho and its king. Adoni-Zedek also heard that the people of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and now lived among them. 2He was very afraid, because Gibeon was a large city, like one of the royal cities. It was larger than Ai, and all its men were warriors. 3So Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, sent this message to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish, and Debir king of Eglon: 4"Come up to me and help me. Let us attack Gibeon, because it has made peace with Joshua and the Israelites." 5The five Amorite kings—the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon—joined forces. They marched up with all their armies, camped around Gibeon, and attacked it.

The LORD Helps Joshua Win

6The men of Gibeon sent a message to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal: "Do not abandon your servants. Come quickly and save us and help us, for all the Amorite kings who live in the hill country have gathered against us." 7So Joshua and all his fighting men, including all the mighty warriors, marched up from Gilgal. 8The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, for I have given them into your hand. Not one of them will stand before you." 9Joshua marched all night from Gilgal and came upon them suddenly. 10The LORD threw them into confusion before Israel. Joshua struck them with a great defeat at Gibeon and chased them along the road to Beth Horon. He struck them down all the way to Azekah and Makkedah. 11As they fled from Israel down the slope of Beth Horon, the LORD hurled large hailstones from the sky on them all the way to Azekah. More died from the hailstones than from the swords of the Israelites.

The Sun Stands Still

12On the day the LORD gave the Amorites over to Israel, Joshua spoke to the LORD while Israel watched. He said, "Sun, stand still over Gibeon, and moon, over the ValleyvalleyValley of Aijalon." 13The sun stood still, and the moon stopped, until the nation took revenge on its enemies. Is this not written in the BookbookBook of Jashar? The sun stopped in the middle of the sky and did not hurry to set for about a full day. 14There has never been a day like it before or since, when the LORD listened to a man's voice, because the LORD fought for Israel. 15Then Joshua and all Israel returned to the camp at Gilgal.

The Five Kings Are Put to Death

16The five kings fled and hid in a cave at Makkedah. 17Joshua was told, "ThetheThe five kings have been found hiding in a cave at Makkedah." 18Joshua said, "Roll large stones to the mouth of the cave and post men there to guard them." 19"But do not stay there. Pursue your enemies and attack them from behind. Do not let them reach their cities, for the LORD your God has given them into your hand." 20After Joshua and the Israelites finished striking them down with a terrible defeat, the survivors fled to fortified cities. 21The whole army returned safely to Joshua at the camp at Makkedah. Not one person spoke against the Israelites. 22Joshua said, "Open the mouth of the cave and bring those five kings out to me." 23They did so and brought out the five kings—the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon. 24When they brought them out to Joshua, he called all the men of Israel and said to the commanders who had gone with him, "Come near and put your feet on the necks of these kings." They came near and put their feet on the kings' necks. 25Joshua said to them, "Do not be afraid or discouraged. Be strong and courageous, for the LORD will do this to all your enemies that you fight." 26Then Joshua struck them and killed them. He hung them on five trees, and they hung there until evening. 27At sunset Joshua commanded that they be taken down from the trees and thrown into the cave where they had hidden. Large stones were placed at the mouth of the cave, and they are still there today.

Joshua Conquers the South

28That same day Joshua captured Makkedah. He struck it and its king with the sword and devoted to destruction every person in it. He left no survivor. He did to Makkedah's king what he had done to Jericho's king. 29Joshua and all Israel moved on from Makkedah to Libnah and fought against it. 30The LORD gave it and its king into Israel's hand. Joshua struck it and everyone in it with the sword and left no survivor. He did to its king what he had done to Jericho's king. 31Joshua and all Israel moved on from Libnah to Lachish, camped around it, and fought against it. 32The LORD gave Lachish into Israel's hand on the second day. Joshua struck it and everyone in it with the sword, just as he had done to Libnah. 33Horam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish, but Joshua struck him and his people until no one was left. 34Joshua and all Israel moved on from Lachish to Eglon, camped around it, and fought against it. 35They captured it that day and struck it with the sword. Joshua devoted to destruction every person in it that day, just as he had done to Lachish. 36Joshua and all Israel went up from Eglon to Hebron and fought against it. 37They captured it and struck its king, all its towns, and everyone in it with the sword. Joshua left no survivor, just as he had done at Eglon. He devoted the city and everyone in it to destruction. 38Joshua and all Israel turned back to Debir and fought against it. 39They captured it, its king, and all its towns. They struck them with the sword and devoted to destruction every person in it. Joshua left no survivor. He did to Debir and its king as he had done to Hebron, and he did the same to Libnah and its king. 40So Joshua struck the whole land—the hill country, the Negev, the western foothills, and the slopes. He struck all its kings and left no survivor. He devoted to destruction everything that breathed, just as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded. 41Joshua struck them from Kadesh Barnea to Gaza and all the land of Goshen as far as Gibeon. 42Joshua captured all these kings and their land at one time, because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel. 43Then Joshua and all Israel returned to the camp at Gilgal.
