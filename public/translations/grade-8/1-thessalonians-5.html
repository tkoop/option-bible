The Day of the Lord Will Come Like a Thief

1Now, brothers, I do not need to write to you about the times and seasons. 2You know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night. 3When people say, "Peace and safety," then sudden destruction will come on them, like labourlabourlabor pains on a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.

Living in the Light

4But you, brothers, are not in darkness, so that day should not surprise you like a thief. 5You are all children of light and children of the day. We are not children of the night or of darkness.

6So let us not sleep like the others do. Instead, let us stay awake and be clear-minded. 7People who sleep, sleep at night, and people who get drunk, get drunk at night. 8But since we belong to the day, let us be clear-minded. Let us put on faith and love like a breastplate, and hope of salvation like a helmet.

9For God did not choose us to face HishisHis anger. He chose us to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ. 10He died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with HimhimHim. 11So encourage each other and build each other up, just as you are already doing.

Final Instructions for the Church

12Brothers, we ask you to respect those who work hard among you, who lead you in the Lord, and who warn you. 13Hold them in the highest respect and love because of their work. Live in peace with each other.

14We urge you, brothers, to warn those who are out of line, comfort those who are discouraged, help the weak, and be patient with everyone. 15Make sure no one pays back evil for evil. Instead, always try to do good for each other and for everyone.

16Rejoice always. 17Pray without stopping. 18Give thanks in everything, because this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.

19Do not put out the Spirit's fire. 20Do not look down on prophecies. 21Test everything, and hold on to what is good. 22Stay away from every kind of evil.

Closing Prayer and Greeting

23May the God of peace make you completely holy. May your whole spirit, soul, and body be kept blameless until our Lord Jesus Christ comes. 24He who calls you is faithful, and HeheHe will do it.

25Brothers, pray for us. 26Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss. 27I ask you to promise by the Lord that this letter will be read to all the brothers. 28The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
