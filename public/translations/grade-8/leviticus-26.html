God's Commands for Worship

1Do not make useless idols for yourselves. Do not set up a carved image or a sacred stone for yourselves. Do not put a carved stone in your land to bow down to it. I am the LORD your God. 2Keep MymyMy Sabbaths and respect MymyMy sanctuary. I am the LORD.

Blessings for Obeying God

3If you walk in MymyMy laws and keep MymyMy commands and do them, 4then I will give you rain at the right time. The land will give its crops, and the trees in the field will give their fruit. 5Your threshing will last until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will last until planting time. You will eat your bread until you are full, and you will live safely in your land. 6I will give peace in the land. You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid. I will remove wild animals from the land, and no sword will pass through your land. 7You will chase your enemies, and they will fall by the sword before you. 8Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand. Your enemies will fall by the sword before you. 9I will turn to you and make you fruitful and multiply you. I will keep MymyMy agreement with you. 10You will eat old grain and clear out the old to make room for the new. 11I will put MymyMy dwelling place among you, and I will not reject you. 12I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be MymyMy people. 13I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt so you would not be slaves. I broke the bars of your yoke and led you to walk proudly.

Warnings for Disobeying God

14But if you will not listen to MemeMe and do not obey all these commands, 15and if you reject MymyMy laws and hate them in your hearts, so you do not obey all MymyMy commands, you break MymyMy agreement. 16Then I will do this to you: I will bring sudden terror on you—wasting disease and fever that harm your eyes and drain your life. You will plant seed for nothing, because your enemies will eat it. 17I will turn against you, and your enemies will defeat you. Those who hate you will rule over you. You will run even when no one is chasing you. 18If after all this you still will not listen to MemeMe, I will punish you seven times more for your sins. 19I will break your stubborn pride. I will make your sky like iron and your ground like bronze. 20Your strength will be used up for nothing. Your land will not give its crops, and the trees in the land will not give their fruit. 21If you keep walking against MemeMe and will not listen to MemeMe, I will add seven more blows for your sins. 22I will send wild animals against you. They will rob you of your children, kill your animals, and make your roads empty, so that few of you are left. 23If even then you are not corrected by MemeMe but keep walking against MemeMe, 24then I will also walk against you in anger. I will strike you seven times for your sins. 25I will bring a sword against you that punishes you for breaking MymyMy agreement. You will gather in your cities, but I will send disease among you, and you will be handed over to your enemy. 26When I cut off your bread supply, ten women will bake bread in one oven. They will weigh out your bread, and you will eat but not be full. 27If after this you still will not listen to MemeMe but keep walking against MemeMe, 28then I will walk against you in MymyMy burning anger. I will punish you seven times for your sins. 29You will eat the flesh of your sons and the flesh of your daughters. 30I will destroy your hilltop shrines and cut down your sun idols. I will pile your dead bodies on the dead bodies of your idols, and I will reject you. 31I will turn your cities into ruins and destroy your holy places. I will not accept your pleasing aroma. 32I will destroy the land so much that your enemies who live there will be shocked by it. 33I will scatter you among the nations and chase you with a sword. Your land will be empty, and your cities will be ruins. 34Then the land will enjoy its Sabbath rest all the time it lies empty, while you are in your enemies' land. Then the land will rest and enjoy its Sabbaths. 35It will rest the whole time it is empty, the rest it did not get during your Sabbaths when you lived there. 36Those of you who are left, I will make so afraid in your enemies' lands that the sound of a blowing leaf will chase them. They will run as if running from a sword and fall, even though no one is chasing them. 37They will stumble over each other as if running from a sword, though no one is chasing them. You will have no strength to stand before your enemies. 38You will be consumed in your enemies' land. 39Those left among you will waste away in your enemies' lands because of their sins and their fathers' sins.

Hope After Exile

40But if they confess their sin and the sin of their fathers—the unfaithfulness they showed against MemeMe, and how they walked against MemeMe— 41then I will also walk against them in anger and bring them into their enemies' land. But if their stubborn hearts become humble and they accept the punishment for their sin, 42then I will remember MymyMy agreement with Jacob, and I will remember MymyMy agreement with Isaac, and I will remember MymyMy agreement with Abraham. I will remember the land. 43The land will be left by them and will enjoy its Sabbath rest while it lies empty without them. They will accept the punishment for their sin, because they rejected MymyMy laws and hated MymyMy rules in their hearts. 44Yet even then, while they are in their enemies' land, I will not reject them or hate them so much that I destroy them and break MymyMy agreement with them. I am the LORD their God. 45For their sake I will remember the first agreement I made when I brought them out of Egypt in front of the nations, to be their God. I am the LORD. 46These are the laws, rules, and teachings that the LORD gave between HimselfhimselfHimself and the Israelites through Moses on Mount Sinai.
