Offerings for the Festival of Trumpets

1On the first day of the seventh month, you must hold a sacred gathering. You must not do any regular work. It is a day for you to sound the trumpets. 2Offer a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 3Their grain offering must be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 4and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 5Also offer one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you. 6These are in addition to the monthly burnt offering and its grain offering, and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings. They are a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.

Offerings for the Day of Atonement

7On the tenth day of this seventh month, you must hold a sacred gathering and humble yourselves. You must not do any work. 8Offer a burnt offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 9Their grain offering must be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for the bull, two-tenths for the ram, 10and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. 11Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the sin offering for atonement and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings.

Offerings for the Feast of Tabernacles

12On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you must hold a sacred gathering. You must not do any regular work, and you must keep a feast to the LORD for seven days. 13Offer a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 14Their grain offering must be fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths for each of the two rams, 15and one-tenth for each of the fourteen lambs. 16Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

17On the second day, offer twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 18Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 19Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings. 20On the third day, offer eleven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 21Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 22Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering. 23On the fourth day, offer ten young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 24Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 25Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

26On the fifth day, offer nine young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 27Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 28Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering. 29On the sixth day, offer eight young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 30Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 31Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings. 32On the seventh day, offer seven young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 33Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 34Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

Offerings for the Eighth Day

35On the eighth day, you must hold a solemn gathering. You must not do any regular work. 36Offer a burnt offering, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to the LORD: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs one year old, all without a flaw. 37Their grain and drink offerings must match the number of animals, as required. 38Also offer one male goat as a sin offering. This is besides the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.

39You must offer these to the LORD at your appointed feasts. This is in addition to your vow offerings and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings, and peace offerings. 40Moses told the people of Israel everything, just as the LORD had commanded him.
