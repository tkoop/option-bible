The Parable of the Tenants

9He began to tell the people this parable: "A man planted a vineyard and rented it to some farmers. Then he went away for a long time. 10At harvest time he sent a servant to the farmers to collect some of the fruit from the vineyard, but they beat him and sent him away with nothing. 11He sent another servant, but they beat him too, treated him badly, and sent him away with nothing. 12He sent a third, and they wounded him and threw him out.

13"Then the owner of the vineyard said, 'What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. Maybe they will respect him.'

14"But when the farmers saw him, they said to one another, 'This is the heir. Let's kill him, so the inheritance will be ours.'

15"So they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. 16What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them? He will come and kill those farmers and give the vineyard to others."
