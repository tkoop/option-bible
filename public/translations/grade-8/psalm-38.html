A Prayer for Help in Suffering

1The LORD, do not rebuke me in YouryourYour anger or discipline me in YouryourYour wrath. 2For YouryourYour arrows have pierced me, and YouryourYour hand has come down on me. 3There is no health in my body because of YouryourYour wrath; there is no peace in my bones because of my sin. 4My guilt has overwhelmed me like a heavy burden too heavy to bear. 5My wounds stink and fester because of my foolishness.

6I am bent over and brought very low; all day long I go about mourning. 7My back is filled with searing pain; there is no health in my body. 8I am feeble and utterly crushed; I groan in anguish of heart. 9Lord, all my longing is before YouyouYou; my sighing is not hidden from YouyouYou. 10My heart pounds, my strength fails me; even the light of my eyes is gone from me.

11My friends and companions keep their distance from my suffering; my relatives stand far off. 12Those who seek my life set traps; those who want my ruin speak of destruction and plan deceit all day long. 13I am like a deaf man, who cannot hear, like a mute, who cannot speak. 14I have become like one who does not hear, whose mouth can offer no reply.

15The LORD, I wait for YouyouYou; YouyouYou will answer, Lord my God. 16For I said, "May they not rejoice over me—those who gloat when my foot slips." 17For I am about to fall, and my pain is ever before me. 18I confess my wrongdoing; I am troubled by my sin.

19But my enemies are vigorous and strong; many are those who hate me without reason. 20Those who repay good with evil oppose me because I follow what is good. 21Do not forsake me, the LORD; do not be far from me, my God. 22Hurry to help me, Lord, my salvation.
