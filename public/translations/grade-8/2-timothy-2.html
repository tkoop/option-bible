Be Strong and Serve Well

1You then, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. 2And what you heard from me among many witnesses, entrust to faithful men who will be able to teach others. 3Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 4No one serving as a soldier gets tangled up in everyday business, because he wants to please his commanding officer. 5Also, if anyone competes, he is not crowned unless he competes by the rules. 6The hardworking farmer should be the first to share in the crops. 7Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.

Remember Jesus Christ

8Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, a descendant of David, as my gospel says. 9I suffer for it to the point of being chained like a criminal, but the word of God is not chained. 10So I endure everything for the sake of the chosen people, so that they too may receive the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory. 11This is a trustworthy saying: If we died with HimhimHim, we will also live with HimhimHim. 12If we endure, we will also reign with HimhimHim. Avoid Useless Arguments

That does no good and only ruins those who listen. 15Make every effort to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, who rightly handles the word of truth. 16But avoid godless chatter, because it will lead to more ungodliness. 17Their message will spread like gangrene. Pursue Righteousness and Be Gentle

22Flee from youthful passions, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart. 23Reject foolish and ignorant arguments, knowing that they lead to fights. 24The Lord's servant must not be quarrelsome. Instead, he should be kind to everyone, able to teach, and patient. 25He should gently correct those who oppose him, hoping that God may grant them repentance that leads to knowing the truth. 26Then they may come to their senses and escape the trap of the devil, who has captured them to do his will.
