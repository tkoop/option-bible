Amon's Reign and Death

19Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. He served the idols his father had served and worshiped them. 22He abandoned the LORD, the God of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of the LORD. 23Amon's servants plotted against him and killed the king in his own house. 24Then the people of the land struck down everyone who had plotted against King Amon, and they made his son Josiah king in his place. 25The rest of Amon's acts are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. 26He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and his son Josiah became king in his place.
