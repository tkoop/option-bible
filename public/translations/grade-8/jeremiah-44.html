The People Refuse to Stop Worshiping the Queen of Heaven

15Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense to other gods, along with all the women standing there—a great crowd—and all the people living in Egypt in Pathros, answered Jeremiah:

16"We will not listen to the message you spoke to us in the name of the LORD. 17Instead, we will do everything we said we would do. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, just as we, our ancestors, our kings, and our officials did in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Back then we had plenty of food. We were well off, and we did not see disaster.

18"But since we stopped burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything. We have been destroyed by sword and hunger. 19When we burned incense to the Queen of Heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, we did not do it behind our husbands' backs. We made cakes for her and poured out offerings to her."

Jeremiah Answers the People

20Jeremiah spoke to all the people, both men and women, who had answered him:

21"The incense you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem—you, your ancestors, your kings, your officials, and the people of the land—the LORD remembered it. He thought about it. 22The LORD could no longer put up with it because of your evil deeds and the terrible things you did. So your land became a ruin, a horror, and a curse, with no one living there, as it is today.

23"This happened because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD. You did not listen to the LORD's voice. You did not walk in HishisHis law, HishisHis rules, or HishisHis agreement. So this disaster has come on you, as it is today."
