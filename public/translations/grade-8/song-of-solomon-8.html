The Bride's Longing for Her Beloved

1"If only you were my brother, nursed at my mother's breast! Then I could kiss you in public, and no one would look down on me. 2I would lead you and bring you to my mother's house, where she taught me. I would give you spiced wine to drink, the juice from my pomegranates. 3His left hand is under my head, and his right hand embraces me."

4"Daughters of Jerusalem, I make you promise: don't stir up love or wake it until it's ready."

A Procession from the Wilderness

5"Who is this coming up from the wilderness, leaning on her beloved?"

"Under the apple tree I awakened you. There your mother conceived you; there the woman who bore you was in labourlabourlabor."

The Power of Love

6"Put me like a seal on your heart, like a seal on your arm. Love is as strong as death, and jealousy is as stubborn as the grave. Its flashes are fiery flashes, the very flame of the LORD. 7Many waters can't put out love, and rivers can't wash it away. If a man gave all the wealth of his house for love, he would be completely scorned."

The Little Sister and the Final Call

8"We have a little sister, and her breasts aren't grown yet. What should we do for our sister on the day someone asks to marry her? 9If she is a wall, we'll build a silver tower on her. If she is a door, we'll cover her with cedar boards."

10"I am a wall, and my breasts are like towers. Then in his eyes I was like someone who brings peace."

11"Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon. He rented it to workers, and each one was to bring a thousand pieces of silver for its fruit. 12My own vineyard is right here before me. Solomon, you can have the thousand pieces, and two hundred go to those who tend the fruit."

13"You who live in the gardens, my friends are listening for your voice. Let me hear it."

14"Hurry, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young deer on the mountains of spices."
