Ezra Weeps Before the Temple

1While Ezra was praying and confessing, weeping and throwing himself down before the house of God, a very large crowd of Israelites—men, women, and children—gathered around him. The people wept bitterly.

Shecaniah's Plan

2Then Shecaniah son of Jehiel, one of the descendants of Elam, spoke up to Ezra: "We have been unfaithful to our God. We have married foreign women from the peoples of the land. But even now there is hope for Israel in this. 3So now let us make a covenant with our God to send away all these women and their children, following the advice of my lord and of those who fear the command of our God. Let it be done according to the LawlawLaw. 4Get up, because this matter is your responsibility. We are with you. Be strong and do it."

5Then Ezra rose and made the leading priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear an oath to do this. They swore.

6Then Ezra got up from in front of the house of God and went to the room of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. He did not eat bread or drink water there, because he was mourning over the unfaithfulness of the exiles.

The Exiles Gather in Jerusalem

7A proclamation was sent throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the exiles to gather in Jerusalem. 8Anyone who did not come within three days would have all his property taken away and would be removed from the assembly of the exiles.

9So within three days all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered in Jerusalem. It was the twentieth day of the ninth month. All the people sat in the square in front of the house of God, shaking with fear because of this matter and because of the heavy rain.

10Then Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, "YouyouYou have been unfaithful. You have married foreign women and added to Israel's guilt. 11Now confess to the LORD, the God of your fathers, and do HishisHis will. Separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from your foreign wives."

12The whole assembly answered in a loud voice, "We will do exactly as you say."

13But the people said, "It is the rainy season. We cannot stand outside, and this is not a one- or two-day job, because many of us have sinned in this matter. 14Let our leaders stand for the whole assembly. Let everyone in our towns who has married a foreign woman come at the set times, along with the elders and judges of each town, until the fierce anger of our God over this matter is turned away."

15Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah opposed this plan. Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supported them.

16So the exiles did this. Ezra the priest chose men who were heads of families, all listed by name. They sat down on the tenth day of the tenth month to look into the matter. 17They finished dealing with all the men who had married foreign women by the first day of the first month.

The Men Who Had Married Foreign Women

18Among the priests, men were found who had married foreign women: from the descendants of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah.

19They promised to send their wives away, and each man brought a ram from the flock as a guilt offering for his sin.

20From the descendants of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah. 21From the descendants of Harim: Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah. 22From the descendants of Pashhur: Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah. 23From the Levites: Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah (that is, Kelita), Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer. 24From the singers: Eliashib. From the gatekeepers: Shallum, Telem, and Uri. 25From the people of Israel: from the descendants of Parosh: Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eleazar, Malchijah, and Benaiah. 26From the descendants of Elam: Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah. 27From the descendants of Zattu: Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza. 28From the descendants of Bebai: Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai. 29From the descendants of Bani: Meshullam, Malluch, Adaiah, Jashub, Sheal, and Jeremoth. 30From the descendants of Pahath-moab: Adna, Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Binnui, and Manasseh. 31From the descendants of Harim: Eliezer, Isshijah, Malchijah, Shemaiah, and Shimeon. 32From the descendants of Benjamin: Malluch and Shemariah. 33From the descendants of Hashum: Mattenai, Mattattah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, and Shimei. 34From the descendants of Bani: Maadai, Amram, and Uel. 35Benaiah, Bedeiah, and Chelluh. 36Vaniah, Meremoth, Eliashib, 37Mattaniah, Mattenai, and Jaasau. 38Bani, Binnui, and Shimei. 39Shelemiah, Nathan, and Adaiah. 40Machnadebai, Shashai, and Sharai. 41Azarel, Shelemiah, and Shemariah. 42Shallum, Amariah, and Joseph. 43From the descendants of Nebo: Jeiel, Mattithiah, Zabad, Zebina, Jaddai, Joel, and Benaiah. 44All these had married foreign women, and some of them had children by these wives.
