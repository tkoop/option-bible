Solomon Begins Building the Temple

1In the four hundred eightieth year after the Israelites came out of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv, the second month, he began to build the house of the the LORD. 2The house that King Solomon built for the the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. 3The porch in front of the main hall was twenty cubits long, across the width of the house, and ten cubits deep in front of the house. 4He made narrow windows for the house.

5Against the walls of the main hall and the inner sanctuary, he built a structure all around the house, and he made side rooms all around. 6The lowest floor was five cubits wide, the middle floor six cubits, and the third floor seven cubits. He made offset ledges around the outside of the house so that the beams would not be inserted into the walls of the house. 7The house was built with stone finished at the quarry, so no hammer, chisel, or any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.

8The entrance to the middle floor was on the right side of the house. They went up by winding stairs to the middle floor, and from the middle to the third. 9So he built the house and finished it, and he covered the house with beams and boards of cedar. 10He built the side rooms against the whole house, each five cubits high, and attached them to the house with cedar beams.

The LORD's Promise to Solomon

The Temple's Interior

14So Solomon built the house and finished it. 15He lined the walls of the house with cedar from floor to ceiling, and he overlaid the floor with cypress. 16He partitioned off twenty cubits at the rear of the house with cedar boards from floor to ceiling to form an inner sanctuary, the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. 17The main hall in front of this room was forty cubits long. 18The cedar inside the house was carved with gourds and open flowers. Everything was cedar; no stone was visible.

19He prepared the inner sanctuary inside the house to set the ark of the covenant of the the LORD there. 20The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high. He overlaid it with pure gold, and he also overlaid the cedar altar. 21So Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold, and he stretched gold chains across the front of the inner sanctuary and overlaid it with gold. 22He overlaid the whole house with gold, until it was completely finished. He also overlaid the entire altar in the inner sanctuary with gold.

23In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits high. 24One wing of the first cherub was five cubits, and the other wing five cubits, so the wingspan was ten cubits from tip to tip. 25The second cherub also measured ten cubits; both cherubim had the same size and shape. 26The height of one cherub was ten cubits, and so was the other. 27He placed the cherubim inside the inner sanctuary. Their wings were spread out so that the wing of one touched one wall, and the wing of the other touched the opposite wall; their other wings touched in the middle of the house. 28He also overlaid the cherubim with gold.

29He carved all the walls of the house, both inner and outer, with carved figures of cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. 30He overlaid the floors of the house, both inner and outer, with gold. 31For the entrance to the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood, with five-sided doorposts. 32The two doors were made of olive wood, and he carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold, spreading the gold evenly over the carved work. 33In the same way he made doorposts of olive wood for the entrance to the main hall, each one-fourth of the wall. 34He made two doors of cypress wood, each with two folding panels. 35He carved on them cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers, and overlaid them with gold, fitted evenly over the carved work.

The Temple Is Completed

36He built the inner courtyard with three rows of cut stone and one row of cedar beams. 37The foundation of the house of the the LORD was laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv. 38In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, the eighth month, the house was finished in every detail, according to all its specifications. So he built it in seven years.
