David Hears of Saul's Death

1After Saul died, David returned from defeating the Amalekites and stayed in Ziklag for two days. 2On the third day, a man came from Saul's camp. His clothes were torn, and dirt was on his head. When he reached David, he fell to the ground and bowed down. 3David asked him, "Where do you come from?"

He answered, "I have escaped from the camp of Israel."

4David said, "What happened? Tell me."

He replied, "ThetheThe people ran from the battle. Many fell and died. Saul and his son Jonathan are dead too."

5David asked the young man who brought the news, "How do you know Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?"

6The young man said, "I happened to be on Mount Gilboa. Saul was leaning on his spear. The chariots and horsemen were close behind him. 7He turned and saw me, and he called to me. I answered, 'Here I am.' 8HeheHe asked, 'Who are you?' I answered, 'I am an Amalekite.' 9HeheHe said, 'Stand beside me and kill me. I am in great pain, but I am still alive.' 10So I stood beside him and killed him. I knew he could not live after he fell. I took the crown from his head and the bracelet from his arm, and I brought them to my lord."

11David grabbed his clothes and tore them. All the men with him did the same. 12They mourned, wept, and fasted until evening for Saul, for his son Jonathan, for the people of the LORD, and for the nation of Israel. They had fallen by the sword.

David Judges the Amalekite

13David asked the young man who brought the news, "Where are you from?"

He answered, "I am the son of a foreigner, an Amalekite."

14David said to him, "Why were you not afraid to raise your hand to destroy the LORD's anointed?"

15Then David called one of his young men and said, "Go and strike him down." The man struck the Amalekite, and he died.

16David said to him, "YouyouYou are to blame for your own death. Your own mouth spoke against you when you said, 'I killed the LORD's anointed.'"

David's Lament for Saul and Jonathan

17David sang this song of sadness for Saul and his son Jonathan. 18He said it should be taught to the people of Judah. It is written in the BookbookBook of Jashar. He said:

"19Israel's glory has fallen on your heights. How the mighty have fallen!

"20Do not tell it in Gath. Do not announce it in the streets of Ashkelon. The daughters of the Philistines might rejoice. The daughters of the uncircumcised might celebrate.

"21You mountains of Gilboa, may no dew or rain fall on you. May your fields give no harvest. For there the shield of the mighty was disgraced. It was Saul's shield, left uncared for.

"22Jonathan's bow did not turn back from the blood of the fallen. Saul's sword did not return empty.

"23Saul and Jonathan were loved and lovely in life. In death they were not parted. They were faster than eagles and stronger than lions.

"24You daughters of Israel, weep for Saul. He clothed you in rich scarlet. He put gold ornaments on your clothes.

"25How the mighty have fallen in the middle of battle! Jonathan lies slain on your heights.

"26I am deeply troubled for you, my brother Jonathan. You were very kind to me. Your love for me was more wonderful than the love of women.

"27How the mighty have fallen, and the weapons of war have perished!"
