Solomon Establishes His Kingdom

1Solomon son of David grew strong in his kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and lifted him up greatly. 2Solomon spoke to all Israel—to the commanders of thousands and hundreds, the judges, and every leader in Israel, the heads of the families.

Solomon Worships at Gibeon

3Solomon and the whole assembly went to the high place at Gibeon, because God’s tent of meeting was there. Moses, the LORD’s servant, had made it in the wilderness. 4David had already brought God’s ark from Kiriath-jearim to the place he had prepared for it, because he had set up a tent for it in Jerusalem. 5The bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, had made stood there in front of the LORD’s tabernacle. Solomon and the assembly sought the LORD there. 6Solomon went up to the bronze altar before the LORD at the tent of meeting and offered a thousand burnt offerings on it.

God Gives Solomon Wisdom

Solomon’s Wealth and Power

13Solomon came from the high place at Gibeon, from the tent of meeting, to Jerusalem, and he reigned over Israel. 14Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen. He stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem. 15The king made silver and gold as common as stones in Jerusalem, and cedar as plentiful as sycamore trees in the lowlands. 16Solomon’s horses were brought from Egypt and from Kue. The king’s merchants bought them from Kue for a set price. 17They brought a chariot out of Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver and a horse for one hundred fifty. They also brought them out for all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.
