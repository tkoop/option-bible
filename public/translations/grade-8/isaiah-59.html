The People's Sins Separate Them from God

1Look, the LORD's hand is not too short to save, and HishisHis ear is not too dull to hear. 2But your wrongs have separated you from your God. Your sins have hidden HishisHis face from you, so HeheHe won't listen. 3Your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with sin. Your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue mutters evil. 4No one calls for justice, and no one pleads for truth. They trust in empty things and speak lies. They plan trouble and give birth to sin.

5They hatch viper eggs and weave spiderwebs. Their plans are deadly and useless—anyone who gets involved is harmed, and even a crushed plan still produces poison. 6Their webs won't become clothes, and they won't cover themselves with their deeds. Their deeds are sinful, and violence is in their hands. 7Their feet run to do evil, and they hurry to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are sinful; ruin and destruction are in their paths. 8They don't know the way of peace, and there is no justice in their paths. They've made their roads crooked, and no one who walks them knows peace.

9So justice is far from us, and righteousness doesn't reach us. We hope for light, but see darkness; we look for brightness, but walk in gloom. 10We feel along the wall like blind people, and we stumble as if we had no eyes. At noon we stumble as if it were night, and among the strong we are like the dead. 11We all growl like bears and moan like doves. We look for justice, but there is none; for salvation, but it is far from us.

12For our rebellions are many before YouyouYou, and our sins testify against us. Our rebellions are with us, and we know our wrongs. 13We have rebelled and lied against the LORD, and turned away from our God. We speak oppression and revolt, and from our hearts we plan and speak lies. 14So justice is pushed back, and righteousness stands far away. Truth has fallen in the street, and honesty can't even enter. 15Truth is missing, and anyone who turns from evil is attacked. The LORD saw this, and HeheHe was displeased that there was no justice.

The LORD Steps In to Save

16He saw there was no one, and HeheHe was amazed that no one stepped in to help. So HishisHis own arm brought salvation, and HishisHis righteousness supported HimhimHim. 17He put on righteousness like armourarmourarmor, and salvation like a helmet. He dressed in vengeance and wrapped HimselfhimselfHimself in zeal. 18He will repay them according to their deeds—fury to HishisHis enemies and punishment to HishisHis foes. He will repay even the distant lands. 19So people will fear the LORD's name from the west, and HishisHis glory from the east. When the enemy comes in like a flood, the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD will raise a banner against him.

God's Promise to His People

20"The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from sin," says the LORD. 21"As for MemeMe, this is MymyMy covenant with them," says the LORD. "My SpiritspiritSpirit, which is on you, and MymyMy words, which I have put in your mouth, will not leave your mouth, or the mouths of your children, or the mouths of your children's children, from now on and forever," says the LORD.
