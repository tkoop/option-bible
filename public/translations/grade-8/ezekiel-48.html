Land for the Tribes in the North

1These are the names of the tribes. Dan will have one share. Its land will run from east to west along the road from Hethlon to Lebo-hamath, as far as Hazar-enan, at the border of Damascus on the north, next to Hamath. 2Asher will have one share next to Dan's land, from east to west. 3Naphtali will have one share next to Asher's land, from east to west. 4Manasseh will have one share next to Naphtali's land, from east to west. 5Ephraim will have one share next to Manasseh's land, from east to west. 6Reuben will have one share next to Ephraim's land, from east to west. 7Judah will have one share next to Reuben's land, from east to west.

The Holy District and the City

8Next to Judah, from east to west, will be the special share you are to set apart. It will be 25,000 cubits wide and as long as one tribal share. The holy place will be in the middle of it. 9The portion you are to set apart for the LORD will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 10This holy portion for the priests will be 25,000 cubits long on the north, 10,000 cubits wide on the west, 10,000 cubits wide on the east, and 25,000 cubits long on the south. The sanctuary of the LORD will be in the middle. 11This land will belong to the priests who have been set apart, the sons of Zadok. They kept MymyMy charge and did not go wrong when the Israelites went wrong, as the Levites did. 12It will be a special gift to them from the land, the most holy portion next to the Levites' land. 13Next to the priests' land, the Levites will have a portion 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. The whole area will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. 14They must not sell any of it or trade it. They must not give away this best part of the land, because it is holy to the LORD. 15The rest of the land will be 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long. It will be ordinary land for the city, for houses and open fields. The city will be in the middle of it, 16and its measurements will be 4,500 cubits on the north, 4,500 on the south, 4,500 on the east, and 4,500 on the west. 17The city's open fields will extend 250 cubits to the north, 250 to the south, 250 to the east, and 250 to the west. 18The rest of the holy portion, next to the holy district, will be 10,000 cubits to the east and 10,000 cubits to the west. Its crops will provide food for the city workers. 19Workers from all the tribes of Israel will farm it. 20The whole portion will be a square, 25,000 cubits by 25,000 cubits. You are to set apart the holy portion along with the city's property. 21The rest of the land on both sides of the holy portion and the city property will belong to the prince. It will stretch eastward from the 25,000 cubits of the holy portion to the eastern border, and westward from the 25,000 cubits to the western border. The holy portion and the temple sanctuary will be in the middle. 22So the Levites' property and the city's property will lie in the middle of the prince's land. The prince's portion will be between the borders of Judah and Benjamin.

Land for the Southern Tribes

23As for the rest of the tribes, Benjamin will have one share next to Judah, from east to west. 24Simeon will have one share next to Benjamin, from east to west. 25Issachar will have one share next to Simeon, from east to west. 26Zebulun will have one share next to Issachar, from east to west. 27Gad will have one share next to Zebulun, from east to west. 28Along Gad's border, from east to west, will be the southern portion. It will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribah Kadesh, then along the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt to the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea. 29This is the land you are to divide as an inheritance for the tribes of Israel, and these are their shares, declares the Lord the LORD.

The City Gates and New Name

30These are the exits of the city. On the north side, which is 4,500 cubits long, 31the city gates will be named for the tribes of Israel. There will be three gates on the north: the gate of Reuben, the gate of Judah, and the gate of Levi. 32On the east side, which is 4,500 cubits long, there will be three gates: the gate of Joseph, the gate of Benjamin, and the gate of Dan. 33On the south side, which is 4,500 cubits long, there will be three gates: the gate of Simeon, the gate of Issachar, and the gate of Zebulun. 34On the west side, which is 4,500 cubits long, there will be three gates: the gate of Gad, the gate of Asher, and the gate of Naphtali. 35The city will measure 18,000 cubits all the way around, and from that day on its name will be the LORD is there.
