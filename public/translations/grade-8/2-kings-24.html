Jehoiakim Rebels Against Babylon

1In Jehoiakim's days, Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came up. Jehoiakim served him for three years, but then he turned and rebelled. 2The LORD sent Babylonian, Aramean, Moabite, and Ammonite raiders against him. He sent them to destroy Judah, just as the LORD had said through HishisHis prophets. 3This happened to Judah only because the LORD commanded it. He wanted to remove them from HishisHis sight because of Manasseh's sins and everything he had done. 4Manasseh had also killed innocent people and filled Jerusalem with innocent blood. The LORD would not forgive that.

Jehoiakim Dies and Egypt Is Defeated

5The rest of Jehoiakim's acts and everything he did are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. 6Jehoiakim died and was buried with his ancestors. His son Jehoiachin became king in his place. 7The king of Egypt did not march out of his land again, because the king of Babylon had taken everything from the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt to the Euphrates River that had belonged to Egypt.

Babylon Takes Jerusalem

8Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for three months. His mother's name was Nehushta. She was the daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem. 9He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father had done.

10At that time, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, went up to Jerusalem. The city was surrounded. 11Nebuchadnezzar himself came to the city while his servants were besieging it. 12Jehoiachin, king of Judah, went out to the king of Babylon. He took his mother, his servants, his officials, and his eunuchs with him. The king of Babylon captured him in the eighth year of Nebuchadnezzar's reign.

13He carried out all the treasures from the LORD's house and from the king's house. He also cut up all the gold articles that Solomon, king of Israel, had made for the LORD's temple, just as the LORD had said. 14He carried all Jerusalem into exile. He took all the officials, all the mighty warriors, all the craftsmen, and all the smiths—ten thousand captives. Only the poorest people in the land were left. 15He sent Jehoiachin to Babylon. He also took the king's mother, the king's wives, his officials, and the leading men of the land from Jerusalem to Babylon. 16The king of Babylon brought to Babylon all seven thousand soldiers, all one thousand craftsmen and smiths, and every strong, battle-ready man.

Zedekiah Becomes King

17The king of Babylon made Mattaniah, Jehoiachin's uncle, king in his place. He changed his name to Zedekiah. 18Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for eleven years. His mother's name was Hamutal. She was the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah. 19He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as Jehoiakim had done. 20Because of the LORD's anger, this happened to Jerusalem and Judah until HeheHe drove them from HishisHis presence. Zedekiah also rebelled against the king of Babylon.
