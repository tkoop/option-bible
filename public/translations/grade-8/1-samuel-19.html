Saul Plans to Kill David

1Saul told his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David. But Jonathan was very fond of David.

2Jonathan told David, "MymyMy father Saul is trying to kill you. Be careful in the morning. Stay in a safe place and hide. 3I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where you are. I will speak to him about you. Then I will see what he says and tell you."

4Jonathan spoke well of David to his father Saul. He said, "KingkingKing, do not sin against your servant David. He has not sinned against you, and what he has done has helped you very much. 5He risked his life when he struck the Philistine. The LORD brought a great victory for all Israel. You saw it and were glad. Why would you sin by killing an innocent man for no reason?"

6Saul listened to Jonathan and swore, "As surely as the LORD lives, David will not be put to death."

7Jonathan called David and told him everything. Then he brought David to Saul, and David was with him as before.

Saul Tries to Kill David Again

8Later, war broke out again. David went out and fought the Philistines. He hit them with a heavy defeat, and they ran away from him.

9But an evil spirit from the LORD came upon Saul while he sat in his house with his spear in his hand. David was playing music for him. 10Saul tried to pin David to the wall with the spear, but David slipped away. The spear struck the wall, and David ran and escaped that night.

Michal Helps David Escape

11Saul sent messengers to David's house to watch him and kill him in the morning. Michal, David's wife, warned him, "If you do not save yourself tonight, tomorrow you will be killed."

12Michal let David down through a window. He left and ran away, and he got away safely.

13Michal took a household idol and put it in the bed. She put goat's hair at its head and covered it with clothes.

14When Saul sent messengers to take David, Michal said, "HeheHe is sick."

15Saul sent the messengers back to see David. He told them, "BringbringBring him to me in the bed so I can kill him." 16The messengers went in and found the idol in the bed, with goat's hair at its head.

17Saul said to Michal, "Why did you trick me like this? Why did you let my enemy go?"

Michal answered, "HeheHe said to me, 'Let me go. Why should I kill you?'"

David Flees to Samuel

18David ran away and escaped. He went to Samuel at Ramah and told him all that Saul had done to him. Then David and Samuel went and stayed in Naioth. 19Someone told Saul, "David is in Naioth in Ramah."

20Saul sent messengers to capture David. But when they saw a group of prophets prophesying, with Samuel standing over them, the SpiritspiritSpirit of God came upon the messengers, and they also prophesied.

21When Saul heard this, he sent another group of messengers, and they also prophesied. Then he sent a third group, and they prophesied too.

22Finally, Saul went to Ramah himself. He came to the large well at Secu and asked, "Where are Samuel and David?"

Someone answered, "They are in Naioth in Ramah."

23Saul went to Naioth in Ramah. The SpiritspiritSpirit of God came upon him too, and he walked along prophesying until he reached Naioth.

24He took off his clothes and prophesied before Samuel. He lay naked all that day and all that night. That is why people say, "IsisIs Saul also among the prophets?"
