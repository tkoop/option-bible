Hezekiah's Illness and Prayer

1In those days Hezekiah became sick and was near death. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, "This is what the the LORD says: Put your house in order, because you are going to die. You will not live."

2Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the the LORD.

3He said, "Please, the LORD, remember how I have walked before YouyouYou in truth and with a whole heart. I have done what is good in YouryourYour sight." Then Hezekiah cried and wept bitterly.

God Promises Healing and a Sign

4Then the word of the the LORD came to Isaiah:

5"Go and say to Hezekiah, 'This is what the the LORD, the God of your father David, says: I have heard your prayer and seen your tears. I will add fifteen years to your life. 6I will rescue you and this city from the king of Assyria, and I will protect this city. 7This will be the sign to you from the the LORD that the the LORD will do what HeheHe has promised: 8I will make the shadow on the steps of Ahaz go back ten steps.' So the sun moved back the ten steps it had gone down.

Hezekiah's Song of Thanks

9This is the writing of Hezekiah king of Judah after he was sick and got well:

10I said, "In the middle of my life I must go through the gates of the grave. I am robbed of the rest of my years." 11I said, "I will not see the the LORD in the land of the living. I will no longer look at people who live in this world."

12My home is pulled up and carried away from me like a shepherd's tent. I have rolled up my life like a weaver. He cuts me off from the loom. Day and night, YouyouYou bring me to an end. 13I waited until morning. Like a lion, HeheHe breaks all my bones. Day and night, YouyouYou bring me to an end.

14I chirp like a swallow or a crane. I moan like a dove. My eyes are weak as I look to the heights. Lord, I am in trouble; be my guarantee. 15What can I say? He has spoken to me, and HeheHe has done it. I will walk carefully all my years because my soul is bitter.

16Lord, by these things people live. My spirit also lives by them. You restore me to health and keep me alive. 17I went through bitter pain, but it led to peace. You have loved my soul and saved it from the pit of destruction, because YouyouYou have thrown all my sins behind YouryourYour back.

18The grave does not thank YouyouYou. Death does not praise YouyouYou. Those who go down into the pit do not hope for YouryourYour truth. 19The living, the living, they thank YouyouYou, as I do today. Fathers tell their children about YouryourYour truth. 20The the LORD came to save me. We will sing my songs with stringed instruments all the days of our lives in the house of the the LORD.
