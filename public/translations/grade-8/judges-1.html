Israel Asks Who Should Fight First

1After Joshua died, the people of Israel asked the LORD, "Which tribe should go first and fight the Canaanites for us?"

2The LORD said, "Judah should go. I have given the land into his hand."

3Judah said to his brother Simeon, "Come with me into the land I have been given by lot, and we will fight the Canaanites. Then I will go with you into the land you have been given." So Simeon went with him.

4Judah went up, and the LORD gave the Canaanites and Perizzites into their hands. They struck down ten thousand men at Bezek. 5There they found Adoni-Bezek and fought him, defeating the Canaanites and Perizzites.

6Adoni-Bezek ran away, but they chased him, caught him, and cut off his thumbs and big toes. 7Adoni-Bezek said, "Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off used to gather scraps under my table. God has paid me back for what I did." They brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

8The men of Judah fought against Jerusalem and captured it. They put the people to the sword and set the city on fire.

9After that, the men of Judah went down to fight the Canaanites living in the hill country, the Negev, and the western foothills. 10They marched against the Canaanites living in Hebron, which used to be called Kiriath Arba, and defeated Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

11From there they went against the people living in Debir, which used to be called Kiriath Sepher. 12Caleb said, "I will give my daughter Achsah in marriage to the man who attacks and captures Kiriath Sepher." 13Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, captured it, so Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.

14When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got off her donkey, and Caleb asked her, "What do you want?"

15She said to him, "Give me a blessing. You gave me land in the Negev, so give me springs of water too." So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

16The descendants of Moses' father-in-law, the Kenite, went up from the CitycityCity of Palms with the men of Judah into the Negev of Arad. They went and lived among the people there.

17Judah went with his brother Simeon and struck the Canaanites living in Zephath. They destroyed the city completely and called it Hormah.

18Judah also captured Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron, along with their surrounding lands.

19The LORD was with Judah, and they took the hill country. But they could not drive out the people living in the plains, because those people had iron chariots.

20As Moses had said, Hebron was given to Caleb, and he drove out the three sons of Anak from there.

21The Benjamites did not drive out the Jebusites living in Jerusalem. So the Jebusites have lived with the Benjamites in Jerusalem to this day.

The House of Joseph Captures Bethel

22The house of Joseph also went up to attack Bethel, and the LORD was with them. 23They sent spies to Bethel, a city that used to be called Luz. 24The spies saw a man coming out of the city and said to him, "Please show us the way into the city, and we will treat you kindly." 25He showed them the way in, and they put the city to the sword. But they let the man and all his family go. 26The man went to the land of the Hittites and built a city. He called it Luz, and that is its name to this day.

Tribes That Did Not Drive Out the Canaanites

27Manasseh did not drive out the people of Beth Shan, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, and Megiddo, or their surrounding towns. The Canaanites were determined to stay in that land. 28When Israel grew strong, they made the Canaanites do forced labourlabourlabor, but they did not drive them out completely.

29Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer. The Canaanites stayed among them in Gezer.

30Zebulun did not drive out the people of Kitron or Nahalol. The Canaanites stayed among them and were put to forced labourlabourlabor.

31Asher did not drive out the people of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphek, or Rehob. 32The Asherites lived among the Canaanites who lived in the land, because they did not drive them out.

33Naphtali did not drive out the people of Beth Shemesh or Beth Anath. They lived among the Canaanites in the land, but the people of Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath were put to forced labourlabourlabor.

34The Amorites pushed the Danites back into the hill country and did not let them come down into the plains. 35The Amorites were determined to stay in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. But when the house of Joseph grew strong, they made the Amorites do forced labourlabourlabor.

36The border of the Amorites ran from Scorpion Pass to Sela and beyond.
