The Bridegroom Comes to His Garden

1I have come into my garden, my sister, my bride. I have gathered my myrrh and my spices. I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey, and I have drunk my wine with my milk. Eat, my friends; drink as much as you want, my loved ones.

The Bride Searches for Her Beloved

2I am asleep, but my heart is awake. Listen! My love is knocking. He says, “Open to me, my sister, my darling, my dove, my perfect one. My head is wet with dew, and my hair is damp with the night.”

3I have taken off my robe. Must I put it on again? I have washed my feet. Must I get them dirty again?

4My love put his hand through the opening of the door, and my heart began to pound for him.

5I got up to open the door for my love. My hands dripped with myrrh, and my fingers were wet with flowing myrrh on the handles of the lock.

6I opened the door for my love, but my love had turned away and was gone. My heart sank when he left. I looked for him but could not find him. I called him, but he did not answer.

7The watchmen who make their rounds in the city found me. They beat me and bruised me. They took my cloak from me, those watchmen on the walls.

8Daughters of Jerusalem, I beg you, if you find my love, tell him I am weak with love.

The Daughters Ask About Her Beloved

9“What makes your love better than any other, most beautiful of women? What makes your love better than any other, that you made us promise this?”

The Bride Describes Her Beloved

10“My love is bright and strong, the best of ten thousand. 11His head is like pure gold. His hair is wavy and black like a raven. 12His eyes are like doves beside streams of water, washed in milk and set like jewels. 13His cheeks are like beds of spices that give off perfume. His lips are lilies dripping with liquid myrrh. 14His arms are like gold rods set with topaz. His body is like smooth ivory decorated with sapphires. 15His legs are like marble pillars set on bases of pure gold. He looks like Lebanon, as fine as its cedars. 16His mouth is sweet, and everything about him is lovely. This is my love. This is my friend, daughters of Jerusalem.”
