God Has Not Rejected His People

1I ask then: Did God reject HishisHis people? Never! For I myself am an Israelite, from Abraham's family line, from the tribe of Benjamin. 2God did not reject HishisHis people, whom HeheHe knew long ago. Don't you know what ScripturescriptureScripture says about Elijah? He pleaded with God against Israel:

3"Lord, they killed YouryourYour prophets and tore down YouryourYour altars. I'm the only one left, and they're trying to kill me."

4But what did God's answer say? "I have kept seven thousand men for MyselfmyselfMyself, men who didn't bow to Baal."

5So too, right now there is a small group chosen by grace. 6And if it's by grace, then it's no longer by works. Otherwise grace wouldn't be grace. 7What then? Israel didn't find what it was looking for, but the chosen ones did. The rest were hardened. 8As it is written: "God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that don't see and ears that don't hear, to this very day."

9And David says: "May their table become a snare and a trap, a stumbling block and a payback to them. 10May their eyes be darkened so they can't see, and may their backs be bent forever."

Salvation Comes to the Gentiles

11I ask then: Did they stumble so as to fall? No! Instead, because they fell, salvation came to the Gentiles, to make Israel jealous. 12Now if their fall brought riches to the world, and their loss brought riches to the Gentiles, how much greater riches will come when they are fully restored! 13I am speaking to you Gentiles. Because I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I take pride in my work, 14hoping I might make my own people jealous and save some of them. 15For if their rejection brought the world back to God, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?

16If the first part of the dough is holy, then the whole batch is holy. And if the root is holy, so are the branches.

The Olive Tree and the Gentiles

17If some of the branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among them and now share in the good sap from the olive root, 18don't brag over those branches. If you do brag, remember that you don't hold up the root, but the root holds you up. 19You will say then, "Those branches were broken off so I could be grafted in." 20That's true. They were broken off because of unbelief, and you stand by faith. Don't be proud, but stand in awe. 21For if God didn't spare the natural branches, HeheHe won't spare you either. 22So look at both God's kindness and HishisHis sternness: sternness to those who fell, but kindness to you, if you keep on in HishisHis kindness. Otherwise, you too will be cut off. 23And if they don't keep on in unbelief, they will be grafted in again, for God is able to graft them in again. 24For if you were cut out of an olive tree that is wild by nature, and against nature were grafted into a good olive tree, how much more will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree!

All Israel Will Be Saved

25I don't want you to miss this secret, brothers and sisters, so you won't be proud: A hardening has come upon Israel in part, until the full number of the Gentiles has come in. 26And in this way all Israel will be saved, as it is written: "The Deliverer will come from Zion; HeheHe will turn ungodliness away from Jacob. 27And this is MymyMy covenant with them when I take away their sins."

28As far as the gospel is concerned, they are enemies for your sake; but as far as God's choice is concerned, they are loved because of their ancestors, 29for God's gifts and HishisHis call can't be taken back. 30Just as you were once disobedient to God but now have received mercy because of their disobedience, 31so they have now become disobedient in order that they too may now receive mercy as a result of God's mercy to you. 32For God has locked everyone up in disobedience so that HeheHe may have mercy on everyone.

33Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable HishisHis judgmentsjudgementsjudgments and untraceable HishisHis paths! 34"For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been HishisHis counsellorcounsellorcounselor?" 35"Or who has ever given to God, that God should repay him?" 36For from HimhimHim and through HimhimHim and to HimhimHim are all things. To HimhimHim be glory forever. Amen.
