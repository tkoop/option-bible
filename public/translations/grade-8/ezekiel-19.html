A Sad Song for Israel's Leaders

1As for you, take up a sad song for Israel's leaders. 2Say, "What was your mother? A lioness among lions. She lay down among young lions and raised her cubs.

3She brought up one of her cubs, and he became a young lion. He learned to tear his prey, and he killed people. 4The nations heard about him, so they caught him in their pit. They dragged him with hooks to Egypt.

5When she saw that her hope was gone, she took another cub and made him a young lion. 6He walked among the lions and became a young lion. He learned to tear his prey, and he killed people. 7He tore down their strong places and destroyed their cities. The land and everything in it was ruined by the sound of his roaring.

8Then nations from all around came against him. They spread their net over him, and he was caught in their pit. 9They dragged him with hooks into a cage and brought him to the king of Babylon. They took him to their forts so his voice would never again be heard on Israel's mountains.

The Vine in the Vineyard

10Your mother was like a vine planted in your vineyard by the water. It was fruitful and full of branches because of plenty of water. 11Its branches were strong enough to be a ruler's staff. It grew tall above the thick branches, and people could see it because of its height and many leaves.

12But it was torn up in anger and thrown to the ground. The east wind dried up its fruit. Its strong branches broke off and dried up, and fire burned them. 13Now it is planted in the desert, in a dry and thirsty land. 14Fire has come out from its main branch and burned its fruit. No strong branch is left to be a ruler's staff." This is a sad song, and it will be used as a sad song.
