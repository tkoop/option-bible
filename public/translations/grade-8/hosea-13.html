Ephraim's Sin and False Worship

1When Ephraim spoke, people trembled; he was lifted up in Israel. But he became guilty because of Baal worship, and he died.

2Now they sin more and more. They make metal idols for themselves from their silver, shaped by their own skill. All of them are made by craftsmen. People say about them, “They sacrifice people and worship calf idols.”

3So they will be like the morning mist, like the early dew that disappears, like chaff blown from a threshing floor, and like smoke drifting through a window.
