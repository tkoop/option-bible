David Gets Holy Bread at Nob

1David went to Nob to see Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech trembled when he met David. He asked, "Why are you alone? No one is with you."

2David answered, "ThetheThe king sent me on a secret mission. He told me, 'No one is to know anything about this mission or what I commanded you. I have told the young men to meet me at a certain place.'" 3"Now, what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever you can find."

4The priest answered, "I have no ordinary bread here. But there is holy bread if the young men have kept themselves from women."

5David answered, "Women have been kept away from us, as usual when I travel. The young men's bodies are holy even on ordinary trips. How much more so today!"

6So the priest gave him the holy bread. No other bread was there except the bread of the PresencepresencePresence, which had been removed from before the LORD to be replaced by hot bread on the day it was taken away. 7That day one of Saul's servants was held back before the LORD. His name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul's herdsmen.

David Asks for a Weapon

8David asked Ahimelech, "Do you have a spear or sword here? I did not bring my sword or other weapons, because the king's business was urgent."

9The priest said, "ThetheThe sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you killed in the ValleyvalleyValley of Elah, is here. It is wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want it, take it. There is no other sword here."

David said, "There is none like it. Give it to me."

David Escapes to Gath

10That same day David got up and fled from Saul. He went to Achish, king of Gath.

11Achish's servants said to him, "Isn't this David, the king of the land? Didn't the women sing about him in their dances, 'Saul has killed his thousands, and David his ten thousands'?"

12David was deeply troubled by these words and was very afraid of Achish, king of Gath.

David Pretends to Be Insane

13So he changed his behaviourbehaviourbehavior before them. He acted like a madman in their hands. He scratched on the doors of the gate and let saliva run down his beard.

14Achish said to his servants, "Look, you can see the man is insane. Why did you bring him to me? 15Do I not have enough madmen already? Why did you bring this man to act insane in front of me? Is he going to come into my house?"
