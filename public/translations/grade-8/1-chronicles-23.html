David Prepares Solomon to Rule

1When David was old and had lived a long life, he made his son Solomon king over Israel. 2He gathered all the leaders of Israel, along with the priests and the Levites.

Levites Are Counted and Assigned

3He counted the Levites who were thirty years old or older. There were 38,000 men. 4Of these, 24,000 were to oversee the work of the the LORD’s temple. Six thousand were officers and judges. 5Four thousand were gatekeepers, and 4,000 praised the the LORD with the instruments David had made for praising HimhimHim.

Levite Families

6David divided them into groups for the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

7The sons of Gershon were Ladan and Shimei. 8The sons of Ladan were Jehiel, the leader, Zetham, and Joel—three in all. 9The sons of Shimei were Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran—three in all. These were the leaders of Ladan’s families.

10The sons of Shimei were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These four were Shimei’s sons. 11Jahath was the leader, and Zizah was second. Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one family.

12The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in all.

13The sons of Amram were Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to care for the most holy things. He and his sons were to burn incense before the the LORD, serve HimhimHim, and bless in HishisHis name forever.

14As for Moses, the man of God, his sons were counted with the tribe of Levi. 15Moses’ sons were Gershom and Eliezer. 16Gershom’s son was Shebuel, the leader. 17Eliezer’s son was Rehabiah, the leader. Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah’s sons were very many.

18Izhar’s son was Shelomith, the leader. 19Hebron’s sons were Jeriah the leader, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. 20Uzziel’s sons were Micah the leader and Isshiah the second.

21The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli’s sons were Eleazar and Kish. 22Eleazar died with no sons, only daughters. Their relatives, the sons of Kish, married them. 23Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three in all.

Duties of the Levites

24These were Levi’s sons by family, the leaders of families. They were counted by name, head by head, twenty years old and older, and they did the work for service in the the LORD’s temple. 25David said, “The ThetheThe LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to HishisHis people and has come to live in Jerusalem forever.” 26So the Levites no longer had to carry the tabernacle or any of its equipment for its service.

27By David’s last words, the Levites twenty years old and older were counted. 28Their place was beside the sons of Aaron to serve in the the LORD’s temple: caring for the courtyards and rooms, keeping all holy things clean, and doing the work of serving God’s house. 29They were also in charge of the bread on the table, the fine flour for grain offerings, the unleavened wafers, the baking pan, the mixed flour, and every measure and amount.

30They stood every morning to thank and praise the the LORD, and they did the same every evening. 31They also offered all the burnt offerings to the the LORD on the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the special feasts, in the right numbers, always before the the LORD. 32They guarded the tent of meeting, the holy place, and their relatives, the sons of Aaron, as they served in the the LORD’s temple.
