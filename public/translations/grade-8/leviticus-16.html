1The LORD spoke to Moses after two of Aaron's sons died. They had come near to the LORD, and they died.

2The LORD said to Moses, “Tell your brother Aaron not to enter the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace behind the curtain at just any time. He must not go in front of the mercy seat on the ark, or he will die, because I will appear in the cloud over the mercy seat.

Aaron Prepares for the Day of Atonement

3“Aaron must enter the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace with a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering. 4He must wear the holy linen tunic, with linen undergarments against his body, a linen sash around him, and a linen turban on his head. These are holy clothes. He must wash his whole body with water before he puts them on. 5He must take from the Israelite community two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

6“Aaron must bring the bull for his own sin offering and make atonement for himself and his household. 7He must take the two goats and present them before the LORD at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 8Aaron must cast lots for the two goats. One lot is for the LORD, and the other lot is for Azazel. 9Aaron must present the goat chosen by lot for the LORD and sacrifice it as a sin offering. 10The goat chosen by lot for Azazel must be kept alive and presented before the LORD. Atonement must be made over it, and then it must be sent into the wilderness to Azazel.

Atonement in the Holy Place

11“Aaron must bring the bull for his sin offering and make atonement for himself and his household. He must slaughter the bull. 12He must take a censer full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD, and he must fill both hands with finely ground incense. He must bring them behind the curtain. 13He must put the incense on the fire before the LORD, so that the cloud of incense covers the mercy seat above the ark of the TestimonytestimonyTestimony. Then he will not die. 14He must take some of the bull's blood and sprinkle it with his finger on the front of the mercy seat on the east side. He must sprinkle some of the blood with his finger seven times in front of the mercy seat.

15“Then he must slaughter the goat for the people's sin offering and bring its blood behind the curtain. He must do with its blood what he did with the bull's blood. He must sprinkle it on the mercy seat and in front of the mercy seat. 16In this way he will make atonement for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace because of the uncleanness and rebellion of the Israelites, whatever their sins. He must do the same for the tent of meeting, which stays among them in the middle of their uncleanness. 17No one may be in the tent of meeting when Aaron goes in to make atonement in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace until he comes out. He must make atonement for himself, his household, and the whole community of Israel.

18“Then he must go out to the altar that is before the LORD and make atonement for it. He must take some of the bull's blood and some of the goat's blood and put it on all the horns of the altar. 19He must sprinkle some of the blood on it with his finger seven times. In this way he will cleanse it and set it apart from the uncleanness of the Israelites. 20After he has finished making atonement for the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, the tent of meeting, and the altar, he must bring the live goat.

The Scapegoat

21“Aaron must place both hands on the head of the live goat and confess over it all the wickedness, rebellion, and sins of the Israelites. He must put them on the goat's head. Then he must send the goat into the wilderness with a man appointed for the task. 22The goat will carry all their sins to a desolate place. The man must release the goat in the wilderness.

23“Then Aaron must go into the tent of meeting and take off the linen clothes he wore when he entered the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. He must leave them there. 24He must wash his body with water in a holy place and put on his own clothes. Then he must come out and offer his burnt offering and the people's burnt offering. He must make atonement for himself and for the people. 25He must burn the fat of the sin offering on the altar.

26“The man who released the goat for Azazel must wash his clothes and bathe his body with water. After that, he may come into the camp. 27The bull for the sin offering and the goat for the sin offering, whose blood was brought into the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace to make atonement, must be taken outside the camp. Their hides, flesh, and dung must be burned in the fire. 28The person who burns them must wash his clothes and bathe his body with water. After that, he may come into the camp.

A Lasting Law

29“This will be a permanent rule for you: On the tenth day of the seventh month, you must deny yourselves and not do any work. This applies to the native-born and to the foreigner living among you. 30Because on this day atonement will be made for you to cleanse you. Then you will be clean before the LORD from all your sins. 31It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you must deny yourselves. This is a permanent rule.

32“The priest who is anointed and ordained to serve in his father's place must make atonement. He must wear the holy linen clothes. 33He must make atonement for the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, the tent of meeting, and the altar. He must make atonement for the priests and for all the people of the community. 34This will be a permanent rule for you. Once a year, atonement must be made for the Israelites for all their sins.”

He did as the LORD commanded Moses.
