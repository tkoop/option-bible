Paul's Ministry to the Gentiles

14I am sure about you, my brothers and sisters. You are full of goodness and filled with all knowledge. You are able to teach one another. 15Still, I have written to you quite boldly in part, to remind you of these things. I do this because of the grace God gave me. 16He made me a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles. I serve like a priest for God's good news. I do this so that the Gentiles may be an offering God accepts, made holy by the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit.

17So in Christ Jesus I can be proud of my work for God. 18I will not talk about anything except what Christ has done through me. He led the Gentiles to obey God by what I said and did. 19He worked through the power of signs and wonders, and through the power of God's SpiritspiritSpirit. In this way, I have fully shared the good news of Christ from Jerusalem all the way around to Illyricum.

20I have made it my goal to preach the good news where Christ is not known. I do not want to build on someone else's foundation. 21As it is written, “Those who were not told about HimhimHim will see. Those who have not heard will understand.”

Paul's Plans to Visit Rome

22That is why I have often been kept from coming to you. 23But now I have finished my work in these areas. I have wanted to visit you for many years. 24I hope to see you on my way to Spain. I hope you will help me on my journey after I have enjoyed being with you for a while.

25Right now, though, I am going to Jerusalem to serve God's people there. 26For Macedonia and Achaia were happy to make a gift for the poor among God's people in Jerusalem. 27They were happy to do it, and they owed it to them. The Gentiles have shared in the spiritual blessings of the believers in Jerusalem, so they should help them with material blessings.

28So after I finish this task and safely deliver this gift, I will go to Spain and visit you on the way. 29I know that when I come to you, I will come with the full blessing of Christ.

30I urge you, brothers and sisters, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the SpiritspiritSpirit, to join me in my struggle. Pray to God for me. 31Pray that I will be rescued from those in Judea who do not believe. Pray that my service in Jerusalem will be accepted by God's people there. 32Then, by God's will, I may come to you with joy and be refreshed with you. 33May the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.
