Elisha Sends a Prophet to Anoint Jehu

1Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets. He said, "Get ready, take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-Gilead.

2When you get there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Go in, make him stand up from among his brothers, and take him into an inner room.

3Take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and say, 'This is what the LORD says: I have anointed you king over Israel.' Then open the door and run. Don't wait.

4So the young prophet went to Ramoth-Gilead.

5He arrived and found the army commanders sitting together. He said, "I have a message for you, commander."

Jehu asked, "For which of us?"

The prophet answered, "For you, commander."

6Jehu got up and went into the house. The prophet poured the oil on his head and said, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'I have anointed you king over the the LORD's people Israel.'"

7ThetheThe prophet added, "YouyouYou must strike down the family of Ahab, your master. I will punish Jezebel for the blood of MymyMy servants the prophets and all the the LORD's servants. 8I will wipe out every male in Ahab's family, both slave and free, in Israel. 9I will make Ahab's family like the family of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the family of Baasha son of Ahijah. 10Dogs will eat Jezebel in the plot of ground at Jezreel, and no one will bury her. Then the prophet opened the door and ran.

Jehu Plans to Overthrow Joram

11Jehu went out to his master's servants. They asked him, "IsisIs everything all right? Why did this crazy man come to you?"

He answered, "YouyouYou know the man and what he says."

12They said, "That's not true. Tell us now."

He told them, "HeheHe said this and that to me. He said, 'This is what the LORD says: I have anointed you king over Israel.'"

13Then they hurried. Each man took his coat and put it under Jehu on the bare steps. They blew the trumpet and shouted, "Jehu is king!"

14So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, made a plan against Joram. (Joram and all Israel had been guarding Ramoth-Gilead against Hazael king of Aram. 15But King Joram had gone back to Jezreel to heal from the wounds the Arameans had given him while fighting Hazael king of Aram.) Jehu said, "If you are willing, let no one escape from the city to go and tell the news in Jezreel."

16Then Jehu got into his chariot and went to Jezreel, because Joram was lying there. Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.

The Watchman Sees Jehu Coming

17The watchman standing on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu's troops coming. He said, "I see a group coming."

Joram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them. Have him ask, 'Is everything all right?'"

18So a horseman rode out to meet Jehu and said, "ThetheThe king says, 'Is everything all right?'"

Jehu said, "What do you have to do with peace? Get behind me."

The watchman reported, "ThetheThe messenger reached them, but he is not coming back."

19Then Joram sent a second horseman. He came to them and said, "ThetheThe king says, 'Is everything all right?'"

Jehu answered, "What do you have to do with peace? Get behind me."

20The watchman reported, "HeheHe reached them, but he is not coming back. The driving is like Jehu's driving. He drives like a madman."

21Joram said, "Harness the chariot." They harnessed his chariot. Then Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah each went out in his chariot. They met Jehu in the field of Naboth the Jezreelite.

22When Joram saw Jehu, he said, "IsisIs it peace, Jehu?"

Jehu answered, "How can there be peace while your mother Jezebel is so unfaithful and uses so much witchcraft?"

23Joram turned his chariot to run. He said to Ahaziah, "It's a trap, Ahaziah!"

24Jehu drew his bow and shot Joram between the shoulders. The arrow went through his heart, and he sank down in his chariot.

25Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, "Pick him up and throw him into the field of Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember when you and I rode together behind his father Ahab? The LORD put this heavy burden on him. 26I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons, says the the LORD. I will pay you back in this field, says the the LORD. So now pick him up and throw him into the field, just as the the LORD said."

27When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he ran toward Beth-haggan. Jehu chased him and said, "Shoot him too!" They shot him in his chariot on the way up to Gur near Ibleam. He escaped to Megiddo and died there.

28His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him with his fathers in his tomb in the CitycityCity of David.

29Ahaziah had become king over Judah in the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab.

Jezebel Is Killed

30When Jehu came to Jezreel, Jezebel heard about it. She painted her eyes, fixed her hair, and looked out the window.

31As Jehu entered the gate, she said, "IsisIs everything all right, Zimri, you murderer of your master?"

32He looked up at the window and said, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three eunuchs looked down at him.

33He said, "Throw her down." They threw her down. Some of her blood splashed on the wall and on the horses, and Jehu trampled her underfoot.

34Then he went in and ate and drank. He said, "Take care of this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

35They went to bury her, but they found only her skull, her feet, and the palms of her hands.

36They went back and told Jehu. He said, "This is what the the LORD said through HishisHis servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'In the plot at Jezreel, dogs will eat Jezebel's flesh. 37Her body will be like dung on the ground in the plot at Jezreel, so that no one can say, "This is Jezebel."'"
