Rules for Skin Diseases

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. 2When a person has a swelling, rash, or bright spot on the skin, and it could be a skin disease, that person must be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons who are priests. 3The priest must look at the sore on the skin. If the hair in the sore has turned white and the sore looks deeper than the skin, it is a skin disease. The priest must say the person is unclean.

4If the spot is white but does not look deeper than the skin, and the hair has not turned white, the priest must keep the person apart for seven days. 5On the seventh day the priest must look again. If the sore has not changed and has not spread, the priest must keep the person apart for another seven days. 6On the seventh day the priest must look again. If the sore has faded and has not spread, the priest must say the person is clean. It is only a rash. The person must wash their clothes and will be clean.

7But if the rash spreads after the person has been shown to the priest to be made clean, that person must come back to the priest. 8The priest must look, and if the rash has spread, he must say the person is unclean. It is a skin disease.

9When a person has a skin disease, that person must be brought to the priest. 10The priest must look, and if there is a white swelling that has turned the hair white and there is raw flesh in the swelling, 11it is a long-lasting skin disease. The priest must say the person is unclean. He must not keep the person apart, because the person is already unclean.

12If the disease breaks out all over the skin and covers the person from head to foot, as far as the priest can see, 13the priest must look. If the disease has covered the whole body, he must say the person is clean. Since all the skin has turned white, the person is clean.

14But whenever raw flesh shows on the person, that person is unclean. 15When the priest sees the raw flesh, he must say the person is unclean. The raw flesh is unclean. It is a skin disease.

16If the raw flesh changes and turns white, the person must go to the priest. 17The priest must look, and if the sore has turned white, the priest must say the person is clean. Then the person is clean.

Boils, Burns, and Sores

18When a person has a boil on the skin and it heals, 19and a white swelling or reddish-white spot appears where the boil was, that person must show it to the priest. 20The priest must look, and if it looks deeper than the skin and the hair in it has turned white, the priest must say the person is unclean. It is a skin disease that has broken out in the boil.

21But if there is no white hair in it, and it is not deeper than the skin, and it has faded, the priest must keep the person apart for seven days. 22If it spreads in the skin, the priest must say the person is unclean. It is a disease. 23But if the spot stays the same and does not spread, it is only a scar from the boil. The priest must say the person is clean.

24When a person has a burn on the skin, and a reddish-white or white spot appears in the raw flesh of the burn, 25the priest must look at the spot. If the hair in it has turned white and it looks deeper than the skin, it is a skin disease that has broken out in the burn. The priest must say the person is unclean. It is a skin disease.

26But if there is no white hair in the spot, and it is not deeper than the skin, and it has faded, the priest must keep the person apart for seven days. 27On the seventh day the priest must look, and if it has spread in the skin, the priest must say the person is unclean. It is a skin disease. 28If the spot stays the same and has not spread, but has faded, it is a swelling from the burn. The priest must say the person is clean. It is only a scar from the burn.

Sores on the Head or Chin

29If a man or woman has a sore on the head or chin, 30the priest must look at the sore. If it looks deeper than the skin and the hair in it is yellow and thin, the priest must say the person is unclean. It is a skin disease on the head or chin. 31But if the sore does not look deeper than the skin and there is no black hair in it, the priest must keep the person apart for seven days.

32On the seventh day the priest must look at the sore. If it has not spread and there is no yellow hair in it and it does not look deeper than the skin, 33the man or woman must shave, but not the sore. The priest must keep the person apart for another seven days. 34On the seventh day the priest must look at the sore. If it has not spread in the skin and does not look deeper than the skin, the priest must say the person is clean. The person must wash their clothes and will be clean.

35But if the sore spreads in the skin after the person has been made clean, 36the priest must look. If the sore has spread, the priest does not need to look for yellow hair. The person is unclean. 37If the sore stays the same and black hair grows in it, the sore has healed. The person is clean, and the priest must say so.

38When a man or woman has white spots on the skin, 39the priest must look. If the spots are dull white, it is a harmless rash on the skin. The person is clean.

40A man who has lost his hair and is bald is clean. 41A man who has lost hair from the front of his head and has a bald forehead is clean.

42But if he has a reddish-white sore on his bald head or forehead, it is a skin disease breaking out on his baldness. 43The priest must look, and if the swollen sore on his bald head or forehead is reddish-white like a skin disease, 44the man is unclean. The priest must say he is unclean. He has a skin disease on his head.

45Anyone with such a disease must wear torn clothes. He must let his hair hang loose. He must cover his mouth and cry out, "Unclean! Unclean!"

46As long as the person has the disease, that person stays unclean. The person must live alone, outside the camp.

Mildew in Clothing

47When clothing is spoiled by a damaging mold—wool or linen clothing— 48or woven or knitted material of linen or wool, or leather, or anything made of leather, 49and the affected area is greenish or reddish, it is a spreading mold. That item must be shown to the priest.

50The priest must look at the affected area and keep the item apart for seven days. 51On the seventh day he must look again. If the mold has spread in the fabric, leather, woven or knitted material, or leather article, it is a lasting mold. The item is unclean.

52The priest must burn the fabric, wool or linen clothing, or leather article that has the mold, because the mold is lasting. It must be burned.

53But if the mold has not spread when the priest looks, 54he must order the affected item to be washed. Then he must keep it apart for another seven days.

55After the item has been washed, the priest must look again. If the mold has not changed its look and has not spread, it is unclean. Burn it, whether the mold has eaten into the fabric or the leather.

56If the mold has faded after the item has been washed, the priest must tear the spoiled part out of the fabric, leather, woven or knitted material, or leather article. 57But if it shows up again, it is a spreading mold. Burn the item.

58Any fabric, woven or knitted material, or leather article that has been washed and is free of the mold must be washed again and will be clean. 59These are the rules for mold in wool or linen clothing, woven or knitted material, or any leather article. They tell how to make such things clean or unclean.
