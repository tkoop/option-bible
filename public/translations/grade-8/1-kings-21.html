Ahab Wants Naboth's Vineyard

1After these things, Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard in Jezreel, next to the palace of Ahab, king of Samaria. 2Ahab said to Naboth, "Give me your vineyard so I can make it a vegetable garden. It is close to my house. I will give you a better vineyard than it, or, if you like, I will pay you its price in money."

3But Naboth said to Ahab, "May ThetheThe LORD keep me from giving you the land my fathers left me." 4Ahab went home angry and upset because Naboth had said, "I will not give you the land my fathers left me." He lay on his bed, turned his face away, and would not eat.

Jezebel's Plan Against Naboth

5His wife Jezebel came to him and asked, "Why are you so upset that you will not eat?"

6He answered, "I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said, 'Give me your vineyard for money, or, if you prefer, I will give you another vineyard for it.' But he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

7Jezebel his wife said to him, "Are you the king of Israel? Get up, eat, and be happy. I will get Naboth's vineyard for you."

8So she wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and sent them to the elders and nobles who lived in Naboth's city. 9In the letters she wrote, "Call for a day of fasting, and put Naboth in a place of honourhonourhonor among the people. 10Seat two scoundrels in front of him and have them say, 'YouyouYou have cursed God and the king.' Then take him out and stone him to death."

11The men of the city—the elders and nobles who lived there—did exactly what Jezebel had written. 12They called for a fast and put Naboth in a place of honourhonourhonor. 13Then two scoundrels came in and sat across from him. They spoke against Naboth in front of the people and said, "Naboth has cursed God and the king." They took him outside the city, stoned him, and he died.

14Then they sent word to Jezebel: "Naboth has been stoned to death."

15When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, she said to Ahab, "Get up and take possession of Naboth's vineyard in Jezreel. He would not sell it to you for money, but now he is dead."

16As soon as Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he went down to take possession of the vineyard.

Elijah Confronts Ahab

17Then the word of the the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite: 18"Get up and go down to meet Ahab, king of Israel, who is in Samaria. He is in Naboth's vineyard, where he has gone to take possession of it. 19Say to him, 'This is what the the LORD says: Have you murdered someone and then taken his property? This is what the the LORD says: In the place where dogs licked up Naboth's blood, dogs will lick up your blood—yes, yours.'"

20Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, my enemy?"

Elijah answered, "I have found you, because you have given yourself over to doing evil in the sight of the the LORD."

21"Look, I am bringing disaster on you. I will wipe you out and kill every male in Israel who belongs to Ahab, whether slave or free. 22I will make your family like the family of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the family of Baasha son of Ahijah, because you have made MemeMe angry and led Israel into sin. 23The LORD also says this about Jezebel: 'Dogs will eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.' 24Anyone from Ahab's family who dies in the city will be eaten by dogs, and anyone who dies in the field will be eaten by birds."

25There was no one like Ahab, who gave himself over to doing evil in the sight of the the LORD, because his wife Jezebel pushed him to do it. 26He acted in a very disgusting way by following idols, just as the Amorites had done, whom the the LORD drove out before the Israelites.

Ahab Repents

27When Ahab heard these words, he tore his clothes, put on sackcloth, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and walked around quietly.

28Then the word of the the LORD came to Elijah the Tishbite: 29"Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before MemeMe? Because he has humbled himself before MemeMe, I will not bring disaster on him in his own lifetime. I will bring disaster on his family in the days of his son."
