The Sabbath Command

1Moses gathered the whole community of Israelites and said, “These are the things the LORD has commanded you to do. 2For six days, work may be done, but the seventh day must be holy to you. It is a Sabbath of rest to the LORD. Anyone who does work on that day must be put to death. 3Do not light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day.”

An Offering for the Tabernacle

4Moses said to the whole community of Israelites, “This is what the LORD has commanded: 5Take from among you an offering for the LORD. Everyone who is willing may bring the LORD’s offering: gold, silver, and bronze; 6blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; fine linen; goat hair; 7ram skins dyed red; fine leather; and acacia wood; 8olive oil for the light; spices for the anointing oil and for the sweet incense; 9and onyx stones and other stones to be set in the ephod and the breastpiece.

10“Let every skilled person among you come and make all that the LORD has commanded: 11the tabernacle, its tent and covering, its clasps, frames, crossbars, posts, and bases; 12the ark with its poles, the atonement cover, and the curtain; 13the table with its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the PresencepresencePresence; 14the lampstand for the light with its utensils and lamps, and the oil for the light; 15the incense altar with its poles; the anointing oil and the fragrant incense; the curtain for the entrance to the tabernacle; 16the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grating, poles, and utensils; the basin and its stand; 17the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and bases, and the screen for the courtyard gate; 18the tent pegs for the tabernacle and courtyard, and their ropes; 19and the woven garments for serving in the holy place, the sacred garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons to serve as priests.”

The People Bring Their Gifts

20Then the whole community of Israelites left Moses. 21Everyone whose heart moved them and whose spirit was willing came and brought an offering to the LORD for the work on the tent of meeting, for all its service, and for the sacred garments. 22Men and women came and brought brooches, earrings, rings, and ornaments, all kinds of gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry. Everyone who waved a gold offering to the LORD brought it. 23Everyone who had blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, fine linen, goat hair, ram skins dyed red, or fine leather brought them. 24Everyone who could give silver or bronze brought it as an offering to the LORD. Everyone who had acacia wood brought it for all the work.

25All the skilled women spun with their hands and brought what they had spun: blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine linen. 26All the women whose hearts moved them spun the goat hair. 27The leaders brought onyx stones and stones to be set in the ephod and the breastpiece. 28They also brought spices and olive oil for the light, for the anointing oil, and for the fragrant incense. 29So all the Israelite men and women whose hearts moved them brought freewill offerings for all the work the LORD had commanded through Moses.

Bezalel and Oholiab Are Chosen

30Moses said, “See, the LORD has chosen Bezalel son of Uri, the son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah. 31He has filled him with the SpiritspiritSpirit of God, with wisdom, understanding, and knowledge, and with all kinds of skill— 32to make artistic designs for work in gold, silver, and bronze; 33to cut and set stones; to carve wood; and to do all kinds of skilled work. 34He has also given him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, the ability to teach others. 35He has filled them with skill to do all kinds of work: engraving, designing, and embroidering with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen, and weaving. They will do all kinds of work and carry out every design.”
