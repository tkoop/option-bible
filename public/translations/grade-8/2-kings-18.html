Hezekiah Becomes King of Judah

1In the third year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, Hezekiah son of Ahaz became king of Judah. 2He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-nine years. His mother was Abi, the daughter of Zechariah. 3He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as King David had done. 4He removed the high places, smashed the stone pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles. He broke the bronze snake Moses had made into pieces, because the Israelites had been burning incense to it. People called it Nehushtan.

5Hezekiah trusted in the LORD, the God of Israel. No king of Judah before or after him was like him. 6He held tightly to the LORD and did not turn away from HimhimHim. He kept the commands the LORD had given through Moses. 7The LORD was with him, and he succeeded in everything he did. He rebelled against the king of Assyria and refused to serve him. 8He defeated the Philistines all the way to Gaza and the land around it, from watchtower to fortified city.

Samaria Falls to Assyria

9In the fourth year of King Hezekiah, which was the seventh year of Hoshea son of Elah, king of Israel, Shalmaneser king of Assyria attacked Samaria and surrounded it. 10After three years they captured it. In the sixth year of Hezekiah, which was the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel, Samaria was taken. 11The king of Assyria carried the people of Israel away to Assyria and settled them in Halah, in Gozan on the Habor River, and in the towns of the Medes. 12This happened because they did not obey the LORD their God. They broke HishisHis covenant and did not do everything Moses, the servant of the LORD, had commanded. They did not listen or obey.

Sennacherib Invades Judah

13In the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib king of Assyria attacked all the fortified cities of Judah and captured them. 14So Hezekiah king of Judah sent a message to the king of Assyria at Lachish. He said, "I have done wrong. Withdraw from me, and I will pay whatever you demand." The king of Assyria demanded three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold from Hezekiah king of Judah. 15Hezekiah gave him all the silver found in the temple of the LORD and in the king's storehouses. 16At that time Hezekiah king of Judah stripped the gold from the doors and doorposts of the temple of the LORD, which he had covered with gold, and gave it to the king of Assyria.

The Assyrian Army Threatens Jerusalem

17The king of Assyria sent his top commander, his chief officer, and his field commander with a large army from Lachish to King Hezekiah in Jerusalem. They marched up to Jerusalem and stopped at the water channel of the Upper PoolpoolPool, on the road to the Washerman's FieldfieldField. 18They called for the king. Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the secretary, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, went out to meet them.

19The field commander said to them, "Tell Hezekiah: 'This is what the great king, the king of Assyria, says: What gives you this confidence? 20You think you have plans and strength for war, but you are only relying on empty words. Who are you depending on, that you rebel against me? 21Look, you are depending on Egypt, that broken reed of a staff. It will pierce the hand of anyone who leans on it. That is what Pharaoh king of Egypt is to all who depend on him. 22But if you say to me, 'We are depending on the LORD our God,' isn't this the God whose high places and altars Hezekiah removed? Didn't Hezekiah tell Judah and Jerusalem, 'YouyouYou must worship before this altar in Jerusalem'? 23Come now, make a deal with my master, the king of Assyria. I will give you two thousand horses, if you can put riders on them! 24How then can you push back one officer of the least of my master's officials, even though you are depending on Egypt for chariots and horses? 25Besides, do you think I came to attack and destroy this place without the LORD's permission? The LORD HimselfhimselfHimself told me to march against this country and destroy it.'"

26Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebna, and Joah said to the field commander, "Please speak to your servants in Aramaic, since we understand it. Do not speak to us in Hebrew where the people on the wall can hear."

27But the commander replied, "Did my master send me to say these things only to your master and you? Didn't he also send me to the people sitting on the wall? They, like you, will have to eat their own waste and drink their own urine."

28Then the commander stood and called out in a loud voice in Hebrew, "Hear the word of the great king, the king of Assyria! 29The king says: Do not let Hezekiah trick you. He cannot rescue you from my hand. 30Do not let Hezekiah talk you into trusting the LORD when he says, 'ThetheThe LORD will surely save us; this city will not be handed over to the king of Assyria.' 31Do not listen to Hezekiah. This is what the king of Assyria says: Make peace with me and come out to me. Then each of you will eat from your own vine and fig tree and drink water from your own well. 32I will come and take you to a land like your own—a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of olive oil and honey. Live, and do not die! Do not listen to Hezekiah, for he is misleading you when he says, 'ThetheThe LORD will rescue us.' 33Did the god of any nation save his land from the king of Assyria? 34Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? Did any of them save Samaria from my hand? 35Which of all the gods of these lands has saved his land from me? How then can the LORD save Jerusalem from my hand?"

36But the people stayed silent and said nothing, because the king had commanded, "Do not answer him." 37Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the secretary, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, went to Hezekiah with their clothes torn and told him everything the field commander had said.
