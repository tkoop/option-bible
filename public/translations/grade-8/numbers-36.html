Leaders from Gilead Ask About Inheritance

1The family leaders from the clan of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh, one of Joseph’s family groups, came to Moses and to the leaders of Israel. These leaders were the heads of the Israelite families.

2“The LORD told my lord to give the land to the Israelites by lot as their inheritance. My lord was also told by the LORD to give our brother Zelophehad’s inheritance to his daughters. 3But if they marry men from other Israelite tribes, their inheritance will be taken from our fathers’ inheritance and added to the inheritance of the tribe they join. Then part of our share of the land would be lost. 4When the Jubilee year comes for the Israelites, their inheritance will be added to the tribe they join and taken from our fathers’ tribe.”

Moses Gives the LORD’s Command

5So, at the LORD’s command, Moses gave this order to the Israelites: “The tribe of Joseph’s descendants is right. 6This is what the LORD has commanded about Zelophehad’s daughters: They may marry anyone they choose, as long as they marry within their father’s tribe. 7No inheritance in Israel may pass from one tribe to another, because each Israelite must keep the land of his fathers’ tribe. 8Also, every daughter who inherits land in any Israelite tribe must marry within her father’s tribe, so each Israelite keeps the land passed down from his fathers. 9No inheritance may move from one tribe to another, because each Israelite tribe must keep its own land.”

Zelophehad’s Daughters Obey

10So Zelophehad’s daughters did what the LORD commanded through Moses. 11Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married their cousins. 12They married into the family of Manasseh son of Joseph, so their inheritance stayed with their father’s tribe.

Final Commands on the Plains of Moab

13These are the commands and rules the LORD gave the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab beside the Jordan River near Jericho.
