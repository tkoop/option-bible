Warning to the Rich

1Come now, you rich people! Weep and cry out because of the suffering that is coming on you. 2Your wealth has rotted, and moths have eaten your clothes. 3Your gold and silver have rusted, and that rust will be a witness against you. It will eat your flesh like fire. You have stored up treasure in these last days. 4Look! The pay you kept back from the workers who harvested your fields is crying out against you. The cries of those harvesters have reached the ears of the Lord of Hosts. 5You have lived in luxury on the earth and chased pleasure. You have pampered your hearts for the day of slaughter. 6You have condemned and murdered the righteous person, and he does not fight back against you.

Be Patient and Speak Honestly

7So be patient, brothers, until the Lord comes. Look at the farmer who waits for the valuable crop from the earth. He is patient with it until it gets the early and late rains. 8You too must be patient. Make your hearts strong, because the Lord's coming is near. 9Don't complain about each other, brothers, so that you won't be judged. Look! The JudgejudgeJudge is standing at the door. 10Brothers, take the prophets who spoke in the Lord's name as an example of suffering and patience. 11We say that those who endured are blessed. You have heard about Job's endurance, and you have seen what the Lord finally did, because the Lord is full of compassion and mercy. 12Above all, my brothers, do not swear, not by Heavenheavenheaven, not by EarthEarthearth, and not by any other oath. Let your "Yes" be yes and your "No" be no, so that you won't fall under judgmentjudgementjudgment.

Prayer and Healing

13Is anyone among you suffering? He should pray. Is anyone cheerful? He should sing praises. 14Is anyone among you sick? He should call for the elders of the church, and they should pray over him. They should anoint him with oil in the Lord's name. 15The prayer of faith will save the sick person, and the Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven. 16So confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. A righteous person's prayer is powerful and effective. 17Elijah was an ordinary man like us. He prayed hard that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the earth for three and a half years. 18Then he prayed again, and the sky gave rain. The EarthEarthearth produced its crops.

Restore a Wandering Brother

19My brothers, if someone among you wanders from the truth and someone brings him back, 20let that person know that whoever turns a sinner from his wrong path will save his soul from death and cover many sins.
