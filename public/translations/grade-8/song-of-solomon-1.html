The Song of Songs

1The SongsongSong of SongssongsSongs, which is Solomon's.

The Bride Longs for Her Beloved

2"Let him kiss me with kisses from his mouth, for your love is better than wine. 3Because of the fragrance of your perfumes, your name is like perfume poured out; that is why the young women love you. 4Draw me after you, and let us run. The king has brought me into his rooms. We will rejoice and be glad in you. We will remember your love more than wine. They are right to love you."

5"I am dark but lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar, like Solomon's curtains. 6Do not stare at me because I am dark, because the sun has darkened my skin. My mother's sons were angry with me. They made me guard the vineyards, but I did not guard my own vineyard. 7Tell me, you whom I love, where you feed your flock, where you let it rest at noon. Why should I be like a woman who covers her face beside the flocks of your friends?"

The Bridegroom Praises His Bride

8"If you do not know, most beautiful of women, follow the tracks of the flock and graze your young goats beside the shepherds' tents. 9I compare you, my darling, to a mare among Pharaoh's chariots. 10Your cheeks are lovely with ornaments, your neck with strings of jewels. 11We will make you chains of gold with beads of silver."

The Bride and Bridegroom Praise Each Other

12"While the king was at his table, my perfume gave off its fragrance. 13My beloved is to me a small bag of myrrh that lies between my breasts. 14My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms in the vineyards of En-gedi."

15"Look, you are beautiful, my darling; look, you are beautiful; your eyes are like doves."

16"Look, you are handsome, my beloved; yes, you are pleasant. Our couch is green. 17The beams of our house are cedars; our rafters are firs."
