The Bride and Bridegroom

1"I am a flower of Sharon, a lily of the valleys."

2"Like a lily among thorns, so is my darling among the young women."

3"Like an apple tree among the trees of the forest, so is my beloved among the young men. 4He has brought me to the banquet hall, and his love is like a banner over me."

5"Strengthen me with raisin cakes, refresh me with apples, for I am lovesick." 6His left arm is under my head, and his right arm embraces me.

7"Daughters of Jerusalem, I make you promise by the gazelles or the wild does: do not stir up or awaken love until it pleases."

The Bridegroom Approaches

8"Listen! My beloved! Look, he comes, leaping over the mountains, bounding over the hills." 9"My beloved is like a gazelle or a young stag. Look, he stands behind our wall, gazing through the windows, peering through the lattice."

The Bridegroom Invites His Bride

10My beloved spoke and said to me, "Arise, my darling, and come away with me, my beautiful one. 11For winter is past; the rain is over and gone. 12Flowers appear in the land; the time of singing has come, and the turtledove's voice is heard in our land. 13The fig tree is ripening its early fruit, and the grapevines are blossoming and giving off their fragrance. Arise, come away with me, my darling; come with me, my beautiful one.

14"My dove in the clefts of the rock, in the hiding places of the mountainside, let me see your face, let me hear your voice; for your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely."

15"Catch the foxes for us—the little foxes that ruin the vineyards, while our vineyards are in bloom."

The Bride's Devotion

16"My beloved is mine, and I am his; he tends his flock among the lilies." 17"Until the evening breeze blows and the shadows flee, turn, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young stag on the mountains of Bether."
