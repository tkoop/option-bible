Ezra Reads the Law to the People

1All the people gathered as one man in the square in front of the Water GategateGate. They asked Ezra the scribe to bring out the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses, which the LORD had commanded for Israel. 2So on the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the LawlawLaw before the men and women and everyone else who could understand. 3He read from it from early morning until noon in the square in front of the Water GategateGate, to the men, women, and those who could understand. All the people listened closely to the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw.

4Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform made for this. Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah stood on his right. Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam stood on his left. 5Ezra opened the book where all the people could see it, because he was above them. When he opened it, all the people stood up.

6Ezra praised the LORD, the great God, and all the people answered, “Amen, AmenamenAmen,” as they raised their hands. Then they bowed down and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.

7The Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the other Levites—helped the people understand the LawlawLaw while the people stayed in their places. 8They read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of God clearly. They explained it so the people could understand what was read.

The People Weep and Rejoice

9Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who were teaching the people said to all the people, “This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not mourn or weep.” All the people were weeping because they heard the words of the LawlawLaw.

10Then he said to them, “Go, eat rich food, drink something sweet, and send portions to those who have nothing ready. This day is holy to our Lord. Do not be sad, for the joy of the LORD is your strength.”

11So the Levites calmed all the people. They said, “Be still, for the day is holy. Do not be sad.”

12Then all the people went to eat and drink, to send portions, and to celebrate with great joy, because they understood the words that had been explained to them.

The People Celebrate the Feast of Booths

13On the second day, the heads of all the families, along with the priests and Levites, gathered around Ezra the scribe to study the words of the LawlawLaw. 14They found written in the LawlawLaw that the LORD had commanded through Moses that the Israelites should live in booths during the feast in the seventh month.

15They also found that they should make this known and spread it through all their cities and in Jerusalem: “Go out to the hills and bring branches of olive, wild olive, myrtle, palm, and other leafy trees to make booths, as it is written.”

16So the people went out and brought them. They made booths for themselves, each on his own roof, in their courtyards, in the courts of the house of God, and in the squares by the Water GategateGate and the GategateGate of Ephraim. 17The whole group of those who had returned from exile made booths and lived in them. From the days of Joshua son of Nun until that day, the Israelites had not celebrated like this. There was very great rejoicing. 18Day by day, from the first day to the last, Ezra read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of God. They celebrated the feast for seven days, and on the eighth day there was a solemn assembly, as required by the LawlawLaw.
