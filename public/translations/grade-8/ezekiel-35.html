A Warning to Mount Seir

1The word of the the LORD came to me.

2"Son of man, oppose Mount Seir and prophesy against it. 3Say to it, 'This is what the Lord the the LORD says: I am against you, Mount Seir. I will punish you and make you a wasteland and a ruin. 4I will destroy your cities, and you will become a wasteland. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.

Edom's Ancient Hatred

5"'Because you have hated Israel for a long time and handed the Israelites over to the sword when they were in trouble, at the time of their final punishment, 6as surely as I live, declares the Lord the the LORD, I will hand you over to bloodshed, and bloodshed will follow you. Since you did not hate bloodshed, bloodshed will follow you.

Mount Seir Will Be Emptied

7"'I will make Mount Seir a wasteland and a ruin, and I will remove everyone who travels through it. 8I will fill its mountains with the dead. Those killed by the sword will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your ravines. 9I will make you a lasting ruin, and your cities will not be lived in. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.

The LORD Heard Edom's Insults

10"'Because you said, "These two nations and these two lands will be mine, and I will take them over," even though the the LORD was there, 11as surely as I live, declares the Lord the the LORD, I will treat you the way your anger and jealousy deserve, which you showed in your hatred of them. I will make MyselfmyselfMyself known among them when I judge you. 12Then you will know that I, the the LORD, have heard all the insults you spoke against the mountains of Israel. You said, "They are ruined and given to us to destroy." 13You bragged against MemeMe with your mouth and piled up your words against MemeMe. I heard it.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment When the World Rejoices

14"'This is what the Lord the the LORD says: When the whole world rejoices, I will make you a wasteland. 15Just as you rejoiced over Israel's inheritance because it was ruined, I will do the same to you. You will become a wasteland, Mount Seir, and all of Edom. Then they will know that I am the the LORD.'"
