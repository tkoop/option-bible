Woe to the Proud Crown of Ephraim

1Woe to the proud crown of Ephraim! Its fading flower of glory sits on the head of a rich valley, but its people are beaten down by wine. 2Look, the Lord has a strong and mighty one. He comes like a hailstorm and a destroying wind, like a flood of mighty waters, and HeheHe will throw that crown down to the earth. 3The proud crown of Ephraim will be trampled underfoot. 4Its fading flower of glory, on the head of the rich valley, will be like a ripe fig picked before summer. As soon as someone sees it, he swallows it.

The Lord Will Be a Crown for His People

5In that day the LORD of Hosts will be a crown of glory and a beautiful wreath for the rest of HishisHis people. 6He will give a spirit of justice to the one who sits in judgmentjudgementjudgment, and strength to those who turn back the attack at the gate. 7But even these stagger with wine and strong drink. Priest and prophet stagger with strong drink; they are swallowed up by wine and stagger with strong drink. They make mistakes in their visions and stumble in their judgmentsjudgementsjudgments. 8All their tables are covered with vomit and filth, so there is no clean place.

9They ask, "Who is he trying to teach? Who can understand his message? Is it babies just taken from milk, or little ones taken from the breast?" 10They say it is rule after rule, rule after rule, line after line, line after line, a little here, a little there.

11So the LORD will speak to this people through people with strange lips and a foreign tongue. 12He told them, "This is rest; let the weary rest. This is a quiet place." But they would not listen. 13So the word of the LORD will come to them as rule after rule, rule after rule, line after line, line after line, a little here, a little there. They will go and fall backward, be broken, be trapped, and be caught.

A Warning to Jerusalem's Rulers

14So listen to the word of the LORD, you mockers who rule this people in Jerusalem. 15You say, "We made a deal with death, and we have an agreement with the grave. When the destroying flood passes through, it will not reach us, because we made lies our shelter and hid behind falsehood."

A Sure Foundation in Zion

16So the Lord, the LORD, says: "Look, I am placing a stone in Zion. It is a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation. Whoever trusts in it will not be shaken. 17I will use justice as a measuring line and righteousness as a plumb line. Hail will sweep away the shelter of lies, and water will flood the hiding place. 18Your deal with death will be cancelledcancelledcanceled, and your agreement with the grave will not stand. When the destroying flood passes through, you will be trampled by it. 19As often as it passes through, it will take you. Morning after morning it will pass through, day and night. Understanding the message will bring sheer terror. 20The bed is too short to stretch out on, and the blanket is too narrow to wrap up in."

21The LORD will rise up like HeheHe did at Mount Perazim. He will be angry like HeheHe was in the ValleyvalleyValley of Gibeon. He will do HishisHis strange work, HishisHis unusual task. 22So now do not mock, or your chains will become stronger. I have heard a decision of destruction from the Lord, the LORD of Hosts, against the whole EarthEarthearth.

The Farmer Knows How to Work

23Listen and hear my voice; pay attention and hear my words. 24Does a farmer plow all day just to plant? Does he keep turning and breaking up his soil? 25When he has levelledlevelledleveled the ground, does he not scatter dill and sow cumin? Does he not plant wheat in rows, barley in its place, and spelt along the edge? 26His God teaches him the right way and instructs him. 27Dill is not threshed with a heavy threshing board, and a cart wheel is not rolled over cumin. Dill is beaten out with a stick, and cumin with a rod. 28Grain is crushed for bread, but not forever. He drives his cart and horses over it, but he does not destroy it. 29This too comes from the LORD of Hosts, who is wonderful in counsel and great in wisdom.
