Wisdom for Daily Life

1Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring. 2Let someone else praise you, and not your own mouth; let a stranger praise you, and not your own lips. 3A stone is heavy, and sand is a burden, but a fool's anger is heavier than both. 4Anger is cruel, and fury is like a flood, but who can stand up to jealousy?

Friends, Family, and Counsel

5Open correction is better than love that stays hidden. 6The wounds of a true friend can be trusted, but an enemy's kisses are deceitful. 7A full stomach turns away from honey, but to a hungry person even bitter food tastes sweet. 8Like a bird that wanders from its nest, so is a person who wanders from home. 9Oil and perfume make the heart glad, and a friend's honest advice brings sweetness to the soul. 10Do not abandon your friend or your father's friend, and do not go to your brother's house on the day of your trouble; a nearby neighbourneighbourneighbor is better than a brother far away.

11Be wise, my son, and make my heart glad, so I can answer the one who insults me. 12A careful person sees danger and hides, but foolish people keep going and suffer. 13Take the clothes of someone who promises to pay for a stranger's debt; hold them as security if he does it for a wayward woman. 14If someone loudly blesses a neighbourneighbourneighbor early in the morning, it will be taken as a curse.

15A quarrelsome wife is like a steady drip on a rainy day. 16Trying to stop her is like trying to stop the wind, or like holding oil in your right hand. 17As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another. 18Whoever takes care of a fig tree will eat its fruit, and whoever guards his master will be honouredhonouredhonored.

Work, Wealth, and Diligence

19As water shows a face, so a person's heart shows the person. 20The grave and destruction are never satisfied, and human eyes are never satisfied. 21A crucible tests silver, and a furnace tests gold, but praise tests a person. 22Even if you grind a fool in a mortar with a pestle along with grain, his foolishness will not leave him.

23Be sure you know how your flocks are doing, and pay attention to your herds. 24Riches do not last forever, and a crown does not pass to every generation. 25When the grass is gone and new growth appears, and the plants on the hills are gathered, 26the lambs will give you clothing, and the goats will give you the price of a field. 27You will have plenty of goats' milk to feed your family and to nourish your servant girls.
