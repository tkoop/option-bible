The Fifth Trumpet and the Locusts

1When the fifth angel blew his trumpet, I saw a star that had fallen from Heavenheavenheaven to EarthEarthearth. The star was given the key to the shaft of the bottomless pit. 2He opened the pit, and smoke rose from it like smoke from a huge furnace. The sun and the air grew dark because of the smoke. 3Locusts came out of the smoke and went down to the earth. They were given power like the power of scorpions on the earth. 4They were told not to harm the grass, the green plants, or the trees. They could hurt only people who did not have God's seal on their foreheads. 5They were not allowed to kill those people. Instead, they were to torment them for five months. Their torment was like the sting of a scorpion. 6In those days, people will look for death but will not find it. They will long to die, but death will run away from them.

7The locusts looked like horses ready for battle. They seemed to wear crowns that looked like gold on their heads, and their faces looked like human faces. 8Their hair looked like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth. 9They had chest armourarmourarmor that looked like iron. The sound of their wings was like the sound of many horses and chariots rushing into battle. 10They had tails with stings like scorpions. Their tails had the power to hurt people for five months. 11They had a king over them: the angel of the bottomless pit. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek it is Apollyon. 12The first woe has passed. Look, two more woes are still coming.

The Sixth Trumpet and a Huge Army

13The sixth angel blew his trumpet. I heard a voice come from the four horns of the golden altar that stands before God. 14The voice said to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, "Release the four angels who are tied up at the great river Euphrates." 15So the four angels were released. They had been kept ready for that hour, day, month, and year. They were released to kill one-third of all people. 16I heard the number of the horsemen in the armies: two hundred million.

17In the vision, I saw the horses and their riders. They wore chest armourarmourarmor that was red like fire, blue, and yellow like sulphursulphursulfur. The horses' heads looked like lions' heads. Fire, smoke, and sulphursulphursulfur came out of their mouths. 18One-third of all people were killed by these three plagues: the fire, the smoke, and the sulphursulphursulfur that came out of the horses' mouths. 19The power of the horses was in their mouths and in their tails. Their tails were like snakes with heads, and they used them to hurt people.

The Survivors Still Refuse to Repent

20The rest of the people, who were not killed by these plagues, still did not turn from their evil deeds. They did not stop worshiping demons and idols made of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood. Those idols cannot see, hear, or walk. 21They also did not turn from their murders, their magic, their sexual sins, or their thefts.
