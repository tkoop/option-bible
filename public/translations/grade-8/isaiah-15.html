Moab's Ruin and Mourning

1This is a message about Moab. The city of Ar in Moab is ruined—destroyed in one night. The city of Kir in Moab is also ruined—destroyed in one night.

2The people of Dibon go up to their temple to cry. Moab wails at its high places of worship. It wails over Nebo and Medeba. Everyone shaves their head, and every beard is cut off. 3In the streets, people wear sackcloth. On the rooftops and in the squares, everyone wails and weeps.

4Heshbon and Elealeh cry out. Their voices are heard as far as Jahaz. So Moab's soldiers cry out, and their hearts tremble.

My Heart Cries for Moab

5My heart cries out for Moab. Her refugees run as far as Zoar and Eglath-shelishiyah. They go up the road to Luhith, weeping as they go. On the road to Horonaim, they cry out that everything is destroyed.

The Land Dries Up

6The waters of Nimrim are dried up, and the grass is withered. The pasture is dried up, and the green plants are gone. 7So the wealth they saved and stored up, they carry away across the BrookbrookBrook of the Willows.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Reaches Moab

8Their cry of grief has spread around Moab's borders. It reaches Eglaim and Beer-elim. 9The waters of Dimon are full of blood. But I will bring even more trouble on Dimon. I will send a lion against those who escape from Moab and against those left in the land.
