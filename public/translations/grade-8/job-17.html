Job's Spirit Is Broken

1My spirit is broken; my days are running out; the grave waits for me. 2Mockers surround me, and my eyes must stay on their insults.

Job Asks God for Help

3Please put up a pledge for me with YourselfyourselfYourself. Who else will shake hands with me? 4You have hidden their hearts from understanding, so YouyouYou will not lift them up. 5If someone betrays friends for a share of the reward, his children's eyes will grow weak.

Job's Suffering and Others' Reactions

6He has made me a joke to people; I have become someone they laugh at. 7My eyes have grown weak with sorrow; my whole body is only a shadow. 8Good people are shocked by this; innocent people are stirred up against the wicked. 9But good people will stay on their path; those with clean hands will grow stronger. 10But come back, all of you; I won't find a wise person among you.

Job Looks Toward the Grave

11My days have passed; my plans are broken; the desires of my heart are gone. 12They turn night into day and say, "LightlightLight is near," even though it is dark.

13If I hope for the grave as my home and make my bed in darkness, 14I have said to decay, "YouyouYou are my father," and to the worm, "MymyMy mother and my sister."

15So where is my hope? Who can see any hope for me? 16Will my hope go down to the gates of the grave? Will we rest together in the dust?
