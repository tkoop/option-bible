David Becomes King over All Israel

1All Israel gathered around David at Hebron and said, "Look, we are your own people. 2Even when Saul was king, you led Israel out and brought them back. The LORD your God said to you, 'YouyouYou will shepherd MymyMy people Israel, and you will be ruler over MymyMy people Israel.'" 3So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron. David made a covenant with them there before the LORD, and they anointed David king over Israel, just as the LORD had promised through Samuel.

David Captures Jerusalem

4David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which was Jebus, where the Jebusites lived. 5The Jebusites said to David, "YouyouYou will not come in here." But David captured the stronghold of Zion, which is the CitycityCity of David. 6David had said, "Whoever strikes the Jebusites first will become chief and commander." Joab son of Zeruiah went up first, so he became chief. 7David lived in the stronghold, so they called it the CitycityCity of David. 8He built up the city all around, from the Millo to the surrounding wall, and Joab rebuilt the rest of the city. 9David became greater and greater, and the LORD of Hosts was with him.

David's Mighty Warriors

The people fled from the Philistines. 14But they took their stand in the middle of the field, defended it, and struck down the Philistines. The LORD saved them with a great victory.

15Three of the thirty chiefs went down to David at the rock, to the cave of Adullam, while the Philistine army camped in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim. 16David was in the stronghold then, and the Philistine camp was in Bethlehem. 17David longed for water and said, "Oh, that someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem by the gate!" 18So the three broke through the Philistine camp, drew water from the well of Bethlehem by the gate, carried it back, and gave it to David. But David would not drink it. Instead, he poured it out to the LORD. 19He said, "I would never do that, my God. I cannot drink water that cost these men their lives. They brought it at the risk of their lives." So he would not drink it. These were the deeds of the three mighty men.
