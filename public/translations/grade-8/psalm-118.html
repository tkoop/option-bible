Give Thanks to the LORD

1Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis faithful love endures forever. 2Let Israel say, "HishisHis faithful love endures forever." 3Let the house of Aaron say, "HishisHis faithful love endures forever." 4Let those who fear the LORD say, "HishisHis faithful love endures forever."

The LORD Answers in Trouble

5When I was in trouble, I cried out to the LORD, and HeheHe answered me and set me free. 6The LORD is with me; I will not be afraid. What can a person do to me? 7The LORD is with me; HeheHe is my helper. I will look in triumph on those who hate me. 8It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in people. 9It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in human leaders.

Surrounded by Enemies

10All nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I defeated them. 11They surrounded me on every side, but in the name of the LORD I defeated them. 12They swarmed around me like bees; they burned out like thorns in a fire, but in the name of the LORD I defeated them. 13I was pushed hard, and I was about to fall, but the LORD helped me. 14The LORD is my strength and my song, and HeheHe has become my salvation.

Joy in the LORD's Victory

15Shouts of joy and victory are in the tents of those who do right: "The right hand of the LORD does mighty things!" 16The right hand of the LORD is lifted high; the right hand of the LORD does mighty things! 17I will not die but live, and I will tell what the LORD has done. 18The LORD has disciplined me severely, but HeheHe has not handed me over to death.

Entering the LORD's Gates

19Open the gates of righteousness to me, so I can enter through them and thank the LORD. 20This is the gate of the LORD; those who do right will enter through it. 21I will thank YouyouYou, for YouyouYou answered me and became my salvation. 22The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. 23This came from the LORD, and it is amazing in our eyes. 24This is the day the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.

Blessed Is He Who Comes

25O the LORD, save us, we pray; O the LORD, grant us success, we pray. 26Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the LORD. From the house of the LORD we bless you. 27The LORD is God, and HeheHe has made HishisHis light shine on us. Tie the festival sacrifice with cords to the horns of the altar. 28You are my God, and I will thank YouyouYou; YouyouYou are my God, I will praise YouyouYou. 29Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis faithful love endures forever.
