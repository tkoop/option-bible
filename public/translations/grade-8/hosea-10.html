Israel’s Empty Vine

1Israel was like a vine that produced fruit for itself. The more fruit it had, the more altars it built. The better its land was, the better its sacred pillars were. 2Their hearts are divided, so now they are guilty. He will break down their altars and destroy their sacred pillars.

No King to Save Them

3Now they will say, “We have no king because we did not fear the LORD. What can a king do for us?” 4They speak empty words and make false promises. They make agreements, but justice grows like a poisonous weed in the rows of a plowed field.

The Calf at Beth-aven

5The people of Samaria are afraid for the calf idol at Beth-aven. Its people mourn for it, and its priests rejoice over its glory, because it has been taken from them. 6It will be carried to Assyria as a gift for the great king. Ephraim will be put to shame, and Israel will be ashamed of its plan. 7Samaria’s king will be destroyed like foam on the water.

8The sinful shrines of Aven will be destroyed—the sin of Israel. Thorns and thistles will grow on their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall on us!” 9Israel, you have sinned since the days of Gibeah. You have stood your ground there. Will war at Gibeah not catch up with these wicked people? 10When I choose, I will punish them. Nations will gather against them and put them in chains for their double sin.

Plowing Wickedness, Reaping JudgmentJudgementJudgment

11Ephraim is like a trained young cow that loves to thresh grain. But I put a yoke on her fair neck. I will make Ephraim pull a load; Judah will plow; Jacob will break up the hard ground. 12Sow righteousness for yourselves. Reap faithful love. Break up your unplowed ground, for it is time to seek the LORD, until HeheHe comes and sends righteousness on you like rain. 13You have plowed wickedness and reaped injustice. You have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you trusted in your own way and in your many warriors, 14a storm will rise among your people, and all your forts will be destroyed. It will be like when Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel in battle—when mothers were killed along with their children. 15So Bethel will do to you because of your great wickedness. At dawn the king of Israel will be completely destroyed.
