God's Glory in the Sky

For the director of music. A psalm of David. 1The heavens tell about God's glory, and the sky shows the work of HishisHis hands. 2Day after day they pour out speech; night after night they show knowledge. 3They have no speech and use no words. No sound is heard from them. 4Yet their voice goes out through all the earth, and their words reach the ends of the world. In the heavens God has set a tent for the sun.

5The sun is like a bridegroom coming out of his room. It runs its path with joy, like a strong man. 6It rises from one end of the sky and travels to the other. Nothing can hide from its heat.

God's Perfect Law

7The law of the the LORD is perfect. It gives new life to the soul. The the LORD's testimony is trustworthy. It makes the simple wise. 8The the LORD's rules are right. They bring joy to the heart. The the LORD's command is pure. It gives light to the eyes. 9Respect for the the LORD is pure. It lasts forever. The the LORD's decisions are true and right in every way.

10They are more desirable than gold, even more than much pure gold. They are sweeter than honey, even sweeter than honey from the comb. 11By them YouryourYour servant is warned. Keeping them brings a great reward.

A Prayer for Forgiveness

12Who can notice his own mistakes? Clear me of hidden faults. 13Keep YouryourYour servant from wilfulwilfulwillful sins. Do not let them rule over me. Then I will be blameless and free from great sin. 14May the words of my mouth and the thoughts of my heart be pleasing to YouyouYou, O the LORD, my RockrockRock and my Redeemer.
