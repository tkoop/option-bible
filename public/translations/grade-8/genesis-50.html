Joseph Buries His Father

1Joseph fell on his father's face, wept over him, and kissed him. 2Joseph commanded his servants, the physicians, to embalm his father Israel, and the physicians embalmed him. 3The embalming took forty days, for that is the full time for embalming. The Egyptians mourned him for seventy days.

4When the days of mourning had passed, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh's household and said, "If I have found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, please tell Pharaoh, 5'My father made me swear, "Look, I am about to die. Bury me in the grave I dug for myself in the land of Canaan." Now let me go up and bury my father, and I will return.'"

6Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, just as he made you swear."

7So Joseph went up to bury his father. All of Pharaoh's servants went with him, along with the elders of his household and all the elders of Egypt. 8Joseph's whole household, his brothers, and his father's household went too. Only their children, flocks, and herds stayed in Goshen. 9Chariots and horsemen also went up with him. It was a very large group.

10They came to the threshing floor of Atad, beyond the Jordan, and mourned there with a very great and heavy lament. Joseph mourned for his father seven days. 11When the Canaanites who lived there saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad, they said, "This is a heavy mourning for the Egyptians." So that place was named Abel-mizraim, beyond the Jordan. 12Jacob's sons did for him exactly as he had commanded. 13They carried him to Canaan and buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field near Mamre. Abraham had bought that field from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place. 14After burying his father, Joseph returned to Egypt with his brothers and everyone who had gone with him.

Joseph Reassures His Brothers

15When Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, "What if Joseph holds a grudge against us and pays us back for all the wrong we did to him?" 16So they sent a message to Joseph, saying, "YouryourYour father gave this command before he died: 17'Please forgive your brothers' wrongdoing and sin, because they treated you badly. Now please forgive the wrongdoing of the servants of your father's God.'" When Joseph heard this, he wept. 18Then his brothers came, fell down before him, and said, "We are your servants."

19Joseph said to them, "Don't be afraid. Am I in the place of God?" 20"You planned evil against me, but God planned it for good, to keep many people alive, as HeheHe is doing today." 21"So don't be afraid. I will provide for you and your children." He comforted them and spoke kindly to them.

Joseph's Final Days

22Joseph stayed in Egypt with his father's household. He lived 110 years. 23Joseph saw Ephraim's children to the third generation, and the children of Machir, Manasseh's son, were born while Joseph was still alive. 24Joseph said to his brothers, "I am about to die, but God will surely come to help you. He will bring you out of this land and take you to the land HeheHe promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob." 25Then Joseph made the sons of Israel swear an oath, saying, "God will surely come to help you, and you must carry my bones up from here." 26Joseph died at 110 years old. They embalmed him and placed him in a coffin in Egypt.
