God Calls Jacob to Bethel

1God said to Jacob, "Get up, go up to Bethel, and live there. Make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau."

2Jacob said to his household and to everyone with him, "Put away the foreign gods among you, make yourselves clean, and change your clothes. 3Let's get up and go up to Bethel. I will make an altar there to the God who answered me on the day I was in trouble and stayed with me on the road I travelledtravelledtraveled."

4So they gave Jacob all the foreign gods they had and the earrings in their ears. Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem. 5They travelledtravelledtraveled on, and God put fear into the cities around them, so no one chased Jacob's sons. 6Jacob and all the people with him came to Luz, which is Bethel, in the land of Canaan. 7There he built an altar and named the place El Bethel, because God had shown HimselfhimselfHimself to him there when he fled from his brother Esau. 8Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died. She was buried under the oak below Bethel, and Jacob named it Allon Bacuth.

God Renews His Promise

9When Jacob came back from Paddan-aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.

10God said to him, "YouryourYour name is Jacob. You will not be called Jacob anymore. Your name will be Israel." Then HeheHe called him Israel.

11God also said, "I am God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation and a group of nations will come from you, and kings will come from your descendants. 12The land I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give it to your descendants after you."

13God went up from him in the place where HeheHe had spoken to him. 14Jacob set up a stone pillar there. He poured a drink offering and oil on it. 15Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

Rachel Dies on the Road

16They left Bethel. While they were still a little way from Ephrath, Rachel began to give birth, and she had a hard labourlabourlabor. 17When the labourlabourlabor was hardest, the midwife said to her, "Do not be afraid, for this is another son for you." 18As Rachel was dying, she named her son Ben-oni, but his father named him Benjamin.

19Rachel died and was buried on the road to Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem. 20Jacob set up a pillar over her grave. It marks Rachel's tomb to this day. 21Israel moved on and set up his tent beyond Migdal Eder. 22While Israel lived in that land, Reuben went in and slept with Bilhah, his father's concubine. Israel heard about it. Jacob now had twelve sons.

Jacob's Sons and Isaac's Death

23The sons of Leah: Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. 24The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin. 25The sons of Bilhah, Rachel's servant: Dan and Naphtali. 26The sons of Zilpah, Leah's servant: Gad and Asher. These were the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-aram.

27Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath Arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had lived. 28Isaac lived 180 years. 29Isaac took his last breath and died. He was old and had lived a full life. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
