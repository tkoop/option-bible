Paul’s Desire for Israel

1Brothers, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for them is that they may be saved. 2I can testify that they have a strong zeal for God, but it is not based on true knowledge.

3Because they did not know God’s righteousness, and they tried to set up their own righteousness, they did not submit to God’s righteousness. 4Christ is the end of the law, bringing righteousness to everyone who believes.

Righteousness by Faith

5Moses writes about the righteousness that comes from the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.”

6But the righteousness that comes from faith says this: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will go up to Heavenheavenheaven?’” (That is, to bring Christ down.) 7“Or, ‘Who will go down into the abyss?’” (That is, to bring Christ up from the dead.)

8But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart.” (That is, the word of faith we are preaching.)

9If you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised HimhimHim from the dead, you will be saved. 10For with the heart a person believes and is made right with God, and with the mouth a person confesses and is saved.

11For the ScripturescriptureScripture says, “Everyone who believes in HimhimHim will not be put to shame.”

The Gospel for Everyone

12For there is no difference between Jew and Greek. The same Lord is Lord of all, and HeheHe richly blesses all who call on HimhimHim. 13For, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”

14But how can they call on HimhimHim if they have not believed in HimhimHim? And how can they believe if they have not heard about HimhimHim? And how can they hear without someone preaching? 15And how can anyone preach unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!”

Israel’s Unbelief

16But not all of them obeyed the good news. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our message?” 17So faith comes from hearing, and hearing comes through the word of Christ.

18But I ask, did they not hear? Yes, they did: “Their voice has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.”

19But I ask, did Israel not know? First Moses says, “I will make you jealous with those who are not a nation; I will make you angry with a nation that has no understanding.”

20And Isaiah boldly says, “I was found by those who were not looking for MemeMe; I showed MyselfmyselfMyself to those who did not ask for MemeMe.”
