The Holy Portion of the Land

1When you divide the land by lot as an inheritance, you are to set aside a holy portion for the LORD. It will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. This whole area will be holy. 2Inside it, there will be a square space for the holy place, 500 cubits by 500 cubits, with 50 cubits of open land around it. 3From this measured area, you are to measure off 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. The holy place, the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace, will be there.

4This holy portion of the land will belong to the priests who serve in the holy place and come near to serve the LORD. It will be a place for their houses and a holy area for the holy place. 5The Levites who serve in the temple will receive an area 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide. It will be their land to live in, with twenty rooms. 6You are to give the city an area 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, next to the holy district. It will belong to all the people of Israel.

Land for the Prince

7The prince will have land on both sides of the holy district and the city's land. It will stretch west from the west side and east from the east side, running from the western border to the eastern border. 8This land will be his property in Israel. My princes will no longer mistreat MymyMy people. They will give the rest of the land to the people of Israel by their tribes.

Fair Rules and Offerings

9This is what the Lord the LORD says: “Enough, princes of Israel! Stop your violence and robbery. Do what is fair and right. Stop taking land from MymyMy people,” declares the Lord the LORD. 10You must use honest scales, a fair ephah, and a fair bath. 11The ephah and the bath must be the same size. A bath will hold one-tenth of a homer, and an ephah will hold one-tenth of a homer. So a homer will be the standard measure. 12A shekel will be twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels plus twenty-five shekels plus fifteen shekels will equal one mina. 13This is the offering you are to give: one-sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat, and one-sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley. 14The set amount of oil will be one-tenth of a bath from each cor. A cor is ten baths, or one homer. 15Also, one sheep must be given from every flock of two hundred from the pastures of Israel. These will be used for grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for the people, declares the Lord the LORD.

16All the people of the land must take part in this offering for the prince in Israel. 17It will be the prince's job to provide the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the feasts, NewnewNew MoonsmoonsMoons, and Sabbaths—for all the appointed feasts of the people of Israel. He will provide sin offerings, grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for the people of Israel.

Cleansing the Temple and Keeping the Feasts

18This is what the Lord the LORD says: “On the first day of the first month, you are to take a young bull without a flaw and purify the sanctuary. 19The priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the temple, on the four corners of the upper ledge of the altar, and on the doorposts of the gate of the inner courtyard. 20You are to do the same on the seventh day of the month for anyone who sins by mistake or without knowing it. In this way you will make atonement for the temple.

21“On the fourteenth day of the first month, you are to observe the Passover. It is a feast of seven days, and unleavened bread is to be eaten. 22On that day the prince is to provide a bull as a sin offering for himself and for all the people of the land.

23“Each day during the seven days of the feast, he is to provide seven bulls and seven rams without a flaw as a burnt offering to the LORD, along with a male goat for a sin offering. 24He is to provide a grain offering of one ephah for each bull and one ephah for each ram, along with a hin of oil for each ephah. 25During the seven days of the feast that begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, he is to make the same provision for sin offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings.”
