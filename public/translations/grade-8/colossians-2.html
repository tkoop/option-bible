Paul's Concern for the Colossians

1For I want you to know how much I am struggling for you and for those in Laodicea, and for all who have never met me in person. 2I want your hearts to be encouraged. I want you to be joined together in love and to gain the full riches of complete understanding, so you can know the mystery of God, which is Christ. 3In HimhimHim are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. 4I say this so no one will trick you with smooth talk. 5Even though I am away from you in body, I am with you in spirit. I am happy to see your good order and the firmness of your faith in Christ.

Walking in Christ

6So, just as you received Christ Jesus as Lord, keep walking in HimhimHim. 7Be rooted and built up in HimhimHim. Be made strong in the faith you were taught, and overflow with thankfulness.

Beware of Empty Philosophy

8Watch out so no one takes you captive through philosophy or empty deceit. These follow human tradition and the basic principles of the world, not Christ. 9For in HimhimHim all the fullness of Deity lives in bodily form. 10And you have been made complete in HimhimHim, who is the head of every rule and authority.

Alive With Christ

11In HimhimHim you were also circumcised. This was not done by human hands. It was the removal of the body of flesh, through the circumcision of Christ. 12You were buried with HimhimHim in baptism, and in baptism you were raised with HimhimHim through faith in God's power. God raised Christ from the dead. 13You were dead in your sins and in the uncircumcision of your flesh. But God made you alive with Christ. He forgave us all our sins. 14He cancelledcancelledcanceled the record of debt that stood against us, with its legal demands. He took it away by nailing it to the cross. 15He stripped the rulers and authorities of their power. He made a public show of them and triumphed over them through Christ.

Freedom From Human Rules

16So let no one judge you about food or drink, or about a festival, a new moon, or a Sabbath. 17These are only a shadow of things to come. The reality is Christ. 18Let no one disqualify you by insisting on false humility and worship of angels. Such a person goes into detail about visions. He is puffed up without reason by his fleshly mind. 19He does not hold on to the Head. From the Head, the whole body is nourished and joined together through its joints and ligaments. It grows with the growth that comes from God. 20Since you died with Christ to the basic principles of the world, why do you still submit to rules as if you were living in the world? 21"Do not handle! Do not taste! Do not touch!" 22These things are all meant to be used up and destroyed. They come from human commands and teachings. 23They look wise because they promote self-made religion, false humility, and harsh treatment of the body. But they do not really stop the flesh from sinning.
