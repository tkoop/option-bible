The Destroyers and the Mark

1Then, in my hearing, a loud voice called out, "BringbringBring the city's destroyers near. Each one carries a weapon of destruction." 2I saw six men coming from the upper gate, the one that faces north. Each man had a weapon for breaking things in his hand. One man among them wore linen clothes and had a writer's inkhorn at his waist. They came and stood beside the bronze altar.

3The glory of the God of Israel had moved up from the cherub where it rested to the threshold of the house. He called to the man in linen who had the writer's inkhorn at his waist.

4The LORD said to him, "Go through the city, through Jerusalem. Put a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the horrible things done there."
