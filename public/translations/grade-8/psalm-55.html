A Prayer When a Friend Betrays You

1Listen to my prayer, God, and do not hide from my plea for mercy. 2Pay attention to me and answer me. I am upset by my thoughts, and I am troubled.

3I am troubled by my enemy's voice and by the pressure from wicked people, because they bring trouble on me and hate me in anger. 4My heart is in pain inside me, and the fear of death falls on me. 5Fear and shaking come over me, and horror covers me.

6I said, "If only I had wings like a dove, I would fly away and rest. 7Look, I would go far away and stay in the desert. Selah 8I would hurry to a safe place away from the wild wind and storm."

Trouble in the City

9Lord, confuse their speech, for I see violence and arguing in the city. 10Day and night they walk around on its walls, and trouble and sorrow are inside it. 11Ruin is in the middle of it. Oppression and lies do not leave its streets.

Betrayed by a Close Friend

12It is not an enemy who insults me, or I could bear it. It is not someone who hates me who has turned against me, or I could hide from him. 13But it is you, a man like me, my companion and close friend. 14We used to share friendly advice, and we walked together in God's house with the crowd.

15Let death surprise them. Let them go down alive to the grave, because evil is in their homes and among them. 16But I will call on God, and the LORD will save me. 17Morning, noon, and evening I complain and groan, and HeheHe hears my voice. 18He rescues my life in peace from the battle against me, because many stand with me.

God Will Judge the Wicked

19God will hear and humble them, the OneoneOne who has ruled from long ago. Selah Because they do not change and do not fear God. 20He has reached out against his friends and broken his promise. 21His words were smooth as butter, but war was in his heart. His words were softer than oil, but they were like drawn swords.

22Give your worries to the LORD, and HeheHe will take care of you. He will never let those who do right be shaken. 23But YouyouYou, O God, will bring them down to the pit of destruction. Violent and dishonest men will not live out half their days. But I will trust in YouyouYou.
