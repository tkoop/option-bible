Jacob Calls His Sons Together

1Jacob called his sons and said, "Gather together, and I will tell you what will happen to you in the days to come. 2Assemble and listen, sons of Jacob. Listen to Israel, your father."

Jacob Blesses His Sons

3"Reuben, you are my firstborn. You are my strength and the first sign of my power. You were to have the highest honourhonourhonor and the greatest strength. 4But you are as unstable as water. You will not lead, because you went up to your father's bed and dishonoureddishonoureddishonored it."

5"Simeon and Levi are brothers. Their swords are weapons of violence. 6I will not join their secret plans. I will not take part in their meetings. In their anger they killed a man. They crippled an ox for no good reason. 7Their anger is cursed because it is fierce. Their fury is cursed because it is cruel. I will scatter them among Jacob's people. I will spread them throughout Israel."

8"Judah, your brothers will praise you. Your hand will be on your enemies' necks. Your father's sons will bow down to you. 9Judah is a young lion. My son, you have gone up from the kill. He crouches and lies down like a lion. Like a lioness, who would dare to wake him?"

10"The sceptresceptrescepter will not leave Judah. The ruler's staff will stay with him. It will last until Shiloh comes. Then the nations will obey him. 11He ties his donkey to a grapevine. He ties his young donkey to the best vine. He washes his clothes in wine. He washes his robe in grape juice. 12His eyes are darker than wine. His teeth are whiter than milk."

13"Zebulun will live by the sea. He will be a harbourharbourharbor for ships. His border will reach toward Sidon. 14Issachar is a strong donkey, lying down between the sheep pens. 15He saw that rest was good and that the land was pleasant. So he bent his shoulder to the load. He became a servant who had to work for others."

16"Dan will judge his people. He will be one of the tribes of Israel. 17Dan will be a snake by the road. He will be a viper by the path. He bites the horse's heels, and the rider falls backward. 18I wait for YouryourYour salvation, O the LORD."

19"Gad will be attacked by raiders, but he will strike back at them. 20Asher's food will be rich. He will provide food fit for a king. 21Naphtali is a deer set free. He speaks beautiful words."

22"Joseph is a fruitful vine. He is a fruitful vine by a spring. His branches climb over a wall. 23Archers attacked him. They shot at him and hated him. 24But his bow stayed strong. His arms stayed quick. This strength came from the MightymightyMighty OneoneOne of Jacob. It came from the ShepherdshepherdShepherd, the RockrockRock of Israel."

25"May the God of your father help you. May the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty bless you with blessings from Heavenheavenheaven above, from the deep below, and from the breasts and womb. 26Your father's blessings are greater than the blessings of my ancestors. They reach to the highest hills. They rest on Joseph's head. They rest on the prince among his brothers."

27"Benjamin is a hungry wolf. In the morning he eats the prey. In the evening he divides the spoil."

28All these are the twelve tribes of Israel. This is what their father said to them. He blessed each one with the blessing meant for him.

Jacob's Burial Instructions

29Then he commanded them, "I am about to die and join my ancestors. Bury me with my fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite, 30the cave in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre in Canaan. Abraham bought the field from Ephron the Hittite to use as a burial place. 31Abraham and his wife Sarah are buried there. Isaac and his wife Rebekah are buried there. I buried Leah there. 32The field and the cave in it were bought from the Hittites."

33When Jacob finished commanding his sons, he pulled his feet into the bed. He breathed his last and joined his ancestors.
