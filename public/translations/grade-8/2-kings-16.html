Ahaz Becomes King of Judah

1In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham became king of Judah. 2Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD his God. 3He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel and even sacrificed his son in the fire. He followed the terrible practices of the nations the LORD had driven out before the Israelites. 4He offered sacrifices and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree.

Judah Is Attacked and Turns to Assyria

5Then Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel marched up to fight against Jerusalem. They besieged Ahaz, but they could not overpower him. 6At that time Rezin king of Aram recovered Elath for Aram. He drove the people of Judah out of Elath, and the Arameans came and settled there. They are still there today.

7Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria with this message: "I am your servant and your son. Come up and save me from the king of Aram and the king of Israel, who are attacking me."

8Ahaz took the silver and gold found in the temple of the LORD and in the royal treasuries and sent it as a gift to the king of Assyria. 9The king of Assyria listened to him. He marched against Damascus, captured it, and took its people to Kir as captives. He also put Rezin to death.

Ahaz Copies a Foreign Altar

10King Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria. There he saw the altar. King Ahaz sent Uriah the priest a model of the altar and a plan for building it, with every detail of its design. 11Uriah the priest built the altar. He followed all the instructions King Ahaz had sent from Damascus. Uriah finished it before King Ahaz returned.

12When the king returned from Damascus and saw the altar, he approached it and offered sacrifices on it. 13He burned his burnt offering and grain offering on it. He poured out his drink offering and sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings on the altar. 14He also moved the bronze altar that stood before the LORD. It had been in front of the temple, between the new altar and the the LORD's temple. He placed it on the north side of the new altar.

15King Ahaz gave orders to Uriah the priest: "On the large altar burn the morning burnt offering and the evening burnt offering, the king's grain offering and his burnt offering, and the grain offering and burnt offering of all the people of the land. Also pour their drink offerings on it. Sprinkle on it all the blood of the burnt offerings and sacrifices. But the bronze altar will be for me to use for seeking guidance."

16Uriah the priest did everything King Ahaz commanded.

Ahaz Changes the Temple

17King Ahaz cut off the panels from the movable stands and removed the basins from them. He also took down the SeaseaSea from the bronze oxen that supported it and set it on a stone pavementroadwaypavement. 18He also removed the covered walkway used on the Sabbath and the king's outer entrance that had been built at the temple of the LORD. He did this because of the king of Assyria.

19The rest of the acts of Ahaz, and everything he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? 20Ahaz died and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. His son Hezekiah became king in his place.
