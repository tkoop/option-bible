Masters and Servants

1Masters, treat your slaves fairly and justly, because you know that you also have a Master in Heavenheavenheaven.

Prayer and Wise Living

2Stay devoted to prayer, and stay alert in it with thanksgiving. 3Pray for us too, so that God will open a door for us to share HishisHis message and explain the mystery of Christ. That is why I am in chains. 4Pray that I will make it clear, as I should. 5Live wisely among people outside the church, and make good use of every opportunity. 6Let your speech always be gracious and thoughtful, so that you will know how to answer each person.

Greetings and Final Words

7Tychicus will tell you everything about my situation. He is a dear brother, a faithful servant, and a fellow worker in the Lord. 8I am sending him to you for this very reason, so that you may know how we are doing and so that he may encourage your hearts. 9With him is Onesimus, the faithful and dear brother, who is one of you. They will tell you everything that is happening here.

10Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, sends his greetings, and so does Mark, the cousin of Barnabas. You have already received instructions about him; if he comes to you, welcome him. 11Jesus, who is called Justus, also greets you. These are the only Jewish believers among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me.

12Epaphras, who is one of you and a servant of Christ Jesus, greets you. He always struggles in prayer for you, asking that you may stand firm, mature and fully sure of God's will in every way. 13I can testify that he works hard for you and for the people in Laodicea and Hierapolis.

14Luke, the dear doctor, and Demas greet you. 15Greet the brothers in Laodicea, and greet Nympha and the church that meets in her home. 16After this letter has been read among you, have it read also to the church of the Laodiceans, and be sure that you also read the letter from Laodicea.

17Tell Archippus, "See that you complete the ministry you received in the Lord." 18I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
