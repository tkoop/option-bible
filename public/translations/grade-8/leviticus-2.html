Grain Offerings of Fine Flour

1When anyone brings a grain offering to the LORD, his offering must be fine flour. He must pour oil on it and put frankincense on it. 2He must bring it to Aaron’s sons, the priests. The priest will take a handful of the fine flour and oil, along with all the frankincense, and burn the memorial portion on the altar. It is an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 3The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings made by fire to the LORD.

Baked Grain Offerings

4If you bring a grain offering baked in an oven, it must be fine flour. It can be unleavened cakes mixed with oil or unleavened wafers coated with oil.

Grain Offerings Cooked on a Griddle or in a Pan

5If your offering is a grain offering cooked on a griddle, it must be fine flour mixed with oil, without yeast. 6Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering. 7If your offering is a grain offering cooked in a pan, it must be made of fine flour with oil.

Bringing the Grain Offering to the Altar

8When you bring to the LORD a grain offering made in any of these ways, it must be given to the priest. He will bring it to the altar. 9The priest will take the memorial portion from the grain offering and burn it on the altar. It is an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. 10The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the offerings made by fire to the LORD.

Rules for All Grain Offerings

11No grain offering that you bring to the LORD may contain yeast, because you must not burn any yeast or honey as an offering made by fire to the LORD. 12You may bring them as an offering of firstfruits to the LORD, but they must not go up in smoke on the altar as a pleasing aroma. 13Season all your grain offerings with salt. Do not leave out the salt of the covenant of your God from your grain offering. Add salt to all your offerings.

Grain Offering of Firstfruits

14If you bring a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you must bring crushed new grain roasted with fire. It is a grain offering from your firstfruits. 15You must put oil on it and add frankincense. It is a grain offering. 16The priest will burn the memorial portion—taken from the crushed grain and the oil—along with all the frankincense. It is an offering made by fire to the LORD.
