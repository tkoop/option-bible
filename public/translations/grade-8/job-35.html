Elihu Answers Job

1Then Elihu spoke again: 2"Do you think this is fair? You say, 'I am more righteous than God.' 3YouyouYou ask, 'What good does it do me? What do I gain by not sinning?' 4I will answer you and your friends who are with you."

Human Sin Does Not Hurt God

5"Look up at the sky and see; look at the clouds high above you. 6If you sin, what do you do to HimhimHim? If your sins are many, what do you do to HimhimHim? 7If you are righteous, what do you give HimhimHim? Or what does HeheHe receive from your hand? 8Your wrongdoing affects only people like you, and your right living affects only other people."

Why God May Not Answer

9"Because of heavy oppression, people cry out; they call for help against the power of the mighty. 10But no one says, 'Where is God my MakermakerMaker, who gives songs in the night, 11who teaches us more than the animals of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?' 12There they cry out, but HeheHe does not answer, because of the pride of evil people. 13Surely God does not listen to empty cries, and the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty does not pay attention to them. 14How much less, then, when you say you do not see HimhimHim, that your case is before HimhimHim and you must wait for HimhimHim! 15And now, since HeheHe does not punish in anger and does not pay much attention to wrongdoing, 16Job opens his mouth for nothing and piles up words without knowledge."
