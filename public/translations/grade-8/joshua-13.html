Land Still to Be Conquered

1When Joshua was old and well along in years, the LORD said to him, “You are old and well along in years, and a lot of land still needs to be taken. 2This is the land that remains: all the regions of the Philistines and all the Geshurites, 3from the Shihor on the border of Egypt to the border of Ekron in the north. It counts as Canaanite land. The five Philistine rulers are those of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, along with the Avvites. 4From the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek and the border of the Amorites. 5Also the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to the entrance of Hamath. 6I will drive out all the people who live in the hill country, from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, and all the Sidonians, before the Israelites. But allot this land to Israel as an inheritance, just as I commanded you. 7Now divide this land as an inheritance for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”

The Eastern Tribes' Inheritance

8The Reubenites and Gadites had already received their inheritance, which Moses gave them east of the Jordan, just as Moses, the servant of the LORD, had given them: 9from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the plain of Medeba as far as Dibon; 10and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites; 11and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah; 12all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei. He was one of the last of the Rephaim. Moses struck them down and took the land. 13Yet the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites. Geshur and Maacah still live among Israel to this day. 14Only the tribe of Levi did not receive an inheritance. The offerings made by fire to the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, as HeheHe had promised.

Reuben and Gad's Territories

15Moses gave an inheritance to the clans of the tribe of Reuben: 16Their territory was from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon ValleyvalleyValley, including the city in the middle of the valley, and all the plain near Medeba, 17with Heshbon and all its cities on the plain: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon, 18Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath, 19Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill of the valley, 20Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth, 21and all the cities of the plain, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon. Moses struck him down along with the leaders of Midian: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon who lived in the land. 22The Israelites also killed Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, with the sword among those they struck down. 23The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan. This was the inheritance of the Reubenites by their clans, with their cities and villages.

24Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to its clans: 25Their territory was Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, which is east of Rabbah; 26from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir; 27and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, with the Jordan as its border, eastward to the edge of the SeaseaSea of Chinnereth. 28This was the inheritance of the Gadites by their clans, with their cities and villages.

Half of Manasseh's Territory

29Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it was for the clans of the half-tribe of the descendants of Manasseh: 30Their territory was from Mahanaim through all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the towns of Jair in Bashan—sixty cities. 31Half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the cities of Og's kingdom in Bashan, went to the clans of Machir son of Manasseh, that is, to half the descendants of Machir. 32These are the inheritances Moses gave on the plains of Moab, east of the Jordan, across from Jericho. 33But to the tribe of Levi, Moses gave no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as HeheHe had promised them.
