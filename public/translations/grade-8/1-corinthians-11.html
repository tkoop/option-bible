Head Coverings in Worship

1Be like me, just as I am like Christ. 2I praise you because you remember me in everything and hold on to the teachings I gave you. 3But I want you to know that Christ is the head of every man, man is the head of woman, and God is the head of Christ.

4Every man who prays or speaks God's message with his head covered brings shame on his head. 5But every woman who prays or speaks God's message with her head uncovered brings shame on her head. It is the same as if her head were shaved. 6If a woman will not cover her head, she should cut her hair short. But if it is shameful for a woman to cut her hair short or shave her head, then she should cover her head.

7A man should not cover his head, because he is the image and glory of God. But woman is the glory of man. 8Man did not come from woman, but woman came from man. 9Man was not created for woman, but woman was created for man. 10That is why a woman should have a sign of authority on her head, because of the angels.

11But in the Lord, woman is not separate from man, and man is not separate from woman. 12Just as woman came from man, man is born through woman. But everything comes from God. 13Decide for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered? 14Does not nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a disgrace to him? 15But if a woman has long hair, it is her glory, because her hair was given to her as a covering. 16If anyone wants to argue about this, we have no other custom, and neither do God's churches.

Problems at the Lord's Supper

17But in giving these instructions, I do not praise you, because when you come together it is not for the better but for the worse. 18First of all, I hear that when you meet as a church there are divisions among you, and I partly believe it. 19There must be differences among you so that those who are genuine may be recognizedrecogniserecognized. 20So when you come together, it is not really the Lord's SuppersupperSupper you are eating. 21For when it is time to eat, each person goes ahead with his own meal. One person is hungry, and another has too much to drink. 22Don't you have homes where you can eat and drink? Or do you look down on God's church and shame those who have nothing? What should I say to you? Should I praise you? I will not praise you for this.

How the Lord's Supper Began

23For I received from the Lord what I also passed on to you: On the night HeheHe was betrayed, the Lord Jesus took bread. 24After giving thanks, HeheHe broke it and said, "This is MymyMy body, which is for you. Do this in memory of MemeMe."

25In the same way, after supper HeheHe took the cup and said, "This cup is the new covenant in MymyMy blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, in memory of MemeMe." 26For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you announce the Lord's death until HeheHe comes.

Taking the Supper Seriously

27So then, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy way is guilty of the body and blood of the Lord. 28Let each person examine himself, and then eat the bread and drink from the cup. 29For anyone who eats and drinks without recognizing the body eats and drinks judgmentjudgementjudgment on himself. 30That is why many among you are weak and sick, and some have died. 31But if we judged ourselves, we would not be judged. 32When we are judged by the Lord, we are being trained so that we will not be condemned along with the world. 33So, my brothers and sisters, when you come together to eat, wait for one another. 34If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that when you come together it will not lead to judgmentjudgementjudgment. I will give directions about the other matters when I come.
