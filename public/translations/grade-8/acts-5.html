Ananias and Sapphira Lie to God

1Now a man named Ananias, with his wife Sapphira, sold a piece of property. 2With his wife knowing about it, he kept back part of the money. He brought only part of it and laid it at the apostles' feet.

3Peter said, "Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart to lie to the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit and keep back part of the money from the land? 4Didn't it belong to you before it was sold? And after it was sold, wasn't the money yours to do with as you wanted? Why did you decide to do this? You didn't lie to people. You lied to God."

5When Ananias heard these words, he fell down and died. Great fear came on everyone who heard about it. 6The young men got up, wrapped his body, carried it out, and buried it.

7About three hours later, his wife came in. She did not know what had happened.

8Peter asked her, "Tell me, did you sell the land for this much?"

She said, "Yes, for that much."

9Peter said to her, "Why did you two agree to test the SpiritspiritSpirit of the Lord? Listen! The feet of the men who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out too."

10At once she fell at his feet and died. The young men came in, found her dead, carried her out, and buried her beside her husband. 11Great fear came on the whole church and on everyone who heard about these things.

Many People Are Healed

12The apostles did many signs and wonders among the people. They were all together in Solomon's Porch. 13No one else dared join them, but the people respected them greatly. 14More and more people believed and were added to the Lord, both men and women.

15People even brought sick people into the streets and laid them on cots and mats, hoping that Peter's shadow might fall on at least one of them as he passed by. 16Crowds also came from the towns around Jerusalem, bringing sick people and those troubled by evil spirits, and all of them were healed.

The Apostles Are Arrested

17The high priest and all his associates, who were Sadducees, became very jealous. They got up, 18arrested the apostles, and put them in public jail.

19But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the jail doors, led them out, and said, 20"Go and stand in the temple. Tell the people all the words of this life."

21At daybreak they went into the temple and began to teach. The high priest and his associates arrived, called the council together with all the elders of Israel, and sent men to the jail to bring the apostles. 22The officers went to the jail but did not find them there. They came back and reported, 23"We found the jail locked tight and the guards standing at the doors. But when we opened it, no one was inside."

24The temple guard captain and the chief priests heard this and wondered what had happened. 25Then someone came and told them, "Look! The men you put in jail are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

26The captain went with the officers and brought them back. They did not use force because they were afraid the people might stone them.

Peter Speaks to the Council

27They brought the apostles and made them stand before the council. The high priest questioned them. 28He said, "We gave you strict orders not to teach in this name. But look, you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching. You want to bring this man's blood on us."

29Peter and the apostles answered, "We must obey God rather than people. 30The God of our fathers raised Jesus, whom you killed by hanging HimhimHim on a tree. 31God raised HimhimHim to HishisHis right hand as Prince and SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior, to give Israel a chance to turn from sin and receive forgiveness of sins. 32We are witnesses of these things, and so is the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, whom God gave to those who obey HimhimHim."

33When they heard this, they were furious and wanted to kill them. 34But a Pharisee named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law who was respected by all the people, stood up in the council. He ordered the men to be put outside for a short time. 35Then he said to them, "Men of Israel, be careful what you plan to do to these men.

"Some time ago Theudas appeared. He claimed to be someone important, and about four hundred men joined him. He was killed, and all his followers were scattered. They came to nothing. 3637After him, Judas the Galilean appeared during the census. He drew people after him, but he died too, and all his followers were scattered.

"So now I tell you, stay away from these men and leave them alone. If this plan or work comes from people, it will fail. 3839But if it is from God, you will not be able to stop them. You might even find yourselves fighting against God."

40They agreed with him. They called the apostles in, beat them, ordered them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go. 41The apostles left the council rejoicing because they had been counted worthy to suffer shame for the NamenameName. 42Every day, in the temple and from house to house, they never stopped teaching and telling the good news that Jesus is the Christ.
