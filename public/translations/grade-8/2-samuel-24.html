David Orders a Census

1Again, the LORD was angry with Israel, and HeheHe stirred David to act against them. He told David, “Go and count the people of Israel and Judah.”

2So the king said to Joab, the commander of the army who was with him, “Go now through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, and count the people. Then I will know how many there are.”

3Joab replied to the king, “May the LORD your God multiply the people a hundred times, and may my lord the king see it with his own eyes. But why does my lord the king want to do this?”

4But the king's word won out over Joab and the army commanders. So Joab and the commanders left the king's presence to count the people of Israel. 5They crossed the Jordan and camped near Aroer, south of the town in the middle of the valley. Then they moved toward Gad and Jazer. 6They went to Gilead and the land of Tahtim Hodshi. Then they went to Dan Jaan and around toward Sidon. 7They came to the fortress of Tyre and to all the cities of the Hivites and Canaanites. Then they went south to the Negev of Judah at Beersheba. 8After they had gone through the whole land, they returned to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days. 9Joab reported the number of fighting men to the king: Israel had 800,000 brave men who could draw a sword, and Judah had 500,000.

David's Sin and Punishment

10After David had counted the people, he felt guilty. He said to the LORD, “I have sinned greatly in what I have done. Now, the LORD, please take away the guilt of YouryourYour servant, for I have acted very foolishly.”

11When David got up the next morning, the word of the LORD came to Gad the prophet, David's seer. 12“Go and tell David, ‘This is what the LORD says: I am giving you three choices. Choose one of them, and I will carry it out against you.’”

13So Gad went to David and told him, “Should seven years of famine come on your land? Or should you run for three months from your enemies while they chase you? Or should there be three days of plague in your land? Now think about it and decide what answer I should give to the OneoneOne who sent me.”

14David said to Gad, “I am in great trouble. Please let us be punished by the LORD, because HishisHis mercy is great. But don't let us be punished by people.”

15So the LORD sent a plague on Israel from that morning until the set time. Seventy thousand men died, from Dan to Beersheba. 16When the angel reached out his hand to destroy Jerusalem, the LORD changed HishisHis mind about the disaster and said to the angel who was destroying the people, “Enough! Withdraw your hand.” The angel of the LORD was standing at the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

17When David saw the angel striking down the people, he said to the LORD, “I have sinned and done wrong, but these people are like sheep. What have they done? Please let YouryourYour hand fall on me and on my father's family.”

David Builds an Altar

18That day Gad came to David and said, “Go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.”

19So David went up, as the LORD had commanded through Gad. 20When Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants coming toward him, he went out and bowed with his face to the ground before the king.

21Araunah said, “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?”

David replied, “To buy the threshing floor from you, to build an altar to the LORD, so that the plague will stop for the people.”

22Araunah said to David, “My lord the king may take whatever he wants and offer it up. Here are oxen for the burnt offering, and here are the threshing sledges and the ox yokes for the wood.”

23Araunah gave all of this to the king. He also said, “May the LORD your God accept you.”

24But the king replied to Araunah, “No, I insist on paying you for it. I will not offer to the LORD my God burnt offerings that cost me nothing.” So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.

25There David built an altar to the LORD and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. Then the LORD answered the prayer for the land, and the plague on Israel was stopped.
