The Blood of the Covenant

19After Moses had told all the people every command of the law, he took the blood of calves and goats, along with water, red wool, and hyssop. He sprinkled the scroll and all the people. 20He said, "This is the blood of the covenant, which God has commanded you to keep." 21In the same way, he sprinkled the tabernacle and all the tools used in worship. 22The law says that almost everything is made clean with blood. Without blood being poured out, there is no forgiveness.
