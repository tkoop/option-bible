A Warning for Pharaoh

1On the first day of the third month in the eleventh year, the word of the the LORD came to me.

2“Son of man, speak to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and to his people: ‘Who are you like in your greatness?’

Assyria Was Like a Great Tree

“3Look, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, with beautiful branches, a shady forest, and very tall. Its top reached up among thick branches. 4Water made it grow, and the deep sea made it tall. Rivers flowed around where it was planted, and channels carried water to all the trees in the field. 5So it grew taller than every tree in the field. Its branches spread out, and its boughs grew long because water was plentiful. 6All the birds of the sky nested in its branches. All the wild animals gave birth under its boughs. Many nations lived in its shade. 7It was beautiful in its greatness and in its long branches, because its roots reached down to plenty of water. 8The cedars in God’s garden did not compare with it. The cypress trees did not match its branches. The plane trees were not like its boughs. No tree in God’s garden was as beautiful as it. 9I made it beautiful by giving it many branches. All the trees of Eden in God’s garden were jealous of it.

God Judges Assyria

“10So this is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: Because you grew so tall and lifted your top among the thick branches, and because your heart became proud of your height, 11I will hand it over to a powerful ruler among the nations. He will punish it for its wickedness. I have thrown it out. 12Strangers, the cruel nations, cut it down and left it. Its branches fell on the mountains and in every valley. Its boughs broke and lay in all the riverbeds. All the peoples of the earth came out from under its shade and left it. 13All the birds of the sky settled in its ruins, and all the wild animals lived among its branches. 14This happened so that no tree growing by water would become proud of its height or lift its top among the thick branches, and so that no well-watered tree would stand tall on its own. They are all handed over to death, to the lower parts of the earth, among the people, to those who go down to the pit.

The World Mourns Assyria’s Fall

“15This is what the Lord, the the LORD, says: On the day it went down to the grave, I made people mourn for it. I covered the deep sea over it and stopped its rivers. The many waters were held back. I made Lebanon mourn for it, and all the trees in the field fainted because of it. 16I shook the nations with the sound of its fall when I sent it down to the grave with those who go down to the pit. Then all the trees of Eden—the best and finest of Lebanon, all that drink water—were comforted in the lower parts of the earth. 17Its allies, who had lived in its shade among the nations, also went down to the grave with it, to those killed by the sword. 18Who else are you like in glory and greatness among the trees of Eden? Yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the lower parts of the earth. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his people, declares the Lord, the the LORD.”
