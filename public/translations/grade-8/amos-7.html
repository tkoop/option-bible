Amos Sees Locusts

1This is what the Lord the LORD showed me: He was forming swarms of locusts just as the late spring crop was starting to grow. It was after the king's harvest. 2When they had eaten every plant in the land, I cried out, "Lord the LORD, please forgive! How can Jacob survive? He is so small!" 3So the LORD changed HishisHis mind. "It will not happen," said the LORD.

Amos Sees Fire

4This is what the Lord the LORD showed me: The Lord the LORD was calling for judgmentjudgementjudgment by fire. It burned up the great deep and ate up the land. 5I said, "Lord the LORD, please stop! How can Jacob survive? He is so small!" 6So the LORD changed HishisHis mind. "This will not happen either," said the Lord the LORD.

Amos Sees a Plumb Line

7This is what HeheHe showed me: The Lord was standing by a wall built with a plumb line, and HeheHe held a plumb line in HishisHis hand. 8The LORD asked me, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "Look, I am putting a plumb line among MymyMy people Israel. I will not spare them anymore. 9The high places of Isaac will be ruined, and Israel's holy places will be destroyed. I will rise up against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

Amaziah Opposes Amos

10Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam king of Israel: "Amos has plotted against you among the people of Israel. The land cannot stand all his words. 11For this is what Amos says: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will be carried away from its land.'" 12Then Amaziah said to Amos, "Go away, you seer! Flee to Judah. Earn your living there and prophesy there. 13But do not prophesy anymore at Bethel, because it is the king's holy place and a royal temple."

14Amos answered Amaziah, "I was not a prophet, and I was not a prophet's son. I was a shepherd, and I took care of sycamore trees. 15But the LORD took me from following the flock and said to me, 'Go, prophesy to MymyMy people Israel.' 16Now listen to the word of the LORD: You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach against the house of Isaac.' 17So this is what the LORD says: 'YouryourYour wife will become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and daughters will fall by the sword. Your land will be divided up, and you will die in an unclean land. Israel will surely be taken from its land.'"
