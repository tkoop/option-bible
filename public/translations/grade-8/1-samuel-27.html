David Flees to Philistia

1David said to himself, "OneoneOne day Saul will kill me. The only good thing for me is to escape to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will give up looking for me anywhere in Israel, and I will be safe from him."

2So David and the six hundred men with him crossed over to Achish son of Maoch, king of Gath.

David Settles in Gath

3David and his men stayed in Gath with Achish. Each man brought his family. David had his two wives, Ahinoam from Jezreel and Abigail, who had been Nabal's wife from Carmel.

4When Saul heard that David had fled to Gath, he stopped looking for him.

5David said to Achish, "If I have found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, give me a place in one of the country towns so I can live there. Why should your servant live in the royal city with you?"

6So Achish gave him Ziklag that day, and Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah ever since.

7David lived in the land of the Philistines for a year and four months.

David Raids Neighboring Peoples

8David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites. These people had lived in the land from ancient times, from Shur all the way to Egypt.

9David attacked the land and did not leave anyone alive, man or woman. He took sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothing. Then he returned to Achish.

Achish Believes David

10Achish asked, "Where did you raid today?"

David answered, "Against the Negev of Judah, the Negev of the Jerahmeelites, and the Negev of the Kenites."

11David did not leave anyone alive to bring to Gath, because he thought, "They might tell on us and say, 'This is what David did, and this is how he acts,' as long as he lives in the land of the Philistines."

12Achish believed David and said, "HeheHe has made himself hated by his people Israel, so he will be my servant forever."
