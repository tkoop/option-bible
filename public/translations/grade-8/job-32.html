Elihu Becomes Angry

1These three men had stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. 2Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram, became very angry. He was angry at Job for justifying himself rather than God. 3He was also angry with Job's three friends, because they had found no answer, yet they had condemned Job. 4Elihu had waited to speak to Job because they were older than he was. 5When Elihu saw that the three men had no answer, he became very angry.

Elihu Explains Why He Speaks

6"I am young in years, and you are old. That is why I was afraid and did not dare to tell you what I know. 7I thought age should speak, and many years should teach wisdom. 8But there is a spirit in man, and the breath of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty gives understanding. 9It is not only the old who are wise, nor only the aged who understand justice. 10So I say, 'Listen to me; I too will declare what I know.'

Elihu Criticizes the Three Friends

11"Look, I waited for your words; I listened to your reasoning while you searched for words. 12I paid close attention, but none of you proved Job wrong; none of you answered his words. 13Do not say, 'We have found wisdom; God will drive him out, not a man.' 14HeheHe has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your arguments.

Elihu Says He Must Speak

15"They are upset and answer no more; words have failed them. 16Should I wait because they do not speak, because they stand there and answer no more? 17I too will answer; yes, I will declare what I know. 18For I am full of words; the spirit within me forces me to speak. 19Look, I am so full of words that I feel ready to burst. 20I must speak so that I may find relief; I will open my lips and answer. 21I will show no favouritismfavouritismfavoritism to anyone, and I will not flatter any man. 22For I do not know how to flatter; if I did, my MakermakerMaker would soon take me away."
