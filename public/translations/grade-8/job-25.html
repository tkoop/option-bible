Bildad Speaks Again

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

God's Greatness and Human Weakness

2“God rules with power and fear. He brings peace in HishisHis high places. 3Can anyone count HishisHis armies? Is there anyone HishisHis light does not reach? 4How can a person be right with God? How can someone born of a woman be clean? 5Even the moon does not shine brightly, and the stars are not pure in HishisHis eyes. 6How much less a person, who is like a maggot, and a human being, who is like a worm!”
