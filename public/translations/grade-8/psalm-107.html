Give Thanks to the LORD

1Give thanks to the LORD, for HeheHe is good; HishisHis faithful love lasts forever. 2Let the redeemed of the LORD say so—those HeheHe rescued from the enemy's hand. 3He gathered them from the lands, from the east and the west, from the north and the south.

The LORD Rescues People in Trouble

4Some wandered in desert wastelands and could not find a road to a city where they could live. 5They were hungry and thirsty, and their strength was failing. 6Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe delivered them from their distress. 7He led them by a straight path to a city where they could live. 8Let them thank the LORD for HishisHis faithful love and HishisHis wonders for people. 9For HeheHe satisfies the thirsty and fills the hungry with good things.

10Some sat in darkness, in the shadow of death, held as prisoners in suffering and chains, 11because they had rebelled against God's words and rejected the advice of the MostmostMost HighhighHigh. 12So HeheHe humbled their hearts with hard labourlabourlabor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help. 13Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them from their distress. 14He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and broke their chains. 15Let them thank the LORD for HishisHis faithful love and HishisHis wonders for people. 16For HeheHe broke the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron.

17Fools suffered because of their rebellious ways and their sins. 18They hated all food and came near the gates of death. 19Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe saved them from their distress. 20He sent HishisHis word and healed them; HeheHe rescued them from the pit. 21Let them thank the LORD for HishisHis faithful love and HishisHis wonders for people. 22Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and tell of HishisHis works with shouts of joy.

23Those who sail the sea in ships and work on the great waters 24have seen the works of the LORD and HishisHis wonders in the deep. 25For HeheHe spoke and raised a stormy wind that lifted up the waves. 26They rose to the heavens and sank to the depths; their strength melted away in their trouble. 27They reeled and staggered like drunk men, and all their skill was useless. 28Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and HeheHe brought them out of their distress. 29He calmed the storm to a whisper, and the waves of the sea were hushed. 30They rejoiced that the waters were quiet, and HeheHe guided them to the harbourharbourharbor they wanted. 31Let them thank the LORD for HishisHis faithful love and HishisHis wonders for people. 32Let them exalt HimhimHim in the assembly of the people and praise HimhimHim in the council of the elders.

The LORD Changes Deserts and Cities

33He turns rivers into a desert, springs of water into thirsty ground, 34and a fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of its people. 35He turns a desert into pools of water and a dry land into springs of water. 36There HeheHe brings the hungry to live, and they establish a city to dwell in. 37They sow fields and plant vineyards that yield a fruitful harvest. 38He blesses them, and they multiply greatly; HeheHe does not let their herds diminish. 39When they are decreased and humbled by oppression, trouble, and sorrow, 40HeheHe pours scorn on princes and makes them wander in a wasteland with no path. 41But HeheHe lifts the needy out of affliction and makes their families grow like flocks. 42The upright see and rejoice, and all wrongdoing is silenced. 43Whoever is wise, let him heed these things and consider the faithful love of the LORD.
