A New Song for the LORD

1Sing a new song to the LORD, because HeheHe has done amazing things. His right hand and holy arm have won the victory for HimhimHim. 2The LORD has made HishisHis saving power known. He has shown HishisHis justice to the nations. 3He remembered HishisHis faithful love and HishisHis faithfulness to Israel. Everyone to the ends of the earth has seen the saving power of our God.

All the Earth Praises Him

4Shout for joy to the LORD, all the earth. Burst into song, sing for joy, and make music. 5Sing praises to the LORD with the harp, with music and a joyful song. 6Play trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn. Shout for joy before the LORD, the KingkingKing.

Creation Joins the Praise

7Let the sea roar, and everything in it. Let the world and everyone who lives in it do the same. 8Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the mountains sing together with joy. 9Let them stand before the LORD, because HeheHe is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with justice and the peoples with fairness.
