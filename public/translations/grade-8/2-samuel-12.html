Nathan Confronts David with a Parable

1The LORD sent Nathan to David. When he arrived, he said to him, "There were two men in one city. One was rich, and the other was poor. 2The rich man had very many sheep and cattle. 3But the poor man had nothing except one little ewe lamb that he had bought. He raised it, and it grew up with him and his children. It ate from his food, drank from his cup, and slept in his lap. It was like a daughter to him. 4A travellertravellertraveler came to the rich man, but the rich man did not want to take from his own sheep or cattle to feed the guest. Instead, he took the poor man's lamb and prepared it for the man who had come to him."

5David became very angry with the man and said to Nathan, "As surely as the LORD lives, the man who did this deserves to die! 6He must pay back the lamb four times over, because he did this and showed no pity."

David Prays for the Child

15Then Nathan went home. The LORD struck the child that Uriah's wife had borne to David, and the child became very sick. 16David sought God on behalf of the child. He fasted, went in, and lay on the ground all night. 17The elders of his house stood over him to help him up from the ground, but he would not get up, and he would not eat with them.

18On the seventh day the child died. David's servants were afraid to tell him the child was dead, because they said, "While the child was alive, we spoke to him, but he would not listen to us. How can we tell him the child is dead? He might do something terrible."

19David saw his servants whispering together, and he understood the child was dead. He asked his servants, "IsisIs the child dead?"

They said, "HeheHe is dead."

20Then David got up from the ground, washed, anointed himself, changed his clothes, went into the house of the LORD, and worshiped. Then he came to his house and asked for food. They set food before him, and he ate.

21His servants asked him, "What is this thing you have done? While the child was alive, you fasted and wept. But when the child died, you got up and ate."

22He said, "While the child was alive, I fasted and wept, for I thought, 'Who knows? The LORD may be gracious to me, and the child may live.' 23But now that he is dead, why should I fast? Can I bring him back again? I will go to him, but he will not return to me."

24David comforted his wife Bathsheba, went in to her, and lay with her. She gave birth to a son, and he named him Solomon. The LORD loved him. 25He sent word through Nathan the prophet and named him Jedidiah because of the LORD.

David Captures Rabbah

26Joab fought against Rabbah of the Ammonites and captured the royal city. 27Joab sent messengers to David and said, "I have fought against Rabbah and captured the water city. 28Now gather the rest of the people, camp against the city, and capture it. Otherwise I will capture the city, and it will be named after me."

29David gathered all the people, went to Rabbah, fought against it, and captured it. 30He took the crown from the head of their king. Its weight was a talent of gold, and it had a precious stone. It was placed on David's head. He also brought out a great amount of plunder from the city. 31He brought out the people who were in it and put them to work with saws, iron picks, and iron axes, and made them work at the brick kilns. David did the same to all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all the people returned to Jerusalem.
