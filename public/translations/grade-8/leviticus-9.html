Aaron Prepares the Offerings

1On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel. 2He said to Aaron, "Take for yourself a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering. Both must have no flaw. Bring them before the LORD. 3Then tell the Israelites, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering. Take a calf and a lamb, each one year old and without flaw, for a burnt offering. 4Also bring a bull and a ram for peace offerings to sacrifice before the LORD. Bring a grain offering mixed with oil. Do this because today the LORD will appear to you.'" 5They brought what Moses had commanded to the front of the tent of meeting. The whole community came near and stood before the LORD. 6Moses said, "This is what the LORD commanded you to do, so the glory of the LORD may appear to you." 7Moses said to Aaron, "Go to the altar. Offer your sin offering and your burnt offering. Make atonement for yourself and for the people. Offer the people's gift and make atonement for them, as the LORD has commanded."

Aaron's Sin and Burnt Offerings

8Aaron went to the altar and killed the calf for his sin offering. 9His sons brought the blood to him. He dipped his finger in it and put some on the horns of the altar. He poured the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. 10He burned the fat, the kidneys, and the covering of the liver from the sin offering on the altar. He did this as the LORD had commanded Moses. 11He burned the meat and the skin outside the camp. 12Then he killed the burnt offering. His sons brought him the blood, and he sprinkled it all around the altar. 13They handed him the burnt offering, cut into pieces, along with the head. He burned all of it on the altar. 14He washed the inner parts and the legs. Then he burned them on the altar with the burnt offering.

Offerings for the People

15He brought the people's offering. He took the goat for the people's sin offering, killed it, and made it a sin offering like the first one. 16He brought the burnt offering and offered it following the rules. 17He brought the grain offering. He took a handful and burned it on the altar, besides the morning burnt offering. 18He killed the bull and the ram for the people's peace offerings. His sons brought him the blood, and he sprinkled it all around the altar. 19They took the fat from the bull and the ram: the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys, and the covering of the liver. 20They put the fat on the breast pieces. Then he burned the fat on the altar. 21Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh before the LORD as an offering. He did this as Moses had commanded.

God's Glory Appears

22Aaron lifted his hands toward the people and blessed them. Then he came down after offering the sin offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings. 23Moses and Aaron went into the tent of meeting. When they came out, they blessed the people. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people. 24Fire came out from before the LORD. It burned up the burnt offering and the fat on the altar. All the people saw it. They shouted with joy and fell on their faces.
