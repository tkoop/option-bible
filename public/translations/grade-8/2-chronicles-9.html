The Queen of Sheba Visits Solomon

1The queen of Sheba heard how famous Solomon was. She came to Jerusalem to test him with hard questions. She brought a large group with her. Her camels carried spices, gold, and precious stones. When she met Solomon, she told him everything she had on her mind.

2Solomon answered all her questions. He did not leave anything unexplained.

3When the queen of Sheba saw Solomon's wisdom and the palace he had built,

4and the food on his table, the seats of his officials, the work of his servants and their clothes, his cupbearers and their clothes, and the burnt offerings he made at the house of the LORD, she was amazed.

5She said to the king, "ThetheThe report I heard in my own country about your words and your wisdom is true.

6"I did not believe it until I came and saw it with my own eyes. But I now see that I had heard only half of it, and your wisdom is far greater than what people told me.

7"How happy your people must be! How happy your servants must be, who stand before you all the time and hear your wisdom!

8"Blessed be the LORD your God, who has been pleased with you and placed you on HishisHis throne to be king for the LORD your God. Because of HishisHis love for Israel, HeheHe has made you king forever, so you can do what is right and fair."

9She gave the king 120 talents of gold, a large amount of spices, and precious stones. There had never been spices like those the queen of Sheba gave King Solomon.

10The servants of Hiram and the servants of Solomon also brought gold from Ophir. They brought algum wood too.

11King Solomon used the algum wood to make steps for the house of the LORD and for the palace. He also made harps and lyres for the singers. No wood like this had ever been seen in Judah before.

12King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba everything she wanted and asked for. This was besides what she had brought to the king. Then she and her servants went back to her own country.

Solomon's Wealth and SplendourSplendourSplendor

13The gold that came to Solomon in one year weighed 666 talents.

14This was besides what traders and merchants brought. All the kings of Arabia and the governors of the land also brought gold and silver to Solomon.

15King Solomon made 200 large shields of hammered gold. Each one used 600 shekels of gold.

16He also made 300 smaller shields of hammered gold. Each one used 300 shekels of gold. The king put them in the Palace of the ForestforestForest of Lebanon.

17The king made a large throne of ivory. He covered it with pure gold.

18The throne had six steps. A footstool of gold was attached to it. There were armrests on both sides, with a lion standing beside each one.

19Twelve lions stood on the six steps, one on each side of every step. No other kingdom had a throne like this.

20All King Solomon's drinking cups were gold. All the cups in the Palace of the ForestforestForest of Lebanon were gold too. Silver was not worth much in Solomon's time.

21The king had ships that sailed with Hiram's men. Once every three years they came back carrying gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.

22King Solomon was richer and wiser than any other king on EarthEarthearth.

23All the kings of the earth wanted to meet Solomon. They wanted to hear the wisdom God had given him.

24Year after year, everyone who came brought gifts. They brought silver and gold items, clothes, weapons, spices, horses, and mules.

25Solomon had 4,000 stalls for horses and chariots. He also had 12,000 horsemen. He kept some in the chariot cities and some with him in Jerusalem.

26He ruled over all the kings from the Euphrates River to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt.

27The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones. He made cedar wood as common as sycamore trees in the lowlands.

28Solomon's horses were brought from Egypt and from all other lands.

The End of Solomon's Reign

29The rest of Solomon's acts, from beginning to end, are written in the records of Nathan the prophet, in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer about Jeroboam son of Nebat.

30Solomon ruled in Jerusalem over all Israel for forty years.

31Then Solomon died and was buried in the city of his father David. His son Rehoboam became king after him.
