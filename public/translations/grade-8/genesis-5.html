Adam's Family Line

1This is the book of Adam's family line. In the day God created man, HeheHe made him in HishisHis own likeness. 2Male and female HeheHe created them, and HeheHe blessed them. He called them "Mankind" on the day they were created.

3Adam lived 130 years, and he had a son in his own likeness, after his image. He named him Seth. 4After Seth was born, Adam lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. 5Adam lived 930 years in all, and then he died.

6Seth lived 105 years, and he had a son named Enosh. 7After Enosh was born, Seth lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters. 8Seth lived 912 years in all, and then he died.

9Enosh lived 90 years, and he had a son named Kenan. 10After Kenan was born, Enosh lived 815 years and had other sons and daughters. 11Enosh lived 905 years in all, and then he died.

12Kenan lived 70 years, and he had a son named Mahalalel. 13After Mahalalel was born, Kenan lived 840 years and had other sons and daughters. 14Kenan lived 910 years in all, and then he died.

15Mahalalel lived 65 years, and he had a son named Jared. 16After Jared was born, Mahalalel lived 830 years and had other sons and daughters. 17Mahalalel lived 895 years in all, and then he died.

18Jared lived 162 years, and he had a son named Enoch. 19After Enoch was born, Jared lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. 20Jared lived 962 years in all, and then he died.

Enoch Walks with God

21Enoch lived 65 years, and he had a son named Methuselah. 22After Methuselah was born, Enoch walked with God 300 years and had other sons and daughters. 23Enoch lived 365 years in all. 24Enoch walked with God, and then he was gone, because God took him.

25Methuselah lived 187 years, and he had a son named Lamech. 26After Lamech was born, Methuselah lived 782 years and had other sons and daughters. 27Methuselah lived 969 years in all, and then he died.

Noah Is Born

28Lamech lived 182 years, and he had a son. 29He named him Noah and said, "This one will bring us comfort in our work and in the toil of our hands, because of the ground the LORD has cursed."

30After Noah was born, Lamech lived 595 years and had other sons and daughters. 31Lamech lived 777 years in all, and then he died. 32Noah was 500 years old when he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
