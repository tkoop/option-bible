The LORD Speaks Through Haggai

1In the second year of King Darius, on the first day of the sixth month, the word of the LORD came through the prophet Haggai to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest. 2This is what the LORD of Hosts says: "These people say, 'The time has not come. It is not time to build the LORD's house.'"

The LORD Challenges Their Priorities

3Then the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet: 4"Is it time for you to live in your panelledpanelledpaneled houses while this house is in ruins?" 5Now this is what the LORD of Hosts says: "Think carefully about how you are living. 6You plant a lot, but you harvest little. You eat, but you are still hungry. You drink, but you are still thirsty. You put on clothes, but you are still cold. And the worker who earns money puts it in a bag with holes."

The Call to Build the Temple

7This is what the LORD of Hosts says: "Think carefully about how you are living. 8Go up to the mountains, bring wood, and build the house. Then I will take pleasure in it and be honouredhonouredhonored," says the LORD. 9"You expected a big harvest, but it was small. And when you brought it home, I blew it away. Why?" declares the LORD of Hosts. "Because MymyMy house is in ruins while each of you is busy with his own house. 10That is why the sky has held back its dew and the ground has held back its crops. 11I have called for a drought on the land and hills, on grain, new wine, olive oil, and on everything the ground produces, on people and animals, and on all the work of your hands."

The People Obey and Work

12Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and all the rest of the people obeyed the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, because the LORD their God had sent him. And the people feared the LORD. 13Then Haggai, the messenger of the LORD, gave the LORD's message to the people: "I am with you," declares the LORD. 14Then the LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and the spirit of all the rest of the people. They came and worked on the house of the LORD of Hosts, their God. 15They did this on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.
