The LORD Judges Moab

1This is what the LORD says: "For Moab's many sins, and even more, I will not hold back MymyMy anger. Because he burned the bones of Edom's king to lime. 2I will send fire on Moab, and it will burn up the forts of Kerioth. Moab will die in the noise of war, with shouts and the blast of a trumpet. 3I will destroy Moab's ruler and kill all its officials with him," says the LORD.

The LORD Judges Judah

4This is what the LORD says: "For Judah's many sins, and even more, I will not hold back MymyMy anger. They rejected the law of the LORD and did not keep HishisHis rules. Their lies led them astray, the same lies their ancestors followed. 5I will send fire on Judah, and it will burn up the forts of Jerusalem."

The LORD Judges Israel

6This is what the LORD says: "For Israel's many sins, and even more, I will not hold back MymyMy anger. They sell innocent people for silver and poor people for a pair of sandals. 7They trample the heads of the poor into the dust of the ground and deny justice to the oppressed. Father and son sleep with the same young woman, and so they dishonourdishonourdishonor MymyMy holy name. 8They lie down beside every altar on clothes taken as security, and in the house of their god they drink wine paid as fines."

9"Yet I destroyed the Amorites before them. They were as tall as cedars and as strong as oaks. I destroyed their fruit above and their roots below. 10I brought you up out of Egypt and led you forty years in the desert to give you the land of the Amorites. 11I raised up prophets from among your sons and Nazirites from among your young men. Is this not true, people of Israel?" declares the LORD.

12"But you made the Nazirites drink wine and commanded the prophets not to prophesy."

The LORD's Coming Punishment

13"Now then, I will crush you as a cart crushes when loaded with grain. 14The fast will not escape, the strong will not keep their strength, and the warrior will not save his life. 15The archer will not stand his ground, the fast runner will not get away, and the horseman will not save his life. 16Even the bravest warriors will run away naked on that day," declares the LORD.
