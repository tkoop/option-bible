Job Curses the Day He Was Born

1After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day. 2Job answered and said, 3"Let the day die on which I was born, and the night that said, 'A boy has been conceived.' 4Let that day be darkness. Let God above not look for it, and let no light shine on it. 5Let darkness and deep shadow claim it. Let a cloud settle on it. Let the blackness of the day terrify it. 6As for that night, let thick darkness take it. Let it not be counted among the days of the year. Let it not enter the number of the months. 7Yes, let that night be empty. Let no shout of joy come into it. 8Let those who curse days curse it—those who are ready to wake up Leviathan. 9Let the stars of its twilight go dark. Let it wait for light but find none. Let it not see the dawn. 10For it did not shut the doors of my mother's womb or hide trouble from my eyes.

Job Wishes He Had Died at Birth

"11Why did I not die at birth? Why did I not breathe my last when I came from the womb? 12Why did knees welcome me? Why did breasts let me nurse? 13For now I would be lying down and quiet. I would be asleep and at rest. 14I would be with kings and counselors of the earth, who rebuilt ruins for themselves. 15Or I would be with princes who have gold and fill their houses with silver. 16Or why was I not hidden like a baby born too soon, like infants who never saw light? 17There the wicked stop causing trouble, and the weary rest. 18There prisoners rest together and do not hear the voice of the slave driver. 19The small and the great are there, and the slave is free from his master.

Job Asks Why the Suffering Still Live

"20Why is light given to one who suffers, and life to those bitter in soul, 21who long for death, but it does not come, and dig for it more than for hidden treasure? 22They greatly rejoice and are glad when they find the grave. 23Why is light given to a man whose way is hidden, whom God has shut in? 24For my sighs come before my food, and my groans pour out like water. 25What I feared has come upon me, and what I dreaded has happened to me. 26I am not at peace. I have no quiet. I have no rest, only trouble."
