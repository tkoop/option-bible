The People Refuse Jeremiah's Warning

1After Jeremiah finished telling all the people everything the LORD their God had sent him to say—all the words the LORD their God had sent him to speak to them— 2Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah, and all the arrogant men said to Jeremiah, "YouyouYou are lying! The LORD our God has not sent you to say, 'YouyouYou must not go to Egypt to live there.' 3Instead, Baruch son of Neriah is turning you against us so we will be handed over to the Babylonians. They will kill us or carry us away to Babylon!"

The Remnant Flees to Egypt

4So Johanan son of Kareah, all the army commanders, and all the people refused to obey the voice of the LORD and stay in the land of Judah. 5Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders took the whole remnant of Judah—those who had returned from all the nations where they had been scattered—to live in the land of Judah. 6They took the men, women, and children, the king's daughters, and everyone else Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. They also took Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah. 7They went to the land of Egypt because they did not obey the voice of the LORD, and they travelledtravelledtraveled as far as Tahpanhes.

Jeremiah Hides Stones in Egypt

8Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes: 9"Take some large stones in your hand and hide them in the clay at the brick kiln near the entrance to Pharaoh's palace in Tahpanhes. Do this where the men of Judah can see. Then say to them, 'This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I will send for MymyMy servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and I will set his throne over these stones I have hidden. He will spread his royal canopy over them. 11He will come and strike the land of Egypt. Those meant for death will die; those meant for captivity will be taken captive; and those meant for the sword will be killed by the sword. 12He will set fire to the temples of Egypt's gods and burn them. He will take their gods captive and wrap himself in the land of Egypt like a shepherd wraps himself in his coat, and he will leave there safely. 13He will break the sacred pillars of the temple of the sun in Egypt, and he will burn down the temples of Egypt's gods.'
