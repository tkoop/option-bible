Paul Appeals with Gentleness

1Now I, Paul, appeal to you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ. I am humble when I am face to face with you, but I am bold toward you when I am away.

2I beg you that when I come, I will not have to be as bold as I expect to be toward some people. They think we live by human standards.

3We do live in the body, but we do not fight our battles the way the world does. 4The weapons we fight with are not human weapons. They are powerful through God to tear down strongholds. 5We tear down arguments and every proud idea that rises against the knowledge of God. We take every thought captive and make it obey Christ. 6We are ready to punish every act of disobedience, but only after your obedience is complete.

Paul's Authority and Boldness

7You look at the outside of things. If anyone is sure he belongs to Christ, he should think again. We belong to Christ just as much as he does.

8Even if I boast a little too much about the authority the Lord gave us to build you up, not tear you down, I will not be ashamed. 9I do not want to seem like I am trying to scare you with my letters.

10Some say, "HishisHis letters are heavy and strong, but when he is here in person he is weak, and his speaking is not impressive."

11Anyone who says that should remember this: What we say in our letters when we are away, we will also do when we are there with you.

12We do not dare to put ourselves in the same class as some people who praise themselves. They measure themselves by their own standards and compare themselves with themselves, and that shows no wisdom.

Boasting Only in the Lord

13We will not boast about things beyond our limits. We will stay within the area God has given us, and that area includes you.

14We are not going too far, as if we had not reached you. We were the first to bring the good news of Christ to you.

15We do not boast about work done by others. Instead, we hope that as your faith grows, our work among you will grow even more. 16Then we can preach the good news in places beyond you. We do not want to boast about work already done in someone else's area.

17"Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord."

18It is not the person who praises himself who is approved. It is the person the Lord praises.
