The Borders of Canaan

1The LORD spoke to Moses. 2He said, "Command the Israelites and tell them that when they enter Canaan, this land will be their inheritance. It is the land of Canaan with these borders:

3"The southern border will be the WildernesswildernessWilderness of Zin along the border of Edom. It will start at the southern end of the Dead SeaseaSea. 4It will cross south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continue to Zin, and end south of Kadesh Barnea. Then it will go to Hazar Addar and pass on to Azmon. 5There it will turn toward the BrookbrookBrook of Egypt and end at the Mediterranean SeaseaSea.

6"The western border will be the coast of the Mediterranean SeaseaSea. This will be their border on the west.

7"The northern border will be like this: Draw a line from the Mediterranean SeaseaSea to Mount Hor. 8From Mount Hor draw a line to Lebo Hamath. Then the border will go to Zedad, 9continue to Ziphron, and end at Hazar Enan. This will be their border on the north.

10"The eastern border will be like this: Draw a line from Hazar Enan to Shepham. 11Then the border will go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain. It will continue along the slopes east of the SeaseaSea of Galilee. 12Then it will go down along the Jordan River and end at the Dead SeaseaSea. This will be their land, with borders on every side."

Land for the Tribes

13Moses commanded the Israelites, "This is the land you are to receive by lot as your inheritance. The the LORD has commanded that it be given to the nine and a half tribes. 14The tribes of Reuben and Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their inheritance. 15These two and a half tribes received their inheritance east of the Jordan, across from Jericho, to the east."

Leaders to Divide the Land

16The LORD spoke to Moses. 17He said, "These are the names of the men who will divide the land for you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun. 18Appoint one leader from each tribe to help divide the land.

"19These are their names: from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh; 20from the tribe of Simeon, Shemuel son of Ammihud; 21from the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon; 22from the tribe of Dan, Bukki son of Jogli; 23from the tribe of Manasseh, Hanniel son of Ephod; 24from the tribe of Ephraim, Kemuel son of Shiphtan; 25from the tribe of Zebulun, Elizaphan son of Parnach; 26from the tribe of Issachar, Paltiel son of Azzan; 27from the tribe of Asher, Ahihud son of Shelomi; 28from the tribe of Naphtali, Pedahel son of Ammihud.

"29These are the men the the LORD commanded to give the Israelites their inheritance in the land of Canaan."
