Jacob Meets God's Angels

1Jacob went on his way, and God's angels met him. 2When Jacob saw them, he said, "This is God's camp." So he called that place Mahanaim.

Jacob Prepares to Meet Esau

3Jacob sent messengers ahead to his brother Esau in the land of Seir, the country of Edom. 4He told them, "Say to my lord Esau, 'I, your servant Jacob, have been staying with Laban until now. 5I have oxen, donkeys, sheep, and male and female servants. I am sending this message to find favourfavourfavor with you.'" 6ThetheThe messengers came back to Jacob and said, "We went to your brother Esau, and he is coming to meet you with four hundred men." 7Jacob was very afraid and upset. He divided the people who were with him into two groups, along with the flocks, herds, and camels. 8He said, "If Esau comes to one group and attacks it, the other group may escape."

9Then Jacob said, "O God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, O the LORD, who said to me, 'Go back to your country and your relatives, and I will make things go well for you,' 10I am not worthy of all the kindness and faithfulness YouyouYou have shown YouryourYour servant. I crossed this Jordan with only my staff, and now I have become two camps. 11Please save me from my brother, from Esau. I am afraid he will come and attack me, along with the mothers and their children. 12But YouyouYou have said, 'I will surely make things go well for you and make your descendants like the sand of the sea, too many to count.'"

Jacob Sends a Gift to Esau

13That night Jacob stayed there. From what he had with him, he took a gift for his brother Esau: 14two hundred female goats and twenty male goats, two hundred ewes and twenty rams, 15thirty female camels with their young, forty cows and ten bulls, twenty female donkeys and ten male donkeys.

16He put them into the hands of his servants, each herd by itself. He told his servants, "Go ahead of me and keep some space between each herd." 17He told the first servant, "When my brother Esau meets you and asks, 'Who do you belong to? Where are you going? And whose are these animals in front of you?' 18say, 'They belong to your servant Jacob. They are a gift sent to my lord Esau. And he is coming behind us.'" 19HeheHe also told the second, third, and all the others who followed the herds, "Say the same thing to Esau when you meet him. 20Say, 'your servant Jacob is coming behind us.'" HeheHe thought, "I will calm his anger with the gift that goes ahead of me. After that, I will see his face. Maybe he will accept me."

21So the gift went ahead of him, while he stayed that night in the camp.

Jacob Wrestles with God

22During the night Jacob got up. He took his two wives, his two female servants, and his eleven sons, and crossed the ford of the Jabbok. 23He took them and sent them across the stream, and he sent over everything he had. 24Jacob was left alone, and a man wrestled with him until daybreak.

25When the man saw that he could not overpower Jacob, he touched the socket of Jacob's hip. So Jacob's hip was put out of joint as he wrestled with him. 26Then the man said, "Let me go, for the dawn is breaking." But Jacob said, "I will not let YouyouYou go unless YouyouYou bless me." 27The man asked him, "What is your name?" "Jacob," he answered.

28Then the man said, "your name will no longer be Jacob, but Israel, because you have struggled with God and with men and have overcome." 29Jacob said, "Please tell me YouryourYour name." But he replied, "Why do you ask my name?" Then he blessed him there. 30So Jacob called the place Peniel, saying, "I have seen God face to face, and my life was spared."

31The sun rose as he passed Penuel, and he was limping because of his hip. 32That is why the people of Israel do not eat the tendon of the hip socket to this day, because the man touched the socket of Jacob's hip near the tendon.
