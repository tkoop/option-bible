The Righteous Are Taken Away

1The righteous die, and no one pays attention. Faithful people are taken away, and no one understands that they are spared from evil. 2Those who live right go in peace; they find rest in their beds.

Israel Condemned for Idolatry

3“But you, come here, you sons of a sorceress, children of an adulterer and a prostitute. 4Who are you mocking? Who are you sneering at and sticking out your tongue at? Are you not children of rebellion, offspring of lies, 5who burn with lust among the oaks, under every green tree, who slaughter children in the valleys, under the cracks in the rocks?

6“Your share is among the smooth stones of the valley; yes, they are your lot. You have even poured out a drink offering to them and offered a grain offering. Should I overlook these things? 7On a high and lofty mountain you set your bed, and there you went up to offer sacrifices. 8Behind the door and doorpost you set up your memorial. Turning away from MemeMe, you uncovered your bed; you climbed into it and made it wide. You made an agreement with those whose bed you loved; you looked at their nakedness.

9“You went to the king with oil and increased your perfumes. You sent your messengers far away; you went down even to the grave. 10You wore yourself out on your many journeys, but you did not say, ‘It is hopeless.’ You found new strength, so you did not give up. 11Who did you fear and dread, so that you lied and did not remember MemeMe or take it to heart? Have I not been silent for a long time, and you do not fear MemeMe?

12“I will point out your righteousness and your works, but they will not help you. 13When you cry out, let your collection of idols save you! The wind will carry them off; a breath will take them away. But whoever takes refuge in MemeMe will inherit the land and possess MymyMy holy mountain.”

God Promises Healing and Peace

14And it will be said: “Build up, build up, prepare the way, remove the stumbling blocks from the path of MymyMy people.”

15For this is what the high and lifted up OneoneOne says, the OneoneOne who lives forever, whose name is HolyholyHoly: “I live in the high and holy place, and also with the crushed and humble in spirit, to revive the spirit of the lowly and to revive the heart of the crushed.

16“For I will not argue forever, nor will I always be angry, because then the human spirit would give out before MemeMe, and the people I created. 17I was angry because of his sinful greed, so I struck him. I hid MymyMy face, and I was angry. Yet he kept going, turning away in the stubbornness of his heart. 18I have seen his ways, but I will heal him. I will guide him and give comfort to him and to those who mourn for him,

19“bringing praise to their lips. Peace, peace to those far away and those near,” says the LORD, “and I will heal them.”

No Peace for the Wicked

20But the wicked are like the restless sea, which cannot be still. Its waves churn up mud and dirt. 21“There is no peace,” says my God, “for the wicked.”
