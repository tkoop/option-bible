Bildad Answers Job

1Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:

Bildad Rebukes Job

2“How long will you keep talking? Understand this, and then we will speak. 3Why are we treated like cattle? Why do we look stupid to you? 4You who tear yourself apart in anger—should the earth be abandoned for you? Should the rock be moved from its place?”

The Wicked Person's Ruin

5“Yes, the light of the wicked goes out, and the spark of his fire stops glowing. 6The light in his tent grows dark, and his lamp above him goes out. 7His strong steps get shorter, and his own plan knocks him down. 8His own feet throw him into a net, and he walks right into a trap. 9A trap grabs him by the heel, and a snare holds him tight. 10A rope is hidden in the ground for him, and a trap waits for him on the path. 11Terrors scare him from every side and chase him at his heels.”

Forgotten and Destroyed

12“His strength gives out, and disaster waits to trip him up. 13It eats away pieces of his skin, and death’s firstborn devours his body. 14He is torn from the tent he trusted and marched to the king of terrors. 15Nothing of his remains in his tent; sulphursulphursulfur is scattered over his home. 16His roots dry up below, and his branches wither above. 17His name is forgotten on EarthEarthearth, and he has no fame in the streets. 18He is pushed from light into darkness and driven from the world. 19He has no child or grandchild among his people, and no one survives where he lived. 20People who come later are shocked by his ruin, and those who came before are seized with horror. 21Surely these are the homes of the wicked, and this is the place of someone who does not know God.”
