A Call to All People

1Listen to this, all you nations; pay attention, everyone living in the world. 2Both common people and leaders, rich and poor together. 3My mouth will speak wisdom, and my heart will think about understanding. 4I will turn my ear to a wise saying and explain my riddle with a harp.

Wealth Cannot Buy Life

5Why should I be afraid in hard times, when the wrongs I have done surround me? 6They trust in their wealth and brag about how rich they are. 7No one can buy back another person's life or pay God the price to save him. 8It costs too much to redeem a life; the price can never be enough. 9No one can pay enough to live forever and never decay. 10Everyone sees that wise people die; fools and ignorant people perish too and leave their wealth to others. 11Their graves are their homes forever, their houses for all time, even if they named lands after themselves. 12But a person does not keep his glory; he is like an animal that dies. 13This is the way of foolish people, and those who come after them agree with what they say.

God Redeems the Soul

14Like sheep they are headed for the grave; death will be their shepherd. In the morning, good people will rule over them. Their bodies will wear away in the grave, and they will have no home. 15But God will buy back my life from the grave, because HeheHe will take me to HimselfhimselfHimself.
