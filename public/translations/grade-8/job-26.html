Job Replies to Bildad

1Then Job answered:

Questions for Bildad

2“How have you helped the powerless? How have you saved the weak arm? 3What advice have you given to someone without wisdom? What knowledge have you shown so clearly? 4To whom have you spoken these words? Whose breath came from you?”

God's Power Over Creation

5“The dead tremble beneath the waters, and so do those who live there. 6Sheol is naked before HimhimHim, and Abaddon has no cover.

7“He stretches out the north over empty space; HeheHe hangs the earth on nothing. 8He wraps the waters in HishisHis thick clouds, but the clouds do not burst under their weight. 9He covers the face of the full moon, spreading HishisHis cloud over it. 10He has marked a boundary on the water's surface, where light and darkness meet.

11“The pillars of Heavenheavenheaven tremble and are amazed at HishisHis rebuke. 12By HishisHis power HeheHe stilled the sea; by HishisHis wisdom HeheHe shattered Rahab. 13By HishisHis breath the skies were cleared; HishisHis hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

The Mystery of God's Power

14“Indeed, these are only the edges of HishisHis ways. What a faint whisper we hear of HimhimHim! Who can understand the thunder of HishisHis power?”
