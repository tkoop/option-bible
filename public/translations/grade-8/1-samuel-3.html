Samuel Hears God's Call

1Now the boy Samuel was serving the LORD before Eli. In those days the word of the LORD was rare, and visions were not common. 2One day Eli, whose eyes were so weak he could not see, was lying down in his place. 3The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was lying down in the LORD's temple, where the ark of God was.

4Then the LORD called Samuel. He answered, "Here I am."

5Samuel ran to Eli and said, "Here I am, because you called me."

But Eli said, "I did not call you. Go back and lie down." So Samuel went back and lay down.

6The LORD called Samuel again. Samuel got up and went to Eli. He said, "Here I am, because you called me."

But Eli said, "MymyMy son, I did not call you. Go back and lie down."

God Gives Samuel a Message

10Then the LORD came and stood there. He called as before, "Samuel! Samuel!"

Samuel answered, "Speak, for your servant is listening."

11The LORD said to Samuel, "Look, I am about to do something in Israel that will make both ears of everyone who hears it tingle.

"On that day I will carry out against Eli everything I spoke about his family, from beginning to end.

"I have told him that I will judge his family forever because of the sin he knew about. His sons cursed God, and he did not stop them.

"Therefore I swore to Eli's family that their sin will never be forgiven by sacrifice or offering."

15Samuel lay down until morning. Then he opened the doors of the LORD's house. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.
