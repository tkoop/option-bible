Rules About Vows

1Moses spoke to the leaders of Israel’s tribes and said, “This is what the LORD has commanded: 2If a man makes a vow to the LORD or swears an oath to bind himself, he must not break his word. He must do everything he promised.

Vows Made by Young Women

3If a woman makes a vow to the LORD or binds herself with a promise while she is young and living in her father’s house, 4and her father hears about her vow or promise but says nothing, then all her vows and promises will stand. 5But if her father forbids her on the day he hears about it, then none of her vows or promises will stand. The LORD will forgive her, because her father forbade her.

Vows After Marriage

6If a woman marries while she is under a vow or a thoughtless promise she made, 7and her husband hears about it but says nothing, then her vows and promises will stand. 8But if her husband forbids her when he hears about it, he cancels the vow or promise she made, and the LORD will forgive her.

9Every vow or promise made by a widow or a divorced woman will stand against her.

10If a woman in her husband’s house makes a vow or binds herself with an oath, 11and her husband hears about it but says nothing and does not forbid her, then all her vows and promises will stand. 12But if her husband cancels them on the day he hears about them, then nothing she vowed or promised will stand. 13Her husband can confirm or cancel any vow or oath she makes to deny herself. 14But if her husband says nothing to her day after day, then he confirms all her vows and promises. He confirms them by saying nothing on the day he hears about them. 15If he cancels them after he hears about them, then he will carry her guilt.

Summary of the Law

16These are the rules the LORD commanded Moses about a man and his wife, and about a father and his young daughter who still lives in his house.
