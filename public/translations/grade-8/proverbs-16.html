The LORD Directs Our Plans

1People may plan what they want to say, but the answer comes from the LORD. 2People often think their own ways are right, but the LORD looks at what is in their hearts.

3Give your work to the LORD, and HeheHe will make your plans succeed. 4The LORD made everything for a reason, even wicked people for a day of trouble.

5The LORD hates a proud heart, and a proud person will not go unpunished. 6Love and truth can cover sin, and respect for the LORD turns a person away from evil. 7When a person's ways please the LORD, even his enemies will be at peace with him.

8It is better to have a little with what is right than great wealth gained unfairly. 9People plan their path, but the LORD decides their steps.

Wisdom, Words, and Kings

10A king's words carry great weight, and he will not be wrong in judgmentjudgementjudgment. 11Honest scales and balances belong to the LORD, and all the weights are HishisHis work. 12It is wrong for kings to do evil, because a throne is built on doing what is right.

13Kings delight in honest words, and they love those who speak the truth. 14A king's anger is like a messenger of death, but a wise person can calm it. 15A king's smile brings life, and his favourfavourfavor is like refreshing rain.

16Getting wisdom is better than getting gold, and choosing understanding is better than silver. 17The path of good people turns away from evil, and those who guard their way protect their lives. 18Pride comes before ruin, and a proud spirit comes before a fall.

19It is better to be humble among poor people than to share stolen goods with the proud. 20A person who thinks carefully about a matter will find good, and blessed is the one who trusts in the LORD. 21A wise person is called understanding, and pleasant words add to learning.

22Understanding is a source of life to those who have it, but fools suffer because of their own foolishness. 23A wise person's heart guides his mouth, and his words add to learning. 24Pleasant words are like honey, sweet to the soul and healing to the body.

Right Paths and Wrong Choices

25There is a way that looks right to a person, but it leads to death. 26A worker's hunger drives him to work hard. 27A worthless person digs up trouble, and his words burn like fire.

28A twisted person stirs up fights, and a gossip separates close friends. 29A violent person tricks his neighbourneighbourneighbor and leads him down a bad path. 30Someone who narrows his eyes to plan evil and tightens his lips to cause harm is plotting trouble.

31GreyGreyGray hair is a crown of glory when it is found in a life of doing right. 32Being slow to anger is better than being a strong warrior, and controlling your spirit is better than capturing a city. 33People cast lots to decide things, but the LORD controls how they fall.
