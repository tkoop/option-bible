Job Describes His Pain

1Job answered: 2“If only my grief were weighed, and my trouble were put on the scales with it! 3Then it would be heavier than the sand of the seas, so my words have been rash. 4The arrows of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty are in me; my spirit drinks their poison. The terrors of God are lined up against me.

5“Does a wild donkey bray over grass, or an ox low over its feed? 6Is bland food eaten without salt, or is there taste in the white of an egg? 7I refuse to touch them. They are like sickening food to me.

8“Oh, that I might get what I ask for, that God would grant my desire! 9That God would be willing to crush me, to let loose HishisHis hand and cut me off! 10Then I would still have this comfort. Yes, I would rejoice in pain that will not stop, because I have not denied the words of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne.

11“What strength do I have, that I should wait? What end do I have, that I should be patient? 12Is my strength like stone, or is my flesh bronze? 13Is there no help in me? Has wisdom been driven from me?

Friends Who Fail Like Dry Streams

14“A friend should show kindness to someone who is suffering. Anyone who withholds it forsakes the fear of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty. 15My brothers have been as false as a stream, like brooks that dry up, 16dark with ice and hidden by snow. 17But when it gets hot, they dry up; in the heat, they vanish from their place.

18“Caravans turn off their path; they go up into the wasteland and die. 19The caravans of Tema look for water; the travelers of Sheba hope to find it. 20They are ashamed because they trusted; they arrive and are confused. 21So now you are nothing. You see terror and are afraid.

Job Asks for Honest Help

22“Did I ever say, ‘Give me something,’ or, ‘Pay a bribe for me from your wealth’? 23Or, ‘Save me from the enemy’s hand,’ or, ‘Redeem me from the hand of the cruel’? 24Teach me, and I will be quiet. Help me understand what I have done wrong.

25“How powerful are honest words! But what do your arguments prove? 26Do you mean to correct my words and treat a suffering person’s words as wind? 27You would even cast lots for orphans and sell your friend.

28“But now, please look at me. I will not lie to your face. 29Turn back; let there be no injustice. Turn back; my innocence is still there. 30Is there injustice on my tongue? Does my mouth not know what is evil?”
