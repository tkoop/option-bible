God Speaks to Israel

1Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, people of Israel, against the whole family I brought up from Egypt. 2“Only you have I chosen among all the families on EarthEarthearth. That is why I will punish you for all your sins.”

Cause and Effect

3Do two people walk together unless they have agreed to meet? 4Does a lion roar in the forest when it has no prey? Does a young lion growl from its den unless it has caught something? 5Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground if there is no bait for it? Does a trap spring up from the ground without catching something? 6If a trumpet sounds in a city, won’t the people tremble? If trouble comes to a city, hasn’t the LORD done it?

7Surely the Lord the LORD does nothing without first showing HishisHis plan to HishisHis servants the prophets. 8A lion has roared—who will not be afraid? The Lord the LORD has spoken—who can keep from prophesying?

Warnings for Samaria

9Announce this to the palaces of Ashdod and to the palaces of Egypt: “Gather on the mountains of Samaria and see the great trouble inside her and the cruelty among her people.”

10“They do not know how to do what is right,” declares the LORD, “those who store up violence and robbery in their palaces.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Israel

11Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: “An enemy will surround the land. He will tear down your strongholds and rob your palaces.” 12This is what the LORD says: “As a shepherd saves from a lion’s mouth only two legs or a piece of an ear, so the people of Israel living in Samaria will be saved—with just a corner of a bed or a piece of a couch.”

13Hear and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord the LORD, the God of Armies. 14“On the day I punish Israel for their sins, I will also punish the altars of Bethel. The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground. 15I will strike the winter house along with the summer house. The houses of ivory will be destroyed, and the great houses will come to an end,” declares the LORD.
