Better a Dry Crust with Peace

1Better a dry crust with peace and quiet than a house full of feasting with strife. 2A wise servant will rule over a shameful son and share the inheritance with the brothers. 3A crucible is for silver and a furnace for gold, but the LORD tests the heart. 4A wicked man listens to evil lips; a liar gives ear to a destructive tongue.

Mocking the Poor Brings Trouble

5Whoever mocks the poor shows contempt for their MakermakerMaker; whoever rejoices at disaster will not go unpunished. 6Grandchildren are the crown of the aged, and the glory of children is their fathers. 7Smooth words don't fit a fool; lying words are even worse for a noble person. 8A bribe is like a lucky charm to the one who gives it; wherever he turns, he succeeds.

Love, Rebuke, and Friendship

9Whoever covers an offenceoffenceoffense seeks love, but whoever repeats a matter separates close friends. 10A rebuke goes deeper into a person of understanding than a hundred lashes into a fool. 11An evil person seeks only rebellion, so a cruel messenger will be sent against him. 12Better to meet a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool lost in his folly. 13If anyone repays evil for good, evil will never leave his house. 14Starting a quarrel is like letting water break through a dam; so drop the matter before a fight breaks out. 15Acquitting the guilty and condemning the innocent—both are detestable to the LORD. 16Why should a fool have money in hand to buy wisdom when he has no sense?

Family, Friends, and Foolishness

17A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for trouble. 18A person lacking judgmentjudgementjudgment shakes hands in pledge and puts up security for his neighbourneighbourneighbor. 19Whoever loves sin loves strife; whoever builds his gate high invites destruction. 20A person with a crooked heart will not prosper; one with a deceitful tongue will bring him down. 21Whoever fathers a fool brings grief; the father of a fool has no joy. 22A cheerful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones. 23A wicked man accepts a bribe in secret to twist the course of justice. 24Wisdom is the goal of the discerning, but a fool's eyes wander to the ends of the earth. 25A foolish son brings grief to his father and bitterness to his mother. 26It is not good to punish an innocent person or to flog nobles for their honesty. 27A person of knowledge restrains his words, and a person of understanding stays calm. 28Even a fool is thought wise if he keeps silent; he seems discerning if he holds his tongue.
