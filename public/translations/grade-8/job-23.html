Job Speaks Again

1Then Job answered:

2"Even today, my complaint is bitter, and my burden feels heavy despite my groans.

3"If only I knew where to find HimhimHim, I would go to HishisHis throne. 4I would plead my case before HimhimHim and fill my mouth with arguments. 5I would know how HeheHe would answer me and understand what HeheHe would say.

6"Would HeheHe use HishisHis great power against me? No—HeheHe would pay attention to me. 7There an upright person could argue with HimhimHim, and I would be set free forever from my JudgejudgeJudge.

Job Searches for God

8"If I go east, HeheHe is not there; if I go west, I cannot see HimhimHim. 9If HeheHe turns to the north, I cannot find HimhimHim; if HeheHe turns to the south, I do not see HimhimHim.

Job Trusts His Upright Life

10"Yet HeheHe knows the path I have taken; when HeheHe has tested me, I will come out like gold. 11My feet have followed HishisHis tracks, and I have kept HishisHis way without turning aside. 12I have not turned away from the commands of HishisHis lips; I have treasured HishisHis words more than my daily bread.

Job Fears God's Power

13"But HeheHe never changes, and who can oppose HimhimHim? He does what HeheHe wants. 14He carries out HishisHis plan for me, and HeheHe has many plans like it. 15So I am terrified in HishisHis presence; when I think about it, I am afraid of HimhimHim. 16God has made my heart weak; the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty has terrified me. 17Yet I am not silenced by the darkness, or by the thick darkness that covers my face."
