False Prophets in Israel

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2"SonsonSon of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are now prophesying. Tell those who prophesy out of their own imagination: 'Hear the word of the LORD!'"

3This is what the Lord the LORD says: "Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing! 4Your prophets, O Israel, are like foxes prowling through the rubble. 5You have not stepped into the gaps or built up a wall for the house of Israel to stand in the battle on the day of the LORD."

6"They see false visions and speak lying divinations. They say, 'This is the LORD's declaration,' when the LORD has not sent them, yet they hope their words come true. 7Have you not seen a false vision and spoken a lying divination when you say, 'This is the LORD's declaration,' though I have not spoken?"

8Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: "Because you have spoken empty words and seen lies, look, I am against you, declares the Lord the LORD. 9My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and speak lying divinations.
