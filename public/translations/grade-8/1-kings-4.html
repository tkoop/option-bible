Solomon's Rule and His Officials

1Solomon was king over all Israel. 2These were his top officials: Azariah son of Zadok was the priest. 3Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were secretaries. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder. 4Benaiah son of Jehoiada was in charge of the army. Zadok and Abiathar were priests. 5Azariah son of Nathan was in charge of the district governors. Zabud son of Nathan was a priest and the king's friend. 6Ahishar was in charge of the palace. Adoniram son of Abda was in charge of forced labourlabourlabor.

District Governors and Their Regions

7Solomon had twelve district governors over all Israel. They provided food for the king and his household. Each governor was responsible for one month of the year. 8These are their names: Ben-Hur was in the hill country of Ephraim. 9Ben-Deker was in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan. 10Ben-Hesed was in Arubboth. Sokoh and all the land of Hepher belonged to him. 11Ben-Abinadab was in charge of all Naphath-dor. Solomon's daughter Taphath was his wife. 12Baana son of Ahilud was in Taanach, Megiddo, and all Beth-shean near Zarethan, below Jezreel. His area ran from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and beyond Jokneam. 13Ben-Geber was in Ramoth-gilead. He also controlled the villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead and the region of Argob in Bashan, which had sixty large cities with walls and bronze bars. 14Ahinadab son of Iddo was in Mahanaim. 15Ahimaaz was in Naphtali. He had married Basemath, another daughter of Solomon. 16Baana son of Hushai was in Asher and Bealoth. 17Jehoshaphat son of Paruah was in Issachar. 18Shimei son of Ela was in Benjamin. 19Geber son of Uri was in the land of Gilead, including the lands of Sihon king of the Amorites and Og king of Bashan. There was one governor in that land.

Solomon's Wealth and Peace

20Judah and Israel were as many as the sand on the seashore. They ate, drank, and rejoiced. 21Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the River to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt. Those kingdoms brought tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life. 22Solomon's food for one day was thirty cors of fine flour and sixty cors of meal. 23He also had ten fat oxen, twenty pasture-fed oxen, and one hundred sheep, plus deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened poultry. 24He ruled over everything west of the River, from Tiphsah to Gaza, and he had peace on all sides. 25During Solomon's life, Judah and Israel lived in safety, from Dan to Beersheba. Each man lived in peace and plenty in his own home. 26Solomon had forty thousand stalls for chariot horses and twelve thousand horses. 27Those governors also provided food for King Solomon and everyone who came to his table. They did not fail to bring what was needed. 28Each man brought his supplies to the right place at the right time: barley and straw for the chariot horses and other horses.

Solomon's Great Wisdom

29God gave Solomon great wisdom, very great understanding, and a broad mind like the sand on the seashore. 30Solomon's wisdom was greater than the wisdom of all the people of the East and all the wisdom of Egypt. 31He was wiser than all men—wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. His fame spread to all the surrounding nations. 32He spoke three thousand proverbs and one thousand and five songs. 33He spoke about trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop that grows out of the wall. He also spoke about animals, birds, creeping things, and fish. 34People came from all nations to hear Solomon's wisdom. They were sent by all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom.
