A Man Measures Jerusalem

1I looked up and saw a man. He was holding a measuring line in his hand. 2I asked him, “Where are you going?” He said, “I am going to measure Jerusalem. I want to see how wide it is and how long it is.”

3Then the angel who had been talking with me came out. Another angel came out to meet him. 4The other angel said to him, “Run and tell that young man, ‘Jerusalem will be a city without walls because of the many people and animals living in it.’”

5“For I will be a wall of fire around her,” declares the LORD. “I will be the glory inside her.”
