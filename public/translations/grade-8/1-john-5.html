Born of God and Overcoming the World

1Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God, and everyone who loves God also loves the one born of HimhimHim. 2This is how we know that we love God's children: when we love God and keep HishisHis commandments. 3For loving God means keeping HishisHis commandments, and HishisHis commandments are not hard to carry. 4For everyone born of God overcomes the world. This is the victory that has overcome the world—our faith. 5Who can overcome the world? Only the one who believes that Jesus is the SonsonSon of God.

The Testimony of Water, Blood, and Spirit

6This is the OneoneOne who came by water and blood—Jesus Christ. He did not come by water only, but by water and blood. And the SpiritspiritSpirit is the OneoneOne who testifies, because the SpiritspiritSpirit is the truth. 7For there are three that testify: 8the SpiritspiritSpirit, the water, and the blood. These three agree as one. 9If we accept human testimony, God's testimony is greater, because God has testified about HishisHis SonsonSon. 10The one who believes in the SonsonSon of God has this testimony in himself. The one who does not believe God has made HimhimHim a liar, because he has not believed the testimony God gave about HishisHis SonsonSon. 11And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in HishisHis SonsonSon. 12The one who has the SonsonSon has life; the one who does not have the SonsonSon of God does not have life.

Confidence in Prayer

13I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the SonsonSon of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life. 14This is the confidence we have before HimhimHim: If we ask anything according to HishisHis will, HeheHe hears us. 15And if we know that HeheHe hears us—whatever we ask—we know that we already have what we asked of HimhimHim.

16If anyone sees his brother sinning a sin that does not lead to death, he should ask, and God will give him life. This applies to those who sin in a way that does not lead to death. There is a sin that leads to death; I am not saying he should pray about that. 17All wrongdoing is sin, but there is sin that does not lead to death.

Knowing the True God

18We know that everyone born of God does not keep on sinning. The OneoneOne born of God protects him, and the evil one does not touch him. 19We know that we are of God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one. 20And we know that the SonsonSon of God has come and has given us understanding so that we may know the true OneoneOne. We are in the true OneoneOne—in HishisHis SonsonSon Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life. 21Little children, keep yourselves from idols.
