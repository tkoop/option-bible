The Purpose of Proverbs

1The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel: 2for gaining wisdom and instruction; for understanding words of insight; 3for receiving instruction in wise living, in doing what is right, just, and fair; 4for giving good sense to those who are simple, and knowledge and good judgmentjudgementjudgment to the young; 5let the wise listen and grow in learning, and let the discerning get guidance; 6for understanding proverbs and parables, the sayings and riddles of the wise. 7The fear of the the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

Warnings Against Bad Company

8Listen, my son, to your father's instruction and do not forsake your mother's teaching. 9They are a garland to grace your head and a chain to adorn your neck. 10My son, if sinful men try to lead you astray, do not go along with them. 11If they say, "Come with us; let's wait to ambush someone and kill an innocent person for no reason, 12let's swallow them alive, like the grave, and whole, like those who go down to the pit; 13we will find all kinds of valuable things and fill our houses with stolen goods; 14join us, and we will all share the money"— 15my son, do not walk in their path; keep your feet from their way. 16For their feet run to evil; they are quick to shed blood. 17It is useless to spread a net where every bird can see it. 18But these men set a trap for their own lives; they ambush only themselves. 19Such is the end of everyone who chases dishonest gain; it takes the life of those who get it.

Wisdom Calls Out

20Wisdom calls out in the street; she raises her voice in the public squares. 21She cries out at the busy corners; at the city gates she makes her speech: 22"How long will you who are simple love your simple ways? How long will mockers enjoy mocking and fools hate knowledge? 23Turn back at my warning! Then I will pour out my thoughts to you and make my words known to you. 24But because you refused when I called and no one listened when I reached out my hand, 25because you ignored all my advice and would not accept my warning, 26I will laugh at your trouble; I will mock when terror strikes you— 27when terror strikes you like a storm, when trouble comes on you like a whirlwind, when distress and hardship overwhelm you. 28Then they will call to me, but I will not answer; they will look for me, but will not find me. 29Because they hated knowledge and did not choose to fear the the LORD, 30because they would not accept my advice and spurned my warning, 31they will eat the fruit of their own ways and be filled with the results of their own plans. 32For the stubbornness of the simple will kill them, and the overconfidence of fools will destroy them. 33But whoever listens to me will live in safety and be at ease, without fear of harm."
