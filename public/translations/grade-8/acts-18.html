Paul's Work in Corinth

1After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth. 2There he found a Jew named Aquila, who was from Pontus. Aquila had recently come from Italy with his wife, Priscilla. They had left Rome because Claudius had ordered all Jews to leave the city. Paul went to them. 3Because Paul and Aquila were both tentmakers, Paul stayed with them and worked. 4Every Sabbath, he talked in the synagogue. He tried to persuade both Jews and Greeks.

5When Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia, Paul gave all his time to teaching the message. He told the Jews that Jesus is the Christ. 6But the Jews opposed him and insulted him. So Paul shook out his clothes and said to them, “You are responsible for your own fate. I am innocent. From now on, I will go to the Gentiles.”

7Paul left the synagogue and went into the home of Titius Justus, a man who worshiped God. His house was next door to the synagogue. 8Crispus, the leader of the synagogue, believed in the Lord, along with his whole family. Many people in Corinth also heard Paul, believed, and were baptized.

9One night the Lord spoke to Paul in a vision. He said, “Do not be afraid. Keep speaking. Do not stay silent. 10For I am with you, and no one will attack you to hurt you. I have many people in this city.” 11So Paul stayed there for a year and six months. He taught God's word to the people.

Paul Before Gallio

12While Gallio was governor of Achaia, the Jews joined together against Paul. They brought him to the judge's seat. 13They said, “This man is talking people into worshiping God in a way that breaks our law.”

14Paul was about to speak, but Gallio said to the Jews, “If this were a crime or a serious wrong, I would listen to you. 15But this is about words, names, and your own law. You will have to settle it yourselves. I do not want to be the judge of these things.” 16Then he sent them away from the judge's seat. 17The crowd grabbed Sosthenes, the leader of the synagogue, and beat him in front of the judge's seat. Gallio did not care about any of this.

Paul Leaves Corinth

18Paul stayed in Corinth for many more days. Then he said goodbye to the brothers and sisters. He had his hair cut in Cenchreae because he had made a vow. Then he sailed for Syria, taking Priscilla and Aquila with him. 19They came to Ephesus. Paul left Priscilla and Aquila there. He went into the synagogue and talked with the Jews. 20They asked him to stay longer, but he would not. 21He said goodbye and told them, “I will come back to you again, if God is willing.” Then he sailed away from Ephesus.

22He landed at Caesarea. He went up and greeted the church. Then he went down to Antioch. 23After spending some time there, he left. He travelledtravelledtraveled through the areas of Galatia and Phrygia. He strengthened all the believers.

Apollos Teaches in Ephesus

24A Jew named Apollos came to Ephesus. He was born in Alexandria. He was a skilled speaker and knew the ScripturesscripturesScriptures well. 25He had been taught about the Lord's way. He was very eager in spirit. He spoke and taught the truth about Jesus. But he knew only John's baptism. 26He began to speak boldly in the synagogue. Priscilla and Aquila heard him. They took him aside and explained God's way to him more clearly. 27Apollos wanted to go to Achaia. The believers encouraged him. They wrote to the disciples there to welcome him. When he arrived, he helped the believers very much. They had believed because of God's grace. 28In public, he strongly showed the Jews that Jesus is the Christ. He used the ScripturesscripturesScriptures to prove it.
