The LORD Remembers Israel's Early Faithfulness

1The word of the the LORD came to me.

2“Go and shout this so Jerusalem can hear: This is what the the LORD says. I remember the kindness you showed MemeMe when you were young. I remember the love you had for MemeMe when you were first married to MemeMe. You followed MemeMe through the desert, through land no one had planted. 3Israel was holy to the the LORD. It was the first part of HishisHis harvest. Anyone who ate from it became guilty. Trouble came on them, declares the the LORD.

4“Hear the word of the the LORD, family of Jacob, and all the families of Israel. 5This is what the the LORD says: What wrong did your fathers find in MemeMe? Why did they go far from MemeMe and follow worthless idols until they became worthless themselves?

6“They did not say, ‘Where is the the LORD, who brought us up from Egypt and led us through the desert, through a dry and broken land, through a land of darkness and deep shadow, a land no one travels through and no one lives in?’ 7I brought you into a rich land to eat its fruit and good things. But you came in and made MymyMy land unclean. You made the land I gave you a disgusting thing. 8The priests did not ask, ‘Where is the the LORD?’ Those who handle the law did not know MemeMe. The leaders rebelled against MemeMe. The prophets prophesied by Baal and followed worthless idols.

9“So I will still argue with you, declares the the LORD. I will argue with your children and grandchildren too. 10Cross over to the coasts of Cyprus and look. Send to Kedar and study it carefully. See if anything like this has ever happened. 11Has any nation ever changed its gods, even though they are not real gods? But MymyMy people changed their GlorygloryGlory for something that does not help them. 12Be shocked by this, heavens. Be very afraid and empty, declares the the LORD. 13My people have done two evil things: They left MemeMe, the spring of living water, and dug broken cisterns for themselves. Those cisterns cannot hold water.

The Cost of Turning Away from God

14“Is Israel a slave? Is he someone born in the house? Why has he been plundered? 15Young lions roar at him. They raise their voices. They make his land a wasteland. His cities are burned, with no one living in them.

16“Even the people of Memphis and Tahpanhes have shaved the top of your head. 17Didn’t you bring this on yourself by leaving the the LORD your God while HeheHe was leading you on the way? 18Now what do you gain by going to Egypt to drink the water of the Nile? What do you gain by going to Assyria to drink the water of the Euphrates?

19“Your own evil will punish you. Your turning away will correct you. Know and see that it is evil and bitter to leave the the LORD your God. You do not fear MemeMe, declares the Lord the the LORD of Hosts. 20Long ago I broke your yoke and tore off your chains. But you said, ‘I will not serve.’ On every high hill and under every green tree you bowed down like a prostitute. 21I planted you as a choice vine from the best seed. How did you become a wild vine that is useless to MemeMe?

22“Even if you wash yourself with strong soap and use much lye, your guilt is still stained before MemeMe, declares the Lord the the LORD. 23How can you say, ‘I am not defiled. I did not follow the Baals’? Look at your path in the valley. Know what you have done. You are like a fast young camel running back and forth. 24You are like a wild donkey used to the desert. She sniffs the wind because she is in heat. Who can turn her back? Anyone who looks for her will not get tired. In her mating month, they will find her. 25Keep your feet from going bare and your throat from thirst. But you said, ‘It is no use. I love strangers, and I will follow them.’

26“As a thief is ashamed when caught, so the people of Israel are ashamed. Their kings, officials, priests, and prophets are ashamed too. 27They say to a piece of wood, ‘You are my father,’ and to a stone, ‘You gave birth to me.’ They have turned their backs to MemeMe, not their faces. But when trouble comes, they say, ‘Get up and save us.’ 28Where are the gods you made for yourself? Let them get up and save you when trouble comes. You have as many gods as you have cities, Judah.

29“Why do you argue with MemeMe? You have all sinned against MemeMe, declares the the LORD. 30I punished your children for nothing. They did not learn from it. Your sword destroyed your prophets like a destroying lion. 31You people of this generation, listen to the word of the the LORD: Have I been a desert to Israel, or a land of deep darkness? Why do MymyMy people say, ‘We are free. We will not come to YouyouYou anymore’? 32Can a young woman forget her jewelleryjewelleryjewelry, or a bride forget her sash? Yet MymyMy people have forgotten MemeMe for days without number.

33“How well you plan your way to look for love! So you have even taught evil women your ways. 34On your clothes is found the blood of innocent poor people. You did not catch them breaking in. Yet in spite of all this, 35you say, ‘I am innocent. Surely HishisHis anger has turned away from me.’ But I will judge you because you say, ‘I have not sinned.’

36“How you rush around to change your way! You will be ashamed of Egypt just as you were ashamed of Assyria. 37You will leave there with your hands on your head. For the the LORD has rejected the people you trusted, and you will not succeed with them.”
