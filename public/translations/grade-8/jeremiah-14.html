Drought in Judah

1This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah about the droughts. 2Judah is in mourning, and her city gates grow weak. Her people sit in black on the ground, and Jerusalem cries out. 3The leaders send their servants for water. They come to the cisterns, but find no water. They return with empty jars, ashamed and disgraced, and they cover their heads. 4The ground is cracked because no rain has fallen in the land. The farmers are ashamed and cover their heads. 5Even the doe in the field gives birth and leaves her fawn, because there is no grass. 6Wild donkeys stand on the bare hills and pant like jackals. Their eyes grow weak because there is no grass.
