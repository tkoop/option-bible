Divorce and Remarriage

1If a man marries a woman and she does not please him because he finds something shameful in her, he may write her a certificate of divorce, put it in her hand, and send her away from his house. 2She leaves his house, goes away, and becomes another man's wife. 3If her second husband hates her and writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her away from his house, or if that second husband dies, 4then her first husband, who sent her away, may not take her back as his wife after she has been made unclean. That is detestable to the LORD. You must not bring sin on the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.

Newlyweds and Military Service

5When a man takes a new wife, he must not go out with the army or be given any other duty. For one year he is to stay home and make his wife happy.

Pledges, Lending, and Fair Treatment

6Do not take a hand mill or an upper millstone as a pledge, because that would be taking a person's livelihood as a pledge. 7If a man is found kidnapping one of his fellow Israelites and treats him as a slave or sells him, that thief must die. You must remove the evil from among you.

8In cases of leprous disease, be very careful to do exactly all that the Levitical priests instruct you. Follow carefully what I commanded them. 9Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the way when you came out of Egypt.

10When you make a loan of any kind to your neighbourneighbourneighbor, you must not go into his house to take his pledge. 11You must stand outside, and the man to whom you lend must bring the pledge out to you. 12If he is a poor man, you must not go to sleep while keeping his pledge. 13You must surely return the pledge to him when the sun goes down, so that he may sleep in his cloak and bless you. That will be counted as righteousness for you before the LORD your God.

14You must not cheat a hired worker who is poor and needy, whether he is one of your brothers or a foreigner living in your land in your towns. 15You must pay him his wages the same day, before the sun goes down, because he is poor and needs the money to live. Otherwise he may cry out against you to the LORD, and you will be guilty of sin.

16Fathers must not be put to death for their children, and children must not be put to death for their fathers. Each person must be put to death for his own sin. 17You must not deny justice to the foreigner or the fatherless, and you must not take a widow's garment as a pledge. 18You must remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and the LORD your God redeemed you from there. That is why I command you to do this.

Leaving Harvest for the Needy

19When you reap your harvest in your field and forget a bundle of grain in the field, you must not go back to get it. It will belong to the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands. 20When you beat your olive tree, you must not go over the branches again. It will belong to the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow. 21When you pick grapes from your vineyard, you must not go over it again. It will belong to the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow. 22You must remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. That is why I command you to do this.
