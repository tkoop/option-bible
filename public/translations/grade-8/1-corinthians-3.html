Spiritual Babies in Christ

1Brothers, I could not speak to you as spiritual people. I had to speak to you as people who still follow the world. You were like babies in Christ. 2I gave you milk, not solid food, because you were not ready for it. Even now you are not ready. 3You are still following the world. When jealousy and arguing are among you, doesn't that show you are still following the world? Aren't you living like ordinary people? 4When one person says he follows Paul, and another says he follows Apollos, aren't you just acting like ordinary people?

God Gives the Growth

5So what is Apollos? And what is Paul? We are only servants. Through us you believed, and the Lord gave each of us our job. 6I planted the seed. Apollos watered it. But God made it grow. 7So the one who plants is not important, and the one who waters is not important. Only God, who makes things grow, is important. 8The one who plants and the one who waters are working together. Each will get his own reward for his own work. 9We work together with God. You are God's field. You are God's building.

Building on the Right Foundation

10Because of God's grace given to me, I laid a foundation like a wise builder. Someone else is building on it. But let each person be careful how he builds. 11No one can lay a different foundation. The foundation already laid is Jesus Christ. 12If anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw, 13his work will be shown for what it is. The day will show it, because it will be revealed in fire. The fire will test the quality of each person's work. 14If what he built lasts, he will receive a reward. 15If it burns up, he will lose his work. But he himself will be saved, though only as through fire.

God's Temple and True Wisdom

16Don't you know that you are God's temple? Don't you know that God's SpiritspiritSpirit lives in you? 17If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy him. God's temple is holy, and you are that temple. 18Let no one fool himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, he should become a fool so he can become wise. 19The wisdom of this world is foolishness to God. It is written, "HeheHe catches the wise in their clever tricks."

20And again, "ThetheThe Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are useless." 21So let no one boast about people. All things are yours. 22Whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come—all are yours. 23And you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.
