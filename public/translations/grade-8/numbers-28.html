Daily and Sabbath Offerings

1The LORD said to Moses, 2"Command the Israelites and tell them, 'Be sure you bring MemeMe the food for MymyMy fire offerings, a pleasing aroma, at the proper time.

"'3This is the fire offering you must bring to the LORD: two perfect male lambs, one year old, each day, as a regular burnt offering. 4Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight. 5Along with it, bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter hin of olive oil as a grain offering.

"'6This regular burnt offering began at Mount Sinai. It is a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the LORD. 7Its drink offering is a quarter hin of wine for each lamb. Pour out the drink offering to the LORD in the holy place. 8Offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. It is a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

"'9On the Sabbath day, present two perfect male lambs, one year old, with two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, along with its drink offering. 10This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.'"

Monthly Offerings

"'11At the beginning of every month, you are to present to the LORD a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven perfect male lambs, one year old. 12Along with them, bring three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each bull, and two-tenths of an ephah for the ram.

"'13And bring a tenth of an ephah for each lamb. This is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to the LORD. 14Their drink offerings are half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for a ram, and a quarter hin for each lamb. This is the burnt offering for each month throughout the year. 15In addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering, offer one male goat to the LORD as a sin offering.'"

Passover and Unleavened Bread

"'16ThetheThe Passover to the LORD is on the fourteenth day of the first month. 17On the fifteenth day of that month there is to be a feast. For seven days eat unleavened bread. 18On the first day there is to be a sacred assembly. You must not do any regular work.

"'19Present to the LORD a fire offering, a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven perfect male lambs, one year old. 20Along with them, bring grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each bull, two-tenths for the ram, 21and a tenth for each lamb. 22Include one male goat as a sin offering to pay for your sins.

"'23Offer these in addition to the regular morning burnt offering. 24Offer the same food each day for seven days as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It is to be offered with its drink offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering. 25On the seventh day you are to hold a sacred assembly. You must not do any regular work.'"
