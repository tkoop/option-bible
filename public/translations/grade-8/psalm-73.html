1Surely God is good to Israel, to those with pure hearts. 2But as for me, my feet almost slipped; my steps nearly gave way.

3For I envied the proud when I saw how well the wicked were doing. 4They have no pain when they die; their bodies are well fed. 5They don't suffer like other people, and they aren't troubled like everyone else. 6So pride is their necklace, and violence covers them like a robe. 7Their wealth leads them to sin, and their minds overflow with wild plans. 8They mock others and speak evil; from their high place they threaten people. 9They set their mouths against Heavenheavenheaven, and their tongues strut through the earth. 10So their people turn to them and drink in their words. 11They say, "How can God know?
