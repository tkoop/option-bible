The LORD Reigns in Majesty

1The LORD is King! He is dressed in majesty. the LORD is dressed in strength and armed with power. The world is set firmly in place, so it cannot be shaken. 2Your throne was set up long ago. You are from everlasting.

The LORD Is Stronger Than the Floods

3The rivers rise, O the LORD. The rivers raise their voice. The rivers throw up their crashing waves. 4The LORD on high is stronger than the loud roar of many waters and the mighty waves of the sea.

God's House Is Holy

5Your laws are completely true. Holiness makes YouryourYour house beautiful, O the LORD, for all time.
