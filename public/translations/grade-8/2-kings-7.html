Elisha Promises Food for Samaria

1Elisha said, “Hear the word of the the LORD. This is what the the LORD says: ‘About this time tomorrow, at the gate of Samaria, a seah of fine flour will sell for a shekel, and two seahs of barley for a shekel.’”

2The officer who helped the king stand answered the man of God, “Look, even if the the LORD opened windows in Heavenheavenheaven, could this happen?”

Elisha answered, “You will see it with your eyes, but you will not eat any of it.”

Four Lepers Find the Aramean Camp Empty

3Four men with leprosy were at the city gate. They said to each other, “Why are we sitting here until we die?”

4“If we say, ‘Let’s go into the city,’ the famine is there, and we will die. If we stay here, we will die too. So let’s go over to the Aramean camp. If they spare us, we will live; if they kill us, we will only die.”

5They got up at twilight and went to the Aramean camp. When they reached the edge of the camp, no one was there.

6For the Lord had made the Aramean camp hear the sound of chariots, horses, and a large army. They said to each other, “Look, the king of Israel has hired the Hittite and Egyptian kings to attack us!”

7So they ran away in the twilight. They left their tents, horses, and donkeys, and the whole camp just as it was. They ran for their lives.

8When these lepers reached the edge of the camp, they went into one tent and ate and drank. They carried off silver, gold, and clothes, hid them, then went to another tent, took things from there, and hid them too.

9Then they said to each other, “We are not doing right. Today is a day of good news, and we are keeping quiet. If we wait until morning, we will be punished. Let’s go tell the king’s family.”

10So they went and called to the city gatekeepers. They told them, “We went to the Aramean camp, and no one is there—no sound of people. The horses and donkeys are tied up, and the tents are just as they were.”

The King Investigates the Report

11The gatekeepers shouted the news to the king’s palace.

12The king got up at night and said to his servants, “I will tell you what the Arameans have done to us. They know we are hungry, so they left the camp to hide in the fields. They are thinking, ‘When they come out of the city, we will capture them alive and get into the city.’”

13One of his servants answered, “Please let some men take five of the horses left in the city. They are like all the Israelites left here—like all the Israelites who are already gone. Let us send them and see.”

14So they took two chariots with horses, and the king sent them after the Aramean camp, saying, “Go and see.”

15They followed them to the Jordan. The whole road was full of clothes and supplies the Arameans had thrown away in their hurry. The messengers returned and told the king.

The Prophecy Comes True

16Then the people went out and took things from the Aramean camp. So a seah of fine flour sold for a shekel, and two seahs of barley sold for a shekel, just as the the LORD had said.

17Now the king had put the officer who helped him stand in charge of the gate. The people trampled him there, and he died, just as the man of God had said when the king came to him.

18It happened just as the man of God had told the king: “At this time tomorrow, in the gate of Samaria, two seahs of barley will sell for a shekel, and a seah of fine flour for a shekel.”

19The officer had answered the man of God, “Look, even if the the LORD opened windows in Heavenheavenheaven, could this happen?”

The man of God had said, “You will see it with your eyes, but you will not eat any of it.”

20And so it happened to him: the people trampled him in the gate, and he died.
