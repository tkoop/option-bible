Praise the LORD's Greatness

1Praise the the LORD! Praise HimhimHim, servants of the the LORD; praise the name of the the LORD. 2Blessed be the name of the the LORD from now and forever. 3From sunrise to sunset, the name of the the LORD is praised.

4The ThetheThe LORD is high above all nations; HishisHis glory is above the heavens. 5Who is like the the LORD our God, who sits enthroned on high, 6who looks down on the heavens and the earth?

7He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, 8to seat them with princes, with the princes of HishisHis people. 9He settles the childless woman in a home as a joyful mother of children. Praise the the LORD!
