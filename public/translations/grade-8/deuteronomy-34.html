Moses Sees the Promised Land

1Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, across from Jericho. The LORD showed him all the land: Gilead as far as Dan, 2all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the Western SeaseaSea, 3the Negev, the plain of the ValleyvalleyValley of Jericho, the CitycityCity of Palms, as far as Zoar.

4And the LORD said to him, "This is the land I swore on oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross over into it."

Moses Dies in Moab

5So Moses, the servant of the LORD, died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD. 6And HeheHe buried him in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor, and no one knows where his grave is to this day.

7Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eyes were not dim, and his strength had not faded.

Israel Mourns Moses

8The Israelites wept for Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days, until the days of mourning for Moses were over.

Joshua Leads Israel

9Now Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom, because Moses had laid his hands on him. So the Israelites listened to him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses. 10Since then, no prophet has arisen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face. 11He was the one the LORD sent to do all the signs and wonders in the land of Egypt against Pharaoh, all his officials, and all his land. 12He also did all the mighty acts and all the great and terrible deeds that Moses did before the eyes of all Israel.
