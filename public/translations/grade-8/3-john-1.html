Greeting to Gaius

1The elder writes to Gaius, the one I love and who is dear to me, whom I love in the truth. 2Dear friend, I pray that you may do well in every way and stay healthy, just as your soul is doing well.

Joy in the Truth

3I was very glad when some fellow believers came and told me how faithful you are to the truth and how you live by it. 4I have no greater joy than hearing that my children are living in the truth.

Supporting Gospel Workers

5Dear friend, you are faithful in what you do for the brothers, even for those you do not know. 6They told the church about your love. You will do well to send them on their way in a way that honourshonourshonors God. 7They went out for the sake of the NamenameName and took nothing from non-believers. 8So we should welcome people like them, so that we can work together for the truth.

Warning About Diotrephes

9I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first, will not accept us. 10So if I come, I will point out what he is doing. He talks nonsense and says evil things about us, and he is not satisfied with that. He will not welcome the brothers, he stops those who want to welcome them, and he throws them out of the church. 11Dear friend, do not copy what is evil, but copy what is good. The one who does good belongs to God, but the one who does evil has never seen God.

Final Greetings

12Everyone speaks well of Demetrius, and the truth itself speaks well of him. We also speak well of him, and you know our words are true. 13I have much more to write to you, but I do not want to use pen and ink. 14I hope to see you soon, and then we can talk face to face. Peace to you. The friends send you their greetings. Greet the friends for me by name.
