Hard Times in the Last Days

1But know this: In the last days, hard times will come. 2People will love only themselves and money. They will be boastful, proud, and abusive. They will disobey their parents, be ungrateful, and be unholy. 3They will have no natural affection, refuse to forgive, slander others, lack self-control, be brutal, and hate what is good. 4They will be traitors, reckless, full of pride, and lovers of pleasure instead of lovers of God. 5They will keep up the look of godliness, but deny its power. Have nothing to do with them.

False Teachers and Their Victims

6Some of them sneak into homes and take advantage of weak-willed women who are loaded down with sins and led astray by all kinds of evil desires. 7These women are always learning, but they can never come to know the truth. 8Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, these people also oppose the truth. Their minds are corrupted, and they are rejected in matters of faith. 9But they will not get far. Their foolishness will be clear to everyone, just as the foolishness of those men was.

Paul's Example for Timothy

10You, however, know all about my teaching, my way of life, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, and my endurance. 11You know about my persecutions and sufferings—what happened to me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. I endured those persecutions, and the Lord rescued me from all of them. 12Everyone who wants to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted. 13But evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse. They will deceive others and be deceived themselves.
