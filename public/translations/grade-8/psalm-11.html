David Trusts in the LORD

1I trust in the LORD. So how can you tell me, "Run away to your mountain like a bird"?

2Look, the wicked are bending their bows and getting their arrows ready. They want to shoot from the dark at people whose hearts are right. 3If the foundations are destroyed, what can good people do?

4The LORD is in HishisHis holy temple. The LORD sits on HishisHis throne in Heavenheavenheaven. His eyes see everything, and HeheHe tests all people.

5The LORD tests both good people and wicked people. He hates those who love violence.

6He will rain down fire and burning sulphursulphursulfur on the wicked. A scorching wind will be their punishment. 7For the LORD is good and right. He loves what is right. Good people will live in HishisHis presence.
