Advice for Wives and Husbands

1Wives, in the same way, submit yourselves to your own husbands. That way, even if some do not believe the message, they may be won over without words by the way you live. 2They will see your pure and respectful lives. 3Your beauty should not come from the outside, like fancy hairstyles, gold jewelleryjewelleryjewelry, or fine clothes. 4Instead, it should come from the inner self, with the lasting beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit. That is very valuable to God. 5This is how the holy women of the past who hoped in God made themselves beautiful. They submitted to their own husbands. 6Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her lord. You are her daughters if you do what is right and do not give in to fear.

7Husbands, in the same way, live with your wives with understanding. Treat them with respect as the weaker partner, and as heirs with you of the gracious gift of life. Do this so that nothing blocks your prayers.

Living in Harmony

8Finally, all of you, live in harmony, show sympathy, love one another, be kind, and be humble. 9Do not pay back evil for evil or insult for insult. Instead, pay back with a blessing. You were called to this so that you may receive a blessing.

10“Whoever wants to love life and see good days must keep their tongue from evil and their lips from lying. 11They must turn from evil and do good. They must seek peace and go after it. 12For the eyes of the Lord are on those who do right, and HishisHis ears listen to their prayers. But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”

Suffering for Doing Right

13Who will hurt you if you are eager to do good? 14But even if you suffer for doing right, you are blessed. “Do not fear what they threaten; do not be shaken.”

Be Ready to Explain Your Hope

15But in your hearts honourhonourhonor Christ as Lord. Always be ready to give an answer to anyone who asks you for the reason for the hope you have. Do it with gentleness and respect. 16Keep a clear conscience. Then those who speak badly about your good life in Christ will be ashamed of their lies. 17It is better, if God wants it, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil.

Christ’s Victory

18Christ also suffered once for sins. The innocent suffered for the guilty to bring you to God. He was put to death in the body but made alive by the SpiritspiritSpirit. 19In that SpiritspiritSpirit HeheHe went and preached to the spirits in prison. 20These were the spirits of those who disobeyed long ago, when God waited patiently in the days of Noah while the ark was being built. Only a few people, eight in all, were saved through water. 21This water stands for baptism, which now saves you too. It is not about washing dirt from the body. It is about asking God for a clear conscience. It saves you through the resurrection of Jesus Christ. 22He has gone into Heavenheavenheaven and is at God’s right hand. Angels, authorities, and powers are under HishisHis control.
