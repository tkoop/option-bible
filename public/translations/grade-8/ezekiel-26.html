Tyre Boasts About Jerusalem's Fall

1In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me. He said,

2“Son of man, because Tyre has said about Jerusalem,

‘Aha! The gate to the nations is broken. It has swung open to me. Now that she is ruined, I will be filled.’

3“So this is what the Lord the LORD says: Look, I am against you, Tyre. I will bring many nations against you, just as the sea sends up its waves.

4“They will destroy your walls and tear down your towers. I will scrape the soil off you and leave you a bare rock.

5“She will become a place where people spread nets in the sea, because I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD. She will be taken as plunder by the nations.

6“Her towns on the mainland will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

Nebuchadnezzar Will Destroy Tyre

7“For this is what the Lord the LORD says: I will bring Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, king of kings, against you, Tyre. He will come with horses, chariots, horsemen, and a large army.

8“He will kill your towns on the mainland with the sword. He will build siege ramps against you, pile up a siege mound against you, and raise a shield against you.

9“He will aim the blows of his battering rams at your walls and tear down your towers with his axes.

10“His many horses will cover you with dust. Your walls will shake at the noise of horsemen, wagons, and chariots when he enters your gates like an army entering a broken city.

11“With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword, and your strong pillars will fall to the ground.

12“They will plunder your wealth and loot your goods. They will destroy your walls and tear down your fine houses. They will throw your stones, wood, and rubble into the sea.

13“I will silence your songs, and no one will hear your harps again.

14“I will make you a bare rock, and you will become a place to spread nets. You will never be rebuilt, because I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.”

Tyre's Fall Will Shake the Nations

15“This is what the Lord the LORD says to Tyre: Will not the coastlands tremble at the sound of your fall, when the wounded cry out and slaughter happens among you?

16“All the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones. They will take off their robes and strip off their embroidered clothes. They will put on trembling and sit on the ground. They will tremble every moment and be shocked at you.

17“They will sing a funeral song about you and say to you,

‘How you have been destroyed, O famous city, once lived in by seafaring people! She was strong at sea, she and her people, and she put fear into everyone who lived near her.’

18“Now the coastlands tremble on the day of your fall. The islands in the sea are terrified at your going away.

Tyre Will Sink to the Grave

19“For this is what the Lord the LORD says: When I make you a ruined city like other deserted cities, when I bring the deep over you and the waters cover you,

20“I will bring you down with those who go down to the grave, to the people of long ago. I will make you live in the world below, like the old ruins, with those who go down to the grave, so that you will no longer be lived in or have a place among the living. I will show MymyMy glory in the land of the living.

21“I will make you a horror, and you will be no more. People may look for you, but they will never find you again, declares the Lord the LORD.”
