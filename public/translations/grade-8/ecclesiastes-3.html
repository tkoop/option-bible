A Time for Everything

1There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity on EarthEarthearth. 2There is a time to be born and a time to die, a time to plant and a time to pull up what was planted. 3There is a time to kill and a time to heal, a time to tear down and a time to build. 4There is a time to weep and a time to laugh, a time to mourn and a time to dance. 5There is a time to throw stones and a time to gather stones, a time to hug and a time to turn away from hugging. 6There is a time to search and a time to lose, a time to keep and a time to throw away. 7There is a time to tear and a time to sew, a time to be quiet and a time to speak. 8There is a time to love and a time to hate, a time for war and a time for peace.

God's Plan for People

9What does a worker gain from all his hard work? 10I have seen the hard work God has given people to do. 11He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also put eternity in people's hearts, yet no one can understand everything God has done from beginning to end. 12I know that there is nothing better for people than to be joyful and do good while they live. 13So everyone can eat and drink and enjoy the good from all their work—this is God's gift. 14I know that everything God does lasts forever. Nothing can be added to it, and nothing can be taken away. God does this so that people will fear HimhimHim. 15What exists has already been, and what will be has already been. God brings back what has already happened.

Justice, Death, and Our Work

16I also saw in this world that where justice should be, there was wickedness, and where what is right should be, there was wickedness. 17I said in my heart, “God will bring the righteous and the wicked into judgmentjudgementjudgment, for there is a time for every activity and every deed.” 18I also said in my heart about people, “God tests them so they can see that they are like animals.” 19For people and animals share the same fate. As one dies, so dies the other. They all have the same breath, and people have no advantage over animals. Everything is meaningless. 20All go to the same place. All come from dust, and to dust all return. 21Who knows whether a person's spirit goes up and an animal's spirit goes down into the earth? 22So I saw that the best thing a person can do is enjoy their work, because that is their share. Who can show them what will happen after them?
