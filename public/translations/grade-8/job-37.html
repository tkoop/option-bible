Elihu Trembles at God's Power

1At this my heart trembles and leaps out of its place. 2Listen carefully to the thunder of HishisHis voice and the rumble that comes from HishisHis mouth. 3He sends it out under the whole sky, and HishisHis lightning to the ends of the earth. 4Afterward HishisHis voice roars. He thunders with HishisHis majestic voice, and HeheHe does not hold back the lightning when HishisHis voice is heard. 5God thunders marvelously with HishisHis voice; HeheHe does great things we cannot understand.

God Commands the Weather

6He tells the snow, "Fall on the earth," and to the rain shower, "Pour down hard." 7He stops every person's work, so that everyone may know what HeheHe has done. 8Then wild animals go into their dens and stay in their hiding places. 9The storm comes from its chamber, and cold comes from the scattering winds. 10By the breath of God, ice is made, and the wide waters are frozen solid. 11He fills the thick cloud with water, and the cloud scatters HishisHis lightning. 12They turn around and around by HishisHis guidance, to carry out everything HeheHe commands on the face of the whole world. 13He sends it for correction, for HishisHis land, or as an act of mercy.

Elihu Challenges Job

14Listen to this, O Job; stand still and think about the wonderful works of God. 15Do you know how God gives them their orders and makes the lightning flash from HishisHis cloud? 16Do you know how the clouds are balanced, the amazing works of HimhimHim who is perfect in knowledge—17you whose clothes grow hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? 18Can you spread out the sky with HimhimHim, hard as a mirror made of cast metal? 19Teach us what we should say to HimhimHim; we cannot put our case in order because of the darkness. 20Should someone tell HimhimHim that I want to speak? If a person spoke, would he not be swallowed up?

No One Can Stand Before the Almighty

21Now people cannot look at the bright light in the sky after the wind has passed and cleared the skies. 22Out of the north comes golden splendoursplendoursplendor; God is clothed with terrifying majesty. 23The AlmightyalmightyAlmighty—we cannot find HimhimHim; HeheHe is great in power, and in justice and great goodness HeheHe will not wrong anyone. 24So people fear HimhimHim; HeheHe does not look with favourfavourfavor on those who are wise in their own eyes.
