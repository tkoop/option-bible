3As I urged you when I was going to Macedonia, stay in Ephesus so you can command certain people not to teach false ideas. 4They must not spend time on myths and endless family lists. These things lead to useless arguments instead of God's plan, which depends on faith. 5The goal of this command is love that comes from a pure heart, a good conscience, and a real faith. 6Some people have missed this goal and turned to empty talk. 7They want to be teachers of the law, but they do not understand what they are saying or what they are so sure about.

The Right Use of the Law

8We know the law is good if someone uses it the right way. 9We also know the law is not made for good people. It is made for lawbreakers and rebels, for ungodly people and sinners, for unholy and unclean people, for those who attack their fathers or mothers, and for murderers. 10It is also for sexually immoral people, for men who have sex with men, for slave traders, for liars, for people who break their promises under oath, and for anything else that goes against healthy teaching. 11This teaching agrees with the glorious good news of the blessed God, which HeheHe trusted me to share.

Timothy's Mission in Ephesus

18Timothy, my child, I entrust this command to you in keeping with the prophecies made about you, so that by them you may fight the good battle. 19Hold on to faith and a good conscience. Some people have rejected these and ruined their faith. 20Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander. I have handed them over to Satan so they may learn not to blaspheme.
