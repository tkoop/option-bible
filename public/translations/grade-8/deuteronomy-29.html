1These are the words of the covenant the the LORD commanded Moses to make with the Israelites in Moab, besides the covenant HeheHe made with them at Horeb.

Moses Reminds Israel of God's Works

“2Your eyes have seen all that the the LORD did in Egypt to Pharaoh, to all his officials, and to all his land. 3With your own eyes you saw those great trials, those signs, and those great wonders. 4But to this day the the LORD has not given you a mind to understand, or eyes to see, or ears to hear. 5The ThetheThe LORD has led you forty years in the wilderness. Your clothes have not worn out, and your sandals have not worn out on your feet. 6You ate no bread and drank no wine or strong drink. I did this so you would know that I am the the LORD your God.

“7When you reached this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to fight us, but we defeated them. 8We took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. 9So keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you may succeed in everything you do.

All Israel Stands Before the the LORD

“10All of you are standing today before the the LORD your God—your leaders, your tribes, your elders, and your officers, every man in Israel. 11along with your children and wives, and the foreigners in your camps, from those who cut your wood to those who carry your water. 12You are standing here to enter into the covenant of the the LORD your God, the covenant the the LORD is making with you today, along with its curses. 13He is doing this to make you HishisHis people today and to be your God, as HeheHe promised you and as HeheHe swore to your fathers—to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

“14I am making this covenant and this oath not only with you who are standing here with us today before the the LORD our God, 15but also with those who are not here with us today.

“16You know how we lived in Egypt and how we passed through the nations on the way. 17You saw their disgusting things and their idols of wood, stone, silver, and gold.

A Warning Against Turning Away

“18Make sure no man or woman, family or tribe among you today turns away from the the LORD your God to worship the gods of those nations. Make sure no root among you grows into bitter poison. 19When someone like that hears the words of this curse, he will bless himself and say, ‘I will be safe, even though I keep going my own way.’ This will bring disaster on the watered land as well as the dry. 20The ThetheThe LORD will never be willing to forgive him. Instead, the the LORD’s anger and jealousy will burn against that man, and every curse written in this book will fall on him. The ThetheThe LORD will erase his name from under Heavenheavenheaven. 21The ThetheThe LORD will set him apart from all the tribes of Israel for disaster, according to all the curses of the covenant written in this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw.

“22Your children who come after you, and foreigners from far away, will see the troubles that have hit this land and the sicknesses the the LORD has sent on it. 23The whole land will be a wasteland of burning sulphursulphursulfur and salt, with nothing planted and nothing growing, not even grass. It will be like Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the the LORD destroyed in HishisHis fierce anger.

“24All the nations will ask, ‘Why has the the LORD done this to this land? Why this fierce, burning anger?’ 25And people will answer, ‘It is because they abandoned the covenant of the the LORD, the God of their ancestors, the covenant HeheHe made with them when HeheHe brought them out of Egypt.’ 26They went and worshiped other gods and bowed down to them—gods they did not know and that the the LORD had not given them. 27So the the LORD’s anger burned against this land, and HeheHe brought on it all the curses written in this book. 28In HishisHis fierce anger and great wrath the the LORD uprooted them from their land and banished them to another land, as we see today.

The Secret Things Belong to the the LORD

“29The secret things belong to the the LORD our God, but the things revealed belong to us and to our children forever, so that we may follow all the words of this law.”
