David's Prayer from the Cave

1A wise poem by David, when he was in the cave. A prayer. I cry out to the the LORD; I lift my voice to the the LORD and beg for mercy. 2I pour out my complaint to HimhimHim; I tell HimhimHim my troubles.

3When my spirit grows faint within me, YouyouYou know my path. In the way I walk, they have hidden a trap for me. 4Look to my right and see; no one is concerned for me. I have no refuge; no one cares for my life.

5I cry to YouyouYou, O the LORD; I say, "YouyouYou are my refuge, my share among the living." 6Listen to my cry, for I am very weak. Rescue me from those who chase me, for they are too strong for me.

7Set me free from prison, so I can praise YouryourYour name. Then the righteous will gather around me because YouyouYou have been good to me.
