Israel Asks for a King

1When Samuel was old, he appointed his sons as judges over Israel. 2The first son was named Joel, and the second was named Abijah. They served as judges in Beersheba. 3But his sons did not follow his ways. They turned aside after money, took bribes, and twisted justice.

4So all the elders of Israel gathered and came to Samuel at Ramah. 5They said to him, "YouyouYou are old, and your sons do not follow your ways. Now appoint a king for us to judge us, like all the other nations." 6But when they said, "Give us a king to judge us," Samuel was displeased, and he prayed to the the LORD.

7The the LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to the people in everything they say to you. They have not rejected you; they have rejected MemeMe as their KingkingKing. 8They are doing the same thing they have done since the day I brought them out of Egypt. They have left MemeMe and served other gods, and now they are doing it to you. 9So listen to them. But you must warn them strongly and tell them how the king who rules over them will treat them."

Samuel Warns About a King

10Samuel told all the words of the the LORD to the people who asked him for a king. 11He said, "This is how the king who rules over you will treat you: He will take your sons and put them in his chariots and cavalry. They will run in front of his chariots. 12He will choose commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties. He will make some plow his fields and reap his harvest. Others will make his weapons and equipment for his chariots. 13He will take your daughters to make perfume, cook, and bake. 14He will take your best fields, vineyards, and olive groves and give them to his servants. 15He will take a tenth of your grain and vineyards and give it to his officials and servants. 16He will take your male and female servants, your best young men, and your donkeys, and put them to work for him. 17He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you will become his servants. 18On that day you will cry out because of the king you chose, but the the LORD will not answer you."

The People Insist on a King

19But the people refused to listen to Samuel. "No!" they said. "We want a king over us. 20Then we will be like all the other nations, with a king to judge us and to go out before us and fight our battles."

The LORD Agrees to Give a King

21Samuel heard all the people's words and repeated them to the LORD.

22The the LORD said to Samuel, "Listen to their voice. Appoint a king for them."

Then Samuel told the men of Israel, "Each of you go back to his own town."
