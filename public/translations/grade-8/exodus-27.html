The Bronze Altar

1You should make the altar out of acacia wood. It should be square, five cubits long and five cubits wide, and three cubits high. 2You should make horns on its four corners. The horns should be one piece with the altar, and you should cover the altar with bronze. 3You should make bronze tools for it: pots for ashes, shovels, bowls, forks, and fire pans. All of its tools should be bronze.

4You should make a bronze grate for it, like a net. You should make four bronze rings on its four corners. 5You should put the grate under the rim of the altar, so it hangs halfway down the altar.

6You should make poles for the altar out of acacia wood, and cover them with bronze. 7The poles should be put through the rings, so they rest on two sides of the altar when it is carried. 8You should make it hollow, with boards. Make it just as it was shown to you on the mountain.

The Courtyard

9You should make the courtyard around the tabernacle. On the south side, hang fine twisted linen curtains that are one hundred cubits long. 10It will have twenty bronze pillars with twenty bronze bases. The hooks and bands on the pillars should be silver. 11Do the same on the north side: hangings one hundred cubits long, twenty bronze pillars with bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands.

12On the west side, the courtyard should be fifty cubits wide, with hangings, ten pillars, and ten bases. 13The east side should also be fifty cubits wide. 14On one side of the entrance, hang curtains fifteen cubits long, with three pillars and three bases. 15On the other side, hang curtains fifteen cubits long, with three pillars and three bases.

16For the courtyard entrance, make a screen twenty cubits long from blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, with skilfulskilfulskillful needlework. It needs four pillars and four bases. 17All pillars around the courtyard should be joined with silver bands. Their hooks should be silver, and their bases bronze. 18The courtyard should be one hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide, with curtains five cubits high made of fine twisted linen. The bases should be bronze. 19All tools used in the tabernacle and all its tent pegs, along with the pegs for the courtyard, should be bronze.

Oil for the Lamp

20You should command the Israelites to bring you pure olive oil, pressed for the lamp, so it can burn steadily. 21In the tent of meeting, outside the curtain that is before the TestimonytestimonyTestimony, Aaron and his sons should keep the lamp burning before the LORD from evening to morning. This is a lasting rule for the Israelites for all generations.
