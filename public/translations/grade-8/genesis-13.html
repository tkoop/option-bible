Abram Returns from Egypt

1Abram went up from Egypt to the Negev. He took his wife, everything he owned, and Lot with him. 2Abram was very rich. He had livestock, silver, and gold. 3He travelledtravelledtraveled from the Negev, moving from place to place, until he reached Bethel. He camped between Bethel and Ai, where his tent had been before. 4He went back to the place where he had built an altar the first time. There Abram called on the name of the LORD.

Abram and Lot Separate

5Lot, who travelledtravelledtraveled with Abram, also had flocks, herds, and tents. 6The land could not support both of them living together. They had too much, so they could not stay together. 7Quarrels broke out between Abram's herdsmen and Lot's herdsmen. The Canaanites and Perizzites were also living in the land at that time.

8Abram said to Lot, “Let's not have any arguing between you and me, or between our herdsmen. We are brothers. 9The whole land is open before you. Please separate from me. If you go to the left, I'll go to the right. If you go to the right, I'll go to the left.”

10Lot looked up and saw the whole Jordan ValleyvalleyValley. It was well watered everywhere, before the the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah. It looked like the garden of the the LORD and like the land of Egypt toward Zoar. 11So Lot chose the whole Jordan ValleyvalleyValley and travelledtravelledtraveled east. The two men separated. 12Abram stayed in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled in the cities of the valley and camped near Sodom. 13The men of Sodom were very wicked sinners against the the LORD.

The LORD Promises the Land to Abram

14After Lot left, the the LORD spoke to Abram. He said, “Look up from where you are. Look north, south, east, and west. 15I will give you and your descendants all the land you see, forever. 16I will make your descendants as many as the dust of the earth. If anyone can count the dust of the earth, then your descendants can be counted too. 17Get up and walk through the land, from one end to the other. I am giving it to you.”

18So Abram moved his tent and settled near the great trees of Mamre at Hebron. There he built an altar to the the LORD.
