Food Offered to Idols

1Now about food offered to idols: We know that all of us have knowledge. But knowledge makes people proud, while love builds them up. 2If anyone thinks he knows something, he does not yet know as he should. 3But if anyone loves God, that person is known by HimhimHim.

One God and One Lord

4So about eating food offered to idols: We know that an idol is nothing in this world, and that there is only one God. 5For even if there are so-called gods in Heavenheavenheaven or on EarthEarthearth—and there are many gods and many lords— 6yet for us there is one God, the FatherfatherFather. All things come from HimhimHim, and we live for HimhimHim. There is also one Lord, Jesus Christ. All things come through HimhimHim, and we live through HimhimHim.

Protecting Weaker Believers

7But not everyone has this knowledge. Some people are so used to idols that they still eat food as if it were offered to an idol, and their weak conscience becomes unclean. 8Food does not bring us closer to God. We are not worse off if we do not eat, and we are not better off if we do. 9But be careful that your freedom does not become a stumbling block to those who are weak. 10For if someone with a weak conscience sees you, who have knowledge, eating in an idol's temple, won't that person feel encouraged to eat food offered to idols? 11So the weak brother, for whom Christ died, is destroyed by your knowledge. 12When you sin against the brothers this way and wound their weak consciences, you sin against Christ. 13Therefore, if food causes my brother to stumble, I will never eat meat again, so that I won't cause my brother to stumble.
