Living in Love and Light

1Therefore, be imitators of God, as dearly loved children. 2Live in love, just as Christ loved us and gave HimselfhimselfHimself up for us as a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God.

3But sexual immorality and every kind of impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is proper for God's holy people. 4There should be no obscenity, foolish talk, or coarse joking, which are not fitting. Instead, there should be thanksgiving. 5For you can be sure of this: no one who is sexually immoral, impure, or greedy—that is, an idolater—has any share in the kingdom of Christ and God. 6Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things God's wrath is coming on those who disobey. 7So do not join them.

8For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light. 9The light produces goodness, righteousness, and truth. 10Test and approve what pleases the Lord.

11Have nothing to do with the fruitless deeds of darkness. Instead, expose them. 12It is shameful even to talk about what disobedient people do in secret. 13But everything exposed by the light becomes visible, because anything shown by the light becomes light. 14That is why it says, "Wake up, sleeper, rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."

Walking Wisely

15So be very careful how you live. Do not live like foolish people but like wise people, making the most of every opportunity, because the days are evil. 1617Therefore, do not be foolish, but understand what the Lord wants.

18Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to wild living. Instead, be filled with the SpiritspiritSpirit. 19Speak to one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs. Sing and make music in your heart to the Lord. 20Always give thanks to God the FatherfatherFather for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. 21Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ.

Wives and Husbands

22Wives, submit to your own husbands as to the Lord, 23because the husband is the head of the wife, just as Christ is the head of the church and the SavioursaviourSaviourSavioursaviourSaviourSaviorsaviorSavior of the body. 24But as the church submits to Christ, so wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

25Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave HimselfhimselfHimself up for her, 26to make her holy. He cleansed her by washing her with water through the word, 27so that HeheHe could present her to HimselfhimselfHimself as a glorious church without stain or wrinkle or anything like that, but holy and blameless.

28In the same way, husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. A man who loves his wife loves himself. 29After all, no one ever hated his own body, but he feeds and cares for it, just as Christ does for the church, 30because we are members of HishisHis body.

31"For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh."

32This is a great mystery, but I am talking about Christ and the church. 33Still, each one of you must love his wife as he loves himself, and the wife must respect her husband.
