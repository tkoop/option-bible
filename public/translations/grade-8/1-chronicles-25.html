David Appoints Temple Musicians

1David and the army leaders set apart for service the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun. These men prophesied with lyres, harps, and cymbals. This was the number of men who did the work for their service.

2The sons of Asaph were Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah. They served under Asaph, who prophesied under the king's direction.

3The sons of Jeduthun were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah—six in all. They served under their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the lyre. He gave thanks and praise to the the LORD.

4The sons of Heman were Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-Ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.

5All these were the sons of Heman, the king's seer in the words of God. They were to show God's power. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

6All these served under their fathers in the house of the the LORD. They played cymbals, harps, and lyres for the service of God's house. They served under Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman, at the king's direction.

7With their brothers, all of them were skilled in singing to the the LORD. There were 288 of them.

8They cast lots for their duties. The small and the great were treated the same, and the teacher served along with the student.

The Musicians Are Assigned by Lot

9The first lot was for Asaph and fell to Joseph. 10The second lot was for Gedaliah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 11The third lot was for Zaccur, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 12The fourth lot was for Izri, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 13The fifth lot was for Nethaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 14The sixth lot was for Bukkiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 15The seventh lot was for Jesarelah, with his sons and brothers—twelve.

16The eighth lot was for Jeshaiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 17The ninth lot was for Mattaniah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 18The tenth lot was for Shimei, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 19The eleventh lot was for Azarel, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 20The twelfth lot was for Hashabiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 21The thirteenth lot was for Shubael, with his sons and brothers—twelve.

22The fourteenth lot was for Mattithiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 23The fifteenth lot was for Jeremoth, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 24The sixteenth lot was for Hananiah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 25The seventeenth lot was for Joshbekashah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 26The eighteenth lot was for Hanani, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 27The nineteenth lot was for Mallothi, with his sons and brothers—twelve.

28The twentieth lot was for Eliathah, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 29The twenty-first lot was for Hothir, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 30The twenty-second lot was for Giddalti, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 31The twenty-third lot was for Mahazioth, with his sons and brothers—twelve. 32The twenty-fourth lot was for Romamti-Ezer, with his sons and brothers—twelve.
