Praise the LORD and Tell of His Wonders

1Give thanks to the LORD. Call on HishisHis name. Make HishisHis deeds known among the nations. 2Sing to HimhimHim; sing praise to HimhimHim. Tell about all HishisHis wonderful acts. 3Glory in HishisHis holy name. Let the hearts of those who seek the LORD be glad. 4Seek the LORD and HishisHis strength; seek HishisHis face always. 5Remember the wonders HeheHe has done, HishisHis miracles, and the judgmentsjudgementsjudgments HeheHe spoke.

The LORD's Covenant with Abraham

6Descendants of Abraham, HishisHis servant, and sons of Jacob, HishisHis chosen ones. 7He is the LORD our God. His judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are over all the earth. 8He remembers HishisHis covenant forever—the word HeheHe commanded for a thousand generations. 9He made this covenant with Abraham and swore an oath to Isaac. 10He made it a law for Jacob and an everlasting covenant for Israel. 11He said, "I will give you the land of Canaan as your inheritance."

12When they were only a few people—very few—and strangers in that land, 13they wandered from nation to nation and from one kingdom to another. 14He let no one oppress them. For their sake HeheHe warned kings: 15"Do not touch MymyMy anointed ones; do no harm to MymyMy prophets."

Joseph Sent Ahead to Egypt

16He called famine on the land and broke every supply of bread. 17He sent a man ahead of them—Joseph, who was sold as a slave. 18They hurt his feet with chains, and iron pierced his soul. 19He stayed there until the time came for the LORD's word to come true; HishisHis promise tested him.

20Then the king sent for him and set him free; the ruler of the peoples released him. 21He made Joseph master of his house and ruler over all he owned. 22He did this so Joseph could guide the king's officials as he pleased and teach wisdom to the elders.

From Egypt to the Promised Land

23Then Israel came to Egypt; Jacob lived as a foreigner in the land of Ham. 24The LORD made HishisHis people grow very strong and more numerous than their enemies. 25He turned the enemies' hearts to hate HishisHis people and to plot against HishisHis servants.

26He sent Moses, HishisHis servant, and Aaron, whom HeheHe had chosen. 27They did HishisHis signs and wonders in the land of Ham. 28He sent darkness, and it became dark; they did not disobey HishisHis words. 29He turned their water into blood and killed their fish. 30Their land swarmed with frogs, even in the royal bedrooms. 31He spoke, and flies came; gnats spread through all their country.

32He gave them hail instead of rain, and lightning flashed across their land. 33He struck their grapevines and fig trees and broke the trees throughout their country. 34He spoke, and locusts came—young locusts too many to count. 35They ate every plant in the land and the fruit of the soil. 36He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the first and best of their strength.

37He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and no one among HishisHis tribes was weak. 38Egypt was glad when they left, because fear of Israel had fallen on them. 39He spread a cloud to cover them and fire to give light at night. 40They asked, and HeheHe brought quail; HeheHe filled them with bread from Heavenheavenheaven. 41He opened the rock, and water poured out; it ran like a river in the dry land.

42He did this because HeheHe remembered HishisHis holy promise to Abraham, HishisHis servant. 43So HeheHe brought out HishisHis people with joy and HishisHis chosen ones with singing. 44He gave them the lands of the nations, and they took over what other peoples had worked for. 45He did this so they would keep HishisHis laws and obey HishisHis teachings. Praise the LORD!
