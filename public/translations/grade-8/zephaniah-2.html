A Call to Gather and Seek the LORD

1Gather together, yes, gather, O nation with no shame, 2before the decree takes effect and the day passes like chaff, before the LORD's fierce anger comes on you, before the day of the LORD comes on you.

3Seek the LORD, all you humble people of the land, who carry out HishisHis justice. Seek what is right; seek humility. Perhaps you will be hidden on the day of the LORD's wrath.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Philistia

4For Gaza will be left empty, and Ashkelon will be ruined. Ashdod will be driven out at noon, and Ekron will be torn up. 5How terrible for the people who live by the sea, the nation of the Cherethites! The LORD's word is against you, O Canaan, land of the Philistines. I will destroy you until no one is left. 6The seacoast will become pastures, with wells for shepherds and sheep pens. 7It will belong to the remnant of the house of Judah; there they will graze. In the houses of Ashkelon they will lie down in the evening, for the LORD their God will care for them and bring back their captives.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Moab and Ammon

8I have heard the insults of Moab and the taunts of the Ammonites, when they insulted MymyMy people and made threats against their land. 9Therefore, as surely as I live, declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, Moab will become like Sodom, and the Ammonites like Gomorrah—a place of weeds and salt pits, ruined forever. The remnant of MymyMy people will plunder them, and the survivors of MymyMy nation will take their land. 10This is what they will get for their pride, because they insulted and boasted against the people of the LORD of Hosts. 11The LORD will be terrifying to them when HeheHe starves all the gods of the earth. Then the nations on every shore will bow down to HimhimHim, each in its own place.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Cush and Assyria

12You Cushites will also be killed by MymyMy sword. 13He will stretch out HishisHis hand against the north and destroy Assyria. He will make Nineveh a wasteland, dry as the desert. 14Flocks and herds will lie down in her, along with all kinds of animals. Even the owl and the porcupine will live among her pillars. Their calls will echo from the windows; rubble will be on the thresholds, for her cedar beams will be exposed. 15This is the city that used to be so happy, that lived in safety and said in her heart, "I am the one, and there is no one else." What a ruin she has become, a place where wild animals rest! Everyone who passes by her hisses and shakes his fist.
