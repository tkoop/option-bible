Greeting and Comfort

1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, and Timothy our brother, to God's church in Corinth, along with all the saints throughout Achaia: 2Grace and peace to you from God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ.

3Blessed be the God and FatherfatherFather of our Lord Jesus Christ, the FatherfatherFather of mercies and God of all comfort. 4He comforts us in all our troubles, so we can comfort anyone who is in trouble with the comfort we ourselves received from God. 5For just as Christ's sufferings overflow to us, so also through Christ our comfort overflows. 6If we are troubled, it is for your comfort and salvation. If we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which helps you patiently endure the same sufferings we go through. 7Our hope for you is strong, because we know that just as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in our comfort.

Trouble in Asia

8We do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the hardship we faced in the province of Asia. We were weighed down far beyond what we could handle, so much that we gave up hope of living. 9In fact, we felt we had already received a death sentence. But that happened so we would not trust in ourselves, but in God, who raises the dead. 10He has delivered us from such a deadly danger, and HeheHe will deliver us. We have put our hope in HimhimHim that HeheHe will keep delivering us. 11You also help us by your prayers. Then many people will thank God for us because of the gracious gift given to us through the prayers of many.

Paul's Clear Conscience

12This is what we are proud of: Our conscience tells us that we lived in the world, and especially with you, with the holiness and sincerity that come from God. We did not do it with worldly wisdom, but with God's grace. 13We are not writing you anything other than what you can read and understand. I hope you will understand us fully, 14just as you have partly understood us. Then you can be proud of us, just as we will be proud of you on the day of our Lord Jesus.

Paul's Travel Plans

15Because I was sure of this, I planned to visit you first, so you could receive a double blessing. 16I planned to pass through you on my way to Macedonia, then come back to you from Macedonia, and have you send me on to Judea. 17When I planned this, did I do it lightly? Or do I make plans in a merely human way, so that I would say "Yes, yes" when I mean "No, no"? 18But as God is faithful, our message to you is not "Yes" and "No." 19For the SonsonSon of God, Jesus Christ, whom we preached among you—by me, Silvanus, and Timothy—was not "Yes" and "No." In HimhimHim it has always been "Yes." 20For all of God's promises are "Yes" in Christ. And so through HimhimHim our "AmenamenAmen" is spoken to God's glory. 21Now God is the OneoneOne who makes us stand firm with you in Christ and anointed us. 22He has also sealed us and given us the SpiritspiritSpirit in our hearts as a pledge. 23I call on God as my witness that it was to spare you that I did not come back to Corinth. 24Not that we rule over your faith, but we are fellow workers with you for your joy, because you stand firm by faith.
