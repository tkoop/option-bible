Greeting and Thanksgiving

1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, in keeping with the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus. 2To Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the FatherfatherFather and Christ Jesus our Lord.

3I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience, as my ancestors did. I remember you constantly, night and day, in my prayers. 4I long to see you, remembering your tears, so that I may be filled with joy. 5I've been reminded of the sincere faith that lives in you, which first lived in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice. I'm convinced that it lives in you too.

Encouragement to Be Bold

Faithfulness and Desertion

15You know that everyone in Asia has turned away from me. Phygelus and Hermogenes are among them. 16May the Lord show mercy to the household of Onesiphorus.
