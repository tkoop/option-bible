Habakkuk's First Complaint

1This is the message that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

2“How long, the LORD, must I call for help, but YouyouYou do not listen? I cry out to YouyouYou, ‘Violence!’ but YouyouYou do not save. 3Why do YouyouYou make me see wrongdoing, and why do YouyouYou make me look at trouble? Harm and violence are in front of me. Fighting and arguing keep happening. 4So the law has lost its power, and justice never happens. Wicked people surround good people, so justice is turned upside down.”

The LORD's Answer

5The LORD says, “Look at the nations and watch. Be completely amazed, because I am doing something in your days that you would not believe, even if someone told you. 6I am raising up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hot-tempered nation. 8“Their horses are faster than leopards and fiercer than wolves at evening. Their horsemen charge ahead. Their riders come from far away. They fly like an eagle swooping down to eat. 9They all come to do violence. Their faces look straight ahead. They gather captives like sand. 10They make fun of kings and laugh at rulers. They laugh at every strong city. They pile up dirt and capture cities. 11Then they sweep by like the wind and move on. They are guilty, because their strength is their god.”
