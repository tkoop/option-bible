The Useless Vine Wood

1The word of ThetheThe LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, what is vine wood compared with all other wood? What is a vine branch among the trees of the forest? 3Can wood be taken from it to make something useful? Can a peg be taken from it to hang things on? 4See, it is given to the fire for fuel. The fire burns both ends, and the middle is charred. Can it be used for anything then? 5Even when it was whole, it could not be used for work. How much less after fire has burned it and charred it!”
