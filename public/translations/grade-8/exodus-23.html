Rules for Fairness and Justice

1You must not spread false rumoursrumoursrumors. Don't team up with evil people to give a false witness. 2Don't follow the crowd to do wrong. When you speak in a case, don't twist justice by siding with the crowd. 3Don't show special favourfavourfavor to a poor person in court.

4If you meet your enemy's ox or donkey wandering away, you must bring it back to him. 5If you see the donkey of someone who hates you fallen under its load, don't leave it there. Help him get it up.

6Don't deny justice to a poor person in his lawsuit. 7Stay far from a false charge. Don't kill innocent or honest people, because I won't let the guilty go free. 8Don't take a bribe, because a bribe blinds people who can see and twists the words of honest people. 9Don't mistreat a foreigner. You know how it feels to be a foreigner, because you were foreigners in Egypt.

Rest for Land and People

10Plant your land for six years and gather its crops. 11But in the seventh year, let it rest and leave it alone. Then the poor among your people can eat from it, and wild animals can eat what they leave. Do the same with your vineyard and olive grove. 12Work for six days, but rest on the seventh day. That way your ox and your donkey can rest, and your servant's son and the foreigner can rest too.

13Be careful to do everything I have told you. Don't call on the names of other gods, and don't let those names be heard from your lips.

Don't boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

God's Angel and the Promised Land

20Look, I am sending an angel ahead of you to guard you on the way and bring you to the place I have prepared. 21Pay attention to him and listen to his voice. Don't rebel against him, because he won't forgive your sins, since MymyMy name is in him. 22But if you listen carefully to his voice and do everything I say, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and oppose those who oppose you.

23My angel will go before you and bring you to the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will wipe them out. 24Don't bow down to their gods or serve them. Don't follow their ways. Instead, tear down their idols and smash their sacred stones. 25Serve the LORD your God, and HeheHe will bless your bread and your water. I will take sickness away from you.

26No woman in your land will miscarry or be unable to have children. I will give you a full life. 27I will send MymyMy terror ahead of you, and I will throw into confusion every nation you meet. I will make all your enemies turn and run. 28I will send hornets ahead of you to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites before you.

29I won't drive them out in one year, or the land would become empty and wild animals would multiply against you. 30Little by little I will drive them out before you, until you grow in number and take over the land. 31I will set your borders from the Red SeaseaSea to the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert to the Euphrates River. I will hand the people who live in the land over to you, and you will drive them out.

32Don't make a treaty with them or with their gods. 33They must not live in your land, or they will cause you to sin against MemeMe. If you serve their gods, it will surely become a trap for you.
