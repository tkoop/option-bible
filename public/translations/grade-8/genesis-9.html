God Blesses Noah and Gives New Rules

1God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth. 2Every animal on EarthEarthearth will fear and dread you. Every bird of the sky, every creature that moves on the ground, and every fish in the sea are given into your hands. 3Everything that lives and moves will be food for you. Just as I gave you the green plants, I now give you everything.

4"But you must not eat meat that still has its lifeblood in it. 5I will surely hold every animal and every person responsible for taking a human life. I will hold each person responsible for the life of his brother. 6Whoever sheds human blood will have his blood shed by man, for God made man in HishisHis image. 7And as for you, be fruitful and multiply. Spread out over the earth and multiply on it."

God Makes a Covenant with Noah

8Then God said to Noah and his sons with him, 9"I am now making MymyMy covenant with you and with your descendants after you, 10and with every living creature that is with you—the birds, the livestock, and all the wild animals, every creature that came out of the ark. 11I promise that never again will all life be cut off by floodwaters. Never again will there be a flood to destroy the earth. 12This is the sign of the covenant I am making between MemeMe and you and every living creature with you, for all generations to come:

13"I have set MymyMy rainbow in the clouds, and it will be the sign of the covenant between MemeMe and the earth. 14Whenever I bring clouds over the earth and the rainbow appears in the clouds, 15I will remember MymyMy covenant between MemeMe and you and all living creatures. The waters will never again become a flood to destroy all life. 16The rainbow will be in the clouds, and I will see it and remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh on the earth. 17This is the sign of the covenant I have made between MemeMe and all life on the earth."

Noah's Family and the Curse of Canaan

18The sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan. 19These three were Noah's sons, and from them the whole EarthEarthearth was populated.

20Noah, a man of the soil, began by planting a vineyard. 21He drank some of the wine, became drunk, and lay uncovered in his tent. 22Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father's nakedness and told his two brothers outside. 23Shem and Japheth took a garment, laid it across their shoulders, and walked backward to cover their father's nakedness. Their faces were turned away, so they did not see their father's nakedness.

24When Noah woke from his wine, he learned what his youngest son had done to him. 25He said, "Cursed be Canaan! He will be the lowest servant to his brothers." 26He also said, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD, the God of Shem! May Canaan be their servant. 27May God enlarge Japheth, and may he live in the tents of Shem, and may Canaan be their servant."

28After the flood, Noah lived 350 more years. 29Noah lived a total of 950 years, and then he died.
