Solomon Counts the Foreign Workers

17Solomon counted all the foreign men living in Israel, using the census his father David had taken. There were 153,600 of them. 18He made 70,000 of them carry loads, 80,000 of them quarry stone in the mountains, and 3,600 of them supervise the people.
