Peace Offerings from Cattle

1If someone brings a peace offering from the cattle, whether male or female, he must offer an animal without a flaw before the LORD. 2He must lay his hand on the head of his offering and kill it at the entrance to the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons, the priests, must throw the blood against the altar on all sides. 3From the peace offering, he must bring an offering made by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat on them. 4He must remove the two kidneys and the fat on them near the loins, along with the lobe above the liver, and keep them with the kidneys. 5Aaron's sons must burn this on the altar on top of the burnt offering that is on the wood and fire. It is an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

Peace Offerings from Sheep

6If someone's offering for a peace offering to the LORD is from the flock, whether male or female, he must offer it without a flaw. 7If he brings a lamb as his offering, he must present it before the LORD. 8He must lay his hand on the head of his offering and kill it before the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons, the priests, must throw its blood against the altar on all sides. 9From the peace offering, he must bring an offering made by fire to the LORD: its fat, the whole fat tail cut close to the backbone, the fat that covers the inner parts, and all the fat on them. 10He must remove the two kidneys and the fat on them near the loins, along with the lobe above the liver, and keep them with the kidneys. 11The priest must burn these on the altar. This is food, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

Peace Offerings from Goats

12If someone's offering is a goat, he must present it before the LORD. 13He must lay his hand on its head and kill it before the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons, the priests, must throw its blood against the altar on all sides. 14He must present from it, as his offering made by fire to the LORD, the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat on them. 15He must remove the two kidneys and the fat on them near the loins, along with the lobe above the liver, and keep them with the kidneys. 16The priest must burn them on the altar. This is food, an offering made by fire, a pleasing aroma. All fat belongs to the LORD.

A Lasting Rule

17This is a lasting rule for all your generations, in all the places where you live: you must not eat any fat or any blood.
