A New Leader from Jesse's Family

1A shoot will come up from the stump of Jesse, and a BranchbranchBranch from his roots will bear fruit. 2The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD will rest on HimhimHim—the SpiritspiritSpirit of wisdom and understanding, the SpiritspiritSpirit of counsel and might, the SpiritspiritSpirit of knowledge and the fear of the LORD. 3He will delight in the fear of the LORD. He will not judge by what HishisHis eyes see, or decide by what HishisHis ears hear. 4But with righteousness HeheHe will judge the poor, and with fairness HeheHe will decide for the humble of the earth. He will strike the earth with the rod of HishisHis mouth, and with the breath of HishisHis lips HeheHe will slay the wicked. 5Righteousness will be HishisHis belt, and faithfulness the sash around HishisHis waist.

Peace in God's Kingdom

6The wolf will live with the lamb, the leopard will lie down with the goat, the calf and the lion and the fattened calf will be together, and a little child will lead them. 7The cow and the bear will feed; their young will lie down together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox. 8The nursing child will play by the cobra's hole, and the weaned child will put his hand into the viper's den. 9They will not hurt or destroy on all MymyMy holy mountain, for the earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.

The LORD Will Gather His People

10In that day the RootrootRoot of Jesse will stand as a banner for the peoples; the nations will seek HimhimHim, and HishisHis resting place will be glorious. 11In that day the Lord will reach out HishisHis hand a second time to bring back the remnant of HishisHis people who are left—from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea. 12He will raise a banner for the nations and gather the exiles of Israel; HeheHe will collect the scattered people of Judah from the four corners of the earth. 13Ephraim's jealousy will fade away, and those who trouble Judah will be cut off. Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah, and Judah will not trouble Ephraim. 14They will swoop down on the Philistines to the west; together they will plunder the people of the east. They will lay their hand on Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites will be subject to them. 15The LORD will dry up the gulf of the Egyptian sea; with a scorching wind HeheHe will wave HishisHis hand over the Euphrates. He will split it into seven streams, so that people can cross in sandals. 16There will be a highway for the remnant of HishisHis people who are left from Assyria, as there was for Israel when they came up from the land of Egypt.
