Trouble and a Promised Ruler

1Now, O city of soldiers, gather your troops. They have set a siege against us. They will strike the ruler of Israel on the cheek with a rod.

2But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, are small among the clans of Judah. Yet from you will come for MemeMe a ruler over Israel. His origins are from long ago, from ancient times. 3So HeheHe will let them go until the woman in labourlabourlabor has her child. Then the rest of HishisHis brothers will return to the people of Israel. 4He will stand and shepherd HishisHis people with the strength of the LORD, in the majesty of the name of the LORD HishisHis God. They will live in safety, because now HeheHe will be great to the ends of the earth.

Deliverance from Assyria

5This OneoneOne will be our peace. When Assyria invades our land and marches through our palaces, we will raise up seven shepherds and eight leaders against him. 6They will rule the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod at its gates. He will rescue us from Assyria when it invades our land and crosses our borders.

Israel's Remnant and Victory

7The remnant of Jacob will be among many nations like dew from the LORD, like showers on the grass. It comes without waiting for people and does not depend on human help. 8The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the middle of many peoples, like a lion among forest animals and like a young lion among sheep. It tramples and tears as it passes through, and no one can rescue them. 9Your hand will be raised over your enemies, and all your foes will be destroyed.

Idols and Cities Destroyed

10On that day, says the LORD, I will remove your horses from among you and destroy your chariots. 11I will destroy the cities of your land and tear down all your strongholds. 12I will remove sorcery from your hand, and you will have no more fortune-tellers. 13I will remove your carved idols and your sacred pillars from among you, and you will no longer bow down to what your hands have made. 14I will tear out your Asherah poles from among you and destroy your cities. 15In anger and wrath I will take revenge on the nations that did not listen.
