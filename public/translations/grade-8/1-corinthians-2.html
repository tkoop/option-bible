Paul Preached Christ Crucified

1Brothers, when I came to you, I did not come with fancy words or human wisdom. I came to tell you God’s truth. 2I had decided to know only one thing among you: Jesus Christ, and HimhimHim nailed to a cross. 3I was with you in weakness, fear, and much shaking. 4My words and my preaching were not clever speeches meant to win you with human wisdom. They were proof of the SpiritspiritSpirit and HishisHis power. 5I did this so your faith would not rest on human wisdom, but on God’s power.

God’s Hidden Wisdom

6Yet among mature believers, we do speak wisdom. But it is not the wisdom of this world or of its rulers, who are fading away. 7Instead, we speak God’s secret wisdom, which was hidden. God planned it before time began for our glory. 8None of the rulers of this world understood it. If they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. 9But as it is written, “No eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no human heart has imagined what God has prepared for those who love HimhimHim.”

The Spirit Reveals God’s Thoughts

10But God has shown it to us through the SpiritspiritSpirit. The SpiritspiritSpirit searches everything, even the deep things of God. 11Who knows a person’s thoughts except that person’s own spirit? In the same way, no one knows God’s thoughts except the SpiritspiritSpirit of God. 12We have not received the spirit of the world. We have received the SpiritspiritSpirit who comes from God, so we can know the gifts God has given us. 13We speak about these things not with words taught by human wisdom, but with words taught by the SpiritspiritSpirit. We explain spiritual things with spiritual words. 14A person who follows only human nature does not accept the things of God’s SpiritspiritSpirit. To him they seem foolish, and he cannot understand them, because they can be understood only by the SpiritspiritSpirit. 15But a spiritual person judges all things, and no one judges him. 16“Who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to teach HimhimHim?” But we have the mind of Christ.
