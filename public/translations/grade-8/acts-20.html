Paul's Journey After the Riot in Ephesus

1After the uproar ended, Paul sent for the disciples, encouraged them, said goodbye, and left for Macedonia. 2He travelledtravelledtraveled through those regions and, after giving them much encouragement, came to Greece. 3He stayed there three months. When the Jews plotted against him as he was about to sail for Syria, he decided to go back through Macedonia instead.

4He was accompanied by Sopater son of Pyrrhus from Berea; Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica; Gaius from Derbe; and Timothy, Tychicus, and Trophimus from Asia. 5These men went on ahead and waited for us in Troas. 6After the Festival of UnleavenedunleavenedUnleavened BreadbreadBread, we sailed from Philippi. Five days later we reached them in Troas, where we stayed seven days.

The Miracle at Troas

7On the first day of the week, we gathered to break bread. Paul planned to leave the next day, so he spoke to them and kept talking until midnight. 8There were many lamps in the upstairs room where we were gathered. 9A young man named Eutychus was sitting in the window. As Paul kept on speaking, Eutychus became very sleepy, fell into a deep sleep, and fell from the third floor. He was picked up dead.

10Paul went down, threw himself on the young man, and took him in his arms. "Don't be alarmed," he said. "He is still alive." 11Then Paul went back upstairs, broke bread, and ate. He talked with them for a long time, until daylight, and then left. 12The people took the young man home alive and were greatly comforted.

Paul Travels Toward Miletus

13We went on ahead to the ship and sailed for Assos, where we planned to take Paul on board. He had arranged this because he wanted to go there on foot. 14When he met us at Assos, we took him aboard and went to Mitylene. 15We sailed from there and arrived the next day off Chios. The day after that we reached Samos, and the next day we arrived at Miletus.

16Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus so he wouldn't have to spend time in Asia. He was in a hurry to get to Jerusalem, if possible, by the day of Pentecost.

Paul's Farewell to the Ephesian Elders

17From Miletus he sent to Ephesus for the elders of the church.

18When they arrived, he said to them, "YouyouYou know how I lived among you the whole time I was in Asia, from the first day I arrived. 19I served the Lord with all humility, with tears, and with the trials that came to me because of the plots of the Jews. 20I didn't hold back anything that would help you. I taught you in public and from house to house. 21I told Jews and Greeks alike that they must turn to God in repentance and have faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.

"22And now, compelled by the SpiritspiritSpirit, I am going to Jerusalem. I don't know what will happen to me there. 23Except that in every city the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit warns me that prison and suffering are waiting for me. 24But I don't count my life as valuable to myself, if only I can finish my race and complete the work the Lord Jesus gave me: to tell the good news of God's grace.

"25Now I know that none of you, among whom I went about preaching the kingdom, will ever see me again. 26So I tell you today that I am innocent of the blood of all people. 27For I didn't shrink from telling you God's whole plan. 28Watch yourselves and all the flock. The HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit has made you overseers to shepherd the church of God, which HeheHe bought with HishisHis own blood.

"29I know that after I leave, fierce wolves will come in among you and won't spare the flock. 30Even from your own group, men will rise up and twist the truth to pull the disciples away after themselves. 31So stay alert. Remember that for three years I never stopped warning each of you night and day with tears. 32Now I commit you to God and to the word of HishisHis grace, which can build you up and give you an inheritance among all those who have been made holy.

"33I didn't want anyone's silver, gold, or clothing. 34You know that these hands of mine worked to meet my own needs and the needs of those who were with me. 35In everything I did, I showed you that by working hard you must help the weak. Remember the words of the Lord Jesus, who said, 'It is more blessed to give than to receive.'"

36After saying this, he knelt down with all of them and prayed. 37They all wept loudly. They fell on Paul's neck and kissed him. 38They were especially sad because he had said they would never see his face again. Then they walked with him to the ship.
