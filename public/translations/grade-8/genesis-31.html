Jacob Leaves Laban

1Jacob heard what Laban's sons were saying: "Jacob has taken everything that belonged to our father, and he has gotten all this wealth from what was our father's." 2Jacob also saw that Laban was not as friendly toward him as he had been before.

3Then the LORD said to Jacob, "Return to the land of your fathers and to your relatives, and I will be with you."

4So Jacob sent for Rachel and Leah and called them to the field where his flock was. 5He said to them, "I can see that your father is not as friendly toward me as he was before, but the God of my father has been with me. 6You know that I have served your father with all my strength. 7Yet your father has cheated me and changed my wages ten times, but God did not let him harm me. 8If he said, 'The spotted ones will be your wages,' then all the flock had spotted young. If he said, 'The speckled ones will be your wages,' then all the flock had speckled young. 9So God has taken your father's livestock and given them to me.

"10At the time the flock were mating, I looked up and saw in a dream that the male goats mating with the flock were striped, speckled, and spotted. 11The angel of God said to me in the dream, 'Jacob,' and I said, 'Here I am.' 12HeheHe said, 'Look up and see that all the male goats mating are striped, speckled, and spotted, for I have seen everything Laban has been doing to you.' 13'I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed a pillar and where you made a vow to MemeMe. Now get up, leave this land, and go back to the land where you were born.'"

14Rachel and Leah answered him, "IsisIs there still any share or inheritance for us in our father's house? 15Does he not count us as foreigners? He sold us, and he has used up the money he got for us. 16Surely all the wealth God took from our father belongs to us and our children. So do whatever God has told you."

17Then Jacob got up and put his sons and wives on camels. 18He drove off all his livestock and all his property that he had gained, including the livestock he had acquired in Paddan Aram, to go to the land of Canaan to his father Isaac.

19When Laban had gone to shear his sheep, Rachel stole her father's household gods. 20Jacob also tricked Laban the Aramean by not telling him that he was running away. 21So he fled with everything he had. He set out, crossed the Euphrates River, and headed toward the hill country of Gilead.

Laban Chases Jacob

22On the third day Laban was told that Jacob had fled. 23He took his relatives with him and chased Jacob for seven days, catching up with him in the hill country of Gilead.

24But God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream at night and said to him, "Be careful not to say anything to Jacob, either good or bad."

25Laban caught up with Jacob. Jacob had set up his tent in the hill country, and Laban and his relatives camped in the hill country of Gilead.

26Then Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done? You have tricked me and carried off my daughters like captives taken in war. 27Why did you run away in secret and trick me? Why didn't you tell me, so I could have sent you off with joy and singing, with tambourines and harps? 28You didn't even let me kiss my grandchildren and daughters goodbye. You have acted foolishly.

"29I have the power to hurt you, but the God of your father spoke to me last night and said, 'Be careful not to speak to Jacob either good or bad.' 30Now you have gone because you longed so much for your father's house. But why did you steal my gods?"

31Jacob answered Laban, "I was afraid, because I thought you might take your daughters from me by force. 32But if you find your gods, the one who has them will not live. In front of our relatives, point out anything of mine that is yours and take it." Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.

33So Laban went into Jacob's tent, Leah's tent, and the tent of the two female servants, but he did not find anything. He came out of Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent. 34Rachel had taken the household gods and put them in the camel's saddle, and she was sitting on them. Laban searched the whole tent but did not find them. 35She said to her father, "MymyMy lord, don't be angry that I cannot stand up in front of you, because I am having my period." So he searched, but he did not find the household gods.

36Then Jacob became angry and argued with Laban. Jacob said to Laban, "What is my crime? What is my sin, that you have chased after me so hotly? 37You have searched through all my belongings. What have you found from all your household goods? Put it here in front of my relatives and yours, so they can decide between the two of us."

"38These twenty years I have been with you. Your sheep and goats have not miscarried, and I have not eaten the rams of your flocks. 39I did not bring you animals killed by wild animals. I took the loss myself. You demanded payment from me for anything stolen by day or by night. 40I suffered in the heat by day and the cold by night, and sleep left my eyes.

"41These twenty years I have been in your house. I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flock, and you changed my wages ten times. 42If the God of my father, the God of Abraham, and the FearfearFear of Isaac, had not been on my side, you would have sent me away empty-handed. But God has seen my hardship and the work of my hands, and HeheHe rebuked you last night."

Jacob and Laban Make a Covenant

43Laban answered Jacob, "ThetheThe daughters are my daughters, the children are my children, the flocks are my flocks, and everything you see is mine. But what can I do today about these daughters of mine or about the children they have had? 44Come now, let us make an agreement, you and I, and let it be a witness between you and me."

45So Jacob took a stone and set it up as a pillar. 46Jacob said to his relatives, "Gather stones." So they gathered stones and made a heap, and they ate there by the heap.

47Laban called it Jegar Sahadutha, but Jacob called it Galeed. 48Laban said, "This heap is a witness between you and me today." Therefore he named it Galeed.

49Laban also called it Mizpah, because he said, "May the LORD watch between you and me when we are away from each other. 50If you mistreat my daughters or take other wives besides my daughters, even though no one else is with us, see, God is a witness between you and me."

51Laban said to Jacob, "Look at this heap and this pillar that I have set up between you and me. 52This heap and this pillar are witnesses that I will not cross over to your side of them to harm you, and you will not cross over to my side of them to harm me."

53"May the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, the God of their father, judge between us." So Jacob swore by the FearfearFear of his father Isaac.

54Then Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain and invited his relatives to eat bread. They ate and spent the night on the mountain. 55Early in the morning Laban got up, kissed his sons and daughters, and blessed them. Then Laban left and went back home.
