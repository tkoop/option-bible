Love Is Greater Than Spiritual Gifts

1If I speak in human languages or even in the language of angels, but I do not have love, I am only a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal. 2If I have the gift of prophecy and understand all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so that I could move mountains, but I do not have love, I am nothing. 3If I give everything I own to feed the poor, and if I hand over my body so that I can brag, but I do not have love, I gain nothing.

What Love Looks Like

4Love is patient and kind. Love does not envy, does not brag, and is not proud. 5It is not rude, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, and it keeps no record of wrongs. 6It does not rejoice in evil, but it rejoices with the truth. 7It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, and always perseveres.

Love Never Fails

8Love never fails. But prophecies will cease, tongues will be stilled, and knowledge will pass away. 9For we know in part and we prophesy in part, 10but when completeness comes, what is partial will disappear. 11When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child, and I reasoned like a child. When I became a man, I set aside childish ways. 12For now we see only a dim reflectionreflectionreflection, as in a mirror; then we will see face to face. Now I know in part; then I will know fully, even as I am fully known. 13And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love. But the greatest of these is love.
