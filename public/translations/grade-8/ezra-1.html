Cyrus Allows the Jews to Return

1In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, the LORD carried out the word HeheHe had spoken through Jeremiah. The LORD stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and Cyrus sent a message through all his kingdom. Cyrus also put it in writing:

2“This is what Cyrus king of Persia says: ‘The LORD, the God of Heavenheavenheaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth and has appointed me to build a house for HimhimHim in Jerusalem in Judah. 3Whoever among you belongs to HishisHis people, may his God be with him, and may he go up to Jerusalem in Judah and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem. 4And let every survivor, wherever he lives, be helped by the men of his area with silver, gold, goods, and livestock, along with a freewill offering for the house of God in Jerusalem.’”

The People Prepare to Rebuild

5Then the family leaders of Judah and Benjamin, the priests, and the Levites rose up. God had stirred the spirit of each of them, and they went up to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. 6All their neighbors supported them with silver and gold items, goods, livestock, and costly gifts, in addition to all the freewill offerings.

The Temple Articles Are Returned

7King Cyrus brought out the articles from the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar had taken from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his god. 8Cyrus king of Persia had Mithredath the treasurer bring them out and count them to Sheshbazzar, the leader of Judah.

9There were 30 gold basins, 1,000 silver basins, and 29 silver utensils. 10There were also 30 gold bowls, 410 silver bowls of a second kind, and 1,000 other articles. 11In all, there were 5,400 gold and silver articles. Sheshbazzar brought all of them when the exiles went up from Babylon to Jerusalem.
