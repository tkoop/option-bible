The Ziphites Tell Saul About David

1The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Isn't David hiding on the hill of Hakilah, facing Jeshimon?"

2So Saul got up and took three thousand chosen men of Israel with him. He went down to the wilderness of Ziph to look for David. 3Saul camped beside the road on the hill of Hakilah, facing Jeshimon. David stayed in the wilderness. When he saw that Saul had come after him there, 4David sent out spies and learned for sure that Saul had come.

David Finds Saul Asleep in Camp

5David got up and went to the place where Saul had camped. He saw where Saul and Abner son of Ner, the army commander, were lying. Saul was lying in the camp, and the people were camped around him.

6David said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, "Who will go down with me to Saul in the camp?"

Abishai said, "I will go down with you."

7So David and Abishai went to the people by night. There was Saul, sleeping in the camp. His spear was stuck in the ground at his head. Abner and the people were sleeping around him.

David Spares Saul and Calls Out

8Abishai said to David, "Today God has given your enemy into your hand. Now let me pin him to the ground with one spear thrust. I won't need a second."

9But David said to Abishai, "Don't destroy him. Who can hurt the the LORD's anointed and not be guilty?" 10David also said, "As the the LORD lives, the the LORD will strike him down, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go into battle and be swept away." 11"The ThetheThe LORD forbid that I should hurt the the LORD's anointed. Now take the spear by his head and the water jar, and let's go."

12So David took the spear and the water jar from beside Saul's head, and they left. No one saw them or knew about it, and no one woke up, because they were all asleep. A deep sleep from the the LORD had fallen on them.

13David crossed over to the other side and stood on top of the hill far away, with a large space between them. 14David called out to the people and to Abner son of Ner, "Won't you answer, Abner?"

Abner answered, "Who are you who is calling to the king?"

15David said to Abner, "Aren't you a man? Who is like you in Israel? Why didn't you guard your lord the king? Someone came in to destroy the king, your lord. 16What you did was not good. As the the LORD lives, you deserve death because you did not guard your lord, the the LORD's anointed. Now look where the king's spear and water jar are, the ones that were by his head."

18David said, "Why does my lord chase his servant? What have I done? What wrong is in my hand? 19Now please, my lord king, listen to your servant's words. If the the LORD has stirred you up against me, may HeheHe accept an offering. But if people have done it, may they be cursed before the the LORD. The king of Israel has come out to look for one tiny flea, like hunting a bird in the hills."

21Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my son David. I will not harm you again, because my life was precious in your eyes today. I have acted foolishly and made a great mistake."

22David answered, "Here is the king's spear. Let one of the young men come over and get it. 23The ThetheThe LORD will repay each man for his right living and faithfulness. The the LORD handed you over to me today, but I would not hurt the the LORD's anointed. 24Just as your life was precious in my eyes today, so may my life be precious in the the LORD's eyes, and may HeheHe save me from all trouble."
