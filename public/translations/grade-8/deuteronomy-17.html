Rules for Sacrifices

1Do not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect or any flaw. That is an abomination to the LORD your God.

Punishing Idolatry

2If a man or woman among you in one of your towns that the LORD your God gives you is found doing evil in the sight of the LORD your God by breaking HishisHis covenant, 3and that person has gone and served other gods and worshiped them, or the sun or the moon or all the host of Heavenheavenheaven, which I have not commanded, 4and it has been told to you, and you have heard it and searched carefully, and behold, the thing is true and certain, that this abomination has been done in Israel, 5then you shall bring out to your gates that man or woman who has done this evil thing, and you shall stone them with stones so that they die. 6A person must be put to death only on the testimony of two or three witnesses. He must not be put to death on the testimony of just one witness. 7The witnesses must be the first to put him to death, and then all the people. So you shall purge the evil from your midst.

Judging Difficult Cases

8If a case is too hard for you to judge—between one case of bloodshed and another, between one legal ruling and another, or between one injury and another—these disputed matters in your towns, then you shall arise and go up to the place that the LORD your God will choose. 9You shall come to the Levitical priests and to the judge who is in office in those days, and you shall inquire of them, and they shall declare to you the sentence of judgmentjudgementjudgment. 10You shall act according to the decision they announce to you from that place the the LORD will choose. Be careful to do everything they instruct you. 11According to the law they teach you and according to the judgmentjudgementjudgment they tell you, you shall do. You must not turn aside from what they declare to you, to the right or to the left. 12But the man who deliberately refuses to obey the priest who stands there to serve the LORD your God, or the judge, that man shall die. So you shall purge the evil from Israel. 13Then all the people will hear and be afraid, and they will not act so arrogantly again.

Rules for a King

14When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, take possession of it and settle there, and then you say, "Let me set a king over me, like all the nations around me."

15You must be sure to set over you a king whom the LORD your God chooses. You must appoint a king from among your brothers. You may not put a foreigner over you, someone who is not your brother. 16He must not get many horses for himself, and he must not make the people return to Egypt to get more horses, for the LORD has said to you, "YouyouYou must never return that way again."

17He must not take many wives for himself, or his heart will turn away. He must not collect large amounts of silver and gold for himself. 18When he sits on his royal throne, he is to write for himself a copy of this law on a scroll, from the copy kept by the Levitical priests. 19He must keep it with him and read it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear the LORD his God and carefully follow all the words of this law and these statutes. 20Then his heart will not become proud and think he is better than his brothers, and he will not turn from this command to the right or to the left. Then he and his sons will rule for a long time over his kingdom in Israel.
