Eliphaz Answers Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

2“If I try to speak to you, will you be upset? But who can keep from speaking? 3Look, you have taught many people, and you have strengthened weak hands. 4Your words have supported those who stumble, and you have strengthened weak knees. 5But now it has come to you, and you are weary; it touches you, and you are dismayed. 6Isn't your reverence for God your confidence, and your blameless life your hope?

7“Remember, who has ever died while innocent? Or when have honest people been destroyed? 8As I have seen, those who do wrong reap what they sow. 9By God's breath they perish, and by the blast of HishisHis anger they are consumed. 10The roaring of the lion, the growl of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken. 11The old lion dies for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

A Vision in the Night

12“Now a word was secretly brought to me, and my ear received a whisper of it. 13In thoughts from night visions, when deep sleep falls on people, 14fear and trembling came upon me and made all my bones shake. 15A spirit glided past my face, and the hair on my body stood up. 16It stopped, but I could not tell what it looked like. A shape stood before my eyes. Then I heard a quiet voice:

Can Anyone Be Right Before God?

17“‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God? Can a man be more pure than his MakermakerMaker?’ 18‘If HeheHe does not trust HishisHis own servants and finds fault with HishisHis angels, 19how much more are people who live in clay houses, built on dust, crushed like a moth! 20They are broken from morning to evening; they perish forever, and no one notices. 21Aren't their lives taken away? They die without wisdom.’”
