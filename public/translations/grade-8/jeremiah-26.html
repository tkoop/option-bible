The LORD's Warning to Judah

1In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came from the LORD, saying:

2“Thus says the LORD: Stand in the courtyard of the LORD’s house, and speak to all the people from the cities of Judah who come to worship in the LORD’s house. Tell them everything I have commanded you to say. Do not leave out a single word. 3Perhaps they will listen and each turn from his evil way. Then I will change MymyMy mind and not bring on them the disaster I plan to bring because of the evil things they have done. 4And say to them, ‘This is what the LORD says: If you do not listen to MemeMe and walk in MymyMy law, which I have set before you, 5and if you do not listen to the words of MymyMy servants the prophets, whom I send to you again and again, though you have not listened, 6then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make this city a curse to all the nations of the earth.’”

Jeremiah Is Seized and Accused

7The priests, the prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah speak these words in the house of the LORD.

8When Jeremiah finished saying everything the the LORD had commanded him to say to all the people, the priests, the prophets, and all the people seized him. They said, “You must die!”

9“Why have you prophesied in the name of the LORD that this house will be like Shiloh and this city will be destroyed and left empty?” Then all the people gathered around Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.

10When the officials of Judah heard about this, they came up from the king’s palace to the house of the LORD and sat at the entrance of the NewnewNew GategateGate of the LORD’s house.

11Then the priests and prophets said to the officials and all the people, “This man should be sentenced to death, because he has prophesied against this city. You have heard it with your own ears.”

Jeremiah Defends Himself

12Then Jeremiah said to all the officials and all the people, “The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and this city. I have spoken all the words you have heard. 13Now change your ways and your actions, and obey the LORD your God. Then the LORD will change HishisHis mind and not bring on you the disaster HeheHe has announced. 14As for me, I am in your hands. Do to me whatever you think is good and right. 15But be sure of this: If you kill me, you will make yourselves guilty of an innocent man’s blood. That guilt will fall on you, on this city, and on everyone who lives here. For the LORD has truly sent me to speak all these words to you.”

Jeremiah Is Spared

16Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and prophets, “This man does not deserve to die, because he has spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God.”

17Some of the elders of the land stood up and said to the whole crowd, “18Micah of Moresheth prophesied during the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. He told all the people of Judah, ‘This is what the LORD of Hosts says: Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the temple hill will become a forest.’ 19Did Hezekiah king of Judah or anyone in Judah put him to death? No. Hezekiah feared the LORD and asked the LORD for help, and the LORD changed HishisHis mind and did not bring on them the disaster HeheHe had threatened. But if we kill Jeremiah, we will bring great harm on ourselves.”

20There was another man who prophesied in the name of the LORD. His name was Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath Jearim. He prophesied against this city and this land in the same way Jeremiah did.

21When King Jehoiakim and all his officers and officials heard his words, the king wanted to kill him. Uriah heard about it, became afraid, and fled to Egypt.

22But King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt. Elnathan son of Achbor went, along with other men.

23They brought Uriah back from Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim. The king killed him with a sword and threw his body into the burial place of the common people.

24But Ahikam son of Shaphan stood with Jeremiah and kept the people from putting him to death.
