The LORD Calls the Nations to Listen

1"Be quiet before MemeMe, you islands. Let the nations gain new strength. Let them come near and speak. Let us come together to judge.

2"Who stirred up a ruler from the east and called him to do what is right? The LORD gives nations to him and makes kings fall. He turns them to dust with his sword and to straw with his bow.

3"He chases them and moves on safely, along a path his feet have never walked.

4"Who has done this and made it happen? He calls the generations from the beginning. I am the LORD. I am the first and the last. I am HeheHe."

5The islands see this and are afraid. The ends of the earth shake with fear. They come close and gather together.

6Each person helps the next and tells his brother to be strong.

7The worker encourages the metalworker. The one who smooths with a hammer praises the one who hits the anvil. He says the soldering is good. Then he fastens it with nails so it will not move.

God Strengthens His Servant Israel

8"But you, Israel, are MymyMy servant. Jacob, I chose you. You are the descendants of Abraham, MymyMy friend.

9"I brought you from the far ends of the earth. I called you from its farthest corners. I said you are MymyMy servant. I chose you and did not reject you.

10"Do not be afraid, because I am with you. Do not lose heart, because I am your God. I will make you strong. I will help you. I will hold you up with MymyMy right hand, which always does what is right.

11"Look, all who are angry with you will be ashamed and disgraced. Those who argue with you will be as nothing and will die.

12"You will look for them, but you will not find them. Those who fight against you will be as nothing, with no power.

13"I am the LORD your God. I hold your right hand. I say to you not to be afraid, because I will help you.

14"Do not be afraid, Jacob, you worm. Israel, you are only a few people. I will help you," says the LORD. "Your Redeemer is the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel.

15"Look, I will make you like a new, sharp threshing tool with many teeth. You will crush mountains and break them apart. You will make hills like straw.

16"You will scatter them, and the wind will carry them away. A storm will spread them out. But you will be glad in the LORD. You will praise the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel."

The LORD Provides for the Needy

17"The poor and needy look for water, but there is none. Their tongues are dry with thirst. But I, the LORD, will answer them. I, the God of Israel, will not leave them.

18"I will open rivers on bare hills and springs in the valleys. I will turn the desert into a pool of water and dry land into flowing springs.

19"I will plant cedar, acacia, myrtle, and olive trees in the desert. I will put juniper, elm, and cypress trees together in the dry land.

20"Then people will see and know. They will think and understand that the hand of the LORD has done this. The HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel has created it."

The LORD Challenges the Idols

21"Bring your case to court," says the LORD. "Give your best reasons," says the KingkingKing of Jacob.

22"Let them bring out and tell us what will happen. Tell us what happened before, so we can think about it and know how it ended. Or tell us what is coming.

23"Tell us what will happen later, so we may know that you are gods. Yes, do something, good or bad, so we can be afraid and amazed.

24"Look, you are nothing, and your work is worth nothing. Anyone who chooses you is disgusting.

25"I stirred up someone from the north, and he came. From the east, he calls on MymyMy name. He walks on rulers like mud, like a potter trampling clay.

26"Who told this from the start, so we would know? Who said it long ago, so we could say he is right? No one told it. No one heard your words.

27"I was the first to tell Zion that they are coming. I sent good news to Jerusalem.

28"I look, but there is no one. There is no adviser. I ask them, but they give no answer.

29"Look, they are all false. Their work is nothing. Their metal statues are only wind and empty air."
