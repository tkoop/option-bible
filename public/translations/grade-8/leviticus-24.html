Oil for the Lampstand

1The LORD said to Moses, "2Command the Israelites to bring you clear oil from pressed olives for the light, so the lamps can burn all the time. 3Outside the curtain of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron must arrange it all the time, from evening to morning, before the LORD. This is a lasting rule for all your generations. 4He must arrange the lamps on the pure lampstand before the LORD all the time.

"5You must take fine flour and bake twelve loaves of bread. Each loaf should use two-tenths of an ephah of flour. 6Put the loaves in two rows, six in each row, on the pure table before the LORD. 7Put pure frankincense on each row. It will be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering made by fire to the LORD. 8Every Sabbath day, arrange it before the LORD all the time. This is a lasting promise from the Israelites. 9It belongs to Aaron and his sons. They must eat it in a holy place, because it is most holy to him from the offerings made by fire to the LORD. This is a lasting rule."

A Blasphemer in the Camp

10The son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian father went out among the Israelites. He and an Israelite man got into a fight in the camp. 11The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the NamenameName and cursed. They brought him to Moses. His mother was Shelomith, daughter of Dibri, from the tribe of Dan. 12They put him under guard until the LORD's will was made clear to them.

Punishment for Blasphemy and Harm

13The LORD said to Moses, "14BringbringBring out the one who cursed to outside the camp. Let all who heard him lay their hands on his head. Then let the whole congregation stone him. 15Speak to the Israelites and say, 'Anyone who curses his God will bear his sin. 16Whoever blasphemes the NamenameName of the LORD must surely be put to death. The whole congregation must stone him. It does not matter whether he is a foreigner or a native-born Israelite; when he blasphemes the NamenameName, he must be put to death. 17Whoever takes a human life must surely be put to death. 18Whoever kills an animal must pay for it, life for life. 19If a man injures his neighbourneighbourneighbor, he must be treated the same way he treated the other person. 20A broken bone for a broken bone, an eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth. The injury he causes another person must be given back to him. 21Whoever kills an animal must pay for it, but whoever kills a person must be put to death. 22You must use the same law for the foreigner and the native-born, for I am the LORD your God.'"

23Moses spoke to the Israelites. They brought the man who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him. The Israelites did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
