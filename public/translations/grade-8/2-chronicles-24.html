Joash Becomes King

1Joash was seven years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Zibiah, and she was from Beersheba. 2Joash did what was right in the eyes of the LORD all the days of Jehoiada the priest. 3Jehoiada chose two wives for him, and he had sons and daughters.

The Temple Is Repaired

4After this, Joash decided to repair the house of the LORD. 5He gathered the priests and Levites and said, “Go out to the cities of Judah and collect money from all Israel year by year to repair the house of your God. Hurry with this.” But the Levites did not hurry. 6So the king called Jehoiada the chief priest and said, “Why haven’t you required the Levites to bring in from Judah and Jerusalem the tax that Moses, the servant of the LORD, set for Israel for the tent of meeting?”

7For the sons of that wicked woman Athaliah had broken into the house of God and had used all the dedicated things of the house of the LORD for the Baals. 8At the king’s command, they made a chest and set it outside the gate of the house of the LORD. 9Then they made a proclamation in Judah and Jerusalem that the people should bring to the LORD the tax Moses, God’s servant, had set for Israel in the wilderness. 10All the leaders and all the people rejoiced. They brought their gifts and dropped them into the chest until it was full.

11Whenever the chest was brought by the Levites to the king’s officials, and they saw that there was much money, the king’s secretary and the chief priest’s officer would empty the chest and carry it back to its place. They did this day by day and collected a large amount of money. 12The king and Jehoiada gave it to those who did the work on the house of the LORD. They hired stonecutters and carpenters to repair the house of the LORD, and also workers in iron and bronze to strengthen it. 13The workers labouredlabouredlabored, and the repair work went forward. They rebuilt the house of God according to its plan and strengthened it. 14When they finished, they brought the rest of the money to the king and Jehoiada. They made articles for the house of the LORD—articles for service, for burnt offerings, and bowls, and gold and silver vessels. Burnt offerings were offered regularly in the house of the LORD all the days of Jehoiada.

15Jehoiada grew old and full of days and died. He was 130 years old when he died. 16They buried him with the kings in the CitycityCity of David, because he had done good in Israel, for God, and for HishisHis house.

Judah Turns Away from God

17After Jehoiada died, the leaders of Judah came and bowed down to the king, and the king listened to them. 18They left the house of the LORD, the God of their fathers, and worshiped the Asherah poles and idols. Because of this guilt, God’s anger came on Judah and Jerusalem. 19He sent prophets to bring them back to the LORD, and they warned them, but they would not listen.

20Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of God came on Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest. He stood above the people and said, “This is what God says: Why do you break the the LORD’s commands? You will not succeed. Because you have left the LORD, HeheHe has left you.” 21They plotted against him at the king’s command and stoned him to death in the courtyard of the house of the LORD. 22King Joash did not remember the kindness that Jehoiada, Zechariah’s father, had shown him. Instead, he killed Jehoiada’s son. As Zechariah was dying, he said, “May the LORD see this and hold you accountable.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Comes to Joash

23At the end of the year, the army of Aram came against Joash. They invaded Judah and Jerusalem and killed all the leaders of the people. They sent all the plunder to the king of Damascus. 24The army of Aram came with a small force, but the LORD handed over a very large army to them, because Judah had left the LORD, the God of their fathers. So they carried out judgmentjudgementjudgment on Joash. 25When the Arameans left, they left Joash badly wounded. His own servants plotted against him because he had shed the blood of the son of Jehoiada the priest. They killed him on his bed, and he died. He was buried in the CitycityCity of David, but not in the tombs of the kings.

26Those who plotted against him were Zabad son of Shimeath, an Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith, a Moabite woman. 27The accounts of his sons, the many prophecies against him, and the rebuilding of the house of God are written in the commentary on the BookbookBook of the Kings. His son Amaziah became king after him.
