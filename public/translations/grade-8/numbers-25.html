Israel Turns to Baal of Peor

1While Israel was staying in Shittim, the people began to be unfaithful with the women of Moab. 2The Moabite women invited the people to sacrifices for their gods. The people ate the food and bowed down to those gods. 3So Israel joined itself to Baal of Peor, and the LORD became very angry with Israel.

The LORD Orders JudgmentJudgementJudgment

4The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and hang them in the sun before the LORD, so the LORD's fierce anger will turn away from Israel." 5Moses told the judges of Israel, "Each judge must kill the men in his own group who joined themselves to Baal of Peor."

Phinehas Stops the Plague

6An Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his relatives. He did this right in front of Moses and all the people of Israel while they were crying at the entrance to the tent of meeting. 7When Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw this, he got up from among the people and took a spear in his hand. 8He followed the Israelite man into the tent and drove the spear through both of them, the Israelite man and the woman, through her body. Then the plague among the Israelites stopped. 9But twenty-four thousand people had already died in the plague.

The LORD's Covenant with Phinehas

14The name of the Israelite who was killed with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu. He was a leader of a family group among the Simeonites. 15The woman's name was Cozbi daughter of Zur. She was the head of a family group in Midian.

The LORD Commands War Against Midian

16The LORD said to Moses, 17"Attack the Midianites and strike them down. 18They are your enemies. They tricked you with their plan about Peor and about Cozbi, the daughter of a Midianite leader. She was their sister, and she was killed on the day of the plague because of Peor."
