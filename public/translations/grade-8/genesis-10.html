The Families of Noah After the Flood

1These are the family lines of Noah. His sons were Shem, Ham, and Japheth, and sons were born to them after the flood. 2The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. 3The sons of Gomer were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. 4The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. 5From these people the coastlands of the nations spread out in their lands. Each group had its own language, clan, and nation.

The Descendants of Ham

6The sons of Ham were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. 7The sons of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan. 8Cush was the father of Nimrod, who began to be a mighty man on the earth. 9He was a mighty hunter before the the LORD. That is why people say, "Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the the LORD." 10The first cities of his kingdom were Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in the land of Shinar. 11From that land he went to Assyria and built Nineveh, Rehoboth Ir, and Calah. 12He also built Resen between Nineveh and Calah, which is the great city.

13Mizraim was the father of the Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, and Naphtuhim. 14He was also the father of the Pathrusim, Casluhim (from whom the Philistines came), and Caphtorim.

15Canaan was the father of Sidon his firstborn, and of Heth. 16He was also the father of the Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites, 17Hivites, Arkites, Sinites, 18Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites. Later the clans of the Canaanites spread out. 19The territory of the Canaanites stretched from Sidon toward Gerar as far as Gaza, and toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Lasha.

20These were the sons of Ham, grouped by their clans, languages, lands, and nations.

The Descendants of Shem

21Sons were also born to Shem. He was the forefather of all the descendants of Eber, and he was Japheth's brother. 22The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram. 23The sons of Aram were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. 24Arpachshad was the father of Shelah, and Shelah was the father of Eber. 25Two sons were born to Eber. One was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided. His brother was named Joktan.

26Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, 27Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, 28Obal, Abimael, Sheba, 29Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

30Their home was in the hill country of the east, from Mesha toward Sephar. 31These were the sons of Shem, grouped by their clans, languages, lands, and nations. 32These were the clans of Noah, listed by their generations and nations. From them the nations spread out over the earth after the flood.
