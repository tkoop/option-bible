The LORD Commands a Lament for Tyre

1The word of the LORD came to me:

2"Son of man, raise a lament for Tyre. 3Say to Tyre, who sits at the gateway to the sea and trades with many nations on the coastlands: This is what the Lord, the LORD, says: You, Tyre, said, 'I am perfect in beauty.'

Tyre's Pride and SplendourSplendourSplendor

"4Your borders are in the heart of the seas; your builders made your beauty complete. 5They built all your planks from cypress trees from Senir. They took cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you. 6They made your oars from oaks of Bashan. They made your deck from ivory inlaid with wood from the coasts of Kittim. 7Your sail was fine linen from Egypt, so it could be your banner. Your awning was blue and purple cloth from the coasts of Elishah.

"8The people of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers. Your wise men, Tyre, were your pilots. 9The elders of Gebal and its wise men repaired your seams. All the ships of the sea and their sailors were with you to trade your goods. 10Men of Persia, Lud, and Put served in your army. They hung shield and helmet in you. They gave you splendoursplendoursplendor. 11Men of Arvad and your army stood on your walls all around. The men of Gamad were in your towers. They hung their shields all around your walls. They made your beauty complete.

Tyre's Trading Partners

"12Tarshish traded with you because of your great wealth. They paid for your goods with silver, iron, tin, and lead. 13Greece, Tubal, and Meshech traded with you. They paid for your goods with slaves and bronze vessels. 14From Beth Togarmah they gave horses, war horses, and mules for your goods. 15The men of Dedan traded with you. Many coastlands were your market. They brought you ivory tusks and ebony as payment. 16Aram traded with you because of your many works. They paid for your goods with turquoise, purple cloth, embroidered cloth, fine linen, coral, and rubies. 17Judah and the land of Israel traded with you. They paid for your goods with wheat from Minnith, pannag, honey, oil, and balm.

"18Because of your many works and great wealth, Damascus traded with you. They paid with wine from Helbon and wool as white as snow. 19Dan and Greece traded with you. They brought wrought iron, cassia, and cane. 20Dedan traded with you in saddle blankets. 21Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were your customers. They traded with you in lambs, rams, and goats. 22The traders of Sheba and Raamah traded with you. They paid for your goods with the best spices, gold, and precious stones. 23Haran, Canneh, Eden, and the traders of Sheba, along with Asshur and Chilmad, traded with you. 24They traded with you in beautiful clothes, blue cloth, embroidered cloth, and carpets of richly colouredcolouredcolored yarn, all tied with cords and packed in cedar boxes.

Tyre's Fall and Mourning

"25The ships of Tarshish carried your goods. You were full and very glorious in the heart of the seas. 26Your rowers brought you into deep waters. The east wind broke you in the heart of the seas. 27Your wealth, your goods, your merchandise, your sailors, your pilots, your repairers, your traders, all your soldiers, and everyone in your assembly will fall into the heart of the seas on the day you are destroyed.

"28The shores will shake at the sound of your pilots' cry. 29All who handle the oar, the sailors, and all the pilots of the sea will come down from their ships and stand on the shore. 30They will raise their voices over you and cry bitterly. They will throw dust on their heads and roll in ashes. 31They will shave their heads for you and wear sackcloth. They will weep for you with bitter grief. 32In their wailing they will take up a lament for you and mourn over you: 'Who was ever like Tyre, destroyed in the midst of the sea?'

"33When your goods went out from the seas, you satisfied many nations. With your great wealth and trade, you enriched the kings of the earth. 34Now you are broken by the seas in the depths of the waters. Your merchandise and all your company have fallen with you. 35All who live on the coastlands are appalled at you. Their kings shudder with horror. Their faces are twisted in fear. 36The merchants among the nations hiss at you. You have become a horror, and you will be no more forever."
