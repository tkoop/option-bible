1The word of the the LORD came to me:

Shepherds Who Feed Themselves

2"Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord the the LORD says: How terrible for the shepherds of Israel who only feed themselves! Should not the shepherds feed the flock? 3You eat the fat, wear the wool, and slaughter the fattened sheep, but you do not feed the flock. 4You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bandaged the injured, brought back the strays, or searched for the lost. Instead, you have ruled them with force and cruelty. 5So they were scattered because there was no shepherd, and when they were scattered they became food for all the wild animals. 6My flock strayed on all the mountains and every high hill. They were scattered over the whole EarthEarthearth, and no one searched or looked for them.
