Four Kings Fight Five Kings

1In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of Goiim, 2these kings made war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar. 3All of them joined together in the ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim, which is the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea. 4They had served Chedorlaomer for twelve years, but in the thirteenth year they rebelled.

5In the fourteenth year, Chedorlaomer and the kings with him came and defeated the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim, 6and the Horites in their hill country of Seir, all the way to El-paran near the desert. 7Then they turned back and came to En-mishpat, which is Kadesh, and defeated all the country of the Amalekites and also the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.

8The king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar, went out and lined up for battle against them in the ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim. 9They fought four kings against five. 10The ValleyvalleyValley of Siddim was full of tar pits, and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled and fell into them, while the rest fled to the hills. 11The invaders took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food and left. 12They also took Lot, Abram's nephew, and his possessions, because he was living in Sodom, and they left.

Abram Rescues Lot

13Then a man who had escaped came and told Abram the Hebrew. Abram was living near the oak trees of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and the brother of Aner. These men had made an agreement with Abram. 14When Abram heard that his relative had been captured, he led out his trained men, born in his house—318 of them—and chased the enemy as far as Dan. 15He split his forces against them at night, he and his servants, and defeated them. He chased them as far as Hobah, north of Damascus. 16He brought back all the goods. He also brought back his relative Lot and his goods, and the women and the people.

Abram Refuses the King of Sodom's Offer

21The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people, but you may keep the goods." 22Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I have sworn to the LORD, God MostmostMost HighhighHigh, Possessor of Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth, 23that I will not take a thread or a sandal strap from anything that is yours, so that you cannot say, 'I made Abram rich.' 24I will take nothing except what the young men have eaten, and the share for the men who went with me—Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre.
