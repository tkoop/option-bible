Enemies Lose Heart

1When all the Amorite kings west of the Jordan and all the Canaanite kings along the coast heard that the LORD had dried up the Jordan River for the Israelites until they crossed over, their hearts melted. They lost all courage because of the Israelites.

Circumcision at Gilgal

2At that time the LORD said to Joshua, "Make flint knives and circumcise the Israelites a second time." 3So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelites at the Hill of Foreskins.

4Joshua did this because all the men who had left Egypt, the soldiers, had died in the wilderness on the way after they left Egypt. 5The people who left Egypt had been circumcised, but those born in the wilderness along the way had not.

6The Israelites had travelledtravelledtraveled in the wilderness for forty years, until all the fighting men who left Egypt were gone. They had not obeyed the LORD. So the LORD swore they would not see the land HeheHe had promised their ancestors, a land flowing with milk and honey. 7So Joshua circumcised their sons, who took their place. They had not been circumcised on the journey, so they were still uncircumcised. 8After the whole nation had been circumcised, they stayed in the camp until they healed.

9Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Today I have removed the disgrace of Egypt from you." So that place was named Gilgal, and it still has that name today.

Passover in the Promised Land

10The Israelites camped at Gilgal on the plains of Jericho. On the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, they kept the Passover. 11The day after the Passover, they ate unleavened bread and roasted grain from the land. 12The manna stopped the day after they ate food from the land. The Israelites no longer had manna. That year they ate from the crops of Canaan.

Joshua Meets a Heavenly Commander

13While Joshua was near Jericho, he looked up and saw a man standing in front of him with a drawn sword in his hand. Joshua went to him and asked, "Are you for us or for our enemies?"

14"No," he said. "I am the commander of the LORD's army, and I have come now."

Then Joshua fell with his face to the ground and worshiped. He said, "What does my Lord have to say to HishisHis servant?"

15The commander of the LORD's army said to Joshua, "Take off your sandals, because the place where you are standing is holy." So Joshua did as he was told.
