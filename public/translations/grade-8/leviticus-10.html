Instructions for the Priests

6Then Moses said to Aaron and his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Do not let your hair hang loose or tear your clothes, or you will die and the LORD will be angry with the whole community. The Priests' Share of the Offerings

12Moses said to Aaron and his surviving sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Take the grain offering left from the food offerings to the LORD and eat it without yeast beside the altar, because it is most holy. 13You are to eat it in a holy place, because it is your share and your sons' share of the food offerings to the LORD; for this is what I have been commanded. 14And you and your sons and daughters may eat the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution in a clean place, because these are your portion and your children's portion from the peace offerings of the Israelites. 15They are to bring the thigh of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering, along with the fat parts of the offerings, to be waved as a wave offering before the LORD. It will belong to you and your sons forever, as the LORD has commanded."

Moses Questions the Priests

16Then Moses asked about the goat of the sin offering, and saw that it had been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's surviving sons, and asked, 17"Why didn't you eat the sin offering in the holy place? It is most holy. He gave it to you to take away the guilt of the community and to make atonement for them before the LORD. 18Since its blood was not brought into the holy place, you should have eaten it there, as I commanded." 19Aaron replied to Moses, "Look, today they presented their sin offering and burnt offering before the LORD. But such things as these have happened to me. Would it have been good in the LORD's sight if I had eaten the sin offering today?" 20When Moses heard this, he was satisfied.
