Jeremiah's Letter to the Exiles

1This is the text of the letter Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem to the rest of the elders among the exiles, and to the priests, the prophets, and all the people Nebuchadnezzar had carried into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon. 2This was after King Jeconiah, the queen mother, the court officials, the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem, the craftsmen, and the metalworkers had been taken from Jerusalem into exile. 3The letter was carried by Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to King Nebuchadnezzar in Babylon. It said:

God's Message and Promise to the Exiles

4"This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says to all the exiles I carried away from Jerusalem to Babylon: 5Build houses and settle down. Plant gardens and eat their fruit. 6Take wives and have sons and daughters. Take wives for your sons and give your daughters in marriage, so they too may have sons and daughters. Have many children there; don't let your numbers drop. 7Work for the good of the city where I have sent you into exile. Pray to the LORD for it, because if it does well, you will do well too."

8"For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Don't let your prophets and fortune-tellers trick you, and don't listen to the dreams you dream up. 9They are prophesying lies to you in MymyMy name. I didn't send them," declares the LORD.

10"For this is what the LORD says: When Babylon's seventy years are finished, I will take care of you and keep MymyMy good promise to bring you back to this place. 11For I know the plans I have for you," declares the LORD, "plans for your good and not for harm, to give you a future and a hope.

12"Then you will call on MemeMe and come and pray to MemeMe, and I will listen to you. 13You will seek MemeMe and find MemeMe when you search for MemeMe with all your heart.

14"I will be found by you," declares the LORD, "and I will bring you back from captivity. I will gather you from all the nations and places where I scattered you," declares the LORD. "I will bring you back to the place from which I carried you away into exile."

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Those Left Behind

15"Because you may say, 'ThetheThe LORD has raised up prophets for us in Babylon,' 16this is what the LORD says about the king who sits on David's throne and about all the people still in this city—your brothers who didn't go with you into exile: 17'I will send sword, famine, and plague against them, and I will make them like rotten figs that are too bad to eat. 18I will chase them with sword, famine, and plague. I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth—a curse, a ruin, a thing people hiss at, and a disgrace among all the nations where I have scattered them. 19For they haven't listened to MymyMy words,' declares the LORD. 'I sent MymyMy servants the prophets to them again and again, and you exiles haven't listened either,' declares the LORD."

20So now, all you exiles, hear the word of the LORD, which HeheHe sent from Jerusalem to Babylon. 21"This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying to you in MymyMy name: I will hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will put them to death before your eyes. 22Because of them, all the exiles of Judah in Babylon will use this curse: 'May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and Ahab, whom the king of Babylon burned to death in the fire!' 23For they have done a shameful thing in Israel; they have committed adultery with their neighbors' wives and have spoken a lying word in MymyMy name, which I didn't command them to speak. I am the OneoneOne who knows, and I am a witness," declares the LORD.

Shemaiah's False Letter and God's JudgmentJudgementJudgment

24"And you are to tell Shemaiah the Nehelamite," 25"This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Because you sent letters in your own name to all the people in Jerusalem, to the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, and to all the priests, saying, 26'ThetheThe LORD has made you priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to be the chief officer in the house of the LORD, responsible for any crazy person who claims to be a prophet. You are to put him in the stocks and chains. 27So why haven't you rebuked Jeremiah from Anathoth, who prophesies to you? 28For he has sent to us in Babylon, saying, "It will be a long time. Build houses and settle down, and plant gardens and eat their fruit."'"

29Zephaniah the priest read this letter to Jeremiah the prophet.

30Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, saying, 31"Send a message to all the exiles: 'This is what the LORD says: Because Shemaiah the Nehelamite has prophesied to you, even though I didn't send him, and has made you trust in a lie, 32therefore, this is what the LORD says: I am about to punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. None of them will survive among this people, and none will see the good I am about to do for MymyMy people,' declares the LORD, 'because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD.'"
