Tola and Jair Judge Israel

1After Abimelech died, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo, a man from Issachar, rose up to save Israel. He lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim. 2He judged Israel for twenty-three years. Then he died and was buried in Shamir.

3After him, Jair the Gileadite rose up and judged Israel for twenty-two years. 4He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys. They also had thirty towns in the land of Gilead, and those towns are still called Havvoth Jair today. 5Jair died and was buried in Kamon.

Israel Serves False Gods

6The Israelites again did evil in the sight of the the LORD. They served the Baals and the Ashtoreths, along with the gods of Aram, Sidon, Moab, the Ammonites, and the Philistines. They left the the LORD and stopped serving HimhimHim.

7So the the LORD's anger burned against Israel. He sold them into the hands of the Philistines and the Ammonites. 8That year they crushed and oppressed the Israelites for eighteen years. This affected all the Israelites who lived east of the Jordan in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead. 9The Ammonites also crossed the Jordan to fight Judah, Benjamin, and the people of Ephraim. Israel was in great trouble.

The LORD Answers Israel

10The Israelites cried out to the the LORD, saying, "We have sinned against YouyouYou. We have left our God and served the Baals."

11The LORD said to the Israelites, "Did I not save you from the Egyptians, the Amorites, the Ammonites, and the Philistines? 12The Sidonians, the Amalekites, and the Maonites oppressed you, and you cried out to MemeMe. I saved you from their power. 13But you left MemeMe and served other gods. So I will not save you again. 14Go and cry out to the gods you chose. Let them save you in your time of trouble."

15The Israelites said to the the LORD, "We have sinned. Do to us whatever seems good to YouyouYou, but please save us today." 16They got rid of the foreign gods among them and served the the LORD. He could no longer bear Israel's suffering.

Israel Prepares to Fight Ammon

17The Ammonites were called together and camped in Gilead. The Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah. 18The leaders of Gilead said to one another, "Who is the man who will start the fight against the Ammonites? He will be the leader of everyone who lives in Gilead."
