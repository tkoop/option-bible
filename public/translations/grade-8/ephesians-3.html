Paul's Mission to the Gentiles

1Because of this, I, Paul, am a prisoner of Christ Jesus for you Gentiles. 2Surely you have heard about the work God gave me to share HishisHis grace with you. 3God showed me HishisHis secret plan by revealing it to me, just as I wrote briefly before. 4When you read this, you can understand the insight I have into Christ's secret plan. 5In past times, this was not made known to people the way it is now. God has revealed it through the SpiritspiritSpirit to HishisHis holy apostles and prophets. 6The secret plan is this: through the good news, the Gentiles share in God's inheritance, belong to the same body, and share in the promise in Christ Jesus. 7I became a servant of this good news by the gift of God's grace, given to me by the working of HishisHis power. 8I am the least of all God's holy people, yet this grace was given to me to preach to the Gentiles the riches of Christ, which are too great to measure. 9I was also sent to make clear to everyone how this secret plan works. It was hidden in God, who made everything, for ages past. 10Now God's many-sided wisdom is made known through the church to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places. 11This was God's eternal purpose, which HeheHe carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord. 12Through HimhimHim and through faith in HimhimHim, we can come to God freely and with confidence.

13So I ask you not to lose heart because of my troubles for you. They are your glory.

Paul Prays for the Church

14For this reason I bow my knees before the FatherfatherFather, 15from whom every family in Heavenheavenheaven and on EarthEarthearth gets its name. 16I pray that, out of the riches of HishisHis glory, HeheHe will give you strength through HishisHis SpiritspiritSpirit in your inner self. 17I pray that Christ will live in your hearts through faith, and that you will be rooted and grounded in love. 18Then you will be able, with all God's holy people, to understand how wide, long, high, and deep Christ's love is. 19You can know this love that goes beyond knowledge, so that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

Praise to God

20Now to HimhimHim who is able to do far more than all we ask or think, by the power working in us, 21be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus for all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
