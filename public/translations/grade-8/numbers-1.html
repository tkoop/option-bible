God Commands a Census

1On the first day of the second month, in the second year after the Israelites left Egypt, the LORD spoke to Moses in the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting in the Sinai DesertdesertDesert. He said:

2"Take a census of all the Israelites. List them by their clans and families, and write down the name of every male, one by one.

3"You and Aaron must count every man who is twenty years old or older and can serve in Israel's army. Count them by their groups.

4"A man from each tribe will help you. Each one is the leader of his family.

The Men Chosen to Help

5"These are the names of the men who will help you: From Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur; from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai; from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab; from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar; from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon; from the sons of Joseph—from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur; from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni; from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran; from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel; and from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

16These men were chosen from the community. They were the leaders of their tribes and the heads of Israel's family groups.

17So Moses and Aaron took these men, whose names had been given.

18On the first day of the second month, they gathered the whole community and recorded each person's family line. They counted every man who was twenty years old or older, one by one. 19They did this just as the LORD had commanded Moses, and he counted them in the Sinai DesertdesertDesert.

The Census Results

20The descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn son, were counted by their clans and families. Every male twenty years old or older who could serve in the army was counted. 21Reuben's tribe had 46,500 men. 22Simeon's descendants were counted the same way. 23Simeon's tribe had 59,300 men. 24Gad's descendants were counted the same way. 25Gad's tribe had 45,650 men. 26Judah's descendants were counted the same way. 27Judah's tribe had 74,600 men. 28Issachar's descendants were counted the same way. 29Issachar's tribe had 54,400 men. 30Zebulun's descendants were counted the same way. 31Zebulun's tribe had 57,400 men.

32The descendants of Joseph were counted through his sons Ephraim and Manasseh. 33Ephraim's tribe had 40,500 men. 34Manasseh's descendants were counted the same way. 35Manasseh's tribe had 32,200 men. 36Benjamin's descendants were counted the same way. 37Benjamin's tribe had 35,400 men. 38Dan's descendants were counted the same way. 39Dan's tribe had 62,700 men. 40Asher's descendants were counted the same way. 41Asher's tribe had 41,500 men. 42Naphtali's descendants were counted the same way. 43Naphtali's tribe had 53,400 men.

44These were the men Moses and Aaron counted, along with the twelve leaders of Israel. Each leader stood for his family. 45All the Israelites who were twenty years old or older and could serve in Israel's army were counted by their families. 46The total number was 603,550.

The Levites Are Set Apart

47The Levites were not counted with the other tribes.

48The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 49"Do not count the tribe of Levi. Do not include them in the census of the Israelites.

50"Instead, put the Levites in charge of the tabernacle that holds the covenant law and everything that belongs to it. They must carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings. They must serve it and camp around it.

51"When the tabernacle is to move, the Levites must take it down. When it is to be set up, they must put it up. Anyone else who comes near it must be put to death.

52"The Israelites must camp by their groups. Each man must stay in his own camp under his own banner.

53"But the Levites must camp around the tabernacle that holds the covenant law. Then God's anger will not fall on the Israelite community. The Levites are in charge of caring for the tabernacle."

54The Israelites did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
