Workers in the Vineyard

1For the kingdom of Heavenheavenheaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard. 2He agreed to pay them a denarius for the day and sent them into his vineyard.

3About nine o'clock in the morning he went out again and saw others standing idle in the marketplace. 4He said to them, “You go into the vineyard too, and I will pay you what is right.” So they went. 5He went out again about noon and about three in the afternoon and did the same thing.

6About five o'clock in the afternoon he went out and found others standing there. He asked them, “Why have you been standing here all day doing nothing?” 7They said, “Because no one hired us.” He said to them, “You go into the vineyard too.”

8When evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his foreman, “Call the workers and pay them their wages. Begin with the last ones hired and go to the first.”

9The workers who were hired at five o'clock came and each received a denarius. 10When the first workers came, they thought they would get more, but each of them also received a denarius.

11When they received it, they grumbled against the landowner. 12They said, “These last men worked only one hour, and you have made them equal to us. We carried the load and worked in the heat all day.”

13He answered one of them, “Friend, I am not cheating you. Didn't you agree with me for a denarius? 14Take what is yours and go. I want to give this last man the same as you. 15Don't I have the right to do what I want with my own money? Or is your eye jealous because I am generous?”

16So the last will be first, and the first will be last.”
