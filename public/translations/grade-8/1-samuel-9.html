Saul Searches for His Father’s Donkeys

1There was a man from Benjamin named Kish. He was the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah, a Benjamite, and he was a brave and strong man. 2He had a son named Saul, young and good-looking. No one among the Israelites was better looking than he was. From his shoulders up, he was taller than anyone else.

3Now the donkeys of Saul’s father, Kish, were lost. So Kish said to his son Saul, “Take one of the servants with you and go look for the donkeys.”

4So Saul passed through the hill country of Ephraim and through the land of Shalishah, but they did not find them. Then they passed through the land of Shaalim, but the donkeys were not there. Then he passed through the land of Benjamin, but they still did not find them.

5When they came to the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant, “Come, let us return, or my father will stop worrying about the donkeys and start worrying about us.”

6But the servant answered, “Look, there is a man of God in this city. He is respected, and everything he says comes true. Let us go there now. Maybe he can tell us which way to go.”

7Then Saul said to his servant, “If we go, what can we bring the man? The bread in our bags is gone, and we have no gift to bring to the man of God. What do we have?”

8The servant answered Saul again, “Look, I have a quarter shekel of silver. I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way.”

9In Israel in those days, when a man went to ask God something, he would say, “Come, let us go to the seer,” because a prophet was then called a seer.

10Saul said, “Good idea; come, let us go.” So they went to the city where the man of God was.

11As they were going up the hill to the city, they met some young women coming out to draw water. They asked them, “Is the seer here?”

12“Yes,” they answered, “he is just ahead of you. Hurry now, for today he has come to the city because the people have a sacrifice on the high place. 13As soon as you enter the city, you will find him before he goes up to the high place to eat. The people will not eat until he comes, because he must bless the sacrifice. After that, those who are invited will eat. Go up now; today you will find him.”

14So they went up to the city. As they were entering it, there was Samuel coming out to meet them on his way to the high place.

God Tells Samuel About Saul

15Now the the LORD had told Samuel one day before Saul came, 16“Tomorrow at this time I will send you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him ruler over MymyMy people Israel. He will save MymyMy people from the Philistines, for I have looked at MymyMy people, because their cry has reached MemeMe.” 17When Samuel saw Saul, the the LORD said to him, “Here is the man I told you about. He will rule over MymyMy people.”

18Then Saul came up to Samuel at the gate and said, “Please tell me where the seer’s house is.”

19Samuel replied, “I am the seer. Go up ahead of me to the high place, for today you shall eat with me. In the morning I will send you on your way, and I will tell you all that is on your heart. 20As for the donkeys you lost three days ago, do not worry about them, for they have been found. And who does all that Israel wants belong to, if not to you and your father’s house?”

21Saul answered, “Am I not a Benjamite from the smallest tribe of Israel, and is not my clan the least of all the clans of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me like this?”

22Then Samuel took Saul and his servant and brought them into the hall. He gave them a place at the head of the guests. There were about thirty men. 23Samuel said to the cook, “Bring the portion I gave you, the one I told you to set aside.” 24So the cook brought the leg with what was on it and set it before Saul. Then Samuel said, “Here is what was kept for you. Eat, for it was saved for you for this special time, when I said, ‘I have invited the people.’” So Saul ate with Samuel that day.

Samuel Sends Saul Home with God’s Message

25After they came down from the high place into the city, Samuel spoke with Saul on the roof.

26They got up early, and at dawn Samuel called to Saul on the roof, “Get up, so I can send you on your way.” So Saul got up, and both he and Samuel went outside.

27As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, “Tell your servant to go on ahead of us, but you stay here a while, so that I may tell you the word of God.”
