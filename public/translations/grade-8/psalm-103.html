David Praises the LORD

1Bless the LORD, O my soul; with all my heart, bless HishisHis holy name. 2Bless the LORD, O my soul, and don't forget all HishisHis blessings.

3He forgives all your sins; HeheHe heals all your diseases. 4He rescues your life from destruction; HeheHe crowns you with faithful love and compassion. 5He fills your years with good things, so your youth is renewed like the eagle's.

The LORD's Compassion for His People

6The LORD does what is right and fair for all who are treated unfairly. 7He showed Moses HishisHis ways and the people of Israel HishisHis deeds. 8The LORD is kind and gracious, slow to anger, and full of faithful love. 9He will not always accuse, and HeheHe will not hold HishisHis anger forever. 10He has not treated us as our sins deserve, and HeheHe has not paid us back for our wrongs.

11For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is HishisHis faithful love for those who fear HimhimHim. 12As far as the east is from the west, so far has HeheHe removed our wrongdoings from us. 13As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear HimhimHim. 14For HeheHe knows how weak we are; HeheHe remembers that we are dust.

15A person's days are like grass; he blooms like a wildflower. 16When the wind blows over it, it is gone, and its place no longer remembers it. 17But the faithful love of the LORD lasts forever for those who fear HimhimHim, and HishisHis goodness reaches to their children and grandchildren. 18It is for those who keep HishisHis promise and remember to obey HishisHis commands.

All Creation Praises the LORD

19The LORD has set up HishisHis throne in Heavenheavenheaven, and HishisHis kingdom rules over all. 20Bless the LORD, you HishisHis angels, mighty in strength, who do what HeheHe says and listen to HishisHis word. 21Bless the LORD, all you HishisHis heavenly armies, HishisHis servants who do HishisHis will. 22Bless the LORD, all HishisHis works, in every place HeheHe rules. Bless the LORD, O my soul.
