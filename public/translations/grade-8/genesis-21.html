Isaac Is Born

1The LORD visited Sarah, just as HeheHe had promised, and the LORD did for her what HeheHe had said. 2So Sarah became pregnant and had a son for Abraham in his old age. It happened at the exact time God had promised. 3Abraham named his son Isaac. 4When Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him, just as God had commanded him. 5Abraham was one hundred years old when Isaac was born. 6Sarah said, "God has made me laugh, and everyone who hears about this will laugh with me." 7She also said, "Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet I have had a son for him in his old age."

Hagar and Ishmael Are Sent Away

8The child grew and was weaned. On the day Isaac was weaned, Abraham held a great feast. 9But Sarah saw that the son Hagar the Egyptian had borne to Abraham was mocking. 10So she said to Abraham, "Send away this servant woman and her son, because the son of this servant woman will not share the inheritance with my son Isaac." 11This matter deeply troubled Abraham because it concerned his son.

12But God said to Abraham, "Do not be upset about the boy and your servant woman. Listen to everything Sarah tells you, because your descendants will come through Isaac. 13I will also make the son of the servant woman into a nation, because he is your offspring."

14Early the next morning Abraham took some food and a leather bag of water and gave them to Hagar. He placed them on her shoulders, along with the boy, and sent her away. She left and wandered in the DesertdesertDesert of Beersheba. 15When the water in the bag was gone, she put the boy under a bush. 16Then she went and sat down a good distance away, about as far as a bow can shoot, because she thought, "I cannot watch the boy die." As she sat there, she began to cry.

17God heard the boy crying, and the angel of God called to Hagar from Heavenheavenheaven and said to her, "What is wrong, Hagar? Do not be afraid, because God has heard the boy's cry where he is. 18Get up, lift the boy, and hold him by the hand, because I will make him into a great nation." 19Then God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water. She went and filled the bag with water and gave the boy a drink.

20God was with the boy as he grew. He lived in the desert and became an archer. 21While he lived in the DesertdesertDesert of Paran, his mother chose a wife for him from Egypt.

Abraham's Treaty with Abimelech

22At that time Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, said to Abraham, "God is with you in everything you do. 23Now swear to me here before God that you will not deal falsely with me or my children or my descendants. Show the same kindness to me and to the land where you are living as a foreigner that I have shown to you."

24Abraham said, "I swear it."

25Then Abraham complained to Abimelech about a well of water that Abimelech's servants had seized. 26Abimelech said, "I did not know who did this. You did not tell me, and I heard about it only today."

27So Abraham brought sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech, and the two men made a treaty. 28Abraham set apart seven female lambs from the flock.

29Abimelech asked Abraham, "What is the meaning of these seven female lambs you have set apart?"

30He replied, "Accept these seven lambs from my hand as proof that I dug this well."

31So that place was called Beersheba, because the two of them swore an oath there. 32After they made the treaty at Beersheba, Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, returned to the land of the Philistines. 33Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beersheba, and there he called on the name of the LORD, the Eternal God. 34Abraham stayed in the land of the Philistines for a long time.
