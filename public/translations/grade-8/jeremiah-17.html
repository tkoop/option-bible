Judah's Sin Is Deeply Engraved

1Judah's sin is written with an iron pen and a diamond tip. It is carved into the tablet of their heart and onto the horns of your altars. 2Their children remember their altars and their sacred poles beside green trees on the high hills, on the mountains in the fields. 3O MymyMy mountain in the countryside, I will give your wealth and all your treasures to be looted, because of the sin of your high places in all your borders. 4You will lose, yes, lose, the inheritance I gave you. I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know, for you have lit a fire in MymyMy anger; it will burn forever.

Trusting People or Trusting the LORD

5This is what the the LORD says: "Cursed is the person who trusts in people and depends on human strength, whose heart turns away from the the LORD. 6He will be like a shrub in the desert. He will not see good when it comes. He will live in dry places in the wasteland, in a salty land where no one lives."

7"Blessed is the person who trusts in the the LORD, whose confidence is in HimhimHim. 8He will be like a tree planted by water, sending out its roots by a stream. It will not fear when heat comes; its leaves will stay green. It will not worry in a dry year, and it will not stop bearing fruit."

9The heart is deceitful above all things and beyond cure. Who can understand it? 10I, the the LORD, search the heart and test the mind, to give each person what his ways deserve, according to the fruit of his deeds. 11Like a bird that hatches eggs it did not lay is the person who gets rich unfairly. In the middle of his life it will leave him, and in the end he will be a fool. 12A glorious throne, exalted from the beginning, is the place of our sanctuary. 13O the LORD, the hope of Israel, all who leave YouyouYou will be put to shame. Those who turn away from YouyouYou will be written in the dust, because they have forsaken the the LORD, the spring of living water.

Jeremiah Prays for Help

14Heal me, O the LORD, and I will be healed; save me, and I will be saved, for YouyouYou are my praise. 15Look, they keep saying to me, "Where is the word of the the LORD? Let it come now!" 16But I have not run away from being a shepherd after YouyouYou, and I have not wanted the day of incurable pain. You know; what came from my lips was right before YouyouYou. 17Do not be a terror to me; YouyouYou are my refuge in the day of disaster. 18Let my persecutors be put to shame, but do not let me be put to shame. Let them be dismayed, but do not let me be dismayed. Bring on them the day of disaster; destroy them with double destruction!

Keep the Sabbath Day Holy

19This is what the the LORD said to me: "Go and stand at the gate used by the people, through which the kings of Judah enter and leave, and at all the gates of Jerusalem. 20Say to them: 'Hear the word of the the LORD, you kings of Judah, all Judah, and all the people of Jerusalem who enter through these gates.'"

21This is what the the LORD says: "Be careful not to carry a load or bring it through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. 22Do not carry a load out of your houses on the Sabbath day or do any work, but keep the Sabbath day holy, as I commanded your fathers. 23Yet they did not listen or pay attention, but they stiffened their necks and would not listen or accept discipline."

24"If you listen to MemeMe," declares the the LORD, "and do not bring a load through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day, and if you keep the Sabbath day holy by doing no work on it, 25then kings and princes who sit on David's throne will enter through the gates of this city. They will come with their officials, riding in chariots and on horses, along with the men of Judah and those living in Jerusalem, and this city will be inhabited forever. 26People will come from the cities of Judah and from the places around Jerusalem, from the land of Benjamin, from the lowlands, from the hill country, and from the Negev. They will bring burnt offerings and sacrifices, grain offerings and frankincense, and thank offerings to the house of the the LORD. 27But if you do not listen to MemeMe to keep the Sabbath day holy, and not to carry a load and enter the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, then I will set a fire in its gates that will burn the palaces of Jerusalem and not be put out."
