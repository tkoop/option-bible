A Prayer for Mercy

For the choirmaster, with stringed instruments, according to Sheminith. A psalm of David.

1The LORD, do not rebuke me in YouryourYour anger, and do not discipline me in YouryourYour wrath. 2Be gracious to me, the LORD, for I am weak; heal me, the LORD, for my bones ache. 3My soul is deeply troubled. How long, the LORD? How long? 4Turn, the LORD, and rescue me; save me because of YouryourYour faithful love.

5For there is no memory of YouyouYou in death; who can praise YouyouYou from the grave? 6I am worn out from groaning; all night I soak my bed with tears; I drench my couch with weeping. 7My eyes grow dim with grief; they waste away because of all my foes.

Confidence in the LORD's Answer

8Get away from me, all you who do evil, for the LORD has heard my crying. 9The LORD has heard my plea; the LORD accepts my prayer. 10All my enemies will be ashamed and terrified; they will turn back in sudden shame.
