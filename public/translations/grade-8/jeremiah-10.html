The LORD Warns Israel About Idols

1Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, O house of Israel. 2This is what the LORD says: "Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by signs in the heavens, though the nations are terrified by them. 3For the customs of the peoples are worthless; they cut a tree out of the forest, and a craftsman shapes it with his chisel. 4They adorn it with silver and gold; they fasten it with hammer and nails so it will not topple. 5Like a scarecrow in a cucumber field, their idols cannot speak; they must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no harm, nor can they do any good."

6No one is like YouyouYou, O the LORD; YouyouYou are great, and YouryourYour name is mighty in power. 7Who would not fear YouyouYou, O KingkingKing of the nations? For this is YouryourYour due. Among all the wise leaders of the nations and in all their kingdoms, there is no one like YouyouYou. 8They are altogether senseless and foolish; instruction from idols is wood! 9Hammered silver is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz. The work of the craftsman and of the goldsmith is clothed in blue and purple—all the work of skilled men. 10But the LORD is the true God; HeheHe is the living God and eternal King. The EarthEarthearth trembles at HishisHis wrath, and the nations cannot endure HishisHis anger. 11Tell them this: 'These gods, who did not make the heavens and the earth, will perish from the earth and from under the heavens.'

The LORD Made Everything

12He made the earth by HishisHis power; HeheHe established the world by HishisHis wisdom and stretched out the heavens by HishisHis understanding. 13When HeheHe thunders, the waters in the heavens roar; HeheHe causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He sends lightning with the rain and brings the wind from HishisHis storehouses. 14Every man is senseless and without knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idols. For his molten images are a fraud, and there is no breath in them. 15They are worthless, a work of mockery. In the time of their punishment they will perish. 16The Portion of Jacob is not like these, for HeheHe is the MakermakerMaker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of HishisHis inheritance. the LORD of Hosts is HishisHis name.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Is Coming on Judah

17Gather up your belongings from the ground, O daughter living under siege. 18For this is what the LORD says: "Look, I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this time, and I will bring distress on them so that they may be captured." 19Woe to me because of my injury; my wound is painful. Yet I said, 'This is my sickness, and I must bear it.' 20MymyMy tent is destroyed, and all my ropes are snapped. My children have left me and are no more; no one is left to pitch my tent or to set up my curtains. 21For the shepherds are senseless and do not seek the LORD; therefore they do not prosper, and all their flock is scattered. 22Listen! The sound of a report is coming—a great commotion from the land of the north to make the cities of Judah a wasteland, a haunt of jackals.

Jeremiah Prays to the LORD

23I know, O the LORD, that a man's way is not his own; no one who walks directs his own steps. 24Correct me, O the LORD, but only with justice—not in YouryourYour anger, or YouyouYou will bring me to nothing. 25Pour out YouryourYour wrath on the nations that do not acknowledge YouyouYou, and on the families that do not call on YouryourYour name. For they have devoured Jacob; they have devoured him and consumed him, and they have laid waste his homeland.
