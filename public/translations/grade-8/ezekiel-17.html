A Riddle for Israel

1The word of the the LORD came to me: 2"Son of man, give the people of Israel a riddle and tell them a story. 3Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: A great eagle with big wings and long wing feathers, covered with many colourfulcolourfulcolorful feathers, came to Lebanon and took the top of a cedar tree. 4He broke off its highest young twigs and carried them to a trading country. He set them in a city of merchants. 5Then he took some seed from that land and planted it in fertile soil. He placed it by plenty of water and set it out like a willow branch. 6It grew and became a low, spreading vine. Its branches reached toward him, and its roots stayed where they were. So it became a vine and grew branches and sent out shoots. 7But there was another great eagle with big wings and many feathers. This vine bent its roots toward him and sent its branches toward him from the garden bed where it was planted, so that eagle could water it. 8It had been planted in good soil by plenty of water so it could grow branches, bear fruit, and become a fine vine.' 9Say, 'This is what the Lord the LORD says: Will it thrive? Won't he pull up its roots and cut off its fruit so it withers? All its fresh new leaves will dry up. It won't need a strong arm or many people to pull it up by the roots. 10Look, it is planted. Will it thrive? Won't it completely wither when the east wind hits it? It will wither in the beds where it sprouted.'"

The Meaning of the Riddle

12"Say to this rebellious people, 'Don't you know what these things mean? Tell them: The king of Babylon came to Jerusalem. He took its king and its leaders and brought them back to Babylon. 13He took one of the royal family and made a covenant with him. He made him swear an oath. He also took away the leading men of the land, 14so the kingdom would be humble and not try to rise up, and so it would keep his covenant and stand. 15But he rebelled against the king of Babylon by sending his messengers to Egypt to get horses and a large army. Will he succeed? Can a man who does such things escape? Can he break the covenant and still escape? 16As surely as I live, declares the Lord the LORD, "he will die in Babylon, in the city of the king who made him king. He looked down on that king's oath and broke his covenant." 17Pharaoh, with his mighty army and large force, will not help him in war when siege ramps are built and walls are raised to kill many people. 18He looked down on the oath and broke the covenant. He made a pledge, yet he did all these things. So he will not escape. 19So this is what the Lord the LORD says: "As surely as I live, I will bring on his head the oath he looked down on and the covenant he broke. 20I will spread MymyMy net over him, and he will be caught in MymyMy trap.
