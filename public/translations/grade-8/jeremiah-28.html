Hananiah's False Prophecy

1In the fourth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, at the start of his rule, in the fifth month, Hananiah son of Azur, a prophet from Gibeon, spoke to me in the house of the the LORD. He spoke before the priests and all the people. He said:

2"This is what the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon. 3Within two years, I will bring back to this place all the items from the house of the the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took from here and carried to Babylon. 4I will also bring back to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and all the people of Judah who were taken to Babylon,' says the the LORD. 'I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.'"

Jeremiah Answers Hananiah

5Then the prophet Jeremiah spoke to the prophet Hananiah before the priests and all the people standing in the house of the the LORD.

6Jeremiah the prophet said, "AmenamenAmen! May the the LORD do that. May the the LORD make your words come true. May HeheHe bring back the items from the house of the the LORD and all the captives from Babylon to this place. 7But listen to this word that I am speaking to you and to all the people. 8The prophets who lived long ago, before us, prophesied against many countries and great kingdoms. They spoke about war, disaster, and disease. 9A prophet who promises peace proves he was truly sent by the the LORD only when his words come true."

Hananiah Breaks the Yoke

10Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke off Jeremiah's neck and broke it.

11Hananiah spoke before all the people. He said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'In the same way, within two years, I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the necks of all the nations.'" Then the prophet Jeremiah went on his way.

The LORD's JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Hananiah

12After Hananiah the prophet broke the yoke from Jeremiah's neck, the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah.

13The the LORD said to Jeremiah, "Go and tell Hananiah, 'This is what the the LORD says: You broke wooden yokes, but now you must make yokes of iron.'"

14"For this is what the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'I have put an iron yoke on the necks of all these nations, so they will serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. They will serve him. I have even given him the wild animals.'"

15Then the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, "Listen, Hananiah. The the LORD has not sent you. You have made these people trust in a lie. 16So this is what the the LORD says: 'Look, I am removing you from the earth. You will die this year, because you have led people to rebel against the the LORD.'"

17So Hananiah the prophet died that same year, in the seventh month.
