David Sends Comfort to Hanun

1After this, Nahash, king of the Ammonites, died, and his son reigned in his place. 2David said, "I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, because his father showed kindness to me." So David sent messengers to comfort him over his father's death. His servants arrived in the land of the Ammonites to comfort Hanun.

3Then the princes of the Ammonites said to Hanun, "Do you think David is honouringhonouringhonoring your father by sending comforters to you? Have his servants not come to you to explore the land, spy it out, and overthrow it?"

4So Hanun seized David's servants, shaved them, cut their garments in half at the hips, and sent them away. 5When someone told David what had happened, he sent messengers to meet the men, because they were deeply ashamed. The king said, "Stay in Jericho until your beards grow back, then return."

The Ammonites Prepare for War

6When the Ammonites realizedrealisedrealized they had made themselves hateful to David, Hanun and the Ammonites sent 1,000 talents of silver to hire chariots and horsemen from Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah, and Zobah. 7They hired 32,000 chariots and the king of Maacah with his army. These forces came and camped near Medeba. The Ammonites also came out from their cities and gathered for battle.

Joab Leads Israel's Army

8When David heard about this, he sent Joab with the whole army of mighty men. 9The Ammonites came out and lined up for battle at the entrance to their city, while the kings who had come stayed by themselves in the open country.

10Joab saw that the battle was set against him both in front and behind, so he chose some of Israel's best fighters and lined them up against the Arameans. 11He put the rest of the troops under the command of his brother Abishai, and they lined up against the Ammonites.

12Joab said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, then you must come to my rescue; and if the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will come to your rescue. 13Be strong! Let us fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. Then the the LORD will do what is good in HishisHis eyes."

14Joab and the troops with him advanced to fight the Arameans, and they fled before him. 15When the Ammonites saw that the Arameans had fled, they too fled from Joab's brother Abishai and went back into the city. Then Joab returned to Jerusalem.

David Defeats the Arameans

16When the Arameans saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they sent messengers and brought out the Arameans who were beyond the Euphrates. Shophach, commander of Hadadezer's army, led them. 17When David was told, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, and came to them. David lined up his men for battle against them, and they fought.

18The Arameans fled from Israel. David killed 7,000 charioteers and 40,000 foot soldiers of the Arameans, and he killed Shophach, the commander of the army. 19When Hadadezer's servants saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they made peace with David and became his subjects. The Arameans were no longer willing to help the Ammonites.
