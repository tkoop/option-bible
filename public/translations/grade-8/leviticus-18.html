1The LORD spoke to Moses and said,

2"Speak to the Israelites and tell them: I am the LORD your God. 3You must not live like the people in Egypt, where you used to live, and you must not live like the people in Canaan, where I am bringing you. Forbidden Sexual Relations

6"No one may have sexual relations with a close relative. I am the LORD.

7"You must not have sexual relations with your mother; she is your mother. You must not have sexual relations with her. 8You must not have sexual relations with your father's wife; that would dishonourdishonourdishonor your father. 9You must not have sexual relations with your sister, whether she is your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, born at home or born somewhere else.

14"You must not have sexual relations with your father's brother's wife; you must not go near her. Warnings for the Whole Land
