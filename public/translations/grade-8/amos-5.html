A Lament for Israel

1Listen to this message I speak against you, people of Israel. It is a sad song: 2Virgin Israel has fallen and will never rise again. She lies abandoned in her own land, and no one will help her up. 3For this is what the Lord, the LORD, says: "The city that sent out a thousand soldiers will have only a hundred left. The city that sent out a hundred will have only ten left for the people of Israel."

Seek the the LORD and Live

4For this is what the LORD says to the people of Israel: "Seek MemeMe, and you will live." 5Do not seek help at Bethel. Do not go to Gilgal. Do not travel to Beersheba. Gilgal will surely be taken away, and Bethel will become worthless. 6Seek the the LORD, and you will live. Otherwise, HeheHe may sweep through like fire against the family of Joseph and burn it up, and no one in Bethel will be able to put it out.

7You turn justice into bitterness and throw righteousness to the ground. 8He made the Pleiades and Orion. He turns the deepest darkness into morning and makes day dark as night. He calls the waters of the sea and pours them out over the earth. The LORD is HishisHis name. 9He brings sudden destruction on the strong, so that their strongholds are destroyed.

10They hate anyone who corrects them at the city gate, and they despise anyone who speaks honestly. 11Therefore, because you crush the poor and take grain taxes from them, you have built stone houses but will not live in them. You have planted beautiful vineyards but will not drink their wine. 12For I know how many wrongs you have done and how great your sins are. You crush the righteous, take bribes, and push the needy aside in the courts. 13So the wise person stays quiet at such a time, because these are evil days.

14Seek good, not evil, so that you may live. Then the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, just as you have claimed. 15Hate evil and love good. Make justice fair in the courts. Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will show mercy to the people of Joseph who are left.

16Therefore, this is what the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says: "There will be crying in every square, and in every street people will cry, 'How terrible! How terrible!' They will call the farmers to mourn, and those who know how to sing funeral songs will wail. 17There will be crying in all the vineyards too, because I will pass through your land," says the the LORD.

The Day of the the LORD

18How terrible for you who long for the day of the the LORD! Why do you want that day? It will be darkness, not light. 19It will be like a man running from a lion, only to meet a bear; or like someone going home, leaning his hand on the wall, and getting bitten by a snake. 20The day of the the LORD will be dark, not light, with no brightness in it.

God Rejects Empty Worship

21"I hate and reject your festivals. I do not enjoy your sacred gatherings."

22"Even if you bring MemeMe burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. I will not even look at your fellowship offerings from your fattened animals." 23"Take away the noise of your songs from MemeMe. I will not listen to the music of your harps."

24Instead, let justice flow like a river, and let righteousness flow like a never-ending stream.

25"Did you bring MemeMe sacrifices and offerings during the forty years in the wilderness, people of Israel? 26You will carry Sikkuth, your king, and Chiun, your star god, along with the idols you made for yourselves. 27I will send you into exile beyond Damascus," says the the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts.
