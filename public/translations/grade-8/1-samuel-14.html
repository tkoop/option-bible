Saul's Rash Oath

24Now the men of Israel were worn out that day, because Saul had made the people take an oath: "Cursed is the man who eats food before evening, before I have taken revenge on my enemies." So no one ate. 25The whole army entered the forest, and there was honey on the ground. 26When the army entered the forest, they saw honey dripping, but no one put his hand to his mouth, because they feared the oath. 27But Jonathan had not heard his father make the people swear. Saul's Kingdom and Family

46Then Saul stopped pursuing the Philistines, and the Philistines went to their own place. 47After Saul had secured his kingdom over Israel, he fought against all his enemies on every side: Moab, the Ammonites, Edom, the kings of Zobah, and the Philistines. Wherever he turned, he defeated them. 48He fought bravely and defeated the Amalekites, saving Israel from those who had plundered them. 49Saul's sons were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malchishua. His older daughter was named Merab, and his younger daughter was named Michal. 50Saul's wife was Ahinoam daughter of Ahimaaz. The commander of his army was Abner son of Ner, Saul's uncle. 51Kish was the father of Saul, and Ner was the father of Abner and the son of Abiel. 52There was hard fighting against the Philistines all the days of Saul. Whenever Saul saw a mighty man or any brave man, he took him into his service.
