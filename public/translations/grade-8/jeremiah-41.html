Gedaliah Is Murdered

1In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, who was from the royal family and one of the king’s top officers, came to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. He brought ten men with him. While they were eating together there, 2Ishmael and the ten men with him got up and struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, with a sword. They killed the man the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land. 3Ishmael also killed all the Jews who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah, along with the Chaldean soldiers who were there.

Ishmael Attacks Travelers

4The day after Gedaliah was killed, before anyone knew about it, 5eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria. Their beards were shaved, their clothes were torn, and they had cut themselves. They were bringing grain offerings and incense to the house of the the LORD.

6Ishmael son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them, crying as he walked. When he met them, he said, “Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam.”

7When they came into the city, Ishmael and his men killed them and threw their bodies into a cistern.

8But ten of the men said to Ishmael, “Don’t kill us! We have hidden treasures in the field—wheat, barley, oil, and honey.” So he stopped and did not kill them with the rest.

9The cistern where Ishmael threw all the bodies of the men he had killed, including those killed with Gedaliah, was the one King Asa had made because he feared Baasha king of Israel. Ishmael filled it with the dead. 10Then Ishmael took captive all the rest of the people in Mizpah—the king’s daughters and all the people left there. Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, had placed them under Gedaliah son of Ahikam. Ishmael took them captive and started toward the Ammonites.

Johanan Rescues the Captives

11But when Johanan son of Kareah and all the army commanders with him heard about all the evil Ishmael son of Nethaniah had done, 12they took all their men and went to fight Ishmael. They caught up with him at the large pool in Gibeon. 13When all the people with Ishmael saw Johanan and the commanders with him, they were glad. 14So all the people Ishmael had taken captive from Mizpah turned and went over to Johanan. 15But Ishmael escaped from Johanan with eight men and went to the Ammonites.

The Survivors Head for Egypt

16Then Johanan and all the commanders with him took all the survivors from Mizpah whom he had rescued from Ishmael after Ishmael had struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam. They included soldiers, women, children, and eunuchs whom he brought back from Gibeon. 17They set out and stayed at Geruth Chimham near Bethlehem, planning to go to Egypt 18because they were afraid of the Chaldeans. Ishmael had struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had appointed over the land.
