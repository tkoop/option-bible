A Cry for Help in Trouble

1Why do YouyouYou stand so far away, the LORD? Why do YouyouYou hide when trouble comes? 2The wicked proudly hunt down the poor. Let them be caught in the traps they set. 3The wicked brag about what they want. They praise greedy people and insult the the LORD. 4In his pride, the wicked man does not seek God. All his plans say, "There is no God." 5He always seems to get his way. Your judgmentsjudgementsjudgments are far from his mind. He sneers at everyone who opposes him.

6He says to himself, "I will never be shaken. I will never face trouble, not for generations."

7His mouth is full of curses, lies, and threats. Trouble and evil hide under his tongue. 8He hides near villages and waits. In secret places he kills innocent people. His eyes watch for the helpless. 9He lurks in secret like a lion in its den. He waits to catch the poor and drags them into his net. 10The helpless are crushed and beaten down. They fall under his power.

11He says to himself, "God has forgotten. He has hidden HishisHis face. He will never notice."

12Arise, the LORD! Take action, O God! Do not forget those who are hurting! 13Why does the wicked man insult God? He tells himself, "YouyouYou will not hold me accountable."

14But YouyouYou have seen the trouble and grief. You take it into YouryourYour own hands. The victim trusts YouyouYou. You have been the helper of orphans. 15Break the power of the wicked and those who do evil. Search out his wrongdoing until none is left.

16The LORD is KingkingKing forever and ever. The nations will perish from HishisHis land. 17You have heard the longing of those who are hurting. You will strengthen their hearts. You will listen closely. 18You will bring justice to orphans and those who are crushed, so that mere mortals will no longer terrify them.
