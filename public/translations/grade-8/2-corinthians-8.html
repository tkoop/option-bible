The Macedonians Give Generously

1Now, brothers, we want you to know about the grace God has given to the churches in Macedonia. 2In the middle of a very hard test, their overflowing joy and their extreme poverty produced rich generosity. 3I can say they gave as much as they could, and even more than they could. 4They begged us again and again for the chance to share in this service to God's people. 5They did more than we expected. They gave themselves first to the Lord, and then to us, because that was God's will.

Finish What You Started

6So we urged Titus to finish this act of grace among you, just as he had started it. 7You already do well in everything—in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in full dedication, and in the love we stirred up in you. So make sure you also do well in this grace of giving. 8I am not giving an order. I want to test how real your love is by comparing it with the dedication of others. 9For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. He was rich, but for your sake HeheHe became poor, so that through HishisHis poverty you might become rich.

10Here is my advice. It is best for you to finish this. You were the first to start giving last year, and you were the first to want to do it. 11Now finish the work, so your eager willingness may be matched by your finishing it, using what you have. 12If the willingness is there, the gift is accepted based on what a person has, not on what they do not have.

13I do not want others to be relieved while you are hard pressed. I want things to be fair. 14Right now your plenty can supply what they need, so that later their plenty can supply what you need. The goal is fairness. 15As it is written, "ThetheThe one who gathered much did not have too much, and the one who gathered little did not have too little."

Titus and His Helpers

16Thanks be to God, who put the same dedicated care for you into Titus's heart. 17He accepted our request, and because he was already eager, he went to you on his own. 18We are sending with him the brother who is praised by all the churches for his work in spreading the Good News. 19And that is not all. He was also chosen by the churches to travel with us as we carry this gift, which we are handling for the glory of the Lord and to show our eagerness.

20We are taking care with this so that no one can blame us for how we handle this generous gift. 21We want to do what is right, not only before the Lord but also before people. 22We are sending with them our brother, whom we have often found to be eager in many things, and who is now even more eager because of his confidence in you.

23As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker among you. As for our brothers, they are representatives of the churches and an honourhonourhonor to Christ. 24So show them, in front of the churches, proof of your love and of the pride we have in you.
