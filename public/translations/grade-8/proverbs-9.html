Wisdom Invites the Simple

1Wisdom has built her house and carved out seven pillars. 2She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine, and she has set her table. 3She has sent out her young women, and she calls from the highest points of the city.

4"Whoever is simple, come in here!" she says to him who lacks understanding. 5"Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed. 6Leave your foolish ways and live, and walk in the path of understanding."

Wisdom and Correction

7Anyone who corrects a mocker brings shame on himself, and anyone who rebukes a wicked person gets a stain. 8Do not correct a mocker, or he will hate you; correct a wise person, and he will love you. 9Teach a wise person, and he will become wiser; let a righteous person know, and he will add to his learning.

The Fear of the LORD

10The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne is understanding. 11For by me your days will be multiplied, and years of life will be added to you. 12If you are wise, you are wise for yourself, and if you scoff, you will bear it alone.

Folly's Deadly Invitation

13A foolish woman is loud; she is simple and knows nothing. 14She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the city, 15to call to those who pass by, who go straight on their ways.

16"Whoever is simple, come in here!" she says to him who lacks understanding. 17"Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant."

18But he does not know that the dead are there, that her guests are in the depths of the grave.
