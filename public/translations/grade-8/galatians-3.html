Paul Rebukes the Galatians

1You foolish Galatians! Who has put a spell on you? Jesus Christ was shown to you as crucified, right before your eyes. 2I want to know just one thing from you: Did you receive the SpiritspiritSpirit by doing what the law says, or by hearing and believing? 3Are you so foolish? You started with the SpiritspiritSpirit. Are you now trying to finish by your own human effort? 4Have you suffered so much for nothing? I hope it was not all for nothing. 5Does the OneoneOne who gives you the SpiritspiritSpirit and works miracles among you do this because you follow the law, or because you heard and believed?

Abraham and the Curse of the Law

6Abraham believed God, and God counted that as righteousness. 7So you should know that people who have faith are Abraham's children. 8The ScripturescriptureScripture saw ahead that God would make the Gentiles right with HimhimHim through faith. So it gave Abraham the good news ahead of time: "All nations will be blessed through you." 9So people who have faith are blessed along with Abraham, the faithful man.

10All who depend on obeying the law are under a curse. Scripture says, "Cursed is everyone who does not keep doing everything written in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw." 11It is clear that no one is made right with God by the law, because "ThetheThe righteous will live by faith." 12The law does not rest on faith. Instead, "ThetheThe person who does these things will live by them." 13Christ bought us back from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us. Scripture says, "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree." 14He did this so that the blessing Abraham received would come to the Gentiles through Christ Jesus, and so that we could receive the promised SpiritspiritSpirit by faith.

The Law and God's Promise

15Brothers, I am using a human example. Even a person's agreement, once it is approved, cannot be cancelledcancelledcanceled or added to by anyone. 16The promises were given to Abraham and to his descendant. Scripture does not say "and to descendants," as if many. It says "and to your descendant," meaning one person, and that person is Christ.

17Here is what I mean. The law came 430 years later. It does not cancel the agreement God had already approved, and it does not make the promise useless. 18If the inheritance comes from the law, then it does not come from a promise. But God gave it to Abraham through a promise.

19So why was the law given? It was added because of people's sins, until the descendant came to whom the promise was made. The law was given through angels and put into effect by a go-between. 20A go-between means there are at least two sides. But God is one. 21So is the law against God's promises? Not at all! If a law had been given that could give life, then people would be made right with God by following the law. 22But ScripturescriptureScripture has locked up everything under sin. It did this so that the promise, which comes through faith in Jesus Christ, could be given to those who believe.

Children of God Through Faith

23Before faith came, we were kept under guard by the law. We were locked up until faith was revealed. 24So the law was like a guardian for us until Christ came. Its purpose was to lead us to be made right with God by faith. 25But now that faith has come, we are no longer under that guardian.

26For through faith, you are all children of God in Christ Jesus. 27For all of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ. 28There is no Jew or Greek, no slave or free person, no male or female. You are all one in Christ Jesus. 29And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's descendants and heirs according to the promise.
