Jehoshaphat Returns and Is Warned

1Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, returned safely to his home in Jerusalem. 2Jehu son of Hanani, a prophet, went out to meet him. He said to King Jehoshaphat, "Should you help wicked people and love those who hate the the LORD? Because of this, the the LORD is angry with you. 3Even so, good things are found in you. You removed the Asherah poles from the land, and you were determined to seek God."

Jehoshaphat Brings the People Back to God

4Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. He went out among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and led them back to the the LORD, the God of their fathers.

Judges Are Appointed in Judah

5He appointed judges throughout the land in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city. 6He said to the judges, "Think about what you are doing. You are not judging for people. You are judging for the the LORD. He is with you when you make decisions. 7So let the fear of the the LORD be on you. Be careful in what you do, because the the LORD our God does not do wrong. He does not play favorites, and HeheHe does not take bribes."

A Court System Is Set Up in Jerusalem

8In Jerusalem, Jehoshaphat also appointed some Levites, priests, and family leaders of Israel to handle the the LORD's cases and settle arguments. They returned to Jerusalem. 9He told them, "Do this in the fear of the the LORD, faithfully, and with your whole heart. 10When a case comes to you from your brothers who live in their cities—whether it is about bloodshed, law, commands, rules, or decisions—warn them so they do not become guilty before the the LORD. If you do not warn them, HishisHis anger will come on you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not be guilty. 11Amariah the chief priest is in charge of all matters about the the LORD. Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the leader of Judah, is in charge of all the king's matters. The Levites will serve as officials before you. Be brave, and may the the LORD be with those who do good."
