David Sends Comfort to Hanun

1After this, the king of the Ammonites died, and his son Hanun became king in his place. 2David said, "I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, just as his father showed kindness to me." So David sent his servants to comfort Hanun after his father's death. David's servants came to the land of the Ammonites.

The Ammonites Insult David's Men

3The Ammonite leaders said to Hanun, their lord, "Do you think David is honouringhonouringhonoring your father by sending men to comfort you? Hasn't David sent his servants to you to look over the city, spy on it, and destroy it?"

4So Hanun took David's servants, shaved off half their beards, cut their clothes in half at the buttocks, and sent them away. 5When David heard about it, he sent men to meet them, because they were very ashamed. The king said, "Stay at Jericho until your beards grow, then come back."

Ammon Hires Aramean Armies

6When the Ammonites saw they had made David angry, they hired twenty thousand Aramean foot soldiers from Beth-rehob and Zobah, one thousand men from the king of Maacah, and twelve thousand men from Tob. 7When David heard about it, he sent Joab and all his best soldiers.

Joab Faces Two Armies

8The Ammonites came out and lined up for battle at the city gate. The Arameans from Zobah and Rehob, along with the men from Tob and Maacah, stood by themselves in the field. 9Joab saw that he was being attacked from the front and the back. He chose the best soldiers in Israel and lined them up against the Arameans. 10He put the rest of the army under the command of his brother Abishai and lined them up against the Ammonites.

11Joab said, "If the Arameans are too strong for me, you will help me. If the Ammonites are too strong for you, I will go help you. 12Be strong. Let us be strong for our people and for the cities of our God. May the the LORD do what seems good to HimhimHim."

The Enemy Armies Flee

13Joab and his men moved forward to fight the Arameans, and the Arameans ran away from him. 14When the Ammonites saw the Arameans running, they also ran away from Abishai and went back into the city. Then Joab returned from the Ammonites and went to Jerusalem.

Aram Gathers Again

15When the Arameans saw they had been beaten by Israel, they gathered together. 16Hadadezer brought in Arameans from beyond the Euphrates River, and they came to Helam. Shobach, the commander of Hadadezer's army, led them.

David Defeats Aram

17When David was told about it, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan River, and came to Helam. The Arameans lined up to face David and fought against him. 18The Arameans ran away from Israel. David killed seven hundred chariot drivers and forty thousand horsemen. He also struck down Shobach, their army commander, and Shobach died there.

19When all the kings who served Hadadezer saw they had been beaten by Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them. The Arameans were afraid to help the Ammonites again.
