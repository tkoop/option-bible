Josiah Renews the Covenant

1Then the king sent for them, and they gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem to him. 2The king went up to the house of the LORD, along with all the men of Judah, all the people of Jerusalem, the priests, and the prophets—everyone, from the least to the greatest. He read to them, in their hearing, all the words of the BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant that had been found in the house of the LORD. 3The king stood by the pillar and made a covenant before the LORD. He promised to follow the LORD and to keep HishisHis commandments, rules, and laws with all his heart and all his soul. He also promised to carry out the words of this covenant written in this book. All the people agreed to the covenant.

Josiah Removes Idol Worship

4Then the king ordered Hilkiah the high priest, the priests of the second rank, and the doorkeepers to bring out of the LORD's temple all the items made for Baal, Asherah, and all the stars of Heavenheavenheaven. He burned them outside Jerusalem in the fields of Kidron and took their ashes to Bethel. 5He also got rid of the pagan priests the kings of Judah had appointed to burn incense on the high places in Judah's cities and around Jerusalem. These priests had burned incense to Baal, the sun, the moon, the constellations, and all the stars of Heavenheavenheaven. 6He brought the Asherah pole out of the house of the LORD to the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley outside Jerusalem. There he burned it, ground it to powder, and threw the dust on the graves of the common people. 7He tore down the rooms of the male cult prostitutes that were in the house of the LORD, where women wove hangings for Asherah.

8He brought all the priests from the cities of Judah and made the high places unclean, from Geba to Beersheba, where the priests had burned incense. He also tore down the high places by the gates, including the high place at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the city official, which was on the left side of the city gate. 9The priests of the high places did not serve at the LORD's altar in Jerusalem, but they ate unleavened bread with their fellow priests. 10He made Topheth, in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom, unclean so no one could make his son or daughter pass through the fire to Molech.

11He removed from the entrance to the house of the LORD the horses the kings of Judah had given to the sun. They were in the courtyard near the room of Nathan-melech, an official. Josiah burned the chariots of the sun. 12He tore down the altars the kings of Judah had built on the roof of Ahaz's upper room, and the altars Manasseh had built in the two courtyards of the house of the LORD. He took them away, carried their ashes, and threw them into the Kidron ValleyvalleyValley. 13The king made unclean the high places east of Jerusalem, south of the Mount of Corruption. Solomon, king of Israel, had built them for Ashtoreth, the hated god of the Sidonians; Chemosh, the hated god of Moab; and Milcom, the hated god of the Ammonites. 14He smashed the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherah poles, and filled their places with human bones.

15He even tore down the altar at Bethel, the high place made by Jeroboam son of Nebat, who led Israel into sin. He burned that high place, ground it to powder, and burned the Asherah pole. 16As Josiah turned, he saw the tombs on the hillside. He sent for the bones in the tombs and burned them on the altar to make it unclean. This happened as the LORD had said through the man of God who predicted these things.

17Then he asked, "What is that monument I see?"

The men of the city answered, "It is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah and announced these things you have done to the altar at Bethel."

18"Leave it alone," he said. "Let no one move his bones." So they left his bones alone, along with the bones of the prophet who had come from Samaria.

19Josiah also removed all the shrines of the high places in the cities of Samaria. The kings of Israel had built them to make the LORD angry. He treated them the same way he had treated Bethel. 20On the altars he killed all the priests of the high places and burned human bones on them. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

Josiah Celebrates Passover

21Then the king commanded all the people, "Celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, as it is written in this BookbookBook of the CovenantcovenantCovenant."

22No Passover like this had been celebrated since the days of the judges who led Israel, or during the days of the kings of Israel and Judah. 23But in the eighteenth year of King Josiah's reign, this Passover was celebrated to the LORD in Jerusalem.

24Josiah also got rid of the mediums, spiritists, household gods, idols, and all the other disgusting things seen in Judah and Jerusalem. He did this to carry out the words of the law written in the book Hilkiah the priest had found in the house of the LORD. 25No king before or after Josiah was like him. He turned to the LORD with all his heart, all his soul, and all his strength, following all the LawlawLaw of Moses.

26Still, the LORD did not turn from HishisHis great anger. His anger burned against Judah because of all that Manasseh had done to provoke HimhimHim. 27The LORD said, "I will remove Judah from MymyMy sight, just as I removed Israel. I will reject this city, Jerusalem, which I chose, and the house where I said, 'My name will be there.'"

Josiah's Death and the Next Kings

28The rest of Josiah's acts and all he did are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah.

29In Josiah's days, Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, marched against the king of Assyria at the Euphrates River. King Josiah went out to meet him, but Neco killed him at Megiddo as soon as he saw him. 30Josiah's servants carried his body in a chariot from Megiddo, brought him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his own tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah, anointed him, and made him king in his father's place.

31Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for three months. His mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah; she was from Libnah. 32He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his fathers had done.

33Pharaoh Neco put him in prison at Riblah in the land of Hamath, so he could not rule in Jerusalem. He also forced the land to pay a tax of one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold. 34Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim son of Josiah king in place of his father Josiah. He changed Eliakim's name to Jehoiakim. He took Jehoahaz to Egypt, where he died.

35Jehoiakim paid the silver and gold to Pharaoh Neco. To raise the money, he taxed the land and collected the silver and gold from the people of the land, each man paying his share.

36Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for eleven years. His mother was Zebidah daughter of Pedaiah; she was from Rumah. 37He did evil in the eyes of the LORD, just as his fathers had done.
