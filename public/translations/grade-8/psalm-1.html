The Two Paths in Life

1Blessed is the man who does not walk in the advice of the wicked, or stand in the way of sinners, or sit in the seat of mockers. 2But his delight is in the law of the LORD, and he thinks about HishisHis law day and night. 3He is like a tree planted by streams of water. It gives its fruit in season, and its leaves do not dry up. Everything he does succeeds.

4The wicked are not like that. They are like chaff that the wind blows away. 5So the wicked will not stand when God judges, and sinners will not be in the group of the righteous. 6For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will be destroyed.
