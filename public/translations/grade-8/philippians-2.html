United in Christ

1So if there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any sharing in the SpiritspiritSpirit, and any kindness and mercy, 2then make my joy complete by being of one mind. Have the same love, be united in spirit, and think the same way.

3Do nothing out of selfish ambition or pride. Instead, in humility, think of others as more important than yourselves. 4Let each of you look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others.

Living as God's Children

12So, my dear friends, just as you have always obeyed, not only when I was with you but even more now that I am away, work hard to live out the salvation God has given you, with deep respect and awe. 13For God is the OneoneOne working in you, giving you the desire and the power to do what pleases HimhimHim.

14Do everything without complaining or arguing. 15Then you will be pure and without fault, God's children without blame in the middle of a crooked and twisted generation. Among them, you shine like lights in the world.

16Hold tightly to the message that gives life. Then on the day Christ returns, I can be proud that my work and effort were not wasted. 17Even if I give my life for your faith, I am glad and share your joy. 18In the same way, you should be glad and share my joy.

Timothy and Epaphroditus

19I hope, with the Lord Jesus' help, to send Timothy to you soon, so I can be encouraged by news about you. 20I have no one else like him who truly cares about your well-being. 21Everyone else looks out for their own interests, not Christ's. 22But you know how he has proven himself. He served with me in spreading the good news, like a son serving with his father. 23So I hope to send him as soon as I know how things will turn out for me. 24I trust in the Lord that I will come to you soon too.

25I felt I had to send Epaphroditus back to you. He is my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier. He is also your messenger and the one who helped meet my needs. 26He has been longing to see all of you, and he was upset because you heard he was sick. 27He really was sick, almost to the point of death. But God had mercy on him, and on me too, so I would not have grief on top of grief. 28So I am even more eager to send him, so you can be glad to see him again and I can worry less. 29Welcome him in the Lord with great joy, and honourhonourhonor people like him. 30He almost died for Christ's work. He risked his life to make up for the help you were unable to give me.
