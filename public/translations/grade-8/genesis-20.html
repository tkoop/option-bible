Abimelech Confronts Abraham

9Then Abimelech called Abraham and said, "What have you done to us? How have I sinned against you, that you brought such a great sin on me and my kingdom? You have done things to me that should not be done. 10What were you thinking when you did this?"

Abraham Explains

11Abraham answered, "I thought, 'People here do not respect God, and they will kill me because of my wife.' 12She is indeed my sister. She is my father's daughter, but not my mother's daughter. Abimelech Makes Things Right

14Then Abimelech took sheep, cattle, male and female servants, and gave them to Abraham. He also returned his wife Sarah to him. 15Abimelech said, "Look, my land is open to you. Live wherever you like." 16To Sarah he said, "I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver. This is to show everyone with you that you are innocent. You are cleared of all blame."

17Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants, so they could have children again. 18For the LORD had kept all the women in Abimelech's household from having children because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
