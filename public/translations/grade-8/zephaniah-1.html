The LORD's Message to Zephaniah

1This is the word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah. It came during the time of King Josiah son of Amon of Judah.

2“I will sweep away everything from the face of the earth,” declares the LORD. 3“I will sweep away people and animals. I will sweep away the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea. I will remove the idols that make wicked people fall. I will destroy the wicked and remove people from the earth,” declares the LORD.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Judah and Jerusalem

4“I will stretch out MymyMy hand against Judah and against all who live in Jerusalem. I will remove from this place what is left of Baal worship, along with the names of the pagan priests and the other priests. 5I will punish those who bow down on their rooftops to the stars, those who bow down and swear loyalty to the LORD but also swear loyalty to Milcom, 6and those who turn away from following the LORD, and those who do not look for the LORD or ask HimhimHim for help.”

The Day of the LORD Is Near

7Be silent before the Lord the LORD, for the day of the LORD is near. The LORD has prepared a sacrifice; HeheHe has set apart HishisHis guests.

8“On the day of the LORD's sacrifice, I will punish the leaders, the king's sons, and everyone who wears clothes from other nations. 9On that day I will punish everyone who jumps over the doorway and fills his master's house with violence and lies.”

10“On that day,” declares the LORD, “a cry will come from the FishfishFish GategateGate, a wail from the SecondsecondSecond District, and a loud crash from the hills.”

11“Wail, you who live in the market district, for all the traders are ruined; everyone who weighs silver is destroyed.”

12“At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps and punish the men who are comfortable in their sins and do not care, who say in their hearts, ‘The LORD will not do good or evil.’”

13Their wealth will be taken, and their houses will be destroyed. They will build houses but not live in them; they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.

The Great Day of the LORD

14The great day of the LORD is near, very near and coming fast. The sound of the day of the LORD is bitter; even strong warriors cry out. 15That day is a day of anger, a day of trouble and distress, a day of ruin and destruction, a day of darkness and gloom, a day of clouds and thick darkness. 16It is a day of trumpet blasts and war cries against the fortified cities and the high towers.

17“I will bring trouble on people, so they walk like blind men, because they sinned against the LORD. Their blood will be poured out like dust, and their bodies like garbage.”

18Neither their silver nor their gold will save them on the day of the LORD's anger. The whole EarthEarthearth will be burned up by the fire of HishisHis jealousy. For HeheHe will quickly destroy all who live on the earth.
