Message Against the Ruler of Tyre

1The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 2“Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: Your heart became proud, and you said, “I am a god. I sit on a throne of gods in the heart of the seas.” But you are only a man, not a god, and you have made your heart like the heart of a god. 3Look, you are wiser than Daniel. No secret is hidden from you. 4By your wisdom and understanding you have gained wealth for yourself and gathered gold and silver into your treasuries. 5By your great skill in trade you have increased your wealth, and your heart has become proud because of your wealth. 6Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you have made your heart like the heart of a god, 7I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against your beautiful wisdom and defile your splendoursplendoursplendor. 8They will bring you down to the pit, and you will die a violent death in the heart of the seas. 9Will you still say, “I am a god,” before your killers? You are a man, not a god, in the hands of those who wound you. 10You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners. I have spoken, declares the Lord the LORD.’”

A Lament for the King of Tyre

11The word of the LORD came to me, saying, 12“Son of man, raise a lament for the king of Tyre and say to him, ‘This is what the Lord the LORD says: You were the model of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty. 13You were in Eden, the garden of God. Every precious stone was your covering: ruby, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald. Your settings and mountings were made of gold. They were prepared on the day you were created. 14You were an anointed guardian cherub. I placed you on God's holy mountain, and you walked among the fiery stones. 15You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created until wrongdoing was found in you. 16Through your many trades you were filled with violence, and you sinned. So I drove you in disgrace from God's mountain, and I destroyed you, O guardian cherub, from among the fiery stones. 17Your heart became proud because of your beauty. You corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendoursplendoursplendor. So I threw you to the earth and put you on display before kings. 18By your many sins and dishonest trade you have defiled your sanctuaries. So I brought fire from within you, and it consumed you. I turned you to ashes on the earth in the sight of all who saw you. 19All who knew you among the nations are appalled at you. You have come to a horrible end and will be no more forever.’”
