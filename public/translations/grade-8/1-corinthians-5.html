A Serious Sin in the Church

1It is actually reported that there is sexual sin among you, and the kind of sin that even people who don't know God don't commit: a man is in a sexual relationship with his father's wife.

Pride and Church Discipline

2And you are proud! Shouldn't you be sad instead and remove the man who did this from among you? 3Even though I am not with you in body, I am with you in spirit. I have already judged the man who did this, as if I were there. 4When you meet together in the name of our Lord Jesus, and my spirit is with you with the power of our Lord Jesus, 5hand this man over to Satan to destroy his sinful nature, so that his spirit may be saved on the day of the Lord.

Clean Out the Old Yeast

6Your pride is not good. Don't you know that a little yeast makes the whole batch of dough rise? 7Get rid of the old yeast so you can be a new batch of dough without yeast. You are already like dough without yeast, because Christ, our Passover LamblambLamb, has been sacrificed. 8So let us celebrate, not with old yeast or yeast of evil and wickedness, but with bread without yeast, which stands for sincerity and truth.

Avoiding Sinful Believers

9I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people. 10I did not mean people in this world who are sexually immoral, or greedy people, or cheats, or people who worship idols. If that were true, you would have to leave the world. 11But now I am telling you not to associate with anyone who calls himself a brother if he is sexually immoral, greedy, worships idols, insults people, gets drunk, or cheats. Don't even eat with a person like that. 12Why should I judge people outside the church? Aren't you the ones who should judge people inside? 13God will judge those outside. "Remove the evil person from among you."
