God Tells Israel to Camp by the Sea

1Then the LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2"Tell the Israelites to turn back and camp before Pi Hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea. Camp by the sea, directly across from Baal Zephon. 3Pharaoh will think the Israelites are wandering around the land in confusion. He will say, 'The desert has trapped them.' 4I will make Pharaoh stubborn, so he will chase them. Then I will gain glory through Pharaoh and his whole army. The Egyptians will know that I am the LORD." So the Israelites did this.

Pharaoh Chases the Israelites

5When the king of Egypt heard that the people had fled, Pharaoh and his officials changed their minds. They said, "What have we done? We let Israel go, and now they won't serve us." 6So he ordered his chariot ready and took his army with him. 7He took six hundred of his best chariots and all the other chariots of Egypt, with officers over each of them. 8The LORD made Pharaoh stubborn, and he chased the Israelites as they marched out with confidence. 9The Egyptians chased them with all Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and horsemen. They caught up with the Israelites while they camped by the sea near Pi Hahiroth, across from Baal Zephon.

God Promises Rescue and Parts the Sea

10As Pharaoh came closer, the Israelites looked up and saw the Egyptians coming after them. They were very afraid and cried out to the LORD. 11They said to Moses, "Were there no graves in Egypt? Is that why you brought us to the desert to die? What have you done to us by bringing us out of Egypt? 12Didn't we tell you in Egypt, 'Leave us alone so we can serve the Egyptians'? It would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in the desert!"

13Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid. Stand firm, and you will see the rescue the LORD will give you today. The Egyptians you see today, you will never see again. 14The LORD will fight for you; you only need to be still."

15Then the LORD said to Moses, "Why are you crying out to MemeMe? Tell the Israelites to move on. 16Raise your staff and stretch out your hand over the sea. Divide the water so the Israelites can walk through the sea on dry ground. 17I will make the Egyptians stubborn so they will follow them. Then I will gain glory through Pharaoh, his whole army, his chariots, and his horsemen. 18The Egyptians will know that I am the LORD when I gain glory through Pharaoh, his chariots, and his horsemen."

19The angel of God had been leading Israel's camp. He moved and went behind them. The pillar of cloud also moved from in front and stood behind them. 20It came between the Egyptian army and the Israelite army. All night the cloud was dark on one side and bright on the other. Neither army came near the other all night.

21Then Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. All night the LORD pushed the sea back with a strong east wind and made it dry land. The waters split apart. 22The Israelites walked through the sea on dry ground, with a wall of water on their right and on their left.

The Egyptians Are Destroyed

23The Egyptians chased them into the sea—all Pharaoh's horses, chariots, and horsemen. 24In the early morning, the LORD looked down from the pillar of fire and cloud at the Egyptian army and threw them into confusion. 25He made their chariot wheels stick so they had trouble driving. The Egyptians said, "ThetheThe LORD is fighting for them against Egypt!"

26Then the LORD said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea. The waters will come back over the Egyptians, their chariots, and their horsemen." 27So Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. At daybreak the sea returned to its normal place. The Egyptians were running toward it, and the LORD swept them into the sea. 28The waters came back and covered the chariots and horsemen—the whole army of Pharaoh that had followed the Israelites into the sea. Not one of them survived.

29But the Israelites had walked through the sea on dry ground, with a wall of water on their right and on their left. 30That day the LORD saved Israel from the Egyptians. Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the shore. 31When the Israelites saw the great power the LORD had used against the Egyptians, the people feared the LORD. They trusted in the LORD and in Moses, HishisHis servant.
