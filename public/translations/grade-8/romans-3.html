The Advantage of the Jews

1What advantage, then, does a Jew have? Or what good does circumcision do? 2Much in every way! First of all, the Jews were trusted with God's very words.

3What if some did not believe? Does their lack of faith cancel God's faithfulness? 4Not at all! Let God be true, and every person a liar. As it is written: “So that YouyouYou may be proved right in YouryourYour words, and win when YouyouYou are judged.”

5But if our wrong shows how right God is, what should we say? Is God unfair to bring HishisHis anger? (I am speaking in human terms.) 6Not at all! If that were true, how could God judge the world?

7But if my lie makes God's truth stand out more and brings HimhimHim glory, why am I still judged as a sinner? 8And why not say, as some falsely claim we say, “Let us do evil so that good may come”? They deserve to be condemned.

No One Is Righteous

9What then? Are we better than others? Not at all! We have already shown that Jews and Greeks alike are all under sin.

10As it is written: “No one is right with God, not even one. 11No one understands; no one looks for God. 12They have all turned away and become worthless. No one does good, not even one. 13Their throats are open graves. They use their tongues to lie. The poison of snakes is on their lips. 14Their mouths are full of curses and bitterness. 15Their feet are quick to spill blood. 16Ruin and pain are in their paths. 17They do not know the way of peace. 18They do not fear God.”

19Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law. It stops every mouth and makes the whole world answer to God. 20So no one will be made right with God by obeying the law. The law only shows us our sin.

Right with God Through Faith

21But now God's way of making people right with HimhimHim has been shown. It does not depend on the law, but the LawlawLaw and the ProphetsprophetsProphets point to it. 22This right standing with God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe. There is no difference. 23For all have sinned and fall short of God's glory. 24Yet they are made right with God freely by HishisHis grace through the rescue that is in Christ Jesus.

25God offered Jesus as the sacrifice that turns away HishisHis anger. We take hold of this through faith in HishisHis blood. God did this to show that HeheHe is right. In HishisHis patience, HeheHe had not punished sins committed earlier. 26He did this to show that HeheHe is right at this time. He is right, and HeheHe makes right the person who has faith in Jesus.

No Room for Boasting

27So where is there room to boast? It is gone. What kind of law removes boasting? Not a law of works, but a law of faith. 28For we say that a person is made right with God by faith, without obeying the law.

29Is God only the God of the Jews? Is HeheHe not also the God of the Gentiles? Yes, HeheHe is the God of the Gentiles too. 30There is only one God. He will make people who are circumcised right with HimhimHim by faith, and HeheHe will make people who are not circumcised right with HimhimHim through that same faith. 31Do we destroy the law by this faith? Not at all! Instead, we show that the law is true.
