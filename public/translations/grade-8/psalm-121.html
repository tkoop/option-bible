The Lord Is My Help

1I look up to the hills. Where does my help come from? 2My help comes from the LORD, who made Heavenheavenheaven and EarthEarthearth.

3He won't let your foot slip. He who keeps you won't fall asleep. 4See, the OneoneOne who keeps Israel never slumbers or sleeps.

5The LORD is your keeper. The LORD is your shade at your right hand. 6The sun won't hurt you during the day, and the moon won't hurt you at night.

7The LORD will keep you from all evil. He will keep your life safe. 8The LORD will watch over your coming and going from now on and forever.
