Sarai Gives Hagar to Abram

1Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had not had any children. She had an Egyptian servant named Hagar. 2So Sarai said to Abram, "ThetheThe LORD has kept me from having children. Please sleep with my servant. Maybe I can have a family through her." Abram listened to Sarai.

3After Abram had lived ten years in Canaan, Sarai took her Egyptian servant Hagar and gave her to Abram as his wife. 4Abram slept with Hagar, and she became pregnant. When she saw she was pregnant, she looked down on her mistress.

5Then Sarai said to Abram, "YouyouYou are to blame for this wrong against me. I gave my servant to you, and now that she is pregnant, she looks down on me. May the LORD decide between you and me."

6Abram said, "YouryourYour servant is in your hands. Do what you think is best." Then Sarai treated her badly, and Hagar ran away.

The Angel Meets Hagar

7The angel of the LORD found her by a spring in the desert, on the road to Shur.

8He said, "Hagar, servant of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?"

She answered, "I am running away from my mistress Sarai."

9The angel of the LORD said to her, "Go back to your mistress and obey her."

10The angel of the LORD also said, "I will increase your family so much that no one can count them."

11The angel of the LORD continued, "YouyouYou are pregnant, and you will have a son. Name him Ishmael, because the the LORD has heard your suffering. He will be like a wild donkey. He will fight with everyone, and everyone will fight with him. He will live in conflict with all his brothers."

Hagar Names the LORD

13She called the the LORD who spoke to her, "YouyouYou are the God who sees me," because she said, "Have I really seen the OneoneOne who sees me?"

Ishmael Is Born
