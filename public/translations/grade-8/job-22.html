Eliphaz Answers Job

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:

2"Can a man be of use to God? Can even a wise man benefit HimhimHim? 3Is it any pleasure to the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty that you are righteous? Or is it gain to HimhimHim that you make your ways perfect? 4Is it for your reverence that HeheHe rebukes you and brings you to trial?

Eliphaz Accuses Job

5"Is not your wickedness great? Are not your sins endless? 6For you have taken pledges from your brothers without cause, and stripped the naked of their clothing. 7You have not given water to the weary, and you have withheld bread from the hungry. 8But the land belonged to the powerful, and the favouredfavouredfavored lived in it.

9"You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the strength of the fatherless has been crushed. 10That is why snares surround you, and sudden terror overwhelms you, 11or darkness, so that you cannot see, and a flood of water covers you.

God Sees All

12"Is not God in the heights of Heavenheavenheaven? See the highest stars, how lofty they are! 13Yet you say, 'What does God know? Can HeheHe judge through the thick darkness?' 14Thick clouds veil HimhimHim so that HeheHe cannot see, as HeheHe walks on the vault of Heavenheavenheaven. 15Will you keep to the old path that wicked men have trod? 16They were snatched away before their time, and their foundation was swept away by a flood.

17"They said to God, 'Depart from us!' What can the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty do to us? 18Yet HeheHe filled their houses with good things. But the counsel of the wicked is far from me. 19The righteous see and are glad; the innocent mock them, 20saying, 'Surely our foes are destroyed, and fire has consumed their wealth.'

Eliphaz Urges Job to Repent

21"Reconcile now with HimhimHim, and be at peace; thereby good will come to you. 22Receive instruction from HishisHis mouth, and lay up HishisHis words in your heart. 23If you return to the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, you will be restored. If you remove wickedness far from your tent, 24and lay your gold in the dust and the gold of Ophir among the rocks in the ravines, 25then the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty will be your gold and the choicest silver for you.

26"For then you will delight in the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty and look to God with confidence. 27You will pray to HimhimHim, and HeheHe will hear you, and you will fulfillfulfilfulfill your vows. 28What you decide will be done, and light will shine on your ways. 29When men are brought low, you will say, 'Lift them up!' Then HeheHe will save the lowly. 30He will deliver the one who is not innocent, who will be delivered through the cleanness of your hands."
