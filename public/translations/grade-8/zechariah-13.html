A Fountain Opened for Cleansing

1On that day a spring will be opened for the house of David and for the people living in Jerusalem. It will wash away sin and impurity.

Idols and False Prophets Removed

2The LORD of Armies says, “On that day I will wipe out the names of the idols from the land, so they will not be remembered anymore. I will also remove the prophets and the unclean spirit from the land.”

3If anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who gave birth to him will say, “You must not live, because you have spoken lies in the name of the LORD.” If he keeps prophesying, they will stab him.

4On that day the prophets will be ashamed of the visions they prophesy. They will not wear a hairy coat to trick people.

5He will say, “I am not a prophet. I work the land. I have been a farmer since I was young.”

6If someone asks him, “What are these wounds on your body?” he will answer, “I was wounded in the house of my friends.”

The Shepherd Struck and the Sheep Scattered

7“Awake, sword, against MymyMy shepherd, against the man who is MymyMy companion,” declares the LORD of Armies. “Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered. I will turn MymyMy hand against the little ones.”

JudgmentJudgementJudgment and a Refined Remnant

8“In all the land,” declares the LORD, “two-thirds will be cut off and die, but one-third will be left.”

9“I will bring that third through fire and refine them like silver. I will test them like gold. They will call on MymyMy name, and I will answer them. I will say, ‘They are MymyMy people,’ and they will say, ‘The LORD is my God.’”
