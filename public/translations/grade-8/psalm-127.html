A Song About God's Help and Family Blessings

1Unless the LORD builds the house, the builders work for nothing. Unless the LORD guards the city, the guards watch for nothing. 2It is useless for you to get up early and stay up late, working hard for food to eat, for HeheHe gives sleep to those HeheHe loves.

3Children are a gift from the LORD. They are a reward from HimhimHim, the fruit of the womb. 4Like arrows in a warrior's hand are children born in a man's youth. 5Happy is the man whose quiver is full of them. They won't be put to shame when they argue with their enemies at the city gate.
