Gideon Answers the Ephraimites

1The men of Ephraim said to Gideon, "What have you done to us? Why didn't you call us when you went to fight Midian?" They argued with him fiercely.

2Gideon answered, "What have I done compared with you? Aren't the leftovers of Ephraim better than the full grape harvest of Abiezer? 3God gave Oreb and Zeeb, the Midianite leaders, into your hands. What could I have done compared with you?" After he said this, their anger against him died down.

Gideon Pursues the Midianite Kings

4Gideon and his three hundred men came to the Jordan River. They were tired, but they kept chasing the enemy.

5Gideon said to the men of Succoth, "Please give some bread to the men with me. They are tired, and I am chasing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian."

6The leaders of Succoth asked, "Are Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand? Why should we give bread to your army?"

7Gideon said, "When the LORD gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will tear your skin with thorns and briers from the desert."

8From there he went up to Penuel and asked them the same thing. The men of Penuel answered him the same way the men of Succoth had. 9So he said to the men of Penuel, "When I come back safely, I will tear down this tower."

10Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor with their army. About fifteen thousand men were left from the armies of the eastern peoples. One hundred twenty thousand swordsmen had already fallen. 11Gideon went up by the road used by the people who live in tents east of Nobah and Jogbehah. He attacked the camp while it felt safe. 12Zebah and Zalmunna ran away, but Gideon chased them and captured the two Midianite kings. He also scattered their whole army. 13Then Gideon son of Joash returned from the battle by the pass of Heres.

Gideon Punishes Succoth, Penuel, and the Kings

14Gideon captured a young man from Succoth and questioned him. The young man wrote down for him the names of Succoth's leaders and elders—seventy-seven men. 15Then Gideon went to the men of Succoth and said, "Here are Zebah and Zalmunna. You taunted me about them and said, 'Are Zebah and Zalmunna already in your hand? Why should we give bread to your tired men?'"

16HeheHe took the elders of the city, along with thorns and briers from the desert, and used them to punish the men of Succoth. 17He also tore down the tower of Penuel and killed the men of that city.

18Gideon asked Zebah and Zalmunna, "Where are the men you killed at Tabor?"

"They were like you," they said. "Each one looked like a son of a king."

19Gideon said, "They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As the LORD lives, if you had spared them, I would not kill you."

20Then he said to Jether, his oldest son, "Get up and kill them." But the young man did not draw his sword, because he was afraid. He was still young.

21Zebah and Zalmunna said, "Get up and kill us yourself, because a man's strength matches his courage."

So Gideon got up and killed Zebah and Zalmunna. He took the crescent ornaments from their camels' necks.

Israel Asks Gideon to Rule

22The men of Israel said to Gideon, "Rule over us—you, your son, and your grandson—because you saved us from Midian."

23Gideon said, "I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you. The LORD will rule over you."

24Gideon also said, "Let me ask you for one thing: each of you give me an earring from the things you took in battle." (The Midianites had worn gold earrings because they were Ishmaelites.)

25They said, "We will gladly give them." So they spread out a cloth, and each man threw his earring onto it.

26The gold earrings Gideon asked for weighed about seventeen hundred shekels. That was besides the crescents, the pendants, the purple clothes worn by the Midianite kings, and the chains on the camels' necks. 27Gideon made the gold into a sacred vest and put it in his city, Ophrah. All Israel was unfaithful to God by worshiping it there, and it became a trap for Gideon and his family.

28Midian was brought low before Israel and did not raise its head again. The land had peace for forty years during Gideon's life. 29Jerubbaal son of Joash went home and lived there.

30Gideon had seventy sons of his own, because he had many wives. 31His concubine in Shechem also bore him a son, and he named him Abimelech.

32Gideon son of Joash died at a good old age and was buried in the tomb of his father Joash in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

33As soon as Gideon died, the Israelites turned again and were unfaithful to God by worshiping the Baals. They made Baal-berith their god. 34The Israelites did not remember the LORD their God, who had saved them from all their enemies around them. 35They also did not show kindness to the family of Jerubbaal—that is, Gideon—for all the good he had done for Israel.
