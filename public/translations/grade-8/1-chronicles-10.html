The Battle on Mount Gilboa

1The Philistines fought against Israel. The Israelites ran away, and many fell dead on Mount Gilboa. 2The Philistines chased Saul and his sons and killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua. 3The fighting grew hard around Saul. The archers found him, and he was badly wounded. 4Saul said to his armourarmourarmor-bearer, "Draw your sword and kill me with it. I don't want these godless men to come and mistreat me." But the armourarmourarmor-bearer was very afraid and refused. So Saul took his own sword and killed himself with it. 5When the armourarmourarmor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died. 6So Saul, his three sons, and his whole family died together.

7When the Israelites in the valley saw that their army had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they left their cities and ran away. The Philistines moved in and lived there. 8The next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead. They found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa. 9They stripped Saul, took his head and armourarmourarmor, and sent word throughout Philistia to tell their idols and their people. 10They put his armourarmourarmor in the temple of their gods and put his head on display in the temple of Dagon.

The Men of Jabesh-gilead Recover Saul's Body

11When the people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, 12all their brave men went and took the bodies of Saul and his sons. They brought them to Jabesh and buried their bones under the oak tree there. Then they fasted for seven days.

Why Saul Died

13Saul died because he was unfaithful to the LORD. He did not keep the LORD's word, and he even asked a medium for advice. 14He did not ask the LORD for help. So the LORD put him to death and gave his kingdom to David, the son of Jesse.
