The LORD Is My Shepherd

1The LORD is my shepherd. I will not be in need. 2He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside quiet waters. 3He gives me new strength. He guides me on the right paths for the sake of HishisHis name.

4Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, because YouyouYou are with me. Your rod and YouryourYour staff comfort me.

5You prepare a feast for me where my enemies can see. You pour oil on my head, and my cup runs over. 6Goodness and faithful love will follow me all my life, and I will live in the house of the LORD forever.
