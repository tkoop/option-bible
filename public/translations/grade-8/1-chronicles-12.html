Warriors Who Joined David at Ziklag

1These are the men who came to David at Ziklag while he was still kept from Saul son of Kish. They were among the mighty warriors who helped in war. 2They were bowmen who could shoot with either hand, throwing stones or shooting arrows from a bow. They were from Saul's relatives in Benjamin. 3Ahiezer was the leader, along with Joash, sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite; Jeziel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Berachah; and Jehu the Anathothite. 4Ishmaiah the Gibeonite was a hero among the ThirtythirtyThirty and their leader. Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad the Gederathite also came. 5Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite came. 6Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam the Korahites came. 7Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham from Gedor, came.

Gadite Warriors Join David

8Some Gadites joined David at his stronghold in the wilderness. They were brave soldiers, trained for war, who knew how to use shield and spear. Their faces were as fierce as lions, and they ran as fast as gazelles on the mountains. 9Ezer was the leader, Obadiah was second, Eliab was third, 10Mishmannah was fourth, Jeremiah was fifth, 11Attai was sixth, Eliel was seventh, 12Johanan was eighth, Elzabad was ninth, 13Jeremiah was tenth, and Machbannai was eleventh. 14These Gadites were army officers. The least led one hundred men, and the greatest led one thousand. 15They crossed the Jordan in the first month, when the river overflowed its banks. They chased away everyone living in the valleys to the east and to the west.

Men from Benjamin and Judah Join David

16Some men from Benjamin and Judah also came to David at the stronghold. 17David went out to meet them and said, "If you have come to me in peace to help me, I will join with you wholeheartedly. But if you have come to betray me to my enemies, even though I have done no wrong, the God of our fathers will see it and judge."

18Then the SpiritspiritSpirit came upon Amasai, chief of the ThirtythirtyThirty, and he said, "We belong to you, David, and we are with you, son of Jesse. Peace, peace to you, and peace to those who help you, for your God has helped you." David accepted them and made them leaders of his troops.

19Some Manassites came over to David when he went with the Philistines to fight Saul. But they did not help the Philistines, because the Philistine rulers sent David away. They said, "HeheHe might turn against us and go back to his master Saul, and then we would lose our heads."

20As David travelledtravelledtraveled to Ziklag, these Manassites joined him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, leaders of thousands from Manasseh. 21They helped David fight raiders, because all of them were brave soldiers and army commanders. 22Day after day men came to help David, until his army was as large as God's army.

David Is Made King at Hebron

23These are the numbers of armed leaders who came to David at Hebron to turn Saul's kingdom over to him, as the LORD had said. 24From Judah: 6,800 armed men ready for war, carrying shields and spears. 25From Simeon: 7,100 brave soldiers ready for war. 26From the Levites: 4,600. 27Jehoiada, leader of Aaron's family, came with 3,700 men. 28Zadok, a brave young warrior, came with 22 leaders from his father's family. 29From Benjamin, Saul's own tribe, came 3,000 men. Until then, most of them had guarded Saul's family.

30From Ephraim: 20,800 brave soldiers, famous men in their family groups. 31From half the tribe of Manasseh: 18,000 men chosen by name to come and make David king. 32From Issachar: 200 leaders who understood the times and knew what Israel should do. All their relatives followed their orders. 33From Zebulun: 50,000 soldiers ready for war, armed with every kind of weapon and fully committed.

34From Naphtali: 1,000 commanders, along with 37,000 men with shields and spears. 35From Dan: 28,600 men ready for battle. 36From Asher: 40,000 soldiers ready to fight. 37From east of the Jordan, from Reuben, Gad, and half of Manasseh: 120,000 men armed with every kind of weapon. 38All these soldiers came to Hebron ready to fight. They came with one purpose: to make David king over all Israel. The rest of Israel also agreed with one heart to make David king.

Israel Celebrates David's Kingship

39They stayed there three days, eating and drinking with David, because their relatives had prepared food for them. 40Their neighbors, as far away as Issachar, Zebulun, and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, camels, mules, and oxen. They brought flour, fig cakes, raisins, wine, oil, cattle, and sheep in large amounts, because there was joy in Israel.
