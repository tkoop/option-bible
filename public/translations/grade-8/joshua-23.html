Joshua's Final Charge to Israel

1Many days after the LORD had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, Joshua was old and advanced in years. 2Joshua called all Israel together—its elders, leaders, judges, and officers—and said to them, "I am old and advanced in years.

3"You have seen everything the LORD your God did to these nations for you, because HeheHe is the LORD your God, and HeheHe fights for you. 4See, I have given you these remaining nations as an inheritance for your tribes, along with all the nations I destroyed, from the Jordan to the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea in the west. 5The LORD your God will push them out and drive them away before you. You will take their land, just as the LORD your God promised you.

6"So be very strong. Keep and do everything written in the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses. Do not turn away from it to the right or to the left. 7Do not mix with these remaining nations among you. Do not even speak the names of their gods. Do not swear by them, serve them, or bow down to them. 8Instead, stay close to the LORD your God, as you have done up to now.

The LORD Fought for You

9"The LORD has driven out great and strong nations before you. No one has been able to stand against you to this day. 10One of you will chase a thousand, because the LORD your God fights for you, just as HeheHe promised you. 11So guard yourselves very carefully, and love the LORD your God.

Do Not Turn to Other Gods

12"If you turn back and join the rest of these nations still among you, if you intermarry with them, 13then know for sure that the LORD your God will no longer drive them out before you. They will become a trap and a snare to you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you are destroyed and driven from this good land the LORD your God has given you.

14"Look, I am about to die, the way all people do. You know with all your heart and your soul that not one of the good promises the LORD your God made to you has failed. Every one has come true. 15But just as all the good things the LORD your God promised have come to you, so the LORD will bring on you all the harm HeheHe warned about, until HeheHe destroys you and drives you from this good land the LORD your God has given you. 16If you break the covenant of the LORD your God that HeheHe commanded you, and go serve other gods and bow down to them, then the LORD's anger will burn against you, and you will quickly be wiped off the good land HeheHe has given you."
