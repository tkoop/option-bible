Wisdom Calls Out

1Does not Wisdom call out? Does not Understanding raise her voice? 2She stands on the high places along the road, at the crossroads. 3Beside the city gates, at the entrance, she cries out: 4“To you, I call out, and my voice is for everyone. 5Simple ones, learn to be wise; fools, gain understanding. 6Listen, because I speak noble things, and my lips say what is right. 7My mouth speaks truth, and wickedness is hateful to my lips. 8All my words are right; none are twisted or crooked. 9They are clear to anyone who understands, and right to those who find knowledge.”

Wisdom Is Better Than Wealth

10Receive my teaching instead of silver, and knowledge instead of fine gold. 11Wisdom is better than rubies, and nothing anyone desires compares with her. 12I, Wisdom, live with Prudence, and I find knowledge and good judgmentjudgementjudgment. 13To fear the the LORD is to hate evil. I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and crooked speech. 14I have good advice and sound judgmentjudgementjudgment; I have understanding and strength. 15By me kings rule, and rulers make just laws. 16By me princes govern, and nobles judge with justice. 17I love those who love me, and those who seek me early find me. 18With me are riches and honourhonourhonor, lasting wealth and righteousness. 19My fruit is better than gold, even pure gold, and what I produce is better than silver. 20I walk in the way of righteousness, along the paths of justice. 21I give wealth to those who love me and fill their treasuries.

Wisdom and Creation

22The ThetheThe LORD possessed me at the beginning of HishisHis work, before HishisHis deeds of old. 23I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth began. 24When there were no depths, I was born; when there were no springs heavy with water. 25Before the mountains were settled and before the hills, I was born. 26Before HeheHe made the earth and its fields, or the first dust of the world. 27I was there when HeheHe established the heavens and drew a circle on the face of the deep. 28When HeheHe made the clouds above and made the springs of the deep strong. 29When HeheHe set a boundary for the sea so its waters would not cross HishisHis command, and when HeheHe laid the earth’s foundations. 30Then I was beside HimhimHim like a skilled worker, and I was HishisHis delight day by day, rejoicing before HimhimHim at all times. 31I rejoiced in HishisHis whole world and delighted in people.

The Blessing of Finding Wisdom

32Now, my sons, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways. 33Listen to instruction and be wise; do not ignore it. 34Blessed is the person who listens to me, watching daily at my doors and waiting at my doorway. 35For whoever finds me finds life and receives favourfavourfavor from the the LORD. 36But whoever sins against me harms himself; all who hate me love death.
