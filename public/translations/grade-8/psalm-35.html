David Prays for Help Against His Enemies

1The LORD, fight those who fight me; stand against those who attack me. 2Take up YouryourYour shield and armourarmourarmor, and rise to help me. 3Draw out YouryourYour spear and stop those who chase me. Say to my soul, ‘I am the OneoneOne who saves you.’

4Let those who want to kill me be ashamed and disgraced. Let those who plan to hurt me turn back in fear. 5Let them be like dry husks blown by the wind, while the angel of the LORD drives them away. 6Let their road be dark and slippery, while the angel of the LORD chases them.

7For no reason, they hid a net for me. For no reason, they dug a pit to trap me. 8Let ruin hit them by surprise. Let the net they hid catch them. Let them fall into their own pit and be destroyed.

9Then I will be glad in the LORD. I will rejoice in HishisHis help. 10My whole being will say, “the LORD, who is like YouyouYou? You save the poor from those too strong for them. You save the poor and needy from those who rob them.”

11Lying witnesses stand up and ask me things I do not know. 12They pay me back with evil for the good I did, and they crush my spirit.

13But when they were sick, I wore sackcloth and went without food to humble myself. I prayed for them, and my prayers came back to me unanswered. 14I walked around as if they were my friends or brothers. I bowed down in sorrow as if I were mourning my mother.

15But when I stumbled, they were happy and gathered together. Strangers I did not know attacked me. They lied about me without stopping. 16Like people who mock others at a party, they ground their teeth at me.

17How long, O Lord, will YouyouYou just watch? Rescue my life from their attacks. Save my one life from these lions. 18Then I will thank YouyouYou in the great gathering. I will praise YouyouYou among many people.

19Do not let my enemies be glad over me for no reason. Do not let those who hate me without cause wink at me in mockery. 20They do not speak peace. They plan lies against quiet people in the land. 21They open their mouths wide against me and say, “Aha! Aha! Our eyes have seen it!”

22You have seen this, the LORD. Do not stay silent. Lord, do not stay far from me. 23Wake up and rise to defend my case. My God and my Lord, take up my cause. 24Judge me by YouryourYour righteousness, the LORD my God. Do not let them be glad over me.

25Do not let them say in their hearts, “Aha! We have swallowed him up!” Do not let them say, “We have eaten him alive!” 26Let all who are glad when I suffer be ashamed and confused. Let those who brag about me be covered with shame and disgrace.

27Let those who support my right cause shout with joy and be glad. Let them always say, “The LORD is great! He is happy with the peace of HishisHis servant.” 28Then my tongue will talk about YouryourYour righteousness and praise YouyouYou all day long.
