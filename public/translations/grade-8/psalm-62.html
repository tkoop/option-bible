David Finds Rest in God Alone

1For the director of music. For Jeduthun. A psalm of David. I wait quietly for God; my salvation comes from HimhimHim. 2He alone is my rock and my salvation; HeheHe is my fortress, so I will not be shaken. 3How long will you keep attacking one man? Will all of you try to knock him down—like a leaning wall or a shaky fence? 4They only plan to push him out of his high place. They enjoy lies. With their mouths they bless, but in their hearts they curse. Selah

God Is Our Safe Place

5Wait quietly for God alone, my soul, because my hope comes from HimhimHim. 6He alone is my rock and my salvation; HeheHe is my fortress, so I will not be shaken. 7My rescue and my honourhonourhonor depend on God. He is my mighty rock, my safe place. 8Trust in HimhimHim at all times, O people; pour out your hearts to HimhimHim, for God is our safe place. Selah

Trust God, Not Wealth

9Ordinary people are only a breath, and important people are a lie. Put together on a scale, they are lighter than a breath. 10Do not trust in money taken by force, and do not hope in stolen goods. Even if your wealth grows, do not set your heart on it. 11God has said one thing, and I have heard two: You, O God, are strong, 12and YouyouYou, O Lord, are loving. You will pay back each person for what he has done.
