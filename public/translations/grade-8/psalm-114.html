God's Power in the Exodus

1When Israel came out of Egypt, Jacob's family left a people whose language they did not know. 2Judah became HishisHis sanctuary, and Israel became HishisHis kingdom.

3The sea saw and fled; the Jordan turned back. 4The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.

5What is wrong with you, sea, that you flee? Why do you turn back, Jordan? 6Mountains, why do you skip like rams? Hills, why do you skip like lambs?

The Earth Trembles Before the Lord

7Tremble, EarthEarthearth, before the Lord, before the God of Jacob. 8He turned the rock into a pool of water and the flint into a flowing spring.
