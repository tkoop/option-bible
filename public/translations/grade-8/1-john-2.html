A Helper When We Sin

1My dear children, I am writing this to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have a Helper with the FatherfatherFather—Jesus Christ, who is right with God. 2He is the sacrifice that pays for our sins. This is not only for us, but also for the sins of the whole world.

Knowing God and Loving Others

3We know that we have come to know HimhimHim if we keep HishisHis commands. 4Anyone who says, "I know HimhimHim," but does not keep HishisHis commands is a liar. The truth is not in that person. 5But if anyone keeps HishisHis word, God's love is made complete in that person. This is how we know we are in HimhimHim: 6whoever says he stays in HimhimHim must live the way Jesus lived.

7Dear friends, I am not writing you a new command. I am writing an old command that you have had since the beginning. This old command is the message you heard. 8Yet I am also writing you a new command. It is true in HimhimHim and in you, because the darkness is passing away, and the true light is already shining.

9Anyone who says he is in the light but hates his brother is still in the dark. 10Anyone who loves his brother stays in the light, and there is nothing in him that makes others stumble. 11But anyone who hates his brother is in the dark. He walks in the dark. He does not know where he is going, because the dark has blinded his eyes.

Growing in Faith and Not Loving the World

12I am writing to you, dear children, because your sins are forgiven through HishisHis name. 13I am writing to you, fathers, because you know HimhimHim who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one. I have written to you, children, because you know the FatherfatherFather. 14I have written to you, fathers, because you know HimhimHim who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, God's word stays in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

15Do not love the world or anything in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in that person. 16All that is in the world comes down to these things: the cravings of the body, the cravings of the eyes, and pride in what we own. These things are not from the FatherfatherFather. They are from the world. 17The world and its desires are passing away. But whoever does God's will lives forever.

The Last Hour and False Teachers

18Children, this is the last hour. You have heard that the antichrist is coming. Even now many antichrists have come. This is how we know it is the last hour. 19They went out from us, but they did not really belong to us. If they had belonged to us, they would have stayed with us. But they left so it would be clear that not all of them belong to us.

20But you have an anointing from the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne, and you all know the truth. 21I did not write to you because you do not know the truth. I wrote because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.

22Who is the liar? It is the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ. This person is the antichrist. He denies the FatherfatherFather and the SonsonSon. 23No one who denies the SonsonSon has the FatherfatherFather. Anyone who accepts the SonsonSon has the FatherfatherFather too.

24Let what you heard from the beginning stay in you. If it does, you will stay in the SonsonSon and in the FatherfatherFather. 25This is the promise HeheHe made to us: eternal life.

26I have written these things to you about those who are trying to lead you astray. 27The anointing you received from HimhimHim stays in you. You do not need anyone to teach you. His anointing teaches you about all things. It is true and not a lie. Stay in HimhimHim, just as it taught you.

28And now, dear children, stay in HimhimHim. Then when HeheHe appears, we can be confident and not be ashamed before HimhimHim when HeheHe comes. 29If you know that HeheHe is right with God, you also know that everyone who does what is right has been born of HimhimHim.
