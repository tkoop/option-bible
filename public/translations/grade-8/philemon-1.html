Greetings and Thanksgiving

1Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon our dear friend and fellow worker, 2to Apphia our sister, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets in your home: 3Grace and peace to you from God our FatherfatherFather and the Lord Jesus Christ.

4I always thank my God as I remember you in my prayers, 5because I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints. 6I pray that your partnership in the faith will be active as you know every good thing we have in Christ. 7Your love has given me great joy and comfort, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother.

Paul's Appeal for Onesimus

8So, although in Christ I could be bold and order you to do what is right, 9I would rather appeal to you out of love. I, Paul, am an old man, and now I am a prisoner of Christ Jesus. 10I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whom I fathered while in chains.

11He was once useless to you, but now he is useful to both you and me. 12I am sending him back to you—he is my very heart.

13I wanted to keep him with me so that he could serve me on your behalf while I am in chains for the gospel. 14But I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your kindness would not be forced but freely given.

15Maybe this is why he was separated from you for a short time, so that you could have him back forever. 16He is no longer just a slave, but a beloved brother—especially to me, and even more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.

17So if you consider me a partner, welcome him as you would welcome me. 18If he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account. 19I, Paul, write this with my own hand: I will pay it back. I will not even mention that you owe me your very self.

20Yes, brother, let me have this benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ. 21I write this trusting your obedience, knowing that you will do even more than I ask.

Final Greetings and Blessing

22Also, prepare a guest room for me, because I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you.

23Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends you greetings. 24So do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers. 25The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
