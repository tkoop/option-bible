Letter to the Church in Ephesus

1To the angel of the church in Ephesus write: "These are the words of HimhimHim who holds the seven stars in HishisHis right hand and walks among the seven golden lampstands.

2"I know your deeds, your hard work, and your patient endurance. I know you cannot tolerate wicked people. You have tested those who claim to be apostles but are not, and you have found them false. 3You have persevered and endured hardships for MymyMy name, and you have not grown weary.

4"Yet I hold this against you: You have forsaken the love you had at first.

5"So remember how far you have fallen. Repent and do the things you did at first. If you do not repent, I will come to you and remove your lampstand from its place.

6"But you have this in your favourfavourfavor: You hate the practices of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

7"Whoever has ears, let them hear what the SpiritspiritSpirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will give the right to eat from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God."

Letter to the Church in Smyrna

8To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: "These are the words of the FirstfirstFirst and the LastlastLast, who died and came to life again.

9"I know your suffering and your poverty, yet you are rich. I also know the slander from those who say they are Jews but are not. They are a synagogue of Satan. 10Do not be afraid of what you are about to suffer. Look, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison to test you, and you will suffer for ten days. Be faithful, even to the point of death, and I will give you the crown of life.

11"Whoever has ears, let them hear what the SpiritspiritSpirit says to the churches. The one who overcomes will not be hurt at all by the second death."

Letter to the Church in Pergamum

12To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: "These are the words of the OneoneOne who has the sharp, double-edged sword.

13"I know where you live—where Satan has his throne. Yet you remain true to MymyMy name. You did not renounce your faith in MemeMe, even in the days of Antipas, MymyMy faithful witness, who was put to death in your city, where Satan lives. 14But I have a few things against you: You have there some who hold to the teaching of Balaam. He taught Balak to entice the Israelites to sin by eating food sacrificed to idols and by committing sexual immorality.

15"In the same way, you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans. 16So repent! Otherwise, I will come to you soon and fight against them with the sword of MymyMy mouth.

17"Whoever has ears, let them hear what the SpiritspiritSpirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will give some of the hidden manna. I will also give a white stone with a new name written on it, known only to the one who receives it."

Letter to the Church in Thyatira

18To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: "These are the words of the SonsonSon of God, whose eyes are like blazing fire and whose feet are like polished bronze.

19"I know your deeds—your love, your faith, your service, and your patient endurance. I also know that your recent deeds are greater than your first ones. 20But I have this against you: You tolerate that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess. By her teaching she misleads MymyMy servants into sexual immorality and eating food sacrificed to idols.

21"I have given her time to repent of her immorality, but she is unwilling. 22So I will cast her on a bed of suffering, and I will make those who commit adultery with her suffer intensely, unless they repent of what they have done.

23"I will strike her children dead. Then all the churches will know that I am HeheHe who searches hearts and minds, and I will repay each of you according to your deeds. 24Now I say to the rest of you in Thyatira, to those who do not hold to her teaching and have not learned what some call the deep things of Satan: I will not impose any other burden on you.

25"Just hold on to what you have until I come.

26"To the one who overcomes and keeps MymyMy works to the end, I will give authority over the nations. 27He will rule them with an iron sceptresceptrescepter and will dash them to pieces like pottery—just as I have received authority from MymyMy FatherfatherFather. 28I will also give that one the morning star.

29"Whoever has ears, let them hear what the SpiritspiritSpirit says to the churches."
