Enemies March Against Judah

1After this, the Moabites, Ammonites, and some of the Meunites came to fight against Jehoshaphat. 2People came and told Jehoshaphat, “A huge army is coming against you from Aram, from beyond the sea. They are already at Hazazon-tamar,” which is En Gedi.

Jehoshaphat Prays for Help

3Jehoshaphat was afraid. He decided to seek the LORD and called for a fast in all Judah. 4The people of Judah came together to ask the LORD for help. They came from every city in Judah to seek the LORD. 5Jehoshaphat stood in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem in the house of the LORD, in front of the new courtyard.

6He said, “The LORD, God of our fathers, are YouyouYou not God in Heavenheavenheaven? You rule all the kingdoms of the nations. Power and might are in YouryourYour hand, and no one can stand against YouyouYou.

7“Our God, did YouyouYou not drive out the people who lived in this land before YouryourYour people Israel? Did YouyouYou not give it forever to the descendants of Abraham, YouryourYour friend? 8They have lived in it and built a sanctuary there for YouryourYour name. 9If disaster comes on us—sword, judgmentjudgementjudgment, plague, or famine—we will stand before this house and before YouyouYou, because YouryourYour name is in this house. We will cry to YouyouYou in our trouble, and YouyouYou will hear and save us.

10“Now look! The Ammonites, Moabites, and people of Mount Seir are coming. You did not let Israel invade them when Israel came from Egypt, so Israel turned away from them and did not destroy them. 11Now they are paying us back by coming to drive us out of the land YouyouYou gave us as our possession. 12Our God, will YouyouYou not judge them? We have no strength against this large army that is coming against us. We do not know what to do, but our eyes are on YouyouYou.”

13All the people of Judah stood before the LORD with their wives and children, even their little ones.

God Gives Victory

14The SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD came upon Jahaziel son of Zechariah, son of Benaiah, son of Jeiel, son of Mattaniah, a Levite from the descendants of Asaph, in the middle of the assembly.

15He said, “Listen, all you people of Judah and Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat. This is what the LORD says to you: Do not be afraid or discouraged because of this huge army. The battle is not yours but God's. 16Tomorrow go down against them. They will come up by the Ascent of Ziz, and you will find them at the end of the valley before the wilderness of Jeruel.

17“You will not need to fight this battle. Take your positions, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD for you. O Judah and Jerusalem, do not be afraid or discouraged. Go out against them tomorrow, and the LORD will be with you.”

18Jehoshaphat bowed with his face to the ground. All the people of Judah and Jerusalem fell down before the LORD to worship HimhimHim. 19The Levites from the Kohathites and Korahites stood up to praise the LORD, the God of Israel, with a very loud voice.

20Early in the morning they got up and went out to the wilderness of Tekoa. As they were leaving, Jehoshaphat stood and said, “Listen to me, people of Judah and Jerusalem! Believe in the LORD your God, and you will be made strong. Believe HishisHis prophets, and you will succeed.”

21After talking with the people, he chose singers for the LORD. They praised the LORD in holy splendoursplendoursplendor as they marched in front of the army. They sang, “Give thanks to the LORD, for HishisHis mercy endures forever.”

22As they began to sing and praise, the LORD set ambushes against the Ammonites, Moabites, and people of Mount Seir who had come against Judah, and they were defeated. 23The Ammonites and Moabites turned against the people of Mount Seir to destroy and wipe them out. After they finished with the people of Seir, they helped destroy one another. 24When Judah came to the watchtower in the wilderness, they looked at the crowd. There were dead bodies lying on the ground, and no one had escaped.

25Jehoshaphat and his people came to take the plunder. They found a lot of goods, clothing, and valuable items. They took more than they could carry. It took them three days to collect the plunder because there was so much. 26On the fourth day they gathered in the ValleyvalleyValley of Berachah, because there they blessed the LORD. That is why the place is called the ValleyvalleyValley of Berachah to this day.

27Then all the men of Judah and Jerusalem returned, with Jehoshaphat leading them. They went back to Jerusalem with joy, because the LORD had given them joy over their enemies. 28They came to Jerusalem and went to the house of the LORD with harps, lyres, and trumpets. 29The fear of God came on all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against the enemies of Israel. 30So Jehoshaphat's kingdom was at peace, and his God gave him rest on every side.

Jehoshaphat's Later Years

31Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi. 32He walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn away from it. He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD. 33But the high places were not removed. The people still had not set their hearts on the God of their fathers.

34The rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, from first to last, are written in the records of Jehu son of Hanani, which are included in the book of the kings of Israel. 35After this, Jehoshaphat king of Judah made an alliance with Ahaziah king of Israel, who did evil. 36He joined with him to build ships to go to Tarshish. They built the ships at Ezion-geber.

37Eliezer son of Dodavahu from Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat: “Because you have joined with Ahaziah, the LORD will destroy what you have made.” The ships were wrecked and could not sail to Tarshish.
