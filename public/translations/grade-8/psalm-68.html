God Rises and His Enemies Scatter

1For the director of music. A psalm of David. A song. God rises up. His enemies scatter, and those who hate HimhimHim run away from HimhimHim. 2As smoke is blown away, YouyouYou blow them away. As wax melts before a fire, so the wicked perish before God. 3But those who do right will be glad. They will rejoice before God and celebrate with joy.

Praise the God Who Rides the Clouds

4Sing to God, sing praises to HishisHis name. Praise HimhimHim who rides on the clouds. His name is the LORD, and rejoice before HimhimHim. 5God is a father to the fatherless and a defender of widows. He lives in HishisHis holy home. 6God gives a home to those who are alone. He leads prisoners to freedom, but rebels live in a dry land.

God Marched with His People from Sinai

7God, when YouyouYou went out ahead of YouryourYour people, when YouyouYou marched through the desert. Selah. 8The EarthEarthearth shook, and rain poured from the sky before God, the God of Sinai, before God, the God of Israel. 9You sent plenty of rain, O God; YouyouYou refreshed YouryourYour tired land. 10Your people settled there. In YouryourYour goodness, O God, YouyouYou provided for the poor.

11The LORD gives the command, and a large group of women spread the news. 12Kings and armies run away quickly. Even the woman who stays home shares the plunder. 13Even if you rest among the sheep pens, you will be like a dove's wings covered with silver, with shining gold feathers. 14When the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty scattered kings, it was like snow falling on Mount Zalmon.

The Lord's Victory Procession

15God's mountain is like Mount Bashan, a mountain with many peaks. 16Why do you look with envy, you mountains with many peaks? This is the mountain God chose for HishisHis home. The LORD will live there forever. 17God's chariots number in the tens of thousands—thousands upon thousands. The Lord is among them in HishisHis holy place, just as HeheHe was at Sinai. 18You went up to the high place. You took captives with YouyouYou. You received gifts from people, even from rebels, so that the LORD God may live there.

19Blessed be the Lord, who carries our loads every day—the God who saves us. Selah. 20Our God is a God who saves; the Lord—the LORD—gives us a way out of death. 21Surely God will crush the heads of HishisHis enemies, the hairy scalps of those who keep sinning. 22The Lord said, "I will bring them back from Bashan; I will bring them back from the depths of the sea, 23so your feet can wade in their blood, and your dogs can lick their share from the enemy."

24People see YouryourYour procession, O God, the procession of my God and KingkingKing into the holy place. 25Singers lead the way, musicians follow, and young women play tambourines in the middle. 26Bless God among the great gathering. Bless the LORD, you who come from Israel's fountain. 27There is Benjamin, the youngest, leading them; the princes of Judah in their group; the princes of Zebulun and Naphtali.

28Use YouryourYour power, O God; show YouryourYour strength, O God, which YouyouYou have worked for us. 29Because of YouryourYour temple in Jerusalem, kings will bring gifts to YouyouYou. 30Rebuke the beast of the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the peoples, until they trample those who want silver. Scatter the peoples who love war. 31Messengers will come from Egypt. Cush will stretch out her hands to God. 32Sing to God, kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord—Selah. 33To HimhimHim who rides across the ancient heavens. Listen—HeheHe speaks with a mighty voice. 34Give power to God; HishisHis majesty is over Israel, and HishisHis power is in the skies. 35God, YouyouYou are awesome in YouryourYour holy place; the God of Israel gives power and strength to HishisHis people. Blessed be God!
