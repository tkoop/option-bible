Run the Race with Endurance

1We are surrounded by a huge crowd of witnesses. So let us throw off everything that slows us down and the sin that trips us up. Let us run with patience the race that is set before us. 2Let us keep our eyes on Jesus. He is the one who starts and finishes our faith. He endured the cross for the joy that was set before HimhimHim. He thought nothing of the shame. Now HeheHe sits at the right hand of God's throne. 3Think about HimhimHim. He endured so much hate from sinners. Think about HimhimHim so that you will not grow weary and lose heart. 4In your fight against sin, you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding blood.

God Disciplines His Children

5Have you forgotten the words that speak to you as sons? They say, "MymyMy son, do not take the Lord's discipline lightly, and do not lose heart when HeheHe corrects you. 6For the Lord disciplines the one HeheHe loves, and HeheHe punishes every son HeheHe accepts."

7Endure hardship as discipline. God is treating you as sons. What son is there whose father does not discipline him? 8If you are not disciplined, and everyone undergoes discipline, then you are not true sons. You are illegitimate children. 9Besides, we had human fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. Should we not obey the FatherfatherFather of spirits even more and live? 10They disciplined us for a short time as they thought best. But God disciplines us for our good, so that we can share in HishisHis holiness. 11No discipline feels good at the time. It hurts. Later, though, it produces a harvest of right living and peace for those who have been trained by it.

Pursue Peace and Holiness

12So strengthen your weak arms and your weak knees. 13Make straight paths for your feet, so that the lame will not be turned aside but healed. 14Try hard to live in peace with everyone and to be holy. Without holiness, no one will see the Lord. 15Watch out that no one misses God's grace. Watch out that no bitter root grows up to cause trouble and make many people unclean. 16Watch out that no one is sexually immoral or godless like Esau. He sold his rights as the oldest son for one meal. 17You know that later he wanted to receive the blessing. But he was rejected. He could not change what had happened, even though he begged for it with tears.

Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

18You have not come to a mountain that can be touched, a mountain burning with fire. You have not come to darkness, gloom, and storm. 19You have not come to the sound of a trumpet or to a voice speaking words. The people who heard that voice begged that no more be said to them.

20They could not bear what was commanded: "If even an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned."

21The sight was so terrifying that Moses said, "I am trembling with fear."

22But you have come to Mount Zion, to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem. You have come to thousands upon thousands of angels gathered in joy. 23You have come to the gathering of the firstborn, whose names are written in Heavenheavenheaven. You have come to God, the JudgejudgeJudge of all, and to the spirits of righteous people made perfect. 24You have come to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel.

25See to it that you do not refuse HimhimHim who speaks. If the people did not escape when they refused the OneoneOne who warned them on EarthEarthearth, how much less will we escape if we turn away from the OneoneOne who warns us from Heavenheavenheaven?

26At that time HishisHis voice shook the earth. But now HeheHe has promised, "Once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens."

27The words "once more" show that what can be shaken—that is, created things—will be removed, so that what cannot be shaken will remain.

28So, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us be thankful. Then we can worship God in a way that pleases HimhimHim, with reverence and awe. 29For our God is a consuming fire.
