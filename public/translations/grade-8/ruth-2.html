Ruth Finds Work in Boaz's Field

1Now Naomi had a relative on her husband's side. He was a wealthy and respected man from the family of Elimelek, and his name was Boaz.

2Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Let me go to the fields and pick up leftover grain behind anyone who is kind to me."

Naomi said, "Go ahead, my daughter."

3So Ruth went out, entered a field, and began to glean behind the harvesters. It happened that the field belonged to Boaz, who was from the family of Elimelek.

Boaz Notices Ruth

4Just then Boaz arrived from Bethlehem and said to the harvesters, "ThetheThe LORD be with you!"

They answered, "ThetheThe LORD bless you!"

5Boaz asked the man in charge of the harvesters, "Whose young woman is this?"

6The overseer replied, "She is the Moabite who came back with Naomi from the country of Moab."

7The overseer added, "She asked, 'Please let me glean and gather among the sheaves after the harvesters.' She came into the field and has been on her feet from morning until now, except for a short rest in the shelter."

Boaz Shows Kindness to Ruth

8So Boaz said to Ruth, "Listen to me, my daughter. Do not go to glean in another field, and do not leave this one. Stay here with my young women. 9Watch the field where the men are harvesting, and follow along after my young women. I have told the men not to touch you. When you are thirsty, go and drink from the jars the men have filled."

10At this, she bowed down with her face to the ground and said, "Why have I found favourfavourfavor in your eyes, that you notice me, a foreigner?"

11Boaz replied, "I have been told all about what you have done for your mother-in-law since your husband died. You left your father and mother and your homeland and came to live with a people you did not know before. 12May the LORD repay you for what you have done. May you be richly rewarded by the LORD, the God of Israel, under HishisHis wings you have come to take refuge."

13"May I continue to find favourfavourfavor in your eyes, my lord," she said. "You have put me at ease by speaking kindly to your servant, though I do not have the standing of one of your servants."

14At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come over here. Have some bread and dip it in the vinegar." When she sat down with the harvesters, he offered her some roasted grain. She ate all she wanted and had some left over.

15As she got up to glean, Boaz gave orders to his men, "Let her gather among the sheaves, and do not embarrass her. 16Even pull out some stalks for her from the bundles and leave them for her to pick up, and do not rebuke her."

17So Ruth gleaned in the field until evening. Then she threshed the barley she had gathered, and it amounted to about an ephah.

Ruth Returns to Naomi

18She carried it back to town, and her mother-in-law saw how much she had gathered. Ruth also brought out and gave her what she had left over after she had eaten enough.

19Her mother-in-law asked her, "Where did you glean today? Where did you work? Blessed be the man who took notice of you!"

Then Ruth told her mother-in-law about the one at whose place she had been working. "The name of the man I worked with today is Boaz," she said.

20"The LORD bless him!" Naomi said to her daughter-in-law. "He has not stopped showing his kindness to the living and the dead." She added, "That man is our close relative; he is one of our guardian-redeemers."

21Then Ruth the Moabite said, "HeheHe even said to me, 'Stay with my workers until they finish harvesting all my grain.'"

22Naomi said to Ruth, her daughter-in-law, "It will be good for you, my daughter, to go with his young women, so that you will not be bothered in another field."

23So Ruth stayed close to the young women of Boaz to glean until the barley and wheat harvests were finished. And she lived with her mother-in-law.
