Boaz Settles the Matter at the Gate

1Boaz went up to the town gate and sat down. The other family redeemer, the one Boaz had talked about, came by. Boaz said, "Come over here, friend, and sit down." So the man came over and sat down.

2Boaz took ten of the town leaders and said, "Sit here." They sat down.

3Then Boaz said to the redeemer, "Naomi, who came back from Moab, is selling the field that belonged to our relative Elimelech.

4"I wanted to tell you about it. Buy it here in front of the people sitting here and in front of the leaders of my people. If you want to redeem it, redeem it. If you do not, tell me, so I will know. You are the only one who can redeem it before me, and I come next."

The other man said, "I will redeem it."

5Then Boaz said, "On the day you buy the field from Naomi, you also take Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man, as your wife. You do this to keep the dead man's name alive on his land."

The Other Redeemer Steps Aside

6The other redeemer said, "I cannot redeem it for myself. If I did, I might ruin my own family's land. You take my right to redeem it, because I cannot redeem it."

7In Israel, people used to settle a land buy-back or a trade this way. To make it official, one man took off his sandal and gave it to the other man. Everyone knew this proved the deal.

8So the other redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself." Then he took off his sandal.

Boaz Buys the Land and Marries Ruth

9Then Boaz said to the leaders and all the people, "YouyouYou are witnesses today that I have bought from Naomi all that belonged to Elimelech, Chilion, and Mahlon. 10I have also bought Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's wife, to be my wife. I do this to keep the dead man's name alive on his land, so his name will not disappear from his family or from his hometown. You are witnesses today."

The Town Celebrates and a Grandson Is Born

11All the people at the gate and the leaders said, "We are witnesses. May the LORD make the woman coming into your house like Rachel and Leah, who together built the family of Israel. May you prosper in Ephrathah and be famous in Bethlehem. 12May your family be like the family of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah, because of the children the LORD will give you through this young woman."

13So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He slept with her, and the LORD gave her a child. She had a son.

14Then the women said to Naomi, "Praise the LORD! He did not leave you today without a family redeemer. May his name be famous in Israel. 15He will give you new life and take care of you in your old age. Your daughter-in-law, who loves you and is better to you than seven sons, has given birth to him."

16Naomi took the child, held him close, and cared for him.

17The women living nearby said, "Naomi has a son!" They named him Obed. He became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.

18This is the family line of Perez: Perez was the father of Hezron. 19Hezron was the father of Ram. Ram was the father of Amminadab. 20Amminadab was the father of Nahshon. Nahshon was the father of Salmon. 21Salmon was the father of Boaz. Boaz was the father of Obed. 22Obed was the father of Jesse. Jesse was the father of David.
