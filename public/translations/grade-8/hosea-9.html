Do Not Celebrate Like Other Nations

1Do not celebrate, Israel, with joy like other nations, because you turned away from your God. You loved the pay a prostitute gets on every threshing floor. 2The threshing floor and winepress will not feed the people, and the new wine will run short.

3They will not stay in the LORD’s land. Ephraim will go back to Egypt and eat unclean food in Assyria. 4They will not pour out wine to the LORD, and their sacrifices will not please HimhimHim. Their bread will be like bread eaten at a funeral. Anyone who eats it will become unclean, because their food is only for themselves. It will not go into the LORD’s house.

5What will you do on a feast day, on the LORD’s festival day? 6Look, they are going to Assyria. Egypt will gather them up. Memphis will bury them. Their silver treasures will be overgrown with weeds, and thorns will fill their tents.

The Days of Punishment Have Come

7The days of punishment have come. The days of paying back have come. Israel will know it. The prophet is a fool. The man of the SpiritspiritSpirit is crazy, because your sin is great and your hostility is strong. 8The prophet is Ephraim’s lookout with my God, but a trap waits on all his paths. Hostility is in the house of his God.

9They have become deeply corrupt, like in the days of Gibeah. He will remember their guilt and punish their sins. 10I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness. I saw your fathers like the first ripe fig on a fig tree in its first season. But they went to Baal Peor and gave themselves to shame. They became as disgusting as the thing they loved.

Ephraim Will Lose Its Glory

11Ephraim’s glory will fly away like a bird. There will be no birth, no pregnancy, and no conception. 12Even if they raise children, I will take them away. Yes, it will be terrible for them when I leave them. 13I have seen Ephraim, planted like Tyre in a pleasant field. But Ephraim will bring its children out to be killed.

14Give them, O the LORD—what will YouyouYou give? Give them wombs that miscarry and breasts that give no milk. 15All their wickedness is in Gilgal, for there I hated them. Because of their evil deeds, I will drive them out of MymyMy house. I will no longer love them. All their leaders are rebellious.

16Ephraim is struck down. Its root is dried up. It will bear no fruit. Even if they have children, I will kill the children they love most. 17My God will reject them because they did not listen to HimhimHim. They will wander among the nations.
