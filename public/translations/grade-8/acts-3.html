Peter and John Go to the Temple

1One day Peter and John were going up to the temple at the time of prayer—three in the afternoon. 2Now a man who had been lame since birth was being carried to the temple gate called Beautiful. He was put there every day to beg from people going into the temple.

3When he saw Peter and John about to go in, he asked them for money. 4Peter looked straight at him, as did John. “Look at us!” Peter said.

5So the man gave them his attention, expecting to get something from them. 6Then Peter said, “I don’t have silver or gold, but what I do have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk.”

7Taking him by the right hand, he helped him up, and right away the man’s feet and ankles became strong. 8He jumped to his feet and began to walk. Then he went with them into the temple, walking and jumping and praising God.

9All the people saw him walking and praising God. 10They recognizedrecogniserecognized him as the same man who used to sit and beg at the Beautiful GategateGate of the temple, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.

11While the man held on to Peter and John, all the people were astonished and came running to them in the place called Solomon’s Colonnade.
