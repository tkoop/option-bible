Warning About False Prophets

1If a prophet or dreamer of dreams rises among you and gives you a sign or wonder, 2and the sign or wonder he spoke about comes true, and he says, “Let us go after other gods—gods you have not known—and let us worship them,” 3do not listen to that prophet or dreamer. The LORD your God is testing you to know whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul. 4Follow the LORD your God, fear HimhimHim, keep HishisHis commands, listen to HishisHis voice, serve HimhimHim, and hold fast to HimhimHim. 5That prophet or dreamer must be put to death, because he preached rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt and redeemed you from slavery, and he tried to turn you from the path the LORD your God commanded you to follow. Remove the evil from among you.

Do Not Spare a Secret Tempter

6If your brother, your son or daughter, the wife you love, or your close friend secretly says, “Let us go and worship other gods”—gods neither you nor your fathers have known, 7gods of the peoples around you, whether near or far, from one end of the earth to the other— 8do not yield to him or listen to him. Do not pity him; do not spare him; and do not protect him. 9You must put him to death. Your hand must be the first to put him to death, and then the hands of all the people. 10Stone him to death, because he tried to turn you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery. 11Then all Israel will hear and be afraid, and no one among you will do such an evil thing again.

Punishing a Town That Turns Away

12If you hear it said about one of the towns the LORD your God is giving you to live in, 13that worthless men have gone out from among you and have led the people of their town astray, saying, “Let us go and worship other gods”—gods you have not known— 14then you must inquire, probe, and investigate thoroughly. If it is true and confirmed that this detestable thing has been done among you, 15you must put to the sword all who live in that town. Destroy the town and everything in it, including its livestock, completely by the sword. 16Gather all its plunder into the middle of the public square and burn the town and all its plunder as a whole burnt offering to the LORD your God. It must remain a ruin forever, never to be rebuilt.

Blessing for Obeying the LORD

17None of the devoted things shall stick to your hands, so that the LORD may turn from HishisHis fierce anger and show you mercy, have compassion on you, and increase you as HeheHe promised your fathers. 18For you will be obeying the LORD your God by keeping all HishisHis commands that I am giving you today, and by doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.
