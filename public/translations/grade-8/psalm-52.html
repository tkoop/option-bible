David Warns a Boastful Enemy

For the music leader. A Maskil by David. This happened when Doeg the Edomite came to Saul and said, "David has gone to Ahimelech's house."

1Why do you brag about evil, mighty man? God's faithful love lasts all day. 2Your tongue plans ruin. It is like a sharp razor that tricks people. 3You love evil instead of good, and lies instead of truth. Selah. 4You love every word that destroys, O lying tongue.

5But God will break you down forever. He will snatch you up and tear you from your tent. He will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah.

6Righteous people will see this and be afraid. They will laugh at him. 7Look! This man did not make God his safe place. Instead, he trusted in his great wealth and grew strong in his evil.

8But I am like a healthy olive tree in God's house. I trust in God's faithful love forever and ever. 9I will praise YouyouYou forever, because YouyouYou did this. I will put my hope in YouryourYour name, for it is good, among YouryourYour faithful people.
