Promises to Restore Israel and Judah

1This is the message that came to Jeremiah from the LORD: 2The LORD, the God of Israel, said, "Write in a book all the words I have spoken to you. 3For the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will bring back MymyMy people Israel and Judah from exile. I will bring them back to the land I gave their ancestors, and they will own it again."

A Time of Trouble and Rescue

4These are the words the LORD spoke about Israel and Judah:

5"This is what the LORD says: We have heard a cry of terror, of fear and no peace. 6Ask and see: Can a man give birth? Why then do I see every strong man with his hands on his stomach like a woman in labourlabourlabor, with every face turned pale? 7How terrible that day will be! No other day is like it. It will be a time of trouble for Jacob, but he will be saved out of it. 8In that day, declares the LORD AlmightyalmightyAlmighty, I will break the yoke off their necks and tear off their bonds. No longer will foreigners make them slaves. 9Instead, they will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them."

Healing for a Wounded Nation

10"So do not be afraid, Jacob MymyMy servant, declares the LORD. Do not be dismayed, Israel. I will surely save you from a faraway place, and your descendants from the land of their exile. Jacob will return and live in peace and safety, and no one will make him afraid. 11I am with you and will save you, declares the LORD. Though I completely destroy all the nations where I scattered you, I will not completely destroy you. I will discipline you, but only with justice. I will not leave you unpunished."

12"This is what the LORD says: Your wound is incurable, and your injury cannot be healed. 13No one will plead your case. There is no medicine for your sore, and no healing for you. 14All your allies have forgotten you; they do not care about you. I have struck you like an enemy would, with the punishment of a cruel foe, because your guilt is so great and your sins so many. 15Why do you cry out over your wound, your pain that has no cure? Because of your great guilt and many sins I have done these things to you. 16But those who devour you will be devoured; all your enemies will go into exile. Those who plunder you will be plundered, and all who steal from you I will hand over to be plundered. 17But I will restore you to health and heal your wounds, declares the LORD, because you are called an outcast, Zion, whom no one cares for."
