Our Heavenly Home

1For we know that if the tent we live in on EarthEarthearth is destroyed, we have a building from God. It is an eternal house in Heavenheavenheaven, not made by human hands. 2In this tent we groan, longing to be clothed with our heavenly dwelling. 3If we are clothed with it, we will not be found naked. 4While we are in this tent, we groan and feel weighed down. We do not want to be unclothed, but clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life. 5God made us for this very purpose, and HeheHe gave us the SpiritspiritSpirit as a guarantee.

Living to Please the Lord

6So we are always confident. We know that while we are at home in the body, we are away from the Lord. 7For we walk by faith, not by sight. 8We are confident, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord. 9So we make it our goal to please HimhimHim, whether we are at home or away. 10For we must all appear before the judgmentjudgementjudgment seat of Christ, so that each of us may receive what is due for what we did in the body, whether good or bad. 11Since we know the fear of the Lord, we persuade people. But we are well known to God, and I hope we are also well known in your consciences. 12We are not trying to praise ourselves to you again. Instead, we are giving you a reason to be proud of us, so that you can answer those who boast about outward things and not about what is in the heart. 13If we are out of our minds, it is for God; if we are in our right minds, it is for you.

Christ’s Love Compels Us

14For the love of Christ controls us, because we have decided this: One died for all, so all died. 15He died for all so that those who live would no longer live for themselves, but for HimhimHim who died for them and was raised. 16So from now on we do not view anyone from a human point of view. Even though we once knew Christ from a human point of view, we do not know HimhimHim that way anymore. 17So if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away; look, new things have come.
