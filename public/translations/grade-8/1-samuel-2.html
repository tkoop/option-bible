Samuel Serves the LORD

11Then Elkanah went home to Ramah, but the boy stayed to serve the LORD under Eli the priest.

Eli's Wicked Sons

12Eli's sons were worthless men who did not know the LORD.

13This was the priests' custom with the people: Whenever anyone offered a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come with a three-pronged fork in his hand while the meat was boiling. 14He would stick it into the pot, kettle, cauldron, or pan, and the priest would take whatever the fork brought up. They did this to all the Israelites who came to Shiloh.

15Even before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give the priest meat to roast. He will not take boiled meat from you, only raw meat."

16If the man said that the fat should be burned first and then the priest could take whatever he wanted, the servant would answer, "No, give it now. If not, I will take it by force." 17So the young men's sin was very great before the LORD, because they treated the LORD's offering with contempt.

18Samuel served the LORD as a boy. He wore a linen ephod. 19Each year his mother made him a little robe and brought it when she went with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

20Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife and say, "May the LORD give you children by this woman in place of the one she gave to the LORD." Then they would go home.

21The LORD visited Hannah, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. The boy Samuel grew up with the LORD.

22Eli was very old. He heard everything his sons were doing to all Israel, and that they were sleeping with the women who served at the entrance to the tent of meeting.

23So he said to them, "Why do you do these things? I hear from all the people about your evil deeds. 24No, my sons; the report I hear is not good. You are making the LORD's people sin. 25If one person sins against another, God may mediate for them. But if a person sins against the LORD, who can pray for them?" Yet they would not listen to their father, because the LORD wanted to put them to death.

26The boy Samuel kept growing. He was in favourfavourfavor with the LORD and with people.
