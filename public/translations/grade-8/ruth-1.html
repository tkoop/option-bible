A Family Leaves Bethlehem During a Famine

1In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. A man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live in the land of Moab for a while. He went with his wife and his two sons. 2The man's name was Elimelech, his wife's name was Naomi, and his two sons were named Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. They went to Moab and stayed there. 3But Naomi's husband, Elimelech, died, and she was left with her two sons. 4Elimelech's sons married Moabite women. One was named Orpah, and the other was named Ruth. They lived there about ten years. 5Then both Mahlon and Chilion died, and Naomi was left without her husband and her two sons.

Naomi Decides to Return to Judah

6She heard in Moab that the LORD had taken care of HishisHis people and given them food. So she and her daughters-in-law got ready to leave Moab and go back to Judah. 7She left the place where she had been living, along with her two daughters-in-law, and they started down the road back to Judah.

Naomi Urges Her Daughters-in-Law to Stay Behind

8Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go back, each of you, to your mother's home. May the LORD be kind to you, just as you were kind to your husbands who died and to me. 9May the LORD give you a peaceful home with a new husband." Then she kissed them, and they cried loudly.

10They said to her, "No, we'll go back with you to your people."

11Naomi said, "Turn back, my daughters. Why would you come with me? Do I still have sons who could become your husbands? 12Turn back, my daughters; go your own way. I'm too old to have a husband. Even if I thought I still had hope—even if I got married tonight and had sons— 13would you wait for them to grow up? Would you stay single for them? No, my daughters. My life is much more bitter than yours, because the LORD has turned against me."

14They cried loudly again. Then Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth held on to her.

15Naomi said, "Look, your sister-in-law has gone back to her people and her gods. Go back with her."

16Ruth said, "Don't ask me to leave you or to stop following you. Where you go, I'll go. Where you stay, I'll stay. Your people will be my people, and your God will be my God. 17Where you die, I'll die, and there I'll be buried. May the LORD punish me severely if anything but death separates us."

18When Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her, she stopped arguing.

Naomi and Ruth Arrive in Bethlehem

19So the two of them walked on until they reached Bethlehem. When they arrived, the whole town was excited about them. The women asked, "IsisIs this Naomi?"

20She said to them, "Don't call me Naomi. Call me Mara, because the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty has made my life very bitter. 21I left here full, but the LORD has brought me back empty. Why call me Naomi when the LORD has spoken against me and the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty has brought disaster on me?"

22So Naomi came back from Moab with her daughter-in-law Ruth, who was from Moab. They arrived in Bethlehem just as the barley harvest was starting.
