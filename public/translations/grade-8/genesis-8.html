The Floodwaters Go Down

1God remembered Noah and every wild animal and farm animal that was with him in the ark. God sent a wind over the earth, and the water went down. 2He closed the springs deep under the earth and the windows of the sky. He stopped the rain from the sky. 3The water slowly went back from the earth. After 150 days, the water had gone down a lot. 4On the seventeenth day of the seventh month, the ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat. 5The water kept going down until the tenth month. On the first day of the tenth month, the tops of the mountains could be seen.

Noah Sends Out Birds

6Forty days later, Noah opened the window he had made in the ark. 7He sent out a raven. It flew back and forth until the water dried up from the earth. 8Then he sent out a dove to see if the water had gone down from the ground. 9But the dove could not find a place to rest. So it came back to him in the ark, because water still covered the whole EarthEarthearth. Noah reached out his hand, caught the dove, and brought it back into the ark. 10He waited seven more days and sent the dove out again. 11In the evening the dove came back. It had a fresh olive leaf in its beak. Then Noah knew the water had gone down from the earth. 12He waited seven more days and sent the dove out again, but it did not come back.

The Earth Dries and Noah Leaves the Ark

13In Noah's six hundred and first year, on the first day of the first month, the water had dried up from the earth. Noah took off the cover of the ark and looked out. He saw that the ground was dry. 14By the twenty-seventh day of the second month, the earth was dry.

15Then God spoke to Noah. 16He said, "Go out of the ark. Take your wife, your sons, and your sons' wives with you. 17Bring out with you every living thing that is with you—every bird, animal, and creature that moves on the ground—so they can spread across the earth, have many young, and fill the earth."

18So Noah went out with his wife, his sons, and his sons' wives. 19Every animal, every creature that moves, and every bird—everything that moves on the ground—left the ark by families.

Noah's Offering and God's Promise

20Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took one of every clean animal and clean bird and burned them on the altar.

21The LORD smelled the pleasing smell. Then the LORD said in HishisHis heart, "I will never again curse the ground because of people, because people's hearts are set on evil from the time they are young. I will never again destroy every living thing, as I have done. 22As long as the earth lasts, planting and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, and day and night will not stop."
