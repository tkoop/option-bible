Paul Defends Himself Before King Agrippa

1King Agrippa said to Paul, "YouyouYou may speak for yourself." Then Paul stretched out his hand and defended himself.

2"King Agrippa, I consider myself fortunate to defend myself before you today about all that the Jews accuse me of. 3You are an expert in all the Jewish customs and questions, so I beg you to listen to me patiently.

4"All the Jews know my life from the start, from my youth among my own people and in Jerusalem. 5They have known me for a long time, and if they are willing, they can say that I lived as a Pharisee, following the strictest part of our religion.

6"Now I stand on trial because of the hope in God's promise to our ancestors. 7Our twelve tribes hope to reach that promise as they worship day and night. King, it is because of this hope that the Jews accuse me. 8Why do you think it is unbelievable that God raises the dead?

9"I myself thought I should do many things against the name of Jesus of Nazareth. 10I did this in Jerusalem. I got authority from the chief priests, and I put many holy people in prison. When they were killed, I voted against them. 11I punished them often in every synagogue and tried to make them speak against God. I was very angry with them, and I even chased them to cities outside our land.

Paul Meets Jesus on the Road to Damascus

Paul Preaches Repentance to All People

19"So, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision. 20First to those in Damascus, then in Jerusalem and all Judea, and also to the Gentiles, I preached that they should repent and turn to God, doing works worthy of repentance.

21"For this reason the Jews seized me in the temple and tried to kill me. 22But I have had God's help to this day, and I stand testifying to everyone, from the least to the greatest, saying nothing beyond what the prophets and Moses said would happen: 23that the Christ would suffer, that HeheHe would be the first to rise from the dead, and that HeheHe would proclaim light to the people and to the Gentiles."

Festus and Agrippa React to Paul's DefenceDefenceDefense

24As Paul was saying this in his defencedefencedefense, Festus shouted loudly, "Paul, you are out of your mind! Your great learning is driving you insane."

25Paul said, "I am not insane, most excellent Festus, but I am speaking true and sensible words. 26The king knows about these things, and I speak openly to him. I am sure none of this is hidden from him, because it was not done in secret. 27King Agrippa, do you believe the prophets? I know you believe."

28Agrippa said to Paul, "In a short time you are trying to persuade me to become a Christian."

29Paul replied, "I would pray to God that whether in a short time or a long time, not only you but all who hear me today would become like me, except for these chains."

30Then the king, the governor, Bernice, and those sitting with them got up. 31As they left, they talked to each other and said, "This man has done nothing deserving death or chains."

32Agrippa said to Festus, "This man could have been set free if he had not appealed to Caesar."
