Sarah Dies

1Sarah lived to be one hundred twenty-seven years old. 2She died in Kiriath Arba, which is Hebron, in the land of Canaan. Abraham came to mourn for Sarah and to weep for her.

Abraham Seeks a Burial Place

3Then Abraham got up from beside his dead wife and spoke to the Hittites. He said, 4"I am a foreigner and a stranger among you. Sell me a burial place here so I can bury my dead."

5The Hittites answered Abraham, 6"Listen to us, my lord. You are a great leader among us. Bury your dead in the best of our tombs. None of us will keep you from burying your dead."

7Then Abraham stood up and bowed down to the people of the land, the Hittites. 8He said to them, "If you are willing to let me bury my dead, listen to me and speak to Ephron son of Zohar for me. 9Ask him to sell me the cave of Machpelah at the end of his field. I will pay the full price for it as a burial place among you."

Abraham Buys the Cave of Machpelah

10Ephron the Hittite was sitting among the people. He answered Abraham in front of all the Hittites who had come to the gate of his city. 11"No, my lord," he said. "Listen to me. I give you the field, and I give you the cave in it. I give it to you in front of my people. Bury your dead."

12Again Abraham bowed down to the people of the land. 13He said to Ephron in their hearing, "Listen to me, if you will. I will pay for the field. Take the money from me so I can bury my dead there."

14Ephron answered Abraham, 15"Listen to me, my lord. The land is worth four hundred shekels of silver. But what is that between you and me? Bury your dead."

16Abraham agreed to Ephron's price. He weighed out four hundred shekels of silver for him in front of the Hittites, using the standard weight for merchants. 17So Ephron's field in Machpelah near Mamre was made Abraham's property. The field, the cave in it, and all the trees inside its borders became his. 18This happened in front of all the Hittites who had come to the gate of the city.

19After that, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave in the field of Machpelah near Mamre, which is Hebron, in the land of Canaan. 20So the field and the cave in it were given to Abraham by the Hittites as a burial place.
