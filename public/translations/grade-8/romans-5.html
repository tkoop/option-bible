Peace with God Through Faith

1So, since we have been made right with God by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. 2Through HimhimHim we have also gained access by faith into this grace where we now stand. We boast in the hope of God's glory. 3Not only that, but we also boast in our troubles. We know that trouble produces patience. 4Patience produces character, and character produces hope. 5That hope will not disappoint us, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit, who has been given to us.

God's Love Shown in Christ

6While we were still weak, Christ died for ungodly people at just the right time. 7It is rare for someone to die for a righteous person. Maybe someone would dare to die for a good person. 8But God shows HishisHis love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us. 9Since we have now been made right with God by HishisHis blood, we will be saved from God's anger through HimhimHim even more. 10If we were brought back to God through the death of HishisHis SonsonSon while we were enemies, then we will be saved by HishisHis life even more, now that we are brought back. 11Not only that, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through HimhimHim we have now been brought back to God.

Sin and Death Through Adam

12So, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, death spread to all people because all sinned. 13Sin was in the world before the law was given, but sin is not counted when there is no law. 14Yet death ruled from Adam to Moses, even over people who did not sin in the same way Adam did. Adam is a pattern of the OneoneOne who was to come.

Grace and Life Through Christ

15But God's free gift is not like Adam's sin. If many died because of one man's sin, then God's grace and the gift that come through the grace of one man, Jesus Christ, overflowed to many people even more. 16The gift is not like the result of one man's sin. One sin brought judgmentjudgementjudgment and punishment, but the free gift brings right standing with God after many sins. 17If death ruled because of one man's sin, then those who receive God's rich grace and the gift of being made right with HimhimHim will rule in life through one man, Jesus Christ, even more.

18So, just as one sin brought punishment to all people, one act of doing right brings life and being made right with God to all people. 19Just as one man's disobedience made many people sinners, one man's obedience will make many people right with God. 20The law was added so that sin would increase. But where sin increased, grace increased even more. 21Sin once ruled in death. Now grace rules through being made right with God, leading to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
