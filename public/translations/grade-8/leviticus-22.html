Offerings That Are Acceptable

17The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 18"Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites. Say to them: When any man from the family of Israel or from the foreigners living in Israel brings his offering to the LORD, whether for a special promise or a gift he chooses to give, as a burnt offering to the LORD,

"19you must bring a male animal without a flaw from the cattle, sheep, or goats so it will be accepted for you. 20Do not bring an animal with a flaw, because it will not be accepted for you. 21When a man brings a peace offering to the LORD to keep a promise or as a gift, from the herd or the flock, it must be perfect to be accepted. It must have no flaw. 22Do not bring an animal that is blind, injured, maimed, or has a running sore, a scab, or a rash. Do not place any of these on the altar as a food offering to the LORD. 23An ox or sheep that is deformed or stunted may be given as a freewill offering, but it will not be accepted to keep a promise. 24Do not offer to the LORD an animal that is bruised, crushed, torn, or cut. Do not do this in your land. 25Do not bring the food of your God from a foreigner's hand from any of these animals. Their defects are in them; they have a flaw, so they will not be accepted for you."
