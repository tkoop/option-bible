Dinah Is Violated

1Dinah was Leah’s daughter, whom she had borne to Jacob. Dinah went out to see the young women of the land. 2Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite and ruler of that area, saw her. He took her, lay with her, and forced her. 3But he felt deeply attached to Dinah, Jacob’s daughter. He loved the young woman and spoke kindly to her. 4Shechem said to his father Hamor, “Get this young woman for me as a wife.” 5Jacob heard that Shechem had defiled his daughter Dinah. His sons were out in the fields with the livestock, so Jacob said nothing until they came home.

Hamor and Shechem Ask for Dinah

6Hamor, Shechem’s father, went to Jacob to talk with him. 7Jacob’s sons came in from the fields when they heard what happened. They were deeply hurt and very angry, because Shechem had done a shameful thing in Israel by sleeping with Jacob’s daughter. That should never be done. 8Hamor spoke with them and said, “My son Shechem longs for your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife. 9Marry into our families. Give your daughters to us, and take our daughters for yourselves. 10You can live with us. The land is open before you. Live here, trade here, and buy property here. 11Shechem said to Dinah’s father and brothers, “Let me find favourfavourfavor in your eyes. I’ll give whatever you ask. 12Set the bride price and gift as high as you want; I’ll pay it. Just give me the young woman as a wife.”

13Jacob’s sons answered Shechem and his father Hamor deceitfully, because Shechem had defiled Dinah their sister. 14They said to them, “We can’t do this. We can’t give our sister to a man who isn’t circumcised, because that would bring shame on us. 15We will agree only if you become like us by having every male among you circumcised. 16Then we’ll give our daughters to you, and we’ll take your daughters for ourselves. We’ll live with you and become one people. 17But if you won’t listen to us and be circumcised, then we’ll take our daughter and leave.” 18Hamor and Shechem, son of Hamor, were pleased with their words. 19The young man didn’t delay doing this, because he delighted in Jacob’s daughter. He was the most honouredhonouredhonored man in his father’s house.

The Men of the City Are Circumcised

20Hamor and Shechem went to the gate of their city and spoke to the men there. They said, “21These men are peaceful toward us. Let them live in the land and trade in it. The land is wide enough for them. We can take their daughters as wives and give them our daughters. 22But they will agree to live with us and become one people only on this condition: every male among us must be circumcised, just as they are. 23Won’t their livestock, property, and all their animals become ours? So let’s agree with them, and they’ll live with us.” 24All the men who went out through the city gate listened to Hamor and Shechem. Every male was circumcised.

Simeon and Levi Take Revenge

25On the third day, when the men were still sore, two of Jacob’s sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brothers, took their swords. They came into the city boldly and killed every male. 26They killed Hamor and Shechem with the sword, took Dinah from Shechem’s house, and left. 27Jacob’s other sons came upon the dead bodies and plundered the city because their sister had been defiled. 28They took the flocks, herds, donkeys, and everything else in the city and in the fields. 29They carried off all the wealth, wives, and children, and plundered everything in the houses.

30Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me by making me hated among the people of this land, the Canaanites and Perizzites. I have only a few men. They may gather against me and attack me, and then I and my household will be destroyed.”
