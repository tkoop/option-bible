Ezra Hears About the People's Sin

1After these things, when the leaders had finished their work, they came to me and said, "ThetheThe people of Israel, including the priests and Levites, have not kept themselves separate from the peoples of the lands. They have followed the disgusting practices of the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites. 2They have taken some of their daughters as wives for themselves and their sons. So the holy seed has mixed with the peoples of the lands. Even worse, the officials and leaders were the first to do this."

3When I heard this, I tore my clothes and my robe. I pulled hair from my head and beard, and I sat there shocked. 4Everyone who trembled at the words of the God of Israel gathered around me because of the sin of the exiles. I sat there shocked until the evening sacrifice.

Ezra Prays to the LORD

5At the evening sacrifice, I rose from my fasting with my clothes and robe torn. I fell to my knees and spread out my hands to the LORD my God. 6I said, "O my God, I am too ashamed and disgraced to lift up my face to YouyouYou, my God, because our sins are piled higher than our heads and our guilt has reached the heavens. 7Since the days of our ancestors, we have been deeply guilty. Because of our sins, we, our kings, and our priests have been handed over to the kings of other lands. We have faced the sword, captivity, robbery, and public shame, just as we do today. 8But now, for a short time, the LORD our God has shown us grace. He has left us a remnant and given us a secure place in HishisHis holy place. Our God has given us new hope and a little strength in our slavery. 9We are slaves, but our God has not abandoned us in our slavery. He has shown us kindness before the kings of Persia. He gave us new life to rebuild the house of our God, repair its ruins, and give us a protective wall in Judah and Jerusalem. 10And now, our God, what can we say after all this? We have disobeyed YouryourYour commands.

Do not seek their peace or prosperity forever. Then you will be strong, enjoy the good things of the land, and leave it to your children forever.' 13After all that has happened to us because of our evil deeds and great guilt, YouyouYou, our God, have punished us less than we deserved. You have left us this remnant. 14Should we break YouryourYour commands again and marry into these peoples who do such disgusting things? Would YouyouYou not be angry enough to destroy us completely, leaving no remnant or escape? 15O the LORD, God of Israel, YouyouYou are righteous. That is why we are left as a remnant today. Here we are before YouyouYou in our guilt. No one can stand before YouyouYou because of this."
