Manasseh's Evil Reign

1Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he ruled fifty-five years in Jerusalem. 2He did evil in the sight of the LORD, following the disgusting practices of the nations the LORD had driven out before the Israelites. 3He rebuilt the high places his father Hezekiah had torn down. He set up altars for the Baals, made Asherah poles, and bowed down to worship and serve all the stars in the sky.

4He built altars in the the LORD's house, even though the LORD had said, "MymyMy name will be in Jerusalem forever." 5He built altars for all the stars in the sky in the two courtyards of the the LORD's house. 6He sacrificed his sons by fire in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom. He practisedpractisedpracticed fortune-telling, magic, and sorcery, and he talked with mediums and spirits. He did many evil things that made the the LORD angry.

7He even put a carved idol, which he had made, in God's house. God had said to David and his son Solomon, "In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, I will put MymyMy name forever. 8I will not again remove Israel from the land I gave your ancestors, if only they are careful to obey all I commanded them through Moses—all the law, the rules, and the commands." 9Manasseh led Judah and the people of Jerusalem to do more evil than the nations the the LORD had destroyed before the Israelites.

God Disciplines Manasseh

10The LORD spoke to Manasseh and his people, but they would not listen. 11So the the LORD brought the army commanders of the king of Assyria against them. They captured Manasseh with hooks, tied him with bronze chains, and took him to Babylon. 12While he was suffering, he begged the the LORD his God and humbled himself greatly before the God of his ancestors.

13He prayed to the the LORD, and the the LORD answered him. He heard Manasseh's prayer and brought him back to Jerusalem and put him back on his throne. Then Manasseh knew that the the LORD is God.

Manasseh Turns Back to God

14After that, he built an outer wall for the CitycityCity of David west of Gihon in the valley, reaching to the FishfishFish GategateGate. He made the wall around Ophel much higher. He also placed army commanders in all the fortified cities of Judah. 15He removed the foreign gods and the idol from the the LORD's house. He also removed all the altars he had built on the temple hill and in Jerusalem, and he threw them outside the city.

16He repaired the the LORD's altar and offered peace offerings and thank offerings on it. He told Judah to serve the the LORD, the God of Israel. 17But the people still sacrificed on the high places, though only to the the LORD their God. 18The rest of Manasseh's deeds, his prayer to God, and the words of the prophets who spoke to him in the name of the the LORD, the God of Israel, are written in the records of the kings of Israel.

19His prayer and how God was moved by his request, all his sin and wrongdoing, and the places where he built high places and set up wooden poles and carved idols before he humbled himself, are written in the records of the prophets. 20Manasseh died and was buried in his own house. Then his son Amon became king in his place.

Amon's Short Reign

21Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled two years in Jerusalem. 22But he did evil in the the LORD's sight, as his father Manasseh had done. Amon sacrificed to all the carved idols his father Manasseh had made, and he served them. 23He did not humble himself before the the LORD as his father Manasseh had. Instead, Amon sinned more and more.

24Then his servants plotted against him and killed him in his own house. 25But the people of the land killed all those who had plotted against King Amon. Then they made his son Josiah king in his place.
