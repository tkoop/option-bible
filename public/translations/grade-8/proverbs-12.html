Wisdom and Character

1Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but whoever hates correction is stupid. 2A good man wins favourfavourfavor from the the LORD, but the the LORD condemns a man of evil plans. 3No one is made secure by wickedness, but the righteous won't be moved. 4A wife of noble character is her husband's crown, but a shameful wife is like rot in his bones.

5The plans of the righteous are just, but the advice of the wicked is deceitful. 6The words of wicked people set traps to harm others, but the speech of honest people rescues them. 7The wicked are overthrown and are no more, but the house of the righteous stands firm.

Words and Their Power

8A person is praised for his wisdom, but a twisted heart is despised. 9It is better to be lightly regarded and have a servant than to act important and have no food. 10A righteous person cares about the life of his animal, but the kindness of wicked people is cruel. 11Whoever works his land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases empty dreams lacks good sense.

12The wicked want the loot of evil men, but the roots of the righteous grow strong. 13An evil man is trapped by his rebellious speech, but a righteous man escapes trouble. 14A person is filled with good things from the fruit of his mouth, and the work of his hands comes back to him.

Work, Wealth, and the Heart

15The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise man listens to advice. 16A fool shows his anger right away, but a wise person ignores an insult. 17A person who speaks truth tells what is right, but a false witness speaks deceit. 18Careless words stab like a sword, but the tongue of the wise brings healing.

19Truthful lips last forever, but a lying tongue lasts only a moment. 20Deceit is in the hearts of those who plan evil, but those who advise peace have joy. 21No harm comes to the righteous, but wicked people are filled with trouble.

Righteous Living

22Lying lips are disgusting to the the LORD, but those who act faithfully are the the LORD's delight. 23A careful person keeps knowledge to himself, but the heart of fools proclaims foolishness. 24Hardworking hands will rule, but laziness ends in forced labourlabourlabor. 25Anxiety weighs down the heart, but a kind word cheers it up.

26The righteous choose their friends carefully, but the way of the wicked leads them astray. 27A lazy person does not roast his game, but a hardworking person values his possessions. 28In the path of righteousness there is life, and along that way there is no death.
