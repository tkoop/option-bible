God Is Our Refuge and Strength

For the director of music. A song of the sons of Korah. To the tune of Alamoth.

1God is our refuge and strength. He is always ready to help when we are in trouble. 2So we will not be afraid, even if the earth changes and mountains crash into the sea. 3Even if the water roars and foams and the mountains tremble as the seas rise. Selah.

4A river with streams that bring joy to God's city, the holy place where the MostmostMost HighhighHigh lives. 5God is in that city, so it will not be shaken. God will help it at dawn.

6Nations are in uproar and kingdoms fall. He speaks, and the earth melts away. 7The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah.
