A Prophecy Against Babylon

1This is a message against Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz saw. 2"Raise a banner on a bare hill. Raise your voice to them. Wave your hand, and let them enter the gates of the nobles. 3I have commanded MymyMy holy ones. I have also called MymyMy mighty men to carry out MymyMy anger—those who rejoice in MymyMy victory."

4Listen to the roar on the mountains, like a great crowd! Listen to the uproar among the kingdoms, like nations gathering together! The LORD of Hosts is gathering an army for war. 5They come from a faraway land, from the ends of the heavens—the LORD and the weapons of HishisHis wrath—to destroy the whole EarthEarthearth.

The Day of the LORD

6"Wail, for the day of the LORD is near. It will come like destruction from the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty."

7So all hands will go limp, and every heart will melt. 8They will be terrified. Pain and sorrow will seize them. They will twist in pain like a woman giving birth. They will stare at each other, their faces burning with fear.

9See, the day of the LORD is coming—cruel, with fury and burning anger—to make the earth a wasteland and destroy its sinners. 10The stars of Heavenheavenheaven and their constellations will not give their light. The sun will be dark when it rises, and the moon will not shine.

11"I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their sins. I will end the pride of the arrogant and bring down the pride of the ruthless. 12I will make people rarer than pure gold, and mankind rarer than the gold of Ophir. 13So I will shake the heavens, and the earth will move from its place, because of the wrath of the LORD of Hosts and the day of HishisHis burning anger."

14Like a hunted deer and like sheep with no shepherd, each person will turn to his own people and run to his own land. 15Whoever is found will be stabbed. Whoever is caught will fall by the sword. 16Their babies will be smashed before their eyes. Their houses will be looted, and their wives will be abused.

Babylon Will Be Destroyed

17"See, I will stir up the Medes against them. They will not care about silver or want gold. 18Their bows will strike down the young men. They will have no mercy on babies, and they will not spare children."

19Babylon, the jewel of kingdoms, the glory and pride of the Chaldeans, will be like Sodom and Gomorrah when God destroyed them. 20It will never be lived in or settled again. No Arab will pitch a tent there, and no shepherd will make his flock lie down there. 21Desert animals will lie there. Owls will fill its houses. Ostriches will live there, and wild goats will jump around. 22Hyenas will howl in its forts, and jackals will live in its fancy palaces. Its time is near, and its days will not be long.
