Zophar Answers Job

1Then Zophar the Naamathite answered:

2“Should a lot of words go unanswered? Can a person who talks too much be proven right? 3Will your empty words make people quiet? Will you mock with no one to correct you? 4You say, ‘My teaching is pure, and I am clean in YouryourYour eyes.’

God’s Wisdom Is Beyond Understanding

5“But if only God would speak and open HishisHis lips against you, 6HeheHe would show you the secrets of wisdom, for true wisdom has two sides. Then you would know that God makes you pay less than your sin deserves. 7Can you discover the deep things of God? Can you find the limits of the AlmightyalmightyAlmighty? 8They are higher than the heavens—what can you do? They are deeper than the grave—what can you know? 9God’s wisdom is longer than the earth and wider than the sea. 10If HeheHe passes by and locks you up, or calls a court together, who can stop HimhimHim? 11He surely knows deceitful people. When HeheHe sees evil, HeheHe does not overlook it. 12A foolish person can gain understanding only when a wild donkey is born human.

Job Can Find Hope by Turning to God

13“If you prepare your heart and stretch out your hands to HimhimHim, 14if you put away the sin in your hand and let no evil stay in your tent, 15then you will lift up your face without shame; you will stand firm and unafraid. 16You will forget your misery; you will remember it like water that has passed by. 17Life will be brighter than noon; its darkness will be like morning. 18You will be secure because there is hope; you will look around and rest in safety. 19You will lie down without fear, and many will seek your favourfavourfavor. 20But the eyes of the wicked will fail, and escape will slip away from them; their hope will become a dying breath.”
