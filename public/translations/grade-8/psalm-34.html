David Praises the LORD for Rescue

1I will bless the LORD at all times; HishisHis praise will always be on my lips. 2My soul will boast in the LORD; let those who are hurting hear and be glad. 3Magnify the LORD with me; let us lift up HishisHis name together.

4I sought the LORD, and HeheHe answered me; HeheHe rescued me from all my fears. 5Those who look to HimhimHim are radiant with joy; their faces will never be ashamed. 6This poor man cried out, and the LORD heard him; HeheHe saved him from all his troubles. 7The angel of the LORD sets up camp around those who fear HimhimHim, and he rescues them.

8Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the person who takes refuge in HimhimHim. 9Fear the LORD, you HishisHis holy people, for those who fear HimhimHim lack nothing. 10Young lions may grow weak and hungry, but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing.

11Come, my children, listen to me; I will teach you to fear the LORD. 12Who is the man who wants life and loves to see good days? 13Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from lying words. 14Turn from evil and do good; seek peace and chase after it.

15The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous, and HishisHis ears are open to their cry. 16The face of the LORD is against those who do evil, to remove their memory from the earth. 17The righteous cry out, and the LORD hears; HeheHe rescues them from all their troubles. 18The LORD is close to the brokenhearted; HeheHe saves those whose spirits are crushed. 19The righteous face many troubles, but the LORD rescues them from all of them. 20He guards all their bones; not one of them is broken. 21Evil will kill the wicked, and those who hate the righteous will be condemned. 22The LORD redeems the lives of HishisHis servants; no one who takes refuge in HimhimHim will be condemned.
