Jethro Hears About God’s Work

1Now Jethro, the priest of Midian and Moses’ father-in-law, heard about everything God had done for Moses and for HishisHis people Israel. He heard how the the LORD had brought Israel out of Egypt. 2After Moses had sent his wife Zipporah away, his father-in-law Jethro took her back. 3He also brought her two sons. One son was named Gershom, because Moses had said, “I have been a stranger in a foreign land.” 4The other son was named Eliezer, because Moses had said, “My father’s God was my helper. He saved me from Pharaoh’s sword.”

Jethro Visits Moses

5Jethro, Moses’ father-in-law, came to Moses in the wilderness where he was camped at the mountain of God. He brought Moses’ wife and sons with him. 6Jethro sent a message to Moses: “I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you, along with your wife and her two sons.”

7Moses went out to meet his father-in-law. He bowed down and kissed him. They greeted each other and went into the tent.

8Moses told his father-in-law everything the the LORD had done to Pharaoh and the Egyptians for Israel’s sake. He also told him about all the hardships they had faced on the way, and how the the LORD had saved them. 9Jethro was glad about all the good things the the LORD had done for Israel, whom HeheHe had rescued from the Egyptians’ power.

10Jethro said, “Praise the the LORD, who rescued you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of Pharaoh, and who rescued the people from under the Egyptians’ power. 11Now I know that the the LORD is greater than all other gods, because HeheHe showed HishisHis power when they acted proudly against HishisHis people.”

12Then Jethro, Moses’ father-in-law, brought a burnt offering and sacrifices to God. Aaron and all the elders of Israel came to eat a meal with Moses’ father-in-law in God’s presence.

Moses Judges the People

13The next day Moses sat down to judge the people, and they stood around him from morning until evening.

14When Moses’ father-in-law saw all that Moses was doing for the people, he said, “What is this you are doing for the people? Why do you sit alone, while all the people stand around you from morning until evening?”

15Moses answered his father-in-law, “The people come to me to seek God’s will. 16Whenever they have a dispute, they come to me. I judge between them, and I teach them God’s rules and HishisHis instructions.”

Jethro Gives Moses Advice

17Moses’ father-in-law said to him, “What you are doing is not good. 18You and the people with you will surely wear yourselves out, because this work is too heavy for you. You cannot do it alone.

19“Now listen to me. I will give you advice, and God will be with you. You must be the people’s representative before God and bring their concerns to HimhimHim. 20Teach them the rules and laws, and show them the way they should live and the work they should do. 21But choose capable men from all the people—men who fear God, are trustworthy, and hate dishonest gain. Put them in charge of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 22Let them judge the people at all times. They will bring every difficult case to you, but they will decide the smaller cases themselves. That will make your load lighter, and they will share it with you. 23If you do this, and God so commands you, then you will be able to stand, and all these people will go to their place in peace.”

24Moses listened to his father-in-law and did everything he said.

25So Moses chose capable men from all Israel and made them leaders over the people—leaders of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens. 26They judged the people at all times. They brought the hard cases to Moses, but they handled the smaller cases themselves.

27Then Moses sent his father-in-law on his way, and Jethro returned to his own country.
