David Grows Old and Needs Care

1King David was very old.

2His servants said to him, "Let us find a young virgin for my lord the king. She will stand before the king and care for him. She will lie in your bosom, my lord the king, and keep you warm."

3They searched all over Israel for a beautiful young woman. They found Abishag the Shunammite and brought her to the king. 4The young woman was very beautiful. She cared for the king and served him, but the king did not have sexual relations with her.

Adonijah Tries to Become King

5Adonijah son of Haggith made himself important. He said, "I will be king." He got chariots, horsemen, and fifty men to run before him. 6His father had never corrected him or asked, "Why did you do that?" Adonijah was also very handsome. He was born after Absalom.

7He spoke with Joab son of Zeruiah and Abiathar the priest. They supported Adonijah. 8But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei, and David's mighty men did not join Adonijah.

9Adonijah sacrificed sheep, oxen, and fattened calves near the stone of Zoheleth by En Rogel. He invited all his brothers, the king's sons, and all the men of Judah who served the king. 10But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the mighty men, or his brother Solomon.

Nathan and Bathsheba Warn David

11Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, "Have you not heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king? Our lord David does not know it." 12"Now come, let me give you advice. Save your own life and the life of your son Solomon." 13"Go to King David and say, 'My lord the king, didn't you swear to your servant that my son Solomon would be king after you and sit on your throne? Why then has Adonijah become king?'" 14"While you are still there speaking with the king, I will come in after you and support what you have said."

15Bathsheba went to the king in his room. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was serving him. 16Bathsheba bowed low before the king. The king said, "What do you want?"

17She said to him, "MymyMy lord, you swore to your servant by the LORD your God that your son Solomon would be king after me and sit on my throne." 18"Now Adonijah has become king, and my lord the king does not know it." 19"He has sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. He invited all the king's sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab the army commander. But he did not invite your servant Solomon." 20"My lord the king, all Israel is watching you to see who will sit on your throne after you." 21"When my lord the king dies, I and my son Solomon will be punished."

22While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet came in.

23They told the king, "Nathan the prophet is here." He came in and bowed with his face to the ground before the king.

24Nathan said, "MymyMy lord the king, did you say that Adonijah would be king after you and sit on your throne?" 25"Today he went down and sacrificed many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep. He invited all the king's sons, the army commanders, and Abiathar the priest. Now they are eating and drinking with him and saying, 'Long live King Adonijah!'" 26"But he did not invite me, Zadok the priest, Benaiah your servant, or your son Solomon." 27"Did my lord the king do this without telling his servants who should sit on your throne after you?"

28King David answered, "Call Bathsheba to me." She came and stood before the king.

David Promises the Throne to Solomon

29The king swore, "As the LORD lives, who has saved me from every trouble, 30I will do today what I swore to you by the LORD, the God of Israel: Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne in my place." 31Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground and said, "May my lord King David live forever!"

Solomon Is Anointed King

32King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me." They came. 33The king said to them, "Take your lord's servants with you. Put my son Solomon on my own mule and take him down to Gihon." 34"There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet will anoint him as king over Israel. Blow the trumpet and say, 'Long live King Solomon!'" 35"Then come up after him, and he will come and sit on my throne. He will be king in my place. I have appointed him ruler over Israel and Judah."

36Benaiah answered, "AmenamenAmen! May the LORD God of my lord the king say so too." 37"As the LORD has been with my lord the king, so may HeheHe be with Solomon. May HeheHe make Solomon's throne greater than my lord the king's throne."

38So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and Pelethites went down. They put Solomon on King David's mule and brought him to Gihon. 39Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon. They blew the trumpet, and all the people said, "Long live King Solomon!" 40All the people followed him, playing flutes and rejoicing with great joy. The EarthEarthearth shook with their noise.

41Adonijah and all his guests heard it as they finished eating. When Joab heard the trumpet, he asked, "Why is the city so noisy?"

42While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a brave man and bring good news."

43Jonathan answered Adonijah, "No! Our lord King David has made Solomon king." 44"The king sent Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and Pelethites. They put Solomon on the king's mule." 45"Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king at Gihon. They went up from there rejoicing, and the city is in an uproar. That is the noise you heard." 46"Solomon has also sat on the royal throne." 47"The king's servants came to bless our lord King David, saying, 'May God make Solomon's name greater than yours, and his throne greater than your throne.' Then the king bowed on his bed."

48The king said, "BlessedblessedBlessed be the LORD God of Israel, who has given me someone to sit on my throne today while I can still see it."

49All of Adonijah's guests were afraid. They got up and went their own ways. 50Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up and went to hold on to the horns of the altar.

51Someone told Solomon that Adonijah was afraid of King Solomon and was holding on to the horns of the altar, asking Solomon to swear not to kill him with the sword.

52Solomon said, "If he proves to be a worthy man, not a hair of his head will fall to the ground. But if he is found to be wicked, he will die." 53King Solomon sent for him. They brought him down from the altar. He came and bowed before King Solomon. Solomon said to him, "Go home."
