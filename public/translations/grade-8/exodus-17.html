The People Need Water

1All the Israelites travelledtravelledtraveled from the DesertdesertDesert of Sin, moving from place to place as the LORD had commanded. They camped at Rephidim, but there was no water for the people to drink.

2So the people argued with Moses. They said, “Give us water to drink.”

Moses replied, “Why are you arguing with me? Why are you testing the LORD?”

3The people were thirsty there, and they complained against Moses. They said, “Why did you bring us up out of Egypt? Was it to kill us, our children, and our livestock with thirst?”

4Moses cried out to the LORD, “What should I do with these people? They are almost ready to stone me.”

5The LORD said to Moses, “Go on ahead of the people. Take some of the elders of Israel with you, and take in your hand the staff you used to strike the Nile. Then go. 6I will stand there before you on the rock at Horeb. Strike the rock, and water will come out of it for the people to drink.” So Moses did this in the sight of the elders of Israel.

7He named the place Massah and Meribah because the Israelites quarreled and tested the LORD by saying, “Is the LORD among us or not?”

Amalek Attacks Israel

8The Amalekites came and attacked the Israelites at Rephidim. 9Moses said to Joshua, “Choose some men for us and go out to fight the Amalekites. Tomorrow I will stand on top of the hill with God’s staff in my hand.”

10So Joshua did what Moses told him and fought the Amalekites. Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill. 11As long as Moses held up his hand, the Israelites were winning. But when he lowered his hand, the Amalekites were winning. 12Moses’ hands became tired. So they took a stone and put it under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur held up his hands, one on each side. His hands stayed steady until sunset. 13So Joshua defeated the Amalekites and their army with the sword.

God’s Promise and Altar

14Then the LORD said to Moses, “Write this down in a book as a reminder, and tell it to Joshua: I will completely wipe out the memory of Amalek from under Heavenheavenheaven.”

15Moses built an altar and named it “The LORD is my banner.”

16He said, “Because the LORD has sworn that the LORD will be at war against the Amalekites from generation to generation.”
