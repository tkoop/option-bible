Solomon Finishes the Temple Work

1All the work Solomon did for the house of the LORD was finished. Solomon brought in the things his father David had set apart for God. He put the silver, gold, and all the utensils into the storerooms of God's house.

The Ark Is Brought to Jerusalem

2Then Solomon gathered the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the families, in Jerusalem. They were to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the CitycityCity of David, which is Zion. 3All the men of Israel came together to the king at the feast in the seventh month. 4When all the elders of Israel had come, the Levites lifted up the ark. 5They brought up the ark, the tent of meeting, and all the holy items that were in the tent. The Levite priests brought them up. 6King Solomon and all the people of Israel who had gathered before the ark sacrificed sheep and oxen. There were so many that no one could count them.

That was where the LORD made a covenant with the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

The Priests and Singers Praise God

11The priests came out of the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace. All the priests who were there had set themselves apart for God's service, so they did not stay in their assigned groups. 12The Levite singers—Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and relatives—stood east of the altar. They wore fine linen and played cymbals, harps, and lyres. With them stood 120 priests blowing trumpets. 13The trumpeters and singers joined together to praise and thank the LORD with one voice. They raised their voices with trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments as they praised the LORD. They sang, “For HeheHe is good, for HishisHis faithful love endures forever.” Then the house of the LORD was filled with a cloud. 14The priests could not stand to do their work because of the cloud. The glory of the LORD filled the house of God.
