Be Careful About Teaching

1My brothers, not many of you should become teachers, because we who teach will be judged more strictly. 2We all stumble in many ways. If someone never slips in what they say, that person is mature and can control their whole body.

The Tongue Is Small but Powerful

3When we put a bit in a horse's mouth, it obeys us, and we can steer its whole body. 4Look at ships. They may be huge and pushed by strong winds, but a tiny rudder turns them wherever the pilot wants. 5In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it boasts about big things. See how a tiny spark can set a huge fire.

6The tongue is like a fire. It is a world of evil among the parts of our body. It stains the whole body, sets life's path on fire, and is itself set on fire by HellHellhell. 7People can tame all kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and sea creatures. 8But no one can tame the tongue. It is an unstable evil, full of deadly poison.

9With it we bless our Lord and FatherfatherFather, and with it we curse people who are made in God's likeness. 10Blessing and cursing both come from the same mouth. My brothers, this should not happen. 11Can a spring send out both fresh water and bitter water from the same opening? 12My brothers, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine grow figs? No, and a salty spring cannot give fresh water.

True Wisdom Comes from God

13Who among you is wise and understanding? Show it by living well and doing good things with gentle wisdom.

14But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not brag about it or lie against the truth. 15That kind of "wisdom" is not from above. It is earthly, unspiritual, and demonic. 16Where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there is disorder and every kind of evil practice.

17But wisdom from above is first pure, then peace-loving, gentle, willing to yield, full of mercy and good fruit, fair and sincere. 18People who make peace plant seeds of peace, and they harvest a crop of right living.
