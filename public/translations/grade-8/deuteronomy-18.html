Provision for the Priests and Levites

1The Levite priests—that is, the whole tribe of Levi—will have no share or land of their own among the Israelites. They will eat the offerings made by fire to the LORD, because that is their inheritance. 2They will have no inheritance among their brothers. The LORD is their inheritance, just as HeheHe promised them.

3This is what the priests are owed by the people who bring a sacrifice, whether a bull or a sheep: they must give the priest the shoulder, the cheeks, and the stomach. 4You must give him the first share of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the first wool sheared from your flock. 5For the LORD your God has chosen him and his sons from all your tribes to stand and serve in the LORD’s name for all time.

Levites Who Come to Serve

6If a Levite moves from any of your towns in Israel where he lives and comes with all his heart to the place the LORD will choose, 7he may serve in the name of the LORD his God, like all his fellow Levites who serve there before the LORD. 8They will eat equal shares, except for what he receives from selling his family’s property.

Stay Away from Forbidden Practices

9When you enter the land the LORD your God is giving you, do not learn to follow the hateful practices of those nations. 10Let no one be found among you who sacrifices his son or daughter in the fire, or who practiseswho practiseswho practices fortune-telling or sorcery, or reads omens, or does witchcraft. 11No one among you should cast spells, or be a medium or someone who speaks with spirits, or ask the dead for answers.

12Anyone who does these things is hateful to the LORD. Because of these hateful practices, the LORD your God is driving those nations out before you. 13You must be blameless before the LORD your God. 14The nations you are driving out listen to fortune-tellers, but as for you, the LORD your God has not allowed you to do so.

The Prophet God Will Send

15The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to him.

16This is what you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day the people gathered, when you said, "Let us not hear the voice of the LORD our God or see this great fire anymore, so that we will not die."

17Then the LORD said to me, "They have spoken well. 18I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put MymyMy words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him. 19I will hold accountable anyone who does not listen to MymyMy words that the prophet speaks in MymyMy name. 20But if a prophet speaks on his own authority in MymyMy name a word I did not command him to speak, or speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must be put to death."

How to Test a Prophet

21You may ask in your heart, "How can we know when a message has not been spoken by the LORD?" 22When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD and the message does not happen or come true, that is a message the LORD has not spoken. The prophet spoke on his own authority. Do not be afraid of him.
