The LORD's Message About Babylon

1This is the word the LORD spoke through Jeremiah the prophet about Babylon and the land of the Chaldeans. 2"Announce and declare it to the nations. Lift up a banner and proclaim it. Do not hide it. Say, 'Babylon is captured. Bel is disgraced. Marduk is broken. Her false gods are disgraced. Her statues are broken.'" 3A nation from the north will attack her. It will turn her land into a wasteland, and no one will live there. People and animals will run away.

Israel and Judah Will Return

4"In those days and at that time," says the LORD, "the people of Israel and the people of Judah will come together. They will walk along weeping, and they will look for the LORD their God." 5They will ask the way to Zion and turn their faces toward it. They will come and join the LORD in a lasting agreement that will never be forgotten.

6"My people have been like lost sheep. Their shepherds led them the wrong way, turning them away on the mountains. They wandered from mountain to hill. They forgot where to rest."

7All who found them ate them up. Their enemies said, "We are not guilty, because they sinned against the LORD, their true home, the LORD, the hope of their fathers." 8Flee from Babylon! Leave the land of the Chaldeans, and be like the male goats that lead the flock.

Babylon Will Be Destroyed

9Look, I will stir up and bring against Babylon a large group of nations from the north. They will line up against her, and from there she will be captured. Their arrows will be like a skilled soldier's; none will come back empty. 10"Chaldea will be plundered. All who plunder her will be satisfied," says the LORD.

11"Because you rejoice and celebrate, O plunderers of MymyMy people, because you jump around like a young cow treading grain and neigh like horses, 12your mother will be very ashamed. The woman who gave birth to you will be disgraced. Look, she will become the least of the nations, a wasteland, a dry land, and a desert."

13Because of the LORD's anger, Babylon will not be lived in. It will be completely ruined. Everyone who passes by Babylon will be shocked and whistle at all her wounds. 14Line up against Babylon on every side, all you who bend the bow. Shoot at her. Do not spare any arrows, because she has sinned against the LORD. 15Shout against her on every side! She has given up. Her towers have fallen. Her walls are torn down. This is the LORD's revenge. Take revenge on her. Do to her what she did to others.

16Cut off the farmer from Babylon, and the one who uses the sickle at harvest time. Because of the sword of the enemy, each person will turn to his own people, and each will run to his own land. 17Israel is a scattered flock. Lions drove him away. The first to eat him up was the king of Assyria. The last to crush his bones was Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

The LORD Will Free His People and Punish Babylon

18So this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "Look, I will punish the king of Babylon and his land, just as I punished the king of Assyria. 19I will bring Israel back to his pasture. He will feed on Carmel and Bashan. He will be satisfied on the hills of Ephraim and Gilead."

20"In those days and at that time," says the LORD, "Israel's guilt will be searched for, but it will not be found. Judah's sins will not be found either, because I will forgive the people I leave behind."

21"Go up against the land of Merathaim and against the people of Pekod. Put them to the sword and destroy them completely," says the LORD. "Do everything I have commanded you." 22The sound of battle is in the land. It is a great destruction. 23How the hammer of the whole EarthEarthearth has been cut down and broken! How Babylon has become a horror among the nations!

24I set a trap for you, Babylon, and you were caught, but you did not know it. You were found and captured, because you fought against the LORD. 25The LORD has opened HishisHis storehouse and brought out HishisHis weapons of anger. This is the work of the Lord, the LORD of Armies, in the land of the Chaldeans. 26Come against her from the farthest border. Open her storehouses. Pile her up like heaps of grain. Destroy her completely. Leave her no survivors.

27Kill all her young bulls with the sword. Let them go down to the slaughter. How terrible for them! Their day has come, the time of their punishment. 28Listen to the people running away from Babylon. In Zion they tell about the LORD our God's revenge, the revenge for HishisHis temple. 29Summon the archers against Babylon, all who bend the bow. Camp around her. Let no one escape. Pay her back for what she did. Do to her as she did to others, because she defied the LORD, the HolyholyHoly OneoneOne of Israel. 30So her young men will fall in her streets. All her soldiers will be silenced in that day, says the LORD.

31"Look, I am against you, you proud one," says the Lord, the LORD of Armies, "because your day has come, the time of your punishment. 32The proud one will trip and fall, with no one to help her up. I will set fire to her cities, and it will burn all around her."

33This is what the LORD of Armies says: "The people of Israel are treated badly, and the people of Judah too. All who captured them hold them tight and refuse to let them go." 34Their Redeemer is strong. The LORD of Armies is HishisHis name. He will surely argue their case. He will bring rest to the land, but trouble to the people of Babylon.

35A sword is against the Chaldeans, says the LORD, and against the people of Babylon, and against her leaders and wise men. 36A sword is against her fortune-tellers, and they will act like fools. A sword is against her soldiers, and they will be terrified. 37A sword is against her horses and chariots and against all the foreigners among her. They will become like women. A sword is against her treasures, and they will be stolen. 38A drought is on her waters, and they will dry up. She is a land of idols. Her people go crazy over idols.

39So desert animals and hyenas will live there, and ostriches will live in it. It will never be lived in again, from one generation to the next. 40As God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns near them, says the LORD, "no man will live there. No son of man will stay in it."

41Look, a people is coming from the north. A great nation and many kings are stirred up from the ends of the earth. 42They carry bow and spear. They are cruel and show no mercy. Their voice roars like the sea. They ride on horses. They are lined up like a man ready for battle against you, DaughterdaughterDaughter of Babylon. 43The king of Babylon has heard the report about them, and his hands become weak. Fear grips him. Pain grips him like a woman in labourlabourlabor. 44Look, he will come up like a lion from the thick brush of the Jordan to a rich pasture. In an instant I will chase them away from her, and I will choose whoever I want to rule over her. Who is like MemeMe? Who can challenge MemeMe? What shepherd can stand against MemeMe?

45So listen to the plan the LORD has made against Babylon and the thoughts HeheHe has had against the land of the Chaldeans. Surely the young sheep will be dragged away. Surely their pasture will be shocked at them. 46At the sound of Babylon's fall, the earth will shake, and a cry will be heard among the nations.
