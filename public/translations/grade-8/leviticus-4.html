Rules for Sin Offerings

1The LORD said to Moses, 2“Speak to the Israelites and say: If someone sins without meaning to and breaks one of the LORD’s commands by doing something that should not be done, he is still guilty.

3If the anointed priest sins and brings guilt on the people, he must bring a young bull without any flaw to the LORD as a sin offering for the sin he committed. 4He must bring the bull to the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, in front of the LORD. He must lay his hand on the bull’s head and kill the bull in front of the LORD.

5The anointed priest must take some of the bull’s blood and bring it into the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 6He must dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of it seven times in front of the LORD, in front of the curtain of the holy place. 7The priest must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense that is in front of the LORD in the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. He must pour out the rest of the bull’s blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting.

8He must remove all the fat from the bull for the sin offering: the fat that covers the inner parts and all the fat around them. 9He must also remove the two kidneys with the fat on them near the hips, and the long part of the liver, which he removes with the kidneys. 10He must do this just as the fat is removed from the bull in the peace offering. Then the priest must burn these parts on the altar of burnt offering.

11But he must take the bull’s skin, all its meat, its head, its legs, its inner parts, and its dung. 12He must carry the whole bull outside the camp to a clean place, the ash heap. He must burn it there on wood. It must be burned on the ash heap.

When the Whole Community Sins

13If all the Israelites sin without knowing it and do something the LORD has commanded them not to do, they are guilty. 14When they learn about the sin they committed, the community must bring a young bull as a sin offering and bring it in front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 15The elders of the community must lay their hands on the bull’s head in front of the LORD, and someone must kill the bull in front of the LORD.

16Then the anointed priest must bring some of the bull’s blood into the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 17He must dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times in front of the LORD, in front of the curtain. 18He must put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is in front of the LORD in the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. He must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting.

19He must take all the fat from the bull and burn it on the altar. 20He must do with this bull just as he did with the bull for the sin offering. In this way the priest makes atonement for them, and they are forgiven. 21Then he must carry the bull outside the camp and burn it, just as he burned the first bull. It is the sin offering for the community.

When a Leader Sins

22When a leader sins without meaning to and does something the LORD his God has commanded people not to do, he is guilty. 23When he learns about his sin, he must bring a male goat without any flaw as his offering. 24He must lay his hand on the goat’s head and kill it where they kill the burnt offering, in front of the LORD. It is a sin offering.

25The priest must take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering. 26He must burn all its fat on the altar, like the fat from the peace offering. In this way the priest makes atonement for the leader’s sin, and he is forgiven.

When an Ordinary Person Sins

27If one of the common people sins without meaning to and does something the LORD has commanded people not to do, he is guilty. 28When he learns about his sin, he must bring a female goat without any flaw as his offering for the sin he committed. 29He must lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and kill it where they kill the burnt offering.

30The priest must take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out all the rest of its blood at the base of the altar. 31He must remove all its fat, just as the fat is removed from the peace offering, and burn it on the altar. It gives a pleasing smell to the LORD. In this way the priest makes atonement for the person, and he is forgiven.

32If he brings a lamb as his sin offering, he must bring a female without any flaw. 33He must lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and kill it for a sin offering where they kill the burnt offering. 34The priest must take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He must pour out all the rest of its blood at the base of the altar. 35He must remove all its fat, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the peace offering, and burn the fat on the altar as a fire offering to the LORD. In this way the priest makes atonement for the person’s sin, and he is forgiven.
