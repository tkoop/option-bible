Praise for the LORD's Wonderful Works

1The LORD, you are my God. I will lift you up. I will praise your name, because you have done amazing things. Your plans from long ago are faithful and true. 2For you have turned the city into a pile of rubble. You have destroyed the fortified city. The palace of foreigners is gone forever. It will never be rebuilt. 3So strong people will honourhonourhonor you, and nations that are cruel will fear you.

4For you have been a safe place for the poor, a safe place for people in need when they are in trouble. You have been shelter from the storm and shade from the heat. The cruel people's angry breath is like a storm hitting a wall. 5Like heat in a dry land, you quiet the noise of foreigners. Like heat cooled by the shade of a cloud, you bring down the song of cruel people.
