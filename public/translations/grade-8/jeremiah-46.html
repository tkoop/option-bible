A Message About Egypt

1This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the nations. 2It was about Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt. That army was defeated by Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, at Carchemish on the Euphrates River. This happened in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.

Egypt's Army Is Defeated

3"Prepare your shields and small shields; march into battle! 4Harness the horses; mount the riders; take your positions with helmets; sharpen the spears; put on armourarmourarmor! 5Why do I see them terrified and running back? Their warriors are beaten. They run away fast and do not look back. Terror is all around," says the LORD.

6"The fast runner cannot escape, and the strong soldier cannot get away. In the north, by the Euphrates River, they stumble and fall. 7Who is this rising like the Nile, like rivers whose waters surge? 8Egypt rises like the Nile; its waters surge like rivers. He says, 'I will rise and cover the earth. I will destroy the city and everyone in it.' 9Advance, horses! Race, chariots! Let the warriors march out—Cush and Put, who carry shields, and the Lydians, who bend the bow. 10For that day belongs to the Lord, the LORD of Hosts. It is a day of payback, to pay back HishisHis enemies. The sword will eat its fill and drink its fill of their blood. For the Lord, the LORD of Hosts holds a sacrifice in the north, by the Euphrates River. 11Go up to Gilead and get medicine, people of Egypt! You use many cures for nothing; there is no healing for you. 12The nations have heard of your shame, and your cries fill the earth. Warrior stumbles against warrior, and both fall together."

Babylon Attacks Egypt

13This is the word the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, coming to attack the land of Egypt. 14"Announce it in Egypt, and proclaim it in Migdol. Proclaim it in Memphis and in Tahpanhes. Say, 'Take your position and get ready, because the sword is destroying everything around you.'"

15"Why have your strong men been swept away? They did not stand, because the LORD pushed them down. 16They stumble and fall over each other. They say, 'Get up! Let's go back to our people and our homeland, away from the sword of the enemy.' 17There they will cry out, 'Pharaoh, king of Egypt, is only a loud noise. He let the right time slip away.' 18'As surely as I live,' says the KingkingKing, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, 'someone will come like Mount Tabor among the mountains and like Mount Carmel beside the sea.'"

19"Pack your bags for exile, people of Egypt, because Memphis will be ruined and empty, with no one living there. 20Egypt is a beautiful young cow, but a stinging fly from the north is coming against her. 21Even her hired soldiers will be like well-fed calves. They too will turn and run. They will not stand their ground, because the day of disaster is coming on them—the time of their punishment. 22Egypt will hiss like a snake while the enemy marches in. They will come against her with axes, like men cutting wood. 23"They will cut down her forest," says the LORD, "though no one can explore it all, because they are more than locusts and cannot be counted. 24The people of Egypt will be put to shame. They will be handed over to the people of the north."

25The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says, "I am going to punish Amon of Thebes, along with Pharaoh, Egypt, her gods, and her kings, and everyone who trusts in Pharaoh. 26I will hand them over to those who want to kill them—to Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and his servants. But after this, Egypt will be settled again, as it was long ago," says the LORD.

Comfort for Israel

27"But you, O Jacob MymyMy servant, do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed, O Israel. For I will surely save you from far away, and your descendants from the land where they are held captive. Jacob will return and be at rest and secure, with no one to make him afraid. 28And you, O Jacob MymyMy servant, do not be afraid, says the LORD, for I am with you. Though I completely destroy all the nations where I have banished you, I will not completely destroy you. Yet I will discipline you fairly, and I will not leave you unpunished."
