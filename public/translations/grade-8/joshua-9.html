Kings Join Forces Against Israel

1When all the kings west of the Jordan heard what had happened, they joined together. These kings were from the hill country, the western foothills, and the whole coast of the GreatgreatGreat SeaseaSea toward Lebanon. They were Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. 2They came together with one purpose: to fight Joshua and Israel.

The Gibeonites Pretend to Be from Far Away

3The people of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and Ai. 4So they decided to trick him. They packed old sacks on their donkeys, took old wineskins that were cracked and tied up, 5and wore old, patched sandals and old clothes. All their food was dry and moldy.

6They went to Joshua at the camp in Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have come from a far country. Make a treaty with us."

7The men of Israel said to the Hivite, "Maybe you live near us. How can we make a treaty with you?"

8"We are your servants," they said to Joshua. Then Joshua asked them, "Who are you? Where do you come from?"

9They answered him, "YouryourYour servants have come from a very far country because of the name of the the LORD your God. We have heard about HimhimHim and about everything HeheHe did in Egypt. 10We also heard what HeheHe did to the two Amorite kings east of the Jordan: Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth. 11Our elders and all the people of our land told us, 'Take food for the journey and go meet them. Say to them, "We are your servants. Please make a treaty with us."' 12This bread was warm when we packed it at home, but now it is dry and moldy. 13These wineskins were new when we filled them, but now they are cracked. Our clothes and sandals are worn out from the long trip."

14The men of Israel tasted some of their food, but they did not ask the the LORD what to do. 15Joshua made a peace treaty with them and agreed to let them live. The leaders of the people also swore an oath to them.

The Trick Is Discovered

16Three days after they made the treaty, the Israelites heard that these people lived nearby. 17So the Israelites set out and reached their cities on the third day. The cities were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath Jearim.

18But the Israelites did not attack them, because the leaders had sworn to them by the the LORD, the God of Israel. The whole community complained against the leaders. 19All the leaders answered the community, "We have sworn to them by the the LORD, the God of Israel, and now we cannot touch them. 20This is what we will do for them: We will let them live, so the the LORD's anger will not come on us because of the oath we swore to them. 21They will live," the leaders told them, "but they will cut wood and carry water for the whole community."

Joshua Confronts the Gibeonites

22Joshua called the Gibeonites together and said to them, "Why did you trick us? You said, 'We live very far from you,' but you live among us. 23Now you are cursed. None of you will be freed from being slaves. You will cut wood and carry water for the house of my God."

24They answered Joshua, "We were told that the the LORD your God commanded HishisHis servant Moses to give you all this land and to destroy all who live in it. We were very afraid for our lives, so we did this. 25Now we are in your hands. Do to us what seems good and right to you."

26So Joshua saved them from the Israelites, and they were not killed. 27That day he made them cut wood and carry water for the community and for the altar of the the LORD at the place HeheHe would choose.
