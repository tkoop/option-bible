Rules for Sacrifices

1The LORD spoke to Moses and said, 2"Speak to Aaron, his sons, and all the Israelites. Tell them, 'This is what the LORD has commanded: 3Any Israelite who slaughters an ox, a sheep, or a goat in the camp or outside it, 4and does not bring it to the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting to offer it to the LORD before the LORD, is guilty of bloodshed. He has spilled blood and must be removed from his people. 5This is so the Israelites will bring the sacrifices they now make in the open field to the priest at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and sacrifice them as peace offerings to the LORD. 6The priest will sprinkle the blood on the altar of the LORD at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and burn the fat as a pleasing smell to the LORD. 7They must no longer sacrifice to goat demons, which they chase after. This will be a lasting law for them and their descendants.'

8"Say to them, 'Any Israelite or foreigner living among you who offers a burnt offering or sacrifice 9and does not bring it to the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting to make it to the LORD must be removed from his people.'

Blood Must Not Be Eaten

10"Any Israelite or foreigner living among you who eats any blood, I will oppose that person who eats blood and remove him from his people. 11For the life of the body is in the blood, and I have given it to you to make atonement for your lives on the altar. It is the blood that makes atonement because the life is in it. 12Therefore I said to the Israelites, 'No person among you may eat blood, and no foreigner living among you may eat blood.'

Hunting Wild Animals

13"Any Israelite or foreigner living among you who hunts and catches a wild animal or a bird that may be eaten must pour out its blood and cover it with dirt. 14For the life of all flesh is its blood. Therefore I said to the Israelites, 'YouyouYou must not eat the blood of any flesh, for the life of all flesh is its blood. Anyone who eats it will be removed from his people.'

Animals Found Dead

15"Any person, whether a native Israelite or a foreigner, who eats an animal that died on its own or was torn by wild animals must wash his clothes and bathe in water. He will be unclean until evening, and then he will be clean. 16But if he does not wash his clothes or bathe his body, he will carry his guilt."
