Moses Blesses Israel

1This is the blessing Moses, the man of God, spoke to the Israelites before he died.

The Lord Comes with His Law

2He said, “The LORD came from Sinai. He rose over them from Seir. He shone from Mount Paran. He came with thousands of holy ones, and fire burned at HishisHis right hand. 3Surely YouyouYou love the people; all YouryourYour holy ones are in YouryourYour hand. Blessings for the Tribes

6“Let Reuben live and not die, and let his people be many.”

7And this he said about Judah: “Hear, O the LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. His hands are strong for him, and be his help against his foes.”

18About Zebulun he said, “Rejoice, Zebulun, when you go out, and Issachar, in your tents. 19They will call the peoples to the mountain and offer right sacrifices there. For they will feed on the wealth of the seas and on the treasures hidden in the sand.”

20About Gad he said, “Blessed is the one who expands Gad's territory. Gad lives like a lion and tears off arm and head. 21He chose the best land for himself, for a leader's portion was kept there. 23About Naphtali he said, “Naphtali, full of favourfavourfavor and filled with the LORD's blessing, take the west and the south.”

24About Asher he said, “Asher is blessed with children. Let him be favouredfavouredfavored by his brothers and dip his foot in oil. 25Your sandals will be iron and bronze, and your strength will last as long as you live.”

The Lord Is Israel's Refuge

26There is no one like the God of Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to help you and through the skies in HishisHis majesty. 27The eternal God is your refuge, and underneath are HishisHis everlasting arms. He will drive the enemy out before you and destroy them. 28Israel will live safely alone. Jacob's spring is in a land of grain and wine, and the heavens will drip with dew. 29How happy you are, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD?
