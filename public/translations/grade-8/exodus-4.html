Moses Questions His Mission

1Moses answered, "What if they do not believe me or listen to me? They may say the LORD never appeared to you." 2The LORD asked him, "What is that in your hand?" Moses said, "A staff."

3The LORD said, "Throw it on the ground." Moses threw it on the ground, and it became a snake. Moses ran from it. 4Then the LORD said to Moses, "Reach out your hand and grab it by the tail." Moses reached out his hand and took hold of it, and it became a staff again in his hand. 5The LORD said, "This will help them believe that I am the LORD, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and that I have appeared to you."

6The LORD also said, "Put your hand inside your cloak." Moses put his hand inside his cloak. When he took it out, his hand was covered with a skin disease and was white like snow. 7The LORD said, "Put your hand back inside your cloak." Moses put it back, and when he took it out again, it was healthy like the rest of his body. 8The LORD said, "If they do not believe you or listen to the first sign, they may believe the second sign. 9If they still do not believe these two signs or listen to you, take some water from the Nile and pour it on the dry ground. The water you take from the river will turn to blood on the ground."

Moses Asks for Help

10Moses said to the LORD, "Please, Lord, I am not a good speaker. I have never been good with words, not yesterday, not the day before, and not since YouyouYou began speaking to YouryourYour servant. I speak slowly and with difficulty." 11The LORD said to him, "Who made man's mouth? Who makes a person unable to speak or deaf, or able to see or blind? Is it not I, the LORD?" 12"Now go. I will help you speak, and I will teach you what to say." 13But Moses said, "Please, Lord, send someone else."

14Then the LORD became angry with Moses and said, "What about your brother Aaron the Levite? I know he speaks well. He is already on his way to meet you, and he will be glad to see you. 15You will speak to him and tell him what to say. I will help both of you speak, and I will teach you what to do. 16He will speak to the people for you. He will be your spokesman, and you will be like God to him. 17Take this staff in your hand so you can do the signs with it."

Moses Returns to Egypt

18Moses went back to his father-in-law Jethro and said, "Let me go back to my own people in Egypt and see if they are still alive." Jethro said, "Go safely." 19The LORD said to Moses in Midian, "Go back to Egypt, because all the men who wanted to kill you are dead." 20So Moses took his wife and sons, put them on a donkey, and started back to Egypt. He took the staff of God in his hand.

21The LORD said to Moses, "When you return to Egypt, be sure to do all the wonders I have given you the power to do in front of Pharaoh. But I will make his heart stubborn, and he will not let the people go. 22Then say to Pharaoh, 'This is what the LORD says: Israel is MymyMy firstborn son. 23I told you to let MymyMy son go so he could worship MemeMe. But you refused to let him go, so I will kill your firstborn son.'"

24At a place where they stayed for the night on the way, the LORD met Moses and was about to kill him. 25Zipporah took a flint knife, cut off her son's foreskin, touched Moses' feet with it, and said, "YouyouYou are a bloody husband to me." 26So the LORD let him go. At that time she said, "A bloody husband because of circumcision."

The People Believe

27The LORD said to Aaron, "Go into the desert to meet Moses." Aaron went and met him at the mountain of God, and kissed him. 28Moses told Aaron everything the LORD had sent him to say and all the signs HeheHe had commanded him to do. 29Then Moses and Aaron gathered all the elders of the people of Israel. 30Aaron spoke all the words the LORD had spoken to Moses, and he did the signs in front of the people. 31The people believed. When they heard that the LORD had cared for them and had seen their suffering, they bowed down and worshiped.
