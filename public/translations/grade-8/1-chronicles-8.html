Benjamin's Sons and Grandsons

1Benjamin had these sons: Bela, his firstborn; Ashbel, second; Aharah, third; 2Nohah, fourth; and Rapha, fifth. 3Bela had these sons: Addar, Gera, and Abihud; 4Abishua, Naaman, and Ahoah; 5Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

Families Taken to Manahath

6These were Ehud's sons. They were family leaders of the people who lived in Geba, and they were moved to Manahath. 7Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera moved them, and Gera had Uzza and Ahihud.

8Shaharaim had children in the land of Moab after he sent away his wives, Hushim and Baara. 9By his wife Hodesh, he had Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, and Malcam. 10He also had Jeuz, Sakiah, and Mirmah. These were his sons and family leaders. 11From Hushim, he had Abitub and Elpaal.

12Elpaal's sons were Eber, Misham, and Shemed. Shemed built the towns of Ono and Lod and their villages. 13Beriah and Shema were family leaders of the people who lived in Aijalon. They drove out the people of Gath.

14Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth; 15Zebadiah, Arad, and Eder; 16Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

17Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, and Heber; 18Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal; 19Jakim, Zichri, and Zabdi; 20Elienai, Zilletai, and Eliel; 21Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.

22Ishpan, Eber, and Eliel; 23Abdon, Zichri, and Hanan; 24Hananiah, Elam, and Anthothijah; 25Iphdeiah and Penuel were the sons of Shashak.

26Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, 27Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

Family Leaders in Jerusalem

28These were family leaders and chiefs. By their family lines, they lived in Jerusalem. 29Jeiel, the father of Gibeon, lived in Gibeon. His wife's name was Maacah. 30His firstborn son was Abdon. Then came Zur, Kish, Baal, and Nadab. 31He also had Gedor, Ahio, and Zechar. 32Mikloth had Shimeah. They also lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.

The Family of King Saul

33Ner had Kish. Kish had Saul. Saul had Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. 34Jonathan's son was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal had Micah. 35Micah's sons were Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. 36Ahaz had Jehoaddah. Jehoaddah had Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri had Moza. 37Moza had Binea. Raphah was his son, Eleasah was his son, and Azel was his son. 38Azel had six sons: Azrikam, his firstborn; Bocheru; Ishmael; Sheariah; Obadiah; and Hanan. All of these were Azel's sons. 39Azel's brother, Eshek, had these sons: Ulam, his firstborn; Jeush, second; and Eliphelet, third. 40Ulam's sons were mighty warriors and archers. They had many sons and grandsons, one hundred fifty in all. All of these were Benjaminites.
