Joash Becomes King

1In the seventh year of Jehu, Joash became king. He ruled forty years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zibiah from Beersheba. 2Joash did what was right in the eyes of the the LORD all the days that Jehoiada the priest taught him. 3But the high places were not taken away. The people still offered sacrifices and burned incense on them.

Joash Repairs the Temple

4Joash said to the priests, “Collect all the money that is brought as sacred gifts into the temple of the the LORD—the census money, money from vows, and any money a person feels moved to bring into the temple of the the LORD. 5Let the priests take it from the people they know and use it to fix any damage found in the temple.” 6But by the twenty-third year of King Joash, the priests still had not fixed the damage in the temple. 7So King Joash called Jehoiada the priest and the other priests and said to them, “Why have you not fixed the damage in the temple? Now do not take any more money from your acquaintances. Give it for the repair of the temple.” 8The priests agreed not to take money from the people and not to repair the temple themselves.

9Then Jehoiada the priest took a chest, cut a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar on the right side as one enters the temple of the the LORD. The priests who guarded the doorway put into the chest all the money brought into the temple of the the LORD. 10When they saw that a lot of money was in the chest, the king’s secretary and the high priest went up, counted the money found in the temple of the the LORD, and put it into bags. 11They gave the weighed money to the men in charge of the work on the temple of the the LORD. Those men paid it to the carpenters and builders working on the temple of the the LORD. 12They also paid masons and stonecutters. They bought wood and cut stone to fix the damage in the temple of the the LORD, and they paid for everything needed to repair it. 13But the money brought into the temple of the the LORD was not used to make silver cups, snuffers, bowls, trumpets, or any gold or silver vessels for the temple of the the LORD. 14Instead, it was given to the workmen, and they used it to repair the temple of the the LORD. 15The men who received the money to pay the workers were not asked to give a detailed report, because they acted faithfully. 16Money from guilt offerings and sin offerings was not brought into the temple of the the LORD. It belonged to the priests.

Hazael Threatens Jerusalem

17At that time Hazael king of Aram went up and fought against Gath and captured it. Then he turned toward Jerusalem. 18King Joash of Judah took all the sacred things his fathers—Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, kings of Judah—had dedicated. He also took his own sacred things and all the gold found in the storerooms of the temple of the the LORD and the king’s palace. He sent them to Hazael king of Aram, and Hazael turned away from Jerusalem. 19The rest of the acts of Joash, and all he did, are they not written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

Joash Is Killed

20His servants made a plot and struck down Joash at Beth-millo, on the road that goes down to Silla. 21His servants Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer struck him, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the CitycityCity of David, and his son Amaziah became king after him.
