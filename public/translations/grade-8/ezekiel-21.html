God's Sword Against the Land

1The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 2“Son of man, turn your face toward Jerusalem. Speak against the holy places, and prophesy against the land of Israel.

3Say to the land of Israel, “This is what the LORD says: I am against you. I will draw MymyMy sword from its sheath and remove from you both the righteous and the wicked. 4Because I will remove both the righteous and the wicked from you, MymyMy sword will go out of its sheath against all people, from the south to the north. 5Then all people will know that I, the LORD, have drawn MymyMy sword from its sheath, and it will not be put back.”

6“And you, son of man, groan in front of them. Groan with broken hips and bitter sadness. 7When they ask you, ‘Why are you groaning?’ say to them, ‘Because of the news that is coming. Everyone will lose courage, every hand will go limp, every spirit will fail, and every knee will give way. Yes, it is coming, and it will happen,’ says the Lord the LORD.”

The Song of the Sword

8The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 9“Son of man, prophesy and say: ‘This is what the Lord says: A sword, a sword, sharpened and polished! 10Sharpened to kill, polished to flash like lightning! Should we rejoice? The sceptresceptrescepter of MymyMy son despises every tree. 11It has been given to be polished and held in the hand. It is sharpened and polished to be given to the killer. 12Cry out and wail, son of man, because this sword is against MymyMy people and against all the leaders of Israel. They are handed over to the sword along with MymyMy people. So strike your thigh. 13For it has been tested, and what if the sceptresceptrescepter that rejects does not last? declares the Lord the LORD.’”

14“And you, son of man, prophesy and clap your hands. Let the sword strike twice, three times. It is a sword for the slain, a great sword for the slaughter, cutting deep into them. 15So that people lose courage and many stumble, I have set the point of the sword against all their gates. It is made to flash like lightning and polished for slaughter. 16Gather yourself, turn right, set yourself, turn left, wherever your face is pointed. 17I will also strike MymyMy hands together and satisfy MymyMy anger. I, the LORD, have spoken.”

The King of Babylon Chooses a Road

18The word of the LORD came to me. He said, 19“Son of man, make two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to travel. Both roads start from one land. Make a signpost where the road splits toward a city. 20Mark one road for the sword to go against Rabbah of the Ammonites, and another against Judah and fortified Jerusalem. 21For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road, at the head of the two roads, to seek a sign by divination. He shakes his arrows, asks his idols, and looks at the liver. 22In his right hand is the sign for Jerusalem: to set up battering rams, to open the mouth with bloodshed, to shout a battle cry, to set up battering rams against the gates, to build a siege ramp, and to build a siege wall. 23It will seem like a false sign to them, because they have sworn solemn oaths. But he will remind them of their guilt and capture them.”

24“Therefore this is what the Lord the LORD says: Because you have brought your guilt to mind by uncovering your rebellions and showing your sins in all your actions—because you have done this, you will be captured. 25And you, O sinful and wicked prince of Israel, whose day has come at the time of final punishment, 26this is what the Lord the LORD says: Remove the turban and take off the crown. Things will not stay as they are. Raise up the lowly and bring down the exalted. 27I will ruin it, ruin it, ruin it. It will not be restored until HeheHe comes to whom the right to judge belongs, and I will give it to HimhimHim.”

In the place where you were created, in the land of your birth, I will judge you. 31I will pour out MymyMy wrath on you. I will blow on you with the fire of MymyMy fury. I will hand you over to cruel men, skilled in destruction. 32You will be fuel for the fire. Your blood will be in the middle of the land. You will not be remembered, for I, the LORD, have spoken.’”
