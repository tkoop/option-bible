The People Give Willingly

6Then the leaders of the families, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the officials in charge of the king's work gave willingly. 7They gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand gold coins, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron. 8Those who had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the the LORD, under the care of Jehiel the Gershonite. 9The people rejoiced over their willing gifts, because they had given to the the LORD with a whole heart. King David also rejoiced greatly.

Solomon Becomes King

21The next day they offered sacrifices and burnt offerings to the the LORD: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and many other sacrifices for all Israel. 22They ate and drank that day with great joy before the the LORD. Then they made Solomon, son of David, king a second time, anointing him before the the LORD as ruler, and Zadok as priest. 23So Solomon sat on the throne of the the LORD as king in place of his father David. He prospered, and all Israel obeyed him. 24All the leaders and mighty men, and all of King David's sons, pledged their loyalty to King Solomon. 25The LORD greatly exalted Solomon in the sight of all Israel and gave him royal splendoursplendoursplendor such as no king over Israel ever had before. 26David, son of Jesse, reigned over all Israel. 27He reigned over Israel forty years: seven years in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem. 28He died at a good old age, full of days, riches, and honourhonourhonor.
