A Call to Praise the LORD

1Praise the LORD! Praise God in HishisHis sanctuary; praise HimhimHim in HishisHis mighty heavens. 2Praise HimhimHim for HishisHis mighty acts; praise HimhimHim for HishisHis excellent greatness.

3Praise HimhimHim with the sound of the trumpet; praise HimhimHim with the harp and lyre. 4Praise HimhimHim with the tambourine and dancing; praise HimhimHim with strings and flute. 5Praise HimhimHim with sounding cymbals; praise HimhimHim with loud cymbals.

6Let everything that has breath praise the LORD. Praise the LORD!
