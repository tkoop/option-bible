Paul Defends His Ministry

1I hope you will put up with a little foolishness from me. Yes, please put up with me! 2I am jealous for you with a jealousy that comes from God. I promised you to one husband, Christ, so I could present you to HimhimHim as a pure virgin. 3But I am afraid that, just as Eve was tricked by the snake's cleverness, your minds may be led away from your sincere and pure devotion to Christ. 4If someone comes to you and preaches a different Jesus than we preached, or you receive a different spirit than the one you received, or a different good news than the one you accepted, you put up with it easily enough.

5I do not think I am any less than those "super-apostles." 6I may not be a skilled speaker, but I do have knowledge. We have shown this to you in every way. 7Was it a sin for me to lower myself so you could be lifted up, because I preached God's good news to you for free? 8I took support from other churches so I could serve you. 9When I was with you and needed something, I was not a burden to anyone, because the brothers who came from Macedonia supplied what I needed. I have kept myself from being a burden to you in any way, and I will keep doing so. 10As surely as Christ's truth is in me, no one in Achaia will stop me from boasting about this. 11Why? Because I do not love you? God knows I do! 12I will keep doing what I am doing so I can cut off the chance for those who want to be seen as equal to us in what they boast about.

False Apostles Exposed

13Those men are false apostles. They are deceitful workers who disguise themselves as apostles of Christ. 14And no wonder, for Satan himself disguises himself as an angel of light. 15It is not surprising, then, if his servants also disguise themselves as servants of righteousness. Their end will be what their actions deserve.

Paul Boasts Like a Fool

16I say again: No one should think I am a fool. But if you do, then accept me as a fool so I can boast a little. 17When I talk with this confident boasting, I am not speaking the way the Lord would. I am speaking like a fool. 18Since many boast about worldly things, I will boast too. 19You gladly put up with fools since you are so wise! 20In fact, you even put up with anyone who enslaves you or takes advantage of you or acts superior or slaps you in the face.

21I say this to my shame: We were too weak to do that! But if someone else dares to boast about something—I am speaking like a fool—I dare to boast about it too. 22Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they Abraham's descendants? So am I. 23Are they servants of Christ? I am out of my mind to talk like this, but I am more. I have worked much harder, been in prison more often, been beaten more severely, and faced death many times. 24Five times I received from the Jews the forty lashes minus one. 25Three times I was beaten with rods, once I was pelted with stones, three times I was shipwrecked, and I spent a night and a day in the open sea. 26I have been constantly on the move. I have been in danger from rivers, in danger from bandits, in danger from my fellow Jews, in danger from Gentiles; in danger in the city, in danger in the country, in danger at sea; and in danger from false believers. 27I have labouredlabouredlabored and toiled and have often gone without sleep; I have known hunger and thirst and have often gone without food; I have been cold and naked. 28Besides all this, every day I feel the pressure of caring for all the churches. 29Who is weak without me feeling weak? Who is led to sin without me burning inside? 30If I must boast, I will boast of the things that show my weakness. 31The God and FatherfatherFather of the Lord Jesus, who is praised forever, knows that I am not lying. 32In Damascus the governor under King Aretas had the city of the Damascenes guarded in order to arrest me. 33But I was lowered in a basket from a window in the wall and escaped from him.
