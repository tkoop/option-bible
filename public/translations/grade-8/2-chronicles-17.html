Jehoshaphat Becomes King

1Jehoshaphat, Asa’s son, became king after him. He strengthened Judah against Israel. 2He stationed troops in all of Judah’s fortified cities and put garrisons in Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured. 3The LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the early ways of his father David and did not worship the Baals. 4Instead, he sought the God of his father and obeyed HishisHis commands. He did not follow Israel’s practices. 5So the LORD made his kingdom strong. All Judah brought gifts to Jehoshaphat, and he had great wealth and honourhonourhonor. 6His heart was devoted to the ways of the LORD. He also removed the high places and Asherah poles from Judah.

Jehoshaphat Sends Teachers Through Judah

7In the third year of his reign, he sent his officials—Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach in Judah’s cities. 8With them were Levites: Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah. Priests Elishama and Jehoram were with them too. 9They taught in Judah. They had the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD with them. They travelledtravelledtraveled through all of Judah’s cities and taught the people.

Nations Fear Judah

10Fear of the LORD fell on all the kingdoms around Judah. They did not go to war against Jehoshaphat. 11Some Philistines brought Jehoshaphat gifts and silver as tribute. The Arabs also brought him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats. 12Jehoshaphat kept growing stronger. He built fortresses and store cities in Judah. 13He had much property in Judah’s cities and many warriors in Jerusalem.

Judah’s Army

14This was their registration by families: For Judah, commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander, with 300,000 mighty warriors. 15Next to him was Jehohanan the commander, with 280,000. 16Next to him was Amasiah son of Zichri, who volunteered for the LORD, with 200,000 mighty men. 17From Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty man, with 200,000 armed with bows and shields. 18Next to him was Jehozabad, with 180,000 ready for war. 19These served the king, besides the men he had placed in Judah’s fortified cities.
