A New Song for the LORD

1Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. 2Sing to the LORD, praise HishisHis name; tell of HishisHis salvation day after day. 3Tell of HishisHis glory among the nations, and HishisHis wonderful works among all peoples.

4For great is the LORD and worthy of much praise; HeheHe is to be feared above all gods. 5For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. 6SplendourSplendourSplendor and majesty are before HimhimHim; strength and beauty are in HishisHis sanctuary.

7Give to the LORD, you families of nations, give to the LORD glory and strength. 8Give to the LORD the glory HishisHis name deserves; bring an offering and come into HishisHis courts. 9Worship the LORD in the beauty of HishisHis holiness; tremble before HimhimHim, all the earth.

10Say among the nations, "ThetheThe LORD reigns! The world is firmly set; it cannot be shaken. He will judge the peoples fairly."

11Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea roar, and everything in it. 12Let the fields and everything in them be joyful; let all the trees of the forest sing for joy. 13Let all creation rejoice before the LORD, for HeheHe comes, HeheHe comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in HishisHis faithfulness.
