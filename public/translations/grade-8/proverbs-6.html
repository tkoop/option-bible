Warning About Guaranteeing Another's Debt

1My son, if you have promised to pay someone else's debt for your neighbourneighbourneighbor, or given your word with a handshake to guarantee a stranger's loan, 2you are trapped by your own words; you are caught by what you said. 3Do this now, my son, to free yourself, because you have put yourself in your neighbourneighbourneighbor's power: go and humble yourself, and beg your neighbourneighbourneighbor to let you go. 4Do not let your eyes sleep, or your eyelids close. 5Free yourself like a deer from a hunter's hand, like a bird from a birdcatcher's trap.

Learn from the Ant

6Go to the ant, you lazy person; watch its ways and be wise. 7It has no boss, officer, or ruler, 8yet it gets its food ready in summer and gathers its supplies at harvest time. 9How long will you stay in bed, lazy person? When will you get up from your sleep? 10A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, 11and poverty will sneak up on you like a thief, and need like an armed robber.

A Worthless Person and What the LORD Hates

12A worthless person, a wicked man, goes around with crooked talk, 13winking with his eyes, shuffling his feet, and pointing with his fingers, 14with evil plans in his heart, always stirring up trouble and spreading arguments. 15So disaster will hit him suddenly; in an instant he will be broken, with no cure. 16There are six things the the LORD hates, seven that disgust HimhimHim: 17proud eyes, a lying tongue, hands that kill innocent people, 18a heart that plans evil, feet that rush to do wrong, 19a false witness who breathes out lies, and a person who starts fights among brothers.

Warning Against Adultery

20My son, obey your father's command, and don't ignore your mother's teaching. 21Keep them close to your heart always; tie them around your neck. 22When you walk, they will guide you; when you sleep, they will protect you; and when you wake, they will talk with you. 23For the command is a lamp, the teaching is a light, and the correction that trains you is the way to life. 24They will protect you from an immoral woman and from the smooth talk of a wayward wife. 25Do not desire her beauty in your heart, and do not let her eyes capture you. 26A prostitute can be bought for a loaf of bread, but an adulteress hunts for a man's life. 27Can a man carry fire in his lap without his clothes burning? 28Can a man walk on hot coals without his feet getting burned? 29So is the man who sleeps with another man's wife; no one who touches her will go unpunished. 30People don't look down on a thief who steals to fill his stomach. 31But if he is caught, he must pay back seven times and give everything in his house. 32The man who commits adultery has no sense; he destroys himself. 33He will get wounds and shame, and his disgrace will not be erased. 34For jealousy makes a husband furious, and he will show no mercy when he takes revenge. 35He will not accept any payment, and no amount of money will calm him down.
