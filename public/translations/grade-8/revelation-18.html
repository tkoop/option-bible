The Fall of Babylon Is Announced

1After this I saw another angel coming down from Heavenheavenheaven. He had great authority, and the earth was bright with his glory. 2He called out with a loud voice, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a home for demons, a prison for every unclean spirit, a cage for every unclean bird, and a cage for every hated beast.” 3For all the nations have drunk the wine of her passionate sexual sin, the kings of the earth committed sexual sin with her, and the merchants of the earth grew rich from her costly luxury.

God Calls His People Out of Babylon

4Then I heard another voice from Heavenheavenheaven say, “Come out of her, MymyMy people, so that you will not share in her sins and so that you will not receive her plagues, 5for her sins have piled up to Heavenheavenheaven, and God has remembered her crimes. 6Pay her back as she has paid, and double her penalty to match her deeds. Mix a double portion for her in the cup she mixed. 7Give her as much torment and grief as she gave herself glory and luxury, because in her heart she says, ‘I sit as a queen; I am not a widow, and I will never see grief.’ 8Therefore her plagues will come in one day—death, mourning, and famine—and she will be burned with fire, because the Lord God who judges her is strong.”

Kings and Merchants Mourn Babylon

9The kings of the earth who committed sexual sin and lived in luxury with her will weep and mourn over her when they see the smoke of her burning. 10They will stand far away because they fear her torment, and they will say, “Woe, woe to the great city, the strong city, Babylon! For in one hour your judgmentjudgementjudgment has come.”

11The merchants of the earth also weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo anymore: 12cargo of gold, silver, precious stones, pearls, fine linen, purple cloth, silk, scarlet cloth, all kinds of citron wood, all kinds of ivory goods, all kinds of goods made from the finest wood, bronze, iron, and marble; 13and cinnamon, spice, incense, perfume, frankincense, wine, oil, fine flour, wheat, cattle, sheep, horses, chariots, slaves, and human souls. 14“The fruit your soul longed for has left you; all your rich and splendid things are lost, and they will never be found again.”

15The merchants who grew rich from her will stand far away because they fear her torment. They will weep and mourn, 16saying, “Woe, woe to the great city, dressed in fine linen, purple, and scarlet, and decorated with gold, precious stones, and pearls! 17For in one hour such great wealth has been ruined.” And every ship captain, every sailor, and all who work at sea stood far away.

18They cried out when they saw the smoke of her burning, “What city is like the great city?” 19They threw dust on their heads, weeping and mourning, and cried out, “Woe, woe to the great city, where everyone who had ships on the sea grew rich from her wealth! For in one hour she was ruined.”

Heaven Rejoices and Babylon Is Destroyed

20Rejoice over her, O Heavenheavenheaven, and you saints, apostles, and prophets, because God has avenged you against her.

21Then a strong angel picked up a stone like a huge millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, “In the same way Babylon the great city will be thrown down with force, and it will not be found anymore. 22The sound of harp players, musicians, flute players, and trumpet players will never be heard in you again. No craftsman of any trade will be found in you again, and the sound of a mill will never be heard in you again. 23The light of a lamp will never shine in you again, and the voice of bride and groom will never be heard in you again, because your merchants were the great men of the earth, because all nations were led astray by your sorcery.”

24In her was found the blood of prophets and saints and of all who were killed on the earth.
