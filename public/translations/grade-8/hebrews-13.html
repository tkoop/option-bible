Love One Another and Live Purely

1Keep loving one another as brothers and sisters. 2Do not forget to show hospitality to strangers, because some people have welcomed angels without knowing it. 3Remember people in prison as if you were locked up with them. Remember those who are mistreated, because you also have bodies that can suffer.

4Marriage should be honouredhonouredhonored by everyone, and the marriage bed kept pure, because God will judge those who are sexually immoral and those who commit adultery.

5Keep your lives free from the love of money, and be content with what you have, because God has said, "I will never leave you or abandon you."

6So we can say with confidence, "ThetheThe Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid. What can anyone do to me?"

Trust Jesus Christ, Who Never Changes

7Remember your leaders, who spoke God's word to you. Think about how their lives ended, and copy their faith. 8Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. 9Do not be carried away by all kinds of strange teachings. It is good for your heart to be strengthened by grace, not by rules about food, which have not helped those who follow them.

Our Altar and Going Outside the Camp
