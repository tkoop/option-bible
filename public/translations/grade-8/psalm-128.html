The Happy Life of Those Who Fear the LORD

1Blessed is everyone who fears the LORD and follows HishisHis ways. 2You will eat what your hands have worked for. You will be blessed, and things will go well for you. 3Your wife will be like a fruitful grapevine inside your home. Your children will be like young olive trees around your table.

4Look, this is how a man who fears the LORD is blessed. 5The LORD will bless you from Zion. May you see good things in Jerusalem all the days of your life. 6May you see your grandchildren. Peace be on Israel.
