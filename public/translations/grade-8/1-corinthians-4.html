Servants of Christ

1So then, people should think of us as servants of Christ and managers of God's mysteries. 2The main thing asked of a manager is to be faithful.

3I care very little if I am judged by you or by any human court. In fact, I don't even judge myself. 4My conscience is clear, but that doesn't make me right. The Lord is the OneoneOne who judges me.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment Belongs to the Lord

5So don't judge anything before the right time. Wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and show the true motives of people's hearts. Then each person will receive praise from God.

A Warning Against Pride

6Now, brothers and sisters, I have used myself and Apollos as an example for your benefit, so you may learn from us the saying, "Do not go beyond what is written." Then you won't become proud by favoring one of us over the other. 7Who makes you different from anyone else? What do you have that you didn't receive? And if you did receive it, why do you brag as if you didn't? 8You already have everything you want! You are already rich! You have started to reign, and you did it without us! I wish you really had started to reign, so we could reign with you too.

Apostles' Hardships and Fatherly Care

9It seems to me that God has put us apostles on display last, like men sentenced to die. We have become a show for the whole world, for angels and for people. 10We are fools for Christ, but you are so wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are honouredhonouredhonored, but we are dishonoureddishonoureddishonored! 11Right up to this moment, we are hungry and thirsty. We wear rags, we are treated badly, and we have no home. 12We work hard with our own hands. When people insult us, we bless them. When they persecute us, we put up with it. 13When they say bad things about us, we answer with kindness. Even now, we have become like the trash of the world, the garbage everyone throws away.

14I'm not writing this to shame you. I'm writing to warn you, because you are my dear children. 15Even if you had ten thousand guides in Christ, you don't have many fathers. In Christ Jesus, I became your father through the good news. 16So I urge you to follow my example. 17That's why I sent Timothy to you. He is my dear and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my way of life in Christ Jesus, which is the same everywhere in every church.

18Some of you have become proud, as if I weren't coming to you. 19But I will come to you very soon, if the Lord is willing. Then I will find out not just what these proud people are saying, but what power they have. 20For God's kingdom isn't about talk. It's about power. 21What do you want? Should I come to you with strict discipline, or with love and a gentle spirit?
