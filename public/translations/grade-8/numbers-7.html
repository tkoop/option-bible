Moses Finishes the Tabernacle

1On the day Moses finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed it and set it apart as holy. He did the same for all its furnishings, the altar, and all the altar’s tools. 2The leaders of Israel brought offerings. They were the heads of the family groups and the leaders of the tribes who had helped count the people. 3They brought their gifts to the LORD: six covered wagons and twelve oxen. There was one wagon for every two leaders and one ox for each leader. They presented them in front of the tabernacle.

The LORD Gives Wagons to the Levites

4The LORD said to Moses, 5“Take these gifts from them. They are to be used for the work of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. Give them to the Levites. Give each man what fits his work.” 6So Moses took the wagons and oxen and gave them to the Levites. 7He gave two wagons and four oxen to the sons of Gershon for their work. 8He gave four wagons and eight oxen to the sons of Merari for their work. Ithamar son of Aaron the priest was in charge of them. 9He gave none to the sons of Kohath, because they carried the holy things on their shoulders.

Leaders Dedicate the Altar

10The leaders also brought gifts for the dedication of the altar on the day it was anointed. They presented them in front of the altar. 11The LORD said to Moses, “Each leader is to bring his offering on his own day for the dedication of the altar.”

12On the first day, Nahshon son of Amminadab, leader of Judah, brought his offering. 13He brought one silver plate weighing 130 shekels and one silver bowl weighing 70 shekels, both by the sanctuary shekel. Both were full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering. 14He brought one gold dish weighing 10 shekels, full of incense. 15For the burnt offering he brought one young bull, one ram, and one male lamb a year old. 16He brought one male goat for a sin offering. 17For the peace offering he brought two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs a year old. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

Totals of the Dedication Gifts

84This was the dedication offering of the altar from the leaders of Israel when it was anointed: twelve silver plates, twelve silver bowls, and twelve gold dishes. 85Each silver plate weighed 130 shekels, and each silver bowl weighed 70 shekels. All the silver vessels weighed 2,400 shekels by the sanctuary shekel. 86The twelve gold dishes, full of incense, weighed 10 shekels each by the sanctuary shekel. All the gold of the dishes weighed 120 shekels. 87All the animals for the burnt offering were twelve bulls, twelve rams, and twelve male lambs a year old, along with their grain offering. There were also twelve male goats for a sin offering. 88All the animals for the peace offering were twenty-four bulls, sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty male lambs a year old.
