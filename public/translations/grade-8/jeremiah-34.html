God Warns Zedekiah About Jerusalem

1This is the message the the LORD gave Jeremiah when Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, and his whole army, all the kingdoms under his control, and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and its cities.

2"This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Go to Zedekiah king of Judah and tell him, 'This is what the the LORD says:

3'YouyouYou will not escape his power. You will be captured and handed over to him. You will see the king of Babylon with your own eyes, and he will speak to you face to face. Then you will go to Babylon.

4'Yet hear the word of the the LORD, O Zedekiah king of Judah. This is what the the LORD says concerning you: You will not die by the sword.

5'YouyouYou will die in peace. Just as people burned spices for your fathers, the kings who came before you, they will burn spices for you and mourn, saying, "Alas, my lord!" For I have spoken this word, declares the the LORD.'"

6Then Jeremiah the prophet told all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem, 7while the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and all the remaining cities of Judah—against Lachish and Azekah. Those were the only fortified cities left in Judah.

The Broken Promise to Free Slaves

8After King Zedekiah made an agreement with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim freedom, 9each man was to release his Hebrew slaves, both male and female, so no one would enslave a fellow Jew. 10All the officials and all the people who had entered the agreement agreed to free their male and female servants and no longer hold them in bondage. They obeyed and set them free. 11But afterward they changed their minds and took back the men and women they had freed, forcing them to become slaves again.

The LORD Judges the Covenant Breakers

12Then this message came to Jeremiah from the the LORD: 13"This is what the the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I made a covenant with your fathers when I brought them out of Egypt, out of slavery, saying:

14'At the end of seven years, each of you must release his Hebrew brother who has sold himself to you. He may serve you six years, but then you must let him go free. But your fathers did not listen or pay attention.'

15"Recently you turned back and did what is right in MymyMy eyes by proclaiming freedom to one another. You made a covenant before MemeMe in the house called by MymyMy name.

16"But then you turned around and dishonoureddishonoureddishonored MymyMy name. Each of you took back his male and female servants whom you had set free to go where they wanted, and you forced them back into slavery.

17"Therefore this is what the the LORD says: You have not obeyed MemeMe; you have not proclaimed freedom to your brothers and neighbors. So I now proclaim freedom for you, declares the the LORD—not freedom to live, but freedom to fall by the sword, plague, and famine. I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

18"And those who broke MymyMy covenant and did not keep the terms of the covenant they made before MemeMe, I will treat like the calf they cut in two and walked between its pieces.

19"The officials of Judah and Jerusalem, the court officials, the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the pieces of the calf—

20"I will hand them over to their enemies who seek their lives. Their dead bodies will become food for the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth.

21"And I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials to their enemies who seek their lives, to the army of the king of Babylon that has withdrawn from you.

22"Look, I will command, declares the the LORD, and I will bring them back to this city. They will fight against it, capture it, and burn it with fire. I will make the cities of Judah a wasteland, with no one living in them."
