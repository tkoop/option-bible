Israel Gathers to Confess Its Sins

1On the twenty-fourth day of this month, the Israelites gathered together. They fasted, wore sackcloth, and put dust on their heads. 2The people of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners. They stood and confessed their sins and the sins of their ancestors. 3They stood where they were and read from the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of the LORD their God for a quarter of the day. For another quarter, they confessed their sins and worshiped the LORD their God. 4The Levites—Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani—stood on the stairs. They cried out to the LORD their God with a loud voice.
