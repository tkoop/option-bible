Family Life in the Lord

1Children, obey your parents in the Lord, because this is right. 2“HonourHonourHonor your father and mother”—this is the first commandment with a promise— 3“so that it may go well with you and you may live a long time on the earth.” 4Fathers, do not make your children angry, but bring them up with the Lord’s training and teaching.

Slaves and Masters

5Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear, and with an honest heart, just as you would obey Christ. 6Do not work only to please them when they are watching. Instead, work as slaves of Christ, doing what God wants from the heart. 7Serve with a good attitude, as if you were serving the Lord and not people. 8You know that the Lord will reward each person for whatever good they do, whether they are a slave or free.

9And masters, treat your slaves the same way. Do not threaten them, because you know that their Master and yours is in Heavenheavenheaven, and HeheHe does not show favouritismfavouritismfavoritism.
