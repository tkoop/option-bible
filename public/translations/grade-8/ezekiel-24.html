Jerusalem's Siege Begins

1In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me. 2"Son of man, write down the name of this day. This very day the king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem."

The Boiling Pot

3"Son of man, tell this parable to the stubborn people. Say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD says: Put a pot on the fire. Put it on, and pour water into it. 4Put in the pieces of meat, all the good pieces—thigh and shoulder. Fill it with the best bones. 5Take the best sheep. Pile the bones under it. Boil the pieces of meat. Cook the bones in it.'" 6Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: "How terrible for the city of blood! It is a pot whose rust is in it, and the rust has not come out. Take its pieces out one by one, without choosing any by lot. 7For blood is in her. She put it on the bare rock. She did not pour it on the ground to cover it with dust. 8To stir up anger and take revenge, I put her blood on the bare rock, so it would not be covered."

9Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: "How terrible for the city of blood! I too will make the fire great. 10Heap wood on it. Light the fire. Cook the meat well. Mix in the spices. Let the bones burn. 11Then set the empty pot on the coals so its bronze gets hot and scorched. Let its filth melt inside it, and let its rust be burned away. 12She has worn herself out, but her heavy rust has not come out. Even the fire has not removed her rust. 13Because of your unclean ways, I tried to make you clean, but you would not be clean. So you will not be clean again until MymyMy anger against you is finished. 14I, the LORD, have spoken. It is coming, and I will do it. I will not hold back. I will not show pity. I will not change MymyMy mind. They will judge you by your ways and your deeds, says the Lord GOD."

Ezekiel's Wife Dies as a Sign

15The word of the LORD came to me. 16"Son of man, I am about to take from you the one you love most. She will die suddenly, but you must not mourn or weep. Do not let your tears fall. 17Groan quietly. Do not mourn for the dead. Keep your turban on your head and your sandals on your feet. Do not cover your upper lip. Do not eat the food other people bring you."

18So I spoke to the people in the morning. In the evening my wife died. The next morning I did what I had been told to do.

19The people asked me, "Won't you tell us what these things mean for us, since you are acting this way?"
