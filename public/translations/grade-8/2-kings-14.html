Amaziah Becomes King of Judah

1In the second year of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash began to reign as king of Judah. 2He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jehoaddin, and she was from Jerusalem. 3He did what pleased the LORD, but not like his ancestor David. He did everything his father Joash had done. 4But he did not remove the high places. The people still sacrificed and burned incense there.

5Once he had full control of the kingdom, he killed the servants who had murdered his father the king. 6But he did not kill their children, because the BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw of Moses says the LORD commanded, "Parents must not be put to death for their children, and children must not be put to death for their parents. Each person must die for his own sin."

Amaziah Defeats Edom

7He also killed ten thousand Edomites in the ValleyvalleyValley of SaltsaltSalt and captured Sela in battle. He renamed it Joktheel, and it still has that name today.

War with Israel

8Then Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us meet in battle."

9Jehoash king of Israel sent this reply to Amaziah king of Judah: "A thistle in Lebanon sent a message to a cedar in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son in marriage.' Then a wild animal in Lebanon walked by and trampled the thistle."

10"You defeated Edom, and now you are proud. Stay home and enjoy your victory. Why should you stir up trouble and bring disaster on you and Judah?"

11But Amaziah would not listen. So Jehoash king of Israel attacked, and he and Amaziah king of Judah faced each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah. 12Judah was defeated by Israel, and every soldier ran home. 13Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Ahaziah, at Beth-shemesh. Then he went to Jerusalem and broke down its wall from the Ephraim GategateGate to the CornercornerCorner GategateGate, four hundred cubits. 14He took all the gold and silver and all the articles found in the LORD's temple and in the royal treasuries. He also took hostages and returned to Samaria.

15The rest of the acts of Jehoash, including his power and his war with Amaziah king of Judah, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 16Jehoash died and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel. His son Jeroboam became king in his place.

Amaziah's Death and Azariah's Rise

17Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel. 18The rest of the acts of Amaziah are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 19Some men in Jerusalem plotted against him, so he fled to Lachish. But they sent men after him to Lachish and killed him there. 20They brought his body back on horses and buried him in Jerusalem with his ancestors in the CitycityCity of David. 21Then all the people of Judah chose Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. 22He rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah after Amaziah died.

Jeroboam Rules Israel

23In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash became king of Israel in Samaria and reigned forty-one years. 24He did what the LORD considered evil. He did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had led Israel to commit. 25He restored the border of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the SeaseaSea of the Arabah, just as the LORD, the God of Israel, had said through HishisHis servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher. 26The LORD saw how bitterly Israel was suffering. No one was left, slave or free, and there was no one to help Israel. 27The LORD had not said HeheHe would wipe Israel out completely, so HeheHe saved them through Jeroboam son of Joash. 28The rest of the acts of Jeroboam, all he did, his power, his wars, and how he recovered Damascus and Hamath for Israel, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel. 29Jeroboam died and was buried with the kings of Israel. His son Zechariah became king in his place.
