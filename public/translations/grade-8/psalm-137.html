By the Rivers of Babylon

1By the rivers of Babylon, we sat down and wept when we remembered Zion. 2We hung our harps on the willow trees there. 3Our captors asked us to sing, and our tormentors wanted us to be happy. They said, "Sing us one of the songs of Zion."

4But how can we sing the the LORD's song in a foreign land?

Remembering Jerusalem

5If I forget you, Jerusalem, may I lose the use of my right hand. 6May I lose my voice if I do not remember you, if I do not put Jerusalem above my greatest joy.

A Cry for Justice

7Remember, O the the LORD, what the sons of Edom did on the day Jerusalem fell. They said, "Tear it down! Tear it down to its foundations!" 8O daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, happy is the one who pays you back for what you did to us. 9Happy is the one who seizes your little ones and dashes them against the rocks.
