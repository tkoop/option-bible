Dead to Sin and Alive to God

1So what should we say? Should we keep on sinning so that grace can increase? 2Absolutely not! We died to sin. How can we still live in it? 3Don't you know that all of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into HishisHis death? 4We were buried with HimhimHim through baptism into death. This happened so that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the Father's glory, we too can live a new life. 5If we have been joined to HimhimHim in a death like HishisHis, then we will also be joined to HimhimHim in a resurrection like HishisHis. 6We know that our old self was crucified with HimhimHim so that sin's power over the body would be broken. Then we would no longer be slaves to sin. 7Anyone who has died has been set free from sin. 8If we died with Christ, we believe that we will also live with HimhimHim. 9We know that Christ was raised from the dead and will never die again. Death no longer rules over HimhimHim. 10The death HeheHe died, HeheHe died to sin once for all. The life HeheHe lives now, HeheHe lives to God. 11In the same way, you must think of yourselves as dead to sin but alive to God in Christ Jesus.

Do Not Let Sin Rule Your Body

12So do not let sin rule in your mortal body. Do not obey its evil desires. 13Do not offer the parts of your body to sin as tools for wickedness. Instead, offer yourselves to God as people who have been brought from death to life. Offer the parts of your body to HimhimHim as tools for righteousness. 14Sin will not be your master, because you are not under the law but under grace.

Slaves to God, Not Sin

15What then? Should we sin because we are not under the law but under grace? Absolutely not! 16Don't you know that when you offer yourselves as slaves to obey someone, you are slaves to the one you obey? You are slaves either to sin, which leads to death, or to obedience, which leads to righteousness. 17But thanks be to God! You used to be slaves to sin, but now you have obeyed from the heart the teaching you were given. 18You have been set free from sin and have become slaves to righteousness.

The Payment for Sin Is Death

19I am using a human example because your human nature is weak. Just as you once offered the parts of your body as slaves to impurity and to more and more wrong, now offer them as slaves to righteousness that leads to holiness. 20When you were slaves to sin, you were free from the control of righteousness. 21What benefit did you get then from the things you are now ashamed of? The result of those things is death. 22But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves to God, the result you get leads to holiness, and the end is eternal life. 23The payment for sin is death, but God's gift is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.
