Sinners Draw Near to Jesus

1Now all the tax collectors and sinners were coming close to Jesus to hear HimhimHim. 2The Pharisees and the teachers of the law grumbled, saying, "This man welcomes sinners and eats with them." 3So HeheHe told them this parable:

The Lost Sheep

4"What man among you who has a hundred sheep, if he loses one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the open country and go after the lost one until he finds it?

5"And when he finds it, he puts it on his shoulders and rejoices.

6"When he comes home, he calls his friends and neighbors together and says,

"'Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost.'

7"I tell you, there is more joy in Heavenheavenheaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who do not need to repent."

The Lost Coin

8"Or what woman who has ten silver coins, if she loses one of them, does not light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it?

9"When she finds it, she calls her friends and neighbors together and says,

"'Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin I lost.'

10"In the same way, I tell you, there is joy before God's angels over one sinner who repents."

The Prodigal Son

11"There was a man who had two sons.

12"The younger son said to his father,

"'Father, give me my share of the estate.'

"So he divided his property between them.

13"After a few days, the younger son gathered everything he had and left for a distant country. There he wasted his money on wild living.

14"After he had spent it all, a severe famine hit that country, and he began to be in need.

15"So he went and hired himself out to a citizen of that country, who sent him to his fields to feed pigs.

16"He longed to fill his stomach with the pods the pigs were eating, but no one gave him anything.

17"When he came to his senses, he said,

"'How many of my father's hired workers have more than enough bread, while I am starving to death here! 18I will get up and go to my father and say to him, "FatherfatherFather, I have sinned against Heavenheavenheaven and against you. 19I am no longer worthy to be called your son. Treat me like one of your hired workers."'

20"So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still a long way off, his father saw him and felt sorry for him. He ran to his son, threw his arms around him, and kissed him.

21"The son said to him,

"'Father, I have sinned against Heavenheavenheaven and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.'

22"But the father said to his servants,

"And they began to celebrate.

25"Now the older son was in the field. As he came near the house, he heard music and dancing.

26"He called one of the servants and asked what was going on.
