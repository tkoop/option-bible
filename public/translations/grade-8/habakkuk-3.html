Habakkuk's Fear and Faith

16I heard, and my body shook. My lips trembled at the sound. Weakness entered my bones, and I shook all over. Yet I will wait quietly for the day of trouble, when the enemy comes against my people.

Joy in the LORD

17Even if the fig tree does not bloom and the vines have no grapes; even if the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food; even if the sheep are taken from the pen and there are no cattle in the stalls. 18Yet I will rejoice in the the LORD. I will be glad in the God who saves me. 19The LORD, the Lord, is my strength. He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and lets me walk on the high places.
