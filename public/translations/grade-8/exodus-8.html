The Plague of Frogs

1Then the LORD said to Moses, "Go to Pharaoh and tell him, 'This is what the LORD says: Let MymyMy people go, so that they may worship MemeMe. 2If you refuse to let them go, I will plague all your country with frogs. 3The Nile will teem with frogs. They will come up into your palace, into your bedroom and onto your bed, into the houses of your officials and your people, and into your ovens and kneading bowls. 4The frogs will come up on you, your people, and all your officials.'"

5And the LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers, canals, and ponds, and make the frogs come up onto the land of Egypt.'"

6So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land. 7But the magicians did the same thing by their magic, and they also brought frogs up onto the land of Egypt.

8Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Pray to the LORD to take the frogs away from me and my people, and I will let your people go to sacrifice to the LORD."

9Moses said to Pharaoh, "I'll let you choose when I should pray for you, your officials, and your people, so that the frogs will be taken away from you and your houses. They will stay only in the Nile."

10"Tomorrow," Pharaoh said.

Moses replied, "It will be as you say, so that you may know that there is no one like the LORD our God. 11The frogs will leave you and your houses and your officials and your people; they will stay only in the Nile."

12Then Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh's presence, and Moses cried out to the LORD about the frogs HeheHe had brought on Pharaoh.

13And the LORD did as Moses asked, and the frogs died in the houses, courtyards, and fields. 14They were piled into countless heaps, and the land stank.

15But when Pharaoh saw that there was relief, he hardened his heart and would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

The Plague of Gnats

16Then the LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the earth, so that it becomes gnats throughout the land of Egypt.'"

17So Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the earth, and gnats came upon people and animals. All the dust of the earth became gnats throughout the land of Egypt.

18The magicians tried to produce gnats by their magic, but they could not. And the gnats were on people and animals.

19The magicians said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." But Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he would not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

The Plague of Flies

20Then the LORD said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and present yourself before Pharaoh as he goes out to the water. Tell him, 'This is what the LORD says: Let MymyMy people go, so that they may worship MemeMe. 21If you do not let MymyMy people go, I will send swarms of flies on you, your officials, your people, and your houses. The houses of the Egyptians will be full of swarms, and even the ground they stand on. 22But on that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, where MymyMy people live, so that no swarms of flies will be there. Then you will know that I am the LORD in the middle of the earth. 23I will put a difference between MymyMy people and your people. Tomorrow this sign will happen.'"

24And the LORD did so. Thick swarms of flies poured into Pharaoh's palace and the houses of his officials, and throughout Egypt the land was ruined by the swarms.

25Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Go, sacrifice to your God within the land."

26But Moses said, "It would not be right to do that, because the sacrifices we offer to the LORD our God would be disgusting to the Egyptians. If we sacrifice something the Egyptians hate right in front of them, won't they stone us? 27We must go a three days' journey into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God, as HeheHe commands us."

28Pharaoh said, "I will let you go to sacrifice to the LORD your God in the wilderness, but you must not go very far. Pray for me."

29Moses answered, "As soon as I leave you, I will pray to the LORD, and tomorrow the swarms will leave Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. But Pharaoh must not trick anyone again by refusing to let the people go to sacrifice to the LORD."

30So Moses left Pharaoh's presence and prayed to the LORD.

31And the LORD did as Moses asked and removed the swarms from Pharaoh, his officials, and his people. Not one fly was left.

32But this time Pharaoh hardened his heart too, and he did not let the people go.
