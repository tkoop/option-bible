Samson's Anger at the Philistines

1After a few days, during the wheat harvest, Samson went to visit his wife and brought a young goat with him. He said, "I'm going in to my wife in her room." But her father wouldn't let him go in.

2Her father said, "I really thought you hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Isn't her younger sister better than she is? Please take her instead."

3Samson told them, "This time I won't be blamed for the harm I do to the Philistines." 4Then Samson went out and caught three hundred foxes. He took torches, tied the foxes together tail to tail, and put one torch between each pair of tails. 5He set fire to the torches and released the foxes into the Philistines' standing grain. The fire burned everything from the stacked grain to the standing grain, and even the olive vineyards.

6The Philistines asked who had done this. They were told that Samson, the son-in-law of the man from Timnah, had done it because his father-in-law had given Samson's wife to his companion. So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father to death.

7Samson said to them, "If you do that, I won't stop until I've taken revenge on you." 8He struck them down with a great slaughter and then went down to live in a cave in the rock of Etam.

The Philistines Demand Samson

9The Philistines went up and camped in Judah, spreading out near Lehi. 10The men of Judah asked, "Why have you come against us?"

They answered, "We came to tie up Samson and treat him the way he treated us."

11Three thousand men of Judah went down to the rock of Etam and said to Samson, "Don't you know the Philistines rule over us? What have you done to us?"

He said to them, "I only did to them what they did to me."

12They said to him, "We've come down to tie you up and hand you over to the Philistines."

Samson said, "Swear to me that you won't kill me yourselves."

13They said, "No, we'll only tie you up and hand you over to them. We won't kill you." So they tied him with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

Samson's Victory at Lehi

14When he reached Lehi, the Philistines shouted as they met him. Then the SpiritspiritSpirit of the LORD rushed on him. The ropes on his arms became like burned flax, and his bonds fell off his hands. 15He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey, reached out and took it, and struck down a thousand men with it.

16Samson said, "With a donkey's jawbone I've piled them in heaps. With a donkey's jawbone I've killed a thousand men." 17When he finished speaking, he threw the jawbone away and named that place Ramath Lehi.

God Gives Samson Water

18Samson was very thirsty, so he called to the LORD and said, "YouyouYou have given this great victory through YouryourYour servant. Now must I die of thirst and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?"

19Then God split open the hollow place in Lehi, and water came out of it. Samson drank, his strength returned, and he revived. So he named the place En Hakkore, and it is still in Lehi today. 20Samson judged Israel for twenty years during the time of the Philistines.
