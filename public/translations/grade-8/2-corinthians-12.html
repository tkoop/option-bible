Paul's Vision and His Thorn

1I must go on boasting, even though it does no good. I will move on to visions and revelations from the Lord. 2I know a man in Christ who was caught up to the third Heavenheavenheaven fourteen years ago. I do not know whether it happened in the body or out of the body. God knows.

3I know that this man—whether in the body or out of it I do not know, but God knows— 4was caught up to Paradise and heard words that cannot be spoken, words a person is not allowed to tell.

Boasting in Weakness

5I will boast about a man like that, but I will not boast about myself, except in my weaknesses. 6Even if I wanted to boast, I would not be a fool, because I would be telling the truth. But I hold back, so no one will think more of me than what he sees in me or hears from me. 7To keep me from becoming proud because of these great revelations, I was given a thorn in my flesh. It is a messenger from Satan to torment me, to keep me from becoming proud.

8Three times I begged the Lord to take it away from me.

9But HeheHe said to me, "MymyMy grace is enough for you, because MymyMy power is made perfect in weakness."

So I will boast even more gladly in my weaknesses, so that Christ's power may rest on me. 10That is why, for Christ's sake, I am glad to have weaknesses, insults, hard times, persecutions, and troubles. When I am weak, then I am strong.

Paul's Care for the Corinthians

11I have become a fool, but you drove me to it. You should have praised me, because I am not in any way less than the "super-apostles," even though I am nothing.

12The signs of an apostle—signs, wonders, and miracles—were done among you with great patience. 13What did you have that was less than the other churches, except that I did not burden you? Forgive me for this wrong!

14Look, I am ready to come to you a third time, and I will not be a burden. I am not looking for your money but for you. Children should not have to save up for their parents, but parents for their children. 15I will gladly spend and be spent for your souls. If I love you more, am I to be loved less?

16Even so, I did not burden you. Yet, crafty as I am, I caught you by trickery. 17Did I take advantage of you through any of the men I sent to you? 18I urged Titus to go and sent our brother with him. Did Titus take advantage of you? Did we not walk in the same SpiritspiritSpirit and follow the same path? 19Have you been thinking all along that we have been defending ourselves before you? We speak before God in Christ, and everything we do, dear friends, is to build you up.

Paul's Concerns Before His Visit

20I am afraid that when I come, I may not find you as I want, and you may not find me as you want. I fear there may be quarreling, jealousy, anger, selfish ambition, slander, gossip, pride, and disorder. 21I am afraid that when I come again, MymyMy God will humble me before you, and I will be sad about many who sinned before and have not turned from their impurity, sexual sin, and wild living.
