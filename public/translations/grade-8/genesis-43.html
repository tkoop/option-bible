The Famine Worsens

1Now the famine was severe in the land. 2When they had eaten all the grain they brought from Egypt, their father said, "Go back and buy us a little more food."

3But Judah said to him, "ThetheThe man warned us firmly, 'YouyouYou will not see my face unless your brother is with you.' 4If you send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you. 5But if you do not send him, we will not go down, because the man said to us, 'YouyouYou will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

6Israel said, "Why did you treat me so badly by telling the man you had another brother?"

7They said, "ThetheThe man questioned us closely about ourselves and our family. He asked, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have another brother?' We answered him as best we could. How could we know he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?"

Judah Promises to Protect Benjamin

8Judah said to his father Israel, "Send the boy with me, and we will go at once so that we may live and not die—both we and you and our little ones. 9I will be responsible for him. You can hold me accountable. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, then I will be guilty before you all my life. 10If we had not delayed, we could have gone and come back twice by now."

Jacob Sends Benjamin to Egypt

11Their father Israel said to them, "If it must be, then do this: Take some of the best products of the land in your bags and carry a gift down to the man—a little balm and honey, gum, myrrh, pistachios, and almonds. 12Take double the money with you. Carry back the money that was returned in the mouths of your sacks. Maybe it was a mistake. 13Take your brother too, and go back to the man at once. 14May God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty grant you mercy before the man, so that he will let your other brother and Benjamin go with you. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."

15So the men took the gift, double money, and Benjamin. They hurried down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.

Joseph Welcomes and Feasts His Brothers

16When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the steward of his house, "Take these men to my house. Slaughter an animal and prepare it, because these men will eat with me at noon." 17The man did as Joseph said and brought the men to Joseph's house.

18The men were afraid because they were brought to Joseph's house. They said, "We are being brought here because of the money that was returned in our sacks the first time. He wants to attack us, overpower us, and make us slaves, along with our donkeys."

19They went up to the steward at the door of the house and spoke to him. 20"Please, my lord," they said, "we came down the first time to buy food. 21When we reached the lodging place and opened our sacks, each man's money was in the mouth of his sack—the exact amount. So we brought it back with us. 22We have also brought more money to buy food. We do not know who put our money in our sacks."

23The steward said, "Peace to you. Do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has put treasure in your sacks. I received your money." Then he brought Simeon out to them. 24The steward took the men into Joseph's house. He gave them water, and they washed their feet. He gave fodder to their donkeys.

25They prepared the gift for Joseph's arrival at noon, because they had heard they would eat there. 26When Joseph came home, they brought him the gift from their hands and bowed to the ground before him.

27He asked about their well-being and said, "IsisIs your old father well, the one you told me about? Is he still alive?"

28They said, "YouryourYour servant our father is well; he is still alive." Then they bowed down and paid homage.

29Joseph looked up and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son. He said, "IsisIs this your youngest brother you told me about? God be gracious to you, my son." 30Joseph hurried out because he was deeply moved over his brother. He looked for a place to weep and went into his room and wept there. 31He washed his face and came out. He controlled himself and said, "Serve the food."

32They served him by himself, them by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves, because Egyptians could not eat with Hebrews. It was disgusting to Egyptians. 33They sat before him in order of age, from oldest to youngest, and the men stared at one another in wonder. 34He sent portions to them from his table, but Benjamin's portion was five times as much as any of theirs. They drank and were merry with him.
