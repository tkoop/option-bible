1Then Job answered:

Job Replies to His Friends

2"So you think you are the only wise people, and that wisdom will die with you! 3But I have a mind like you; I'm not below you. Who doesn't know things like that?"

4"I've become a joke to my friends, even though I prayed to God and HeheHe answered me. An innocent, honest person has become a joke. 5People who are comfortable look down on trouble, but trouble is ready for anyone who stumbles. 6Robbers' tents are safe, and people who anger God feel secure, because God holds them in HishisHis hand."

Learn from Creation

7"But ask the animals, and they will teach you; or the birds in the air, and they will tell you. 8Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; or let the fish of the sea explain things to you. 9Which of all these doesn't know that the LORD's hand has done this? 10The life of every living thing is in HishisHis hand, along with the breath of all people."

God's Wisdom and Power

11"Doesn't the ear test words the way the tongue tastes food? 12Wisdom is found among the old, and understanding comes with long life. 13Wisdom and strength belong to God; counsel and understanding are HishisHis."

14"What HeheHe tears down can't be rebuilt; the person HeheHe locks up can't be set free. 15If HeheHe holds back the water, it dries up; if HeheHe sends it out, it floods the land. 16Strength and success belong to HimhimHim; both the deceiver and the deceived are HishisHis."

God Brings Down the Proud

17"He leads advisors away barefoot and makes judges look foolish. 18He loosens the bonds kings put in place and ties a belt around their waists. 19He leads priests away barefoot and overthrows those who seemed secure. 20He takes speech from trusted people and takes away the good judgmentjudgementjudgment of elders. 21He pours contempt on nobles and weakens the strong."

22"He reveals deep things hidden in darkness and brings dark shadows into the light. 23He makes nations great, and HeheHe destroys them; HeheHe expands nations, and then HeheHe leads them away. 24He takes away the understanding of EarthEarthearth's leaders and leaves them wandering in a land with no path. 25They feel around in the dark with no light, and HeheHe makes them stagger like drunk people."
