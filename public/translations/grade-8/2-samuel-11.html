David Stays in Jerusalem

1In the spring, when kings go out to war, David sent Joab, his officers, and all Israel to fight the Ammonites. They attacked Rabbah, but David stayed in Jerusalem.

David and Bathsheba

2One evening David got up from his bed and walked on the roof of the king's palace. From the roof he saw a woman bathing. She was very beautiful. 3David sent someone to find out who she was. The man said, "Isn't this Bathsheba, daughter of Eliam and wife of Uriah the Hittite?"

4David sent messengers to get her. She came to him, and he slept with her. She had just finished her monthly purification, and then she went home. 5The woman became pregnant. She sent word to David and said, "I am pregnant."

Uriah Refuses to Go Home

6David sent a message to Joab: "Send me Uriah the Hittite." Joab sent Uriah to David. 7Uriah came to David. David asked how Joab was, how the soldiers were, and how the war was going.

8David said to Uriah, "Go to your house and wash your feet." As Uriah left the palace, the king sent a gift after him. 9But Uriah slept at the entrance to the palace with all his master's servants. He did not go down to his house.

10David was told, "Uriah did not go down to his house." David asked Uriah, "YouyouYou have just come from a trip. Why didn't you go down to your house?"

11Uriah said to David, "ThetheThe ark and Israel and Judah are staying in tents. My master Joab and his servants are camping in the open field. Should I go to my house to eat and drink and sleep with my wife? As surely as you live, I will not do this."

12David said to Uriah, "Stay here today. Tomorrow I will send you back." So Uriah stayed in Jerusalem that day and the next. 13David invited him to eat and drink with him and made him drunk. That evening Uriah went out to sleep on his bed with his master's servants, but he did not go down to his house.

David Has Uriah Killed

14In the morning David wrote a letter to Joab and sent it with Uriah. 15In the letter he wrote, "Put Uriah in the front line where the fighting is hardest. Then pull back from him so he will be struck down and die."

16While Joab was attacking the city, he put Uriah in a place where he knew there were strong soldiers. 17The men of the city came out and fought Joab. Some of David's men fell, and Uriah the Hittite died too.

18Joab sent David a report of the whole battle. 19He told the messenger, "When you have finished telling the king everything about the battle, 20the king may get angry and ask, 'Why did you go so close to the city to fight? Didn't you know they would shoot from the wall? 21Who killed Abimelech son of Jerub-Besheth? Didn't a woman throw a millstone on him from the wall, so he died at Thebez? Why did you go so close to the wall?' Then say, 'YouryourYour servant Uriah the Hittite is dead too.'"

22ThetheThe messenger went and told David everything Joab had sent him to say. 23The messenger said to David, "ThetheThe men overpowered us and came out against us in the open country, but we pushed them back to the entrance of the gate. 24The archers shot at your servants from the wall, and some of the king's servants died. Your servant Uriah the Hittite is dead too."

25David said to the messenger, "Say this to Joab: 'Do not let this upset you. The sword destroys one person as well as another. Keep fighting hard against the city and destroy it.' Encourage him."

26Uriah's wife heard that her husband was dead. She mourned for him. 27When her time of mourning was over, David sent for her and brought her to his house. She became his wife and bore him a son. But what David had done displeased the LORD.
