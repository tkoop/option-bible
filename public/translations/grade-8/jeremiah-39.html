Jerusalem Falls and Zedekiah Is Captured

1In the ninth year of Zedekiah's reign as king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against Jerusalem with his whole army and besieged the city. 2In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was broken into. 3Then all the officials of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the MiddlemiddleMiddle GategateGate: Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the chief official, Nergal-sharezer the chief magician, and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.

4When Zedekiah king of Judah and all the soldiers saw them, they fled. They left the city at night by the king's garden, through the gate between the two walls, and headed toward the Arabah. 5But the Babylonian army chased them and caught Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho. They took him to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, where the king of Babylon sentenced him. 6At Riblah the king of Babylon killed Zedekiah's sons while Zedekiah watched, and he also killed all the nobles of Judah. 7Then he put out Zedekiah's eyes, bound him with bronze chains, and took him to Babylon.

The City Is Destroyed and the People Are Exiled

8The Babylonians burned the royal palace and the houses of the people, and they tore down the walls of Jerusalem. 9Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, carried into exile the rest of the people who remained in the city, along with the deserters who had gone over to him and the rest of the people. 10But Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, left in the land of Judah some of the poorest people, who had nothing, and that same day he gave them vineyards and fields.

Jeremiah Is Protected

11Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had given orders about Jeremiah through Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard. he said, 12"Take him and watch over him. Do not harm him in any way, but do for him whatever he asks you."

13So Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard, Nebushazban the chief official, Nergal-sharezer the chief magician, and all the other officials of the king of Babylon sent for Jeremiah. 14They took him out of the courtyard of the guard and handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, to take him home. So he stayed among the people.

A Promise to Ebed-Melech

15While Jeremiah was still locked up in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the the LORD came to him. He said, 16"Go and say to Ebed-Melech the Cushite, 'This is what the the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am bringing MymyMy words against this city for disaster, not for good. They will come true before you on that day. 17But I will rescue you on that day, says the the LORD. You will not be handed over to the men you fear. 18I will surely save you. You will not die by the sword. Instead, you will get to keep your life, because you trusted in MemeMe, says the the LORD.'"
