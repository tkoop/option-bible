Hezekiah Becomes Sick

1In those days Hezekiah became so sick that he was going to die. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, "This is what the LORD says: Put your house in order, because you are going to die. You will not live."

2Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD:

3"Please, the LORD, remember how I have walked before you in truth and with a whole heart. I have done what is good in your sight." Then Hezekiah cried and wept bitterly.

God Answers Hezekiah's Prayer

4Before Isaiah had left the middle courtyard, the word of the LORD came to him. 5The LORD said, "Go back and tell Hezekiah, the ruler of MymyMy people: This is what the LORD, the God of your father David, says: I have heard your prayer. I have seen your tears. Look, I will heal you. On the third day you will go up to the house of the LORD. 6I will add fifteen years to your life. I will rescue you and this city from the power of the king of Assyria. I will defend this city for MymyMy own sake and for the sake of MymyMy servant David."

7Then Isaiah said, "BringbringBring a fig cake." They brought it and put it on the sore, and he recovered.

A Sign for Hezekiah

8Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "What sign will show that the LORD will heal me and that I will go up to the house of the LORD on the third day?"

9Isaiah said, "This is the sign to you from the LORD that the LORD will do what HeheHe has promised: Will the shadow go forward ten steps, or go back ten steps?"

10Hezekiah answered, "It would be easy for the shadow to go forward ten steps. No, let it go back ten steps." 11Then Isaiah the prophet cried out to the LORD, and HeheHe made the shadow go back the ten steps it had gone down on the stairway of Ahaz.

Messengers from Babylon

12At that time Merodach-Baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, because he had heard that Hezekiah was sick. 13Hezekiah welcomed the messengers and showed them all his treasure house—the silver, the gold, the spices, and the fine oil—his armouryarmouryarmory, and everything in his storehouses. There was nothing in his palace or in all his kingdom that Hezekiah did not show them.

14Then Isaiah the prophet went to King Hezekiah and asked, "What did these men say, and where did they come from?"

Hezekiah replied, "They came from a distant land, from Babylon."

15Isaiah asked, "What did they see in your palace?"

Hezekiah answered, "They saw everything in my palace. There is nothing in my storehouses that I did not show them."

Isaiah's Warning and Hezekiah's Death

16Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Hear the word of the LORD: 17The time will surely come when everything in your palace, and all that your fathers have stored up until this day, will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left," says the LORD. 18"And some of your descendants, your own flesh and blood, will be taken away to be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon." 19Then Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "ThetheThe word of the LORD that you have spoken is good." For he thought, "WillwillWill there not be peace and security during my lifetime?"

20The rest of Hezekiah's acts, along with all his might and how he made the pool and the tunnel and brought water into the city, are written in the BookbookBook of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah. 21Hezekiah died and was buried with his ancestors, and his son Manasseh became king in his place.
