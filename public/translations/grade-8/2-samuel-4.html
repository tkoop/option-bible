Ish-bosheth Hears of Abner's Death

1When Ish-bosheth, Saul's son, heard that Abner had died in Hebron, he lost his courage, and all Israel was alarmed. 2Saul's son had two men who led raiding bands. One was named Baanah, and the other was Rechab. They were sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, from the tribe of Benjamin, because Beeroth was counted as part of Benjamin. 3The people of Beeroth had run away to Gittaim and lived there as outsiders up to this day.

Mephibosheth's Injury

4Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was lame in both feet. He was five years old when news came from Jezreel that Saul and Jonathan had died. His nurse picked him up and ran, but as she hurried away, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

Ish-bosheth Is Murdered

5Rechab and Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, went to Ish-bosheth's house at the hottest part of the day, around noon, while he was resting. 6They came into the house as if to get wheat, and they stabbed him in the stomach. Then Rechab and his brother Baanah escaped. 7They had come into the house while he lay on his bed in his bedroom. They struck him, killed him, cut off his head, and took it with them. They travelledtravelledtraveled all night through the Arabah. 8They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David in Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who tried to take your life. Today the LORD has given my lord the king revenge against Saul and his family."

David Judges the Murderers

9David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said, "As the LORD lives, who has saved my life from every trouble, 10when someone told me, 'Saul is dead,' thinking he was bringing good news, I grabbed him and killed him at Ziklag. That was the reward I gave him for his news. 11So how much more should I deal with wicked men who killed a good man in his own house on his bed? Should I not now demand his blood from your hand and remove you from the earth?" 12David commanded his young men, and they killed them, cut off their hands and feet, and hung them by the pool in Hebron. They took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in Abner's tomb in Hebron.
