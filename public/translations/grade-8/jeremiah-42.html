The People Ask Jeremiah to Pray

1All the army commanders, Johanan son of Kareah, Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and all the people, from the least to the greatest, came to Jeremiah the prophet.

2They said to him, "Please hear our request and pray to the LORD your God for us—for all of us who are left. We are only a few out of many, as your eyes see us. 3May the LORD your God show us the way we should go and what we should do."

4Jeremiah the prophet said to them, "I have heard you. I am praying to the LORD your God just as you asked. I will tell you everything the LORD answers, and I will not hold back a single word."

5They said to Jeremiah, "May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not do everything the LORD your God sends you to tell us. 6Whether it is good or bad, we will obey the LORD our God, to whom we are sending you, so that things will go well for us, because we will obey the LORD our God."

The LORD Answers After Ten Days

7Ten days later, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah.

8He called Johanan son of Kareah, all the army commanders with him, and all the people, from the least to the greatest.

The LORD Warns Against Going to Egypt

13"But if you say, 'We will not stay in this land,' and you disobey the LORD your God, 14saying, 'No, we will go to Egypt, where we will not see war, hear the trumpet, or go hungry for bread, and we will live there'— 15then listen to the word of the LORD, O remnant of Judah! This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: 'If you are determined to go to Egypt and live there, 16then the sword you fear will catch up with you there in Egypt.
