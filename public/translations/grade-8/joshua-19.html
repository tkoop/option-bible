Land for Simeon and Zebulun

1The second lot came out for the clans of the tribe of Simeon. Their inheritance was inside Judah's territory. 2It included Beersheba, or Sheba, Moladah, 3Hazar-shual, Balah, and Ezem, 4Eltolad, Bethul, and Hormah, 5Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah, 6Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen—thirteen cities with their villages. 7Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan—four cities with their villages. 8It also included all the villages around these cities as far as Baalath-beer, that is, Ramath of the Negev. This was the inheritance of the clans of Simeon. 9Simeon's inheritance came from Judah's territory, because Judah's share was too large. So Simeon received land inside Judah's share.

10The third lot came up for the clans of Zebulun. The border of their land went to Sarid. 11It went west to Maralah, touched Dabbesheth, and met the brook east of Jokneam. 12From Sarid it turned east toward the sunrise, to the border of Kisloth-tabor. Then it went to Daberath and up to Japhia. 13From there it passed east to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, then came out at Rimmon, which borders Neah. 14The border then turned north to Hannathon and ended at the ValleyvalleyValley of Iphtah-el. 15It also included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem—twelve cities with their villages. 16These cities and villages were the inheritance of the clans of Zebulun.

Land for Issachar and Asher

17The fourth lot came out for the clans of Issachar. 18Their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, and Shunem, 19Hapharaim, Shion, and Anaharath, 20Rabbith, Kishion, and Abbez, 21Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez. 22The border reached Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, and ended at the Jordan—sixteen cities with their villages. 23These cities and villages were the inheritance of the clans of Issachar.

24The fifth lot came out for the clans of Asher. 25Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, and Achshaph, 26Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal. On the west the border touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath. 27Then it turned east to Beth-dagon, touched Zebulun and the ValleyvalleyValley of Iphtah-el, and went north to Beth-emek and Neiel, passing Cabul on the left. 28It went on to Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as GreatergreaterGreater Sidon. 29The border then turned back toward Ramah and the fortified city of Tyre, turned toward Hosah, and ended at the SeaseaSea in the region of Achzib. 30It also included Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob—twenty-two cities with their villages. 31These cities and villages were the inheritance of the clans of Asher.

Land for Naphtali and Dan

32The sixth lot came out for the clans of Naphtali. 33Their border went from Heleph and the oak of Zaanannim, past Adami-nekeb and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum, and ended at the Jordan. 34Then the border turned west to Aznoth-tabor and went from there to Hukkok, touching Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan on the east. 35The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth, 36Adamah, Ramah, and Hazor, 37Kedesh, Edrei, and En-hazor, 38Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh—nineteen cities with their villages. 39These cities and villages were the inheritance of the clans of Naphtali.

40The seventh lot came out for the clans of Dan. 41The territory of their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh, 42Shaalabbin, Aijalon, and Ithlah, 43Elon, Timnah, and Ekron, 44Eltekeh, Gibbethon, and Baalath, 45Jehud, Bene-berak, and Gath-rimmon, 46Me-jarkon, and Rakkon, near the border facing Joppa. 47But Dan's territory was too small for them. So the Danites went up, fought against Leshem, captured it, put it to the sword, and took possession of it. They settled there and renamed Leshem Dan, after their father Dan. 48These cities and villages were the inheritance of the clans of Dan.

Joshua's Inheritance

49When they had finished dividing the land by its borders, the Israelites gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them. 50as the the LORD had commanded. They gave him the city he asked for—Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim. He rebuilt the city and settled in it. 51These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the tribes of Israel distributed by lot at Shiloh before the the LORD at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. So they finished dividing the land.
