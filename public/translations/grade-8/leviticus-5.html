Sins That Require a Sin Offering

1If someone sins by not speaking up when he is called to testify in public, even though he saw or knows about it, he will be guilty. 2Or if someone touches anything unclean, such as the dead body of an unclean wild animal, farm animal, or small crawling creature, without knowing it, he is unclean and guilty. 3Or if he touches human uncleanness—any kind of uncleanness that makes a person unclean—and he does not know it, when he finds out, he is guilty. 4Or if someone makes a careless oath with his lips, to do good or harm—whatever a person might say in an oath—and he does not know it, when he learns of it, he is guilty of one of these things. 5When he is guilty of any of these, he must confess the sin he has done. 6He must bring his guilt offering to the the LORD for the sin he committed: a female lamb or goat from the flock, as a sin offering. Then the priest will make atonement for him for that sin.

Offerings for Those Who Cannot Afford a Lamb

7If he cannot afford a lamb, he must bring to the the LORD two turtledoves or two young pigeons as his guilt offering for the sin he committed—one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering. 8He must bring them to the priest, who will first offer the one for the sin offering. He will twist its head from its neck but not pull it off. 9Then he must sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar. The rest of the blood must be poured out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering. 10The second bird he must prepare as a burnt offering, following the proper rule. So the priest will make atonement for him for the sin he committed, and he will be forgiven. 11But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he may bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. He must not put oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering. 12He must bring it to the priest. The priest will take a handful of it as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar on top of the offerings made by fire to the the LORD. It is a sin offering. 13In this way the priest will make atonement for him for any of these sins he has committed, and he will be forgiven. The rest will belong to the priest, as with the grain offering.

Guilt Offerings for Sins Against the LORD

14Then the the LORD said to Moses,

15"If someone sins without meaning to and misuses one of the the LORD's holy things, he must bring his guilt offering to the the LORD: a ram from the flock without defect, worth the proper amount in silver shekels by the sanctuary standard. It is a guilt offering. 16He must pay back for the holy thing he misused, adding one-fifth to its value, and give it to the priest. Then the priest will make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.

17"If someone sins and does something the the LORD has forbidden, even if he does not know it, he is still guilty and must carry his guilt. 18He must bring to the priest a ram from the flock without defect, worth the proper amount, as a guilt offering. Then the priest will make atonement for him for the wrong he did without knowing it, and he will be forgiven. 19It is a guilt offering; he was indeed guilty before the the LORD."
