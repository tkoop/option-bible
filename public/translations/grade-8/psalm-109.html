David’s Prayer for Help Against His Enemies

For the music leader. A psalm of David.

1O God of my praise, do not stay silent. 2Wicked, lying mouths have opened against me. They have spoken lies about me. 3They surround me with hateful words and attack me for no reason. 4In return for my love, they accuse me, but I keep praying. 5They pay me back evil for good and hatred for my love.

6Put a wicked person in charge of him, and let an accuser stand beside him. 7When he is judged, let him be found guilty. Let his prayer be seen as sin. 8May his days be few. May someone else take his position. 9May his children be orphans and his wife a widow. 10May his children wander and beg. May they look for food far from their ruined homes.

11May a moneylender take everything he owns. May strangers steal what he worked for. 12May no one show him kindness. May no one help his orphaned children. 13May his family line end. May their name be wiped out in the next generation. 14May the LORD remember the sins of his ancestors. May his mother’s sin never be forgiven. 15May their sins always be before the LORD, so HeheHe can remove their memory from the earth.

16For he never thought to show kindness. He hunted down the poor and needy and brokenhearted to kill them. 17He loved to curse, so let curses fall on him. He hated blessing, so let it stay far from him. 18He wore curses like clothing. They soaked into his body like water and into his bones like oil. 19May those curses be like a coat he wears, like a belt he puts on every day. 20May the LORD repay my accusers this way, those who speak evil against me.

21But YouyouYou, O the LORD, my Lord, treat me well for YouryourYour name’s sake. Because YouryourYour faithful love is good, rescue me. 22For I am poor and needy, and my heart is wounded inside me. 23I fade away like a shadow that grows long. I am shaken off like a locust. 24My knees are weak from fasting, and my body has grown thin. 25I am a joke to my accusers. When they see me, they shake their heads.

26Help me, O the LORD, my God. Save me because of YouryourYour faithful love. 27Let them know that this comes from YouyouYou, O the LORD. You have done it. 28They may curse, but YouyouYou will bless. They may rise up, but they will be put to shame. I, YouryourYour servant, will be glad. 29May my accusers be covered with shame. May they wear their disgrace like a coat. 30With my mouth I will thank the LORD greatly. I will praise HimhimHim among many people. 31For HeheHe stands beside the needy, to save them from those who condemn them.
