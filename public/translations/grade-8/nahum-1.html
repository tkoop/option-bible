Nahum's Vision About Nineveh

1A message about Nineveh. This is the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

The LORD's Anger and Power

2The LORD is a jealous and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and full of wrath. The LORD takes vengeance on HishisHis foes and stores up wrath for HishisHis enemies. 3The LORD is slow to anger and great in power; the LORD will not let the guilty go unpunished. His path is in the whirlwind and the storm, and clouds are the dust of HishisHis feet.

4He rebukes the sea and dries it up; HeheHe dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel wither, and the bloom of Lebanon fades. 5The mountains shake before HimhimHim, and the hills melt away. The EarthEarthearth trembles at HishisHis presence, along with the world and everyone who lives in it. 6Who can stand before HishisHis anger? Who can survive the heat of HishisHis wrath? His fury is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before HimhimHim.

The LORD Is a Refuge in Trouble

7The LORD is good, a safe place in times of trouble. He knows those who trust in HimhimHim. 8But with a rushing flood HeheHe will completely destroy its place, and darkness will chase HishisHis enemies.

JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Nineveh and Hope for Judah

9What do you plan against the LORD? He will completely destroy; trouble will not rise up a second time. 10For they are tangled like thorns, and like drunkards they are destroyed; they burn like dry stubble. 11From you came a person who plans evil against the LORD, a worthless counsellorcounsellorcounselor.

12This is what the LORD says: "Though they are strong and many, they will be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more. 13Now I will break his yoke off you and snap your bonds."

14The LORD has commanded concerning you: "Your name will no longer be passed on. I will cut off the carved idol and the metal idol from the house of your gods. I will make your grave, for you are worthless."

15Look, on the mountains are the feet of one who brings good news, who announces peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah; keep your vows. For the wicked will never again march through you; he is completely cut off.
