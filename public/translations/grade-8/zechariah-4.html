The Vision of the Gold Lampstand

1The angel who had been speaking with me came back and woke me, like someone waking up from sleep.

2He asked, “What do you see?” I answered, “I see a lampstand made of solid gold, with a bowl on top and seven lamps on it. There are seven channels to the lamps on top.”

3There are two olive trees beside it—one on the right of the bowl and one on the left.

4I asked the angel who was speaking with me, “What are these, my lord?”

5The angel answered, “Don’t you know what these are?” “No, my lord,” I said.

A Message for Zerubbabel

6He said, “This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: ‘Not by might and not by power, but by MymyMy SpiritspiritSpirit,’ says the LORD of Hosts.”

7“What are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become a plain. He will bring out the capstone while people shout, ‘Grace, grace to it!’”

8Then the word of the LORD came to me:

9“The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house. His hands will finish it. Then you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you.”

10“For who despises the day of small things? These seven are the eyes of the LORD. They range through all the earth. They will be glad when they see the plumb line in Zerubbabel’s hand.”

The Two Olive Trees Explained

11Then I asked him, “What are these two olive trees on the right and left of the lampstand?”

12I asked a second time, “What are these two olive branches beside the two gold pipes that pour out golden oil?”

13He said, “Don’t you know what these are?” “No, my lord,” I said.

14He said, “These are the two anointed ones who stand by the Lord of all the earth.”
