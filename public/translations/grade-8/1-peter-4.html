Living for God Instead of Sin

1Since Christ suffered in HishisHis body, you too should arm yourselves with the same way of thinking. The person who has suffered in the body is done with sin. 2From now on, you should live the rest of your earthly life not following human desires, but following God's will. 3You have already spent enough time doing what unbelievers want—living in wild parties, strong desires, drunkenness, wild dancing, drinking parties, and illegal worship of idols. 4They are surprised that you don't join them in the same flood of reckless living, and they insult you. 5They will have to answer to God, who is ready to judge the living and the dead. 6That's why the good news was preached even to those who have died. They were judged in the body as people are, but they live in the spirit the way God wants.

Love and Serve One Another

7The end of all things is near. So be clear-minded and self-controlled so you can pray. 8Above all, love each other deeply, because love covers a lot of sins. 9Welcome one another into your homes without complaining. 10Each person has received a gift. Use it to serve one another, like good managers of God's grace in all its different forms. 11If anyone speaks, he should speak as if he were sharing God's words. If anyone serves, he should serve with the strength God gives. Do this so that in everything God will be praised through Jesus Christ. Glory and power belong to HimhimHim forever and ever. Amen.

Suffering for Being a Christian

12Dear friends, don't be surprised by the painful test that is happening among you, as if something strange were happening to you. 13Instead, be glad that you share in Christ's sufferings. Then you will be full of joy when HishisHis glory is shown. 14If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the SpiritspiritSpirit of glory and of God rests on you. 15Make sure none of you suffers as a murderer, a thief, a person who does evil, or a busybody. 16But if you suffer as a Christian, don't be ashamed. Instead, praise God for that name.

Trusting God in Hard Times

17The time has come for judgmentjudgementjudgment to begin with God's family. If it begins with us, what will happen to those who don't obey the good news of God? 18If it's hard for a good person to be saved, what will happen to ungodly people and sinners? 19So those who suffer because it's God's will should entrust their souls to their faithful CreatorcreatorCreator and keep doing good.
