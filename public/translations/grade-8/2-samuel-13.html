Amnon's Desire for Tamar

1After this, Absalom son of David had a beautiful sister named Tamar, and Amnon, another son of David, loved her.

2Amnon was so upset about his sister Tamar that he made himself sick. She was a virgin, and he felt he could not have her.

3Amnon had a friend named Jonadab, son of David's brother Shimeah. Jonadab was a very clever man.

4Jonadab asked him, "Why do you, the king's son, look so worn out every morning? Won't you tell me?"

Amnon said to him, "I love Tamar, my brother Absalom's sister."

Jonadab's Plan

5Jonadab said, "Lie down on your bed and pretend to be sick. When your father comes to see you, say, 'Please let my sister Tamar come and make me some bread. Let her make it where I can watch, and I'll eat it from her hand.'"

6So Amnon lay down and pretended to be sick. When the king came to see him, Amnon said, "Please let my sister Tamar come and make two cakes for me where I can watch, and I'll eat them from her hand."

7David sent word to Tamar at home: "Go to your brother Amnon's house and make food for him."

8Tamar went to her brother Amnon's house. He was lying down. She took dough, kneaded it, made cakes in front of him, and baked them.

9She took the pan and served them to him, but he refused to eat. Amnon said, "Send everyone out." So everyone left.

10Amnon said to Tamar, "BringbringBring the food into the bedroom and feed me from your hand." Tamar took the cakes she had made and brought them to her brother Amnon in the bedroom.

11As she brought them to him to eat, he grabbed her and said, "Come to bed with me, my sister."

Tamar Is Attacked

12She said, "No, my brother! Don't force me. That shouldn't be done in Israel. Don't do this foolish thing. 13Where would I go with my shame? And you would be like one of the fools in Israel. Please, talk to the king. He won't refuse to give me to you."

14But he wouldn't listen to her. He was stronger than she was, so he forced her and lay with her.

15Then Amnon hated her with a very great hatred. The hatred he felt for her was greater than the love he had felt for her. Amnon said to her, "Get up and get out!"

16She said to him, "No! Sending me away like this is a greater wrong than what you already did to me." But he wouldn't listen to her.

17He called his servant who waited on him and said, "Get this woman out of here and lock the door behind her."

18So the servant put her out and locked the door behind her. She was wearing a long robe with sleeves, because that was what the king's unmarried daughters wore.

19Tamar put ashes on her head and tore the long robe she was wearing. She put her hand on her head and went away, crying as she walked.

20Her brother Absalom said to her, "Has your brother Amnon been with you? Keep quiet for now, my sister. He is your brother. Don't take this to heart." So Tamar stayed in her brother Absalom's house, feeling crushed.

21When King David heard about all this, he was very angry.

22Absalom didn't speak to Amnon either good or bad, because Absalom hated Amnon for forcing his sister Tamar.

Absalom's Revenge

23Two years later, Absalom had sheep shearers at Baal-hazor near Ephraim. Absalom invited all the king's sons.

24Absalom came to the king and said, "YouryourYour servant has sheep shearers. Please let the king and his servants come with your servant."

25The king said to Absalom, "No, my son. We shouldn't all go, or we'd be a burden to you." Absalom urged him, but David didn't want to go and blessed him.

26Absalom said, "If not, please let my brother Amnon go with us."

The king said, "Why should he go with you?"

27Absalom kept pressing him, and David sent Amnon and all the king's sons with him.

28Absalom ordered his servants, "Watch Amnon. When he's feeling good from the wine and I tell you, 'Strike Amnon,' then kill him. Don't be afraid. I'm the one giving the order. Be strong and brave."

29Absalom's servants did to Amnon what Absalom had ordered. Then all the king's sons got up, each mounted his mule, and fled.

30While they were on the way, a report reached David: "Absalom has struck down all the king's sons, and not one is left."

31The king stood up, tore his clothes, and lay on the ground. All his servants stood by with their clothes torn.

32Jonadab, son of David's brother Shimeah, answered, "Don't let my lord the king think all the young men, the king's sons, are dead. Only Amnon is dead. Absalom has been planning this since the day Amnon forced his sister Tamar. 33So now, my lord the king, don't take it to heart that all the king's sons are dead. Only Amnon is dead."

34Meanwhile Absalom fled. The watchman looked up and saw many people coming along the road from the hillside behind him.

35Jonadab said to the king, "Look, the king's sons are coming. It happened just as your servant said."

36As soon as he finished speaking, the king's sons came in, weeping loudly. The king and all his servants also wept very bitterly.

Absalom Flees to Geshur

37Absalom fled and went to Talmai son of Ammihud, king of Geshur. David mourned for his son every day.

38Absalom stayed in Geshur for three years.

39King David longed to go to Absalom, because he had been comforted about Amnon's death.
