God Tells Jeremiah to Write His Words

1In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, the the LORD gave Jeremiah this message: 2“Take a scroll and write on it all the words I have spoken to you against Israel, Judah, and all the nations. Write from the day I first spoke to you in the days of Josiah until today. 3Maybe the people of Judah will hear about all the disaster I plan to bring on them. Then each person can turn from his evil way, and I will forgive his sin and guilt.”

4So Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah. Baruch wrote on the scroll, from Jeremiah’s mouth, all the words the the LORD had spoken to Jeremiah.

Baruch Reads the Scroll in the Temple

5Jeremiah commanded Baruch, “I am confined. I cannot go to the the LORD’s temple. 6You go to the the LORD’s temple on a day of fasting. Read the words of the the LORD from the scroll you wrote from my mouth. Read them to the people, and also to all the people of Judah who come from their towns. 7Maybe their prayer will reach the the LORD. Maybe each person will turn from his evil way. The the LORD has spoken great anger and wrath against these people.”

8Baruch son of Neriah did everything Jeremiah the prophet commanded. He read the words of the the LORD from the book in the the LORD’s temple.

9In the ninth month of the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, a fast was called before the the LORD for all the people in Jerusalem and for all who came to Jerusalem from the towns of Judah.

10Baruch read Jeremiah’s words from the book. He read them in the room of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper courtyard at the entrance of the NewnewNew GategateGate of the the LORD’s temple. He read them to all the people.

The King Burns the Scroll

11Micaiah son of Gemariah son of Shaphan heard all the words of the the LORD from the book.

12He went down to the king’s palace, to the scribe’s room. All the officials were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.

13Micaiah told them everything he had heard when Baruch read the book to the people.

14The officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah son of Shelemiah son of Cushi to Baruch. They said, “Bring the scroll you read to the people, and come here.” So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll and came to them.

15They said to him, “Sit down, please, and read it to us.” So Baruch read it to them.

16When they heard all the words, they looked at each other in fear. They said to Baruch, “We must tell the king all these words.”

17They asked Baruch, “Please tell us how you wrote all these words. Did Jeremiah dictate them to you?”

18Baruch answered, “He dictated all these words to me, and I wrote them with ink on the book.”

19The officials said to Baruch, “Go and hide, you and Jeremiah. Do not let anyone know where you are.”

20They went to the king in the courtyard. They had left the scroll in the room of Elishama the scribe. They told the king everything.

21The king sent Jehudi to get the scroll. He took it from the room of Elishama the scribe. Jehudi read it to the king and to all the officials standing beside the king.

22It was the ninth month, and the king was sitting in the winter house. A fire was burning in a brazier in front of him.

23As Jehudi read three or four columns, the king cut them off with a scribe’s knife and threw them into the fire in the brazier. He kept doing this until the whole scroll was burned up in the fire.

24The king and all his servants who heard all these words were not afraid. They did not tear their clothes.

25Even Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah urged the king not to burn the scroll, but he would not listen to them.

26The king ordered Jerahmeel son of the king, Seraiah son of Azriel, and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to arrest Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet. But the the LORD hid them.

God Gives Jeremiah a New Scroll

27After the king burned the scroll and the words Baruch had written from Jeremiah’s mouth, the word of the the LORD came to Jeremiah:

28“Take another scroll and write on it all the words that were on the first scroll, which Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.

29“About Jehoiakim king of Judah you shall say: ‘This is what the the LORD says: You burned this scroll and said, “Why did you write on it that the king of Babylon will surely come and destroy this land and remove both people and animals from it?”’

30“Therefore this is what the the LORD says about Jehoiakim king of Judah: He will have no one to sit on the throne of David. His dead body will be thrown out into the heat by day and the frost by night.

31“I will punish him, his children, and his servants for their sin. I will bring on them, and on the people of Jerusalem and Judah, all the disaster I spoke against them. But they did not listen.”

32Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah the scribe. Baruch wrote on it, from Jeremiah’s mouth, all the words of the book that Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire. Many similar words were added to them.
