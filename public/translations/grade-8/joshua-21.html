The Levites Receive Their Cities

1The heads of the Levite families came to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the Israelite tribal families. 2They said to them at Shiloh in Canaan, “The LORD commanded through Moses to give us cities to live in and pasturelands for our livestock.” 3So the Israelites gave the Levites these cities and their pasturelands from their own land, just as the LORD had commanded.

4The lot gave the Kohathite clans thirteen cities from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin. These Levites were descendants of Aaron the priest. 5The lot gave the remaining Kohathites ten cities from the clans of Ephraim, Dan, and half of Manasseh. 6The lot gave the Gershonites thirteen cities from the clans of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and half of Manasseh in Bashan. 7The lot gave the Merarites twelve cities from the clans of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. 8So the Israelites gave the Levites these cities and their pasturelands by lot, just as the LORD had commanded through Moses.

Cities for the Priests

9From the tribes of Judah and Simeon they gave these named cities. 10They went to Aaron’s descendants, the Kohathites, because the first lot was theirs. 11They gave them Kiriath Arba, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with its pasturelands around it. Arba was the father of Anak. 12But the fields and villages of that city were given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his own land. 13To Aaron’s sons, the priests, they gave Hebron, a city of refuge for anyone who killed someone by accident, along with Libnah and its pasturelands. 14They also gave Jattir and its pasturelands, and Eshtemoa and its pasturelands. 15They gave Holon and its pasturelands, and Debir and its pasturelands. 16They gave Ain and its pasturelands, Juttah and its pasturelands, and Beth Shemesh and its pasturelands. These nine cities came from the two tribes of Judah and Simeon. 17From Benjamin they gave Gibeon and its pasturelands, Geba and its pasturelands, 18Anathoth and its pasturelands, and Almon and its pasturelands—four cities in all. 19So the priests, Aaron’s sons, received thirteen cities with their pasturelands.

Cities for the Other Levites

20The remaining Kohathite clans received their cities from Ephraim. 21They gave them Shechem, a city of refuge in the hill country of Ephraim, with its pasturelands, and Gezer with its pasturelands. 22Kibzaim and Beth Horon, with their pasturelands—four cities in all. 23From Dan they gave Eltekeh and Gibbethon, with their pasturelands. 24Aijalon and Gath Rimmon, with their pasturelands—four cities. 25From half of Manasseh they gave Taanach and Gath Rimmon, with their pasturelands—two cities. 26So the remaining Kohathite clans received ten cities with their pasturelands.

27The Gershonite clans received cities from half of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan, a city of refuge, with its pasturelands, and Beeshterah with its pasturelands—two cities. 28From Issachar they received Kishion and Daberath, with their pasturelands. 29Jarmuth and En Gannim, with their pasturelands—four cities. 30From Asher they received Mishal and Abdon, with their pasturelands. 31Helkath and Rehob, with their pasturelands—four cities. 32From Naphtali they received Kedesh in Galilee, a city of refuge, with its pasturelands, Hammoth Dor with its pasturelands, and Kartan with its pasturelands—three cities. 33So the Gershonite clans received thirteen cities with their pasturelands.

34The Merarite clans received cities from Zebulun: Jokneam and Kartah, with their pasturelands. 35Dimnah and Nahalal, with their pasturelands—four cities. 36From Reuben they received Bezer and Jahaz, with their pasturelands. 37Kedemoth and Mephaath, with their pasturelands—four cities. 38From Gad they received Ramoth in Gilead, a city of refuge, with its pasturelands, and Mahanaim with its pasturelands. 39Heshbon and Jazer, with their pasturelands—four cities. 40So the remaining Merarite clans received twelve cities.

41In all, the Levites received forty-eight cities with their pasturelands inside the land of Israel. 42Each of these cities had pasturelands around it; this was true for all of them.

The LORD Keeps His Promises

43So the LORD gave Israel all the land HeheHe had promised to their fathers. They took it and settled there. 44The LORD gave them rest on every side, just as HeheHe had sworn to their fathers. None of their enemies could stand against them, because the LORD handed all their enemies over to them. 45Not one of the LORD’s good promises to the people of Israel failed; everything HeheHe said came true.
