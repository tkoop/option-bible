The Bride's Beauty

1How beautiful are your feet in sandals, O noble daughter! The curves of your thighs are like jewels, made by a master's hands. 2Your navel is like a rounded goblet that never runs out of mixed wine. Your waist is a heap of wheat surrounded by lilies.

3Your breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle. 4Your neck is like a tower of ivory; your eyes are like the pools of Heshbon by the gate of Bath-rabbim; your nose is like the tower of Lebanon that looks toward Damascus.

5Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel, and the hair of your head is like purple cloth. The king is captivated by your flowing hair.

Love's Delight

6How beautiful and pleasant you are, O my love, with your delights! 7Your stature is like a palm tree, and your breasts are like clusters of fruit.

8I said, "I will climb the palm tree and take hold of its fruit. May your breasts be like clusters of grapes on the vine, and the fragrance of your breath like apples, 9and your mouth like the best wine."

"May the wine go straight to my beloved, flowing smoothly over lips and teeth."

The Bride's Invitation

10I belong to my beloved, and his desire is for me.

11Come, my beloved, let us go out to the country; let us spend the night among the wildflowers. 12Let us go early to the vineyards to see if the vine has budded, if the blossoms have opened, and if the pomegranates are in bloom. There I will give you my love.

13The mandrakes give off their fragrance, and at our door are all kinds of precious fruits, new and old, that I have saved up for you, my beloved.
