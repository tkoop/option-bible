The Sons of Israel in Egypt

1These are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt with Jacob. Each man came with his family: 2Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah; 3Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin; 4Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher. 5Jacob’s whole family numbered seventy people. Joseph was already in Egypt. 6Joseph and all his brothers and that whole generation died. 7But the Israelites were fruitful and increased greatly. They multiplied and grew very strong, so the land was filled with them.

A New Pharaoh Oppresses Israel

8Then a new king, who did not know Joseph, began to rule in Egypt. 9He said to his people, “Look, the Israelites have become too numerous and too strong for us. 10Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, or they will increase even more. If war comes, they may join our enemies, fight against us, and leave the country.” 11So they put taskmasters over them to oppress them with forced labourlabourlabor. They built Pithom and Rameses as store cities for Pharaoh. 12But the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied and spread. So the Egyptians came to dread the Israelites. 13They worked the Israelites ruthlessly. 14They made their lives bitter with hard labourlabourlabor in brick and mortar and with all kinds of work in the fields. In all their hard labourlabourlabor, the Egyptians worked them ruthlessly.

The Hebrew Midwives

15Then the king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives, one named Shiphrah and the other Puah: 16“When you help the Hebrew women give birth, look at the child. If it is a boy, kill him; but if it is a girl, let her live.” 17But the midwives feared God and did not do what the king of Egypt had told them to do. They let the boys live. 18So the king of Egypt called for the midwives and asked them, “Why have you done this? Why have you let the boys live?”

19The midwives answered Pharaoh, “Hebrew women are not like Egyptian women. They are strong and give birth before the midwives arrive.” 20So God was kind to the midwives, and the people multiplied and became even more numerous. 21And because the midwives feared God, HeheHe gave them families of their own.

Pharaoh Orders the Boys Drowned

22Then Pharaoh gave this order to all his people: “Every Hebrew boy who is born must be thrown into the Nile, but every girl may live.”
