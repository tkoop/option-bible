An Oracle About the Valley of Vision

1This is an oracle about the ValleyvalleyValley of Vision: What is wrong with you now, that you have all gone up to the rooftops? 2O city full of commotion, O town full of noise! Your dead did not die by the sword, nor did they die in battle. 3All your leaders have run away together; they were captured without a bow. All who were found were taken together; they fled far away.

4So I said, "Turn away from me; let me weep bitterly. Do not try to comfort me about the destruction of my people."

The Day of the LORD in the Valley

5For the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts, has a day of uproar, trampling, and confusion in the ValleyvalleyValley of Vision—walls are broken down, and cries for help reach the mountains. 6Elam takes up the quiver, with chariots and horsemen, and Kir gets its shields ready. 7Your best valleys are full of chariots, and horsemen are posted at the gates.

8He has stripped away the defenses of Judah. On that day you looked to the weapons in the House of the ForestforestForest. 9You saw that there were many breaks in the wall of the CitycityCity of David. You gathered the water of the lower pool. 10You counted the houses of Jerusalem and tore down houses to strengthen the wall. 11You made a reservoir between the walls for the water of the old pool, but you did not look to the OneoneOne who made it, or think about HimhimHim who planned it long ago.

A Call to Mourn

12On that day the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts, called for weeping and wailing, for shaved heads and wearing sackcloth. 13But look—joy and gladness! They slaughter cattle and kill sheep, eat meat, and drink wine. "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!" 14The ThetheThe LORD of Hosts has revealed this to me: "Surely this sin will not be forgiven you until you die," says the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts.

Shebna and Eliakim

15This is what the Lord, the the LORD of Hosts, says: "Go, say to Shebna, the steward in charge of the palace:

16"What are you doing here, and who gave you the right to cut out a tomb for yourself here—carving your tomb on the height and hollowing out a resting place for yourself in the rock?

17"Look, you strong man! The ThetheThe LORD is about to shake you violently. He will grab you,

18"roll you into a ball and throw you into a wide land. There you will die, and there your glorious chariots will stay—a shame to your master's house.

19"I will push you out of your job, and you will be pulled down from your place.

20"In that day I will call MymyMy servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah.

21"I will clothe him with your robe and tie your sash around him. I will give him your authority, and he will be a father to the people of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

22"I will place the key to the house of David on his shoulder. What he opens no one can shut, and what he shuts no one can open.

23"I will drive him like a peg into a firm place, and he will become a throne of glory to his father's house.

24"All the glory of his father's house will hang on him: the children and the offshoots—all the small containers, from bowls to jars.

25"In that day, declares the the LORD of Hosts, the peg driven into a firm place will give way; it will be cut off and fall, and the load hanging on it will be cut down. For the the LORD has spoken."
