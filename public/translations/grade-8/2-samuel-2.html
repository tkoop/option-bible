David Becomes King of Judah

2So David went up there with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel. 3David also brought the men who were with him, each with his family, and they settled in the towns around Hebron.

4Then the men of Judah came and anointed David king over the house of Judah.

They told David, "ThetheThe men of Jabesh-gilead buried Saul."

5David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead. He said, "May the LORD bless you, because you showed this kindness to Saul your lord by burying him.

6"Now may the LORD show you kindness and faithfulness. I will also show you good because you did this.

7"Now let your hands be strong and be valiant men, because Saul your lord is dead. Also, the house of Judah has anointed me king over them."

Ish-bosheth Is Made King

8Meanwhile, Abner son of Ner, commander of Saul's army, took Saul's son Ish-bosheth and brought him across to Mahanaim. 9He made him king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all Israel.

10Ish-bosheth son of Saul was forty years old when he became king over Israel, and he ruled for two years. The house of Judah, however, followed David. 11David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah for seven years and six months.

Battle at the Pool of Gibeon

12Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul left Mahanaim for Gibeon.

13Joab son of Zeruiah and David's servants went out and met them at the pool of Gibeon. They sat down, one group on one side of the pool and the other group on the other side.

14Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men get up and compete before us."

Joab said, "Let them get up."

15So they got up and were counted off: twelve for Benjamin and Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve from David's servants.

16Each man grabbed his opponent by the head and thrust his sword into his side. They fell together. So that place was called Helkath-hazzurim, which is at Gibeon.

17The battle was very fierce that day. Abner and the men of Israel were defeated by David's servants.

18The three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel was as fast on his feet as a wild gazelle.

19Asahel chased Abner and did not turn to the right or to the left.

20Abner looked behind him and asked, "Are you Asahel?"

"I am," he answered.

21Abner said, "Turn aside to your right or left. Seize one of the young men and take his armourarmourarmor." But Asahel would not stop chasing him.

22Abner said again, "Stop chasing me! Why should I strike you down? How could I face your brother Joab?"

23But Asahel refused to turn aside. Abner struck him in the stomach with the back end of his spear. The spear came out behind him, and he fell and died there. Everyone who came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died stopped.

The Battle Ends

24Joab and Abishai pursued Abner. The sun was going down when they reached the hill of Ammah, near Giah, on the road to the wilderness of Gibeon.

25The Benjamites rallied behind Abner. They formed one group and stood on top of a hill.

26Abner called to Joab, "Must the sword devour forever? Don't you know it will be bitter in the end? How long before you tell the people to stop chasing their brothers?"

27Joab answered, "As surely as God lives, if you had not spoken, the men would not have stopped until morning from chasing their brothers."

28So Joab blew the trumpet. All the people stopped. They no longer pursued Israel, and they did not fight anymore.

29Abner and his men marched through the Arabah all night. They crossed the Jordan and went through all of Bithron, and they came to Mahanaim. 30Joab returned from pursuing Abner and gathered all the people. Nineteen of David's servants were missing, besides Asahel.

31But David's servants had struck down three hundred sixty Benjamites who were with Abner. 32They took Asahel and buried him in his father's tomb at Bethlehem. Joab and his men marched all night and reached Hebron at daybreak.
