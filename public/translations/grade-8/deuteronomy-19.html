Cities of Refuge

1When the LORD your God destroys the nations whose land the LORD your God gives you, and you take over their land and live in their cities and houses, 2set apart three cities for yourself in the middle of the land that the LORD your God is giving you to own. 3Build roads to them, and divide into three parts the land that the LORD your God gives you, so that anyone who kills someone can run to one of these cities and be safe.

Accidental Killing

4This is the rule for the killer who runs there to stay alive: He killed his neighbourneighbourneighbor without meaning to, and he did not hate him before. 5For example, if a man goes into the forest with his neighbourneighbourneighbor to cut wood, and as he swings his axe to cut down a tree, the iron head slips off the handle and hits his neighbourneighbourneighbor and kills him, that man may run to one of these cities and live. 6Otherwise, the avenger of blood might chase him while angry, catch him because the road is long, and kill him, even though he does not deserve death because he did not hate his neighbourneighbourneighbor before. 7That is why I command you: Set apart three cities for yourself.

8If the LORD your God makes your land larger, as HeheHe swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land HeheHe promised your fathers, 9and if you carefully keep all these commands I am giving you today—to love the LORD your God and walk in HishisHis ways all your life—then you must add three more cities to these three. 10Do this so innocent blood will not be shed in the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and so you will not be guilty of bloodshed.

Murder and the Avenger of Blood

11But if a man hates his neighbourneighbourneighbor, hides and waits for him, attacks him and kills him, and then runs to one of these cities, 12the elders of his city must send for him, bring him back, and hand him over to the avenger of blood to die. 13Do not feel sorry for him. Remove the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, and it will go well with you.

Boundary Lines and Witnesses

14Do not move your neighbourneighbourneighbor’s boundary marker, the one set by your ancestors, in the land you will inherit, which the LORD your God is giving you to own. 15One witness is not enough to convict a person of any crime or sin. A matter must be settled by the testimony of two or three witnesses. 16If a witness comes forward to accuse someone of wrongdoing, 17both people in the dispute must stand before the LORD, before the priests and judges who are serving at that time. 18The judges must investigate carefully. If the witness is lying and has given false testimony against his brother, 19then do to him what he planned to do to his brother. Remove the evil from among you. 20The rest will hear about it and be afraid, and they will not do such an evil thing among you again. 21Do not feel sorry for him: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
