Abimelech's Rise to Power

1Abimelech son of Jerubbaal went to his mother's brothers in Shechem. He spoke to them and to all his mother's family, saying, 2"Please speak to all the leaders of Shechem: 'Which is better for you—that seventy men, all sons of Jerubbaal, rule over you, or that one man rule over you? Remember that I am your own flesh and blood.'" 3HishisHis mother's brothers spoke all these words to the leaders of Shechem. Their hearts turned toward Abimelech, for they said, "HeheHe is our brother." 4They gave him seventy pieces of silver from the temple of Baal-berith. Abimelech used it to hire worthless and reckless men, and they followed him. 5He went to his father's house in Ophrah and killed his brothers, the seventy sons of Jerubbaal, on one stone. But Jotham, the youngest son of Jerubbaal, was left alive, because he hid. 6Then all the leaders of Shechem and all Beth Millo gathered. They went to the great tree at the pillar in Shechem and made Abimelech king.

Jotham's Warning

7When Jotham heard about it, he went and stood on top of Mount Gerizim. He raised his voice and called out, "Listen to me, leaders of Shechem, so that God may listen to you.

He risked his life and saved you from Midian. 18But today you have risen against my father's house. You killed his seventy sons on one stone, and you made Abimelech, the son of his female servant, king over the leaders of Shechem, because he is your brother. 19If you have truly acted faithfully and honestly toward Jerubbaal and his family today, then be happy with Abimelech, and let him be happy with you. 20But if not, may fire come from Abimelech and burn up the leaders of Shechem and Beth Millo, and may fire come from the leaders of Shechem and Beth Millo and burn up Abimelech."

21Jotham ran away and fled. He went to Beer and lived there because of his brother Abimelech.

Trouble in Shechem

22Abimelech ruled over Israel for three years. 23Then God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem. The leaders of Shechem turned against Abimelech. 24This happened to punish the violence done to the seventy sons of Jerubbaal. Their blood was placed on Abimelech, their brother, who killed them, and on the leaders of Shechem, who helped him kill his brothers. 25The leaders of Shechem set ambushes on the hilltops. They robbed everyone who passed by. When Abimelech was told about it, ...

26Gaal son of Ebed came with his brothers and moved into Shechem. The leaders of Shechem trusted him. 27They went out to the fields, picked grapes, and trampled them. They held a festival and went into the temple of their god. They ate and drank, and they cursed Abimelech.

28Gaal son of Ebed said, "Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerubbaal, with Zebul as his officer? Serve the men of Hamor, Shechem's father. Why should we serve Abimelech? 29If only this people were in my hand! Then I would remove Abimelech." Then he said to Abimelech, "Muster your army and come out."

30Zebul, the city ruler, heard Gaal's words, and he became very angry. 31He sent messengers to Abimelech in secret, saying, "Gaal son of Ebed and his brothers have come to Shechem. They are stirring up the city against you. 32Now then, come at night with the people who are with you, and lie in wait in the field. 33In the morning, when the sun rises, get up early and attack the city. When Gaal and the people with him come out against you, do to him whatever your hand finds to do."

34Abimelech and all the people with him got up at night. They lay in wait against Shechem in four groups. 35Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the entrance of the city gate. Abimelech and the people with him got up from their hiding places.

36Gaal saw the people and said to Zebul, "Look, people are coming down from the mountains!"

38Zebul said to him, "Where is your mouth now? You said, 'Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him?' Are not these the people you despised? Go out now and fight them."

39Gaal went out in front of the leaders of Shechem and fought Abimelech. 40Abimelech chased him, and Gaal fled. Many fell wounded all the way to the entrance of the gate. 41Abimelech stayed at Arumah, and Zebul drove Gaal and his brothers out of Shechem.

42The next day the people went out to the field, and Abimelech was told. 43He took the people, divided them into three groups, and lay in wait in the field. When he saw the people coming out of the city, he attacked them. 44Abimelech and his groups rushed forward and stood at the entrance of the city gate. The other two groups rushed on everyone in the field and struck them down. 45Abimelech fought against the city all that day. He captured the city, killed the people in it, tore it down, and sowed it with salt.

The End of Abimelech

46When all the leaders of the tower of Shechem heard this, they went into the stronghold of the temple of El-berith. 47Abimelech was told that all the leaders of the tower of Shechem had gathered there. 48Abimelech and all the people with him went up Mount Zalmon. He took an ax, cut a branch from a tree, lifted it onto his shoulder, and said to the people with him, "Hurry and do what you have seen me do." 49So each man cut a branch and followed Abimelech. They piled the branches against the stronghold and set it on fire. All the people of the tower of Shechem died—about a thousand men and women.

50Then Abimelech went to Thebez. He camped against it and captured it. 51There was a strong tower in the middle of the city. All the men and women and city leaders fled there. They shut themselves in and went up to the roof of the tower. 52Abimelech came to the tower and fought against it. He approached the entrance to burn it with fire. 53But a woman threw an upper millstone down on Abimelech's head and crushed his skull. 54He quickly called his armourarmourarmor-bearer and said, "Draw your sword and kill me, so people won't say, 'A woman killed him.'" HishisHis young man thrust him through, and he died. 55When the Israelites saw that Abimelech was dead, each of them went home. 56So God paid back Abimelech for the evil he had done to his father by killing his seventy brothers. 57God also paid back all the evil of the men of Shechem on their own heads. The curse of Jotham son of Jerubbaal came on them.
