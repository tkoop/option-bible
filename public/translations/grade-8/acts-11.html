The Good News Spreads to Antioch

19Now the believers who had been scattered by the persecution that began after Stephen was killed travelledtravelledtraveled as far as Phoenicia, Cyprus, and Antioch. They spoke the word only to Jews. 20But some of them were men from Cyprus and Cyrene. When they came to Antioch, they spoke to the Greek-speaking people and preached the Lord Jesus. 21The hand of the Lord was with them, and a large number believed and turned to the Lord.

Barnabas and Saul in Antioch

22The news about them reached the church in Jerusalem, and they sent Barnabas to Antioch. 23When he arrived and saw the grace of God, he was glad. He encouraged everyone to stay true to the Lord with all their heart. 24He was a good man, full of the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit and faith, and many people were added to the Lord.

25Then Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul. 26When he found him, he brought him to Antioch. For a whole year they met with the church and taught many people. The disciples were first called Christians in Antioch.

A Coming Famine

27In those days prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch. 28One of them, named Agabus, stood up and predicted through the SpiritspiritSpirit that a great famine would spread over the whole world. This happened during the reign of Claudius. 29So the disciples decided that each one would give what he could to help the brothers living in Judea. 30They did this, sending their gift to the elders through Barnabas and Saul.
