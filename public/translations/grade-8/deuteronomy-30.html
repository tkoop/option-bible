Returning to the LORD

1When all these things happen to you—the blessing and the curse I placed before you—and you take them to heart among all the nations where the LORD your God has sent you, 2and when you and your children return to the LORD your God and obey HishisHis voice with all your heart and all your soul, just as I am commanding you today, 3then the LORD your God will bring back your fortunes, show you mercy, and gather you again from all the nations where HeheHe scattered you. 4Even if you are thrown to the farthest edge of the sky, the LORD your God will gather you from there and bring you back. 5The LORD your God will bring you to the land your fathers owned, and you will own it. He will make you more successful and more numerous than your fathers. 6The LORD your God will change your heart and the hearts of your children, so you can love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, and so you can live. 7The LORD your God will put all these curses on your enemies, on those who hate you, and on those who chased you. 8Then you will obey the LORD again and keep all HishisHis commands that I am giving you today. 9The LORD your God will help you succeed in everything you do—in the fruit of your womb, your animals, and your crops. The LORD will once again be happy with you and make you prosperous, just as HeheHe was happy with your fathers. 10This will happen if you obey the LORD your God by keeping HishisHis commands and rules written in this BookbookBook of the LawlawLaw, and if you turn to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.

God's Command Is Not Far Away

11This command I am giving you today is not too hard for you, and it is not out of your reach. 12It is not up in Heavenheavenheaven, so you would say, “Who will go up to Heavenheavenheaven for us and get it and tell it to us, so we can obey it?” 13It is not across the sea, so you would say, “Who will cross the sea for us and get it and tell it to us, so we can obey it?” 14No, the word is very close to you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so you can obey it.

Choose Life

15Look, today I have set before you life and good, and death and evil. 16If you love the LORD your God, walk in HishisHis ways, and keep HishisHis commands, rules, and laws, then you will live and grow. The LORD your God will bless you in the land you are going in to own. 17But if your heart turns away, and you do not listen, and you are led away to bow down to other gods and serve them,
