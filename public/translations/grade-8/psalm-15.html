Who May Live with the LORD?

1A psalm of David. The LORD, who may stay in YouryourYour tent? Who may live on YouryourYour holy mountain?

2The one who walks with honesty and does what is right, speaking truth in his heart. 3He does not gossip about others, does no harm to his neighbourneighbourneighbor, and does not bring shame on his friend. 4He has no respect for a worthless person, but he honourshonourshonors those who fear the LORD. He keeps his promise even when it hurts. 5He does not charge interest when he lends his money, and he does not take a bribe to hurt an innocent person. The one who does these things will never be shaken.
