For the choir director. According to Mahalath. A teaching psalm of David.

The Foolishness of Denying God

1The foolish person says in his heart, "There is no God." They are corrupt, and they do evil things. No one does what is good.

2God looks down from Heavenheavenheaven at the people on EarthEarthearth. He wants to see if anyone understands or seeks God. 3All of them have turned away. Together they have become corrupt. No one does what is good, not even one person.

4Will those who do wrong never learn? They eat up my people like bread, and they do not call on God.

5There they are, terrified, even though there was no reason to be afraid. God scattered the bones of your enemies. You put them to shame, because God rejected them.

6If only salvation for Israel would come from Zion! When God brings back HishisHis captive people, Jacob will rejoice, and Israel will be glad.
