The LORD Will Ruin the Earth

1Look, the LORD is going to empty the earth and ruin it. He will twist its surface and scatter the people who live there.

2Everyone will face the same fate: the people and the priests, servants and masters, maids and mistresses, buyers and sellers, lenders and borrowers, and creditors and debtors.

3The EarthEarthearth will be completely emptied and stripped bare, because the LORD has spoken this word.

The Earth Mourns

4The EarthEarthearth dries up and withers. The world wastes away and withers. Even the proud leaders of the earth waste away.

5The EarthEarthearth is polluted by those who live on it, because they broke God's laws, changed HishisHis rules, and broke HishisHis lasting agreement.

6So a curse eats up the earth, and the people who live there are guilty. That is why the people of the earth are burned up, and only a few are left.

7The new wine is gone, the vine droops, and everyone who was happy is groaning.

8The happy sound of tambourines stops. The noise of party people ends. The joy of the harp stops.

9No one sings while drinking wine. Strong drink tastes bitter to those who drink it.

10The ruined city is broken down. Every house is shut tight, and no one can enter.

11In the streets people cry out because there is no wine. All joy has faded. The happiness of the earth has gone away.

12The city is left in ruins. Its gate is smashed to pieces.

13This is how it will be on EarthEarthearth among the nations: like shaking an olive tree, or like the last grapes picked after the harvest.

Terror and the Final Shaking

19The EarthEarthearth is completely broken. It is split apart. It shakes violently.

20The EarthEarthearth staggers like a drunk person and sways like a small shelter. Its sin is heavy on it. It falls and will not rise again.
