Joshua Stands Before the Angel of the LORD

1Then I was shown Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the LORD, with Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him.

2And the LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan! The LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you! Is this not a burning stick pulled from the fire?”

3Now Joshua was wearing filthy clothes as he stood before the angel.

Joshua's Dirty Clothes Are Removed

4The angel spoke to those standing before him and said, “Take off his filthy clothes.” Then he said to Joshua, “See, I have taken away your sin, and I will dress you in fine clothes.”

5Then I said, “Put a clean turban on his head.” So they put a clean turban on his head and clothed him, while the Angel of the LORD stood by.

A Charge and a Promise

6The Angel of the LORD gave Joshua this charge:

7“This is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘If you walk in MymyMy ways and keep MymyMy charge, you will judge MymyMy house and guard MymyMy courtyards. I will also let you walk among these who are standing here.

The Branch and the Stone

9‘Look, the stone I have set before Joshua has seven eyes on one stone. I will carve its inscription, declares the LORD of Hosts, and I will remove the sin of this land in one day.
