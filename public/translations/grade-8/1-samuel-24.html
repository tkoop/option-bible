Saul Hunts David at En Gedi

1After Saul came back from fighting the Philistines, someone told him, "David is in the DesertdesertDesert of En Gedi." 2So Saul took three thousand of Israel's best young men and went to look for David and his men near the rocks where the wild goats live.

3He came to the sheep pens along the road. There was a cave there, and Saul went in to use the bathroom. David and his men were sitting far back in the cave.

David Spares Saul in the Cave

4David's men said to him, "This is the day the LORD spoke about when HeheHe said to you, 'I will give your enemy into your hands, and you can do to him whatever seems good to you.'" Then David got up quietly and cut off the corner of Saul's robe.

5Afterward, David felt guilty because he had cut off a corner of Saul's robe.

6He said to his men, "ThetheThe LORD forbid that I should do this thing to my master, the LORD's anointed, or lift my hand against him, because he is the LORD's anointed."

7With these words David stopped his men and did not let them rise up against Saul. Then Saul got up from the cave and went on his way.

David Shows Saul the Cloth

8After that, David got up and went out of the cave. He called after Saul, "MymyMy lord the king!" When Saul looked back, David bowed low with his face to the ground.

9David said to Saul, "Why do you listen to people who say, 'David is trying to hurt you'?

10"This day your own eyes have seen how the LORD gave you into my hand in the cave. Some told me to kill you, but I spared you. I said, 'I will not lift my hand against my lord, because he is the LORD's anointed.'

11"Look, my father, look at this piece of your robe in my hand! I cut off the corner of your robe, but I did not kill you. See that there is no wrong or rebellion in my hands. I have not sinned against you, though you are hunting me to take my life.

12"May the LORD judge between you and me. May the LORD pay you back for what you have done to me, but my hand will not touch you.

13"As the old saying goes, 'Evil comes from evil people,' so my hand will not touch you.

14"Who am I that the king of Israel is chasing me? I'm no more than a dead dog or a flea.

15"May the LORD be the judge and decide between us. May HeheHe look into my case and defend it. May HeheHe save me from your hand."

Saul Admits David Will Be King

16When David finished saying this, Saul asked, "IsisIs that your voice, David my son?" Then Saul cried out loud.

17"You are more righteous than I am. You have done good to me, but I have done evil to you.

18"Today you have shown how well you treated me. When the LORD gave me into your hand, you did not kill me.

19"When a man finds his enemy, does he let him go unharmed? May the LORD reward you with good for what you have done to me today.

20"Now I know for sure that you will be king and that the kingdom of Israel will be established by you.

21"Now swear to me by the LORD that you will not wipe out my family after me or destroy my name from my father's house."

22David swore to Saul. Then Saul went home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.
