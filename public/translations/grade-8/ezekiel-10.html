The Glory above the Cherubim

1I looked, and above the heads of the cherubim there was something that looked like a throne made of sapphire.

Coals Scattered over the City

2He told the man dressed in linen, "Go between the wheels under the cherub. Fill your hands with burning coals from among the cherubim and throw them over the city." The man went in while I watched. 3The cherubim were standing on the south side of the temple when he went in, and a cloud filled the inner court.

4Then the glory of the LORD rose from above the cherub and moved to the threshold of the temple. The temple was filled with the cloud, and the court was filled with the brightness of the glory of the LORD. 5The sound of the cherubim's wings could be heard as far as the outer court. It sounded like the voice of God AlmightyalmightyAlmighty when HeheHe speaks.

Fire Taken from the Cherubim

6When HeheHe commanded the man dressed in linen, "Take fire from among the wheels, from among the cherubim," the man went in and stood beside a wheel. 7Then one of the cherubim reached out his hand to the fire among the cherubim. He took some and put it into the hands of the man dressed in linen, who took it and went out.

The Wheels and the Cherubim

8The cherubim looked like they had human hands under their wings. 9I looked and saw four wheels beside the cherubim, one beside each cherub. The wheels shone like topaz. 10All four wheels looked alike, each like a wheel inside another wheel. 11When they moved, they could go in any of the four directions without turning. They followed wherever the head faced, without turning as they moved. 12Their bodies, backs, hands, and wings were full of eyes all around, and so were their four wheels. 13I heard them called "the whirling wheels." 14Each cherub had four faces: the first was a cherub's face, the second a man's, the third a lion's, and the fourth an eagle's.

15Then the cherubim rose up. They were the living creatures I had seen by the Kebar River. 16When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them. When they lifted their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not leave their side. 17When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still. When they rose, the wheels rose with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

The Glory Leaves the Temple

18Then the glory of the LORD left the threshold of the temple and stopped above the cherubim. 19The cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the earth while I watched. The wheels went with them. They stopped at the east gate of the LORD's house, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them. 20These were the living creatures I had seen under the God of Israel by the Kebar River, and I knew they were cherubim. 21Each had four faces and four wings, and under their wings were what looked like human hands. 22Their faces looked like the faces I had seen by the Kebar River. Each one moved straight ahead.
