Ahaz's Wicked Reign

1Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, like his father David. 2He followed the ways of the kings of Israel. He also made metal images for the Baals. 3He burned incense in the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom and burned his sons in the fire. He followed the terrible practices of the nations that the LORD had driven out before the Israelites. 4He sacrificed and burned incense on the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree.

5So the LORD his God handed him over to the king of Aram. They defeated him and took many captives to Damascus. God also gave him into the hand of the king of Israel, who struck him with a great blow. 6In one day, Pekah son of Remaliah killed 120,000 strong men of Judah, because they had left the LORD, the God of their fathers. 7Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the palace official, and Elkanah, who was second to the king. 8The Israelites took 200,000 of their relatives captive—women, sons, and daughters. They also took much plunder and brought it to Samaria.

Prophet Oded Warns Israel

9But a prophet of the LORD named Oded was there. He went out to meet the army as it returned to Samaria and said to them, "Because the LORD, the God of your fathers, was angry with Judah, HeheHe gave them into your hand. But you have killed them with such fury that it reaches Heavenheavenheaven. 10Now you plan to make the men and women of Judah and Jerusalem your slaves. But are you not also guilty before the LORD your God? 11Now listen to me and send back the captives you took from your brothers, for the LORD's fierce anger is on you."

Ephraim Leaders Free the Captives

12Then some leaders of Ephraim—Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum, and Amasa son of Hadlai—stood up against those coming from the war. 13They said to them, "Do not bring the captives here, or we will be guilty before the LORD. Are you trying to add to our sins and guilt? Our guilt is already great, and fierce anger is on Israel." 14So the armed men left the captives and the plunder before the leaders and the whole assembly. 15The men who were named took charge of the captives. They clothed those who had no clothes from the plunder. They gave them clothes and sandals, food and drink, and oil. They put the weak ones on donkeys and brought them to their brothers at Jericho, the city of palms. Then they returned to Samaria.

Ahaz's Later Troubles

16At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria for help. 17The Edomites had come again and struck Judah and carried away captives. 18The Philistines also raided the cities of the lowlands and the Negev of Judah. They captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon, Gederoth, Soco and its villages, Timnah and its villages, and Gimzo and its villages, and settled there. 19For the LORD humbled Judah because of Ahaz king of Israel, for he had led Judah to live without restraint and had been very unfaithful to the LORD. 20Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria came against him and attacked him instead of strengthening him. 21Although Ahaz took a portion from the LORD's temple and from the king's palace and the officials and gave it to the king of Assyria, it did not help him. 22In the time of his distress King Ahaz became even more unfaithful to the LORD.

23He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus that had defeated him, for he said, "Because the gods of the kings of Aram help them, I will sacrifice to them so that they will help me." But they were the ruin of him and of all Israel. 24Ahaz gathered the vessels of the house of God and cut them in pieces. He shut the doors of the LORD's temple and made altars for himself in every corner of Jerusalem. 25In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and he angered the LORD, the God of his fathers. 26The rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the BookbookBook of the Kings of Judah and Israel. 27Ahaz rested with his fathers and was buried in the city, in Jerusalem, but they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel. His son Hezekiah reigned in his place.
