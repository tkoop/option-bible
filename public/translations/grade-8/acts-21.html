Paul Sails to Tyre

1After we were separated from them, we sailed straight to Cos. The next day we sailed to Rhodes, and from there to Patara. 2There we found a ship crossing to Phoenicia, boarded it, and set sail. 3After we saw Cyprus and left it on our left, we sailed to Syria and landed at Tyre, because the ship was unloading its cargo there.

4In Tyre we found some disciples and stayed with them for seven days. Through the SpiritspiritSpirit, they told Paul not to go to Jerusalem. 5When our time there was over, we left. Everyone, including the women and children, walked with us out of the city. We knelt on the beach and prayed. 6Then we said goodbye to each other, got on the ship, and they went back home.

7We finished the trip from Tyre and arrived at Ptolemais. We greeted the brothers and stayed with them one day. 8The next day we left and came to Caesarea. We stayed in the home of Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven. 9He had four unmarried daughters who prophesied.

Agabus Warns Paul

10While we stayed there for several days, a prophet named Agabus came down from Judea. 11He came to us, took Paul's belt, tied his own feet and hands, and said, "This is what the HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit says: 'The Jews in Jerusalem will tie up the man who owns this belt and hand him over to the Gentiles.'"

12When we heard this, we and the local believers begged Paul not to go up to Jerusalem. 13Paul answered, "Why are you crying and making me so sad? I am ready not only to be tied up but even to die in Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus." 14When he would not be persuaded, we stopped and said, "ThetheThe Lord's will be done."

Paul Arrives in Jerusalem

15After those days, we got ready and went up to Jerusalem. 16Some disciples from Caesarea went with us. They brought us to the home of Mnason, an old disciple from Cyprus, where we were to stay.

17When we arrived in Jerusalem, the brothers welcomed us warmly. 18The next day Paul went with us to see James, and all the elders were there. 19After greeting them, he told them one by one what God had done among the Gentiles through his ministry.

20When they heard this, they praised God. Then they said to Paul, "Brother, you can see how many thousands of Jews have believed. They are all eager to keep the law. 21They have heard that you teach all the Jews who live among the Gentiles to turn away from Moses. You tell them not to circumcise their children or follow our customs. 22What should we do? They will certainly hear that you have come."

"So do this: Four men among us have made a vow. 2324Take them with you, join them in the purification ceremony, and pay their expenses so they can shave their heads. Then everyone will know that the rumoursrumoursrumors about you are false and that you yourself keep the law. 25As for the Gentile believers, we have already written to them. We decided they should stay away from food offered to idols, from blood, from meat of strangled animals, and from sexual sin."

26The next day Paul took the men, joined them in the purification ceremony, and went into the temple. He announced the end of the days of purification, when an offering would be made for each of them.

Paul Is Arrested in the Temple

27When the seven days were almost over, some Jews from Asia saw Paul in the temple. They stirred up the whole crowd and grabbed him. 28They shouted, "Men of Israel, help! This man teaches everyone everywhere against our people, the law, and this place. He has even brought Greeks into the temple and made this holy place unclean." 29They had seen Trophimus, who was from Ephesus, with him in the city, so they thought Paul had brought him into the temple. 30The whole city was stirred up. The people ran together, seized Paul, dragged him out of the temple, and the doors were shut.

31While they were trying to kill him, word reached the commander of the Roman soldiers that all Jerusalem was in an uproar. 32He quickly took soldiers and officers and ran down to the crowd. When they saw the commander and the soldiers, they stopped beating Paul. 33The commander came close, arrested him, and ordered him to be bound with two chains. Then he asked who he was and what he had done. 34Some in the crowd shouted one thing, and others shouted something else. Because he could not learn the truth in all the noise, he ordered Paul to be taken to the barracks.

35When Paul reached the steps, the soldiers had to carry him because of the violence of the crowd. 36The crowd followed and shouted, "Get rid of him!"

37As Paul was about to be brought into the barracks, he said to the commander, "May I say something to you?" The commander asked, "Do you know Greek? 38Aren't you the Egyptian who started a revolt a while ago and led four thousand armed rebels into the desert?" 39Paul said, "I am a Jew from Tarsus in Cilicia. I am a citizen of an important city. Please let me speak to the people." 40The commander gave permission. Paul stood on the steps, motioned to the crowd with his hand, and when there was a great silence, he spoke to them in Hebrew.
