Restoring a Fellow Believer

1Brothers, if someone is caught in a sin, you who are spiritual should restore that person gently. Watch yourself, so you won't be tempted too. 2Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfillfulfilfulfill the law of Christ. 3If anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he fools himself. 4Each person should test his own work. Then he can be proud of himself alone, not by comparing himself to someone else. 5For each person will carry his own load.

6The person who is taught the word should share all good things with the one who teaches.

You Reap What You Sow

7Don't be fooled: God cannot be mocked. A person reaps what he sows. 8The one who sows to please his own sinful nature will reap ruin from that nature. But the one who sows to please the SpiritspiritSpirit will reap eternal life from the SpiritspiritSpirit. 9Let us not get tired of doing good, for at the right time we will reap a harvest if we don't give up. 10So then, while we have the chance, let us do good to everyone, especially to those in the family of faith.

Paul's Final Words

11See what large letters I use to write to you with my own hand. 12Those who want to look good in the flesh try to force you to be circumcised. They do this only so they won't be persecuted for the cross of Christ. 13Even those who are circumcised don't keep the law. They want you circumcised so they can brag about your body.

14But I will never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ. Through that cross, the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. 15For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything. What matters is a new creation.

16Peace and mercy to all who follow this rule, and to the Israel of God. 17From now on, let no one trouble me, for I carry in my body the marks of Jesus. 18The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
