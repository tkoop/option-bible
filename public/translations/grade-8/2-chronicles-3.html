Solomon Begins Building the Temple

1Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah. That was where the LORD had appeared to his father David. David had prepared the place on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. 2He began to build on the second day of the second month in the fourth year of his reign.

The Temple's Size and Gold

3These are the foundations Solomon laid for building the house of God: The temple was sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. 4The porch in front of the temple was twenty cubits long, across the width of the temple. It was one hundred twenty cubits high. Solomon overlaid it inside with pure gold.

5He panelledpanelledpaneled the main hall with cypress wood and overlaid it with fine gold. He decorated it with palm trees and chains. 6He adorned the temple with precious stones to make it beautiful. The gold was gold from Parvaim. 7He overlaid the temple—its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors—with gold. He carved cherubim on the walls.

The Most Holy Place

10In the MostmostMost HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace he made two cherubim of wood and overlaid them with gold. 11The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long. One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple. The other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the other cherub. 12One wing of the second cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple. The other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the first cherub. 13The wings of these cherubim spread out twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, and their faces looked toward the temple.

14He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen. He wove cherubim into it.

Pillars at the Entrance

15In front of the temple he made two pillars. They were thirty-five cubits high, with a capital five cubits high on top of each. 16He made chains for the inner sanctuary and put them on top of the pillars. He made one hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains. 17He set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and one on the left. He called the right pillar Jachin and the left pillar Boaz.
