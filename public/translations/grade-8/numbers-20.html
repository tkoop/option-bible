Miriam Dies at Kadesh

1In the first month, the whole Israelite community came to the DesertdesertDesert of Zin and stayed at Kadesh. There Miriam died and was buried. 2There was no water for the community, so they gathered against Moses and Aaron. 3The people argued with Moses and said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before the LORD! 4Why did you bring the LORD's people into this desert to die here along with our animals? 5Why did you bring us up out of Egypt to bring us to this awful place? It is not a place for grain, figs, vines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink."

The LORD Gives Water from the Rock

6Moses and Aaron left the assembly and went to the entrance of the tent of meeting. They fell facedown, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them. 7The LORD spoke to Moses: 8"Take the staff and gather the community, you and your brother Aaron. Speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give its water. You will bring water out of the rock for them and give the community and their animals a drink." 9So Moses took the staff from before the LORD, as HeheHe had commanded him.

10Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and Moses said to them, "Listen now, you rebels! Must we bring water out of this rock for you?"

11Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff. Water gushed out, and the community and their animals drank.

12But the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not trust MemeMe enough to honourhonourhonor MemeMe as holy before the Israelites, you will not lead this community into the land I gave them." 13These were the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites quarreled with the LORD, and HeheHe showed HishisHis holiness among them.

Edom Refuses Israel Passage

14Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "This is what your brother Israel says: You know all the hardship we have faced. 15Our ancestors went down to Egypt, and we lived there many years. The Egyptians mistreated us and our ancestors. 16We cried out to the LORD, and HeheHe heard our voice. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. Now we are at Kadesh, a town on the edge of your territory. 17Please let us pass through your land. We will not go through fields or vineyards, and we will not drink water from your wells. We will stay on the king's road and not turn aside to the right or left until we have passed through your territory."

18But Edom answered, "YouyouYou may not pass through my land, or I will come out against you with the sword."

19The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the main road. If we or our livestock drink your water, we will pay for it. We only want to pass through on foot—nothing else."

20Edom said, "YouyouYou shall not pass through." Then Edom came out against them with a large and powerful army. 21So Edom refused to let Israel pass through his territory, and Israel turned away from him.

Aaron Dies on Mount Hor

22The whole Israelite community set out from Kadesh and came to Mount Hor. 23At Mount Hor, near the border of Edom, the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, 24"Aaron will die and join his ancestors, because he will not enter the land I gave the Israelites. You both rebelled against MymyMy command at the waters of Meribah. 25Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and bring them up Mount Hor. 26Remove Aaron's garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will die there."

27Moses did as the LORD commanded. They went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole community. 28Moses removed Aaron's garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron died there on top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down the mountain. 29When the whole community saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.
