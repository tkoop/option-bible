Zion, the City of God

1A psalm of the sons of Korah. A song. Its foundation rests on the holy mountains. 2The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the homes of Jacob. 3Glorious things are said about you, city of God. Selah.

4"I will name Rahab and Babylon to those who know MemeMe. Look, Philistia and Tyre are there with Cush. This one was born there." 5"And about Zion it will be said, 'This one and that one were born in her,' and the MostmostMost HighhighHigh HimselfhimselfHimself will establish her."

6The LORD will count the nations in HishisHis book: "This one was born there." Selah.

7Singers and dancers will say, "All my fountains are in you."
