Paul Remembers His Visit

1You know, brothers and sisters, that our visit to you was not a waste. 2As you know, we had already suffered and been insulted in Philippi. But with the help of our God, we were bold enough to tell you HishisHis gospel, even though we faced strong opposition. 3Our appeal doesn't come from false beliefs, impure motives, or trickery. 4Instead, we speak as people God has tested and trusted with the gospel. We aren't trying to please people, but God, who tests our hearts. 5You know we never used flattery or hid greedy motives behind our words. God is our witness. 6We didn't look for praise from people—not from you or anyone else—even though, as Christ's apostles, we could have used our authority.

Gentle Care Like a Mother and Father

7Instead, we were gentle among you, like a nursing mother caring for her children. 8We cared so much for you that we were happy to share with you not only God's gospel but our own lives, because you had become dear to us. 9Surely you remember, brothers and sisters, how hard we worked. We labouredlabouredlabored night and day so we wouldn't be a burden to any of you while we preached God's gospel to you. 10You are witnesses, and so is God, of how holy, right, and blameless we were among you who believed. 11You know we dealt with each of you the way a father deals with his own children. 12We encouraged you, comforted you, and urged you to live in a way that honourshonourshonors God, who calls you into HishisHis kingdom and glory.

The Thessalonians Received God's Word

13We never stop thanking God because, when you received the word of God that you heard from us, you accepted it not as a human word but as God's word, which is truly at work in you who believe. 14Brothers and sisters, you became like the churches of God in Judea that are in Christ Jesus. You suffered the same things from your own people that those churches suffered from the Jews. 15They killed the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and they drove us out. They don't please God, and they are against all people. 16They try to stop us from speaking to the Gentiles so that they won't be saved. In this way, they keep adding to their sins. But God's wrath has finally come upon them.

Paul Hopes to Return

17Brothers and sisters, after we were separated from you for a short time—in person, not in heart—we wanted very much to see you again, so we tried hard to do it. 18We wanted to come to you—I, Paul, tried more than once—but Satan stopped us. 19After all, what is our hope, our joy, or the crown we will boast about in the presence of our Lord Jesus when HeheHe comes? Isn't it you? 20Yes, you are our glory and our joy.
