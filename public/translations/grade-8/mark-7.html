What Makes a Person Unclean

14Again Jesus called the crowd to HimhimHim and said, "Listen to MemeMe, all of you, and understand. 15Nothing that goes into a person from the outside can make that person unclean. It is what comes out of a person that makes him unclean."

17After Jesus left the crowd and went into the house, HishisHis disciples asked HimhimHim about the story HeheHe had told.

18"Are you so slow to understand?" He asked. "Don't you understand that nothing from the outside can make a person unclean? 19It does not go into his heart. It goes into his stomach, and then it leaves the body. In this way, all foods are made clean."

20He went on, "What comes out of a person is what makes him unclean. 21From inside, from the heart, come evil thoughts, sexual sins, stealing, murder, 22adultery, greed, evil actions, lies, wild living, jealousy, speaking against others, pride, and foolishness. 23All these evils come from inside and make a person unclean."

A Woman from Tyre

24Jesus left that place and went to the area around Tyre. He went into a house and did not want anyone to know HeheHe was there, but HeheHe could not stay hidden.

25A woman came right away. Her little daughter had an evil spirit. The woman fell at HishisHis feet. 26She was a Greek woman, born in Syrian Phoenicia. She kept asking HimhimHim to drive the demon out of her daughter.

27Jesus said to her, "Let the children eat first. It is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs."

28"Yes, Lord," she answered. "Even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs."

29He told her, "Because of your answer, go home. The demon has left your daughter."

30She went home and found her child lying on the bed. The demon was gone.
