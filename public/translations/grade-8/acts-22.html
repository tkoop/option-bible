The Crowd Turns Violent

Paul's Roman Citizenship Protects Him

25As they tied him up with straps, Paul said to the centurion standing there, “Is it legal for you to flog a Roman citizen who hasn't been condemned?”

26When the centurion heard this, he went to the commander and reported it. “What are you about to do?” he asked. “This man is a Roman citizen.”

27The commander came and asked Paul, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?”

“Yes,” Paul said.

28The commander replied, “I bought this citizenship for a large sum of money.”

“But I was born a citizen,” Paul said.

29At once those who were about to examine him stepped back. The commander was afraid too, because he had learned that Paul was a Roman citizen and that he had put him in chains.

30The next day, wanting to know exactly what the Jews were accusing him of, the commander released Paul and ordered the chief priests and the whole council to meet. Then he brought Paul down and placed him before them.
