The Israelites Gather at Shiloh

1The whole community of Israel gathered at Shiloh and set up the tent of meeting there. The land had been brought under their control. 2Seven tribes of Israel still had not received their share of the land.

Joshua Urges the People to Take the Land

3Joshua said to the Israelites, “How long will you keep waiting to go and take the land that the the LORD, the God of your fathers, has given you?”

4“Choose three men from each tribe. I will send them out to explore the land and write a description of it, based on the share each tribe should receive. Then they will come back to me. 5They will divide the land into seven parts. Judah will keep its territory in the south, and the house of Joseph will keep its territory in the north. 6You are to write a description of these seven parts of the land and bring it to me. Then I will cast lots for you here before the the LORD our God.”

7“The Levites will have no share among you, because their inheritance is serving the the LORD. Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received the inheritance that Moses, the servant of the the LORD, gave them east of the Jordan.”

8So the men started out. Joshua commanded those who were going to map the land, “Go and walk through the land, write a description of it, and come back to me. I will cast lots for you here before the the LORD at Shiloh.”

The Land Is Mapped and Divided

9The men went and travelledtravelledtraveled through the land. They wrote its description in a book, listing the cities in seven parts. Then they came back to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh. 10There Joshua cast lots for them before the the LORD. In Shiloh, Joshua divided the land among the Israelites according to their tribal groups.

The Inheritance of Benjamin

11The lot came up for the tribe of Benjamin, by its families. The border of their inheritance lay between the people of Judah and the people of Joseph.

12On the north side, its border began at the Jordan. It went up along the north side of Jericho, then west through the hill country, and ended in the wilderness of Beth Aven. 13From there the border passed to Luz, on the south side of Luz (that is, Bethel). Then it went down to Ataroth Addar, on the mountain south of LowerlowerLower Beth Horon. 14The border turned and curved south from the mountain that faces Beth Horon on the south. It ended at Kiriath Baal, which is Kiriath Jearim, a city of the people of Judah. This was the west side. 15The south side began at the edge of Kiriath Jearim. It went west and came out at the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

16Then the border went down to the end of the mountain that faces the ValleyvalleyValley of Ben Hinnom, in the ValleyvalleyValley of Rephaim on the north. It went down the ValleyvalleyValley of Hinnom, along the south side of the Jebusite city, and came down to En Rogel. 17It turned north and went out to En Shemesh. Then it went out to Geliloth, opposite the ascent of Adummim. It went down to the stone of Bohan son of Reuben. 18It passed along the north side, opposite the Arabah, and went down into the Arabah.

19Then the border passed along the north side of Beth Hoglah. It ended at the northern tip of the SaltsaltSalt SeaseaSea, at the south end of the Jordan. This was the south boundary. 20The Jordan formed the eastern boundary. These were the borders around the inheritance of the people of Benjamin, by their families.

21The cities of the tribe of Benjamin, by their families, were Jericho, Beth Hoglah, Emek Keziz, 22Beth Arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel, 23Avvim, Parah, Ophrah, 24Chephar Ammoni, Ophni, Geba—twelve cities with their villages. 25Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth, 26Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah, 27Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah, 28Zela, Eleph, the Jebusite city (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath—fourteen cities with their villages. This was the inheritance of the people of Benjamin, by their families.
