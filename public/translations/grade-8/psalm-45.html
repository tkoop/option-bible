A Wedding Song for the King

1My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses to the king; my tongue is the pen of a skilfulskilfulskillful writer. 2You are the most handsome of men, and grace has been poured on your lips; therefore God has blessed you forever.

The King's Glory and Victory

3Strap your sword on your side, mighty one; appear in your splendoursplendoursplendor and majesty. 4In your majesty ride out victoriously for the cause of truth, humility, and righteousness; let your right hand show awesome deeds. 5Your sharp arrows pierce the hearts of the king's enemies; the nations fall beneath your feet.

The King's Eternal Rule

6Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever; a sceptresceptrescepter of justice is the sceptresceptrescepter of your kingdom. 7You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of joy over your companions. 8All your garments are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from ivory palaces, harps make you glad.

The Queen and Her Wedding

9Daughters of kings are among your honouredhonouredhonored women; at your right hand stands the queen in gold from Ophir. 10Listen, daughter, and pay attention; turn your ear. Forget your people and your father's house. 11Then the king will desire your beauty; bow down to him, for he is your Lord. 12The people of Tyre will bring a gift; the rich among the people will seek your favourfavourfavor.

13All glorious is the princess in her chamber; her clothing is woven with gold. 14In embroidered garments she is led to the king; her young companions are brought to you. 15They are led with joy and gladness; they enter the palace of the king.

A Promise for the Future

16Your sons will take the place of your fathers; you will make them princes throughout the land. 17I will make your name remembered in every generation; therefore the nations will praise you forever and ever.
