The Potter and the Clay

1The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD said, 2"Go down to the potter's house. I will speak to you there." 3So I went down to the potter's house. I saw him working at his wheel. 4The pot he was shaping from clay was ruined in his hands. So he made it into a different pot, the way he wanted.

5Then the word of the LORD came to me: 6"O house of Israel, can I not do with you as this potter does?" declares the LORD. "Look, like the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in MymyMy hand, O house of Israel. 7At one moment I might speak about a nation or kingdom to uproot it, tear it down, and destroy it. 8But if that nation turns from its evil, I will hold back the disaster I planned to bring on it. 9At another moment I might speak about a nation or kingdom to build it and plant it. 10But if it does evil in MymyMy sight and does not obey MymyMy voice, I will hold back the good I had planned to do for it.

The LORD's Warning to Judah

11Now therefore, say to the men of Judah and to the people living in Jerusalem: 'This is what the LORD says: "Look, I am shaping disaster against you and making a plan against you. Turn now, each of you, from his evil way, and make your ways and deeds good."' 12But they say, 'It is hopeless! For we will follow our own plans, and each of us will act according to the stubbornness of his evil heart.' 13Therefore this is what the LORD says: "Ask now among the nations: Who has heard such things? The virgin of Israel has done a very horrible thing. 14Does the snow of Lebanon ever leave the rocky fields? Or do the cold waters that flow from far away ever dry up? 15Yet MymyMy people have forgotten MemeMe. They burn incense to worthless idols, and they stumble in their ways on the old paths, walking on side roads instead of the highway. 16They have made their land a ruined, empty place, a thing people hiss at forever. Plots Against Jeremiah

18Then they said, "Come, let us make plans against Jeremiah. The law will not be lost from the priest, nor advice from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, let us attack him with our words, and let us not pay attention to anything he says."
