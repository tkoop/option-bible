A Warning for Egypt and Cush

1In the year that the Assyrian commander came to Ashdod, when Sargon, king of Assyria, sent him, he fought in Ashdod and captured it.

2At that time the LORD spoke through Isaiah son of Amoz. He said, "Go, take the sackcloth off your waist and the sandals off your feet." Isaiah did as he was told and walked around naked and barefoot.

3Then the LORD said, 4"Just as MymyMy servant Isaiah has walked naked and barefoot for three years as a sign and warning against Egypt and Cush, so the king of Assyria will lead away Egypt's captives and Cush's exiles—young and old—naked and barefoot, with their backsides uncovered, bringing shame on Egypt. 5Those who trusted in Cush and boasted in Egypt will be dismayed and ashamed."

Fear Along the Coast

6On that day the people living along this coast will say, "Look at what happened to those we relied on, those we ran to for help to be saved from the king of Assyria. How can we escape now?"
