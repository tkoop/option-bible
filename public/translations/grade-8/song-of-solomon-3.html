The Bride Searches at Night

1On my bed at night, I looked for the one I love. I looked for him, but I did not find him. 2"I will get up now and walk around the city, through the streets and squares. I will look for the one I love." I looked for him, but I did not find him.

3The watchmen found me as they walked around the city. "Have you seen the one I love?" 4Scarcely had I passed them when I found the one I love. I held him and would not let him go until I brought him to my mother's house, to the room of the one who gave birth to me.

A Warning to Jerusalem's Daughters

5Daughters of Jerusalem, I charge you by the gazelles or the does of the field: Do not stir up love or wake it before it is ready.

The King's Wedding Procession

6Who is this coming up from the wilderness like a column of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and incense made from all the merchant's spices? 7Look! It is Solomon's carriage, escorted by sixty warriors, the noblest of Israel. 8All of them wear swords. They are trained for battle, and each has his sword at his side, ready for danger at night.

9King Solomon made a carriage for himself from the wood of Lebanon. 10He made its posts of silver and its base of gold. Its seat was purple, and its inside was decorated with love by the Daughters of Jerusalem. 11Daughters of Zion, go out and look at King Solomon wearing the crown his mother placed on him on the day of his wedding, the day his heart rejoiced.
