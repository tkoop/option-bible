Moses and Aaron Are Sent to Pharaoh

10Then the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 11“Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites go out of his land.”

12But Moses said to the LORD, “If the Israelites will not listen to me, how will Pharaoh listen to me? I am not a good speaker.”

13Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He commanded them concerning the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt to bring the Israelites out of Egypt.

The Family Lines of Moses and Aaron
