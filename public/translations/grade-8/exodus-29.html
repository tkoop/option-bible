Instructions for Ordaining the Priests

1"Now this is what you are to do to set them apart to serve MemeMe as priests: Take a young bull and two rams that have no defects. 2Use fine wheat flour to make unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil. 3Put them in a basket and bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

4"Then bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and wash them with water. 5Take the special clothes and dress Aaron in the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece. Fasten the ephod on him with its woven belt. 6Put the turban on his head and place the holy crown on the turban. 7Take the anointing oil, pour it on his head, and anoint him.

8"Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 9Tie sashes around them and put head coverings on them. The priesthood will belong to them forever as a law for all time. In this way you will ordain Aaron and his sons.

Offerings for the Priests' Ordination

10"Bring the bull to the front of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on the bull's head. 11Slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 12Take some of the bull's blood and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger. Pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. 13Take all the fat that covers the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, and both kidneys with the fat on them, and burn them on the altar. 14But burn the bull's meat, its hide, and its waste outside the camp. It is a sin offering.

15"Take one of the rams. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head. 16Slaughter the ram and take its blood and sprinkle it all around the altar. 17Cut the ram into pieces. Wash its inner parts and legs, and place them with its head and the other pieces. 18Burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

19"Take the second ram. Aaron and his sons must lay their hands on its head. 20Slaughter the ram. Take some of its blood and put it on the right earlobes of Aaron and his sons, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Sprinkle the rest of the blood on all sides of the altar. 21Take some of the blood from the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle them on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes. Then he and his clothes will be set apart, and his sons and their clothes will be set apart too.

22"Take the fat from the ram, the fat tail, the fat that covers the inner parts, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat on them, and the right thigh, because this is a ram for ordination. 23Take one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD. 24Put all these in the hands of Aaron and his sons, and wave them before the LORD as a wave offering. 25Then take them from their hands and burn them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. It is a pleasing smell before the LORD, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

26"Take the breast of the ram used for ordination and wave it before the LORD as a wave offering. It will be your portion. 27Set apart for Aaron and his sons the breast of the wave offering that is waved and the thigh of the offering that is lifted up from the ram used for ordination. 28It will belong to Aaron and his sons forever as a law from the Israelites. It is their offering that is lifted up to the LORD from their peace offerings.

29"The holy clothes that belong to Aaron will belong to his sons after him, so they can be anointed and ordained in them. 30The son who takes his place as priest and enters the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting to serve in the HolyholyHoly PlaceplacePlace must wear them for seven days.

31"Take the ram used for ordination and boil its meat in a holy place. 32At the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting, Aaron and his sons must eat the meat of the ram and the bread that is in the basket. 33They must eat the things used to make atonement for their ordination and set them apart. No one outside may eat them, because they are holy. 34If any of the meat used for ordination or any bread is left until morning, burn up what is left. It must not be eaten, because it is holy.

35"Do for Aaron and his sons everything I have commanded you. Ordain them for seven days. 36Sacrifice a bull as a sin offering each day to make atonement. Purify the altar by making atonement for it, and anoint it to set it apart. 37For seven days make atonement for the altar and set it apart. Then the altar will be most holy, and whatever touches the altar will become holy.

Daily Sacrifices at the Tabernacle

38"This is what you are to offer regularly on the altar: two lambs a year old, every day, all the time. 39Offer one lamb in the morning and the other at twilight. 40With the first lamb offer one-tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with one-fourth of a hin of oil from pressed olives, and a drink offering of one-fourth of a hin of wine. 41Offer the second lamb at twilight with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning. It is a pleasing smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

God Promises to Dwell with His People

42"For the generations to come, this burnt offering is to be made regularly at the entrance of the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting before the LORD. There I will meet you to speak with you. 43There I will meet with the Israelites, and the place will be set apart by MymyMy glory. 44So I will set apart the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and the altar. I will also set apart Aaron and his sons to serve MemeMe as priests. 45Then I will live among the Israelites and be their God. 46They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I might live among them. I am the LORD their God."
