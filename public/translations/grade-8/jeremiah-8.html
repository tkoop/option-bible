JudgmentJudgementJudgment on Judah

1The LORD says, "At that time, they will bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, their leaders, the priests, the prophets, and the people of Jerusalem from their graves. 2They will spread them out before the sun, the moon, and all the stars of the sky, which they loved, served, followed, prayed to, and worshiped. The bones will not be gathered or buried. They will be like manure on the ground. 3And wherever I have scattered them, the people left from this wicked family will choose death over life," declares the LORD of Hosts.

The People Refuse to Repent

4The LORD says, "Tell them: Do people fall down and not get up? If someone turns away, does he not come back? 5Why then do these people keep turning away? Jerusalem is always turning away. They cling to lies and refuse to come back. 6I listened carefully, but they do not say what is right. No one is sorry for his wrong and says, 'What have I done?' Everyone runs his own way, like a horse rushing into battle. 7Even the stork in the sky knows its seasons. The turtledove, the swift, and the crane know when to come. But MymyMy people do not know what the LORD requires. 8How can you say, 'We are wise, and we have the LORD's law'? Look, the false pen of the scribes has turned it into a lie. 9The wise men are put to shame. They are dismayed and caught. Look, they have rejected the word of the LORD. What wisdom do they have? 10So I will give their wives to others and their fields to new owners. Everyone, from the least to the greatest, is greedy for gain. Prophet and priest alike, all practice deceit. 11They treat the wound of MymyMy people as if it were not serious. They say, 'Peace, peace,' when there is no peace. 12Are they ashamed of the terrible things they do? No, they are not ashamed at all. They do not even know how to blush. So they will fall with those who fall. When I punish them, they will stumble," says the LORD.

The Coming Disaster

13"I will surely take them away," declares the LORD. "There are no grapes on the vine, no figs on the fig tree. Even the leaves have withered. What I gave them is gone.

14Why are we sitting here? Let's gather and go into the strong cities and die there. For the LORD our God has doomed us. He has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD. 15We hoped for peace, but nothing good came. We hoped for healing, but there is only terror. 16The snorting of the enemy's horses is heard from Dan. The sound of his stallions neighing shakes the whole land. They come and devour the land and everything in it, the city and its people.

17"For look, I am sending poisonous snakes among you, snakes that no one can charm, and they will bite you," declares the LORD.

Jeremiah's Sorrow

18My joy is gone. Grief is upon me. My heart is sick. 19Listen to the cry of my people from a far country: "Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her KingkingKing not there?" Why have they provoked MemeMe with their carved images and their worthless foreign idols? 20The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved. 21Because my people are hurt, I am hurt. I mourn, and horror has taken hold of me. 22Is there no healing medicine in Gilead? Is there no doctor there? Then why hasn't my people's healing come?
