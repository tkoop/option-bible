A Sign of Jerusalem Under Siege

1Now you, son of man, take a brick and place it in front of you. Draw a city on it—Jerusalem. 2Then lay siege against it: build a siege wall, heap up a ramp, set up camps, and place battering rams around it. 3Then take an iron pan and set it as an iron wall between you and the city. Turn your face toward it. It will be under siege, and you will besiege it. This will be a sign to the people of Israel.

Bearing Israel’s and Judah’s Sin

4Then lie on your left side and put the sin of the people of Israel on it. You will bear their sin for the number of days you lie on it. 5I have given you 390 days, which matches the years of their sin. So you will bear the sin of the people of Israel. 6When you finish these days, lie again on your right side and bear the sin of the people of Judah. I have given you 40 days, one day for each year. 7You must turn your face toward the siege of Jerusalem with your arm uncovered, and prophesy against it. 8Look, I will tie you with ropes so you cannot turn from side to side until you finish the days of your siege.

Eating Rationed Food

9Take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt. Put them in one container and make bread for yourself. Eat it for the 390 days you lie on your side. 10Weigh out twenty shekels of food each day and eat it at set times. 11Measure out a sixth of a hin of water and drink it at set times. 12You will eat it as a barley cake, baked over human waste in front of them.

A Warning About Unclean Bread

13Then the the LORD said, “This is how the Israelites will eat unclean bread among the nations where I scatter them.”

14I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! I have never made myself unclean. From my youth until now I have never eaten meat from an animal that died on its own or was torn by beasts, and no unclean meat has entered my mouth.”

15He said, “Look, I will let you use cow dung instead of human dung. You may bake your bread over that. 16Son of man, I am going to cut off the bread supply in Jerusalem. They will anxiously eat bread rationed by weight, and in despair drink water rationed by measure. 17So they will lack food and water and be horrified at one another, and waste away because of their sin.”
