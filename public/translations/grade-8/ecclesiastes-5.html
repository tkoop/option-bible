Be Careful When You Worship

1Be careful when you go to God's house. Listening closely is better than giving a foolish offering, because fools don't realizerealiserealize they're doing wrong. 2Don't rush to speak, and don't be quick to say something to God. God is in Heavenheavenheaven, and you are on EarthEarthearth, so keep your words few. 3Dreams come from too much worry, and a fool shows his foolishness by talking too much.

Keep Your Promises to God

4When you make a promise to God, don't delay keeping it. God doesn't delight in fools. Keep what you promised. 5It's better not to make a promise than to make one and not keep it. 6Don't let your mouth cause you to sin. Don't tell the messenger, "It was a mistake." Why should God get angry at your words and destroy what you have worked for? 7With so many dreams and empty words, nothing comes of it. Just fear God.

Don't Be Shocked by Unfairness

8If you see unfair treatment of the poor and justice twisted in a land, don't be surprised. Officials answer to higher officials, and there are even higher ones above them. 9But the land's harvest helps everyone. Even the king depends on the fields.

Money, Work, and God's Gifts

10Anyone who loves money never has enough. Anyone who loves wealth is never satisfied with what they earn. That's empty too. 11When wealth grows, more people consume it. What good does it do the owner except to look at it? 12A worker sleeps well, whether he eats a little or a lot. But a rich man's full stomach keeps him awake. 13I've seen something terrible in this life: people hoard wealth, and it hurts them. 14That wealth is lost in a bad deal, and when he has a son, there's nothing left to give him. 15We come into the world naked, and we leave the same way. We can't take anything we worked for. 16That's another terrible thing: we leave just as we came. What's the point of working so hard for nothing? 17He spends all his days eating in gloom, with frustration, sickness, and anger. 18Here's what I've found is good: eat, drink, and enjoy the work a person does in this life during the few days of life God gives him. That's his share. 19God also gives some people wealth, possessions, and the power to enjoy them, to accept their share, and to be glad in their work. That is God's gift. 20He won't spend much time worrying about his short life, because God keeps him busy with joy in his heart.
