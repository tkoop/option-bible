Wise Words and the LORD's Watchful Eyes

1A gentle reply calms anger, but a harsh word stirs it up. 2Wise people's words share knowledge, but fools' mouths pour out foolishness. 3The LORD's eyes are everywhere, watching both evil and good.

4A gentle tongue is a tree of life, but twisted words break the spirit. 5A fool rejects his father's discipline, but a person who listens to correction is wise. 6The righteous person's house holds great wealth, but the wicked's earnings bring trouble. 7Wise people's lips spread knowledge, but fools' hearts do not.

8The LORD finds the sacrifices of the wicked disgusting, but HeheHe delights in the prayers of the upright. 9The LORD hates the way of the wicked, but HeheHe loves those who chase after what is right. 10There is severe discipline for anyone who leaves the right path; whoever hates correction will die. 11Death and the grave are open before the LORD; how much more people's hearts! 12A mocker does not like to be corrected; he will not go to the wise.

13A glad heart makes a cheerful face, but sorrow of heart crushes the spirit. 14A wise heart seeks knowledge, but fools feed on foolishness. 15All the days of the poor are hard, but a cheerful heart has a continual feast.

Better to Have a Little with Love

16Better a little with fear of the LORD than great treasure with trouble. 17Better a dish of vegetables where love is than a fattened ox with hatred. 18An angry person stirs up arguments, but a patient person calms quarrels. 19The path of the lazy is like a hedge of thorns, but the way of the upright is a highway.

20A wise son makes his father glad, but a foolish person despises his mother. 21Folly brings joy to a person without sense, but a person of understanding walks straight. 22Plans fail without advice, but with many counselors they succeed. 23A person finds joy in a fitting answer, and a word at the right time—how good it is! 24The path of life leads upward for the wise, so they may turn away from the grave below.

The LORD's Justice and the Wise Heart

25The LORD tears down the proud person's house, but HeheHe sets the widow's boundary firm. 26The LORD hates the thoughts of the wicked, but gracious words are pure. 27A greedy person troubles his own home, but whoever hates bribes will live. 28The heart of the righteous thinks carefully before answering, but the mouth of the wicked pours out evil.

29The LORD is far from the wicked, but HeheHe hears the prayers of the righteous. 30The light of the eyes brings joy to the heart, and good news strengthens the bones. 31An ear that listens to life-giving correction will live among the wise. 32Whoever ignores instruction despises himself, but whoever listens to correction gains understanding. 33The fear of the LORD teaches wisdom, and humility comes before honourhonourhonor.
