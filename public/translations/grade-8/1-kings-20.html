Ben-Hadad Threatens Samaria

1Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, gathered all his army. With him were thirty-two kings, along with horses and chariots. He marched up, besieged Samaria, and attacked it. 2He sent messengers into the city to Ahab, king of Israel. 3Ben-Hadad sent this message: "Your silver and gold belong to me, and your best wives and children belong to me too."

4Ahab answered, "As you say, my lord the king, I and everything I have are yours."

5The messengers returned with another message from Ben-Hadad: "I sent to take your silver, gold, wives, and children. But tomorrow about this time I will send my servants to search your house and your officials' houses. 6They will take everything you value most."

7Then Ahab called all the elders of the land and said, "Look and see how this man is looking for trouble. He sent for my wives, children, silver, and gold, and I did not refuse him."

8All the elders and the people said, "Do not listen to him or agree."

9So Ahab told Ben-Hadad's messengers, "Tell my lord the king, 'I will do everything you first asked, but I cannot do this.'" ThetheThe messengers carried his answer back.

10Ben-Hadad sent back this threat: "May the gods punish me if I don't grind Samaria to dust, leaving not even a handful for each man with me."

11Ahab replied, "Don't brag about winning before you have even fought."

12When Ben-Hadad heard this message, he and the kings were drinking in the shelters. He ordered his men, "Get ready to attack the city." So they prepared to attack Samaria.

God Promises Victory

13Just then a prophet came to Ahab, king of Israel, and said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'Do you see that huge army? I will hand it over to you today, so you will know that I am the the LORD.'"

14Ahab asked, "Who will do this?"

The prophet answered, "This is what the the LORD says: 'The young men under the district commanders will do it.'"

"Who will start the battle?" Ahab asked.

"You will," the prophet said.

15Ahab counted the young men under the district commanders: 232. After them he counted all the Israelites: 7,000.

16They marched out at noon, while Ben-Hadad and the thirty-two kings helping him were getting drunk in the shelters. 17The young men under the district commanders marched out first. Ben-Hadad sent out scouts, who reported, "Men are coming out of Samaria."

18Ben-Hadad said, "If they've come in peace, capture them alive. If they've come to fight, capture them alive too."

19Those young men and the army behind them left the city. 20Each Israelite killed his opponent, and the Arameans ran away. Israel chased them, but Ben-Hadad escaped on horseback with some charioteers. 21Ahab went out and struck down the horses and chariots, killing many Arameans.

22Then the prophet came to the king of Israel and said, "Go, strengthen yourself and plan carefully, because at this time next year the king of Aram will attack you again."

Aram Attacks Again

23Meanwhile, the king of Aram's officials said to him, "TheirtheirTheir gods are gods of the hills, so they were stronger than us. But if we fight them on the flat land, we will be stronger than them." 24"Do this: remove the kings from their commands and put governors in their place. 25Raise an army as large as the one you lost, with as many horses and chariots. Then we will fight them on the flat land and surely be stronger." He listened to them and did so.

26The next year, at the same time, Ben-Hadad gathered the Arameans and went up to Aphek to fight Israel. 27The Israelites were also mustered and given supplies. They went out to meet the Arameans and camped opposite them. The Israelites looked like two small flocks of goats, while the Arameans covered the countryside.

28The man of God came to the king of Israel and said, "This is what the the LORD says: 'Because the Arameans said, "ThetheThe the LORD is a god of the hills, but not a god of the valleys," I will hand this huge army over to you. Then you will know that I am the the LORD.'"

29For seven days the two armies camped opposite each other. On the seventh day the battle began, and the Israelites killed 100,000 Aramean foot soldiers in one day. 30The rest fled into Aphek, but the city wall fell on 27,000 of them. Ben-Hadad fled into the city and hid in an inner room.

Ahab Spares Ben-Hadad

31His officials said to him, "We've heard that the kings of Israel are merciful. Let's put sackcloth around our waists and ropes on our heads and go to the king of Israel. Maybe he will spare your life."

32So they wore sackcloth and ropes and went to the king of Israel. They said, "YouryourYour servant Ben-Hadad says, 'Please let me live.'"

Ahab asked, "IsisIs he still alive? He is my brother."

33The men took this as a good sign and quickly said, "Yes, your brother Ben-Hadad!"

"Go get him," Ahab said. Then Ben-Hadad came out to him, and Ahab had him ride in his chariot.

34Ben-Hadad said, "I will return the cities my father took from your father. You may set up trading centers for yourself in Damascus, as my father did in Samaria."

Ahab said, "On these terms I will let you go." So he made a treaty with him and sent him away.

35At the the LORD's command, one of the sons of the prophets said to his companion, "Strike me." But the man refused. 36The prophet said, "Because you did not obey the the LORD's voice, a lion will attack you as soon as you leave." As soon as he left, a lion found him and killed him.

37The prophet found another man and said, "Strike me." The man struck him and wounded him.

38Then the prophet went and stood by the road, waiting for the king. He disguised himself with a bandage over his eyes. 39As the king passed by, the prophet cried out to him, "YouryourYour servant went into the middle of the battle. A man turned aside and brought another man to me and said, 'Guard this man. If he gets away, you will pay with your life, or you will pay a talent of silver.'"

40"While your servant was busy here and there, the man disappeared."

42The prophet said to him, "This is what the the LORD says: 'Because you let go the man I had set apart for destruction, your life will take his place, and your people will take his people's place.'"
