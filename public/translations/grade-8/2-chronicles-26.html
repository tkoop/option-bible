Uzziah Becomes King

1All the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. 2He rebuilt the town of Eloth and brought it back under Judah's control after Amaziah died.

3Uzziah was sixteen when he became king, and he ruled for fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah, and she was from Jerusalem. 4He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done.

Uzziah Seeks God and Wins Battles

5He sought the LORD during the days of Zechariah, who taught him to honourhonourhonor God. As long as he sought the LORD, God gave him success. 6He went to war against the Philistines. He broke down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. Then he built cities near Ashdod and in Philistia. 7God helped him defeat the Philistines, the Arabs who lived in Gur-baal, and the Meunites. 8The Ammonites paid tribute to Uzziah. His fame spread all the way to the border of Egypt, because he had become very strong.

9Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the CornercornerCorner GategateGate, the ValleyvalleyValley GategateGate, and the Angle. He made them stronger. 10He also built towers in the desert and dug many wells, because he had large herds in the western hills and on the plains. He had farmers and grape growers in the hills and in Carmel, because he loved the land.

Uzziah's Army and Weapons

11Uzziah had an army ready for battle. It was organized into groups based on the records kept by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer. They worked under Hananiah, one of the king's officials. 12The total number of family leaders among the fighting men was 2,600. 13Under them served an army of 307,500 trained soldiers. This powerful force helped the king fight his enemies. 14Uzziah gave all the soldiers shields, spears, helmets, armourarmourarmor, bows, and stones for slings. 15In Jerusalem he had skilled workers build machines for the towers and corners. These machines could shoot arrows and throw large stones. His fame spread far and wide, because God helped him in amazing ways until he became strong.

Uzziah's Pride and Leprosy

16But when he became strong, he grew proud, and this led to his ruin. He sinned against the LORD his God and went into the temple of the LORD to burn incense on the incense altar. 17Azariah the priest followed him in, along with eighty brave priests of the LORD. 18They stood against King Uzziah and said to him, "It is not for you, Uzziah, to burn incense to the LORD. That duty belongs to the priests, the sons of Aaron, who have been set apart to burn incense. Get out of the holy place, because you have sinned against God, and you will not be honouredhonouredhonored by the LORD God."

19Uzziah became angry. While he was angry with the priests, a skin disease broke out on his forehead right there in the temple of the LORD, near the incense altar. 20Azariah the chief priest and all the priests looked at him and saw that he had a skin disease on his forehead. They hurried him out of the temple. He also hurried to leave, because the LORD had struck him.

21King Uzziah had the skin disease until the day he died. He lived in a separate house because of it, and he was kept away from the temple of the LORD. His son Jotham was in charge of the palace and ruled the people of the land. 22The other things Uzziah did, from beginning to end, are written in the visions of Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz.

Uzziah's Death

23Uzziah died and was buried with his ancestors in the royal burial field, because people said, "HeheHe has leprosy." Then his son Jotham became king in his place.
