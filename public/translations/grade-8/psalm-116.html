A Psalm of Thanksgiving

1I love the the LORD, because HeheHe hears my voice and my cries for mercy. 2He turned HishisHis ear toward me, so I will call on HimhimHim as long as I live. 3Death closed in on me. The grave's terror came over me. Trouble and sorrow crushed me. 4Then I called on the name of the the LORD: "O the LORD, save my life!"

5The the LORD is kind and does what is right. Our God is full of mercy. 6The the LORD watches over the helpless. I was brought low, and HeheHe saved me. 7My soul, return to your rest, for the the LORD has been good to you. 8He rescued my life from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from stumbling. 9I will walk with the the LORD in the land of the living.

10I believed, so I said, "I am in deep trouble." 11In my fear I said, "Everyone lies."

12What can I give the the LORD for all the good things HeheHe has done for me? 13I will lift the cup that celebrates HishisHis rescue, and I will call on the name of the the LORD. 14I will keep my promises to the the LORD where all HishisHis people can see.

15The death of HishisHis faithful people matters greatly to the the LORD. 16O the LORD, I am truly YouryourYour servant. I am YouryourYour servant, the son of YouryourYour servant woman. You have set me free from my chains. 17I will offer YouyouYou a sacrifice of thanks, and I will call on the name of the the LORD. 18I will keep my promises to the the LORD where all HishisHis people can see. 19I will praise HimhimHim in the courtyards of the the LORD’s house, right in your midst, Jerusalem. Praise the the LORD!
