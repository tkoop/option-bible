The Philistines Take the Ark to Ashdod

1The Philistines took the ark of God and brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod. 2They took the ark of God and brought it into the temple of Dagon and set it beside Dagon.

Dagon Falls Before the Ark

3When the people of Ashdod got up early the next morning, there was Dagon, fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the the LORD. So they took Dagon and put him back in his place. 4But when they got up early the next morning, Dagon had fallen on his face to the ground before the ark of the the LORD. The head of Dagon and both his hands were cut off and were lying on the threshold. Only the body of Dagon was left. 5That is why, to this day, the priests of Dagon and all who enter Dagon's temple in Ashdod do not step on the threshold.

The LORD Strikes the Philistines

6The hand of the the LORD was heavy on the people of Ashdod and its nearby towns. He brought ruin on them and struck them with tumors. 7When the men of Ashdod saw what was happening, they said, "ThetheThe ark of the God of Israel must not stay with us, because HishisHis hand is heavy on us and on our god Dagon."

8So they sent for all the Philistine rulers and asked, "What should we do with the ark of the God of Israel?"

The Philistine rulers answered, "Let the ark of the God of Israel be moved to Gath." So they moved the ark of the God of Israel.

9But after they moved it, the hand of the the LORD was against the city, causing great panic. He struck the people of the city, young and old, with an outbreak of tumors.

The Ark Is Sent to Ekron

10So they sent the ark of God to Ekron. But when the ark of God arrived in Ekron, the people of Ekron cried out, "They have brought the ark of the God of Israel to us to kill us and our people!"

11They sent for all the Philistine rulers and said, "Send away the ark of the God of Israel. Let it go back to its own place, so it will not kill us and our people." For deadly panic had spread through the whole city, and God's hand was very heavy there.
