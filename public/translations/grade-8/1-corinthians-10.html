Lessons from Israel's Past

1I don't want you to be unaware, brothers, that all our ancestors were under the cloud and all passed through the sea. 2They were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea. 3They all ate the same spiritual food. 4They all drank the same spiritual drink, because they drank from a spiritual rock that went with them. That rock was Christ. 5But God was not pleased with most of them, and they died in the wilderness. 6These things happened to teach us a lesson. We should not crave evil things the way they did. 7Do not be idolaters, as some of them were. As it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink, and then they got up to play.” 8We must not be sexually immoral, as some of them were. Twenty-three thousand of them died in one day. 9We must not put the Lord to the test, as some of them did. They were killed by snakes. 10We must not grumble, as some of them did. They were killed by the destroyer. 11All these things happened to them as warnings, and they were written down to teach us. We are the people who are living at the end of the ages. 12So if anyone thinks he is standing firm, he should be careful not to fall. 13No temptation has come to you that people have not faced before. God is faithful. He will not let you be tempted more than you can bear. He will give you a way out when you are tempted, so you can endure it.

Flee from Idol Worship

14So, my dear friends, run away from worshiping idols. 15I am speaking to sensible people. Judge for yourselves what I say. 16The cup of blessing that we give thanks for—isn't it a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break—isn't it a sharing in the body of Christ? 17Because there is one loaf, we who are many are one body. We all share in that one loaf. 18Look at the people of Israel in the usual way. Aren't those who eat the sacrifices partners at the altar? 19What am I saying then? That food offered to idols means anything, or that an idol is anything? 20No. I mean that when people who don't know God offer sacrifices, they are really offering them to demons, not to God. I don't want you to share in what demons do. 21You can't drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons too. You can't share in the Lord's table and the table of demons. 22Are we trying to make the Lord jealous? Are we stronger than HeheHe is?

Freedom, Conscience, and God's Glory

23Everything is allowed, but not everything helps. Everything is allowed, but not everything builds people up. 24No one should look out only for himself. Each person should look out for the good of others. 25Eat anything sold in the meat market. Don't ask questions about it because of conscience. 26For the earth belongs to the Lord, and everything in it. 27If an unbeliever invites you to a meal and you want to go, eat whatever is put in front of you. Don't ask questions about it because of conscience. 28But if someone says to you, “This food was offered to an idol,” then don't eat it. Do it for the sake of the person who told you and for the sake of conscience. 29I'm not talking about your conscience. I'm talking about the other person's conscience. Why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience? 30If I eat with thankfulness, why should I be criticized for food I thank God for? 31So whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do everything for the glory of God. 32Don't cause anyone to stumble—not Jews, not Greeks, and not God's church. 33I try to please everyone in everything I do. I don't look for what helps me. I look for what helps many people, so they can be saved.
