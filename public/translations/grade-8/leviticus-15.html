Rules for a Man with a Discharge

1The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. 2He said to speak to the Israelites and tell them: When any man has a discharge from his body, that discharge makes him unclean. 3This is how his discharge makes him unclean: whether his body lets the discharge flow or holds it back, it is his uncleanness. 4Every bed he lies on is unclean, and everything he sits on is unclean. 5Anyone who touches his bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 6Anyone who sits on something the man with the discharge sat on must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 7Anyone who touches the body of the man with the discharge must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 8If the man with the discharge spits on someone who is clean, that person must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 9Every saddle he rides on is unclean. 10Anyone who touches anything under him is unclean until evening. Whoever carries those things must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 11If the man with the discharge touches someone without first rinsing his hands in water, that person must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 12A clay pot he touches must be broken, and any wooden article must be rinsed with water. 13When the man with the discharge is cleansed from it, he must count seven days for his cleansing. He must wash his clothes and bathe his body in running water; then he is clean. 14On the eighth day he must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons. He must come before the the LORD at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting and give them to the priest. 15The priest must sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest makes atonement for him before the the LORD for his discharge.

Rules About Semen

16When a man has an emission of semen, he must bathe his whole body in water, and he is unclean until evening. 17Any clothing or leather that has semen on it must be washed with water, and it is unclean until evening. 18When a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, both must bathe in water, and they are unclean until evening.

Rules for a Woman's Bleeding

19When a woman has a discharge of blood from her body, she is unclean for seven days. Anyone who touches her is unclean until evening. 20Anything she lies on during her period is unclean, and anything she sits on is unclean. 21Anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 22Anyone who touches anything she sits on must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 23If something is on the bed or on the thing she sits on, and someone touches it, he is unclean until evening. 24If a man lies with her and her period comes on him, he is unclean for seven days, and every bed he lies on is unclean. 25If a woman has a flow of blood for many days at a time other than her monthly period, or if it continues beyond her period, she is unclean all the days of her flow, just as she is during her period. 26Any bed she lies on during the days of her flow is unclean, like her bed during her period. Anything she sits on is unclean, like the uncleanness of her period. 27Anyone who touches them is unclean; he must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he is unclean until evening. 28When she is cleansed from her flow, she must count seven days, and then she is clean. 29On the eighth day she must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the TenttentTent of MeetingmeetingMeeting. 30The priest must sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest makes atonement for her before the the LORD for her unclean flow.

Why These Rules Matter

31You must keep the Israelites separate from their uncleanness, so they will not die in their uncleanness by making MymyMy tabernacle unclean, which is among them. 32These are the rules for the man with a discharge, for anyone who has an emission of semen and becomes unclean by it, 33for the woman in her period, and for anyone, male or female, who has a discharge. This also applies to a man who lies with an unclean woman.
