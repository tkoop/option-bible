Israel Refuses to Enter the Land

1Then the whole community raised their voices and cried out, and the people wept that night. 2All the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole community said to them, "If only we had died in Egypt! Or if only we had died in this wilderness! 3Why is the LORD bringing us into this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and children will become loot. Wouldn't it be better for us to go back to Egypt?" 4So they said to one another, "Let's appoint a leader and return to Egypt."

Joshua and Caleb Urge Faith

5Then Moses and Aaron fell face down before the whole community of Israel. 6Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among the men who scouted the land, tore their clothes 7and said to the whole community of Israel, "ThetheThe land we passed through and explored is very, very good. 8If the LORD is pleased with us, HeheHe will bring us into this land, a land flowing with milk and honey, and HeheHe will give it to us. 9Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not be afraid of the people of the land. They will be easy for us to defeat. Their protection is gone, and the LORD is with us. Do not be afraid of them!"

Israel's Failed Attack

39When Moses told all the Israelites these words, the people mourned deeply. 40Early the next morning they set out for the top of the hill country, saying, "Look, we have sinned! Now we are ready to go up to the place the LORD promised." 41But Moses said, "Why are you now disobeying the LORD's command? This will not succeed. 42Do not go up, or your enemies will strike you down, because the LORD is not with you. 43The Amalekites and Canaanites are there ahead of you, and you will fall by the sword. Because you turned away from the LORD, HeheHe will not be with you." 44But they boldly went up to the top of the hill country, even though the ark of the LORD's covenant and Moses did not leave the camp. 45Then the Amalekites and Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down, attacked them, and chased them all the way to Hormah.
