Sending Unclean People Outside the Camp

1The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 2"Command the Israelites to send out of the camp every person with a skin disease, everyone with a bodily discharge, and everyone who is unclean because of a dead body. 3Send out both men and women. Send them outside the camp so they will not make their camp unclean, where I live among them." 4The Israelites did this. They sent them outside the camp, just as the LORD had told Moses.

Paying Back What Was Wrong

5The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 6"Speak to the Israelites: When a man or woman sins against the LORD by doing something people often do wrong, that person is guilty. 7They must confess the sin they committed. They must pay back in full for the wrong they did, and add one-fifth more. They must give it to the person they wronged. 8If the person has no close relative to receive the payment, the payment for the wrong goes to the LORD for the priest. This is in addition to the ram used to make atonement for them. 9Every holy gift the Israelites bring to the priest belongs to him. 10Each person's holy things belong to that person, but whatever anyone gives to the priest belongs to the priest."

Test for an Unfaithful Wife

11The LORD spoke to Moses. He said, 12"Speak to the Israelites: If a man's wife is unfaithful to him, 13and another man sleeps with her secretly, so her husband does not know, and she becomes unclean, but there is no witness against her and she was not caught, 14and a jealous spirit comes over him, and he is jealous of his wife, whether she has been unclean or not, 15then the man must bring his wife to the priest. He must bring an offering for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering that brings her wrong to mind. 16The priest must bring her near and set her before the LORD. 17The priest must take holy water in a clay jar. He must take some dust from the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water. 18The priest must set the woman before the LORD and let her hair hang loose. He must put the grain offering of remembrance, which is the grain offering of jealousy, into her hands. The priest must hold the bitter water that brings a curse. 19Then the priest must make her take an oath. He must say to the woman, 'If no man has slept with you, and if you have not been unfaithful to your husband, then you will be free from this bitter water that brings a curse. 20But if you have been unfaithful to your husband, and you have made yourself unclean, and a man other than your husband has slept with you, 21then may the LORD make you a curse and an oath among your people. May the LORD make your thigh waste away and your belly swell. 22May this cursed water go into your body and make your belly swell and your thigh waste away.' Then the woman must say, 'Amen, AmenamenAmen.' 23Then the priest must write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water. 24He must make the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse, and the water that brings a curse will enter her and become bitter. 25The priest must take the grain offering of jealousy from the woman's hand and wave it before the LORD. Then he must bring it to the altar. 26The priest must take a handful of the grain offering as a reminder and burn it on the altar. After that, he must make the woman drink the water. 27When he has made her drink the water, if she has made herself unclean and been unfaithful to her husband, the cursed water will enter her and become bitter. Her belly will swell, and her thigh will waste away. The woman will become a curse among her people. 28But if the woman has not made herself unclean and is clean, she will be free and will have children. 29This is the law for jealousy when a wife, while married to her husband, goes astray and makes herself unclean, 30or when a jealous spirit comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife. Then he must set the woman before the LORD, and the priest must do to her all this law. 31The man will be free from guilt, but the woman will carry her guilt."
