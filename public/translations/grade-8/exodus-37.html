Bezalel Makes the Ark

1Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood. It was two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one and a half cubits high. 2He covered it with pure gold inside and out, and he made a gold molding around it. 3He cast four gold rings for it and fastened them to its four corners. He put two rings on one side and two rings on the other side. 4He made poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold. 5He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark so it could be carried.

6He made the mercy seat of pure gold. It was two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 7He made two cherubim of hammered gold at the two ends of the mercy seat. 8One cherub was at one end and the other at the other end. He made them as one piece with the mercy seat. 9The cherubim had wings spread upward, and their wings covered the mercy seat. Their faces looked toward each other, toward the mercy seat.

The Table and the Lampstand

10He made the table of acacia wood. It was two cubits long, one cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high. 11He covered it with pure gold and made a gold molding around it. 12He made a frame around it one handbreadth wide, and he made a gold molding around the frame. 13He cast four gold rings for it and attached them to the four corners by its four legs. 14The rings were close to the frame. They held the poles used to carry the table. 15He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.

16He made the table's pieces of pure gold: its plates, dishes, bowls, and jars used for pouring drink offerings. 17He made the lampstand of pure gold. He hammered it out so its base, stem, branches, cups, buds, and flowers were all one piece. 18Six branches came out from its sides: three from one side and three from the other. 19Each branch had three cups shaped like almond flowers, with a bud and a flower. This was true for all six branches coming out of the lampstand. 20The lampstand itself had four cups shaped like almond flowers, with buds and flowers. 21A bud was under the first two branches that came out of it, a bud was under the next two branches, and a bud was under the last two branches. This was for all six branches. 22The buds and branches were one piece with it. The whole lampstand was one piece of hammered pure gold. 23He made its seven lamps, its wick trimmers, and its trays of pure gold. 24He used one talent of pure gold for the lampstand and all its tools.

The Altar of Incense and Holy Supplies

25He made the altar of incense from acacia wood. It was square, one cubit long, one cubit wide, and two cubits high. Its horns were made as one piece with it. 26He covered its top, sides, and horns with pure gold. He made a gold molding around it. 27He made two gold rings under the molding on two opposite sides. They held the poles used to carry it. 28He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold. 29He made the holy anointing oil and the pure, sweet-smelling incense. A skilled perfumer made them.
