Instructions for the Tabernacle Curtains

1You are to make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine linen. Weave blue, purple, and scarlet yarn into them, and work cherubim into them with skill. 2Each curtain is to be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains are to be the same size. 3Join five curtains together into one set, and join the other five together into another set. 4Make loops of blue yarn along the edge of the last curtain in the first set. Do the same along the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 5Make fifty loops on one curtain and fifty loops on the end curtain of the second set, so the loops face each other. 6Make fifty gold clasps and join the curtains with them, so the tabernacle becomes one piece.

The Goat Hair Tent Covering

7You are to make curtains of goat hair for the tent over the tabernacle. Make eleven curtains in all. 8Each of the eleven curtains is to be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide. 9Join five curtains into one set and the other six into another set. Then fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent. 10Make fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and fifty loops along the edge of the matching curtain in the second set. 11Make fifty bronze clasps and put them in the loops to join the tent into one piece. 12The extra part of the tent curtains, the half curtain left over, is to hang down over the back of the tabernacle. 13The tent curtains will be one cubit longer on each side. The extra part is to hang over the sides of the tabernacle to cover it. 14Make a covering for the tent of ram skins dyed red, and over that a covering of fine leather.

The Wooden Frames

15You are to make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle. 16Each frame is to be ten cubits long and one and a half cubits wide. 17Put two pegs on each frame to join them together. Make all the frames of the tabernacle this way. 18Make twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle. 19Make forty silver bases to go under the twenty frames—two bases for each frame, one under each peg. 20For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, make twenty frames. 21Make forty silver bases—two bases under each frame. 22Make six frames for the rear of the tabernacle, the west side. 23Make two frames for the two back corners of the tabernacle. 24Join them from bottom to top and fit them into a single ring. Make both corners the same way. 25So there are to be eight frames and sixteen silver bases—two bases under each frame. 26You are to make five crossbars of acacia wood for the frames on one side of the tabernacle. 27Make five for the other side, and five for the rear side of the tabernacle, to the west. 28The middle crossbar is to run from end to end, halfway up the frames. 29Overlay the frames with gold and make gold rings to hold the crossbars. Also overlay the crossbars with gold. 30you are to set up the tabernacle according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain.
