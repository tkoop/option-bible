About

Option Bible is based on the Berean Standard Bible. Since this translation is in the public domain, it allows us to customize it as we like.

The rest of the software is licensed under the MIT license.

The word study pages quote Strong's Dictionaries of Hebrew and Greek, by James Strong (1890 Greek, 1894 Hebrew), as published by Open Scriptures under CC-BY-SA. Every other number on those pages is counted out of the BSB's own tables.
